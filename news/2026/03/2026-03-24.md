## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI announces Sora API discontinuation — 30-day notice; full API shut-off 24 September** — OpenAI announced via X on 24 March that Sora — both the mobile app and the API — is being discontinued in stages. Sora 1 was already removed in the U.S. on 13 March; the Sora web/app experience will close 26 April; the Sora API will be discontinued 24 September 2026. OpenAI cited unsustainable economics: roughly $1 M/day in compute and an installed base that fell from a peak of ~1 M actives to fewer than 500 K. Compute is being redirected toward coding tools and enterprise products with more predictable revenue. _Why it matters:_ The first explicit consumer-AI product retirement from OpenAI on cost grounds — and a strong directional signal: text-to-video at consumer prices is not yet a paying business at frontier-model compute economics, while enterprise/coding deployments are. Reinforces the GTC-week framing that inference economics now drive product portfolio decisions. [OpenAI — what to know about the Sora discontinuation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation) · [Tech Insider — Sora shutdown Disney $150 M deal](https://tech-insider.org/openai-sora-shutdown-disney-deal-ai-video-2026/) · [GenMediaLab — OpenAI shuts down Sora](https://www.genmedialab.com/news/openai-shuts-down-sora-app/) · [Futurum Group — what the end of a platform means](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/openai-sora-discontinuation-what-the-end-of-a-platform-means-for-enterprise-ai-strategy/)

**Atlassian lays off 1,600 (10% of workforce), pivots to AI development and enterprise sales** — Atlassian announced a 1,600-headcount reduction (10% of staff), redirecting resources toward AI development and enterprise sales. The pivot lines up with Atlassian Rovo/Atlassian Intelligence shipping cycles and reads as the first big enterprise-SaaS layoff explicitly framed as an AI-spend reallocation. _Why it matters:_ The Cloudflare/AI-displacement layoff on 8 May still six weeks away, but Atlassian's framing — staff out, AI in — becomes a template the broader software-vendor cohort will explicitly copy through Q2. [DevFlokers — AI news March 24 2026](https://www.devflokers.com/blog/ai-news-march-24-2026-releases-breakthroughs)

**NVIDIA NemoCLAW deep coverage — kernel-level sandbox plus "privacy router" for outgoing comms** — Detailed write-ups of NemoCLAW (the managed enterprise-grade security service integrating Nemotron with the OpenShell runtime) landed mid-week. The service ships a kernel-level sandbox for agents and a "privacy router" that monitors all outgoing agent communications — the most concrete answer in market to data-exfiltration concerns for autonomous-agent deployments. _Why it matters:_ Sets the new compliance baseline. Microsoft Defender for AI, Palo Alto Prisma AIRS and CrowdStrike's agentic-AI guardrail products will all be benchmarked against NemoCLAW's primitives through the rest of 2026. [DevFlokers — March 24 daily digest](https://www.devflokers.com/blog/ai-news-march-24-2026-releases-breakthroughs)

**Oracle announces upcoming GA of OCI Enterprise AI — end-to-end build/manage/deploy for production workloads** — Oracle's AI & Data Science blog announced the upcoming general availability of OCI Enterprise AI, an end-to-end developer offering that bundles build/manage/deploy capabilities for production AI workloads regardless of structured or unstructured data sources. The release positions OCI as the "experimentation-to-production" path for Oracle's database installed base. _Why it matters:_ Continues the GTC-week enterprise build-out — Oracle joining Snowflake (SnowWork, 18 Mar), ServiceNow (AI Gateway, 19 Mar), and Cisco (Secure AI Factory, 16 Mar) in the wave of enterprise AI control planes shipping. Each one frames itself as "the place enterprises go from pilot to production." [Oracle AI & Data Science blog — OCI Enterprise AI GA](https://blogs.oracle.com/ai-and-datascience/announcing-oci-enterprise-ai-ga)
