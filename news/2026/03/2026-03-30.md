## Major announcements & releases

**Salesforce previews 30+ new Slackbot AI features ahead of Tuesday launch** — Salesforce pre-briefed analysts and press on Monday for the Tuesday 31 March formal launch of Slackbot's biggest update since the product began. Headline framing: Slack moves from "messaging app with AI" to the agent control plane — meeting transcription across any video platform, desktop activity monitoring, MCP-driven task execution against third-party tools, and a lightweight CRM. Anthropic's Claude is the back-end model; the Slack MCP server reaches general availability with the launch. _Why it matters:_ Slack with MCP is the single biggest enterprise-platform validation of the protocol to date — three days behind Anthropic's MCP 97 M install-count milestone announcement. Sets up Slack as the multi-agent rendezvous point that ServiceNow's Action Fabric (2 April) explicitly has to integrate with. [Fortune Tech — Salesforce reinvents Slack for AI age (April 1 follow-up)](https://fortune.com/2026/04/01/salesforce-reinvents-slack-ai-age-takes-aim-at-microsoft-copilot/) · [Salesforce Break — Slackbot just got 30 new AI features](https://salesforcebreak.com/2026/04/06/slackbot-30-new-ai-features/)

**OpenAI funding round wraps up to $122 B — final-day signing and SoftBank co-lead lock-in** — Final coordination among investors and signatures on the OpenAI mega-round wrapped on Monday, ahead of the formal Tuesday announcement of $122 B closed at an $852 B post-money valuation. SoftBank co-leads alongside Andreessen Horowitz, D.E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, T. Rowe Price; Amazon, Nvidia and Microsoft participate; ~$3 B comes via banks from individual investors. Revolving credit expands to ~$4.7 B with JPMorgan, Citi, Goldman, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, Mizuho, RBC, SMBC, UBS, HSBC, Santander. _Why it matters:_ Largest private funding round in history; sets the funding ceiling for the rest of the frontier-lab race. The Tuesday announcement is choreographed to land alongside the Salesforce/Slack overhaul (effectively a two-day OpenAI/Anthropic split of the news cycle, since Anthropic powers Slack's new AI surface). [OpenAI — accelerating the next phase of AI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/) · [CNBC — record $122 B funding round](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/31/openai-funding-round-ipo.html)

**Anthropic Dario Amodei in Canberra ahead of Tuesday MOU signing with Australian PM** — Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei landed in Canberra ahead of the formal MOU signing with PM Anthony Albanese on Tuesday 31 March. Pre-meeting briefings flagged the AUD$3 M Australian research partnerships (ANU, MCRI, Garvan, Curtin), data-centre investment exploration, and the Sydney office (Anthropic's fourth Asia-Pacific office) opening in 2026. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic's first government-level AI safety MOU outside the U.S./UK; functionally a template for the lab's broader public-sector engagement strategy through 2026. [Bloomberg — Anthropic expands into Australia, New Zealand with Sydney office (Mar 10 lead-in)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-10/anthropic-expands-into-australia-new-zealand-with-sydney-office) · [Anthropic — Sydney fourth office Asia-Pacific](https://www.anthropic.com/news/sydney-fourth-office-asia-pacific)

**Alibaba Qwen team releases Qwen3.5-Omni — 30B MoE multimodal (text/audio/video/image) beating Gemini 3.1 Pro on audio** — Alibaba's Qwen team launched Qwen3.5-Omni on Mar 30: a unified 30B-MoE (3B active) multimodal model handling text, image, audio, and video in a single computational pipeline. Specs: 256k context, 10+ hours of audio, 400+ seconds of 720p video at 1 FPS, speech recognition in 113 languages, speech generation in 36; Plus variant claims 215 SOTA results across audio/video tasks and outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on general audio understanding/translation. Three variants (Plus/Flash/Light), available via Alibaba Cloud API, Qwen Chat, and Hugging Face demo — but closed-weights this time, breaking the open-source streak. _Why it matters:_ Qwen's first closed-weights flagship is a strategic shift toward direct cloud-API monetisation and a credible Asia-Pacific competitor to Gemini Live and OpenAI Realtime in the multimodal-voice category — directly relevant to enterprise agent deployments where Western cloud APIs are politically constrained. [MarkTechPost — Alibaba Qwen team releases Qwen3.5-Omni](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/03/30/alibaba-qwen-team-releases-qwen3-5-omni-a-native-multimodal-model-for-text-audio-video-and-realtime-interaction/) · [eWeek — Qwen3.5-Omni debuts as Alibaba's most advanced multimodal](https://www.eweek.com/news/qwen3-5-omni-alibaba-multimodal-ai-launch/)

**OpenAI ChatGPT Slack and Workspace Agents pre-staging — Slack-companion roll-out begins** — OpenAI began the pre-staging roll-out of two Slack integrations on Monday: a connector that brings Slack context into ChatGPT, and a ChatGPT app for Slack — paired with the Workspace Agents enterprise framework rolling out gradually to ChatGPT Business workspaces. The product surfaces are timed precisely to compete with Salesforce's Tuesday Slack overhaul (Anthropic-powered). _Why it matters:_ The two product roll-outs together formalise the "every SaaS app is now an agent host" market structure ahead of Tuesday's joint announcements. OpenAI is explicitly bringing the fight to Slack-as-Anthropic-surface from inside Slack itself.
