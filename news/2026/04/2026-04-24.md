## Major announcements & releases

**Google to invest up to $40B in Anthropic — $10B now at $350B valuation, $30B contingent + 5GW Google Cloud compute** — Google announced on 24 April it will invest up to **$40B in Anthropic**: **$10B now in cash** at a $350B valuation (same level as February), plus **$30B contingent** on Anthropic hitting performance targets. Google Cloud commits to deliver **5 gigawatts of compute over 5 years** with optionality for several more GW beyond. The structure is similar to Amazon's $25B / $100B-spend deal four days earlier — staged equity tied to commercial milestones, plus a hyperscaler compute commitment. _Why it matters:_ Combined with the Amazon deal (20 April) and the Google + Broadcom 3.5GW TPU partnership (21 April), Anthropic has now disclosed **~$45B+ in committed equity capital** and **8.5GW+ of multi-cloud compute** in a single week — the largest single-company capital + capacity mobilisation in tech history. Google is explicitly diversifying its AI bets beyond Gemini. The $350B Anthropic valuation is identical to the February round — the Motley Fool calls this "a screaming bargain" given the $850–900B preemptive offers being floated. **DeepSeek V4 Preview — V4 Flash + V4 Pro MoE, 1M-token context, Huawei Ascend 950 + Supernode silicon** — DeepSeek released the **V4 Preview** on 24 April: **V4 Pro** (1.6T total / 49B active MoE) and **V4 Flash** (284B total / 13B active MoE). Both have **1M-token context windows**, "top-tier" performance in coding benchmarks, and major advances in reasoning + agentic tasks. **Pricing**: V4 Flash $0.14 / $0.28 per 1M tokens; V4 Pro $0.145 / $3.48 per 1M tokens — undercutting Gemini, GPT, and Claude. To meet compute needs, DeepSeek paired with **Huawei's "Supernode" technology** clustering **Ascend 950** chips for training. Anthropic and OpenAI accused DeepSeek of model distillation; White House OSTP Director Michael Kratsios called out China-based "industrial-scale distillation" of US frontier models. _Why it matters:_ V4 closes a year of the Western-frontier gap discussion. The Huawei silicon integration is the most explicit demonstration to date that the export-control regime is being routed around — DeepSeek trained a near-frontier model on Chinese silicon. The pricing undercuts Western models by ~10× on the cheap end; expect downstream API-pricing pressure. The distillation accusations / OSTP response is the policy escalation. **GPT-5.5 / GPT-5.5 Pro hit the API; system card finalised** — OpenAI moved **GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro into the API** on 24 April with the system card updated to describe additional safeguards applying at the API layer. Doubled per-token pricing ($5 / $30 for 5.5; flat $30 / $180 for Pro) carries through. _Why it matters:_ API GA is the developer-facing moment for GPT-5.5 — application-builders begin pricing decisions today, with the doubled-rate-card forcing serious cost-vs-capability decisions. The system-card update also addresses cyber + biology evaluation at the API level — the first frontier-lab disclosure to include separate API vs ChatGPT evaluation gates. **Cohere acquires Aleph Alpha to form $20B "transatlantic AI powerhouse" — Schwarz Group adds $600M, governments bless deal** — Cohere announced in Berlin on Apr 24 a merger with Germany's Aleph Alpha, creating a $20B combined entity (Cohere shareholders ~90%, Aleph Alpha ~10%) operating under the Cohere brand with dual HQs in Toronto and Germany. Schwarz Group invests $600M into Cohere's Series E. The announcement event included Germany's Digital Minister Karsten Wildberger and Canada's AI Minister Evan Solomon — explicit government endorsement of a "sovereign AI" alternative to U.S. frontier labs. _Why it matters:_ The largest non-U.S. AI consolidation of 2026 and the cleanest sovereign-AI play to date — Cohere absorbs Aleph Alpha's German government / DAX customer base, while Aleph Alpha gets Cohere's enterprise platform and Canadian capital. Schwarz Group's €500M+ structured financing is the most concrete European corporate bet on a non-U.S. frontier-class AI company since Mistral's earliest rounds.
