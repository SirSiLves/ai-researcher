## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic introduces Claude Mythos Preview and Project Glasswing — the first capability-gated frontier model** — Anthropic announced Project Glasswing on 7 April, an initiative to secure the world's most critical software for the AI era, powered by **Claude Mythos Preview** — the company's most capable model to date and the first model Anthropic has publicly deemed too high-risk for public release. Mythos Preview scored 31 percentage points higher than Opus 4.6 on USAMO 2026, and in pre-release testing autonomously identified and exploited thousands of previously unknown vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser — including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug and a 16-year-old FFmpeg flaw. Internal tests showed it could successfully reproduce and exploit vulnerabilities in over 80% of cases. **Launch partners (11):** AWS, Anthropic, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorganChase, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks. Anthropic extended access to over 40 additional organisations maintaining critical software infrastructure. **Commitments:** $100 M in Mythos usage credits to Glasswing partners; $4 M in direct donations to open-source security organisations. _Why it matters:_ This is the first concrete "we built it but we're not shipping it" call from a frontier lab on offensive-cyber risk. The 11-partner consortium becomes the template for future capability-gated frontier-model rollouts. Mythos is also the empirical anchor that Anthropic's $30 B ARR isn't just enterprise demand but capability differentiation. **Anthropic annualized revenue surpasses OpenAI — $30 B run rate confirmed** — Anthropic confirmed on 7 April that its annualized revenue run rate had crossed $30 billion, surpassing OpenAI's $25 B for the first time. Growth trajectory: $87M (Jan 2024) → $1B (Dec 2024) → $9B (end 2025) → $14B (Feb 2026) → $19B (Mar 2026) → $30B (Apr 2026). Enterprise customers spending $1M+ annually have doubled from ~500 (Feb) to over 1,000 (Apr). OpenAI disputes the figure as ~$8B overstated on gross-revenue accounting; that remains unresolved. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic now leads on both the dominant capability narrative (Mythos) and the dominant revenue narrative on the same day — the cleanest "lead change" moment of the AI race so far. The Mythos + $30B ARR pairing also reframes valuation expectations into Anthropic's upcoming round (the $900 B reporting lands 9 May). **Cloudflare fast-tracks post-quantum security to 2029 — adds ML-DSA authentication for origin connections** — Cloudflare announced on 7 April that advances in quantum hardware and software have accelerated the Q-Day timeline, prompting Cloudflare to move its full post-quantum security target forward to 2029. Roadmap milestones: post-quantum authentication using ML-DSA on origin connections by mid-2026; post-quantum end-user connections via Merkle Tree Certificates by mid-2027; Cloudflare One SASE post-quantum authentication by early 2028. All upgrades are free on all plans. _Why it matters:_ The accelerated timeline ties directly to the same "AI agent finds the bug before defenders patch" thesis behind Mythos — quantum-attack capability and AI-discovered classical-crypto exploits are converging faster than the standards process. Cloudflare's post-quantum push is the closest analogue at infrastructure scale to Anthropic's Glasswing partner model. **Cisco joins Project Glasswing — sets up Galileo acquisition on 9 April** — Cisco joined Anthropic's Project Glasswing initiative on 7 April as one of the 11 launch partners. The Mythos-access secured by Cisco directly sets up its 9 April acquisition of Galileo Technologies for AI-agent observability — Cisco is positioning itself as the largest enterprise-network player with both offensive-defensive AI capability access and observability tooling. _Why it matters:_ Cisco's two-day pivot (Glasswing 7 Apr → Galileo announcement 9 Apr) is the cleanest "this is now an industrial-scale AI security play" sequence of the month. The pattern repeats for several Glasswing partners through April. [Anthropic — Project Glasswing launch partners](https://www.anthropic.com/glasswing)

**Cybersecurity sector reacts: Arctic Wolf, Forrester, ASIS frame Glasswing as the new cybersecurity baseline** — Independent analysis from Arctic Wolf, Forrester, ASIS and the Centre for Emerging Technology and Security ran on 7 April framing Glasswing as a turning point. Forrester's "10 consequences nobody's writing about yet" piece argues the gated-access model permanently changes the procurement bar for enterprise cybersecurity vendors — defenders are now required to ship a Glasswing-tier capability story or get repriced. _Why it matters:_ The Glasswing-as-baseline framing is now the operating assumption for Q2 CISO procurement cycles. Splunk-now-Cisco, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks (Glasswing partners) are positioned; Fortinet and SentinelOne (not on the partner list) are not. [Arctic Wolf — Project Glasswing turning point](https://arcticwolf.com/resources/blog/project-glasswing-marks-a-turning-point-for-cybersecurity/) · [Forrester — 10 consequences nobody's writing about](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/project-glasswing-the-10-consequences-nobodys-writing-about-yet/) · [ASIS Online — Project Glasswing initiative](https://www.asisonline.org/security-management-magazine/latest-news/today-in-security/2026/april/project-glasswing/)

**Z.ai (Zhipu) releases GLM-5.1 — open-weight 744B MoE tops SWE-Bench Pro, MIT-licensed** — Z.ai (formerly Zhipu) shipped GLM-5.1 on April 7, a post-training upgrade on the GLM-5 base (744B MoE). It scored 58.4 on SWE-Bench Pro, edging GPT-5.4 (57.7) and Claude Opus 4.6 (57.3) at the top of the global leaderboard. Demoed building a Linux desktop from scratch over 655 iterations and ~8 hours unattended; pushed a vector-DB query-throughput baseline 6.9× over production. Weights on Hugging Face under MIT (no commercial restrictions). _Why it matters:_ The first open-weight model to clearly outscore Western frontier closed models on a serious agentic-coding benchmark. Combined with the same-day Mythos disclosure (Anthropic won't ship its strongest cyber model), this is the cleanest "Anthropic gates, China opens" framing of the year.
