## Major announcements & releases

**Google launches Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform — single end-to-end stack for build/scale/govern/optimise agents** — Day-2 of Cloud Next 2026: Google announced the **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform** — a direct evolution of Vertex AI into a unified workspace for building, deploying, governing, and observing AI agents. Headline primitives: Agent Designer (visual builder), Inbox (agent activity), long-running agents (multi-day state), Skills, Projects, Agent Identity, Agent Registry, Agent Gateway, and a re-engineered Agent Runtime. Model Garden offers first-class access to 200+ models including Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image (Nano Banana 2), Lyria 3 (audio), and Gemma 4. GA from 22 April. _Why it matters:_ This is Google's most coherent enterprise-agent pitch to date and directly answers AWS Bedrock AgentCore + Anthropic + OpenAI Deployment plays. Agent Identity / Registry / Gateway is the governance trinity that enterprise CISOs have asked for; the long-running agent runtime (multi-day state) is the technical primitive that productionises "agents that don't lose context after 30 minutes." [Introducing Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise-agent-platform) · [What's new in Gemini Enterprise](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/whats-new-in-gemini-enterprise) · [The new Gemini Enterprise — one platform for agent development](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/the-new-gemini-enterprise-one-platform-for-agent-development) · [TheNextWeb — A2A protocol, Workspace Studio, full-stack bet](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-cloud-next-ai-agents-agentic-era) · [Bloomberg — Google releases new AI agents to challenge OpenAI and Anthropic](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-22/google-releases-new-ai-agents-to-challenge-openai-and-anthropic)

**Google's 8th-generation TPUs: TPU 8t (Sunfish, Broadcom training) + TPU 8i (Zebrafish, MediaTek inference)** — Also on 22 April Google previewed its **eighth-generation TPU architecture**, splitting training and inference for the first time. **TPU 8t (Sunfish)**: Broadcom-designed, two compute dies + one I/O chiplet + 8 stacks of 12-high HBM3e (~30% more memory bandwidth vs Ironwood). Up to **2.7× perf-per-dollar** vs Ironwood for large-scale training. **TPU 8i (Zebrafish)**: MediaTek-designed, single compute die + 1 I/O die + 6 stacks of HBM3e — engineered for ~20-30% lower inference cost vs the training variant. Up to **80% perf-per-dollar improvement** vs Ironwood for inference, particularly low-latency MoE workloads. Both target TSMC 2nm, late 2027. **Ironwood (TPU 7x)** also went GA today — 4.6 petaFLOPS per chip, 42.5 exaFLOPS in a 9,216-chip superpod. _Why it matters:_ The training/inference split is the chip-architecture analogue to what AWS Graviton5 telegraphed for CPU agentic work — the AI economy is bifurcating between heavy training silicon and ultra-efficient inference silicon. Google's involvement of MediaTek (consumer-mobile heritage) for the inference chip is the most aggressive cost-engineering bet any hyperscaler has made. [Our eighth-generation TPUs: two chips for the agentic era](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/eighth-generation-tpu-agentic-era/) · [TPU 8t and TPU 8i technical deep-dive](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/compute/tpu-8t-and-tpu-8i-technical-deep-dive) · [CNBC — Google unveils chips for AI training and inference](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/22/google-launches-training-and-inference-tpus-in-latest-shot-at-nvidia.html) · [TheNextWeb — Ironwood + 8t/8i split](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-ironwood-tpu-inference-cloud-next) · [DCD — Google unveils 8th-gen TPUs](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-unveils-eighth-generation-tpus-two-dedicated-training-and-inference-chips/)

**Salesforce + Google Cloud expand partnership — Agentforce ↔ Gemini Enterprise integration for cross-platform agentic workflows** — Salesforce and Google Cloud announced on 22 April an expanded partnership to enable AI agents to execute end-to-end workflows across both platforms. New integrations: Agentforce and Gemini Enterprise work as a single context layer; customers deploy agents in Slack and Google Workspace; Informatica data security policies push into Google BigQuery alongside Workday and SAP enterprise data sources. Gemini-Powered Reasoning for Agentforce arrives May 2026; IDMC Google BigQuery Connectors and IDMC Apache Iceberg GCP support GA in April. _Why it matters:_ This is Salesforce's deepest data-cloud collaboration since the 2023 Informatica acquisition. The single-context layer between Agentforce and Gemini Enterprise is the explicit answer to "agents that can't act across SaaS boundaries" — and a clear competitive move against Microsoft 365 / Azure as the default agent fabric. [Salesforce + Google Cloud — agents act across platforms](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/04/22/salesforce-google-cloud-launch-new-integrations-deep-context/)

**Accenture + Google Cloud expand partnership to scale agentic transformation with Gemini Enterprise** — Also on 22 April, Accenture and Google Cloud announced an expanded partnership to scale Gemini Enterprise across Accenture's enterprise customer base — joint go-to-market, joint solution development, sector-specific Gemini Enterprise agent libraries (financial services, retail, healthcare, public sector). Accenture commits expanded Gemini Enterprise practice; Google commits engineering investment in joint sector accelerators. _Why it matters:_ The expanded SI deal is the channel commitment that makes Gemini Enterprise an enterprise default rather than a Google-pure-play product. Combined with Salesforce + Google integration, the day's two announcements transform Gemini Enterprise from a vendor product into a multi-partner ecosystem. [Accenture — partnership to scale agentic transformation with Gemini Enterprise](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-and-google-cloud-expand-partnership-to-scale-agentic-transformation-for-global-enterprises-with-gemini-enterprise)
