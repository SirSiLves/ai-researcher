## Major announcements & releases

**Microsoft–OpenAI restructure their partnership: non-exclusive license, revenue-share cap, Azure-first through 2032** — Microsoft and OpenAI on 27 April announced an amended deal that simplifies their relationship. Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner and OpenAI products ship first on Azure (unless Microsoft cannot or chooses not to support a capability), but Microsoft's IP license is now non-exclusive through 2032, and the revenue-share to Microsoft continues only through 2030 and is now subject to a total cap. Microsoft retains its equity stake. The reset clears the legal path for OpenAI to multi-cloud — including the AWS Bedrock launch that lands the next morning. _Why it matters:_ This is the formal end of the Azure exclusivity that defined LLM hyperscaler economics since 2023. Microsoft trades a wider distribution moat for capex relief; OpenAI trades dependency for optionality. The cap on revenue-share payments is the most-cited change among investors. [Microsoft blog — the next phase of the partnership](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/04/27/the-next-phase-of-the-microsoft-openai-partnership/) · [OpenAI blog — next phase of Microsoft partnership](https://openai.com/index/next-phase-of-microsoft-partnership/) · [CNBC — revenue-share cap](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/27/openai-microsoft-partnership-revenue-cap.html) · [TechCrunch — OpenAI ends Microsoft legal peril over $50B Amazon deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/27/openai-ends-microsoft-legal-peril-over-its-50b-amazon-deal/) · [The Register — "open relationship is now official"](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/27/microsofts_and_openai_change_relationship/)

**Meta signs multi-billion-dollar AWS Graviton5 deal to run agentic AI on CPUs** — Meta announced an agreement with AWS to deploy tens of millions of Graviton5 Arm cores for CPU-intensive agentic AI workloads — real-time reasoning, code generation, search, and multi-step task orchestration. AWS's 3nm Graviton5 has 192 cores per chip with a cache 5× the previous generation, and AWS claims a 25% performance lift and 33% lower inter-core latency. The deal makes Meta one of the five largest Graviton customers worldwide. _Why it matters:_ This reframes the AI-infra story from GPU scarcity to CPU bottleneck for the inference/agent layer. "No single chip architecture can efficiently serve every workload" is the operative quote; the deal is the clearest public signal that agentic workloads are CPU-bound on the orchestration side. **Anthropic opens Sydney office; names Theo Hourmouzis (ex-Snowflake) as ANZ General Manager; signs Canva, Xero and four Australian universities** — Anthropic published its ANZ launch announcement on 27 April. Hourmouzis joins from Snowflake (where he ran the ANZ and ASEAN business) to lead Anthropic's first office in the region. Launch customers and partners disclosed include Canva, Xero, and four Australian universities. Hourmouzis is meeting customers/partners this week alongside Anthropic's global team. _Why it matters:_ The Sydney office is Anthropic's first APAC outpost beyond Tokyo; the Canva/Xero anchor wins double as case studies for SMB and developer-platform deployments respectively. Hiring a Snowflake regional chief signals Anthropic's enterprise GTM playbook is converging on data-cloud-style account coverage. [Anthropic — Hourmouzis appointed GM ANZ](https://www.anthropic.com/news/theo-hourmouzis-general-manager-australia-new-zealand) · [ITBrief AU coverage](https://itbrief.com.au/story/anthropic-names-theo-hourmouzis-as-anz-chief-in-sydney) · [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/anthropic-appoints-theo-hourmouzis-to-lead-anz-as-sydney-office-officially-opens) · [ARN](https://www.arnnet.com.au/article/4163981/anthropic-opens-in-sydney-with-theo-hourmouzis-at-the-helm.html)

**Claude Cowork generally available in Amazon Bedrock as AWS–Anthropic enterprise integration deepens** — As part of AWS's weekly summary on 27 April, Anthropic and AWS announced that Claude Cowork — the desktop-agent that manages workflows on the user's computer, summarising meetings, drafting documents, and executing multi-step tasks — is now available in Amazon Bedrock for enterprise builders. The integration also surfaces the new Amazon Bedrock AgentCore CLI for stitching Bedrock-side agent primitives into existing pipelines. _Why it matters:_ Cowork was Anthropic's most-cited consumer/desktop play after Claude Code; Bedrock GA brings it inside the regulated-enterprise envelope alongside Claude Code and the now-five-gigawatt Trainium compute commitment Amazon disclosed in March. The follow-on AWS event on 28 April formalises the broader OpenAI Bedrock launch, so the Anthropic GA timing is deliberate. [AWS Weekly Roundup — 27 April](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-weekly-roundup-anthropic-meta-partnership-aws-lambda-s3-files-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-cli-and-more-april-27-2026/)
