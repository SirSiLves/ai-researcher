## Major announcements & releases

**Meta's Muse Spark + "scaling ladder" — first proprietary model from Superintelligence Labs, 10× less training compute** — Meta's Superintelligence Labs continued the rollout of Muse Spark (originally code-named Avocado) — its first proprietary model since the SI Labs formation — and surfaced new April commentary on the "scaling ladder" training-efficiency curve. The model needed less than one-tenth the training compute of Llama 4 Maverick a year earlier, achieved via "thought compression" RL that penalises excessive reasoning tokens. Meta's commentary this week framed Muse Spark as a generational compute-efficiency leap — and confirmed it ships **proprietary** with future open-source versions hoped-for rather than committed. _Why it matters:_ Muse Spark is the cleanest concrete proof point this month that the Density Law (capability doubling every ~88 days) is holding even on the training side. It also signals Meta's strategic pivot away from Llama-style open release — closed for the first version, with open-source weights re-evaluated later. [VentureBeat — Meta launches Muse Spark](https://venturebeat.com/technology/goodbye-llama-meta-launches-new-proprietary-ai-model-muse-spark-first-since) · [36kr — Density Law verified by Meta + METR](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3765157269684738)

**Microsoft Foundry's April release wave — MAI-Transcribe-1, MAI-Voice-1, MAI-Image-2 in public preview** — Microsoft's mid-April Foundry release notes wrapped a wave of in-house MAI model availability for builders. MAI-Transcribe-1 (enterprise STT, 25 languages, ~50% lower GPU cost than alternatives), MAI-Voice-1 (60s of expressive audio in <1s on a single GPU), and MAI-Image-2 (#3 on Arena.ai for image families) all in Foundry public preview. April was also a developer-experience-focused release: SDK exposure of session lifecycle, skills/toolboxes, typed evaluation inputs, and safer streaming. _Why it matters:_ Microsoft is filling the speech/voice/image gaps that OpenAI's GPT-5 stack handles in ChatGPT and pricing them at half the GPU cost — a deliberate competitive wedge for Azure-first enterprise builders who want first-party MAI alternatives to OpenAI/Anthropic via Foundry. [What's new in Microsoft Foundry — April 2026](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/foundry/whats-new-in-microsoft-foundry-apr-2026/) · [What's new in Foundry Labs — April 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azure-ai-foundry-blog/whats-new-in-foundry-labs---april-2026/4509714) · [Microsoft AI — state-of-the-art speech recognition with MAI-Transcribe-1](https://microsoft.ai/news/state-of-the-art-speech-recognition-with-mai-transcribe-1/)

**Cloudflare Agents Week day-3: Sandboxes GA, Durable Object Facets, Dynamic Workers** — Cloudflare on 15 April moved Sandboxes to GA — persistent, isolated Linux environments with shell, filesystem, background processes, live preview URLs and credential injection — nine months after the June 2025 beta. The day also shipped Durable Object Facets (a Dynamic Worker can instantiate per-tenant DOs with isolated SQLite) and the broader Dynamic Workers spec, which under-pins the per-tenant runtime for agent platforms. _Why it matters:_ Sandboxes GA is the agent-execution primitive Cloudflare needs to compete with E2B and Daytona — a per-agent ephemeral computer with the network/identity stack already integrated. Durable Object Facets is the multi-tenancy primitive that lets agent-platform builders scale to "millions of unique workflows at near-zero idle cost." [Agents Week 2026 in review](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agents-week-in-review/) · [Agents Week 2026 updates](https://www.cloudflare.com/agents-week/updates/)

**OpenAI's enterprise CRO memo: AI adoption "at a tipping point" — multiproduct push, agent platform, full-stack deployment** — TechCrunch and Information surfaced an internal memo from OpenAI's chief revenue officer detailing the strategic pivot toward becoming a unified enterprise AI platform rather than a product collection. Business customers now ~40% of revenue, up from 20%, projected to reach 50% by year-end. Strategy: multiproduct adoption + agent platform + full-stack deployment to raise switching costs and deepen customer integration. _Why it matters:_ This is the explicit competitive setup against Anthropic's enterprise lead — the framing maps directly to the OpenAI Deployment Company that lands 11 May (Tomoro acquisition, $4B raise). The "switching costs" language tells customers the OpenAI value prop is becoming the suite, not the model. [Background on enterprise pivot via April 2026 roundup](https://www.ptechpartners.com/2026/05/07/april-2026-ai-news-roundup-success-v-expense-popularity-and-code-overload/) · [VentureBeat — Anthropic finally beat OpenAI in business AI adoption](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-finally-beat-openai-in-business-ai-adoption-but-3-big-threats-could-erase-its-lead)

**Salesforce launches Headless 360 at TDX 2026 — entire platform exposed as APIs / MCP tools / CLI** — Salesforce announced Headless 360 on April 15 at TrailblazerDX 2026 — the biggest platform change in 25 years. Everything on the Salesforce platform is now an API, MCP tool, or CLI command. Ships 60+ new MCP tools and 30+ preconfigured coding skills usable from Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf, or any MCP-compatible runtime; DevOps Center MCP brings the same access into CI/CD; Agentforce Vibes 2.0, Session Tracing, and Agentforce Experience Layer are GA. _Why it matters:_ The clearest "open MCP everywhere" platform pivot to date — Salesforce explicitly positions Headless 360 as the alternative to SAP's API-policy lockout (banning external agents in v4/2026). Sets the platform-strategy reference point for ServiceNow / Workday over the rest of Q2. [Salesforce — Introducing Headless 360, no browser required](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-headless-360-announcement/) · [The Register — Headless 360 agentic platform](https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/15/salesforce_headless_360/) · [VentureBeat — turn entire platform into infra for AI agents](https://venturebeat.com/ai/salesforce-launches-headless-360-to-turn-its-entire-platform-into-infrastructure-for-ai-agents) · [CIO — agent-first enterprise workflows](https://www.cio.com/article/4159536/salesforce-launches-headless-360-to-support-agent-first-enterprise-workflows.html)
