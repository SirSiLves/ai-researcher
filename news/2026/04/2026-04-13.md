## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview rattles cybersecurity — thousands of zero-days, public release withheld** — UK regulators and the AI Security Institute disclosed evaluations of Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview (announced 7 April under "Project Glasswing"), and Fortune ran a long-form interview on 13 April with Dario Amodei and security veterans on what Mythos means for defence. In pre-release testing, Mythos autonomously identified and exploited thousands of previously unknown vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser, including CVE-2026-4747, a 17-year-old NFS RCE in FreeBSD. Anthropic withheld general access, instead routing the model to a small consortium (AWS, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorgan Chase, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks). _Why it matters:_ This is the first concrete "we built it but we're not shipping it" call from a frontier lab over offensive-cyber risk, and the AISI report on the same day frames Mythos as a step-change over previous frontier capability. The Glasswing consortium is the de-facto template for future capability-gated rollouts. [AISI evaluation of Mythos Preview](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/our-evaluation-of-claude-mythos-previews-cyber-capabilities) · [Fortune — cybersecurity veteran on Mythos](https://fortune.com/2026/04/13/cybersecurity-anthropic-claude-mythos-dario-amodei-tech-ceo/) · [The Hacker News — thousands of zero-days](https://thehackernews.com/2026/04/anthropics-claude-mythos-finds.html) · [Anthropic — Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/glasswing)

**Cloudflare opens Agents Week 2026 with a 20-launch sprint built around an "Agentic Cloud"** — Cloudflare kicked off Agents Week 2026 on 13 April, the first day of a week-long rollout that ultimately shipped 20+ products and updates by 20 April. Day-1 highlights include the AI Platform reframed as a unified inference layer for agents (14+ providers behind a single Workers AI binding) and a teaser for Sandboxes GA, Cloudflare Mesh, and the Project Think Agents SDK arriving later in the week. _Why it matters:_ Cloudflare is positioning the edge as the agent control plane — the AI Platform reframing puts a multi-provider inference broker (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Meta, Cohere etc.) inside the same Workers runtime that already hosts the agent code, removing a routing layer enterprises had been hand-rolling. [Cloudflare AI Platform — inference layer for agents](https://blog.cloudflare.com/ai-platform/) · [Welcome to Agents Week 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/welcome-to-agents-week/) · [Agents Week 2026 hub](https://www.cloudflare.com/agents-week/)

**MIT Technology Review's "State of AI in charts" lands as Stanford AI Index 2026 companion piece** — MIT Technology Review on 13 April published a chart-driven survey of the AI landscape using Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index. Headline: Anthropic leads the frontier rankings as of March, trailed closely by xAI, Google, and OpenAI; DeepSeek and Alibaba models lag only modestly. The piece also surfaces AI's record-breaking adoption curve (faster than PC or internet adoption) and the widening capex gap. _Why it matters:_ This is the most-cited single primer on April's competitive landscape — it crystallises the "razor-thin frontier" narrative that backstops the GPT-5.5/Mythos/Gemini 3.1/DeepSeek V4 race two weeks later. [MIT Technology Review — state-of-AI charts](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/04/13/1135675/want-to-understand-the-current-state-of-ai-check-out-these-charts/) · [MIT Tech Review — why opinion on AI is so divided](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/04/13/1135720/why-opinion-on-ai-is-so-divided/) · [Stanford HAI — AI Index 2026 takeaways](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report)

**Oracle stock rallies 13% leading software bounce-back as AI risk narrative reverses** — Oracle shares popped nearly 13% on 13 April, leading a wider software rally and reversing the AI-disintermediation panic that had hit ServiceNow, Salesforce and Workday the prior week. The driver: stronger OCI demand commentary and a re-rating of "AI infrastructure" names vs "AI vulnerable" SaaS. _Why it matters:_ The bounce sets up the divergence that will define late-April earnings — Oracle/OCI seen as a picks-and-shovels AI infra winner, while application SaaS gets repriced on whether vendors can defend their workflow real estate against agents. [CNBC — Oracle leads software rally](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/13/oracle-orcl-stock-rally-software-ai-risk.html)
