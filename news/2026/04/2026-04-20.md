## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic + Amazon expand to up to 5GW of AWS compute; $5B fresh equity + up to $20B more; $100B Anthropic AWS-spend pledge** — Anthropic and Amazon announced on 20 April a major expansion of their strategic collaboration. Key terms: Anthropic commits **more than $100B over 10 years** to AWS technologies — Trainium2, Trainium3, Trainium4 and future iterations, plus tens of millions of Graviton CPU cores. Amazon invests **$5B fresh equity** at a $350B pre-money valuation, with **up to $20B more** tied to commercial milestones, layered on top of $8B in prior staged investments. Up to **5 gigawatts** of capacity for training and deploying Claude, with nearly 1GW of Trainium2 + Trainium3 capacity online by end-2026. _Why it matters:_ This is the deepest single-vendor multi-cloud commitment any frontier lab has made — the $100B AWS spend pledge dwarfs the Google compute deal that comes four days later. The 5GW capacity unlock is the supply-side answer to the "infinite demand for Claude" framing Anthropic has been signalling for two months. It also fundamentally changes the AWS-side AI economic story heading into Q1 results. [Anthropic — Amazon collaboration up to 5GW](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute) · [About Amazon — Amazon invests additional $5B in Anthropic](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-invests-additional-5-billion-anthropic-ai) · [CNBC — Amazon to invest up to another $25B in Anthropic](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/20/amazon-invest-up-to-25-billion-in-anthropic-part-of-ai-infrastructure.html) · [TechCrunch — Anthropic takes $5B from Amazon, pledges $100B in cloud](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/20/anthropic-takes-5b-from-amazon-and-pledges-100b-in-cloud-spending-in-return/)

**Marvell co-developing two AI chips with Google — MPU + new inference TPU — stock pops 7%** — Reports surfaced on 20 April that Google and Marvell Technology are co-developing two new AI chips for Google's stack: a **memory processing unit (MPU)** that handles part of the computation rather than just storing data (complementary to Google's TPUs), and a new **inference-optimised TPU**. Marvell shares closed up ~6% at $147.84. Production target: ~2M MPUs in pilot manufacturing within a year. Broadcom stock dropped on the news as the deal makes Marvell Google's third chip design partner alongside Broadcom (training TPUs) and MediaTek (Zebrafish inference TPU). _Why it matters:_ This is the cleanest public signal that the inference side of the AI stack is becoming as architecturally specialised as training — and that Google is deliberately distributing chip design among multiple partners to avoid Broadcom dependency. The MPU concept is novel enough to reshape the memory-bandwidth conversation that has dogged AI inference economics. [CNBC — Marvell stock surges on Google partnership](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/20/marvell-stock-google-custom-ai-chips.html) · [BigGo Finance — Google diversifies beyond Broadcom](https://finance.biggo.com/news/Euk0rJ0BQ45Y7dX6K4Fh) · [Investing.com — Marvell rally on AI chip partnership](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/marvell-shares-rally-on-report-of-new-ai-chip-partnership-with-google-4622519) · [Tech-Insider — Marvell Stock & Google AI chip deal](https://www.techi.com/marvell-stock-mrvl-google-ai-chip-rally/)

**Anthropic's Foreign Policy op-ed: "Claude Mythos Preview changes cyber calculus"** — Foreign Policy on 20 April ran a long-form on the Mythos Preview disclosure, framed by Anthropic security leadership: "Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview changes the cyber calculus." The piece argues that capability-gated rollout via Project Glasswing is the only responsible path when a frontier model can find thousands of zero-days; that defenders' time-to-patch is now the bottleneck; and that nation-state actors are presumed to be building similar capabilities without consortium constraints. _Why it matters:_ Foreign Policy is a flagship venue for the "AI cybersecurity governance" narrative. The op-ed both legitimises Anthropic's withhold-and-coordinate choice and signals to policymakers that the technical capability is real, not theoretical — feeding into both the EU AI Act trilogue and the GP-AI Code of Practice motion. [Foreign Policy — Mythos changes cyber calculus](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/04/20/claude-mythos-preview-anthropic-project-glasswing-cybersecurity-ai-hacking-danger/) · [The Hacker News — Mythos finds thousands of zero-days](https://thehackernews.com/2026/04/anthropics-claude-mythos-finds.html)

**SiliconANGLE preview: Google Cloud Next 2026 — "the real story isn't AI, it's the control plane"** — On 20 April SiliconANGLE published the Cloud Next 2026 preview: Google's pitch will recast the entire cloud stack around the agent control plane, not the model. The preview names the new Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (formerly Vertex AI), eighth-generation TPUs split into training (8t/Sunfish/Broadcom) and inference (8i/Zebrafish/MediaTek), and Workspace agent integrations as the headline themes. _Why it matters:_ SiliconANGLE's framing — "the real story isn't AI, it's the control plane" — captures how Google positions itself differently than OpenAI/Anthropic. Where the labs sell models, Google sells the substrate (TPUs, agent runtime, identity, observability) and lets customers mix in any model via Model Garden. The preview lands two days before the Pichai keynote. [SiliconANGLE — Google Cloud Next 2026 preview](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/20/google-cloud-next-2026-preview-real-story-isnt-ai-control-plane/)
