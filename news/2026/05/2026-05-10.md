## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic ships ten finance agents and Claude add-ins for Microsoft 365** — On May 5, Anthropic released ten ready-to-run agent templates for the most time-consuming work in financial services (pitchbooks, KYC screening, month-end close), and announced Claude add-ins for Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Outlook (coming soon). _Why it matters:_ This is Anthropic's most concrete step yet from "Claude in chat" to "Claude embedded in the Office workflow that knowledge workers already live in." [https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents]

**OpenAI launches three realtime audio models — GPT-Realtime-2, Translate, Whisper** — On May 7, OpenAI introduced a new generation of realtime API audio models that can reason (GPT-5-class), translate live (70 input / 13 output languages), and stream-transcribe. Pricing: $32/$64 per 1M audio in/out tokens for Realtime-2; per-minute pricing for Translate and Whisper. _Why it matters:_ Voice agents move from call-and-response toward "listen, reason, translate, transcribe, act" in one stream — a substantial unlock for telephony, contact center, and field-ops agents. [https://openai.com/index/advancing-voice-intelligence-with-new-models-in-the-api/] (also https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-realtime/)

**Anthropic and OpenAI both launch enterprise-AI joint ventures with PE giants on the same day** — On May 4, Anthropic announced a $1.5B JV with Goldman Sachs, Blackstone, and Hellman & Friedman to deploy Claude inside PE-owned mid-market firms. The same day, OpenAI finalized a separate $10B-valuation JV ("The Development Company") with TPG, Brookfield, Advent, and Bain raising $4B. _Why it matters:_ Two of the three frontier labs are now structurally tied to private equity portfolios — a new go-to-market wedge that bypasses the traditional cloud-provider channel and could reshape enterprise AI procurement in 2026–27. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-ai-services-company] (TC: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/anthropic-and-openai-are-both-launching-joint-ventures-for-enterprise-ai-services/, CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/04/anthropic-goldman-blackstone-ai-venture.html)

**Google makes Gemini API File Search multimodal — managed RAG with page-level citations** — On May 5, Google added image+text retrieval (powered by Gemini Embedding 2), custom metadata filters, and per-page citations to the Gemini API File Search tool. Storage and query-time embeddings are free; pay only for indexing embeddings + standard tokens. _Why it matters:_ Google is making "verifiable RAG" a managed-service primitive — it abstracts away vector DB ops and pushes citations into the retrieval contract. Direct competitive pressure on Pinecone/Weaviate/Chroma and on bespoke RAG stacks. [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/expanded-gemini-api-file-search-multimodal-rag/]

**EU Council and Parliament agree to streamline AI Act — transparency rules tighten, GPAI enforcement countdown begins** — On May 7, EU co-legislators agreed to (a) shorten the grace period for AI-generated-content transparency from 6 to 3 months (now applying from Dec 2, 2026), (b) clarify AI Office competence over GPAI providers with national-authority carve-outs, (c) postpone national regulatory sandbox deadlines to Aug 2, 2027, and (d) explicitly ban non-consensual sexually explicit / CSAM "nudification" apps. Commission GPAI enforcement powers come into force Aug 2, 2026 (1-year adjustment period). _Why it matters:_ The AI Act's final operational shape for GPAI is settling — providers should be planning watermarking infra and GPAI documentation now, not in August. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/]

## Other notable items

- **[AWS MCP Server hits GA](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/05/aws-mcp-server/)** — AWS, May 6. AI coding agents get a single MCP-native tool to call any AWS API (with file uploads + long-running ops) plus sandboxed Python execution against AWS, gated by IAM, CloudWatch, and CloudTrail. No additional charge.
- **[Moonshot AI raises ~$2B at ~$20B valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/chinas-moonshot-ai-raises-2b-at-20b-valuation-as-demand-for-open-source-ai-skyrockets/)** — TechCrunch, May 7. Meituan-led round; Kimi maker is now the top-funded open-source LLM lab in China, with ARR >$200M.
- **[Subquadratic launches SubQ — 12M-token context, ~1000x attention efficiency claim](https://thenewstack.io/subquadratic-12-million-context-window/)** — The New Stack, May 5. Miami startup out of stealth with $29M seed and a sub-quadratic attention architecture; 92.1% needle-in-a-haystack at 12M tokens, 83 on MRCR v2. Independent validation pending.
- **[Apple iOS 27 to let users pick third-party AI models for Apple Intelligence](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/apple-plans-to-make-ios-27-a-choose-your-own-adventure-of-ai-models/)** — TechCrunch, May 5. Internal codename "Extensions"; Gemini and Claude reportedly already in testing. WWDC reveal June 8.

Codebase vulnerability scanner powered by Claude Opus 4.7; Snyk announced an embed partnership May 7. Note: still public beta, not GA.
- **[Cloudflare details Infire — Rust-based multi-GPU LLM inference engine](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/cloudflare-llm-infrastructure/)** — InfoQ, May. Pipeline + tensor + expert parallelism; <20s cold-start for Kimi K2.5; up to ~20% higher tokens/sec. Paired with Unweight (15–22% lossless weight compression).
- **[Mistral Medium 3.5 — 128B dense multimodal, 256K context, modified-MIT open weights](https://mistral.ai/news/vibe-remote-agents-mistral-medium-3-5)** — Mistral, Apr 29 (continuing reverberation through the week). Merges instruction/reasoning/coding into one model; 77.6% on SWE-Bench Verified; ships same-day with Vibe agents and Le Chat. API at $1.50/$7.50 per 1M.

New profile guides critical-infrastructure operators through AI risk management; companion to draft Cybersecurity Framework Profile for AI (NIST IR 8596).
- **[A2A protocol crosses 150 organizations, lands native in Bedrock AgentCore, Azure AI Foundry, Google Cloud](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/a2a-protocol-surpasses-150-organizations-lands-in-major-cloud-platforms-and-sees-enterprise-production-use-in-first-year)** — Linux Foundation, recent. One year after donation to LF, A2A is in production at Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow, Workday, IBM, et al — paired with MCP as the agent-to-agent layer above tool-use.
