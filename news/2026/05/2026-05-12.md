**SAP Sapphire — SAP unveils the Autonomous Enterprise, Claude becomes a core model in Joule, n8n acquired-by-investment** — At Sapphire 2026, SAP introduced the SAP Business AI Platform, the SAP Autonomous Suite (50+ Joule Assistants orchestrating 200+ specialized agents across finance, supply chain, procurement, HCM and CX), and Joule Studio as the agent-building front door. [SAP unveils the Autonomous Enterprise](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/) · [SAP + Anthropic: Claude on SAP Business AI Platform](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/) · [n8n + SAP partnership](https://blog.n8n.io/n8n-sap/) · [n8n valuation doubles to $5.2 B](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/n8n-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-as-sap-makes-strategic-investment-and-plans-to-embed-the-ai-platform-into-joule-studio-302767222.html)

The vehicle embeds Forward Deployed Engineers into enterprises; OpenAI is acquiring AI consultancy Tomoro to seed it with ~150 FDEs and deployment specialists. _Why it matters:_ OpenAI is buying a global SI footprint rather than partnering — same playbook as Palantir, but pointed at frontier-LLM rollouts. Expect Accenture / Deloitte to respond. **EU AI Act Omnibus — Council and Parliament agree to delay high-risk deadlines, ban nudification apps** — On 7 May, EU legislators reached provisional agreement on the AI Omnibus amending the AI Act. High-risk system deadlines (biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, law enforcement, border management) slip from 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Dec 2027; embedded-in-products AI systems get 2 Aug 2028. New ban on "nudification apps" applies to providers and deployers; transparency-for-AI-generated-content grace period cut from 6 to 3 months (deadline 2 Dec 2026). Formal Parliament/Council adoption pending. _Why it matters:_ The hardest EU compliance cliff just slid 16 months, but the political price was a tighter transparency regime and explicit product bans — net effect is more time to build, less ambiguity about what is forbidden. [Council press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) · [IAPP analysis of the Omnibus](https://iapp.org/news/a/ai-act-omnibus-what-just-happened-and-what-comes-next)

**AWS MCP Server is generally available** — AWS announced GA on 6 May 2026. The managed server gives coding agents IAM-guarded, CloudWatch/CloudTrail-audited access to AWS services; a single tool now calls any AWS API and sandboxed Python execution lets agents run scripts. No additional charge — you pay only for the AWS resources the agents consume. _Why it matters:_ With AWS, GitHub, Salesforce (Tableau), ServiceNow and SAP all shipping production MCP servers this week, MCP is now the de facto enterprise tool-use standard — not a "Claude protocol." [AWS MCP Server GA announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/05/aws-mcp-server/)

**Five Eyes joint guidance: "Careful Adoption of Agentic AI Services"** — CISA, NSA, Australia's ASD ACSC, the Canadian Cyber Centre, NZ's NCSC and the UK NCSC published the first coordinated multi-government security guidance for agentic AI on 1 May. Five risk categories (privilege, design/config flaws, behavioral, structural cascade, accountability), explicit warning that agents are already in critical infrastructure with too much access. The message: fold agentic AI into existing zero-trust / least-privilege / defense-in-depth frameworks rather than inventing a parallel discipline. _Why it matters:_ First time governments have collectively named the agent attack surface; expect this to be the citation for procurement / SOC 2 / ISO 42001 agentic add-on questionnaires going forward. [CISA announcement](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/cisa-us-and-international-partners-release-guide-secure-adoption-agentic-ai) · [Full guidance PDF](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Apr/30/2003922823/-1/-1/0/CAREFUL%20ADOPTION%20OF%20AGENTIC%20AI%20SERVICES_FINAL.PDF)
