## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI GPT-5.5 Instant & Product Updates** — OpenAI released GPT-5.5 Instant with 52.5% fewer hallucinations on high-stakes prompts, enhanced memory for personalization, ChatGPT for Spreadsheets (Excel/Sheets), and Advanced Account Security with phishing-resistant sign-in. The company raised $122B to accelerate the next phase of AI and signed a computing deal with the U.S. Department of Energy. _Why it matters:_ ChatGPT's default model now delivers significantly safer, more grounded responses across medicine, law, and finance; spreadsheet integration reduces friction in enterprise workflows. [https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-instant/](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-instant/)

**Anthropic SpaceX Compute Partnership & Claude Opus 4.7** — Anthropic signed a major deal with SpaceX to access 300+ megawatts of compute from Colossus I in Memphis, doubling Pro/Max/Enterprise rate limits and removing peak-hour restrictions. Claude Opus 4.7 launched with improved vision and software engineering capabilities. Financial services agents (10 templates for KYC screening, pitchbooks, month-end close) are now available in Cowork. _Why it matters:_ Eliminates compute bottleneck for Anthropic; OpenAPI-style scaling targets financial services enterprises. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents](https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents)

**Google Gemini Workspace Intelligence & Embedding 2** — Google rolled out Workspace Intelligence for Gemini, integrating real-time context from Gmail, Chat, Calendar, and Drive with admin data-source controls. Gemini Embedding 2 now generally available via API and Vertex AI for stable multimodal (text, image, video, audio) search. Google AI Pro/Ultra got increased usage limits and launched an AI Agents Vibe Coding Course (June 2026). _Why it matters:_ Enterprise integration reaches native Google Workspace; multimodal embeddings move from research to production. [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/next-generation-gemini-deep-research/](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/next-generation-gemini-deep-research/)

**Meta LlamaCon & Llama API Launch** — Meta held its inaugural LlamaCon developer event and launched Llama API, a limited free preview platform with one-click API key creation and model playgrounds for Llama 4 Scout and Maverick. Partnerships with Cerebras and Groq offer faster inference. Llama Protection Tools and a $1.5M second-year Impact Grants program announced. _Why it matters:_ Unified API moves Llama from open weights to managed platform; experimental inference partnerships signal performance roadmap. [https://ai.meta.com/blog/llamacon-llama-news/](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llamacon-llama-news/)

**Mistral Voxtral TTS & Medium 3.5** — Mistral released Voxtral TTS (open weights on Hugging Face + API at $0.016/1k chars) with 20 preset multilingual voices. Mistral Medium 3.5 (128B) launched as default in Vibe and Le Chat with remote agents support, async cloud-based coding, and agentic Work mode. _Why it matters:_ TTS closes the voice I/O gap for agentic workflows; 128B dense model competitive with frontier labs at lower cost. [https://mistral.ai/news/devstral-2-vibe-cli](https://mistral.ai/news/devstral-2-vibe-cli)

**Google DeepMind expands AlphaEvolve into production — TPU optimization, Spanner LSM-tree compaction, 5-sector commercial deploy** — Google DeepMind published a one-year update on AlphaEvolve on May 7, moving the Gemini-powered coding agent from research into industrial production. Now optimizes Google's critical infrastructure (TPU design, cache replacement policies, LSM-tree compaction in Spanner) and is commercially deployed via Google Cloud in five sectors: finance (doubling transformer perf), semiconductors (computational lithography), logistics (TSP), media, healthcare. Open mathematical results with Terence Tao referenced as the science-side highlight. _Why it matters:_ Self-improving coding agents are now producing verified, deployed wins inside frontier labs — concrete data point on the "AI does AI R&D" trajectory. Lines up with Anthropic Institute's "AI-driven R&D" research pillar published the same day. [DeepMind — AlphaEvolve impact](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/) · [JLS blog — May 7 IA news roundup](https://jls42.org/en/news/ia-actualites-07-may-2026)

**The Anthropic Institute publishes four-pillar research agenda** — Anthropic published its institutional research agenda on May 7 for The Anthropic Institute (TAI), the internal organization launched March 2026 to study real-world AI impacts. Four pillars: (1) Economic diffusion (how AI adoption reshapes industries/labor); (2) Threats and resilience (early-warning systems against AI-driven threats); (3) AI systems in the wild (behavioural change AI induces); (4) AI-driven R&D (governance challenges of recursive AI development). Companion: expansion of Anthropic's Public Policy organization. _Why it matters:_ Continues the trend (OpenAI, DeepMind) of frontier labs spinning up dedicated policy/research arms with public agendas. The "fire drill" framing for AI-driven R&D explicitly addresses the intelligence-explosion question Jack Clark went public on the same day. [Anthropic — TAI research agenda](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-institute-agenda) · [Anthropic — Introducing The Anthropic Institute](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-anthropic-institute) · [Axios — Behind the Curtain: Intelligence explosion](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/07/anthropic-jack-clark-ai-intelligence-explosion)

**EU Council and Parliament reach AI Act simplification deal — sandbox deadline → 2027, GenAI transparency tightens to Dec 2** — The Cypriot Council Presidency confirmed political agreement on May 7 to simplify and streamline AI Act rules. Provisional deal pushes the AI regulatory sandbox deadline from 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Aug 2027 and shortens the GenAI transparency grace period from 6 to 3 months (new deadline 2 Dec 2026). The Aug 2 high-risk (Annex III) deadline remains legally binding pending the broader Digital Omnibus. Trilogue continues May 13. _Why it matters:_ Deadline whiplash for compliance teams — Annex III still ticks, sandbox slips a year, GP-AI transparency tightens. Concrete planning impact for any EU AI deployment. [Council EU — Council and Parliament agree to simplify and streamline rules](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) · [European Commission daily news 7/5/2026](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/mex_26_1030)
