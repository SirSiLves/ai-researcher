## TL;DR
- **Frontier capital + capacity went vertical:** OpenAI closed $122B at $852B (Mar 31), Anthropic stacked $45B+ committed equity and ~8.5GW of multi-cloud compute (Amazon $5B+$20B + $100B AWS-spend / Google $10B+$30B + 5GW / Broadcom-TPU 3.5GW) in one week, then fielded reported $50B preemptive offers at ≥$900B by month-end. April closes at $37B of AI funding — 66% of all global VC for the month.
- **Mythos defined the policy frame:** Anthropic disclosed Claude Mythos Preview + Project Glasswing on Apr 7 (11-partner consortium, $100M credits, capability-gated release). Within 96 hours Treasury/Fed convened five Wall Street CEOs; BoE/FCA/HMT/NCSC opened a joint workstream; Mozilla shipped 271 Mythos-found CVE fixes in Firefox 150 by month-end.
- **The frontier-model release cadence compressed:** Gemma 4 (Apr 2), GLM-5.1 (Apr 7), Muse Spark (Apr 8), Opus 4.7 + GPT-Rosalind + Grok 4.3 Beta (Apr 16–17), GPT-5.5 + DeepSeek V4 Preview (Apr 23–24), Mistral Medium 3.5 (Apr 29), Granite 4.1 + Grok 4.3 GA (Apr 30) — roughly a frontier-class release every three days.
- **The Azure-exclusivity era ended:** Microsoft–OpenAI restructure (Apr 27) drops exclusive IP licence, caps revenue-share through 2030; GPT-5.5 lands on AWS Bedrock the next day; Microsoft Agent 365 + M365 E7 reach GA on May 1 with Claude Opus 4.7 as the default Copilot model in Excel/PowerPoint.
- **MCP became infrastructure:** Linux Foundation AAIF takes over MCP governance at the Apr 2–3 NYC Dev Summit, Block donates Goose, Salesforce Headless 360 exposes 60+ MCP tools, Cloudflare Agents Week ships an end-to-end agent OS, SAP API Policy v4/2026 §2.2.2 stakes the lone walled-garden counter-position.
- **Application SaaS got re-rated for agent-disintermediation risk:** Claude Design dents Figma (~7% drop) on Apr 17; ServiceNow plunges ~18% on Apr 23 dragging IBM/Salesforce/Workday/Oracle; Snap discloses 1,000 layoffs + 65% AI-generated code; Meta plans ~8,000 cuts framed by Zuckerberg as the "AI bill."
- **Defense AI bifurcates:** Pentagon awards IL-6/7 contracts to eight vendors (OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, AWS, NVIDIA, SpaceX, Oracle, Reflection AI) on May 1 — Anthropic excluded after refusing to relax usage policy on autonomous weapons and mass surveillance.

## Defining themes

**The Mythos arc and the new disclosure template** — What began Mar 26 as a leaked Anthropic blog draft became the policy story of the month: Apr 7 disclosure of a capability-gated frontier model (Glasswing consortium of AWS/Apple/Broadcom/Cisco/CrowdStrike/Google/JPMC/LF/MS/NVIDIA/PANW), Apr 10 Treasury/Fed CEO convening, Apr 11 Bank of England placement on CMORG, Apr 16 Bloomberg reporting on UK-bank rollout, Apr 25 European financial regulators framing AI cyber as financial-stability risk, Apr 30 Mozilla's 271-CVE testimonial. By month-end the gated-consortium pattern was the new template — GPT-Rosalind partner-gating (Apr 16), GPT-5.5-Cyber Trusted Access (Apr 29), Claude Security beta + five-vendor integration (Apr 30) all follow the same shape. _Why it matters:_ capability disclosure is now a coordinated procurement+regulatory event, not a product launch. ## What changed vs. last month

Comparing to `monthly/2026/2026-03.md` (written same session), the durable shifts heading from March into April:

- **Capability-gated frontier releases became a standing template** rather than a one-off — Mythos/Glasswing → GPT-Rosalind Trusted Access → GPT-5.5-Cyber TAC → Claude Security beta all use the same partner-consortium shape. In March only the leaked Mythos draft hinted at this pattern.
- **Multi-cloud / multi-silicon became the default frontier-lab posture** — Anthropic on AWS + Google + Broadcom (mid-April), OpenAI on Foundry + AWS + Oracle + Google (post-MSFT restructure), DeepSeek on Huawei Ascend; single-cloud exclusivity ends as a viable structure, undoing the Azure-OpenAI arrangement that defined 2023–early 2026.
- **Application-SaaS agent-disintermediation became a market-pricing reality** — Figma -7% on Claude Design (Apr 17), ServiceNow -18% on Q1 (Apr 23), GitHub pausing Copilot individual signups, per-request pricing breaking under agentic workloads, SemiAnalysis's "true blended Opus 4.7 cost ≈ $0.99/Mtok" landing the load-bearing number. March had Atlassian's 1,600 layoffs as a single data point; April has a cascade.
- **Open-weights center of gravity moved East** — March's Lambert "what comes next" piece framed it as a forecast; April's Muse Spark closed-weights pivot (Apr 8) + GLM-5.1 SWE-Bench Pro lead + 41% Chinese HF download share + DeepSeek V4 on Huawei silicon turned it into the operating reality.
- **Defense-AI procurement institutionalised** — March had the U.S. policy split and EU Parliament's 569-45 Digital Omnibus mandate but no enforcement action; May 1's Pentagon eight-vendor IL-6/7 awards with Anthropic deliberately excluded is the first hard outcome of the values-vs-federal-compute fight that the March framework only set up.

What faded from the late-March vibe: the "harness leak" narrative (Claude Code source-map, Apr 1) was the dominant Anthropic story for ~72 hours and then disappeared under Mythos by Apr 7; the "Llama frontier" narrative ends Apr 8 with Muse Spark.
