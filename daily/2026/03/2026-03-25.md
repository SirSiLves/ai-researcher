## Major news & releases

### Agent protocols: MCP at scale

**MCP crosses 97M monthly SDK installs — Anthropic publishes the milestone figure.** Anthropic disclosed that the official MCP SDKs (`@modelcontextprotocol/sdk` on npm and `mcp` on PyPI) crossed 97M monthly installs in March 2026 — the headline metric that anchors MCP's transition from experimental standard to foundational agentic infrastructure. The MCP team's 9 March roadmap shift to priority-area planning (rather than release-milestone planning) framed the moment. _Why it matters:_ The install-count signal underwrites every enterprise MCP-server pitch landing in April and May (AWS MCP Server GA on 6 May, Slack MCP server on 31 March, Jama Connect on 4 May, ServiceNow Action Fabric on 2 April). MCP is now decisively the dominant agent-tool protocol — Google's A2A and OpenAI's Responses-API tool layer compete inside its gravity well. [MCP blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-mcp-roadmap/) · [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mcp-97-million-downloads-model-context-protocol-mainstream) · [MCP roadmap](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/development/roadmap)

**Anthropic ships Claude Code Channels — Telegram and Discord adapters.** Anthropic introduced Claude Code Channels, letting users interact with the Claude Code agent through messaging platforms Telegram and Discord. The launch slots the coding agent into the same ambient-surface pattern as the Slack/Cursor agent integrations. _Why it matters:_ Continues the shift from "agent runs in an IDE" to "agent reachable across all team surfaces" — and competes head-on with GitHub Copilot's chat-platform reach via Microsoft Teams. The Channels framing is the lightweight precursor to the Claude Managed Agents push later in spring. [TLDR Dev](https://tldr.tech/dev/2026-03-27)

### Enterprise platforms: Accenture × Anthropic at RSA

**Accenture launches Cyber.AI at RSA Conference, powered by Anthropic's Claude.** Accenture unveiled Cyber.AI, a new agentic-AI cybersecurity platform combining its proprietary security-agent library with Claude as the reasoning engine, plus Anthropic Agent Shield enterprise-governance controls. Already deployed inside Accenture: secures 1,600 apps and 500K+ APIs; scan turnaround dropped from 3-5 days to under 1 hour; coverage expanded from ~10% to 80%+. _Why it matters:_ The first material consultancy-grade Claude-fronted SOC platform — sets the template for Accenture's broader Anthropic JV announced later in spring and validates the lab+services GTM that Anthropic, OpenAI, and Palantir are all converging on. [Accenture Newsroom](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-and-anthropic-team-to-help-organizations-secure-scale-ai-driven-cybersecurity-operations) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-accenture-partnership)

### Governance & vendor cadence

**McKinsey publishes 2026 AI Trust Maturity Survey: average score 2.3/4, agentic-governance gap widening.** McKinsey's State of AI Trust 2026 report (drawing on ~500 enterprise responses Dec 2025–Jan 2026) lands on 25 March. Average Responsible-AI maturity climbs to 2.3 (from 2.0 in 2025) but only ~one-third of orgs hit level-3 in strategy, governance, or agentic-AI controls. TMT and financial services lead; Asia–Pacific tops regional maturity; orgs with named RAI ownership average 2.6. _Why it matters:_ The headline data point every CISO/Chief AI Officer will quote in board decks through Q2 — concretely names the agentic-AI governance gap that EU AI Act Annex III (Aug 2026) and California SB-53 obligations are about to land into. [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/tech-forward/state-of-ai-trust-in-2026-shifting-to-the-agentic-era)

**OpenAI design playbook for GPT-5.4-driven UI/frontend generation.** OpenAI published a structured playbook on using GPT-5.4 to generate more effective UX and frontend designs — structured prompts, defined design systems, evaluation criteria. _Why it matters:_ Cursor 3's "Design Mode" (launching 2 April) and Lovable's design-agent surface both lean on the same playbook framing; OpenAI is shaping the practitioner conversation ahead of the wave of design-agent launches in April. [Crescendo digest](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**OpenAI's "Spud" model finishes pretraining.** Background reporting that OpenAI's next model, codenamed "Spud," finished pretraining on 25 March. The work feeds into the GPT-5.5 release that lands on 23 April. OpenAI also indefinitely shelved its "adult mode" ChatGPT project alongside the Sora retirement decision. _Why it matters:_ The pretraining-complete signal is the empirical anchor for the GPT-5.5 release timeline — OpenAI's enterprise-platform answer to Anthropic's Mythos. Combined with the Sora retirement and "adult mode" shelving, paints a focused portfolio: enterprise-and-coding, not consumer-multimodal expansion. [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/ai-news-digests/-around-the-horn-digest-everything-that-happened-in-ai-this-weekend-march-28-29-2026/)

**Anthropic adjusts Claude Code session limits during peak hours; "What We Shipped" monthly livestream announced.** Anthropic confirmed adjustments to Claude Code session limits during peak hours and announced a new monthly "What We Shipped" livestream starting 7 April. _Why it matters:_ Throttling at peak hours is a tacit acknowledgement that Claude Code demand is outrunning capacity even with the SpaceX compute deal in the works. ## Research highlights

- **From Static Templates to Dynamic Runtime Graphs: A Survey of Workflow Optimization for LLM Agents** — Ling Yue et al. Synthesizes the workflow-optimization literature into "static template vs. dynamic runtime graph" — conceptual scaffold for the prompt-pipeline-tuning vs. learned-routing debate. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22386 — agent workflows / cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22386)
- **SpecEyes: Accelerating Agentic Multimodal LLMs via Speculative Perception and Planning** — Haoyu Huang et al. Extends speculative decoding to the perception/plan loop in multimodal agents — concrete inference-stack mechanism for VLM-agent latency. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.23483 — multimodal agents / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23483)
- **UniGRPO: Unified Policy Optimization for Reasoning-Driven Visual Generation** — Jie Liu et al. Unified GRPO-style policy optimization across reasoning and generation in visual models — ports RL-from-verifier from text to multimodal generation. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.23500 — RL / multimodal. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23500)
- **Rethinking Token-Level Policy Optimization for Multimodal Chain-of-Thought** — Yunheng Li et al. Token-level credit assignment for multimodal CoT — addresses the gap where coarse trajectory rewards collapse useful intermediate signal. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22847 — multimodal reasoning / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22847)
- **Sparse but Critical: A Token-Level Analysis of Distributional Shifts in RLVR Fine-Tuning of LLMs** — Haoming Meng et al. Empirical study: a small minority of tokens shifts heavily and carries the RLVR gain. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22446 — RL post-training / cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22446)
- **AgentSLR: Automating Systematic Literature Reviews in Epidemiology with Agentic AI** — Shreyansh Padarha et al. Domain-grounded agentic system for systematic-review automation with epidemiology-specific guardrails. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22327 — research agents / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22327)
- **Abstraction as a Memory-Efficient Inductive Bias for Continual Learning** — Elnaz Rahmati et al. Abstraction as the prior that lets continual learners avoid storing raw episodes — relevant to long-horizon agent memory. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17198 — continual learning / cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17198)
- **ThinkJEPA: Empowering Latent World Models with Large Vision-Language Reasoning Model** — Haichao Zhang et al. Couples JEPA-style latent world modeling with a VL reasoning model — instantiation of LeCun's "predict in latent, reason in language." _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22281 — world models / cs.CV. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22281)
