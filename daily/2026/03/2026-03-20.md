## Major news & releases

### Governance — the federal-vs-state fight opens

**White House unveils National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence.** Federal priorities around frontier-model export controls, agentic-AI safety attestations, federal-state pre-emption stance for high-risk deployments. _Why it matters:_ Sets up the U.S. regulatory contrast for spring — EU AI Act trilogue ramps the same month — and gives U.S. labs an explicit policy environment. Pre-emption framing is the key fault line between the framework and California SB-53 / NY RAISE Act. [White House release](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/03/president-donald-j-trump-unveils-national-ai-legislative-framework/) · [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/white-house-releases-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial) · [Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/insights/memo/2026/March/White-House-Releases-National-Policy-Framework-AI) · [CSET Georgetown](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/unpacking-the-white-house-national-policy-framework-for-ai/) · [Crowell & Moring — pre-empting state laws](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/white-house-national-ai-policy-framework-calls-for-preempting-state-laws-protecting-children)

**Congressional Democrats introduce GUARDRAILS Act in counter.** Same day: GUARDRAILS Act introduced to prohibit Trump's Dec 2025 executive order from taking effect, explicitly preserving state authority "to adopt laws to protect the American public in the face of rapidly evolving AI technologies." _Why it matters:_ Sets up the year's most consequential U.S. policy fight — federal-pre-emption-with-light-touch (White House) vs state-level enforcement (California SB-53, NY RAISE, GUARDRAILS). Outcome shapes which AI obligations OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral and enterprise vendors must comply with through 2027. [Ropes & Gray analysis](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/03/the-white-house-legislative-recommendations-national-policy-framework-for-artificial-intelligence-an)

### Enterprise platforms — China makes its move

**Alibaba launches Wukong enterprise multi-agent platform.** Manages multiple agents for document editing, approvals, research; targets Chinese-domestic enterprise users with deep Office 365/WPS integrations; bundled with Alibaba Cloud's Qwen models. _Why it matters:_ Clearest Chinese-domestic answer to ServiceNow AI Control Tower and Salesforce Agentforce; accompanies a wave of Chinese AI agent platforms positioning ahead of DeepSeek V4 (preview lands 24 April). [Crescendo](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Meta-backed Manus launches desktop agent app.** AI agent operates directly on users' local devices — files, applications, workflows — moving from cloud-only to local-host execution. _Why it matters:_ Meaningful local-agent debut joining the desktop-agent category (Claude desktop, Operator-style products, Magic). Local-execution agents are the next axis of competition after MCP-based remote agents. [Crescendo](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

### NVIDIA agent stack consolidates into spec form

**Agent Toolkit + OpenShell + AI-Q in detailed launch coverage.** Friday's roundups crystallized the NVIDIA stack into the form enterprise architects will spec against: Agent Toolkit (open platform), OpenShell (kernel-level secure runtime with a "privacy router" monitoring all outgoing agent communications), Nemotron models, AI-Q blueprints. _Why it matters:_ The "privacy router" reframes the kill-switch debate for autonomous agents — agent-level packet inspection moves from research note to shipping product. Compliance teams now have a concrete answer to "how do you stop an agent from exfiltrating?" ## Research highlights

- **Nemotron-Cascade 2: Post-Training LLMs with Cascade RL and Multi-Domain On-Policy Distillation** — Yang, Liu, Chen et al. (NVIDIA). Post-training recipe combining cascade RL with multi-domain on-policy distillation — "RL-first then distill" pipeline replacing the older SFT-then-RLHF default. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.19220 — post-training. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19220)
- **Memento-Skills: Let Agents Design Agents** — Zhou, Guo, Liu et al. Meta-agent framework where agents construct skill modules that downstream agents reuse. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.18743 — meta-agents. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18743)
- **F2LLM-v2: Inclusive, Performant, and Efficient Embeddings for a Multilingual World** — Zhang, Liao, Yu, Di. Updated open embedding model for multilingual coverage at production efficiency. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.19223 — embeddings / RAG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19223)
- **AndroTMem: From Interaction Trajectories to Anchored Memory in Long-Horizon GUI Agents** — Shi, Li, Zhang et al. Anchored memory representations from GUI agent trajectories; direct response to context-loss across sessions in mobile/computer-use agents. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.18429 — GUI agents / memory. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18429)
- **ProRL Agent: Rollout-as-a-Service for RL Training of Multi-Turn LLM Agents** — Zhang, Liu, Zhang et al. Disaggregates rollout collection from policy updates for multi-turn agent RL; addresses the throughput bottleneck for agent-RL at scale. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.18815 — agent training infra. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18815)
- **VTC-Bench: Evaluating Agentic Multimodal Models via Compositional Visual Tool Chaining** — Zhu, Dong, Wang, Shi. Benchmark for multimodal agents that chain visual tools compositionally — the "tool-use eval for VLM agents" gap. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15030 — multimodal agents. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15030)
- **LVOmniBench: Pioneering Long Audio-Video Understanding Evaluation for Omnimodal LLMs** — Tao, Zheng, Xu, Du. First serious long-form joint AV evaluation for omnimodal LLMs. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.19217 — omnimodal eval. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19217)
- **Tinted Frames: Question Framing Blinds Vision-Language Models** — Fan, Luo, Kutscher, Sigal. VLM answers dominated by linguistic framing; visual evidence overridden. Sharp adversarial-eval signal for "are VLMs actually looking?" debate. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.19203 — VLM eval. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19203)
- **Cognitive Mismatch in Multimodal Large Language Models for Discrete Symbol Understanding** — Li, Kuang, Xing, Liu. Diagnoses systematic mismatch in how MLLMs encode discrete symbols across modalities. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.18472 — multimodal failure modes. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18472)
