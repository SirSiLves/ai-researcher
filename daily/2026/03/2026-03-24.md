## Major news & releases

### Frontier portfolio: Sora exits

**OpenAI announces Sora API discontinuation — 30-day notice; full API shut-off 24 September.** OpenAI announced via X on 24 March that Sora — both mobile app and API — is being discontinued in stages. Sora 1 already pulled in the U.S. on 13 March; the Sora web/app experience closes 26 April; the Sora API discontinues 24 September 2026. OpenAI cited unsustainable economics: roughly $1M/day in compute against an installed base that fell from a peak of ~1M actives to fewer than 500K. Compute is being redirected toward coding tools and enterprise products with more predictable revenue. _Why it matters:_ The first explicit consumer-AI product retirement from OpenAI on cost grounds — strong directional signal that text-to-video at consumer prices is not yet a paying business at frontier compute economics, while enterprise/coding is. Reinforces the GTC-week framing that inference economics now drive portfolio decisions. [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation) · [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/openai-sora-shutdown-disney-deal-ai-video-2026/) · [GenMediaLab](https://www.genmedialab.com/news/openai-shuts-down-sora-app/) · [Futurum Group](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/openai-sora-discontinuation-what-the-end-of-a-platform-means-for-enterprise-ai-strategy/)

### Enterprise platforms: NemoCLAW and OCI Enterprise AI

**NVIDIA NemoCLAW deep coverage — kernel-level sandbox plus "privacy router" for outgoing comms.** Detailed write-ups of NemoCLAW (the managed enterprise-grade security service integrating Nemotron with the OpenShell runtime) landed mid-week. The service ships a kernel-level sandbox for agents and a "privacy router" that monitors all outgoing agent communications — the most concrete answer in market to data-exfiltration concerns for autonomous-agent deployments. _Why it matters:_ Sets the new compliance baseline. Microsoft Defender for AI, Palo Alto Prisma AIRS, and CrowdStrike's agentic-AI guardrail products will all be benchmarked against NemoCLAW's primitives through the rest of 2026. [DevFlokers](https://www.devflokers.com/blog/ai-news-march-24-2026-releases-breakthroughs)

**Oracle announces upcoming GA of OCI Enterprise AI.** Oracle's AI & Data Science blog announced the upcoming general availability of OCI Enterprise AI, an end-to-end developer offering bundling build/manage/deploy capabilities for production AI workloads regardless of data structure. Positions OCI as the "experimentation-to-production" path for Oracle's database installed base. _Why it matters:_ Extends the GTC-week enterprise build-out — Oracle joining Snowflake (SnowWork, 18 Mar), ServiceNow (AI Gateway, 19 Mar), and Cisco (Secure AI Factory, 16 Mar) in the wave of enterprise AI control planes shipping. Each frames itself as "the place enterprises go from pilot to production." [Oracle](https://blogs.oracle.com/ai-and-datascience/announcing-oci-enterprise-ai-ga)

## Research highlights

- **OpenResearcher: A Fully Open Pipeline for Long-Horizon Deep Research Trajectory Synthesis** — Zhuofeng Li et al. Open pipeline for generating the long-horizon research trajectories used to train deep-research agents; concrete answer to the data bottleneck inside industrial labs. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20278 — agent training data / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20278)
- **LongCat-Flash-Prover: Native Formal Reasoning via Agentic Tool-Integrated Reinforcement Learning** — Jianing Wang et al. Agentic tool-integrated RL recipe for native formal-proof generation — concrete instantiation of "tools-as-verifier" with the proof assistant grading trajectories. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21065 — formal reasoning. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21065)
- **On the Direction of RLVR Updates for LLM Reasoning: Identification and Exploitation** — Kexin Huang et al. Decomposes RLVR gradient updates into useful and harmful directions; concrete diagnostic for why some RLVR runs collapse the base policy. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22117 — RL post-training / cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22117)
- **mSFT: Addressing Dataset Mixtures Overfitting Heterogeneously in Multi-task SFT** — Woosung Koh et al. Characterizes the under-discussed failure mode where multi-task SFT overfits some datasets while underfitting others; proposes a heterogeneous mixing scheme. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21606 — SFT / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21606)
- **PivotRL: High-Accuracy Agentic Post-Training at Low Compute Cost** — Junkeun Yi et al. Compute-efficient agentic post-training recipe — entry in the "agent RL without industrial budget" thread, claims accuracy preservation under aggressive cost cuts. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21383 — agent training. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21383)
- **REVERE: Reflective Evolving Research Engineer for Scientific Workflows** — Balaji Dinesh Gangireddi et al. Research-engineer agent that reflects and evolves its own workflow over scientific tasks — useful counterpart to one-shot research-agent benchmarks. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20667 — research agents / cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20667)
- **BubbleRAG: Evidence-Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Black-Box Knowledge Graphs** — Duyi Pan et al. RAG over knowledge graphs whose schema is unknown — realistic enterprise setting where the KG is a queryable black box. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20309 — RAG / cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20309)
- **Omni-WorldBench: Comprehensive Interaction-Centric Evaluation for World Models** — Meiqi Wu et al. World-model benchmark evaluating interaction rather than rollout fidelity — fills the gap between rollout-FID metrics and what agents need from a simulator. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22212 — world models / benchmarks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22212)
