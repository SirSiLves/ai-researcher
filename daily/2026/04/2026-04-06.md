### Policy & framing — OpenAI takes the mic

**OpenAI releases "Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age" — 13-page blueprint with seven big proposals.** OpenAI published "Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age: Ideas to Keep People First" on 6 April. The seven proposals: treat AI access as a fundamental right; tax reform for the AI economy; a four-day work week; support for displaced workers; faster electrical-grid build-out; a public wealth fund; rapid-response social-safety-net programmes; workers given a voice in AI deployment. ## Research highlights

- **GrandCode: Achieving Grandmaster Level in Competitive Programming via Agentic RL** — anonymous. Multi-agent RL system with specialized orchestrated modules ("agentic GRPO") that reports grandmaster-level Codeforces performance. Combines offline post-training with online test-time RL — agentic-RL-for-coding pushes past SWE-bench into competitive-programming territory. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02721 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02721)
- **InCoder-32B-Thinking: Industrial Code World Model** — anonymous. Pretrains/post-trains on error-driven chain-of-thought traces and domain execution traces (Verilog sim, GPU profiling) rather than just successful code — addresses the absence of expert reasoning traces in industrial software. Concrete recipe for the "domain-aware coding model" direction. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.03144 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03144)
- **Self-Distilled RLVR (RLSD)** — anonymous. Combines RL with verifiable rewards and on-policy self-distillation to stabilize training and lift the convergence ceiling. Decomposes the standard RLVR update into magnitude and direction components, with self-distillation supplying the missing fine-grained policy direction. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.03128 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03128)
- **Agentic-MME: What Agentic Capability Really Brings to Multimodal Intelligence?** — anonymous. Process-verified MME-style benchmark that scores tool invocation, stepwise checkpoints, sandboxed code execution, and an "overthinking" metric — not just final answer correctness. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.03016 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03016)
- **AgentHazard: Evaluating Harmful Behavior in Computer-Use Agents** — anonymous. Benchmark targeting computer-use agents' unique threat surface: sequences of individually-benign actions that collectively cause harm; tracks state maintenance across tool/file/exec interactions. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02947 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02947)
- **AgentSocialBench: Evaluating Privacy Risks in Human-Centered Agentic Social Networks** — anonymous. Probes how multi-agent coordination and inter-agent information mediation create persistent leakage even with explicit privacy protections — the "abstraction paradox." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01487 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01487)
- **Test-Time Scaling Makes Overtraining Compute-Optimal** — anonymous. Joint pass@k modeling shifts optimal pretraining decisions into the overtraining regime once inference cost is part of the optimization. Direct correction to Chinchilla-era recipes for any deployment where you'll run inference-time sampling at scale. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01411 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01411)
- **Swift-SVD: Theoretical Optimality Meets Practical Efficiency in Low-Rank LLM Compression** — anonymous. Closed-form, activation-aware SVD compression with dynamic rank allocation driven by layer-wise effective rank. Faster + tighter low-rank baseline for the edge-deployment thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01609 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01609)
