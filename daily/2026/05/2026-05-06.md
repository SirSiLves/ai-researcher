## Research & papers

**Coordination as an Architectural Layer for LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems** — Maksym Nechepurenko, Pavel Shuvalov. Argues that the 41–87% production failure rate in multi-agent LLM systems is a coordination problem, not a model problem; treats coordination as a configurable architecture layer separate from agent logic. _Category:_ cs.MA. [arxiv 2605.03310](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03310)

**RAG over Thinking Traces Can Improve Reasoning Tasks** — Negar Arabzadeh, Wenjie Ma, Sewon Min, Matei Zaharia. Retrieving solution traces (not documents) lifts AIME by +56% on Gemini-2.5-Flash and GPQA-Diamond by +7.6% on GPT-5; a strong counter-argument to the "RAG can't help reasoning" consensus. _Category:_ cs.IR. [arxiv 2605.03344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03344)

**Beyond Similarity Search: A Unified Data Layer for Production RAG Systems** — Budigi & Sirigiri. PostgreSQL+pgvector unified layer eliminates the staleness, tenant-leakage, and query-composition pathologies of split RAG stacks; 92% latency reduction on filtered queries. _Category:_ cs.IR. [arxiv 2605.03275](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03275)

**SURE-RAG: Sufficiency and Uncertainty-Aware Evidence Verification for Selective RAG** — Qiu, Han, Huang. Multi-hop sufficiency as a set-level property, not per-passage; 0.9075 Macro-F1 on HotpotQA-RAG with 37% fewer unsafe answers. _Category:_ cs.IR. [arxiv 2605.03534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03534)

**MemFlow: Intent-Driven Memory Orchestration for Small Language Model Agents** — Chen, Li, Wang. Training-free intent-routing + deterministic evidence preparation; 2× accuracy on a frozen Qwen3-1.7B. Useful counterweight to the "you need a frontier model for agentic memory" assumption. _Category:_ cs.MA. [arxiv 2605.03312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03312)

**Rethinking Reasoning-Intensive Retrieval: Evaluating and Advancing Retrievers in Agentic Search Systems** — Zhao et al. Introduces BRIGHT-Pro (multi-aspect gold evidence) and an aspect-decomposed synthetic corpus to expose retrieval behavior hidden by standard metrics. _Category:_ cs.IR. [arxiv 2605.04018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04018)

**When Safety Geometry Collapses: Fine-Tuning Vulnerabilities in Agentic Guard Models** — Demonstrates fine-tuning-based jailbreaks against safety guard models used in agentic AI. _Category:_ cs.LG. [arxiv 2605.02914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02914)

**Position: Safety and Fairness in Agentic AI Depend on Interaction Topology, Not Model Scale** — Argues system topology dominates model alignment for agent safety/fairness — a useful frame next to today's coordination-architecture work. _Category:_ cs.AI. [arxiv 2605.01147](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01147)

**QKVShare: Quantized KV-Cache Handoff for Multi-Agent On-Device LLMs** — Honavar & GVSL. Token-level mixed-precision quantized KV handoff between on-device agents; 8K-context TTFT 1029.7ms → 397.1ms versus re-prefill. _Category:_ cs.AI. [arxiv 2605.03884](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03884)

**Sparse Memory Finetuning as a Low-Forgetting Alternative to LoRA** — Memory-efficient finetuning that reduces catastrophic forgetting; relevant for ongoing post-training arguments. _Category:_ cs.CL. [arxiv 2605.03229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03229)
