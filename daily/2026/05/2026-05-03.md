## Major news & releases

### Sakana KAME — tandem speech-to-speech

**Sakana AI unveils KAME — tandem speech-to-speech architecture injecting LLM knowledge in real time.** Tokyo-based Sakana AI released KAME ("turtle" in Japanese) on 3 May, a tandem speech architecture that pairs a fast speech-to-speech (S2S) frontend with an asynchronous backend LLM via an "oracle" stream. Partial transcripts flow to the LLM as the user speaks; the LLM emits tentative "oracle" guesses that refine in real time as more speech is processed — flipping the paradigm from "think then speak" to "speak while thinking." Sakana trained KAME using Simulated Oracle Augmentation: a simulator LLM generates synthetic responses at various completeness levels, used to train the front-end to lean on partial backend signals. Result: MT-Bench score climbs from 2.05 (vanilla S2S) to 6.43 while maintaining near-zero latency. Backend-agnostic — swap GPT-4.1, Claude Opus 4.1, or Gemini 2.5 Flash without retraining the front-end. ICASSP 2026 paper; open weights on Hugging Face. _Why it matters:_ The cleanest architectural innovation in voice AI since OpenAI's GPT-Realtime separation of audio reasoning/translation/transcription. The async oracle pattern is portable — likely to show up inside Sierra, Vapi and enterprise voice stacks within weeks. It also reframes the "speech-to-speech vs cascade pipelines" debate as a false dichotomy. ## Research highlights

_Sunday — arXiv does not announce new papers on Sundays. Picks below are (a) Sunday-stamped submissions and (b) Friday-cohort governance/safety items not used in the 05-01 or 05-02 dailies._

- **A Language for Describing Agentic LLM Contexts (ACDL)** — Introduces ACDL, a formal-but-readable language for specifying the structure and dynamics of LLM input contexts as agents accumulate role-message sequences, dynamic content, time-indexed references, and conditional/iterative structure. Tooling at acdlang.org. Concrete primitive for "context engineering as discipline" — gives agent platforms a way to communicate and diff prompt architectures. CAIS '26. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01920 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01920)
- **RefusalGuard: Geometry-Preserving Fine-Tuning for Safety in LLMs** — Shows standard fine-tuning induces systematic drift in safety-relevant representations and distorts their geometric structure; proposes a representation-level fine-tuning recipe that preserves the refusal subspace while still allowing task adaptation. Direct counter to the "fine-tuning quietly breaks alignment" failure mode. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01913 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01913)
- **A Hybrid Retrieval and Reranking Framework for Evidence-Grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Citation-aware RAG pipeline for biomedical/healthcare QA built on Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases — hybrid retrieval plus reranking with explicit evidence grounding so each generated claim ties back to a source span. Concrete vertical instantiation of evidence-grounded-RAG. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01664 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01664)
- **Effect-Transparent Governance for AI Workflow Architectures: Semantic Preservation, Expressive Minimality, and Decidability Boundaries** — Formalizes governance as a semantics-preserving operator that mediates memory access, external calls, and oracle queries inside AI workflow architectures, and proves effect-level governance can be enforced without reducing computational expressivity. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01030 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01030)
- **Governing What the EU AI Act Excludes: Accountability for Autonomous AI Agents in Smart City Critical Infrastructure** — AgentGov-SC, three-layer governance architecture with 25 measures traceable to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and NIST AI RMF — targeted at autonomous-agent deployments in smart-city critical infrastructure that fall outside the AI Act's scope. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01091 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01091)
- **FinSafetyBench: Evaluating LLM Safety in Real-World Financial Scenarios** — Bilingual (English-Chinese) red-teaming benchmark for LLM refusal under financial-compliance-violating requests. Vertical-grounded safety eval beyond generic harm taxonomies. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.00706 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00706)
- **Authorization Propagation in Multi-Agent AI Systems: Identity Governance as Infrastructure** — Treats authorization as a first-class infrastructure concern across A2A and MCP handoffs — direct extension of the AI-identity radar thread; pairs with the topology-driven safety position paper. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.05440 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05440)
- **When Safety Geometry Collapses: Fine-Tuning Vulnerabilities in Agentic Guard Models** — Guard-model fine-tuning on benign data destroys latent safety geometry. Empirical complement to RefusalGuard's mitigation. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.02914 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02914)
