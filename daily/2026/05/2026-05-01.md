## Major news & releases

### Microsoft Agent 365 + E7 GA — the control plane

**Microsoft Agent 365 + Microsoft 365 E7 "Frontier Suite" reach general availability.** Microsoft on 1 May made Agent 365 and the Microsoft 365 E7 Frontier Suite generally available. E7 ($99/user/month) bundles M365 E5, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Agent 365 in a single SKU; Agent 365 standalone ($15/user/month) delivers the control plane to observe, govern and secure agents the same way IT manages people today. The 1 May GA adds Agent 365 registry sync with AWS Bedrock and Google Cloud in public preview — letting IT auto-discover, inventory and govern agents across hyperscalers — plus runtime alerting and Intune/Defender controls in June preview. Same day, Microsoft decommissioned the Anthropic independent-processor (IP) setting, completing the in-Azure data-flow path for Anthropic models in Copilot. _Why it matters:_ The single most consequential enterprise-AI control-plane launch of the quarter. Agent 365 in GA — with cross-cloud registry sync and observability — is Microsoft's direct answer to ServiceNow's AI Control Tower and SAP's API-fencing posture, and the price ($15/user) makes it a default purchase rather than a deliberate one. ### Pentagon AI deals + Anthropic exclusion

**Pentagon signs Impact-Level-6/7 AI deals with 8 vendors — and freezes out Anthropic.** On 1 May the Department of Defense announced classified AI deployment contracts with eight technology companies: OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, AWS, NVIDIA, SpaceX, Oracle, and Reflection AI. The deals cover Impact Level 6 and Impact Level 7 environments — the highest classified data tiers in DoD. Anthropic was deliberately excluded after refusing to remove its usage-policy guardrails for fully autonomous weapons and mass domestic surveillance; Secretary Hegseth formalized a "supply chain risk" designation against Anthropic in March, and although a federal judge in California blocked the designation, the Pentagon proceeded with awards to the other eight. Pentagon officials publicly said the department will "never again" rely on a single AI provider. _Why it matters:_ The largest single defense-AI procurement event of the cycle and the cleanest test of whether a frontier lab can hold a usage policy against federal pressure. Anthropic's exclusion — combined with the >$900B funding round reporting and the Goldman/Blackstone/H&F joint venture — defines a two-track frontier-lab market: defense-aligned vs commercially-positioned. ### Meta acquires ARI

**Meta acquires Assured Robot Intelligence to seed humanoid foundation-model stack.** Meta closed its acquisition of ARI — a humanoid-AI startup co-founded a year ago by former Fauna co-founder Lerrel Pinto and former NVIDIA researcher Xiaolong Wang. Wang, Pinto, Xuxin Cheng and the rest of the ARI team join Meta Superintelligence Labs to train physical agents that learn from human-experience data. Meta's framing: be the Android of humanoid robots — develop sensors, software and AI models, license them to manufacturers Meta does not own. _Why it matters:_ Meta's clearest "physical AGI" bet to date and the cleanest signal that Meta Superintelligence Labs is the focal point for capex post-Q1 earnings. The Android-of-humanoids framing is the same play Google made for mobile in 2008 — pricing it as a moat against Tesla Optimus and Figure AI rather than against Apple. ### Salesforce Headless 360 + 60+ MCP tools live

**Salesforce Headless 360 lights up: 60+ MCP tools wired to Claude Code, Cursor and any MCP runtime.** Salesforce confirmed Headless 360 — its agentic API architecture published in late April — is live across Agentforce, with more than 60 new MCP tools accessible directly from Claude Code, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible runtime. Headless 360 is the deliberate opposite of SAP's API Policy v4/2026 (which blocks external AI agents): every Salesforce data object, workflow and action is reachable via API, MCP tool or CLI without requiring a Salesforce-owned AI interface as the entry point. _Why it matters:_ The most important agentic-platform divergence of the enterprise era. Salesforce + ServiceNow are betting that customers want to compose their own multi-vendor agent stacks; SAP is betting they want the OEM's. Headless 360's MCP-tool count (60+) is the largest enterprise-software MCP exposure to date. ## Research highlights

- **Position: Safety and Fairness in Agentic AI Depend on Interaction Topology, Not on Model Scale or Alignment** — Position paper arguing that in deliberative or vote-aggregating multi-agent setups, three failure modes — ordering instability, information cascades, and functional collapse — emerge from the structure of information flow rather than from individual model alignment or scale. Reframes the agentic-safety conversation toward topology-level properties. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01147 — cs.MA/cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01147)
- **"I Don't Know" — Towards Appropriate Trust with Certainty-Aware Retrieval Augmented Generation** — CERTA wires uncertainty signals from question-context-answer relevance into a RAG pipeline so the system can refuse rather than over-agree. Reduces over-confident answers and produces cautious behavior on moral or ambiguous prompts. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.00957 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00957)
- **MemRouter: Memory-as-Embedding Routing for Long-Term Conversational Agents** — Encodes each turn with recent context, projects through a frozen LLM backbone, and uses lightweight 12M-parameter classification heads to decide what to commit to long-term memory — sidestepping the prompted-LLM "should I remember this?" pattern. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.00356 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00356)
- **Effect-Transparent Governance for AI Workflow Architectures: Semantic Preservation, Expressive Minimality, and Decidability Boundaries** — Formalizes governance over AI workflow architectures as a semantics-preserving operator that mediates memory access, external calls, and oracle queries. Proves effect-level governance can be enforced without reducing computational expressivity. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01030 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01030)
- **Governing What the EU AI Act Excludes: Accountability for Autonomous AI Agents in Smart City Critical Infrastructure** — Introduces AgentGov-SC, a three-layer governance architecture with 25 measures traceable to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and the NIST AI RMF, targeted at autonomous-agent deployments in smart-city critical infrastructure that fall outside the AI Act's scope. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01091 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01091)
- **SAGA: Workflow-Atomic Scheduling for AI Agent Inference on GPU Clusters** — Schedules entire agent workflows as atomic units rather than independent per-step inference calls. 1.64× faster task completion on a 64-GPU cluster serving SWE-bench coding agents and WebArena browser tasks, with better memory efficiency. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.00528 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00528)
- **Towards Multi-Agent Autonomous Reasoning in Hydrodynamics** — Multi-agent prototype using a planner agent to construct query-specific Layer Execution Graphs (LEGs), specialist agents under strict tool allowlists, runtime logs every tool invocation for auditability. 93.6% factual precision / 100% pass on 37 queries with Claude Sonnet 4.6. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01102 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01102)
- **FinSafetyBench: Evaluating LLM Safety in Real-World Financial Scenarios** — Bilingual (English-Chinese) red-teaming benchmark of LLM refusal behavior under financial-compliance-violating requests. Vertical-grounded safety eval beyond generic harm taxonomies. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.00706 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00706)
