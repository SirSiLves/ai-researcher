## Research & papers

**Beyond Semantic Similarity: Rethinking Retrieval for Agentic Search via Direct Corpus Interaction** — _Takeaway:_ Lets agents interact with corpora directly instead of relying on one-shot embedding lookup; breaks the semantic-similarity ceiling that has plagued production RAG. _Category:_ cs.IR/cs.CL. [arxiv 2605.05242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05242)

**Cited but Not Verified: Parsing and Evaluating Source Attribution in LLM Deep Research Agents** — _Takeaway:_ First systematic eval showing citations from deep-research agents (OpenAI/Perplexity-style) often cannot be verified against underlying sources; ships a parser+verifier pipeline that flags fabricated/misattributed claims. _Category:_ cs.CL. [arxiv 2605.06635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06635)

**Superintelligent Retrieval Agent: The Next Frontier of Information Retrieval** — _Takeaway:_ Frames retrieval as an active reasoning loop — agent issues exploratory queries, inspects snippets, and adapts strategy. Practical blueprint for next-gen enterprise RAG. _Category:_ cs.IR. [arxiv 2605.06647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06647)

**STALE: Can LLM Agents Know When Their Memories Are No Longer Valid?** — _Takeaway:_ New benchmark for memory revision in long-running agents; current memory systems fail to invalidate beliefs when new evidence arrives — critical gap for production agent platforms. _Category:_ cs.CL. [arxiv 2605.06527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06527)

**EnterpriseRAG-Bench: A RAG Benchmark for Company Internal Knowledge** — _Takeaway:_ Purpose-built benchmark for RAG over messy internal corpora (tickets, wikis, code) where existing public RAG benchmarks systematically over-estimate quality. Useful for procurement/eval. _Category:_ cs.IR. [arxiv 2605.05253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05253)

**MASPO: Joint Prompt Optimization for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems** — _Takeaway:_ Optimizes role prompts across an entire multi-agent system jointly rather than per-role; reports notable gains over per-agent tuning, relevant for agent-orchestration platforms. _Category:_ cs.AI/cs.MA. [arxiv 2605.06623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06623)

**StraTA: Incentivizing Agentic Reinforcement Learning with Strategic Trajectory Abstraction** — _Takeaway:_ Tackles long-horizon credit assignment in agentic RL by abstracting trajectories into strategy-level units; meaningful gains over reactive RLHF/GRPO baselines on multi-step agent tasks. _Category:_ cs.CL/cs.LG. [arxiv 2605.06642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06642)

**When No Benchmark Exists: Validating Comparative LLM Safety Scoring Without Ground-Truth Labels** — _Takeaway:_ Statistical method for safety-comparing models when no labeled benchmark is available — directly relevant to AI governance / pre-deployment screening. _Category:_ cs.CY. [arxiv 2605.06652](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06652)
