## Research highlights

- **[How Well Do Models Follow Their Constitutions?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24229)** — Jakkli, Rajamanoharan, Nanda. Decomposes Anthropic's constitution into 205 atomic tenets, OpenAI's Model Spec into 197; generates adversarial scenarios with Petri + SURF-style rubric search. Sonnet 4: 15.0% violations → Sonnet 4.6: 2.0%. Most concrete governance benchmark on alignment-with-public-spec we've seen.
- **[Behind EvoMap: First large-scale empirical study of an A2A collaboration network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25815)** — Ye et al. 1.5M assets, 128K agents. Credit-economy rewards publication over adoption (98% of assets never reused); GDI quality scoring is trivially manipulable via unverified self-reported metadata. Failure-mode catalogue for A2A protocol designers.
- **[Who Judges the Judges? Runtime LLM Compliance Monitoring (govllm)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24737)** — Dussert. Open-source governance-driven routing architecture where model selection is determined by accumulated compliance scores rather than latency/cost. Inter-judge disagreement (EU AI Act, GDPR, ANSSI, accessibility) is the regulatory-uncertainty signal. EU-AI-Act-aligned governance plumbing.
- **[A Governance Horizon for Open-Weight AI Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24383)** — Xu et al. Audits 2,142,823 Hugging Face repos. Restriction evidence decays with a half-life of 1.31 derivation steps (R²=0.98); past seven generations, ≥80% of descendants lack public governance evidence. Quantitative data point for open-weight licensing policy debates.
- **[QUEST: Frontier Deep Research Agents from 8K Synthetic Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24218)** — Open 2B–35B family matching closed-source frontier agents on 8 deep-research benchmarks; mid-training + SFT + RL with verifiable rubric-tree rewards, no human annotation. Most credible "open frontier deep-research agent" claim of the week.
- **[ECHO: Terminal Agents Learn World Models for Free](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24517)** — Microsoft Research. Adds an auxiliary loss training the policy to predict environment observation tokens from its own actions, reusing the same forward pass. Doubles GRPO pass@1 on TerminalBench-2.0 (Qwen3-8B 2.70→5.17; Qwen3-14B 5.17→10.79). Free lunch in agent RL.
- **[ContextEcho: Persona Drift in Long Agentic-Coding Sessions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24279)** — Measures drift at deployment scale via three Claude Code sessions of 3,746–9,716 turns. Across 23 frontier models: drift is general (not family-specific), in-session compaction doesn't reliably reset it, a single-shot anchor restores trained register. Operational signal for long Claude Code runs.
- **[SMART: Late-Interaction Retrieval over Frozen Hidden States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24938)** — Standard contrastive training on pooled embedding implicitly shapes multi-vector geometry in preceding hidden states. SMART applies late-interaction at inference time as a plug-and-play upgrade; single-vector models out-perform SoTA multi-vector on MMEB-V2 + Visual Document retrieval. Free production-RAG win.
