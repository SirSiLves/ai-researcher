## Long-form picks

- **Notes on the xAI/Anthropic data center deal** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Under-reported details on the Colossus arrangement — Anthropic gets the older, environmentally troubled Colossus 1 while xAI keeps the bigger Colossus 2; gas turbines reportedly ran without Clean Air Act permits, and the deal sits awkwardly inside a politically loaded compute squeeze. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/7/xai-anthropic/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/7/xai-anthropic/)

- **Vibe coding and agentic engineering are getting closer than I'd like** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues the line between "vibe coding" and "agentic engineering" is collapsing as Claude Code / Codex-style loops normalize unsupervised edits — and lays out why that's a problem for software supply chains. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/6/vibe-coding-and-agentic-engineering/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/6/vibe-coding-and-agentic-engineering/)

- **TIL: Using LLM in the shebang line of a script** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Tiny but practical engineering pattern: shebang-line LLM invocation with tools so a shell script becomes a deterministic agent step; canonical example for the "scripts as agents" idiom. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/llm-shebang/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/llm-shebang/)

- **How open model ecosystems compound** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ AI2/Epoch numbers suggest ~80% of frontier-model compute is R&D, not the final training run; the more open the stack, the more those R&D costs get amortized across labs — a structural reason China's open ecosystem can run longer than naive cost models predict. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/how-open-model-ecosystems-compound](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/how-open-model-ecosystems-compound)

- **Import AI 456: RSI and economic growth; radical optionality for AI regulation; and a neural computer** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Pushes back on the "AI = explosive GDP" trope and proposes "radical optionality" as a regulatory frame — keep multiple governance pathways open instead of betting on one regime; pairs with Narayanan's adoption-ceiling argument. [https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-456-rsi-and-economic-growth](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-456-rsi-and-economic-growth)

- **Import AI 455: AI systems are about to start building themselves** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Tracks the speedup curve on AI-research-engineering benchmarks (Opus 4 at 2.9x → Mythos Preview at 52x in a year) and notes OpenAI's stated goal of an automated AI research intern by September 2026 — frames the RSI debate in concrete numbers. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/)

- **The Great AI Silicon Shortage** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Effective TSMC N3 utilization expected >100% in H2 2026; AI-related N3 demand (accelerators + host CPU + networking) consumes ~60% of N3 output; TSMC cannot add enough capacity for two years. The supply story behind every "compute-constrained" headline. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-great-ai-silicon-shortage](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-great-ai-silicon-shortage)

- **The Agent Development Lifecycle** — LangChain blog (May 9, 2026). _Takeaway:_ Maps frameworks vs platforms across the agent stack and where the LangSmith Fleet / LangGraph split lives; useful baseline for Interrupt 2026 conference week (May 13–14). [https://www.langchain.com/blog/the-agent-development-lifecycle](https://www.langchain.com/blog/the-agent-development-lifecycle)

- **AI Snake Oil: why even rapid AI progress won't produce explosive GDP growth** — Arvind Narayanan. _Takeaway:_ Notes-style post arguing the binding constraint on AI-driven growth is social adoption, not technology or regulation — explosive growth requires social-change rates the public won't accept. [https://substack.com/@aisnakeoil/note/c-236510990](https://substack.com/@aisnakeoil/note/c-236510990)

- **An Interview with Joanna Stern About Living With AI** — Stratechery / Ben Thompson. _Takeaway:_ Long interview on what it actually means for a mainstream tech columnist to live inside AI tools daily — pairs the Aggregation-Theory framing with first-person consumer-AI usage signals. [https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-joanna-stern-about-living-with-ai/](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-joanna-stern-about-living-with-ai/)

- **Stanford merges HAI and Data Science into a single institute** — Stanford Report (May 2026). _Takeaway:_ Big institutional signal: HAI + Stanford Data Science fold into one institute under James Landay; Fei-Fei Li becomes Special Advisor on AI to the president. Combines 400+ scholars, the Marlowe cluster, and the AI Index reporting line. [https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/05/stanford-merges-hai-data-science](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/05/stanford-merges-hai-data-science)

- **Inside Anthropic's 2026 Developer Conference** — Dan Shipper (Chain of Thought / Every). _Takeaway:_ Conference dispatch focused on the Anthropic–SpaceX Colossus capacity deal as the headline product story; useful sibling to Willison's analysis from the practitioner-strategy angle. [https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference](https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference)
