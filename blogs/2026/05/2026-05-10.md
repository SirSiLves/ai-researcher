# AI Blogs — 2026-05-10

## Long-form picks

- **Using Claude Code: The Unreasonable Effectiveness of HTML** — Simon Willison (May 8). _Takeaway:_ Argues that having Claude Code generate HTML explainers (rather than prose) is the highest-leverage pattern of the post-Code-w-Claude moment — a hands-on workflow note backed by concrete examples. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/8/unreasonable-effectiveness-of-html/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/8/unreasonable-effectiveness-of-html/)

- **A quote from Luke Curley: OpenAI's WebRTC Problem** — Simon Willison (May 9). _Takeaway:_ Surfaces a Cloudflare engineer's argument that WebRTC's lossy-by-design transport is the wrong substrate for production voice agents — relevant as every frontier lab pushes Realtime/voice APIs. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/9/luke-curley/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/9/luke-curley/)

- **The distillation panic** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects (May 4). _Takeaway:_ Tracks the new political push to criminalize "distillation attacks" on US APIs and warns that a sweeping legal definition would also criminalize the legitimate self-distillation that powers smaller open and closed models. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-distillation-panic](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-distillation-panic)

- **My bets on open models, mid-2026** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects. _Takeaway:_ Forecasts that sovereigns and local-agent stacks (OpenClaw and friends) — not Western enterprise — will drive the next leg of open-model demand; concludes the open-vs-closed gap is now a business/economics problem, not a capability one. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/my-bets-on-open-models-mid-2026](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/my-bets-on-open-models-mid-2026)

- **AI Value Capture — The Shift To Model Labs** — SemiAnalysis (Dylan Patel et al.). _Takeaway:_ Argues that with N3 utilization >100% H2'26 and DRAM fabs already at 90%+, AI labs, hyperscalers, neoclouds and memory vendors capture nearly all the marginal token-economy value — a sharp data-backed counter to the "value flows to apps" thesis. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/ai-value-capture-the-shift-to-model](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/ai-value-capture-the-shift-to-model)

- **The Great AI Silicon Shortage** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Lays out why TSMC's N3 allocation has become the binding constraint of the 2026 buildout, with AI infra explicitly prioritized over consumer electronics — a concrete supply-side framing of the capex super-cycle. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-great-ai-silicon-shortage](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-great-ai-silicon-shortage)

- **How to Work and Compound with AI** — Eugene Yan (May 3). _Takeaway:_ Practitioner principles for getting compounding leverage from AI — provide good context, encode taste as config, make verification easy, delegate bigger tasks, close the loop. Anthropic-internal lens but broadly applicable. [https://eugeneyan.com/writing/working-with-ai/](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/working-with-ai/)

- **Inside Anthropic's 2026 Developer Conference** — Every / Chain of Thought (May 7). _Takeaway:_ Argues the headline of Code w/ Claude wasn't a model release but the SpaceX/xAI Colossus capacity allocation deal, framing compute as the binding constraint that now shapes vendor strategy. [https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference](https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference)

- **The Pulse: Did capacity shortages turn Anthropic hostile to devs?** — Gergely Orosz, Pragmatic Engineer (May 7). _Takeaway:_ Concrete reporting on Claude Code access being yanked from paid accounts, a "dumber" model rumor, plus the GitHub Copilot ~3x pricing change — capacity scarcity is now visibly degrading the developer experience. [https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-did-capacity-shortages](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-did-capacity-shortages)

- **Microsoft Earnings, Apple Earnings (2026.20)** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery (May 7). _Takeaway:_ Reads Microsoft's earnings as the formal pivot to an "agentic" P&L; pairs it with Apple's chip/memory shortage to argue the AI capex super-cycle is now visible in the income statements of the largest companies in the world. [https://stratechery.com/2026/microsoft-earnings-apple-earnings/](https://stratechery.com/2026/microsoft-earnings-apple-earnings/)

- **Proceedings of the 2025 Workshop on Law-Following AI** — Cullen O'Keefe, Christoph Winter, Matthijs Maas, Lawfare (May 8). _Takeaway:_ Forty-scholar workshop output proposing "law-following AI" — agentic systems specifically designed to refuse illegal orders or illegal means — as a governance primitive for autonomous systems. Concrete research agenda, not just commentary. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/proceedings-of-the-2025-workshop-on-law-following-ai](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/proceedings-of-the-2025-workshop-on-law-following-ai)

- **Why Stanford is restructuring for AI's next era** — Stanford Report / HAI (May 4). _Takeaway:_ HAI and Stanford Data Science merge under James Landay; Fei-Fei Li becomes university-wide AI special advisor and joins John Hennessy on the council. Signal that elite academic centers are reorganizing around agentic AI rather than ML-as-data-science. [https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/05/james-landay-fei-fei-li-john-hennessy-hai-interview](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/05/james-landay-fei-fei-li-john-hennessy-hai-interview)
