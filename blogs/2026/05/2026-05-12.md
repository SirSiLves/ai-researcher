## Long-form picks

- **Your AI Use Is Breaking My Brain** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues the new "zombie internet" of casual AI-generated content is degrading reader trust faster than safety policy can keep up; pushes for clearer norms on disclosing model use in public writing. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/zombie-internet/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/zombie-internet/)

- **Thoughts on GitLab's workforce reduction** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Reads GitLab's layoffs as the first big "AI coding ate the seats" event — a structural rather than cyclical cut, and a preview of how DevTools vendors will reshape headcount around agent throughput in 2026. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/gitlab-act-2/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/gitlab-act-2/)

- **Learning on the Shop floor** — Simon Willison on Shopify's River agent. _Takeaway:_ Detailed walkthrough of Shopify's internal coding agent: how it's used by non-engineers, the eval loop, and why "shop floor" learning beats top-down rollouts. Concrete enterprise pattern. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/learning-on-the-shop-floor/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/11/learning-on-the-shop-floor/)

- **Notes on the xAI / Anthropic data center deal** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Untangles the SpaceX-financed compute swap and what an Anthropic-on-xAI-silicon arrangement implies for the frontier-lab capacity race. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/7/xai-anthropic/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/7/xai-anthropic/)

- **Vibe coding and agentic engineering are getting closer than I'd like** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Distinguishes "vibe coding" (LLM as autocomplete) from agentic engineering (LLM owns the loop) and warns the cultural mash-up is hiding real engineering rigor problems. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/6/vibe-coding-and-agentic-engineering/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/6/vibe-coding-and-agentic-engineering/)

- **The Inference Shift** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Cerebras's IPO bump is the surface signal; the real argument is that agentic inference (long tool-using loops) changes compute economics in ways disaggregated serving and CPO are built for. [https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/)

- **SpaceX and Anthropic, xAI's Two Companies** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Frames the Anthropic-xAI compute deal as Musk being pushed toward a "neutral merchant" posture; reads the politics behind why Anthropic accepted SpaceX-adjacent capacity. [https://stratechery.com/2026/spacex-and-anthropic-xais-two-companies-elon-musk-and-spacexais-future/](https://stratechery.com/2026/spacex-and-anthropic-xais-two-companies-elon-musk-and-spacexais-future/)

- **Import AI 455: Automating AI Research** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Latest issue documents AI-system task horizons doubling (6h → 12h since 2025) and Kaggle-solving agents at 64.4%; Cotra now projects >100h horizons by year-end 2026. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/)

- **AINews: Silicon Valley gets Serious about Services** — smol.ai (swyx et al). _Takeaway:_ Captures the week OpenAI launched the "Deployment Company" (150 FDEs, $4B), Daybreak security, GPT-Realtime-2, and Artificial Analysis's Coding Agent Index — services layer is the new battleground. [https://news.smol.ai/issues/26-05-11-not-much](https://news.smol.ai/issues/26-05-11-not-much)

- **Notes from inside China's AI labs** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects. _Takeaway:_ First-person field notes on lab culture, eval discipline and compute access at Chinese frontier labs after a recent visit; reframes the "fast follower" narrative as a research-velocity story. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/notes-from-inside-chinas-ai-labs](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/notes-from-inside-chinas-ai-labs)

- **AI Agents Must Follow the Law** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Substantive position piece arguing that as computer-using agents take on economically meaningful work, the law must bind the agent, not just the deploying firm — practical implications for liability and audit. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-agents-must-follow-the-law](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-agents-must-follow-the-law)
