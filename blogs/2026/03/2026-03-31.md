## Long-form picks

- **Supply Chain Attack on Axios Pulls Malicious Dependency from npm** — Socket, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Second major supply-chain hit of the month: Axios (101M weekly downloads) shipped 1.14.1 and 0.30.4 with a fresh dependency `plain-crypto-js` that stole credentials and installed a RAT. Likely a leaked long-lived npm token; both malicious releases were published without an accompanying GitHub release — a heuristic that would also have flagged LiteLLM the previous week. Strengthens the case for npm "trusted publishing". [https://socket.dev/blog/axios-npm-package-compromised](https://socket.dev/blog/axios-npm-package-compromised)

- **Apple's 50 Years of Integration** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Return-from-Spring-Break Update on Apple's 50th: Apple's edge has always been hardware/software integration; if AI redefines the integration point (LLM as substrate, agents as the new OS), the historical advantage may not transfer. Companion interview with Asymco's Horace Dediu on the next 50 years. [https://stratechery.com/2026/apples-50-years-of-integration/](https://stratechery.com/2026/apples-50-years-of-integration/)

- **An Interview with Asymco's Horace Dediu About Apple at 50** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Companion interview that pushes hard on the question of whether Apple's integration model survives an AI-substrate transition; Dediu's framing is that it's a question of whether the new computing primitive is the model or the device. [https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-asymcos-horace-dediu-about-apple-at-50/](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-asymcos-horace-dediu-about-apple-at-50/)
