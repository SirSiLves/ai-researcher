# AI Blogs — 2026-03-26

## Long-form picks

- **Quantization from the ground up** — Sam Rose (ngrok blog), via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Spectacular interactive explainer on LLM quantization — including the clearest visual walkthrough of float32 binary representation Simon's seen, an introduction to outlier "super weights" that wreck a model if naively quantized, and a llama.cpp + GPQA experiment showing 16→8-bit costs almost nothing while 16→4-bit lands around ~90% of original quality on Qwen 3.5 9B. [https://ngrok.com/blog/quantization](https://ngrok.com/blog/quantization)
- **My minute-by-minute response to the LiteLLM malware attack** — Callum McMahon (futuresearch.ai), via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Transcripts of how Callum used Claude to confirm the malicious `.pth` file inside a Docker container and decide what to do — Claude even surfaced `security@pypi.org` as the right report-to address. Concrete example of agentic incident-response. [https://futuresearch.ai/blog/litellm-attack-transcript/](https://futuresearch.ai/blog/litellm-attack-transcript/)
