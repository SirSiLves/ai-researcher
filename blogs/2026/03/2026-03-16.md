## Long-form picks

- **How coding agents work** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Chapter 2 of the Agentic Engineering Patterns guide. Explains coding agents as a "harness" wrapping an LLM, extended via invisible prompts and callable tools. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/)
- **Coding agents for data analysis** — Simon Willison (NICAR 2026 handout). _Takeaway:_ Three-hour data-journalism workshop showing how Claude Code and OpenAI Codex can explore, clean and visualize data with Python/SQLite/Datasette. Includes a vibe-coded Leaflet heat-map demo. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/coding-agents-for-data-analysis/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/coding-agents-for-data-analysis/)
- **Use subagents and custom agents in Codex** — OpenAI / via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Subagents now GA in OpenAI Codex with "explorer", "worker", "default" defaults plus TOML-defined custom agents in `~/.codex/agents/`. Confirms subagents are now a near-universal pattern across Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Mistral Vibe, OpenCode, VS Code and Cursor. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/codex-subagents/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/codex-subagents/)
- **Introducing Mistral Small 4** — Mistral / via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Apache-2 119B-parameter MoE (6B active) unifying Magistral (reasoning), Pixtral (multimodal) and Devstral (agentic coding) in one model. Supports `reasoning_effort="none"|"high"`. Mistral also dropped Leanstral, an open-weight model tuned for Lean 4. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/mistral-small-4/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/mistral-small-4/)
- **The Pentagon went to war with Anthropic** — Gideon Lewis-Kraus, The New Yorker (quoted on Simon Willison). _Takeaway:_ Anthropic alignment team frames last year's blackmail-scenario red-team as a policy comms tool: visceral demos to make misalignment risk salient to policymakers. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/blackmail/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/16/blackmail/)

- **ImportAI 449: LLMs training other LLMs; 72B distributed training run; computer vision is harder than generative text** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Monday issue covers PostTrainBench (frontier coding agents now reach ~half the human uplift when post-training smaller models) and a 72B model trained across ~20 distributed peers via Bittensor Subnet 3 + Covenant's Gauntlet software. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/03/16/importai-449-llms-training-other-llms-72b-distributed-training-run-computer-vision-is-harder-than-generative-text/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/03/16/importai-449-llms-training-other-llms-72b-distributed-training-run-computer-vision-is-harder-than-generative-text/)

- **What comes next with open models** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects. _Takeaway:_ Argues that through 2026 closed models will leap ahead in directions open models can't follow, splitting the future into "true frontier", "open frontier", and "small distributed" tiers — and that small open models will mostly serve as cheap complements to closed agents (10× faster, 100× cheaper for repetitive subtasks). [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-next-phase-of-open-models](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-next-phase-of-open-models)
