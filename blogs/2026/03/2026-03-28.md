## Long-form picks

- **An appreciation for (technical) architecture** — Matt Webb (interconnected.org), via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ "Agents grind problems into dust" — given a while loop they'll burn a trillion tokens to solve almost anything. So the bottleneck shifts to architecture: great libraries with great interfaces that make the right thing the easy thing. Webb says he now spends more time on architecture and less time looking at code lines than ever before — and reclaims the verb "vibing" for it. [https://interconnected.org/home/2026/03/28/architecture](https://interconnected.org/home/2026/03/28/architecture)

- **Inside the AI Index: 12 Takeaways from the 2026 Report** — Stanford HAI. _Takeaway:_ Headline numbers: GenAI hit 53% global adoption in 3 years (faster than PC or internet), $172B US consumer surplus annually, US still leads top-tier model count + high-impact patents while China dominates publication/citation/patent volume; the 2.7% US-vs-China frontier gap is the smallest yet recorded. [https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report)
