## Long-form picks

- **Turbo Pascal 3.02A, deconstructed** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Walks through having Claude interpret and "decompile" Borland's 39,731-byte 1985 Turbo Pascal binary into an interactive artifact. Then candidly retracts: a Hacker News commenter (`rep_lodsb`) shows the annotated disassembly is hallucinated slop — invented assembly with plausible labels for ~half the binary. A cautionary case study for AI-assisted reverse engineering. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/20/turbo-pascal/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/20/turbo-pascal/)
- **Cursor Composer 2 was built on Kimi K2.5** — Kimi.ai, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Moonshot confirms Cursor's new Composer 2 is built on top of Kimi-k2.5, accessed via Fireworks AI under a commercial partnership. A notable open-model integration story for the agentic-coding stack. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/20/cursor-on-kimi/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/20/cursor-on-kimi/)

- **Research: SQLite Tags Benchmark — Comparing 5 Tagging Strategies** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Claude-Code-driven micro-benchmark across 100k rows × 100 tags. Many-to-many tables won, FTS5 close second, full-table-scan with LIKE held up better than expected, but `json_each()` over JSON arrays was much slower than the rest. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/20/sqlite-tags-benchmark/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/20/sqlite-tags-benchmark/)

- **White House AI Framework Proposes Industry-Friendly Legislation** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Same-day analysis of the White House's March 20 national AI framework — three months after the December executive order, the framework recalibrates federal-state preemption and pushes a lighter-touch baseline favored by industry. Anchor doc for the week's governance debate. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/white-house-ai-framework-proposes-industry-friendly-legislation](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/white-house-ai-framework-proposes-industry-friendly-legislation)
