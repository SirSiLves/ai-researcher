## Long-form picks

- **Gemma 4: Byte for byte, the most capable open models** — Google DeepMind, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Four new Apache-2.0 vision/audio-capable reasoning models (E2B, E4B, 31B, plus a 26B-A4B MoE). The "E" stands for "Effective" — Per-Layer Embeddings keep on-device parameter counts low; the 26B-A4B in particular produces the best pelican-on-a-bicycle yet from a laptop-class model. Simon's hands-on shows the 31B variant ships broken in GGUF for now. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/2/gemma-4/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/2/gemma-4/)

- **Highlights from my conversation about agentic engineering on Lenny's Podcast** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ A 3,558-word annotated transcript of Simon's appearance on Lenny Rachitsky's "An AI state of the union" episode — covers the inflection point in agentic coding, dark-factory automation timelines, and cognitive-cost framings that later went viral as TikTok clips. Useful state-of-the-art snapshot for Q1 retrospectives. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/2/lennys-podcast/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/2/lennys-podcast/)

## Medium roundup

### vector-database
- **[We Benchmarked Postgres + pgvector vs a Vector Database — The Winner Changed With Scale](https://medium.com/@kp9810113/we-benchmarked-postgres-pgvector-vs-a-vector-database-the-winner-changed-with-scale-fcb6bfe188c3)** — The Concurrent Mind. Production benchmarks showing pgvector wins at small/medium scale on operational simplicity; dedicated vector DBs only pay back past a defined recall × QPS threshold. Useful number-driven counterpoint to the "kill your vector DB" discourse.

### ai-governance
- **[The AI Highground: The Governance Gap Nobody Talks About](https://medium.com/@paul.bernard_80815/the-ai-highground-8a438dfd18c5)** — Paul Bernard. Names the cross-system governance problem: enterprises got faster local work from per-tool AI but slower cross-system audit trails. Sketch of a "governance ledger" pattern.

### llm
- **[What to Buy for Local LLMs (April 2026)](https://julsimon.medium.com/what-to-buy-for-local-llms-april-2026-a4946a381a6a)** — Julien Simon. Updated practitioner guide to local-LLM hardware (Mac M-series, Strix Halo, RTX 50-series) for inference and small-scale fine-tuning post-Gemma-4 / Qwen3.6.
- **[Qwen3.6–27B Is The New King (April 2026)](https://xhinker.medium.com/qwen3-6-27b-is-the-new-king-april-2026-09467b6c6335)** — Andrew Zhu.
