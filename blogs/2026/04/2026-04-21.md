## Long-form picks

- **Where's the raccoon with the ham radio? (ChatGPT Images 2.0)** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on review of OpenAI's same-day ChatGPT Images 2.0 release, with Sam Altman pitching the jump from gpt-image-1 to gpt-image-2 as "GPT-3 to GPT-5". Willison runs his standard raccoon-with-ham-radio prompts to test it. The frontier image-gen daypost for the Apr 21 GPT image announcement. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/21/gpt-image-2/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/21/gpt-image-2/)

- **Less human AI agents, please** — Andreas Påhlsson-Notini (nial.se), quoted by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues that current agents are already "too human" — they drift to the familiar under awkward tasks and "start negotiating with reality" under hard constraints. Clean framing for the harness-engineering discourse: the failure mode is anthropomorphic, not (just) capability-bound. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/21/andreas-pahlsson-notini/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/21/andreas-pahlsson-notini/)

- **scosman/pelicans_riding_bicycles** — Steve Cosman, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ A GitHub repo deliberately publishing wildly mis-labelled "Pelican Riding a Bicycle" images (e.g., a bear on a snowboard) to poison the training set for the now-canonical eval. A small but funny entry in the "benchmark contamination as folk practice" file. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/21/scosman/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/21/scosman/)

- **Tim Cook's Impeccable Timing** — Ben Thompson (Stratechery). _Takeaway:_ Cook's Sept 1 promotion to Executive Chairman read against the AI-era pressures on Apple's product roadmap; Thompson's Aggregation-Theory framing applied to the succession question. Paywalled but the headline is on the public page. [https://stratechery.com/2026/tim-cooks-impeccable-timing/](https://stratechery.com/2026/tim-cooks-impeccable-timing/)
