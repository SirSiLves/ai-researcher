# AI Blogs — 2026-04-15

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notes on Google's new prompt-directed TTS model. The "prompting guide" is unusually elaborate — multi-paragraph audio scenes (e.g., "AUDIO PROFILE: Jaz R / The Morning Hype" in a Brixton accent) feed into short outputs. Willison vibe-coded a UI to swap accents (London → Newcastle → Devon) and posted samples. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/gemini-31-flash-tts/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/gemini-31-flash-tts/)

- **The Future of Everything is Lies, I Guess: New Jobs (quoted)** — Kyle Kingsbury / aphyr, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Coins "meat shields" for human workers who exist primarily to be held accountable for ML-system decisions — Meta moderation reviewers, lawyers sanctioned for LLM lies, DPOs, third-party subcontractors set up to take the blame. Strong piece for the governance/accountability discourse. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/kyle-kingsbury/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/kyle-kingsbury/)

- **My bets on open models, mid-2026** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Lays out four bets — sovereigns and consortium funding flowing into open weights, local/personal agents as the under-priced demand side, AI-native business backends as the open-model adoption flywheel, and open post-training (RLHF pipelines, preference data) becoming the next differentiator. Useful synthesis of the open-vs-closed dynamics. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/my-bets-on-open-models-mid-2026](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/my-bets-on-open-models-mid-2026)

- **Zig 0.16.0 release notes: "Juicy Main"** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notes the new dependency-injected `main(init: std.process.Init)` pattern in Zig 0.16.0 — useful as a concrete example of language ergonomics flowing in the opposite direction from the "everything is a config blob" agent style. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/juicy-main/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/juicy-main/)

- **datasette 1.0a27** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Two notable changes: (1) header-based CSRF replaces Django-style tokens; (2) new `RenameTableEvent` plugin signal — significant for plugins like datasette-comments that attach data to tables by name. Practical reference for anyone building event-driven data plugins. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/datasette/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/15/datasette/)
