## Long-form picks

- **Eight years of wanting, three months of building with AI** — Lalit Maganti (lalitm.com), via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Long-form retro on shipping `syntaqlite` (a high-fidelity SQLite parser/formatter/verifier) with Claude Code. The honest version of the agentic-engineering story: AI got Lalit past 8 years of procrastination on a 400+ rule grammar, but the first vibe-coded prototype had to be thrown away because AI made him defer key architectural decisions. Best long-form piece on AI's weakness in design vs implementation in months. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/5/building-with-ai/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/5/building-with-ai/)

- **ChatGPT usage in U.S. "hospital deserts"** — Chengpeng Mou (Head of Business Finance, OpenAI), quoted by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Anonymized U.S. ChatGPT data shows ~2M weekly messages on health insurance, ~600K weekly from people 30+ min from the nearest hospital, and 7-of-10 of those msgs happen outside clinic hours. Important data point for the "AI as substitute for missing public services" frame. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/5/chengpeng-mou/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/5/chengpeng-mou/)
