## Long-form picks

- **Claude Token Counter, now with model comparisons** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Quantifies Anthropic's "Opus 4.7 uses 1.0–1.35x more tokens per input" claim with hands-on numbers: 1.46x for Opus 4.7's own system prompt vs 4.6, 1.08x on a 30-page PDF; image overhead is dominated by higher resolution support rather than tokenizer changes. Same per-token pricing means a roughly 40% effective price bump for text-heavy workloads. Useful concrete read on the Opus 4.7 cost story. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/20/claude-token-counts/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/20/claude-token-counts/)

- **llm-openrouter 0.6 — and Kimi K2.6 on OpenRouter** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Same-day Kimi K2.6 release; LLM plugin now has a `refresh` command for grabbing new OpenRouter models without waiting for cache expiry. Notable as a tiny but practical signal of how fast the open-Chinese model cadence is forcing tooling iterations. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/20/llm-openrouter/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/20/llm-openrouter/)

- **Import AI 454: Automating alignment research; safety study of a Chinese model; HiFloat4** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Headlines a proof-of-concept of Automated Alignment Research where an Anthropic researcher primes a team of agents on a research direction and they out-perform a human baseline; safety study of a Chinese frontier model; new HiFloat4 numerical-format paper. The Monday issue threading the Opus 4.7-era RSI debate. [https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-454-automating-alignment](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-454-automating-alignment)

- **🔬 Training Transformers to solve 95% failure rate of Cancer Trials — Ron Alfa & Daniel Bear, Noetik** — Latent Space (Brandon Anderson & RJ Haneke). _Takeaway:_ Deep dive on Noetik's spatial-transcriptomics + multimodal-tumor-imaging foundation models, framed around the thesis that many "failed" cancer drugs actually work — they're just being tested on the wrong patient biology. Strong concrete example of "AI for science" beyond LLM-chatbots. [https://www.latent.space/p/noetik](https://www.latent.space/p/noetik)

- **Reading today's open-closed performance gap** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues the single-number "open vs closed gap" framing covers up the actual nuanced dynamics — different capability frontiers have very different gaps, and the perpetual-catch-up posture means open-model strategy needs to be capability-specific, not blanket. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/reading-todays-open-closed-performance](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/reading-todays-open-closed-performance)
