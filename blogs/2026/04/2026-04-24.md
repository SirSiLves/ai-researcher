## Long-form picks

- **DeepSeek V4 — almost on the frontier, a fraction of the price** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ DeepSeek's first V4-series drop since V3.2 in December: two preview models, DeepSeek-V4-Pro and DeepSeek-V4-Flash. Willison's read is "almost frontier, a fraction of the price" — the open-Chinese-MoE story continues to compress the capability-and-cost gap with US frontier labs. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/deepseek-v4/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/deepseek-v4/)

- **An update on recent Claude Code quality reports (postmortem)** — Anthropic, annotated by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ The "models got worse" complaints over the past two months were real — but the cause was three separate harness bugs, not the models. The standout one: a March 26 change to clear stale thinking after 1h idle accidentally fired every turn for the rest of the session, making Claude seem "forgetful and repetitive". Required reading on harness engineering and the inherent fragility of agentic systems. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/recent-claude-code-quality-reports/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/recent-claude-code-quality-reports/)

- **The people do not yearn for automation** — Nilay Patel (The Verge), annotated by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Patel's essay on why AI is unpopular with the general public even as ChatGPT usage skyrockets: people with "software brain" see the world as something to automate; everyone else doesn't yearn for it. Sharp framing for the "AI flattens human experience" critique. Willison flags it as the commentary he'll be chewing on for a long time. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/the-people-do-not-yearn-for-automation/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/the-people-do-not-yearn-for-automation/)

- **Serving the For You feed** — spacecowboy (AT Protocol blog), annotated by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Architecture deep-dive: Bluesky's 72k-user For You feed served from a single Go process + SQLite (~419GB) on a 16-core "gaming PC" in the author's living room, fronted by a $7/month OVH VPS over Tailscale. Total cost $30/month. Estimates it could handle all of Bluesky's ~1M DAUs on the cheapest algorithm. Anti-overengineering reference architecture of the week. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/serving-the-for-you-feed/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/serving-the-for-you-feed/)

- **russellromney/honker — NOTIFY/LISTEN for SQLite** — annotated by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Rust SQLite extension + language bindings implementing Postgres-style NOTIFY/LISTEN with transactional outbox semantics. Workers poll the `.db-wal` file every 1ms for near-real-time without query overhead. Clean implementation of Brandur Leach's transactionally-staged-job-drains pattern. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/honker/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/24/honker/)

- **The Coding Assistant Breakdown: More Tokens Please** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Lands a load-bearing number for the agentic-coding cost story — the true blended price for running Opus 4.7 on agentic tasks lands ~$0.99/Mtok despite the $5/$25 sticker, because workloads have ~300:1 input:output ratios and >90% cache-hit rates. Pairs cleanly with Anthropic's same-day quality postmortem. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-coding-assistant-breakdown-more](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-coding-assistant-breakdown-more)
