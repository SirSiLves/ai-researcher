## Top picks

- **A Language for Describing Agentic LLM Contexts** — Noga Peleg Pelc, Gal A. Kaminka (with Yoav Goldberg; cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.MA/cs.SE submission, CAIS '26). _Takeaway:_ Introduces ACDL (Agentic Context Description Language), a formal-but-readable language for specifying the structure and dynamics of LLM input contexts as agents accumulate role-message sequences, dynamic content, time-indexed references, and conditional/iterative structure. Tooling at acdlang.org. Concrete primitive for the "context engineering as discipline" thread — gives agent platforms a way to communicate and diff prompt architectures across implementations. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01920 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01920)

- **RefusalGuard: Geometry-Preserving Fine-Tuning for Safety in LLMs** — Sadia Asif, Mohammad Mohammadi Amiri. _Takeaway:_ Shows standard fine-tuning induces systematic drift in safety-relevant representations and distorts their geometric structure; proposes a representation-level fine-tuning recipe that preserves the refusal subspace while still allowing task adaptation. Direct counter to the "fine-tuning quietly breaks alignment" failure mode that has dominated 2026 safety research — pairs with 2604.24902's safety-drift evidence. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01913 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01913](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01913)

- **A Hybrid Retrieval and Reranking Framework for Evidence-Grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Fariba Afrin Irany, Sampson Akwafuo. _Takeaway:_ Citation-aware RAG pipeline for biomedical / healthcare QA built on Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases — hybrid retrieval plus reranking with explicit evidence grounding so each generated claim ties back to a source span. Concrete vertical instantiation of the `agentic-retrieval` and `evidence-grounded-RAG` patterns. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01664 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01664)

- **Effect-Transparent Governance for AI Workflow Architectures: Semantic Preservation, Expressive Minimality, and Decidability Boundaries** — Alan L. McCann (Fri 1 May rollover, governance-leaning). _Takeaway:_ Formalizes governance as a semantics-preserving operator that mediates memory access, external calls, and oracle queries inside AI workflow architectures, and proves effect-level governance can be enforced without reducing computational expressivity. Useful theoretical scaffold for governance-graph and policy-on-paths work; surfaced for the Sunday governance lean since 05-01 led with topology-driven safety. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01030 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01030)

- **Governing What the EU AI Act Excludes: Accountability for Autonomous AI Agents in Smart City Critical Infrastructure** — Talal Ashraf Butt, Muhammad Iqbal (Fri 1 May rollover, governance-leaning). _Takeaway:_ AgentGov-SC, a three-layer governance architecture with 25 measures traceable to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and NIST AI RMF — targeted at autonomous-agent deployments in smart-city critical infrastructure that fall outside the AI Act's scope. Concrete bridge from policy text to enforceable controls; useful pairing with the Effect-Transparent Governance paper. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01091 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01091)

- **FinSafetyBench: Evaluating LLM Safety in Real-World Financial Scenarios** — Yutao Hou, Yihan Jiang (Fri 1 May rollover, vertical-safety lean). _Takeaway:_ Bilingual (English-Chinese) red-teaming benchmark for LLM refusal under financial-compliance-violating requests. Vertical-grounded safety eval that goes past generic harm taxonomies to map concrete regulatory failure modes — relevant to the `agent-safety` and `compliance` lines, and a useful complement to the topology-driven safety position paper from 05-01. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.00706 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00706)

## Other interesting items

- **[Position: Safety and Fairness in Agentic AI Depend on Interaction Topology, Not on Model Scale or Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01147)** — Tanav Singh Bajaj, Nikhil Singh (Fri 1 May rollover). Argues structural failure modes (ordering instability, information cascades, functional collapse) dominate model-level alignment in deliberative MAS — already a 05-01 top pick, surfaced again here as the policy/safety reference for any Sunday governance reader.
- **[When Safety Geometry Collapses: Fine-Tuning Vulnerabilities in Agentic Guard Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02914)** — Earlier-week submission with strong dependency on RefusalGuard's argument: guard-model fine-tuning on benign data destroys latent safety geometry. Useful as the empirical complement to RefusalGuard's mitigation.
- **[Authorization Propagation in Multi-Agent AI Systems: Identity Governance as Infrastructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05440)** — Krti Tallam (forward-link to Tue 6 May, pre-printed in the Sun reading list). Treats authorization as a first-class infrastructure concern across A2A and MCP handoffs — direct extension of the AI-identity radar thread; surfaced here so Sunday governance readers see the connection to the topology and EU-AI-Act papers above.
