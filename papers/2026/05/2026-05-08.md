## Top picks

- **Skill1: Unified Evolution of Skill-Augmented Agents via Reinforcement Learning** — Yaorui Shi, Yuxin Chen, Zhengxi Lu, Yuchun Miao. _Takeaway:_ Unified RL recipe for evolving skill-augmented agents — concrete instantiation of the "skill library + RL" pattern that has been splintered across papers using inconsistent definitions of "skill." _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06130 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06130)

- **Beyond Semantic Similarity: Rethinking Retrieval for Agentic Search via Direct Corpus Interaction** — Zhuofeng Li, Haoxiang Zhang, Cong Wei, Pan Lu. _Takeaway:_ Argues vector-similarity retrieval is the wrong primitive for agentic search and proposes direct corpus interaction — sharp pushback on the embeddings-first orthodoxy in RAG, with concrete alternative. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.05242 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05242)

- **SkillOS: Learning Skill Curation for Self-Evolving Agents** — Siru Ouyang, Jun Yan, Yanfei Chen, Rujun Han. _Takeaway:_ Treats skill curation (what to keep, what to retire) as a learned operation rather than a fixed policy — concrete advance for the "skill library" abstraction in long-horizon agent systems. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06614 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06614)

- **StraTA: Incentivizing Agentic Reinforcement Learning with Strategic Trajectory Abstraction** — Xiangyuan Xue, Yifan Zhou, Zidong Wang, Shengji Tang. _Takeaway:_ Strategic trajectory-level abstraction as the credit-assignment substrate for agentic RL — concrete contribution to the credit-assignment thread distinct from token-level methods. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06642 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06642)

- **AI Co-Mathematician: Accelerating Mathematicians with Agentic AI** — Daniel Zheng, Ingrid von Glehn, Yori Zwols, Iuliya Beloshapka. _Takeaway:_ DeepMind agentic AI paper on collaborative-mathematician workflows — important data point on whether the AlphaProof line generalizes from competition-style problems into open-ended research math. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06651 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06651)

- **Can RL Teach Long-Horizon Reasoning to LLMs? Expressiveness Is Key** — Tianle Wang, Zhaoyang Wang, Guangchen Lan, Xinpeng Wei. _Takeaway:_ Argues the bottleneck for long-horizon reasoning is policy expressiveness, not RL signal — sharp diagnostic for the recurring claim that current RL hits a ceiling at multi-step reasoning. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06638 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06638)

- **Auto Research with Specialist Agents Develops Effective and Non-Trivial Training Recipes** — Jingjie Ning, Xiaochuan Li, Ji Zeng, Hao Kang. _Takeaway:_ Specialist-agent ensemble that autonomously develops training recipes — concrete data point on whether research-agents can produce non-trivial methodological output rather than restating known recipes. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.05724 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05724](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05724)

- **A²TGPO: Agentic Turn-Group Policy Optimization with Adaptive Turn-level Clipping** — Dingwei Chen, Zefang Zong, Zhipeng Ma, Leo Luo. _Takeaway:_ Turn-group policy optimization with adaptive clipping for agentic settings — concrete variant of GRPO targeted at the multi-turn-with-tool-calls regime where vanilla GRPO struggles. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06200 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06200)

- **MiA-Signature: Approximating Global Activation for Long-Context Understanding** — Yuqing Li, Jiangnan Li, Mo Yu, Zheng Lin. _Takeaway:_ Compact "signature" approximation of global activation patterns for long-context — concrete architecture-level mechanism distinct from sparse-attention and KV-eviction approaches. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06416 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06416)

- **UniPool: A Globally Shared Expert Pool for Mixture-of-Experts** — Minbin Huang, Han Shi, Chuanyang Zheng, Yimeng Wu. _Takeaway:_ Globally shared expert pool for MoE — direct architectural alternative to the per-layer expert silo that has dominated MoE work. Relevant to the MoE scaling debate. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06665 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06665)
