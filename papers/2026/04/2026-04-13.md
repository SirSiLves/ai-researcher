# AI Papers — 2026-04-13

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **Multi-User Large Language Model Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Most agent stacks are implicitly optimized for a single-principal interaction; this work designs for multiple users with distinct roles, preferences, and authority — a direct match for enterprise agent platforms moving beyond personal-assistant framing. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08567 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08567](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08567)

- **Process Reward Agents for Steering Knowledge-Intensive Reasoning** — anonymous (cs.CL/cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Reasoning in knowledge-intensive domains needs non-local verification (you can't grade a step without synthesizing external evidence). Builds process-reward agents that score intermediate steps via retrieval-conditioned judgments instead of math-style local checks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09482 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09482)

- **Large Language Models Generate Harmful Content Using a Distinct, Unified Mechanism** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Mechanistic-interpretability claim that jailbreaks and fine-tuning-induced emergent misalignment share a common underlying circuit — useful structural data for the safety/alignment radar thread on why current alignment is brittle. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09544 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09544)

- **p1: Better Prompt Optimization with Fewer Prompts** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Studies what makes a task amenable to prompt optimization at all: variance of reward across system-prompt candidates is the binding constraint. Practical guidance for when DSPy/MIPRO-style search actually pays off versus when it's noise-chasing. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08801 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08801)

- **Backdoor Attacks on Decentralised Post-Training** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Decentralised post-training (data + pipeline parallelism across untrusted workers) is vulnerable to coordinated poisoning by malicious participants — formalizes the threat model and demonstrates concrete backdoor channels. Relevant to the open-training and federated-fine-tune threads. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02372 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02372](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02372)

- **Robust Reasoning Benchmark** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ 14-technique perturbation pipeline showing reasoning models are heavily overfit to standard textual formatting on math benchmarks; modest formatting/notation shifts collapse performance. Sharp counter-evidence to "reasoning models generalize." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08571 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08571](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08571)

- **Cactus: Constrained Acceptance Speculative Sampling** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Standard speculative sampling enforces exact distribution match with the verifier; Cactus relaxes this with a constrained acceptance criterion that preserves quality while widening accept rates — concrete decoding-throughput win for production inference stacks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.04987 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04987)

- **EXAONE 4.5 Technical Report** — LG AI Research. _Takeaway:_ First open-weight VLM from LG, integrating a dedicated visual encoder into the EXAONE 4.0 framework for native multimodal pretraining. Adds another open-weights option to the Qwen-VL/Llama-3-Vision/InternVL frontier. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08644 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08644)

- **Initialisation Determines the Basin: Efficient Codebook Optimisation for Extreme LLM Quantization** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Diagnoses why 2-bit additive quantization collapses despite extensive finetuning: codebook initialization picks the loss basin and downstream search can't escape it. Proposes a principled init that preserves quality at extreme compression — direct relevance to edge LLM deployment. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08118 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08118)

- **Beyond the Assistant Turn: User Turn Generation as a Probe of Interaction Awareness** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Reframes evaluation around what comes after the assistant turn: asks the model to generate the user's next move. A probe of whether the model encodes interaction awareness rather than treating responses as independent atoms — relevant to multi-turn agent eval. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02315 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02315](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02315)

## Other interesting items

- **[ScheMatiQ: From Research Question to Structured Data through Interactive Schema Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09237)** — cs.CL/cs.IR. Agentic pipeline that turns a natural-language research question over a document corpus into a structured schema plus exhaustive labels — concrete instantiation of agentic structured-extraction.
- **[AVGen-Bench: Task-Driven Multi-Granular Evaluation of Text-to-Audio-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08540)** — cs.CL. Joint AV evaluation that goes beyond coarse embedding similarity — useful for the multimodal-generation eval thread.
- **[Structured Causal Video Reasoning via Multi-Objective Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04415)** — cs.CV/cs.CL. Forces Video-LLMs to build a structured entity/action/temporal representation rather than leaning on unstructured deductive chains — addresses a known long-tail failure mode in video reasoning.
