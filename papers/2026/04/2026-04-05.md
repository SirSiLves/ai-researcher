## Top picks

- **ASI-Evolve: AI Accelerates AI** — Weixian Xu, Tiantian Mi. _Takeaway:_ GAIR/SII agentic learn-design-experiment-analyze loop that lets AI advance AI across neural architectures, data curation, and RL algorithms. Reports 105 novel linear-attention architectures and gains over human-designed baselines in math + biomedicine — concrete instantiation of the "AI-as-AI-researcher" thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.29640. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29640)
- **Single-Agent LLMs Outperform Multi-Agent Systems on Multi-Hop Reasoning Under Equal Thinking Token Budgets** — Dat Tran, Douwe Kiela. _Takeaway:_ Information-theoretic argument plus empirical results across multiple model families: when reasoning-token budgets are held equal, single-agent setups consistently match or beat multi-agent systems on multi-hop reasoning. Important corrective to the "more agents = better" narrative; pairs with the SoK on agentic RAG. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02460. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02460)
- **Omni-SimpleMem: Autoresearch-Guided Discovery of Lifelong Multimodal Agent Memory** — Jiaqi Liu, Zipeng Ling. _Takeaway:_ UNC autonomous pipeline that discovers improved memory architectures for long-running agents; reports +411% F1 on LoCoMo and +214% on Mem-Gallery, with the gains coming from architectural fixes rather than hyperparameter tuning. Direct relevance to the agent-memory subfield where Mem0/STALE are converging. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01007. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01007)
- **Experience as a Compass: Multi-agent RAG with Evolving Orchestration and Agent Prompts** — Sha Li, Naren Ramakrishnan. _Takeaway:_ HERA jointly evolves agent topologies and prompts in multi-agent RAG, with emergent network self-organization and gains on knowledge-intensive tasks — sits between agentic-RAG and agent-orchestration tooling. Useful contrast to the "single-agent wins" claim above. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.00901. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00901](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00901)
- **One Word at a Time: Incremental Completion Decomposition Breaks LLM Safety** — Samee Arif, Naihao Deng. _Takeaway:_ ICD elicits a sequence of single-word continuations related to a malicious request before the full response, and shows mechanistically that successful trajectories suppress refusal-related representations and shift activations away from safety-aligned states. New attack class for the agent-safety threat model — pairs with later jailbreak work this month. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.25921. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25921)
- **DataFlex: A Unified Framework for Data-Centric Dynamic Training of Large Language Models** — Peking University authors. _Takeaway:_ Unified data-centric training framework that dynamically adjusts data composition during pretraining/finetuning. Relevant to the data-curation discussion that ASI-Evolve and the diversity-vs-quantity arguments (DIVE etc.) have been driving on the radar. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.26164. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26164)

## Other interesting items

- **[UniDriveVLA: Unifying Understanding, Perception, and Action Planning for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02190)** — HUST. Unified VLA stack for AD; one of the cleaner attempts to fold understanding/perception/action under a single agentic interface.
- **[VideoZeroBench: Probing the Limits of Video MLLMs with Spatio-Temporal Evidence Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01569)** — Peking University. Multimodal evaluation surface that holds video MLLMs to evidence-verification standards; agent eval input.
- **[Tex3D: Objects as Attack Surfaces via Adversarial 3D Textures for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01618)** — ECNU. Adversarial 3D textures break VLA agents — extends the agent attack surface from prompts/tools to physical/3D inputs.
