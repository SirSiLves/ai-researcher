# AI Papers — 2026-04-24

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **VLAA-GUI: Knowing When to Stop, Recover, and Search — A Modular Framework for GUI Automation** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Modular GUI-agent framework that explicitly handles termination, recovery, and re-search as first-class control primitives rather than implicit policy behaviors. Concrete answer to the "GUI agents are strong navigators but weak at knowing when to stop" failure mode. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21375 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21375](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21375)

- **Co-Evolving LLM Decision and Skill Bank Agents for Long-Horizon Tasks** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Joint co-evolution of decision policy and skill bank — pushes the Voyager-style skill-library pattern further by training both layers together. Companion to SkillFlow / SkillLearnBench. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20987 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20987)

- **WorldMark: A Unified Benchmark Suite for Interactive Video World Models** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Unified benchmark for interactive video world models — needed eval surface as world-model-augmented agents (cf. MCP-Cosmos in May 13 reference) proliferate. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21686 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21686)

- **TingIS: Real-time Risk Event Discovery from Noisy Customer Incidents at Enterprise Scale** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Production-scale risk-event discovery system over noisy incident streams — concrete enterprise deployment pattern for an LLM-on-noisy-events workload. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21889 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21889)

- **WebGen-R1: Incentivizing LLMs to Generate Functional and Aesthetic Websites** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ RL with explicit functional + aesthetic rewards for website generation — generative agents producing artifacts evaluated on both correctness and design dimensions. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20398 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20398)

- **Trust but Verify: Introducing DAVinCI — A Framework for Dual Attribution and Verification** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Dual-attribution + verification framework — extends provenance and attribution from generation into a structured trust-and-verify primitive. Pairs with ARGUS / AgentTrust on the governance side. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21193 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21193](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21193)

- **Hybrid Policy Distillation for LLMs** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Hybrid (on-policy + off-policy) distillation for LLMs — slots into the very active distillation-mechanism thread (TIP, Rethinking-OPD, Lightning-OPD from Apr 15-16). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20244 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20244)

- **Temporally Extended Mixture-of-Experts Models** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ MoE variant where expert selection is extended over time-steps rather than reset per token — interesting expressivity/efficiency trade-off, relevant to long-context inference. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20156 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20156)

- **3D-VCD: Hallucination Mitigation in 3D-LLM Embodied Agents through Visual Contrastive Decoding** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Visual contrastive decoding extended to 3D-LLM embodied agents — concrete hallucination-mitigation mechanism for embodied agents where hallucinated objects have physical consequences. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08645 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645)

- **Explainable Disentangled Representation Learning for Generalizable Authorship Attribution** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Disentangled, explainable representations for authorship attribution — relevant to the AI-generated-content detection thread and to provenance work more broadly. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21300 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21300](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21300)
