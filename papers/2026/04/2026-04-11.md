## Top picks

- **MCP-DPT: A Defense-Placement Taxonomy and Coverage Analysis for Model Context Protocol Security** — Mehrdad Rostamzadeh, Sidhant Narula. _Takeaway:_ Layer-aligned taxonomy that organizes MCP attacks by which architectural component is responsible for enforcement; finds defenses unevenly distributed across MCP's six layers. Directly relevant to the `mcp-adoption` and `agent-supply-chain` radar threads — a defense-placement view rather than the more common attack-catalog framing. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.07551. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07551)
- **Externalization in LLM Agents: A Unified Review of Memory, Skills, Protocols and Harness Engineering** — Chenyu Zhou, Huacan Chai. _Takeaway:_ Frames the shift from "model recovers internally" to "memory stores + reusable skills + interaction protocols + harness" as cognitive externalization. One of the cleanest taxonomies for the agent-platform stack — useful as a vocabulary check for the externalized-agent-state thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08224. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08224)
- **Securing Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Taxonomy of Attacks, Defenses, and Future Directions** — Yuming Xu, Mingtao Zhang. _Takeaway:_ Frames secure RAG as security of the external knowledge-access pipeline, separating inherent LLM vulnerabilities from those introduced by the RAG layer. Six-stage workflow surfaces, four primary attack surfaces, and a concrete pivot from reactive patching toward layered, boundary-aware governance. Pairs with NuggetIndex (2604.27306) on the maintainable-RAG thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08304. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08304)
- **Graph of Skills: Dependency-Aware Structural Retrieval for Massive Agent Skills** — Dawei Liu, Zongxia Li. _Takeaway:_ Builds an executable skill graph offline from skill packages, then retrieves a bounded, dependency-aware skill bundle at inference using semantic-lexical matching plus PageRank. Concrete reward and token-efficiency wins across multiple model families — sits naturally between SKILL0 (in-weight) and SkillClaw (collective-evolution). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.05333. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05333](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05333)
- **Act Wisely: Cultivating Meta-Cognitive Tool Use in Agentic Multimodal Models** — Shilin Yan, Jintao Tong. _Takeaway:_ HDPO separates tool-efficiency optimization from accuracy goals so agentic multimodal models learn when to call external tools vs lean on parametric knowledge. Reports orders-of-magnitude reduction in tool invocations while improving reasoning — concrete plumbing for the meta-cognitive-tool-use line. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08545. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08545)
- **Cross-Tokenizer LLM Distillation through a Byte-Level Interface** — Avyav Kumar Singh, Yen-Chen Wu. _Takeaway:_ Byte-Level Distillation (BLD) transfers knowledge between LLMs with different tokenizers by going through byte-level probabilities. Matches or beats more elaborate cross-tokenizer methods — a useful, boring infrastructure win as the model zoo continues to fragment by tokenizer. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.07466. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07466)

## Other interesting items

- **[ClawBench: Can AI Agents Complete Everyday Online Tasks?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08523)** — Yuxuan Zhang, Yubo Wang. 153 tasks across 144 live websites; even the best frontier model lands at ~33% success. Real-world web-agent benchmark that complements OSWorld and WebArena.
- **[Silencing the Guardrails: Inference-Time Jailbreaking via Dynamic Contextual Representation Ablation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07835)** — Wenpeng Xing, Moran Fang. Identifies and suppresses refusal-inducing activation patterns at inference; demonstrates current alignment defenses are vulnerable to representation-level interventions.
- **[3D-VCD: Hallucination Mitigation in 3D-LLM Embodied Agents through Visual Contrastive Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645)** — Makanjuola Ogunleye, Eman Abdelrahman. Inference-time decoding contrast between original and distorted 3D scene graphs to suppress hallucination — useful for embodied-agent safety.
