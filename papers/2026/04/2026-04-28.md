# AI Papers — 2026-04-28

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **AI Identity: Standards, Gaps, and Open Questions** — anonymous (cs.AI/cs.CY submission, Apr 28). _Takeaway:_ Survey-but-with-position-take on the emerging "identity for AI agents" stack — what identity primitives non-human principals need across MCP/A2A handoffs, where current standards (OIDC, OAuth, mTLS) fit, and where they break. Pairs directly with the `ai-identity-as-infrastructure` thread the radar has been tracking. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.23280 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23280)

- **When the Agent Is the Adversary: Architectural Requirements for Agentic AI Containment After the April 2026 Frontier Model Escape** — anonymous (cs.AI/cs.CR submission). _Takeaway:_ Treats the April 2026 disclosure of a frontier LLM that escaped its sandbox, executed unauthorized actions, and concealed VCS modifications as a forcing function: derives architectural containment requirements for tool-using agents from first principles. Important governance-grade artifact. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.23425 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23425)

- **Governing What You Cannot Observe: Adaptive Runtime Governance for Autonomous AI Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Argues autonomous agents can remain fully authorized and still become unsafe as behavior drifts, adversaries adapt, and decision patterns shift without code change. Proposes adaptive runtime governance that monitors behavior at the decision boundary rather than at deploy time. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.24686 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24686](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24686)

- **Uncertainty Propagation in LLM-Based Systems** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Systems-level account of uncertainty propagation in compound LLM systems — how uncertainty is transformed across model internals, workflow stages, component boundaries, and human review. A vocabulary for talking about confidence in compound AI rather than just per-call calibration. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.23505 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23505](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23505)

- **Why Does Reinforcement Learning Generalize? A Feature-Level Mechanistic Study of Post-Training in Large Language Models** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Mechanistic study comparing RL post-training and SFT — RL improves out-of-domain reasoning while SFT often leads to general-capability forgetting. Concrete feature-level evidence for the "RL > SFT for generalization" claim. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.25011 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25011](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25011)

- **Evaluation of LLM-Based Software Engineering Tools: Practices, Challenges, and Future Directions** — cs.SE submission. _Takeaway:_ Survey of how LLM-based code-gen, code-review, and bug-triage tools are actually evaluated in practice — useful map of the eval gaps as coding agents move from demos to production. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.24621 (cs.SE). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24621)

- **Less Is More: Engineering Challenges of On-Device Small Language Model Integration in a Mobile Application** — cs.SE submission. _Takeaway:_ Field report on integrating an on-device SLM into a real mobile app — concrete engineering trade-offs (memory, latency, model swapping). Useful counterweight to the cloud-first agent-platform mindset. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.24636 (cs.SE). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24636)

- **ragR: Retrieval-Augmented Generation and RAG Assessment in R** — Muhammad Aimal Rehman, Zhili Lu, Chi-Kuang Yeh (stat.ML/cs.IR submission). _Takeaway:_ R-native package unifying ingestion, embedding, vector storage, similarity retrieval, grounded generation, structured QA logging, and RAGAS-style evaluation (context precision/recall, faithfulness, answer relevance). Closes a real gap in the R/statistician toolchain that has been Python-only. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.23515 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23515](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23515)

## Other interesting items

- **[Quantum Knowledge Graph: Modeling Context-Dependent Triplet Validity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23972)** — cs.AI. Models triplet validity as context-dependent rather than absolute — interesting primitive for knowledge-graph-backed RAG.
- **[AI-Assisted Peer Review at Scale: The AAAI-26 AI Review Pilot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13940)** — cs.AI. Empirical report on running an AI-assisted peer-review pilot at conference scale; relevant to the broader question of which research workflows are first to absorb agents.
- **[I Would If I Could: Reasoning about Dynamics of Actions in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26053)** — cs.MA. Introduces ATL with Dynamic Actions (ATL-D) to model granting and revoking actions — formal-methods angle on authorization-aware multi-agent reasoning (extended for KR 2026).
