# AI Papers — 2026-04-06

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **GrandCode: Achieving Grandmaster Level in Competitive Programming via Agentic RL** — anonymous (cs.AI/cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Multi-agent RL system with specialized orchestrated modules ("agentic GRPO") that reports grandmaster-level Codeforces performance. Combines offline post-training with online test-time RL — concrete data point for the agentic-RL-for-coding line that's pushing past SWE-bench into competitive-programming territory. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02721 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02721)

- **InCoder-32B-Thinking: Industrial Code World Model** — anonymous (cs.CL/cs.SE submission). _Takeaway:_ Pretrains/post-trains on error-driven chain-of-thought traces and domain execution traces (Verilog sim, GPU profiling) rather than just successful code — addresses the absence of expert reasoning traces in industrial software. Concrete recipe for the "domain-aware coding model" direction. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.03144 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03144)

- **Self-Distilled RLVR (RLSD)** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Combines RL with verifiable rewards and on-policy self-distillation to stabilize training and lift the convergence ceiling. Decomposes the standard RLVR update into magnitude and direction components, with self-distillation supplying the missing fine-grained policy direction. Direct contribution to the unstable-RLVR-recipe thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.03128 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03128)

- **Agentic-MME: What Agentic Capability Really Brings to Multimodal Intelligence?** — anonymous (cs.AI/cs.CV submission). _Takeaway:_ Process-verified MME-style benchmark that scores tool invocation, stepwise checkpoints, sandboxed code execution, and an "overthinking" metric — not just final answer correctness. Concrete eval artifact for the multimodal-agent comparison radar. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.03016 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03016)

- **AgentHazard: Evaluating Harmful Behavior in Computer-Use Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Benchmark targeting computer-use agents' unique threat surface: sequences of individually-benign actions that collectively cause harm. Tracks state maintenance across tool/file/exec interactions. Sits alongside ClawKeeper/AgentTrust in the agent-governance thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02947 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02947](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02947)

- **AgentSocialBench: Evaluating Privacy Risks in Human-Centered Agentic Social Networks** — anonymous (cs.AI/cs.MA submission). _Takeaway:_ Probes how multi-agent coordination and inter-agent information mediation create persistent leakage even with explicit privacy protections — the "abstraction paradox." Concrete eval for the AI-identity / authz-propagation governance line. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01487 (cs.MA). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01487)

- **Test-Time Scaling Makes Overtraining Compute-Optimal** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Joint pass@k modeling shifts optimal pretraining decisions into the overtraining regime once inference cost is part of the optimization. Direct correction to Chinchilla-era recipes for any deployment where you'll run inference-time sampling at scale. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01411 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01411](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01411)

- **Swift-SVD: Theoretical Optimality Meets Practical Efficiency in Low-Rank LLM Compression** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Closed-form, activation-aware SVD compression with dynamic rank allocation driven by layer-wise effective rank. Provides a faster + tighter low-rank baseline for LLM compression — relevant to the edge-deployment thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.01609 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01609](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01609)

## Other interesting items

- **[XpertBench: Expert-Level Tasks with Rubrics-Based Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02368)** — cs.CL. Expert-curated professional-domain tasks with a "ShotJudge" LLM grader that targets self-rewarding biases — concrete artifact for the expert-level eval debate.
- **[A Simple Baseline for Streaming Video Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02317)** — cs.CV/cs.CL. Sliding-window over recent frames beats complex memory-augmented streaming methods on OVO-Bench/StreamingBench — cautionary tale for the streaming-video-memory subfield.
- **[Communicating about Space: Language-Mediated Spatial Integration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27183)** — cs.CL. MLLMs hit 72% vs human 95% on collaborative spatial communication — agents fail to build shared mental models over partial views.
