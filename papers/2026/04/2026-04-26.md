## Top picks

- **Evaluating Multi-Hop Reasoning in RAG Systems: A Comparison of LLM-Based Retriever Evaluation Strategies** — Lorenz Brehme, Thomas Ströhle. _Takeaway:_ Compares LLM-as-judge strategies for retriever evaluation in multi-hop RAG; introduces Context-Aware Retriever Evaluation (CARE) and shows it consistently beats existing approaches across HotPotQA, MuSiQue, SQuAD with OpenAI/Meta/Google models. Concrete eval-stack contribution for the agentic-RAG cluster — accepted at SynIRgy/ECIR 2026. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18234. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18234)
- **AeroRAG: Structured Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented LLM for Fine-Grained Aerial Visual Reasoning** — Junxiao Xue, Quan Deng. _Takeaway:_ Converts aerial images into structured visual knowledge (objects, quantities, locations, relations) and retrieves query-relevant slices for VQA. Particular wins in dense aerial scenes and relation-based tasks — useful instantiation of the structured-multimodal-RAG pattern. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17889. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17889)
- **PersonalAI: A Systematic Comparison of Knowledge Graph Storage and Retrieval Approaches for Personalized LLM agents** — Mikhail Menschikov, Dmitry Evseev. _Takeaway:_ Cross-listed Skoltech work surfacing in this cohort: hybrid graph design with standard edges plus hyper-edges for LLM-built personal knowledge graphs, with per-task evaluation across multiple benchmarks. Methodology contribution to the agent-memory subfield — pairs with mnemonic-sovereignty and STALE on the personalized-memory line. _Category:_ arXiv:2506.17001. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17001)
- **Explainable Disentangled Representation Learning for Generalizable Authorship Attribution in the Era of Generative AI** — Hieu Man, Van-Cuong Pham. _Takeaway:_ EAVAE: separates style from content via architectural design, combining supervised contrastive learning with a VAE that uses separate encoders and an explainable discriminator for authorship attribution. Method-level contribution to the AI-text-detection thread that's becoming a governance concern. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21300. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21300](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21300)
- **3D-VCD: Hallucination Mitigation in 3D-LLM Embodied Agents through Visual Contrastive Decoding** — Makanjuola Ogunleye, Eman Abdelrahman. _Takeaway:_ Inference-time decoding contrast between original and distorted 3D scene graphs to suppress hallucination in embodied agents; demonstrates gains on 3D-POPE and HEAL without retraining. Surfaces in this cohort and complements the embodied-safety thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08645. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645)
- **CoFEE: Reasoning Control for LLM-Based Feature Discovery** — Maximilian Westermann, Ben Griffin. _Takeaway:_ Reasoning-control framework that enforces cognitive behaviors via structured prompts only — no model surgery. Reports +15.2% quality, −29% feature count, −50% cost on feature-discovery tasks. A useful "structured-prompt-as-control-plane" data point complementing the agent-orchestration thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21584. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21584)

## Other interesting items

- **[Context Unrolling in Omni Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21921)** — Ceyuan Yang, Zhijie Lin. Unified omni-modality model with a Context Unrolling mechanism that aggregates complementary modal representations across text/image/video/3D — multimodal-agent foundation work.
- **[Seeing Fast and Slow: Learning the Flow of Time in Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21931)** — Yen-Siang Wu, Rundong Luo. Self-supervised speed-change detection enabling speed-conditioned generation and temporal super-resolution; useful primitive for video-grounded agents.
- **[Adaptive Prompt Embedding Optimization for LLM Jailbreaking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24983)** — Miles Q. Li, Benjamin C. M. Fung (surfaces in cohort). White-box attack that optimizes embeddings of original prompt tokens (not appended adversarial suffixes) — extends the jailbreak threat model to embedding-level interventions.
