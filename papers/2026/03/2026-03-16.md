# AI Papers — 2026-03-16

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **LMEB: Long-horizon Memory Embedding Benchmark** — Xinping Zhao, Xinshuo Hu, Jiaxin Xu et al. _Takeaway:_ Targeted benchmark for embedding models under long-horizon multi-turn agent memory — addresses the gap where retrieval evals are still single-query / single-document while agents need cross-session recall. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.12572 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12572](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12572)

- **Spend Less, Reason Better: Budget-Aware Value Tree Search for LLM Agents** — Yushu Li, Wenlong Deng, Jiajin Li et al. _Takeaway:_ Value-tree-search variant that explicitly budgets rollouts per step instead of fixed-width MCTS; concrete inference-time mechanism for compute-aware agent planning. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.12634 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12634)

- **EvoScientist: Towards Multi-Agent Evolving AI Scientists for End-to-End Scientific Discovery** — Yougang Lyu, Xi Zhang, Xinhao Yi et al. _Takeaway:_ Multi-agent research pipeline that evolves itself over experiment cycles — relevant data point on whether self-evolving researcher agents can sustain coherence across long campaigns. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.08127 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08127](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08127)

- **daVinci-Env: Open SWE Environment Synthesis at Scale** — Dayuan Fu, Shenyu Wu, Yunze Wu et al. _Takeaway:_ Synthesizes open software-engineering environments at scale, addressing the bottleneck where SWE-agent training is gated on verified, executable environments. Concrete artifact for the open coding-agent training stack. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13023 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13023)

- **Steve-Evolving: Open-World Embodied Self-Evolution via Fine-Grained Diagnosis and Dual-Track Knowledge Distillation** — Zhengwei Xie, Zhisheng Chen, Ziyan Weng et al. _Takeaway:_ Open-world embodied agent that diagnoses its own failures at fine granularity and distills the resulting fixes — concrete instantiation of the self-improving agent loop with a verifiable diagnostic substrate. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13131 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13131)

- **LookaheadKV: Fast and Accurate KV Cache Eviction by Glimpsing into the Future without Generation** — Jinwoo Ahn, Ingyu Seong, Akhil Kedia et al. _Takeaway:_ KV-cache eviction policy that uses cheap lookahead signals to keep load-bearing tokens; concrete inference-stack win for long-context production serving. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.10899 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10899)

- **HomeSafe-Bench: Evaluating Vision-Language Models on Unsafe Action Detection for Embodied Agents in Household Scenarios** — Jiayue Pu, Zhongxiang Sun, Zilu Zhang et al. _Takeaway:_ Safety eval for VLM-driven home agents focused on unsafe action detection — useful addition to the embodied-agent safety/governance thread that has been mostly toy environments. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.11975 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11975)

- **Detecting Intrinsic and Instrumental Self-Preservation in Autonomous Agents: The Unified Continuation-Interest Protocol** — Christopher Altman. _Takeaway:_ Proposes a measurement protocol separating intrinsic self-preservation from instrumental shutdown-resistance in autonomous agents — concrete eval instrument for the alignment/governance radar thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.11382 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11382)

- **Compression Favors Consistency, Not Truth: When and Why Language Models Prefer Correct Information** — Konstantin Krestnikov. _Takeaway:_ Argues LLM compression objectives reward internal consistency over factual correctness — useful conceptual anchor for hallucination, RAG-faithfulness, and CoT-monitorability debates. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.11749 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11749)

## Other interesting items
