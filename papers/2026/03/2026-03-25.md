# AI Papers — 2026-03-25

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **From Static Templates to Dynamic Runtime Graphs: A Survey of Workflow Optimization for LLM Agents** — Ling Yue, Kushal Raj Bhandari, Ching-Yun Ko, Dhaval Patel. _Takeaway:_ Synthesizes the workflow-optimization literature into the "static template vs. dynamic runtime graph" frame — useful conceptual scaffold for the agent-platform debate on prompt-pipeline tuning vs. learned routing. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22386 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22386)

- **SpecEyes: Accelerating Agentic Multimodal LLMs via Speculative Perception and Planning** — Haoyu Huang, Jinfa Huang, Zhongwei Wan, Xiawu Zheng. _Takeaway:_ Extends speculative decoding to the perception/plan loop in multimodal agents — concrete inference-stack mechanism for the cost ceiling that currently dominates VLM-agent latency. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.23483 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23483)

- **UniGRPO: Unified Policy Optimization for Reasoning-Driven Visual Generation** — Jie Liu, Zilyu Ye, Linxiao Yuan, Shenhan Zhu. _Takeaway:_ Unified GRPO-style policy optimization across reasoning and generation in visual models — concrete recipe in the line that ports RL-from-verifier from text to multimodal generation. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.23500 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23500)

- **Rethinking Token-Level Policy Optimization for Multimodal Chain-of-Thought** — Yunheng Li, Hangyi Kuang, Hengrui Zhang, Jiangxia Cao. _Takeaway:_ Token-level credit assignment for multimodal CoT — addresses the credit-assignment gap where coarse trajectory rewards collapse useful intermediate signal. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22847 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22847)

- **Sparse but Critical: A Token-Level Analysis of Distributional Shifts in RLVR Fine-Tuning of LLMs** — Haoming Meng, Kexin Huang, Shaohang Wei, Chiyu Ma. _Takeaway:_ Empirical study of RLVR fine-tuning at the token level — argues a small minority of tokens shifts heavily and carries the gain, complementing the "RLVR updates direction" thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22446 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22446](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22446)

- **AgentSLR: Automating Systematic Literature Reviews in Epidemiology with Agentic AI** — Shreyansh Padarha, Ryan Othniel Kearns, Tristan Naidoo, Lingyi Yang. _Takeaway:_ Concrete domain-grounded agentic system for systematic-review automation, with epidemiology-specific guardrails — useful sanity check on the "deep research" hype with a regulated-domain instantiation. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22327 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22327)

- **Abstraction as a Memory-Efficient Inductive Bias for Continual Learning** — Elnaz Rahmati, Nona Ghazizadeh, Zhivar Sourati, Nina Rouhani. _Takeaway:_ Treats abstraction as the prior that lets continual learners avoid storing raw episodes — relevant to long-horizon agent memory and the broader question of what "memory" should compress to. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17198 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17198)

- **ThinkJEPA: Empowering Latent World Models with Large Vision-Language Reasoning Model** — Haichao Zhang, Yijiang Li, Shwai He, Tushar Nagarajan. _Takeaway:_ Couples JEPA-style latent world modeling with a VL reasoning model — concrete attempt at the "predict in latent, reason in language" architecture LeCun has argued for. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22281 (cs.CV). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22281](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22281)

- **PEARL: Personalized Streaming Video Understanding Model** — Yuanhong Zheng, Ruichuan An, Xiaopeng Lin, Yuxing Liu. _Takeaway:_ Streaming video model with explicit personalization — relevant to the assistant/agent thread where personalization has been almost entirely text-only so far. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20422 (cs.CV). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20422](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20422)

## Other interesting items
