## Top picks

- **OpenResearcher: A Fully Open Pipeline for Long-Horizon Deep Research Trajectory Synthesis** — Zhuofeng Li, Dongfu Jiang, Xueguang Ma, Haoxiang Zhang. _Takeaway:_ Open pipeline for generating the long-horizon research trajectories used to train deep-research agents — concrete answer to the data-bottleneck that currently keeps strong research agents inside industrial labs. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20278 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20278)

- **LongCat-Flash-Prover: Advancing Native Formal Reasoning via Agentic Tool-Integrated Reinforcement Learning** — Jianing Wang, Jianfei Zhang, Qi Guo, Linsen Guo. _Takeaway:_ Agentic tool-integrated RL recipe for native formal-proof generation — concrete instantiation of the "tools-as-verifier" thread where the proof assistant grades trajectories. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21065 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21065)

- **On the Direction of RLVR Updates for LLM Reasoning: Identification and Exploitation** — Kexin Huang, Haoming Meng, Junkang Wu, Jinda Lu. _Takeaway:_ Decomposes RLVR gradient updates into useful and harmful directions; exploits the structure for cleaner reasoning gains. Concrete diagnostic for why some RLVR runs collapse the base policy. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22117 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22117](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22117)

- **mSFT: Addressing Dataset Mixtures Overfitting Heterogeneously in Multi-task SFT** — Woosung Koh, Jeyoung Jeon, Youngjin Song, Yujin Cheon. _Takeaway:_ Characterizes the under-discussed failure mode where multi-task SFT overfits some datasets while underfitting others, and proposes a heterogeneous mixing scheme. Practical recipe for the post-training stack. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21606 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21606)

- **PivotRL: High Accuracy Agentic Post-Training at Low Compute Cost** — Junkeun Yi, Damon Mosk-Aoyama, Baihe Huang, Ritu Gala. _Takeaway:_ Compute-efficient agentic post-training recipe — concrete entry in the "agent RL without industrial budget" thread, with claims of preserving accuracy under aggressive cost cuts. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21383 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21383)

- **REVERE: Reflective Evolving Research Engineer for Scientific Workflows** — Balaji Dinesh Gangireddi, Aniketh Garikaparthi, Manasi Patwardhan, Arman Cohan. _Takeaway:_ Research-engineer agent that reflects and evolves its own workflow over scientific tasks — useful counterpart to research-agent benchmarks that mostly measure one-shot performance. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20667 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20667)

- **BubbleRAG: Evidence-Driven Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Black-Box Knowledge Graphs** — Duyi Pan, Tianao Lou, Xin Li, Haoze Song. _Takeaway:_ RAG over knowledge graphs where the graph schema is unknown — addresses the realistic enterprise setting where the underlying KG is a queryable black box rather than a fully exposed structure. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.20309 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20309)

- **Omni-WorldBench: Towards a Comprehensive Interaction-Centric Evaluation for World Models** — Meiqi Wu, Zhixin Cai, Fufangchen Zhao, Xiaokun Feng. _Takeaway:_ World-model benchmark explicitly evaluating interaction rather than rollout fidelity — fills the gap between rollout-FID metrics and what agents actually need from a simulator. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22212 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22212](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22212)

- **SEM: Sparse Embedding Modulation for Post-Hoc Debiasing of Vision-Language Models** — Quentin Guimard, Federico Bartsch, Simone Caldarella, Rahaf Aljundi. _Takeaway:_ Post-hoc sparse intervention on VLM embeddings for debiasing — concrete mechanism rather than another fine-tuning recipe, with relevance to the safety/governance thread on cheap-to-deploy mitigations. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.19028 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19028](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19028)
