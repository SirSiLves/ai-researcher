## Top papers
- **Bridging Protocol and Production: Design Patterns for Deploying AI Agents with MCP** — Vasundra Srinivasan. _Why notable:_ Identity propagation, tool budgeting, error handling as the three deployment gaps. [arXiv:2603.13417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13417)
- **CUBE: A Standard for Unifying Agent Benchmarks** — Lacoste, Gontier. _Why notable:_ Universal benchmark protocol built on MCP `tools/call`; removes the per-benchmark integration tax. [arXiv:2603.15798](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15798)
- **OpenSeeker: Democratizing Frontier Search Agents by Fully Open-Sourcing Training Data** — Malay, Nayak, Nair et al. _Why notable:_ Full training-data pipeline behind a competitive deep-search agent — concrete artifact for "open frontier-agent." [arXiv:2603.13594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13594)
- **EnterpriseOps-Gym: Environments and Evaluations for Stateful Agentic Planning** — Zhu, Fang, Liao et al. _Why notable:_ Missing middle between toy ToolBench and SWE-bench-style coding — long-horizon enterprise workflows with role/permission boundaries and persistent context. [arXiv:2603.15619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15619)
- **AC4A: Access Control for Agents** — Sharma, Grossman. _Why notable:_ Granular permission framework — beyond today's all-or-nothing access; enforceable surface for agent governance. [arXiv:2603.20933](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20933)
- **Security Considerations for Multi-agent Systems** — Nguyen, Ndebugre. _Why notable:_ Non-determinism (1.231 mean) and inter-agent data leakage are the two most-neglected domains — argues for MAS-specific controls before A2A scales. [arXiv:2603.09002](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09002)
- **Mixture-of-Depths Attention** — Hauzenberger, Schmidinger, Schmied et al. _Why notable:_ Per-token depth routing for attention; architectural counter to the curse of depth. [arXiv:2603.15590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15590)
- **Nemotron-Cascade 2** — Yang, Liu, Chen et al. (NVIDIA). _Why notable:_ Cascade RL + multi-domain on-policy distillation; "RL-first then distill" replacing SFT-then-RLHF. [arXiv:2603.19220](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19220)
- **AgentProcessBench: Diagnosing Step-Level Process Quality in Tool-Using Agents** — Alastruey et al. _Why notable:_ Step-level evaluation (errors often irreversible vs backtrackable math). [arXiv:2603.16309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16309)
- **Alignment Makes Language Models Normative, Not Descriptive** — Muhtar, Liu et al. _Why notable:_ 120 base/aligned pairs across 10,000+ responses; alignment systematically shifts from descriptive to normative. [arXiv:2603.17621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17621)
