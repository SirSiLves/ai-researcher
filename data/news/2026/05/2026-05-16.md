## Major announcements & releases

**SAP invests in n8n at $5.2B valuation, embeds it in Joule Studio** — In a secondary share sale announced May 12, SAP doubled n8n's valuation from $2.5B to $5.2B and signed a multi-year deal to embed n8n's workflow engine into Joule Studio, SAP's new agent-building environment. The Berlin-based automation startup becomes Germany's most valuable AI company; embedded GA targeted for Q3 2026. _Why it matters:_ the biggest enterprise-AI distribution deal of the week — SAP picks an open-source workflow runtime over building one, and n8n gets a moat across SAP's 400k+ enterprise customer base. [https://blog.n8n.io/n8n-sap/](https://blog.n8n.io/n8n-sap/) (context: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/sap-invests-in-ai-automation-startup-n8n-at-5-2-billion-value))

**SAP unveils the Autonomous Enterprise at Sapphire 2026** — At its Orlando customer event (May 12–14), SAP introduced Joule Studio as its enterprise-grade agentic development environment with 200+ pre-built Joule agents, plus deepening alliances with Anthropic, AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Palantir. Pro-code support extends to LangChain, Pydantic AI, LlamaIndex, and the n8n environment for visual multi-agent orchestration. _Why it matters:_ SAP is reorganizing its AI story around "the system actually runs itself" — and its message has become the enterprise platform layer's collective bet on agentic operations. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/)

**SAP × Anthropic — Claude embedded across SAP Business AI Platform** — Announced at Sapphire, SAP and Anthropic expanded their partnership to make Claude the primary reasoning and agentic backbone across SAP's AI portfolio, powered by Joule and Joule agents. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic now sits inside the world's largest ERP vendor's flagship product story. Combined with the Snowflake Cortex same-day Claude Opus 4.7 launch and the existing ServiceNow Build Agent deal, the enterprise-default-model question is shifting decisively in Anthropic's favor. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/)

**IBM ships Red Hat AI Inference and OpenShift Virtualization on IBM Cloud** — Announced May 12, with Red Hat AI Inference reaching GA May 22. Powered by vLLM and Red Hat AI's inference engine, the managed service supports a curated open-model catalog (Granite 4.0 H Small, Mistral-Small-3.2-24B, Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-OSS-120B, Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-FP8) with built-in governance controls for hybrid-cloud production workloads. _Why it matters:_ IBM finally has an answer to Bedrock/Vertex/Foundry for hybrid customers — and it consolidates "the open-weights inference stack" into a single billable service. [https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-12-ibm-announces-red-hat-ai-inference-and-red-hat-openShift-virtualization-service-on-ibm-cloud](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-12-ibm-announces-red-hat-ai-inference-and-red-hat-openShift-virtualization-service-on-ibm-cloud)

**Snowflake Cortex AI Guardrails reach GA across Intelligence and Agents** — On May 14, Snowflake moved Cortex AI Guardrails from preview to GA for Snowflake Intelligence and Cortex Agents (already in Cortex Code), adding contextual-reasoning detection for prompt injection, jailbreaks, and indirect prompt injections embedded in tool calls. Same week, AI_CLASSIFY gained document-classification support in public preview, and Claude Opus 4.7 landed on Cortex same-day. _Why it matters:_ Snowflake is the first major enterprise data platform to ship runtime adversarial defense for agentic flows as a default product feature, not an add-on. [https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2026/other/2026-05-14-cortex-ai-guardrails-si-cortex-agents](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2026/other/2026-05-14-cortex-ai-guardrails-si-cortex-agents) (vendor blog: [https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/cortex-ai-guardrails-prompt-injection-prevention/](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/cortex-ai-guardrails-prompt-injection-prevention/))

**ServiceNow expands AI agent governance with Microsoft Agent 365 integration** — Announced at Knowledge 2026, ServiceNow AI Control Tower extends across Azure-backed Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, and the Microsoft Agent 365 ecosystem. ServiceNow also opened its full system of action to any AI agent via the generally available MCP Server — meaning Claude-, Copilot-, or custom-built agents can act against ServiceNow workflows under one governance plane. _Why it matters:_ the platform agent-governance category is consolidating quickly; ServiceNow + Microsoft now jointly claim the largest cross-vendor agent control plane in production. [https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-expands-AI-agent-governance-through-deeper-integration-with-Microsoft/default.aspx](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-expands-AI-agent-governance-through-deeper-integration-with-Microsoft/default.aspx)

**AWS MCP Server hits GA** — The AWS MCP Server is generally available as a core component of the Agent Toolkit for AWS, giving coding agents IAM-guardrailed, CloudWatch- and CloudTrail-audited access to any AWS API through a single tool, with sandboxed Python execution for multi-step operations. No additional charge — customers pay only for downstream AWS resources. _Why it matters:_ removes the last hyperscaler holdout from first-party MCP support. With AWS, Microsoft Foundry, and Google Cloud all shipping MCP servers, the protocol's "where do we plug in?" question is effectively answered for enterprise. [https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/05/aws-mcp-server/](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/05/aws-mcp-server/)

**EU Digital Omnibus on AI — provisional trilogue deal reached May 7** — Parliament and Council agreed to postpone Annex III high-risk AI obligations from August 2026 to 2 December 2027, and Annex I (safety-product-embedded) obligations to 2 August 2028, while adding a new Article 5 prohibition on AI-generated CSAM and non-consensual intimate imagery. Formal adoption is targeted before 2 August 2026. _Why it matters:_ the biggest substantive change to the AI Act's enforcement timeline since enactment — the EU openly conceded that technical standards and guidance aren't ready, buying ~16 months of breathing room for compliance investment. [https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/digital-omnibus-on-ai-provisional-agreement-reached-at-the-may-trilogue](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/digital-omnibus-on-ai-provisional-agreement-reached-at-the-may-trilogue) (context: [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/ai-act-omnibus-what-just-happened-and-what-comes-next))

**UK AISI signs frontier-safety partnership with Microsoft** — Announced May 5, the partnership covers three workstreams: high-risk capability evaluation methods, safeguard-effectiveness testing, and societal-resilience research (covering emotional dependency, mental-health interactions, and erosion of trust in human professionals — a domain UK and US AI Safety Institutes had previously approached cautiously). _Why it matters:_ first major frontier-lab partnership for AISI that explicitly funds societal-impact evaluation, not just capability mapping. Signals where the evaluation frontier is shifting. [https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/partnering-with-microsoft-to-strengthen-frontier-ai-safety](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/partnering-with-microsoft-to-strengthen-frontier-ai-safety) (Microsoft side: [https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/05/advancing-ai-evaluation-with-the-center-for-ai-standards-us-and-innovation-and-the-ai-security-institute-uk/](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/05/advancing-ai-evaluation-with-the-center-for-ai-standards-us-and-innovation-and-the-ai-security-institute-uk/))

**Runway pushes past $5.3B valuation with video/world-model bet** — In a TechCrunch profile dated May 15, Runway said it added $40M in ARR in Q2 alone and is staking its next chapter on world models rather than text-first systems. _Why it matters:_ a contrarian wager against the text-LLM playbook from a generative-media incumbent that's now large enough to compete with Google on capability research, not just product. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/runway-started-by-helping-filmmakers-now-it-wants-to-beat-google-at-ai/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/runway-started-by-helping-filmmakers-now-it-wants-to-beat-google-at-ai/)

**xAI ships Grok Build (beta) and retires legacy Grok models** — Grok Build, a terminal-native agentic coding CLI on the Agent Client Protocol, launched in beta for SuperGrok Heavy users. Effective May 15 at 12:00 PT, several legacy Grok models were retired with API requests redirected to grok-4.3. _Why it matters:_ xAI ships its Claude-Code-and-Codex equivalent the same week SAP locks in Claude, and forces the rest of its API customers up the version curve. [https://x.ai/news](https://x.ai/news) (context: [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/xai-launches-grok-build-beta-agentic-coding-cli-explained), [xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/may-15-retirement))
