## Major announcements & releases

**SAP doubles n8n valuation to $5.2B, embeds it as Joule Studio's orchestration layer** — At SAP Sapphire (mid-May), SAP took a strategic stake in Berlin-based n8n via a secondary share sale, jumping its valuation from $2.5B (Oct 2025) to $5.2B and making n8n Germany's most valuable AI company. n8n's workflow engine becomes the agent-orchestration substrate inside Joule Studio, with a multi-year partnership and GA targeted Q3 2026. n8n reports 1,400 enterprise customers and 1.7M monthly active developers. _Why it matters:_ the enterprise stack is consolidating around composable workflow runtimes rather than monolithic agent platforms — and a "low-code automation tool" just became core infra for Europe's biggest software vendor. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/sap-invests-in-ai-automation-startup-n8n-at-5-2-billion-value) ([Tech.eu coverage](https://tech.eu/2026/05/12/n8n-s-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-following-sap-strategic-investment/))

**SAP unveils "Autonomous Enterprise," Joule Studio, and Claude on the SAP Business AI Platform** — At Sapphire, SAP launched Joule Studio (a fully managed environment for building/managing AI agents, applications and workflows), announced embedded partnerships with Vercel and n8n, and confirmed Claude as a primary reasoning model embedded across SAP's AI portfolio via an expanded Anthropic deal. Deeper alliances with AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, NVIDIA and Palantir were also announced; free design-time access to Joule Studio runs through end of 2026. _Why it matters:_ SAP is now explicitly multi-model and agent-first at the platform layer, with Claude as a default reasoning engine for ERP-adjacent work. [SAP News — Joule Studio](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/new-joule-studio-enterprise-scale-agentic-development/) ([Autonomous Enterprise](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/), [Claude on SAP](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/))

**IBM Think 2026: watsonx Orchestrate becomes a multi-agent control plane; Sovereign Core ships on Red Hat OpenShift** — IBM used Think (May 5) to position itself as the agent governance layer: a new generation of watsonx Orchestrate (private preview) acts as an agentic control plane for managing thousands of agents from any source under unified policy enforcement; IBM Bob (GA) ships as an enterprise agentic development partner; Sovereign Core targets data-sovereignty workloads built on Red Hat OpenShift and Red Hat AI. IBM Confluent (real-time data to AI) and IBM Concert (intelligent operations) round out the announcement. _Why it matters:_ the consensus enterprise pattern is shifting from "deploy a few agents" to "govern fleets of them" — and IBM is staking the orchestration/policy layer. [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-05-think-2026-ibm-delivers-the-blueprint-for-the-ai-operating-model-as-the-ai-divide-widens)

**Red Hat AI 3.4 + Red Hat AI Inference on IBM Cloud (GA May 22)** — Red Hat shipped AI 3.4 with model-as-a-service capability (expose internally approved models through governed interfaces), expanded distributed inferencing with speculative decoding (up to 3x text-generation speedup at no quality loss) and Agent Control. IBM announced Red Hat AI Inference on IBM Cloud (GA May 22), powered by vLLM with a catalog spanning Granite 4.0 H Small, Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct, Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-OSS-120B and Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-FP8. _Why it matters:_ Red Hat is building the hybrid-cloud inference plane below SAP/IBM/ServiceNow — and proving the "open frontier" model catalog is now genuinely competitive with proprietary endpoints. [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-12-ibm-announces-red-hat-ai-inference-and-red-hat-openShift-virtualization-service-on-ibm-cloud) ([SiliconANGLE on AI 3.4](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/12/red-hat-expands-agentic-ai-strategy-with-new-inference-automation-and-sovereignty-capabilities/), [Heise summary](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Red-Hat-AI-3-4-with-Agent-Control-and-Extended-Inference-11292072.html))

**OpenAI launches "Deployment Company" with $4B + acquires Tomoro** — On May 11, OpenAI spun up the OpenAI Deployment Company, a $4B-funded entity to embed Forward Deployed Engineers into enterprises building on its stack, and agreed to acquire AI consultancy Tomoro (~150 FDEs/deployment specialists join on day one). _Why it matters:_ OpenAI is industrializing the Palantir/Anthropic-style "do the integration work for the customer" model — a structural bet that distribution, not the model, is the next moat. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/)

**OpenAI launches personal finance in ChatGPT via Plaid (12,000+ institutions)** — On May 15, ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US got a preview of personal-finance tools that let them connect bank accounts (via Plaid) and ask ChatGPT for spending analysis and planning. CFO Sarah Friar said the same day OpenAI may raise more capital on top of the recent $122B round, citing "a vertical wall of demand" and limited 2026 compute. _Why it matters:_ ChatGPT is moving from generic assistant to authenticated financial-data co-pilot — and OpenAI is signaling another fundraise during peak compute scarcity. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/) ([PYMNTS on capital raise](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-considers-raising-more-capital-meet-ai-demand/))

The May 5 Now Assist for Creator Australia GA release added a Test Agent for ATF coverage, a Build Agent that creates AI agents inside custom apps, and MCP client support. _Why it matters:_ ServiceNow is positioning itself as the cross-vendor agent control plane — and explicitly betting on heterogeneous agent ecosystems rather than a captive runtime. **AWS Bedrock AgentCore Payments preview — agents that pay** — On May 7, AWS launched AgentCore Payments in preview (with Coinbase and Stripe), the first managed payment capability purpose-built for autonomous agents: wallet auth, session spending limits, transaction execution and governance. Also in May: AgentCore expanded to GovCloud (US-West) and São Paulo regions, and the AWS MCP Server reached GA on May 6. _Why it matters:_ "agents transacting on the user's behalf" is the next abuse surface — and AWS just shipped the production-grade rail before the threat model is fully understood. AI_CLASSIFY also added document input (PDF, DOCX, CSV) on May 13 for SQL-native document routing. _Why it matters:_ guardrails for indirect prompt injection inside tool calls — the most-cited unsolved agent-security problem — are now table-stakes infrastructure on the data warehouse. [Snowflake docs (May 14)](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2026/other/2026-05-14-cortex-ai-guardrails-si-cortex-agents) ([AI_CLASSIFY for documents](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2026/other/2026-05-13-ai-classify-document))

**EU AI Act Omnibus political deal (May 7) extends HRAIS deadlines, adds new prohibitions** — EU Council and Parliament reached political agreement on the AI Act Omnibus on May 7. High-risk AI system compliance is now tied to standards availability, with long-stop dates of 2 Dec 2027 (high-risk systems) and 2 Aug 2028 (product-embedded). A new prohibition targets "nudifier" apps generating CSAM (effective Dec 2, 2026); GenAI systems on the market before Aug 2, 2026 only need to meet watermarking by Dec 2, 2026 under a grandfathering rule. Fines up to €15M or 3% of worldwide turnover. _Why it matters:_ the most-watched AI regulation in the world just bought industry 12-24 more months — and locked in the substance everyone was lobbying against. [Council of the EU press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) ([Latham & Watkins analysis](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ai-act-update-eu-resolves-to-change-rules-and-extend-deadlines))

[Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2173482/xai-coding-agent-grok-build/) ([xAI model retirement notice](https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/may-15-retirement), [Wall Street push](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/05/14/tech/musk-xai-wall-street-grok-chatbot/))
