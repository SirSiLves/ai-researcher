# AI News — 2026-05-23

_Collector: ai-news. Slice: vendor blogs, tech news, web search._

## Major announcements & releases

- **Anthropic ships self-hosted sandboxes and MCP tunnels for Claude Managed Agents** — Anthropic published a May 22 update putting self-hosted sandboxes into public beta (sensitive files, packages, and services stay in customer infrastructure while orchestration, context, and error recovery stay on Anthropic's side) and the new MCP tunnels into research preview, alongside higher Claude Code / Opus API limits and Managed Agents additions for multi-agent orchestration, dreaming, outcomes, and webhooks. _Why it matters:_ this is the missing piece that lets regulated buyers run Anthropic-orchestrated agents over their own data without giving up sandbox isolation — the same friction that has gated SAP, Workday, and financial-services rollouts so far. [https://www.anthropic.com/news](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

- **MCP 2026-07-28 release candidate published** — The Model Context Protocol team shipped the largest spec revision since launch on May 21: stateless protocol core (scales on ordinary HTTP), an Extensions framework, the Tasks extension for long-running work, MCP Apps for server-rendered UIs, hardened OAuth/OIDC-aligned authorization, and a formal deprecation policy. Final spec lands July 28 after a ten-week implementer validation window. _Why it matters:_ statelessness, MCP Apps, and Tasks fix the three failure modes that kept MCP from scaling in production — sticky-session sprawl, no native UI surface, and no contract for jobs that outlive a single request. Vendors who already shipped servers (AWS, ServiceNow, Snowflake) have a concrete migration target. [https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/)

- **NVIDIA + Google Cloud expand the Rubin/Gemini agent stack** — A May 21 joint announcement formalised the next phase of the Google Cloud–NVIDIA partnership: Vera Rubin-powered A5X instances on Google Cloud, deeper integration with Gemini models, and tooling to train, deploy, and operationalise agents on Google Cloud infra. _Why it matters:_ the Rubin generation is now reachable as a Google Cloud SKU rather than only via custom AI-factory builds, and Google's agent stack (ADK, A2A, Gemini 3.5 Flash) gets first-class Blackwell/Rubin capacity for agent workloads. [https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/google-cloud-developer-community-ai-builders/](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/google-cloud-developer-community-ai-builders/)

- **SAP Sapphire unveils "Autonomous Enterprise" with Joule Studio + Claude embedded** — At Sapphire (May 19–21) SAP launched Joule Studio for enterprise-scale agentic development, the SAP Business AI Platform with Anthropic Claude as a primary reasoning and agentic engine, plus AWS (zero-copy data), Google Cloud + Microsoft (bidirectional A2A interoperability), NVIDIA (secure runtime), Mistral and Cohere (sovereign options), and n8n (visual orchestration) embedded as platform partners. _Why it matters:_ SAP is no longer trying to build the model layer — it is wiring the frontier labs straight into ERP, with n8n providing the workflow canvas. This is the most concrete enterprise agent stack any incumbent ERP vendor has shipped, and it puts Claude squarely inside the SAP installed base. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/) (vendor announcement) and [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/)

- **n8n valuation doubles to $5.2B as SAP takes a strategic stake and embeds it in Joule Studio** — Announced May 12: SAP led a strategic investment that doubles n8n's valuation from ~$2.5B (Oct 2025) to $5.2B, plus a multiyear deal to embed n8n inside Joule Studio with SAP security and compliance, and access to 1,000+ business-tool / DB / model integrations. n8n now reports 1,400+ enterprise customers and 1.7M monthly active developers; the embedded GA target is Q3 2026. Mercedes-Benz separately announced a global workflow-automation platform built on n8n in the same window. _Why it matters:_ this is the most important non-frontier AI business story of the month — Germany's most valuable AI company becomes the de facto agent-orchestration layer for the world's largest ERP installed base. It also legitimises the "low-code/pro-code workflow" pattern over pure agentic LLM orchestration. [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/n8n-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-as-sap-makes-strategic-investment-and-plans-to-embed-the-ai-platform-into-joule-studio-302767222.html](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/n8n-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-as-sap-makes-strategic-investment-and-plans-to-embed-the-ai-platform-into-joule-studio-302767222.html) (also covered by [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/sap-invests-in-ai-automation-startup-n8n-at-5-2-billion-value))

- **EU AI Act Omnibus reaches political agreement; Commission opens draft high-risk guidelines** — Council and Parliament reached political agreement on May 7 to amend the AI Act ("AI Act Omnibus"): Annex III HRAIS obligations deferred from 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Dec 2027 (16-month delay), Annex I HRAIS obligations pushed from 2 Aug 2027 to 2 Aug 2028, plus two new prohibited practices (non-consensual intimate AI imagery and CSAM-generating systems). On May 19 the Commission published draft guidelines on high-risk AI classification with a public consultation open until June 23. _Why it matters:_ enterprises now have meaningful additional time for HRAIS conformity work, but the high-risk classification criteria are still in play — this consultation window is the most important EU AI-Act lobbying moment of the year. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)

- **Pinecone Launch Week ships Nexus + KnowQL, marketplace, and native full-text search** — Pinecone's May 4–8 Launch Week introduced Pinecone Nexus (a "knowledge engine for agents") with KnowQL — the first declarative query language designed for agents rather than humans, replacing multi-hop retrieval with a single declarative call — plus a Pinecone Marketplace of production-ready assistant templates and native full-text search inside the core database. _Why it matters:_ this is Pinecone's bet that the next layer above vector retrieval is structured agent-readable knowledge, not bigger embeddings. KnowQL plus the compilation-stage knowledge layer trend (covered by VentureBeat the same week) signals the start of a real "agentic retrieval" stack distinct from classic RAG. [https://www.pinecone.io/blog/knowledge-infrastructure-for-agents/](https://www.pinecone.io/blog/knowledge-infrastructure-for-agents/)

- **AWS Bedrock AgentCore adds payments (with Coinbase + Stripe), managed AWS MCP Server hits GA** — AWS shipped two foundational pieces in May: AgentCore Payments in preview (May 7), letting agents authenticate wallets, execute transactions, govern spend, and observe payment flows for APIs, MCP servers, web content, and other agents; and the AWS MCP Server going generally available (May 11) as a managed, authenticated entry point to AWS services for coding agents. AgentCore also reached AWS GovCloud (US-West) on May 5. _Why it matters:_ "agents that transact" moves from research demo to first-party AWS surface. Combined with the GovCloud rollout and the OpenAI/Bedrock managed-agents preview, AWS is positioning Bedrock as the enterprise-default agent runtime in the same week SAP and ServiceNow are doing the same in their own clouds. [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-weekly-roundup-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-payments-agent-toolkit-for-aws-and-more-may-11-2026/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-weekly-roundup-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-payments-agent-toolkit-for-aws-and-more-may-11-2026/)

- **IBM Think 2026 lays out the AI Operating Model — watsonx Orchestrate as agentic control plane, Sovereign Core on OpenShift** — At Think (May 5), IBM positioned the next-gen watsonx Orchestrate (private preview) as a multi-agent control plane that ingests agents from any source under consistent policy, launched IBM Sovereign Core (built on Red Hat OpenShift and Red Hat AI) for operational independence, made IBM Bob (an agentic developer partner) generally available, and pulled in the Confluent acquisition to provide a real-time Kafka/Flink data substrate for agents. _Why it matters:_ this is IBM's most coherent AI platform pitch in years — control-plane + sovereign substrate + real-time data — and it explicitly competes with what SAP and ServiceNow announced the same month. [https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-05-think-2026-ibm-delivers-the-blueprint-for-the-ai-operating-model-as-the-ai-divide-widens](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-05-think-2026-ibm-delivers-the-blueprint-for-the-ai-operating-model-as-the-ai-divide-widens)

- **Isomorphic Labs closes $2.1B Series B led by Thrive to scale its AI drug-design engine** — Announced May 12: Alphabet's drug-design spinout raised $2.1B (Thrive Capital lead, with Alphabet, GV, MGX, Temasek, CapitalG, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund) to scale IsoDDE and push its therapeutic pipeline to clinic. Reported as the second-largest biotech round ever. _Why it matters:_ frontier labs going vertical — DeepMind's protein/structural-biology stack is now a $2B+ standalone bet, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund putting capital in a Hassabis-led biotech signals where government AI compute will get steered next. [https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles/isomorphic-labs-announces-series-b-investment-round](https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles/isomorphic-labs-announces-series-b-investment-round)

- **Sierra raises $950M at ~$15B post-money for customer-service agents** — Bret Taylor's Sierra closed $950M (Tiger Global + GV lead; Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks following) on May 4, pushing valuation from ~$10B in late 2025 to over $15B and claiming agents at "more than 40% of the Fortune 50" running billions of customer interactions (mortgage refinance, insurance claims, returns, nonprofit fundraising). _Why it matters:_ Sierra is the first vertical agent company to credibly claim broad Fortune-50 production deployment, and the round's pricing reflects investors believing customer-service agents are the most defensible early enterprise-AI category. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/)

- **OpenAI Deployment Company launches with $4B+ and the Tomoro acquisition** — On May 12, OpenAI announced the OpenAI Deployment Company — a committed partnership with 19 global investment / consultancy / SI firms (led by TPG; Advent, Bain Capital, Brookfield as co-leads) backed by $4B+ in capital — and agreed to acquire London-based applied-AI consultancy Tomoro to seed it with ~150 forward-deployed engineers from day one. _Why it matters:_ OpenAI is industrialising forward-deployed engineering as a corporate vehicle (Palantir-style) rather than scaling it inside the lab — a direct response to Anthropic's KPMG/PwC/Infosys partnerships and a structural bet that "deploy frontier AI into Fortune 500" will be a separate, capital-intensive business. [https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/)
