## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic raises $65B at a $965B valuation, eclipsing OpenAI** — Anthropic closed a Series H led by Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks and Sequoia, nearly tripling its $380B February valuation and making it the most valuable AI startup, just under $1T. The round (which folds in $15B of previously committed money, including $5B from Amazon) lands alongside a reported $47B revenue run-rate, up from $30B earlier this year. Widely read as the last private raise before an IPO. _Why it matters:_ The frontier-lab funding race has a new leader, and the revenue figure suggests the valuation isn't purely narrative. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html] (also Bloomberg, TechCrunch, Axios)

**Anthropic ships Claude Opus 4.8 with parallel-subagent "dynamic workflows"** — Released May 28 at the same $5/$25 per-MTok pricing as 4.7, Opus 4.8 hits 88.6% SWE-bench Verified (69.2% SWE-bench Pro), 74.6% Terminal-Bench 2.1, 93.6% GPQA Diamond, and a leading 1890 Elo on GDPval-AA. The headline capability is dynamic workflows in Claude Code — Claude writes orchestration scripts that spin up tens to hundreds of parallel subagents in one session — plus mid-task system messages on the Messages API and an optional 2.5x fast mode. Live day one across the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI and Microsoft Foundry. _Why it matters:_ Orchestration is moving inside the model; the parallel-subagent pattern is a concrete step toward long-horizon autonomous coding. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-8] (TechCrunch, AWS ML blog)

**OpenAI launches Rosalind Biodefense Program and briefs the White House** — OpenAI is offering its GPT-Rosalind life-sciences model to "trusted" U.S. government and allied partners for outbreak early-warning, diagnostics, preparedness and medical-countermeasure work, with sponsored access and launch support. It briefed the White House and several federal agencies; early partners include Lawrence Livermore, Johns Hopkins APL, and vaccine initiative CEPI. _Why it matters:_ A frontier lab steering a dual-use bio model toward government public-health use — and gating access — sets a template for how CBRN-capable models get deployed. [https://openai.com/index/strengthening-societal-resilience-with-rosalind-biodefense/] (Axios)

**OpenAI publishes a Frontier Governance Framework** — A public governance document mapping OpenAI's safety/security practices to emerging legal obligations, specifically California's Transparency in Frontier AI Act and the EU AI Act's GPAI Code of Practice. It operationalizes the Preparedness Framework across cyber-offense, CBRN, harmful manipulation and loss-of-control risk tiers, with model reporting, incident response, and external/third-party evaluation built in. _Why it matters:_ The first frontier lab to explicitly align its internal risk framework with named statutory regimes — a signal of how compliance will be documented as the EU AI Act and California law bite in late 2026. [https://openai.com/index/openai-frontier-governance-framework/]

**Tencent open-sources Hunyuan Hy3 Preview and brings it to market** — Hy3 Preview (295B total / 21B active MoE, 256K context, fused fast/slow thinking) is the first major model from Tencent's rebuilt pre-training and RL stack, tuned for reasoning and agentic workloads. Weights are open on Hugging Face/GitHub; it surfaced today as a newly priced commercial endpoint via GMICloud (~$0.06 in / $0.21 out). Reported 74.4% SWE-bench Verified. _Why it matters:_ Another competitive Chinese open-weight agentic model with aggressive pricing, broadening the open-weight frontier beyond DeepSeek and Qwen. [https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy3-preview] (Tencent newsroom, Hugging Face)

**Amazon scraps internal AI-usage leaderboard after employees gamed it** — Per the FT, Amazon pulled an internal ranking that scored teams on AI usage after staff inflated numbers with meaningless tasks, driving up cloud costs. _Why it matters:_ A cautionary tale on AI-adoption metrics — measuring usage rather than outcomes produces Goodhart's-law behavior at scale. [https://the-decoder.com/] (Financial Times)

**Inherent emerges from stealth with $50M to fuse human science with AI** — London-based Inherent, aiming to combine human scientific research with AI to produce innovations, came out of stealth today with a $50M round led by Index Ventures. _Why it matters:_ Continued investor appetite for "AI-for-science" labs; the European seed-to-Series scale-up signals the category is heating beyond US incumbents. [https://www.sifted.eu/]
