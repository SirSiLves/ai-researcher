## Major announcements & releases

**xAI launches Grok Build coding agent; retires 8 legacy Grok models, redirects to Grok 4.3** — On May 15, xAI rolled out Grok Build, a "powerful new coding agent and CLI for professional software engineering" in early beta (SuperGrok Heavy, $300/mo). Grok Build plans projects, writes/edits files, executes shell commands, and builds applications from natural-language prompts. At 12:00 PM PT the same day, xAI retired grok-4-1-fast-reasoning, grok-4-1-fast-non-reasoning, grok-4-fast-reasoning, grok-4-fast-non-reasoning, grok-4-0709, grok-code-fast-1, grok-3 and grok-imagine-image-pro, auto-redirecting traffic to grok-4.3. _Why it matters:_ Every frontier lab now ships its own opinionated coding agent (Codex / Claude Code / Mistral Vibe / Grok Build) — Grok Build closes xAI's gap with Anthropic and OpenAI on the agentic-coding axis and forces a Grok-4.3-default world for API consumers. [https://x.ai/news] (See also: [https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/may-15-retirement])

**PwC + Anthropic expand alliance — 30,000 US professionals to be trained on Claude; new Claude-native Office of the CFO group** — Announced May 14 and rolling out through May 15, PwC will train 30,000 US staff on Claude (with extension toward its 364,000-person global workforce in 136 countries) and stand up a joint Center of Excellence plus a Claude-native finance business inside its Office of the CFO practice. Claude is already running PwC's internal "ChatPwC" assistant and three AI incubation pods (finance, supply chain, dealmaking); the firm cites measurable wins in insurance underwriting and cyber response times. _Why it matters:_ The Big Four are now Anthropic's distribution channel into Fortune 500 buyers — PwC alone could put Claude in front of more enterprise CFOs than any direct-sales play would in a decade. Strengthens last week's Anthropic+Blackstone+Hellman&Friedman+Goldman JV narrative: Anthropic is industrialising consulting partnerships, not just selling APIs. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/pwc-expanded-partnership] (See also: [https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/anthropic-pwc-expand-alliance-agentic-enterprise.html])

**Cerebras (CBRS) opens at $350 on Nasdaq, closes ~$311 — first-day market cap ~$70B on $5.55B raise** — Cerebras Systems began trading May 14 under ticker CBRS. The stock opened at $350 (well above its $185 IPO price), peaked intraday at $385, and closed at $311.07, putting first-day market cap at roughly $70B — well above the $56B fully-diluted figure on Wednesday's pricing. With 30M shares sold, the deal raised $5.55B, making CBRS the largest US tech IPO so far in 2026. _Why it matters:_ The disaggregated-serving / non-NVIDIA inference thesis just got marked-to-market at ~$70B in live trading — a stronger validation than the priced book alone. Sets the floor for Groq, SambaNova, and any other 2026 inference IPO candidates. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/14/cerebras-cbrs-stock-trade-nasdaq-ipo.html] (See also: [https://www.cerebras.ai/press-release/cerebras-systems-announces-pricing-of-initial-public-offering])

**Cisco raises full-year AI-orders forecast to $9B (from $5B); cuts 4,000 jobs to refund AI / silicon / cybersecurity** — On Cisco's Q3 earnings call May 14, CEO Chuck Robbins reported $5.3B in AI infrastructure orders YTD (with $1.9B from hyperscalers in the most recent quarter alone vs $600M YoY) and lifted full-year AI-order guidance to $9B. Cisco simultaneously announced a restructuring with ~4,000 layoffs (~5% of headcount) and up to $1B in pre-tax charges to reallocate spend into AI, silicon optics, and cybersecurity. Shares surged ~16% pre-market on the print. _Why it matters:_ Cisco is the highest-credibility "incumbent networking → AI infrastructure" pivot — and the $9B figure is a leading indicator on the data-center-fabric layer that NVIDIA's GPUs sit on. Also fits a now-weekly pattern: large enterprise vendors are funding AI capex with workforce reductions. [https://fortune.com/2026/05/15/cisco-ai-orders-forecast-hit-9-billion-stock-surged/] (See also: [https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/cisco-cuts-nearly-4000-jobs-to-spend-more-on-ai-reports-record-quarterly-revenue/])

**LangChain Interrupt 2026 (May 13–14): LangSmith Engine, Deep Agents Deploy beta, Sandboxes GA, LLM Gateway, Context Hub** — At its annual Interrupt agent conference in San Francisco, LangChain shipped a stack of production-agent infrastructure on May 14: LangSmith Engine watches production traces, clusters failures and opens fix PRs; Managed Deep Agents and Sandboxes are GA; SmithDB is up to 15x faster on core LangSmith workloads; LLM Gateway enforces spend limits and redacts PII pre-egress; Context Hub versions agent instructions/policies; Messages View renders multi-turn traces. Deep Agents Deploy (beta) is positioned as "the fastest way to deploy a model-agnostic, open-source agent harness." _Why it matters:_ The open-source agent-harness layer is consolidating around LangChain's stack and around Microsoft Agent Framework + Foundry on the other side — these two will define how most enterprise multi-agent systems get deployed in 2026. Direct competitive pressure on AWS Bedrock Managed Agents and Microsoft Foundry Agent Service. [https://www.langchain.com/blog/interrupt-2026-overview]

**Google previews "Gemini Spark" — always-on AI agent for Gmail, web tasks, and connected apps** — On May 14, the Google app beta (17.23) introduced the "Gemini Spark" branding (previously "Gemini Agent"), described as "your everyday AI agent, ready 24/7 to help with your inbox, online tasks, and more." This sits alongside the May 12 unveils of Magic Pointer (DeepMind's Gemini-powered cursor that understands context: "move this", "merge those", "add that") and Googlebook (Android-based laptop platform built around Gemini Intelligence). _Why it matters:_ Three months before Apple's next AI push, Google is layering Spark (proactive task agent) + Magic Pointer (interaction primitive) + Googlebook (hardware target) into a coordinated stack. The consumer-AI battleground is no longer the chatbot — it's the OS-level agent fabric. [https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/meet-googlebook/] (See also: [https://9to5google.com/2026/05/14/gemini-spark-insight/], [https://deepmind.google/blog/ai-pointer/])

**IBM + Red Hat: managed Red Hat AI Inference launches on IBM Cloud; OpenShift Virtualization Service follows** — Announced May 12 alongside Think 2026 and the Red Hat Summit, Red Hat AI Inference on IBM Cloud is a managed inference service powered by vLLM and Red Hat AI's engine, with built-in governance for production hybrid-cloud AI. The launch catalog ships Granite 4.0 H Small, Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct, Llama 3.3 70B Instruct, GPT-OSS-120B, and Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-FP8, with additional models from May 2026 onwards. GA is May 22, 2026. Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization Service on IBM Cloud is in limited availability with GA in June. _Why it matters:_ Concrete instantiation of the "watsonx Orchestrate as agentic control plane" thesis from Think 2026 — IBM is shipping the managed inference plumbing under that vision, with a model catalog that's deliberately open-weight-heavy. Direct enterprise alternative to Bedrock and Azure AI Foundry. [https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-12-ibm-announces-red-hat-ai-inference-and-red-hat-openShift-virtualization-service-on-ibm-cloud]

**Snowflake + Dataiku launch "Cobuild on Snowflake" — natural-language AI workflow builder inside the data cloud** — Announced May 15, Cobuild on Snowflake combines Snowflake Cortex AI services with Dataiku's orchestration platform to let business and technical users create AI workflows and agents via natural-language prompts, governed and deployed entirely inside Snowflake. Same week, Snowflake also moved Cortex AI Guardrails to GA (run-time protection against prompt injection / jailbreaks on Cortex Code) and put Cortex AI_COMPLETE multimodal video/audio analysis into public preview. _Why it matters:_ Data-cloud vendors are claiming the "agent control plane lives where your data lives" position against ServiceNow's workflow-side claim and SAP's business-context claim. Cortex Guardrails GA also makes Snowflake the first major data cloud to ship a production prompt-injection mitigation as part of the core service, not as an add-on. [https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/15/755535/]

**Anthropic + SAP: Claude becomes a primary reasoning and agentic engine on the SAP Business AI Platform** — Announced May 14 at SAP Sapphire's closing day, Anthropic and SAP expanded their partnership so that Claude is embedded as a primary reasoning and agentic capability across SAP's AI-enabled solution portfolio (Joule, Joule Studio 2.0, and the 200+ specialized agents in the Autonomous Suite). SAP's Joule agents now span 50+ domain-specific Joule Assistants across finance, supply chain, procurement, HCM, and CX. _Why it matters:_ SAP picking Anthropic as a "primary" reasoning model alongside its existing multi-vendor pattern (OpenAI / Mistral / Google) is the most concrete enterprise win Anthropic has logged outside financial services — and frames Claude as the default for high-stakes structured business reasoning. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/]

**Anthropic Claude for Small Business: 10-city tour kicks off in Chicago May 14, week 2 rolls through May 15** — Anthropic's Claude for Small Business — 15 prebuilt agentic workflows + 15 reusable skills, with native connectors to QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack — entered its first roadshow city May 14 (Chicago), with the Workday Foundation Solopreneurship Accelerator (joint with LISC) running in parallel, granting $150K seed + Claude credits + LISC AI-first curriculum to an initial cohort of 15 founders. _Why it matters:_ Together with the PwC expansion and the Blackstone/Hellman&Friedman JV announced last week, Anthropic is running three enterprise/SMB distribution motions concurrently — services partner, JV operator, and direct SMB. Reports this week show Anthropic now exceeds OpenAI in paid US business subscriptions for the first time. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/finance-agents]
