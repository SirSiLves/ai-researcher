## Major announcements & releases

**Fed + OCC pause US bank cyber exams after Anthropic Mythos shock** — On May 19 (covered widely on May 20), Bloomberg broke that the Federal Reserve and Office of the Comptroller of the Currency are pausing some cyber-related examinations of the largest US banks to give them time to harden systems against threats exposed by Anthropic's **Mythos** model. Anthropic's "Project Glasswing" — limited-access tier already shared with JPMorgan Chase, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft — is the only sanctioned channel; the White House has separately blocked Anthropic's plan to extend access to ~70 more organizations. Mythos has flagged thousands of high-severity vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser, including a 17-year-old FreeBSD RCE (CVE-2026-4747). _Why it matters:_ this is the first time US bank supervisors have rewritten an active exam program in direct response to a single frontier model — and it operationalizes the BSI's May-19 warning on the European side. Mythos is now a regulatory primitive, not just a research artifact. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-19/anthropic-mythos-ai-model-prompts-regulators-to-delay-us-bank-cyberattack-tests) ([Claims Journal — Wall Street watchdogs pause cyber exams](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/05/20/337701.htm), [GovTech](https://www.govtech.com/artificial-intelligence/u-s-regulators-pause-cybersecurity-bank-exams-due-to-mythos), [Cyber Daily](https://www.cyberdaily.au/security/13627-us-banking-regulators-pause-cyber-exams-to-allow-mythos-patching))
