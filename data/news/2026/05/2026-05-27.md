- **German cabinet passes "active cyber-defence" bill — BSI, BKA, Bundespolizei get powers to shut down IT systems, redirect traffic, modify data, conduct counter-attacks; 37 new staff** — On May 27 the German federal cabinet adopted a bill granting the Federal Office for Information Security (BSI), the Federal Criminal Police Office (BKA), and the Federal Police (Bundespolizei) substantially broader cyber-defence powers. Capabilities include prohibiting the operation of threatening IT systems, redirecting data traffic, and reading / deleting / modifying data. The government plans to permit "active cyber defence" beyond preventive measures — counter-attacks may be authorised without the knowledge of the targets. The package funds 37 new staff. Bitkom and other industry groups warned that attack-attribution is unreliable and innocent third parties may be impacted by counter-attacks. _Why it matters:_ this is the most aggressive Western-democracy "hack-back" mandate to date, and it lands in the same week that Anthropic's Project Glasswing has reported 10,000+ critical/high vulnerabilities found by Claude Mythos. The combination — vulnerability discovery at AI speed plus state authorisation for active counter-action — significantly raises the stakes for every European AI-security vendor (Lakera, Apollo Research, Cohere government-tier) and for the Five Eyes agentic-AI guidance work. Expect referenced in the EU AI Act high-risk Article 6 consultation (deadline June 23) under "critical infrastructure" and "law enforcement."
