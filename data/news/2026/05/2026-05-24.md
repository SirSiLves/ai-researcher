## Major announcements & releases

- **Anthropic on the brink of closing $30B+ round at $900B+ valuation, set to overtake OpenAI as the most valuable AI lab** — Bloomberg, then Yahoo Finance / Business Standard, reported on Friday May 22 that Anthropic's Series-G-class financing — co-led by Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter and Greenoaks at roughly $2B each, with Founders Fund and General Catalyst following on — is expected to close "as soon as next week." Anthropic told investors it expects $10.9B in Q2 revenue (more than doubling sequentially) and an annualised run-rate above $50B by the end of June. _Why it matters:_ if this prices, Anthropic moves ahead of OpenAI as the highest-valued private AI company — the first time the frontier-lab pecking order flips on capital, not on a model release. The numbers also normalise "$50B run-rate" as the new floor for a frontier-lab investment thesis. [https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week) (also covered by [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/world-news/anthropic-set-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week-126052300112_1.html))

- **OpenAI confidentially files for IPO, targeting September listing at $852B–$1T** — Multiple sources confirmed on May 22 that OpenAI submitted its draft S-1 to the SEC with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading. Current private-market mark is ~$852B; bankers are sounding out a Q3/Q4 2026 listing. CNBC first surfaced the filing prep on May 20; Fortune and others confirmed the filing on May 22. _Why it matters:_ paired with the Anthropic round, the two largest frontier labs are now racing to be public/private champions in the same quarter — the first time enterprise buyers have a near-term liquidity benchmark for "frontier-lab" cap-table risk. Expect this to harden vendor due-diligence for SAP/ServiceNow/Salesforce-style deals that depend on either lab. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/openai-ipo-filing.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/openai-ipo-filing.html) and [Fortune coverage](https://fortune.com/2026/05/22/openai-ipo-filing-1-trillion-may-finally-answer-these-big-questions/)

- **Anthropic ships Claude Security to public beta, opens Cyber Verification Program, expands Claude Code/Opus limits** — On May 22 Anthropic put Claude Security (formerly Claude Code Security, built on Opus 4.7) into public beta for Enterprise customers — codebase scanning, vulnerability triage, fix generation — and opened the Cyber Verification Program so vetted defenders can use Opus 4.7 without dual-use restrictions. MIND was announced May 20 as the first data-security company admitted to the program. The same week Anthropic published Claude for Small Business (QuickBooks / PayPal / HubSpot / Canva / Docusign / Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 connectors with payroll/invoicing/close workflows) and raised Claude Code and Opus API limits for Managed Agents. _Why it matters:_ this is Anthropic operationalising its "AI for defenders" pitch — the first frontier-lab security product where the model itself is the scanner, plus a verification scheme that gives regulated security teams a sanctioned path past the dual-use guardrails. The Small Business package is a parallel bet that the next million Claude seats come from SMB connectors, not Fortune 500 rollouts. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-code-security](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-code-security) (also [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/anthropic-claude-security-for-ai/))

- **Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic to lead a Claude-accelerated pre-training team** — OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI lead Andrej Karpathy announced May 19 he's joining Anthropic, reporting to pre-training lead Nick Joseph and standing up a team focused on using Claude itself to accelerate pre-training research. _Why it matters:_ pre-training is the part of the stack everyone was supposed to be moving past — Karpathy taking a pre-training role at the lab now best positioned by revenue and capital is a structural counter-signal to "post-training is all you need," and reads alongside Anthropic's $50B run-rate guidance as a deliberate frontier-capability investment. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/)

- **Anthropic acquires SDK platform Stainless for ~$300M, winds down hosted SDK tools used by OpenAI and Google** — Announced May 18: Anthropic is buying NYC-based Stainless (founded 2022, prior $150M Series A in Dec 2024) at roughly double its last private mark. Stainless's AI-powered compiler turns OpenAPI specs into TypeScript/Python/Go/Java/Kotlin SDKs and powers every official Anthropic SDK — and has also been generating SDKs for OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare. Anthropic will wind down the hosted Stainless product; existing customers keep what they've already generated. _Why it matters:_ this is the first time a frontier lab has taken a core piece of competitor developer infrastructure out of the market by acquisition. Combined with Stainless's deep involvement in MCP server tooling, it pulls a layer of agent-ecosystem plumbing inside Anthropic's tent at exactly the moment MCP 2026-07-28 is locking. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless) (also [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/anthropic-has-acquired-the-dev-tools-startup-used-by-openai-google-and-cloudflare/))

- **OpenAI ships GPT-5.5 + GPT-5.2-Codex, partners with Dell to bring Codex on-prem, and disproves an 80-year Erdős conjecture** — OpenAI had a heavy week: GPT-5.5 + GPT-5.5 Pro rolled out to Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise via ChatGPT and Codex; GPT-5.2-Codex landed as OpenAI's "most advanced agentic coding model"; on May 18 OpenAI and Dell announced a Codex / Dell AI Data Platform partnership that lets enterprises run Codex against on-prem data and systems-of-record; and on May 20 OpenAI published a result in which a general-purpose reasoning model autonomously disproved Erdős's 1946 unit-distance conjecture, with Fields medalist Tim Gowers calling it "a milestone in AI mathematics" and Princeton's Will Sawin refining the exponent. _Why it matters:_ the Dell deal is the first major frontier-lab move into hybrid/on-prem deployment (closing the SAP-and-friends gap), and the Erdős result is the first time a frontier model has autonomously settled an open problem central to a math subfield — a concrete reasoning-capability data point heading into the IPO window. [https://openai.com/index/dell-codex-enterprise-partnership/](https://openai.com/index/dell-codex-enterprise-partnership/) and [https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/)

- **Red Hat AI Inference on IBM Cloud goes GA on May 22; OpenShift Virtualization Service rolls out alongside** — IBM and Red Hat announced general availability on May 22 of Red Hat AI Inference as a managed service on IBM Cloud — vLLM-powered, OpenAI-compatible APIs, IBM Cloud IAM / audit / privacy controls, SLA-backed, with a launch model catalog covering Granite 4.0 H Small, Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct, Llama 3.3 70B Instruct, GPT-OSS-120B, and Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-FP8 (more open and custom models from May). OpenShift Virtualization Service GA'd in parallel. _Why it matters:_ this is the first major hyperscale-grade managed inference service that ships with both EU-style governance hooks (audit, IAM, privacy) and a multi-vendor open-weight catalog — the "sovereign-friendly inference" pitch SAP, governments, and regulated banks have been waiting for. [https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-12-ibm-announces-red-hat-ai-inference-and-red-hat-openShift-virtualization-service-on-ibm-cloud](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-12-ibm-announces-red-hat-ai-inference-and-red-hat-openShift-virtualization-service-on-ibm-cloud) (also [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/2026/05/12/red-hat-learns-new-ai-tricks/))

- **Cohere ships Command A+ under full Apache 2.0 — 218B sparse MoE / 25B active, runs on 2 H100s, native citations** — May 20 release: Cohere put Command A+ out under a clean Apache 2.0 license (no revenue caps, no non-compete), with 48-language coverage, multimodal reasoning, and explicit "grounding spans" tags that link every factual claim to a source document or row. Designed to fit on 2x H100 or 1x B200; API at $2.50 / $10.00 per M tokens. _Why it matters:_ this is the first time a frontier-class enterprise-positioned model has shipped without the usual "open weights but no commercial use" tax — and the native-citation architecture is a direct play for regulated/agentic-RAG workloads where every claim needs to be auditable. Sovereign-AI buyers (UK, Canada, Gulf, parts of EU public sector) just got a credible Apache-licensed option that the EU AI Act high-risk regime can sign off on more easily than US-hosted alternatives. [https://venturebeat.com/technology/cohere-cracks-lossless-quantization-and-native-citations-with-first-full-apache-2-0-licensed-open-model-command-a](https://venturebeat.com/technology/cohere-cracks-lossless-quantization-and-native-citations-with-first-full-apache-2-0-licensed-open-model-command-a) (also covered by [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/21/cohere-releases-command-a-a-218b-sparse-moe-model-for-agentic-workflows-that-runs-on-as-few-as-two-h100-gpus/))

- **Mistral acquires Vienna's Emmi AI, opens Linz office, pushes industrial-engineering AI stack** — Announced May 19 on mistral.ai: Mistral has signed to acquire Emmi AI, the Austrian physics-AI spin-out of NXAI that builds CFD, heat-transfer, and material-stress models for aerospace/automotive/semiconductor workflows. Emmi's 30+ researchers and engineers join Mistral's Science and Applied AI teams; Linz becomes an official Mistral office alongside Paris, London, Amsterdam, Munich, San Francisco, and Singapore. This is Mistral's second acquisition after Koyeb in February. _Why it matters:_ Mistral is the first frontier-class lab to make a serious bet that vertical engineering AI (simulation, CAE, CAD-adjacent) is a wedge — relevant for the SAP/Siemens-style industrial buyers SAP Sapphire was courting, and a structural signal that "industrial" is the next domain after coding to be served by specialised models. [https://mistral.ai/news/accelerate-ai-native-industry](https://mistral.ai/news/accelerate-ai-native-industry) (also [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/05/19/mistral-acquires-austria-s-emmi-ai/) and [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/mistral-strikes-second-ma-deal-in-months-with-austrian-ai-startup-emmi))

- **Microsoft Edge opens limited preview of "Browsing with Copilot" for Edge for Business** — Announced May 20–22: agentic browsing comes to the enterprise edition of Edge — Copilot can navigate IT-allow-listed sites, fill forms, and complete multi-step tasks under a dedicated admin policy, available to organisations with Microsoft 365 Copilot licensing (EEA excluded). _Why it matters:_ it's the first IT-governed agentic browser from a major OS vendor — admins control the allow-list rather than the user, which is what unblocks deployments inside banks, hospitals, and the Swiss public sector. Combined with ServiceNow Knowledge 2026's AI Control Tower, this is the year IT functions stop pretending agents are "just another SaaS" and start treating them as a separately governed surface. [Microsoft On the Issues / Edge for Business AI](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/05/advancing-ai-evaluation-with-the-center-for-ai-standards-us-and-innovation-and-the-ai-security-institute-uk/) (general-coverage [Windows Forum write-up](https://windowsforum.com/threads/edge-for-business-ai-governed-agentic-browsing-copilot-new-tab-multi-tab-reasoning.419077/))

- **AISI (UK) publishes its first Frontier AI Trends Report — flags that oversight will erode as models advance** — The UK AI Security Institute released its inaugural Frontier AI Trends Report this week, with five key findings centred on declining controllability and on the increasing difficulty of independent evaluation. AISI also published new research notes on Model Transparency (May 21) and Alignment (May 14). Microsoft separately announced expanded test-and-evaluation agreements with both AISI and the new US Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) on May 5. _Why it matters:_ this is the first time a government safety institute has used a formal trend report — not a one-off advisory — to argue that oversight effectiveness is degrading. Expect it to be cited heavily in the EU AI Act high-risk consultation (open until June 23) and in the next round of GPAI Code of Practice revisions. [https://www.aisi.gov.uk/frontier-ai-trends-report](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/frontier-ai-trends-report) (also [Microsoft On the Issues — CAISI / AISI agreements](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/05/advancing-ai-evaluation-with-the-center-for-ai-standards-us-and-innovation-and-the-ai-security-institute-uk/))

- **EU AI Act: Commission's high-risk classification draft guidelines out for consultation through June 23** — Following the May 7 political agreement on the AI Act Omnibus (Annex III HRAIS deferred to 2 Dec 2027; Annex I to 2 Aug 2028; two new prohibited practices added), the Commission published draft Article 6 guidelines on high-risk AI classification on May 19, with feedback open through June 23. A first draft of the Code of Practice on marking AI-generated content was also published in the same window, with the final code targeted for June 2026. _Why it matters:_ enterprises got their timeline relief on May 7, but the classification criteria — and the deepfake/AI-content marking code — are the next live battlegrounds. This is the consultation window where SAP, Microsoft, Mistral, and the AI Pact signatories will lobby; ignore at your peril if you operate any HR/credit/medical/critical-infrastructure AI in Europe. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) (also [Latham & Watkins analysis](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ai-act-update-eu-resolves-to-change-rules-and-extend-deadlines))

- **CHUV begins clinical pilot of Swiss LLM "Meditron" in the Lausanne emergency department** — swissinfo reported this week that CHUV (Centre hospitalier universitaire vaudois) is starting a live clinical pilot of Meditron, the Swiss medical LLM developed in collaboration with EPFL, initially supporting clinicians in the emergency department. More than 300 medical professionals tested Meditron on hypothetical cases during 2025. _Why it matters:_ this is the first publicly disclosed Swiss-built medical LLM moving from sandbox into front-line clinical decision support — a concrete data point for the sovereign-AI-in-healthcare argument, and a model the rest of Europe will study for the EU AI Act's high-risk medical workflow.
