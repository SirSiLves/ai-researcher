## Major announcements & releases

- **Pope Leo XIV publishes first encyclical "Magnifica Humanitas" — frames human dignity in the age of AI, shares the stage with Anthropic** — Released May 25, the Pope's first encyclical (245 paragraphs, five chapters, signed May 15 — the 135th anniversary of Leo XIII's *Rerum Novarum*) extends Catholic social teaching to artificial intelligence: human dignity is "threatened by new forms of dehumanization," society and AI developers must implement "shared standards of social justice," and AI must serve the common good. The Vatican explicitly chose Anthropic (not Google, not OpenAI) as the AI-lab partner co-presenting at the release, with co-founder Chris Olah on the encyclical's communication panel. _Why it matters:_ a 1.4-billion-member moral institution has just issued a structured doctrinal position on AI — text that EU regulators, ethics committees, and frontier-lab governance teams will cite for years. By picking Anthropic as the on-stage partner, the Vatican is also signalling which lab it considers a credible safety counterpart at the exact moment Anthropic closes its $30B round and overtakes OpenAI on valuation.
