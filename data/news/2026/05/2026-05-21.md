**Pinecone Nexus — context compiler + KnowQL ship, "the RAG era is ending"** — Pinecone publicly launched **Nexus**, repositioned as a knowledge engine rather than a vector retriever — a context compiler that pre-builds task-specific, persistent knowledge artifacts from enterprise data, plus a composable retriever with field-level citations and deterministic conflict resolution. The implicit thesis: better models won't fix agent reliability; better knowledge representation will.
