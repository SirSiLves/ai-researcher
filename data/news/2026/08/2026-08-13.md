## Major announcements & releases

**xAI/SpaceXAI ships Grok 4.6 — frontier parity at roughly a third of the price**
Grok 4.6 landed on 12 August, about five weeks after Grok 4.5, and went live the same afternoon in Cursor, Grok Build, the API and partners including OpenRouter, Vercel and Cloudflare. It scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — level with GPT-5.6 Sol, ahead of Kimi K3, two points behind Claude Opus 5 — with a GDPval-AA v2 Elo of 1753, 69.9% on CursorBench v3.2 and 15.8% on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark. Pricing holds flat at $2/$6 per 1M input/output tokens (vs $5/$30 for GPT-5.6 Sol), but crossing a 200K-token prompt re-bills the whole request at $4/$12; context window stays at 500K. xAI positions it explicitly for long-running agents and ambitious interactive/visual work — establishing an application's structure and visual language in one pass — rather than raw benchmark leadership.
_Why it matters:_ price-to-intelligence, not capability, is now the axis frontier labs compete on for agentic workloads — and a 60%-cheaper model at parity puts direct margin pressure on OpenAI and Anthropic API pricing. [link](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6) (context: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/spacexai-debuts-grok-4-6-overtaking-kimi-k3s-performance-and-matching-gpt-5-6-sol-for-worlds-third-best-on-artificial-analysis), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/blog/grok-4-6))

**DeepSeek takes V4 Pro out of preview — build 0813 is now GA**
The V4 Pro 0813 build appeared on 12 August as the general-availability release, ending the preview period for DeepSeek's flagship. It is published at $0.435 in / $0.87 out per million tokens with a one-million-token context window. This follows V4-Flash going official on 31 July, where DeepSeek published agent-benchmark results showing the re-post-trained Flash build outscoring the V4-Pro-Preview on its own internal coding-agent suites.
_Why it matters:_ a million-token context at sub-$1/M output resets the floor for long-context agent workloads, and lands the same week DeepSeek was signalling API price *increases* — worth watching whether GA pricing holds. **Anthropic in talks to acquire Decart AI for about $6 billion**
Reports emerging late on 12 August have Anthropic negotiating to buy the Israeli startup Decart AI, which builds real-time generative video, world models for simulated environments, and GPU-optimisation technology. Talks are early-stage and could still collapse. If completed, Decart's team would fold into Anthropic's inference organisation; the startup had previously raised $300M with NVIDIA among its investors. It would be one of Anthropic's largest acquisitions to date.
_Why it matters:_ the target here is inference efficiency, not model capability — a signal that compute cost per token, ahead of a possible IPO, is now the binding constraint at frontier labs. **Google opens Gemini to a new slate of third-party connected apps**
Announced 12 August at Made by Google: Gemini is rolling out connectors across productivity and creativity (Granola, Otter.ai, Wix), local and entertainment (Fever, GetYourGuide, Localiza, OpenTable UK, Ticketmaster), music (iHeartRadio, Pandora) and home/health/lifestyle (Angi, Thumbtack, Zocdoc). Rollout is staged over the coming weeks and is opt-in per service.
_Why it matters:_ Google is building a first-party connector directory rather than leaning on an open tool-calling standard — the counterweight to Anthropic's ~950-server MCP connector directory, and a fork in how agent-to-service integration consolidates. [link](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/new-connected-apps-services-gemini-august-2026/)

**Cognition in talks at $40B+ — under three months after its $26B round**
Bloomberg reports Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent, is already in early investor discussions at a valuation of at least $40 billion, a >50% step-up from the $1B raise at $26B in May. The company's annualised revenue run rate is reported near $1 billion, roughly double the $492M it confirmed at the May raise, with enterprise Devin usage growing ~50% month-over-month for six months. Named customers include Mercedes-Benz, NASA and Goldman Sachs.
_Why it matters:_ coding agents are the first genuinely measurable enterprise AI category — tasks closed, bugs fixed, migrations done — and that measurability is what's pulling valuations this far ahead of everything else in the stack. [link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/ai-coding-startup-cognition-reportedly-already-in-talks-to-raise-at-40b-valuation/)

The Swedish vibe-coding platform closed $400 million led by Menlo Ventures and the Scaleup Europe Fund, with more than a dozen additional investors participating, at a confirmed $13.3 billion post-money. Announced 12 August.
_Why it matters:_ the second European AI application company this week to price above $10B — alongside Legora — evidence that the app layer, not the model layer, is where European capital is now landing. [link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/lovable-confirms-new-13-3b-valuation-raises-another-400m/)

**Salesforce puts Agentforce agents inside the U.S. Army at Impact Level 5**
On 5 August, U.S. Army Human Resources Command became the first Department of War organisation to deploy autonomous AI agents at IL5 — the highest sensitivity tier below classified — serving 9.2 million soldiers, veterans and military families. Salesforce also disclosed Agentforce crossing $800 million in ARR, up 169% year-over-year, across 29,000 enterprise deals closed in under 18 months.
_Why it matters:_ IL5 accreditation is the hardest compliance bar in commercial agent deployment; clearing it turns "can agents be trusted with regulated data" from an open question into a procurement checkbox. [link](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/what-is-new/)

**Snowflake's Cortex AI Gateway makes MCP an enterprise-governed protocol**
Launched at Black Hat 2026, Cortex AI Gateway is a centralised control layer that governs how agents — first-party (CoCo, CoWork) and third-party alike (Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT, Amazon Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry, custom LangChain/LlamaIndex apps) — reach models, data, MCP servers and enterprise tools. It builds on Snowflake's May acquisition of Natoma and enforces identity, policy and audit at the tool-call level across 100+ MCP servers, with shadow-MCP discovery, per-agent cost budgets and intelligent model routing. Agent Identity and Native AI Security Posture Management both reached GA alongside; partner integrations cover 1Password, Aembit, Cyera, Linx Security, Okta, SailPoint and Saviynt.
_Why it matters:_ the unmanaged-MCP-sprawl problem now has a named commercial answer — and it's the data platform, not the model vendor, claiming the control plane. [link](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/enterprise-ai-security-agentic-mcp-governance/)

**Hassabis floats an independent industry body to set AI safety standards**
Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis has raised the idea of an independent organisation to codify common safety practices across frontier labs, discussing it with executives at rival labs and with U.S. officials including Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and White House technology adviser Michael Kratsios. He has reportedly drawn a loose analogy to the IAEA, while acknowledging that overseeing software built by competing private firms is a very different problem. Reported by the Wall Street Journal on 13 August.
_Why it matters:_ this is the first serious proposal for a layer between voluntary company policy and statutory regulation — and the open question, whether labs would accept oversight that can slow a launch, is the one that decides if it's real. [link](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/13/top-tech-news-today-august-13-2026-anthropic-deepmind-google-lenovo-microsoft-spacexai-more/)

**EU AI Act GPAI enforcement is live — the AI Office can now fine**
Since 2 August the European Commission, through the AI Office, has been formally entitled to exercise investigation and enforcement powers over general-purpose AI model providers: requesting documentation, conducting evaluations, demanding compliance and market-restriction measures, and imposing fines of up to 3% of global annual turnover or €15 million, whichever is higher. Substantive GPAI obligations have applied since August 2025, but the enforcement machinery only switched on now; models placed on the market before that date generally have until August 2027. The AI Office's stated opening move is "technical compliance dialogues" rather than formal proceedings.
_Why it matters:_ the grace period is over — GPAI documentation, training-data summaries and copyright policies are now legally testable, and the first dialogues will set the de facto compliance bar. [link](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/enforcement-of-chapter-v-under-the-eu-ai-act/)

**Vantage Data Centers weighs an IPO at roughly $100 billion**
The Silver Lake- and DigitalBridge-backed hyperscale operator has held preliminary adviser discussions about a listing that could raise around $10 billion at a ~$100B valuation, with a sale also among the options; any deal is early-stage and could come as soon as next year. Vantage has raised roughly $11 billion since late 2023 and is involved in a Wisconsin campus tied to the OpenAI/Oracle Stargate buildout.
_Why it matters:_ a $100B pure-play data-centre listing would reprice the whole category from real estate to critical AI infrastructure, and give the public market its first clean read on AI capex demand. [link](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/13/top-tech-news-today-august-13-2026-anthropic-deepmind-google-lenovo-microsoft-spacexai-more/)

**India lands its largest single-cluster AI build: L&T wins a ~$1.57B Together AI order**
Larsen & Toubro has secured an AI infrastructure contract worth roughly ₹10,000–15,000 crore (up to ~$1.57 billion) to deploy around 10,000 NVIDIA B300 GPUs at an L&T data centre campus in Chennai for U.S.-based Together AI. It is being positioned as India's largest single-cluster AI computing installation.
_Why it matters:_ frontier-scale training capacity moving outside the U.S./China axis changes who can serve which markets under data-residency rules — and B300 volumes at this size are a real demand datapoint for NVIDIA's current generation. [link](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/13/top-tech-news-today-august-13-2026-anthropic-deepmind-google-lenovo-microsoft-spacexai-more/)

**German audit firms push AI agents into statutory accounts — with criminal liability attached** (original: _"Wirtschaftsprüfer: KI-Agenten erobern die Bilanz – bei Fehlern droht Haft"_)
Handelsblatt reports the large German audit firms are moving beyond AI-assisted sampling to agentic review of balance sheets and year-end accounts. The escalation is that statutory audit sign-off carries personal criminal exposure for the responsible partner — so an agent's error is not a software defect, it is a liability event with a named human attached. Firms are, in parallel, working out how far a partner can delegate judgement to an agent and still sign.
_Why it matters:_ this is the sharpest real-world test yet of the human-oversight requirement the EU AI Act now enforces — a regulated profession discovering that "agent in the loop" and "auditor personally liable" have to be reconciled contractually, not just technically. [link](https://www.handelsblatt.com/unternehmen/dienstleister/wirtschaftspruefer-ki-agenten-erobern-die-bilanz-bei-fehlern-droht-haft/100227890.html)
