**Meta opens Muse Spark private API preview — no waitlist, hand-picked partners only** — Reporting on 11 April confirmed Meta's Muse Spark moved into a private API preview to select partners only — no public waitlist or signup. Zero third-party API providers were benchmarking Muse Spark as of mid-April; no endpoint documentation, pricing page or rate limits disclosed. _Why it matters:_ Muse Spark's distribution choice cements the closed-weights pivot — Meta is going for a Google AI Studio-style first-party UX rather than a Llama-style developer free-for-all. Affects the "next $X B in AI distribution lands where?" question for the rest of the quarter. **MiniMax M2.7 weights ship on Hugging Face — sparse 230 B MoE, 10 B active, 200 K context** — MiniMax put the M2.7 weights on Hugging Face on 11 April: 230 B sparse MoE, 10 B active parameters, 256 experts, 62 layers, 200 K context window. License is a "modified MIT" — non-commercial use broadly permitted but commercial use requires written authorisation from MiniMax. Pricing on MiniMax API at $0.30/M input tokens, $0.60/M output. The model targets self-evolving agent benchmarks (self-play training loop) and reportedly scores competitive with models 3× larger on agentic tasks. _Why it matters:_ The most-watched Chinese open-weight launch of April; the modified-MIT licence is the new flashpoint in the "open weights vs commercial guard-rails" debate. Lands one day before MMX-CLI on 12 April.
