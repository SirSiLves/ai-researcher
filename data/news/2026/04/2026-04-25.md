## Major announcements & releases

**European financial regulators warn AI is accelerating cyberattacks — systemic-risk framing** — Weekend coverage out of European regulatory beats: European financial regulators warned on 25 April that AI is accelerating the speed and sophistication of cyberattacks, increasing systemic risk across markets. The warning emerged via post-week briefings amplifying the Mythos disclosure and the GPT-5.5 cyber-capability uplift. Specific concerns: automated, low-cost attack campaigns; difficulty detecting AI-generated payloads; phishing / social-engineering at scale. _Why it matters:_ This is the most direct regulator response to the Mythos / GPT-5.5 cyber-capability narrative — and it lands ahead of the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) follow-on and the GP-AI Code of Practice motion. The framing positions AI cyber as a financial-stability-risk topic, not just a tech-policy topic. **DeepSeek V4 — first 24-hour reactions and community benchmark wave** — Weekend coverage on DeepSeek's V4 Preview (released 24 April) crystallised on Saturday: V4 Flash and V4 Pro now positioned as the leading open-source-weights system for reasoning + agentic tasks, with the Huawei Ascend 950 / Supernode training infrastructure detail prompting fresh discussion of US export-control gaps. LLM-Stats published its V4 benchmark composite with V4 Pro ranking near top of open-weights coding benchmarks; community deployments (Hugging Face, Together AI) appeared within hours. _Why it matters:_ The first-weekend community reaction will set the V4 narrative for the next month. The Huawei-on-Chinese-silicon validation is the more strategically consequential half of the story — the West-vs-China model-capability gap is now sub-3 months for the open-weights segment. **Anthropic's $45B+ Anthropic capital + 8.5GW compute week — analyst recaps** — Saturday's analyst beat consolidated the week's Anthropic mega-deals: ~$5B AWS + up to $20B contingent (20 April), 3.5GW Google + Broadcom (21 April), $10B + up to $30B Google (24 April). Total: ~$45B+ committed capital, ~8.5GW multi-cloud compute. Combined with $30B raised in February at $380B, Anthropic's mid-2026 capital runway exceeds any frontier lab. _Why it matters:_ Weekend analyst recaps are where the cross-source synthesis lands. **Cohere acquires Aleph Alpha — $20B transatlantic "sovereign AI" play, Schwarz Group commits $600M** — TechCrunch's Saturday long-form (formal announce was Friday 24 April) details Cohere's takeover of Heidelberg-based Aleph Alpha at a combined ~$20B valuation. Schwarz Group (Lidl/Kaufland parent, Aleph Alpha's earlier Series B backer) commits **$600M** in Cohere's upcoming Series E. Combined company runs under Cohere brand with dual HQ in Toronto + Germany; explicitly framed as "sovereign AI" alternative for regulated sectors (public sector, finance, defense, energy, manufacturing, telecoms, healthcare). Both governments endorsed; subject to regulatory close. _Why it matters:_ The most consequential European AI deal of the year — combines two of the strongest non-US-non-China players into a single "sovereign-AI for regulated industries" bet. Sets the GTM template Mistral and Mistral 3 are watching, and gives the European Commission a domestic champion ahead of the GP-AI Code of Practice motion.
