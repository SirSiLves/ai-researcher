## Major announcements & releases

**Meta debuts Muse Spark — first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, closed-source pivot** — Meta announced **Muse Spark** on 8 April, the first model in a new series from Meta Superintelligence Labs and the first major LLM released since CAIO Alexandr Wang joined nine months ago (after Meta's $14.3 B investment in Scale AI for 49% stake). Codenamed "Avocado" internally, Muse Spark powers a smarter, faster Meta AI assistant on the web and rolls into WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook / Messenger / Ray-Ban Meta glasses over the following weeks. Tallied 52 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.0 — 4th place behind Gemini 3.1 Pro (57), GPT-5.4 (57), Claude Opus 4.6 (53). Meta is keeping the weights closed, a pivot from the Llama open-weight strategy, with the company saying it "hopes to open-source future versions." A private API preview is opening to select partners. CapEx for 2026 guided to $115–135 B (~2× 2025). _Why it matters:_ This is the first concrete output from the Wang-led superintelligence push and the most consequential strategic pivot Meta has made on AI distribution since Llama. The shift from open-weight to closed-weight signals the company believes (a) it has finally caught the frontier and (b) the moat lives in the harness, not the weights. **Muse Spark closed-weights pivot draws sharp open-source pushback** — Coverage on 8 April from artificial intelligence-news.com and Simon Willison framed Meta's closed-weight choice as the most material open-source AI setback of 2026. Llama 4 was the open-source baseline for ~40% of Hugging Face download volume going into Q1; Muse Spark sitting behind a private API instead of public weights pulls Meta out of that role. _Why it matters:_ Combined with DeepSeek V4 lining up to ship 24 April under the same Apache-modified-vs-Apache-2.0 tension, this is the day the open-weight thesis loses its biggest U.S. anchor. The Chinese-frontier-lab share of open-weight downloads (already 41% per HF's Spring 2026 report) is structurally set to climb. [Artificial Intelligence News — did Meta sacrifice open-source identity](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/meta-muse-spark-ai-model-open-source/) · [Simon Willison — Meta's new model is Muse Spark](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/8/muse-spark/) · [The Next Web — Muse Spark closed source](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-muse-spark-msl-first-model)

Bloomberg's pickup focused on the electric-grid and safety-net proposals; TechPolicy.Press piece "OpenAI's Industrial Policy is a policymercial" went viral in policy Twitter. _Why it matters:_ Sets the stage for the Treasury/Fed banks briefing on 10 April — the Industrial Policy paper plus Mythos coverage become joined-up reading material for officials briefing financial-services CEOs.
