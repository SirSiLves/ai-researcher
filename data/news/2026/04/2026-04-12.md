## Major announcements & releases

**MiniMax releases MMX-CLI — terminal-native multimodal AI for human developers and AI agents** — MiniMax released MMX-CLI on 12 April, a Node.js command-line interface that gives AI agents native access to image, video, speech, music, vision and search via the MiniMax platform. The CLI is designed both for human developers in a terminal and for AI agents running inside Cursor, Claude Code and OpenCode harnesses — i.e., MMX-CLI is built to be invoked by other agents, not just by humans. Lands alongside the open-weight M2.7 release from 11 April. _Why it matters:_ MMX-CLI is the clearest example yet of "model + tool-server packaged for invocation by other agents." MiniMax is positioning the multimodal stack as a first-class citizen of the Cursor/Claude Code agent ecosystem rather than waiting for those harnesses to support its API. The agent-to-agent invocation pattern is going to define Q3 plumbing. [MarkTechPost — MiniMax MMX-CLI: native access to image, video, speech, music, vision and search](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/12/minimax-releases-mmx-cli-a-command-line-interface-that-gives-ai-agents-native-access-to-image-video-speech-music-vision-and-search/) · [MiniMax News (front page)](https://www.minimax.io/news)

**MiniMax M2.7 trends to #1 on Hugging Face — open-weight commercial-restricted "MIT-modified" debate intensifies** — By 12 April, MiniMax M2.7 was trending on Hugging Face alongside Gemma 4 variants. The model's licence (commercial use requires prior written authorisation) became the central debate point in the open-weight community, with Gemma 4's Apache-2.0 licence held up as the genuine permissive standard. _Why it matters:_ Two of the three most-downloaded weights of April are now under restrictive commercial licences (DeepSeek's preview will be the third). The "open weights" framing is fragmenting into three buckets — Apache-2.0 / OpenRAIL / commercial-restricted — and Apache-2.0 is becoming the minority. [The SOO Group — MiniMax M2.7, Gemma-4 variants lead April trends](https://thesoogroup.com/blog/hugging-face-trending-models-april-2026-minimax-gemma-variants) · [Medium — from API to owned: MiniMax M2.7, Gemma 4](https://medium.com/israeli-tech-radar/from-api-to-owned-minimax-m2-7-gemma-4-and-the-case-for-running-models-on-your-laptop-1680ccd87c0e)

**Mythos / Glasswing impact piece — Bloomberg Opinion: "Anthropic sets the right AI standard with Mythos" preview** — A Bloomberg Opinion preview piece previewed on Sunday 12 April (formally published 13 April) frames Anthropic's gated-release framework as the right new standard for capability-gated frontier rollouts. The argument: the Project Glasswing template — capability disclosure + access gating + 11-partner defender consortium — is the only proven response to dual-use capability levels that exceed defender baseline. _Why it matters:_ The Bloomberg Opinion framing now becomes the operating template policymakers cite for future frontier-AI dual-use disclosures. The piece is the closing bracket on the most-consequential week of AI policy framing in 2026. [Bloomberg Opinion — Anthropic sets the right AI standard with Mythos](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-04-13/anthropic-sets-the-right-ai-standard-with-mythos)

**Sunday open-source recap: Chinese models pull 41% of HF download volume — robotics datasets up 23×** — Sunday open-source-AI roundups recapped Hugging Face's Spring 2026 State of Open-Source report: Chinese-developed models accounted for 41% of HF download volume in Q1 2026, and robotics datasets grew 23× year-over-year (1,145 in 2024 → 26,991 in 2025). _Why it matters:_ The trend-line collides with Muse Spark's closed-weights pivot from 8 April — the gap between U.S.-frontier-lab open-weight commitment and Chinese open-weight share is widening just as enterprise procurement is consolidating around governed agent stacks. [SingularityByte — State of Open-Source AI Spring 2026, 41% Chinese, 23x robotics](https://singularitybyte.com/news/hf-state-of-open-source-spring-2026.html)
