## Major announcements & releases

**Sundar Pichai opens Google Cloud Next 2026 — $240B backlog, 750M Gemini users, agent-first cloud thesis** — Google Cloud Next 2026 opened on 21 April in Las Vegas. Pichai's opening keynote disclosed: Google Cloud at $70B annual revenue, 48% growth, **$240B backlog**, **750M Gemini users**. Theme: "The Agentic Cloud" — Google positions itself as the operating system for the agentic enterprise, not just a cloud that offers AI. Headline: ~75% of new code written at Google is AI-generated and reviewed by engineers; a recent internal code migration completed **6× faster** via agent system vs a year ago. _Why it matters:_ Pichai's framing — Google as the OS for agents, not a model provider — is the strategic positioning that distinguishes it from the OpenAI/Anthropic playbook. The $240B backlog disclosure is also a deliberate signal to investors about demand visibility. The agent-velocity numbers (75% AI-generated, 6× faster migration) re-frame the productivity narrative. [Sundar Pichai shares news from Google Cloud Next 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/cloud-next-2026-sundar-pichai/) · [TheNextWeb — $240B backlog, 750M users, Search as agent manager](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-cloud-next-pichai-agentic-era-scale) · [Welcome to Cloud Next 2026 blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/google-cloud-next/welcome-to-google-cloud-next26) · [Next '26 day-1 recap](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/google-cloud-next/next26-day-1-recap)

**Anthropic, Google, and Broadcom expand 3.5GW TPU compute partnership — capacity online in 2027** — Anthropic published on 21 April the formal expansion of its compute partnership with Google and Broadcom: **~3.5 gigawatts of compute** through Broadcom-designed TPUs, multi-year commitment, capacity online in 2027. The disclosure also re-stated Anthropic's run-rate revenue >$30B (up from ~$9B at end-2025) and 1,000+ business customers spending >$1M annualised (doubled in <2 months). _Why it matters:_ Coming the day after Anthropic's $100B AWS-spend pledge, this proves Anthropic's multi-cloud, multi-supplier strategy: Trainium on AWS, TPU on Google, Broadcom as the silicon partner across both. The 3.5GW on TPU is in addition to the 5GW on AWS — Anthropic is securing ~8.5GW of total compute and locking in chip-supplier diversity. [Anthropic — Google + Broadcom partnership compute](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute) · [Yahoo Finance — Anthropic 3.5GW capacity in Google + Broadcom deal](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/anthropic-secures-access-3-5-124717374.html) · [Silicon Republic — 3.5GW TPU deal](https://www.siliconrepublic.com/machines/anthropic-google-broadcom-announce-3-5gw-tpu-deal) · [AI Magazine — inside the partnership](https://aimagazine.com/news/inside-broadcom-anthropic-googles-ai-compute-partnership)

**Snowflake AI Pulse — Cortex Code, Snowflake Intelligence, MCP connectors as the agentic control plane** — Snowflake's monthly AI Pulse virtual event on 21 April delivered the biggest single-month Cortex update of the year. New: **MCP connectors** for Gmail/Google Calendar/Google Docs/Jira/Salesforce/Slack inside Cortex Agents; **Cortex Code** SDK adds AWS Glue / Databricks / Postgres connectors and native VS Code + Claude Code integration; **Snowflake Intelligence** adds Skills (natural-language workflow descriptions, executed automatically); Python/TypeScript Agent SDK for custom builds; new Cortex AI Guardrails for prompt-injection / jailbreak prevention. _Why it matters:_ This is Snowflake's clearest run at being "the control plane for the agentic enterprise." MCP-as-default-protocol means Cortex now sits at the centre of every major SaaS workflow without bespoke connectors. The Claude Code native integration is the most Anthropic-friendly statement any data cloud has made in 2026. [Snowflake AI Pulse April 2026 recap](https://snowflake.help/snowflake-ai-pulse-april-2026-recap-major-advances-in-agentic-ai-and-cortex-tools/) · [Snowflake — Expands Intelligence and Cortex Code to power the agentic enterprise control plane](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-expands-snowflake-intelligence-and-cortex-code-to-power-the-control-plane-for-the-agentic-enterprise/) · [SiliconANGLE — Snowflake targets agentic enterprise](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/21/snowflake-targets-agentic-enterprise-unified-control-plane-ai-data/) · [TechTarget — Snowflake updates further its control-pane goal](https://www.techtarget.com/searchdatamanagement/news/366642015/Snowflake-updates-further-goal-of-being-control-pane-for-AI)

**Google DeepMind's Deep Research Max — Cloud Next companion launch** — As part of the Cloud Next day-1 stream, Google released **Deep Research Max** — an advanced data-analysis mode for Gemini that handles long-running multi-source synthesis tasks (research-grade output across legal/financial/medical workflows). Bundled with Gemini Enterprise and previewed alongside the eighth-generation TPU disclosure for 22 April. _Why it matters:_ Deep Research Max is Google's direct counter to ChatGPT Deep Research and the Anthropic agent stack — productionised for enterprise workloads where Gemini Enterprise serves as the runtime. The pairing with the agent-platform launch makes it the obvious built-in research agent for new Gemini Enterprise customers. [What Google Cloud announced in AI this month](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/what-google-cloud-announced-in-ai-this-month) · [Google AI announcements from April 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-ai-updates-april-2026/)

**Workday — Illuminate platform of agents (HR, finance, industry)** — Josh Bersin's 21 April long-form "Reinvention of Workday" framed the formal pivot Workday has been telegraphing for two months: Workday is becoming a "platform of agents" rather than a "system of record." Illuminate's catalogue now spans HR, finance, and industry-vertical agents, fuelled by Workday's 1T+ annual transactions dataset and tied to the new Illuminate Agent Builder. _Why it matters:_ Workday's framing borrows the ServiceNow / Salesforce agent-platform vocabulary and stages a competitive answer ahead of Workday Rising. Bersin's analysis is the canonical industry read on whether the platform pivot will hold against ServiceNow Otto (May 11). [The Reinvention of Workday — JOSH BERSIN](https://joshbersin.com/2026/04/the-reinvention-of-workday-from-system-of-record-to-platform-of-agents/)

**OpenAI raises $122B to accelerate next AI phase — secondary tender at $750B** — OpenAI announced on 21 April the formal close of $122B in fresh capital for AI capacity and platform investments, structured as a primary round + secondary tender for employees at a $750B valuation. The announcement explicitly cites compute capex pacing through 2028. _Why it matters:_ This is the largest single capital raise in tech history. Combined with the Anthropic-Amazon $5B + Anthropic-Google $40B (3 days from now), the round defines the new capital-formation regime in AI: the labs are now raising rounds the size of national infrastructure programmes. [OpenAI — accelerating the next phase of AI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/)
