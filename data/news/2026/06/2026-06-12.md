## Major announcements & releases

**SpaceX/xAI debuts on Nasdaq as the largest IPO in history — SPCX pops ~30%** — Shares of the merged SpaceX–xAI–X entity began trading June 12 on Nasdaq under ticker SPCX, opening at $150 vs. the $135 offer price and running above $175 intraday, briefly valuing the company north of $2.25T and making Elon Musk the world's first trillionaire. The $75B raise (555.6M shares) is nearly triple the prior record (Saudi Aramco, ~$30B in 2019); the book was ~3.3x oversubscribed with $250B+ in orders, including $100B+ retail and a $5B+ BlackRock order. One SPCX share bundles the launch business (Falcon/Starship), Starlink, the xAI lab (Grok), and X. _Why it matters:_ A frontier AI lab effectively goes public inside the most valuable IPO ever — the single largest AI-adjacent public-markets event of the year, and a read on just how much capital the market will commit to AI-infrastructure-scale bets. [link](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html) (also [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/spacex-ipo-stock-price-rcna349760))

**Mistral ships Search Toolkit — an open-source, backend-agnostic RAG retrieval framework** — Now in public preview, Search Toolkit unifies ingestion, retrieval (BM25 sparse, dense embeddings, and hybrid), and evaluation (recall/precision/MRR/NDCG) behind one swappable-component interface, runnable in cloud, on-prem, or edge. It pairs indexed semantic search with live MCP Connectors so agents can choose between a high-quality indexed path and fresh source-system data; Mistral says it's battle-tested across financial services, manufacturing, public sector, and media (CMA CGM uses it with Voxtral for sub-15s fake-news alerts). _Why it matters:_ A frontier lab open-sourcing the full retrieval stack — not just a model — directly targets the "teams spend more time on retrieval plumbing than on quality" problem at the heart of enterprise RAG. [link](https://mistral.ai/news/search-toolkit/)
