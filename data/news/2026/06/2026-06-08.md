## Major announcements & releases

**Apple WWDC 2026 — Gemini-powered Siri, and Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini as selectable Apple Intelligence models** — At Tim Cook's final WWDC keynote (June 8, 10am PT), Apple unveiled a rebuilt Siri running on a custom ~1.2T-parameter Gemini model licensed from Google (reportedly ~$1B/year), plus a new Extensions system letting users choose ChatGPT, Google Gemini (default), or Anthropic's Claude to power Apple Intelligence features. Heavy reasoning runs on Apple Private Cloud Compute rather than Google infrastructure. iOS/macOS/iPadOS 27 ship this fall. _Why it matters:_ The most significant AI-platform policy decision Apple has made in a decade — it makes three frontier vendors first-class providers on ~2.2B devices and turns Gemini into a default at unprecedented scale. ([WWDC recap](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-8-2026); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/wwdc-2026-preview-ios-27-siri-ai-features-macos-27-more-apple-will-announce))

**OpenAI declares "chat is dead," plans to rebuild ChatGPT as an agent superapp** — Senior OpenAI staff told the FT the company is undertaking its biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch: a "superapp" bundling coding tools, AI agents, and partner apps (Canva, Booking.com), shifting from answering questions to completing tasks. Rollout begins in the coming weeks via the website and mobile apps ahead of OpenAI's expected IPO. _Why it matters:_ Signals the industry's pivot from chat UI to agent-as-product, and reframes OpenAI's consumer surface as a business-client acquisition channel against Anthropic. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/07/openai-is-still-working-on-that-super-app/); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-says-chat-is-dead-and-plans-to-rebuild-chatgpt-as-a-full-blown-agent-app/))

**OpenAI ships ChatGPT Lockdown Mode against prompt-injection data exfiltration** — OpenAI rolled out an optional Lockdown Mode (plus Elevated Risk labels) to personal and self-serve Business accounts. It constrains ChatGPT's external interactions: browsing is limited to cached content, and Deep Research, Agent Mode, live connectors, Canvas networking, image responses, and file downloads are disabled. OpenAI notes it reduces exfiltration risk but does not stop injected payloads from entering context. _Why it matters:_ First mainstream consumer-facing hardening primitive for agentic AI security — a template enterprises will demand as agents gain tool/web access. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-lockdown-mode-and-elevated-risk-labels-in-chatgpt/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/06/openai-unveils-lockdown-mode-to-protect-sensitive-data-from-prompt-injection-attacks/))

**Perplexity launches "Search as Code" — agents write their own retrieval pipelines** — Perplexity exposed its search stack as SDK primitives (retrieval, ranking, filtering, fanouts, reranking) that a model assembles on-demand into per-request pipelines, generated and executed in a secure sandbox. On a CVE-advisory benchmark it scored 100% accuracy (vs <25% for non-Perplexity systems) with 85% lower token usage. Now the default search mechanism in Perplexity's agent platform. _Why it matters:_ A concrete rethink of retrieval/RAG for the agent era — search as programmable primitives rather than fixed API calls. ([Perplexity Research](https://research.perplexity.ai/articles/rethinking-search-as-code-generation); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/perplexitys-search-as-code-lets-ai-models-write-their-own-search-pipelines-instead-of-calling-fixed-apis/))

**SAP backs n8n at $5.2B, embedding it into Joule Studio** — SAP invested (~$60M) in Berlin workflow-automation startup n8n, doubling its valuation to $5.2B (from $2.5B in Oct 2025) and making it Germany's most valuable AI company. n8n's 1,000+ integrations and visual canvas will embed directly in SAP's Joule Studio by Q3 2026 under SAP's compliance/security envelope, spanning no-/low-/pro-code. _Why it matters:_ The enterprise agentic-AI orchestration layer is consolidating — SAP is buying its way to citizen-developer hyperautomation rather than building it. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/n8n-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-as-sap-makes-strategic-investment-and-plans-to-embed-the-ai-platform-into-joule-studio-302767222.html); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/sap-invests-in-ai-automation-startup-n8n-at-5-2-billion-value))

**ServiceNow opens Action Fabric with a GA MCP Server; expands AI Control Tower** — At Knowledge 2026, ServiceNow launched Action Fabric, exposing its full "system of action" to any AI agent through a generally available Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server, and expanded AI Control Tower to discover, observe, govern, secure, and measure AI across any enterprise system (30 new integrations spanning AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, SAP, Oracle, Workday). _Why it matters:_ A major enterprise platform shipping production MCP at the action layer — concrete evidence MCP is moving from spec to enterprise default. ([ServiceNow Newsroom](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx))

**EU AI Act high-risk obligations now 55 days out (Aug 2); Colorado AI Act takes effect June 30** — The bulk of the EU AI Act begins applying Aug 2, 2026, bringing full high-risk-system requirements and GPAI systemic-risk obligations (models >10^25 FLOPs, covering GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini, Grok 4.3), with fines up to €35M or 7% of global turnover. Separately, Colorado's Consumer Protections for AI Act takes effect June 30 — the first real US AI enforcement deadline — even as the federal Great American AI Act (introduced June 4) proposes a three-year preemption that has not passed. _Why it matters:_ Two hard compliance dates inside 55 days; the regulatory grace period for high-risk AI is ending in both the EU and the first US state. ([WWDC/news roundup](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-8-2026); EU AI Office)

**Microsoft Build 2026 — Foundry hits 11,000+ models, Claude lands in Excel Agent Mode** — Microsoft finalized its Foundry catalog at 11,000+ models (GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.8/Sonnet/Haiku, Gemini, MAI, open-source via Fireworks) under one Azure endpoint and billing. Anthropic models extend into M365 Copilot Researcher, Copilot Studio agents, and a new Claude option in Excel Agent Mode. Microsoft also claimed its own MAI-Thinking-1 was preferred over Claude Sonnet 4.6 in vendor-run blind evals. _Why it matters:_ Puts Claude directly into the spreadsheet ~750M people use, and shows Microsoft hedging between hosting frontier rivals and shipping competitive in-house models. ([Build recap](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-8-2026); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/06/03/microsoft-launches-its-own-ai-models-to-take-on-openai-and-anthropic))
