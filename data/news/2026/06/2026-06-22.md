## Major announcements & releases

**Samsung Electronics deploys ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex company-wide** — Samsung is rolling out ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex to all employees in Korea and to its entire Device eXperience (DX) division worldwide, spanning R&D, manufacturing, marketing, and corporate functions. OpenAI calls it one of its largest enterprise deployments to date; Codex weekly active users in Korea have grown nearly 800% since February 1, 2026, with 5M+ weekly Codex users globally. _Why it matters:_ A flagship manufacturer treating Codex as a company-wide platform — not a dev-only tool — is a strong signal that coding agents are crossing into general knowledge work. [https://openai.com/index/samsung-electronics-chatgpt-codex-deployment/]

**US government forces Anthropic to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 globally** — A legally binding US export-control directive (effective 5:21pm ET, June 12) ordered Anthropic to block all access to its top models Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for any foreign national worldwide, including foreign-national employees. The trigger: officials identified a jailbreak technique unlocking Mythos's cybersecurity capabilities, reportedly after a Korean telecom "suspected of having ties to China" (SK Telecom) was granted Mythos access. Anthropic disputed that a narrow jailbreak justifies recalling a commercially deployed model. _Why it matters:_ The first time the US has used export-control authority to pull a frontier commercial model from the market — a precedent that reframes model distribution as a national-security-regulated activity. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access] (coverage: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-disables-fable-mythos-export-controls-national-security-threat/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/14/us-asks-anthropic-to-block-global-access-to-top-ai-models-why-it-matters/))

**Anthropic opens Seoul office, signs AI-safety MOU with Korean government** — Anthropic's third Asia-Pacific office (after Tokyo and Bengaluru) opened June 17, led by KiYoung Choi. It comes with an MOU with Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT on model-safety testing and AI cyber-threat information sharing, plus enterprise deployments at NAVER (Claude Code org-wide), LG CNS, Samsung SDS, Hanwha Solutions, Nexon, and Channel Corp. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic deepens its Korea footprint the same week US export controls cut Korean access to its top models — a striking juxtaposition of commercial expansion and geopolitical constraint. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/seoul-office-partnerships-korean-ai-ecosystem]

**OpenAI to acquire Ona to power long-running Codex agents** — OpenAI announced (June 11) it will acquire German cloud-execution startup Ona (formerly Gitpod), bringing secure, customer-controlled cloud environments for long-running Codex agents. Ona has helped ~2M developers run reproducible cloud dev environments. _Why it matters:_ Secure cloud sandboxing is the missing infra layer for autonomous coding agents that run for hours; OpenAI is buying it rather than building it. [https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-ona/] (context: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/open-ai-ona-acquisition-codex.html))

**EU AI Act "Digital Omnibus" defers high-risk obligations** — Council, Parliament, and Commission reached provisional agreement (May 7) on the first amendments to the AI Act since adoption: high-risk Annex III obligations deferred to Dec 2, 2027 (Annex I embedded systems to Aug 2, 2028), national regulatory sandboxes pushed to Aug 2027, and two new prohibitions added (non-consensual intimate material and CSAM generation, effective Dec 2, 2026). Formal adoption expected June, publication in July. _Why it matters:_ The most consequential rewrite of the EU's flagship AI law — significant compliance-timeline relief for enterprises, but new hard prohibitions. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/] (analysis: [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/))

**Google ships Gemini 3.5 Flash and restructures pricing** — Google released Gemini 3.5 Flash, positioned for long-horizon agentic and coding tasks at under half the cost of comparable models, claiming it outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on key benchmarks. Alongside it: a new $100/mo Developer tier and an Ultra-tier price cut from $250 to $200/mo. _Why it matters:_ Aggressive price-performance moves on the cheap-but-capable agent tier, where most production inference volume lives. [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/io-2026-google-ai/]

**NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B) fully open** — NVIDIA's largest open-weights model (~550B total / 55B active MoE) became available June 4 on Hugging Face, ModelScope, and OpenRouter, plus as a NIM microservice. NVIDIA released training data, RL environments, and post-training recipes alongside the weights, claiming ~5x faster inference and ~30% lower cost than leading proprietary alternatives. _Why it matters:_ A frontier-scale US open-weights release with full training transparency raises the bar for what "open" means and gives enterprises a self-hostable alternative. [https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-debuts-nemotron-3-family-of-open-models]

**SAP invests in n8n, doubling its valuation to $5.2B** — Announced at SAP Sapphire (Orlando), SAP took a ~1.3% stake in Berlin automation startup n8n (total commitment >€60M), doubling n8n's valuation to $5.2B in under a year and making it among Germany's most valuable AI startups. n8n's workflow tooling will be natively available in SAP's Joule Studio; SAP also invested in customer-service AI firm Parloa. _Why it matters:_ The European enterprise-AI backbone is consolidating — SAP is wiring an agentic-workflow layer directly into its Business AI platform. [https://www.heise.de/en/news/SAP-invests-n8n-becomes-one-of-the-most-valuable-German-AI-startups-11294028.html] (DE: [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/technik/ki/ki-n8n-steigt-dank-sap-zur-wertvollsten-deutschen-ki-firma-auf/100223115.html))
