## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI begins limited GPT-5.6 preview — Sol, Terra, Luna** — OpenAI rolled out its GPT-5.6 family (Sol flagship, Terra balanced, Luna fast/cheap) on June 26, but restricted access to ~20 government-approved partner companies at the behest of the U.S. government, with general availability promised "in the coming weeks." Sol ships with OpenAI's most robust safety stack to date, hardening higher-risk, sensitive-cyber, and repeated-misuse paths. _Why it matters:_ A frontier flagship gated behind a government access list is a new precedent that fuses model release and national-security policy. [https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/] (context: https://www.axios.com/2026/06/26/openai-gpt-sol-terra-luna-trump)

**Google ships Gemini 3.5 Flash, cuts Ultra to $200 at I/O 2026** — Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash, positioned as its strongest agentic and coding model yet — outperforming Gemini 3.1 Pro on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (76.2%), GDPval-AA (1656 Elo), MCP Atlas (83.6%) and leading on multimodal (CharXiv 84.2%). Alongside, Google cut the Ultra tier from $250 to $200/month and added a $100 Developer tier. _Why it matters:_ A frontier-grade agentic model in the cheap "Flash" tier plus an aggressive price cut pressures the whole inference market. [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/io-2026-google-ai/]

**Qualcomm to acquire Modular for ~$3.9B** — Qualcomm confirmed it is buying AI-software company Modular for roughly $3.9 billion in stock to add a hardware-agnostic, AI-native software stack (running models across CPUs, GPUs, NPUs and custom ASICs without per-accelerator rewrites) as it pushes into the data center. The deal is expected to close in H2 2026. _Why it matters:_ Qualcomm was missing the software layer rivals like NVIDIA own; this buys its way into the data-center AI stack overnight. [https://www.modular.com/blog/qualcomm-to-acquire-modular] (context: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/qualcomm-ai-chip-modular-software.html)

**DeepSeek sets V4 official release for mid-July, introduces peak-hour pricing** — On June 30 DeepSeek announced its V4 official version is due mid-July and will introduce peak-hour API pricing (rates double 9am–12pm and 2pm–6pm daily; off-peak unchanged). The 75% discount on V4 Pro became permanent at $0.435/M input and $0.87/M output. No R2 has been confirmed. _Why it matters:_ Time-of-day API pricing is a novel lever for a frontier-class open lab and signals capacity pressure on the cheapest tier of the market. [https://pandaily.com/deepseek-v4-official-july-peak-pricing-jun2026]

**SpaceX Colossus lands $6.3B compute deal with Reflection AI; ~$25B raise reportedly in the works** — Reflection AI signed a deal to pay SpaceX $150M/month from July 1, 2026 (up to ~$6.3B through 2029) for NVIDIA GB300 capacity at the Colossus 2 data center, and is reportedly raising ~$2.5B at a ~$25B valuation (up from $545M a year ago). Reflection pitches itself as "America's open frontier lab," a Western answer to Chinese open-weight models. _Why it matters:_ A pre-product lab locking in multibillion-dollar compute and a ~46x valuation jump shows how far capital is running ahead of shipped models. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/spacex-ai-colossus-data-center-reflection.html] (context: https://techfundingnews.com/reflection-ai-25-billion-valuation-nvidia-deepseek/)

**NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3 Ultra — fully open 550B reasoning model for long-running agents** — NVIDIA published Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B total / 55B active, MoE Hybrid Mamba-Attention) built specifically for long-running agents, scoring 47.7 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — well ahead of the next-best US open-weights models — with ~5x higher throughput in its class. NVIDIA released training data, RL environments, post-training recipes and fine-tuning code, not just weights. _Why it matters:_ A genuinely open frontier-class agent model from NVIDIA reshapes the open-weights leaderboard and pulls developers onto NVIDIA's stack. [https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-debuts-nemotron-3-family-of-open-models]

**EU AI Act: GPAI fines and Article 50 transparency duties go live August 2, 2026 despite Omnibus deferral** — The May 2026 Digital Omnibus pushed Annex III high-risk obligations out to December 2, 2027, but August 2, 2026 remains a hard enforcement date: the AI Office gains power to fine GPAI providers (up to €15M or 3% of global turnover) and Article 50 transparency duties become enforceable. Watermarking for systems already on the market applies from December 2, 2026. _Why it matters:_ The headline "delay" masks the fact that real penalty powers over general-purpose models turn on in five weeks. [https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/]

**Anthropic confidentially files draft S-1 for IPO; Opus 4.8 in market** — Anthropic confirmed it confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC (filed June 1) for a proposed IPO, while its current flagship Claude Opus 4.8 (released May 28) continues to ship with "dynamic workflows" for Claude Code and effort-control on claude.ai. _Why it matters:_ A frontier lab moving toward public markets is a structural event for the whole sector's financing and disclosure norms. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec]

## Other notable items

- **[NVIDIA + ServiceNow expand autonomous-agent partnership](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/servicenow-autonomous-ai-agents-enterprises/)** — ServiceNow's Autonomous Workforce leans on NVIDIA's Agent Toolkit, AI-Q blueprint, OpenShell runtime and Nemotron/Apriel models, governed by ServiceNow AI Control Tower — a concrete enterprise-agent deployment pattern.
- **[Mistral releases OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/)** — SOTA document-intelligence OCR with bounding boxes, block classification and inline confidence scores (June 23), continuing Mistral's enterprise-document push.
- **[Unisound U2 open agent model](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)** — A 266B-total / 10B-active MoE built for agents (86.9% GPQA Diamond, 72.2% SWE-bench Verified) at $0.15/$0.30 per 1M tokens, underscoring how cheap capable open agent models have become.
- **[Switzerland deep-tech / sovereign AI momentum](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/artificial-intelligence-in-switzerland-whats-new-in-2026/90701795)** — The Swiss Deep Tech Report 2026 lands; Swisscom is deploying the open Apertus model (EPFL/ETH/CSCS) on its sovereign-AI platform and CHUV is piloting the Meditron medical LLM in its ER.
- **[Handelsblatt: Europe's AI sector stronger than it looks; 2026 as Europe's AI year](https://www.handelsblatt.com/technik/ki/ki-briefing-europas-ki-branche-ist-viel-staerker-als-sie-denken/100207945.html)** — German business coverage argues European AI is more competitive than perceived, as US tech giants pour up to $725B into AI infrastructure in 2026.
