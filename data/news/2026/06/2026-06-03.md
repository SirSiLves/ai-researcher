## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic launches Services Track and Partner Hub for the Claude Partner Network** — Anthropic formalized its enterprise consulting ecosystem with a three-tier Services Track (Select / Preferred / Global Premier), graded on certified practitioners, production deployments, and public customer stories, plus a Partner Hub dashboard refreshed daily. Since the program launched in March, 40,000+ firms have applied and 10,000+ consultants have earned a Claude certification. _Why it matters:_ Signals Anthropic's push to industrialize enterprise delivery through services partners ahead of its reported IPO process. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub] (coverage: https://qz.com/anthropic-claude-partner-network-services-track-hub-enterprise-060326)

**Snowflake Summit 2026 — Cortex Training, CoWork, and Cortex Agent Sharing** — At Summit on June 2, Snowflake unveiled Cortex Training (build domain-specific models on proprietary data), rebranded Snowflake Intelligence as CoWork (a personal AI agent for business users), Cortex Sense (unified business context with prebuilt plugins and MCP connectors), and Cortex Agent Sharing (deploy agents across accounts and the Marketplace with governance built in). Claude models now power CoWork and CoCo, making Anthropic Snowflake's foundational AI provider. _Why it matters:_ One of the year's biggest enterprise data+AI events; agentic features now ship governed by default. [https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-cowork-powers-the-agentic-enterprise-as-the-personal-agent-for-knowledge-workers-to-work-smarter/]

**Snowflake pioneers open framework for interoperable enterprise data and AI** — Also at Summit, Snowflake announced an open framework letting organizations access, govern, share, and act on data across systems, including a dedicated Skill for SAP inside CoCo (Snowflake's AI coding agent) to connect to and manage SAP data. _Why it matters:_ Interoperability and open data are the battleground as enterprises resist single-vendor agent lock-in. [https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-pioneers-new-open-framework-for-interoperable-enterprise-data-and-ai/]

**ServiceNow opens its full system of action to any AI agent via GA MCP Server** — At Knowledge 2026, ServiceNow announced Action Fabric, exposing its AI Platform to any external agent — Claude, Copilot, or homegrown — through a generally available Model Context Protocol server. _Why it matters:_ A major enterprise platform betting on open MCP-based agent access rather than walling off its workflows. [https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx]

**EU Digital Omnibus delays high-risk AI Act obligations** — Council, Parliament, and Commission reached a provisional deal (7 May) deferring Annex III high-risk obligations from Aug 2026 to Dec 2027 (16-month slip) and Annex I product-regulated systems from Aug 2027 to Aug 2028, plus narrowed "safety component" scope and a 1-year sandbox postponement. Final adoption is anticipated in June with publication expected in July. _Why it matters:_ The single biggest near-term shift in AI regulation — buys enterprises time but draws criticism that high-risk systems dodge oversight. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/]

**Anthropic raises $65B Series H at ~$965B valuation, becomes most valuable AI startup** — CNBC reported (28 May) a round led by Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia that nearly triples Anthropic's February $380B valuation, on a reported $47B revenue run rate. Separately, Anthropic confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC on June 1. _Why it matters:_ Reorders the frontier-lab pecking order and sets up one of the largest tech IPOs in history. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html] (S-1: https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec)

**OpenAI frontier models and Codex go GA on AWS Bedrock; GPT-5.3-Codex ships** — OpenAI made its frontier models and Codex generally available through Amazon Bedrock (commercial and GovCloud), giving enterprises AWS-native security, billing, and compliance. It also launched GPT-5.3-Codex — its most capable agentic coding model, ~25% faster — and added Computer Use support for Codex on Windows. _Why it matters:_ Deepens OpenAI's enterprise distribution beyond Azure and intensifies the agentic-coding race. [https://releasebot.io/updates/openai] (help center: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes)

**Mistral partners with BMW for AI-driven crash simulation** — BMW and Mistral announced a collaboration applying AI to crash simulation to improve accuracy and speed in engineering, alongside Mistral's AI Now Summit updates (Vibe as a single long-running multi-step agent) and a new 10 MW inference site at Les Ulis opening Q3 2026. _Why it matters:_ A flagship European industrial AI deployment and a sign Mistral is anchoring sovereign enterprise use cases. [https://mistral.ai/news/] (coverage: https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/06/01/bmw-mistral-ai-partner-for-ai-use-in-crash-simulation/)

**Red Hat AI Factory with NVIDIA expands support for enterprise autonomous agents** — Red Hat (at Summit 2026) strengthened its AI portfolio — RHEL AI, OpenShift AI, Red Hat AI Enterprise — with hardware-level confidential computing for agents (NVIDIA Confidential Computing in sandboxed containers) and co-developed NVIDIA AI Blueprints for Model-as-a-Service and Enterprise RAG/RAFT. _Why it matters:_ Hardening the open-source enterprise stack for production agents with security at the silicon level. [https://www.redhat.com/en/about/press-releases/red-hat-ai-factory-nvidia-expands-support-new-class-autonomous-agents-enterprise]

**MCP June 2026 spec targets server-as-agent / recursive composition** — The upcoming MCP spec release is expected to add server-as-agent capabilities, letting MCP servers connect to other MCP servers for recursive composition. MCP is now governed by the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation with 18,000+ community-indexed servers; the MCP-for-tools + A2A-for-coordination two-layer architecture has crystallized as the reference model. _Why it matters:_ The protocol layer is consolidating under neutral governance, the precondition for real cross-vendor agent interop. [https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/] (analysis: https://zylos.ai/research/2026-03-26-agent-interoperability-protocols-mcp-a2a-acp-convergence)

**NVIDIA posts record Q1 FY27 with $500B Blackwell+Rubin pipeline** — NVIDIA reported record quarterly revenue of $81.6B (up 85% YoY), Data Center revenue of $75.2B (up 92%), and CFO Colette Kress cited $500B in Blackwell and Rubin revenue visibility through end of calendar 2026. The newly unveiled Vera Rubin platform (six chips) targets up to 10x lower inference token cost vs Blackwell, with AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, and OCI among first deployers. _Why it matters:_ The AI infrastructure supercycle shows no slowdown; Rubin economics reset the cost-per-token baseline. [https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-2026]

**Snowflake + Anthropic deepen partnership for governed enterprise AI** — Snowflake and Anthropic announced an expanded partnership making Claude the foundational model behind Snowflake's agentic platform, citing rising enterprise demand for governed AI. Cognizant reported CoCo usage growing to 2,250+ users across 30+ enterprise use cases and 1.3M+ AI requests. _Why it matters:_ A concrete frontier-lab-meets-data-platform deployment with real adoption metrics, not just a press release. [https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-and-anthropic-accelerate-enterprise-ai-adoption-driven-by-rising-demand-for-governed-ai/]
