## Major announcements & releases

**SpaceX to acquire Cursor (Anysphere) for $60B in all-stock deal** — SpaceX confirmed on June 16 it will buy Anysphere, maker of the Cursor AI coding tool, for $60 billion in stock, exercising an option secured in April and following SpaceX's recent merger with xAI. Cursor runs at roughly $2.6B annualized revenue; the deal is the largest acquisition in AI developer-tooling history and expected to close in Q3 pending regulatory approval. _Why it matters:_ The coding-agent layer is now strategic enough that a frontier-compute/launch company will pay $60B to own it — vertical consolidation of model + agent + distribution. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/spacex-to-acquire-cursor-for-60b-in-stock-days-after-blockbuster-ipo/] (also CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/spacex-spcx-cursor-acquisition-ipo.html)

**Baseten nears $1.5B round at $13B valuation** — AI inference provider Baseten is finalizing a $1.5B raise (split-priced at $11B/$13B) co-led by Spark, Sands, Altimeter and Wellington, per the WSJ on June 18 — a ~160% valuation jump in five months, with run-rate revenue climbing from ~$200M to ~$600M. Baseten routes requests to the best-for-task model, leaning on cheaper open-weight alternatives to OpenAI/Anthropic. _Why it matters:_ Capital is flooding the inference/serving layer ("inference gold rush"); open-weight serving economics are the bet investors are underwriting. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/ai-inference-startup-baseten-reportedly-raising-1-5b-months-after-its-last-mega-round/]

**Vercel launches Eve, an open-source TypeScript-native agent framework** — At its Ship 26 conference in London on June 17, Vercel released Eve in public beta: each agent is a directory of files that compiles to a durable, production-ready service on Vercel Functions, with no separate hosting infrastructure required. It positions against LangGraph (Python-first, bring-your-own-hosting) and Microsoft Agent Framework. _Why it matters:_ The agent-framework field is consolidating around runtime + hosting bundles; a JS/TS-native, deploy-included option targets the large web-developer base LangChain doesn't own. [https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318642/20260618/vercel-eve-launches-open-source-agent-framework-backed-its-own-production-fleet.htm]

**US government export directive forces Anthropic to disable Fable 5 and Mythos 5** — The US Commerce Department invoked an export-control directive barring non-Americans (including Anthropic's own employees) from accessing Fable 5 and Mythos 5, citing a national-security concern; Anthropic shut both models down for all customers on ~June 12 to comply. Reporting (Fortune, June 15) traced the trigger to a narrow "fix this code" jailbreak surfaced by Amazon researchers; Anthropic disputed that a narrow potential jailbreak warranted recalling a model deployed to hundreds of millions. _Why it matters:_ First time US export controls have been used to pull a commercially deployed frontier model — a major precedent for how national-security policy can reach into live AI products. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access] (context: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/15/the-us-governments-anthropic-models-ban-was-never-about-an-ai-jailbreak/)

**EU AI Act "Digital Omnibus" heads to formal adoption — high-risk deadlines slip** — Following the May 7 provisional trilogue agreement, the AI Act's first amendments since 2024 are moving through formal adoption (publication anticipated July). Annex III high-risk obligations are postponed from Aug 2026 to Dec 2027 (16 months), Annex I product-regulated obligations from Aug 2027 to Aug 2028, alongside reinforced central AI Office oversight of GPAI-based systems, SME/small-mid-cap relief, and an EU-level regulatory sandbox. _Why it matters:_ The headline compliance cliff for high-risk AI is now 16 months further out, reshaping enterprise EU rollout timelines and shifting governance weight toward the central AI Office. [https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-agrees-digital-omnibus-deal-simplify-ai-rules]

**SAP pushes "Autonomous Enterprise" with Joule agents; A2A interop targeted for Q4** — At Sapphire 2026 SAP detailed 50+ Joule assistants and 200+ specialized agents, a €100M partner fund, and agent-led migration tooling, with native Microsoft Copilot connection and Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol GA slated for Q4 2026 to bridge to Salesforce Agentforce and ServiceNow. Notably, SAP is gating external agents through Joule rather than adopting the more open headless approach of Salesforce/ServiceNow — drawing pushback (Forrester, Techzine) over gatekeeping. _Why it matters:_ The enterprise-agent war is now about interop posture: SAP's walled-Joule strategy vs. open A2A federation defines what enterprises can actually wire together. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/secure-ai-agents-how-sap-and-nvidia-co-define-enterprise-grade-agent-execution/] (analysis: https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141323/sap-blocks-external-ai-agents-salesforce-and-servicenow-dont/)

**General Intuition in talks to raise $300M at ~$2B valuation** — TechCrunch reported on June 18 that General Intuition is in talks for a $300M round at around a $2B valuation, part of a steady stream of mid-June mega-rounds in the AI tooling/agents space. _Why it matters:_ Even in a "slower week for large deals," AI-native startups are still clearing nine-figure rounds at multi-billion valuations — the funding environment for the application/agent layer remains frothy. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/general-intuition-in-talks-to-raise-300m-at-around-2b-valuation/]

**Anthropic becomes first AI startup to join the Frontier carbon-removal coalition** — On June 17 Anthropic joined the Frontier advance-market-commitment coalition for carbon removal, the first AI company to do so, signaling AI-sector engagement on the energy/emissions footprint of large-scale compute. _Why it matters:_ As data-center power demand becomes a binding constraint on AI scaling, frontier labs are starting to take public positions on the climate cost of compute. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/anthropic-becomes-first-ai-startup-to-join-the-frontier-carbon-removal-coalition/]
