## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI and Broadcom unveil "Jalapeño," an LLM-optimized inference chip** — OpenAI revealed its first in-house Intelligence Processor, co-designed with Broadcom and Celestica and taped out in nine months (which OpenAI claims is the fastest high-performance ASIC cycle ever). Early testing shows "performance per watt substantially better than current state-of-the-art," with gigawatt-scale deployment via Microsoft and other partners starting end of 2026. _Why it matters:_ OpenAI is now vertically integrating down to custom silicon — the clearest signal yet that frontier labs intend to own the full inference stack, not just rent GPUs. [https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/](https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/)

**Anthropic launches Claude Tag — @Claude as a multiplayer Slack teammate** — Claude Tag lets teams add @Claude to Slack channels, scope its tool/data/codebase access per channel, and delegate async tasks that it plans and executes over hours or days. It learns channel context over time, can take ambient initiative, and replaces the old "Claude in Slack" app. Anthropic says 65% of its product team's code now comes from its internal version, running on Opus 4.8. _Why it matters:_ A concrete step beyond IDE-bound coding agents toward ambient, collaborative, multi-user agents embedded where teams already work. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag)

**Mistral ships OCR 4 and tightens connector controls** — Mistral released OCR 4, billed as a state-of-the-art document-intelligence model (Jun 23), and rolled out finer-grained control over connectors in Studio/Le Chat (Jun 24). _Why it matters:_ Document AI and governed enterprise connectors are where Mistral is concentrating its European enterprise pitch; OCR remains one of its strongest commercial differentiators. [https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/) (connectors: [https://mistral.ai/news/more-control-over-connectors/](https://mistral.ai/news/more-control-over-connectors/))

**OpenAI launches GPT-5.5-Cyber and "Patch the Planet" open-source security drive** — GPT-5.5-Cyber (Jun 22) posted 85.6% on CyberGym — OpenAI claims the highest single-model score recorded — and is gated to vetted orgs via a Trusted Access for Cyber program (Cisco, Cloudflare, CrowdStrike, Fortinet, Palo Alto, et al.) plus several governments. Companion program "Patch the Planet" pairs Codex Security + GPT-5.5-Cyber with mandatory Trail of Bits human review before any finding reaches a maintainer; 30+ projects (cURL, Go, Python, Sigstore) have joined. _Why it matters:_ Frontier labs are operationalizing offensive-grade security models under access controls explicitly tied to Trump's June 2 AI executive order. [https://openai.com/index/patch-the-planet/](https://openai.com/index/patch-the-planet/)

**Google expands Gemini API with Managed Agents and GA Gemini 3.5 Flash** — Google moved Gemini 3.5 Flash to GA and launched Managed Agents (public preview): autonomous, stateful agents running in Google-hosted, isolated Linux sandboxes, including a general-purpose Antigravity agent that plans, writes/executes code, manages files, and browses the web. File Search gained multimodal (image) embedding/search via gemini-embedding-2. _Why it matters:_ Server-side managed agent runtimes are becoming a first-party platform feature across the frontier labs, not just a third-party framework concern. [https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/innovations-from-google-io-26-on-google-cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/innovations-from-google-io-26-on-google-cloud)

**SpaceX signs $6.3B compute deal with Reflection AI for Colossus 2** — SpaceX leased GB300 capacity to open-weight frontier challenger Reflection AI ($150M/month through 2029, ~$6.3B total, Jun 22), its fourth external Colossus tenant after Anthropic (~$45B), Google (~$30B), and Cursor. Reflection — founded by ex-DeepMind researchers and now raising at a reported $25B valuation — is positioning as the "American, open-weight, frontier-scale" alternative to closed US labs and Chinese models. _Why it matters:_ Compute is consolidating into a few mega-campuses, and a well-funded open-weight Western lab with sovereign/defense ties is a new competitive vector. [https://thenextweb.com/news/spacex-reflection-ai-6-3bn-compute-deal](https://thenextweb.com/news/spacex-reflection-ai-6-3bn-compute-deal)

**Nobel laureate John Jumper leaves DeepMind for Anthropic** — AlphaFold 2 co-lead and 2024 Chemistry Nobel laureate John Jumper announced (Jun 20) he is joining Anthropic, days after Transformer co-author Noam Shazeer departed Google for OpenAI. Alphabet stock fell as much as 7.2% intraday. _Why it matters:_ A concentrated outflow of foundational AI talent from Google, expected to accelerate Anthropic's AI-for-science work (protein structure, drug discovery, Project Glasswing). [https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/)

**EU finalizes Code of Practice on marking/labelling AI-generated content** — The Commission published the final Code of Practice implementing Article 50 transparency obligations (machine-readable marking of AI content; deployer labelling of deepfakes and AI text on matters of public interest). The voluntary code supports obligations applying from 2 August 2026. _Why it matters:_ The first concrete compliance scaffolding for AI-content transparency in the EU; vendors must add machine-readable provenance marking ahead of the August deadline. [https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-publishes-code-practice-marking-and-labelling-ai-generated-content](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-publishes-code-practice-marking-and-labelling-ai-generated-content)

**Microsoft Build 2026: governed agent stack + first-party MAI models** — Microsoft unveiled a governed agent stack adding governance, security, and identity control to autonomous agents inside Azure and M365, paired with a new family of first-party MAI models. Both are in public preview now, GA targeted for October 2026. _Why it matters:_ The enterprise platform layer is racing to wrap agents in identity/governance controls — the same theme as ServiceNow Action Fabric and SAP's Business AI Platform. [https://windowsnews.ai/article/build-2026-microsofts-governed-agent-stack-locks-down-enterprise-ai-with-new-mai-models.429587](https://windowsnews.ai/article/build-2026-microsofts-governed-agent-stack-locks-down-enterprise-ai-with-new-mai-models.429587)
