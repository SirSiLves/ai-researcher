## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5 — its first public Mythos-class model** — Anthropic made its most capable model generally available as Claude Fable 5, a "Mythos-class" model with conservative safeguards (sensitive queries reroute to Opus 4.8 in <5% of sessions). It is state-of-the-art on nearly all tested benchmarks for software engineering, vision, knowledge work, and scientific research; Stripe reported it compressed a codebase-wide migration that would have taken a team two-plus months into a single day. The unrestricted Mythos 5 variant is reserved for vetted cybersecurity professionals via Project Glasswing. Pricing is $10 input / $50 output per 1M tokens — under half the Mythos Preview rate. _Why it matters:_ A frontier-capability jump shipped to the public the same week Anthropic publicly warned that AI is becoming "too dangerous," underscoring the tension between capability releases and safety posture. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5]

**Microsoft launches seven in-house MAI models at Build 2026** — Microsoft AI unveiled a family of seven self-developed models — including MAI-Thinking-1 (reasoning), MAI-Code-1-Flash (5B active params, deeply integrated into GitHub Copilot and VS Code), MAI-Image-2.5, MAI-Transcribe-1.5, and MAI-Voice-2 — available on Foundry, OpenRouter, Fireworks, and Baseten, with tunable weights for the first time. _Why it matters:_ Microsoft is explicitly building first-party models to lessen reliance on OpenAI and cut developer costs, a structural shift in the Microsoft–OpenAI relationship. [https://microsoft.ai/news/building-a-hillclimbing-machine-launching-seven-new-mai-models/] (also: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/microsoft-unveils-new-ai-models-lessen-reliance-on-openai-lower-costs.html)

**Google ships Gemini 3.5 Flash and cuts Ultra pricing** — Google kicked off the Gemini 3.5 series with 3.5 Flash, claiming frontier-level agentic and coding performance at 4x the speed of comparable models ($1.50/$9 per 1M tokens, 1M context) and outperforming Gemini 3.1 Pro on key benchmarks. Google also cut the AI Ultra subscription from $250 to $200/month and introduced a $100/month Developer tier, alongside a new Managed Agents API for Google-hosted custom agents. _Why it matters:_ Aggressive price/performance moves on a fast frontier model plus a managed-agent runtime put direct pressure on OpenAI and Anthropic in both API and agent-platform markets. [https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/innovations-from-google-io-26-on-google-cloud]

**SAP invests in n8n at $5.2B valuation, embeds it in Joule Studio** — SAP took a strategic stake (~$60M) in workflow-automation startup n8n, doubling its valuation to $5.2B (from $2.5B in October) and signing a multi-year deal to embed n8n's 1,000+ integrations and visual canvas directly into Joule Studio by Q3 2026. n8n — now Germany's most valuable AI company — serves 1,400+ enterprise customers and 1.7M monthly active builders. _Why it matters:_ SAP is telling 300,000 customers that n8n is the orchestration layer for their AI agents; a defining enterprise-platform consolidation move. [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/n8n-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-as-sap-makes-strategic-investment-and-plans-to-embed-the-ai-platform-into-joule-studio-302767222.html] (Bloomberg: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/sap-invests-in-ai-automation-startup-n8n-at-5-2-billion-value)

**SAP unveils the "Autonomous Enterprise" — Joule Studio 2.0 rolling out in June** — At Sapphire 2026 SAP recast Joule as the front door to enterprise AI, launching the SAP Business AI Platform (unifying BTP, Business Data Cloud, and Business AI) and an Autonomous Suite with 50+ domain Joule Assistants orchestrating 200+ specialized agents. Joule Studio 2.0 begins rolling out in June 2026; Anthropic's Claude is among the foundation models powering Joule agents, with new embedded partnerships with Vercel and n8n. _Why it matters:_ The clearest signal yet that the largest enterprise-software vendor is betting its platform strategy on agentic AI grounded in live business data. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/]

**Mistral relaunches Le Chat as "Vibe" — one agent for work and code** — Mistral consolidated its assistant into Vibe, a single agent and licence spanning a long-running Work Mode and a remote-coding Code Mode (with a new VS Code extension and CLI), carrying over conversations, settings, and plans from Le Chat. Mistral also added Emmi AI physics simulation for industrial engineering and announced a new 10 MW inference site at Les Ulis opening Q3 2026. _Why it matters:_ A European frontier lab pushing a unified work+code agent and owning more of its compute stack, framed around enterprise/government full-stack AI. [https://mistral.ai/news/] (context: https://devops.com/mistral-moves-coding-agents-to-the-cloud-and-gets-out-of-your-way/)

**Salesforce Summer '26 brings multi-agent orchestration and Tableau MCP** — Salesforce's Summer '26 release (available June 15) delivers Agentforce Multi-Agent Orchestration (agents acting as a unified team with shared context), a new Customer Engagement Agent, and Tableau MCP — a secure MCP integration letting AI agents query Tableau's analytics engine directly under the Agentforce Trust Layer. Agentforce ARR hit $800M, up 169% YoY. _Why it matters:_ A major CRM vendor pairing multi-agent orchestration with a governed MCP data path is exactly the agent-protocol-meets-enterprise pattern enterprises will copy. [https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/summer-2026-product-release-announcement/]

**NVIDIA's Rubin platform enters full production** — NVIDIA confirmed Rubin — its first six-chip, extreme-codesigned AI platform and Blackwell successor — is in full production, claiming up to 10x lower inference token cost and 4x fewer GPUs to train MoE models vs. Blackwell. Rubin-optimized NIM microservice containers ship for model deployment; partner availability (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, OCI, CoreWeave, Lambda, Nebius, Nscale) lands in H2 2026. _Why it matters:_ Inference economics are the gating factor for agentic AI at scale, and a claimed 10x token-cost reduction reshapes what enterprises can afford to deploy. [https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/rubin-platform-ai-supercomputer]

**Snowflake Summit '26: Cortex Code rebrands to "CoCo" coding agent** — At Snowflake Summit 26 (early June) the company rebranded Cortex Code as Snowflake CoCo, a coding agent for building on Snowflake "anywhere," alongside 25+ announcements; Snowflake stock posted its best day ever (+36%) on the AI momentum. _Why it matters:_ Data-platform vendors are moving up the stack into coding agents, putting retrieval and governance closer to where enterprise data already lives. [https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-coco-redefines-enterprise-ai-development-as-the-coding-agent-built-for-faster-easier-and-more-powerful-innovation-anywhere/]

**Redis Iris launches as enterprises shift from RAG to runtime context** — Redis introduced Iris with Agent Memory (preview) for short/long-term agent state across sessions and Redis Flex, a storage engine running 99% of data on SSDs / 1% in RAM for petabyte-scale retrieval at sub-millisecond latency. The launch rides a broader 2026 shift documented by VentureBeat: agentic systems are pushing past basic vector search toward hybrid, graph-augmented, and "compilation-stage" knowledge layers (Pinecone pitched KnowQL, a declarative query language for agents). _Why it matters:_ Retrieval infra is being re-architected around long-running agents rather than one-shot RAG — a structural change in the knowledge-layer stack. [https://venturebeat.com/data/the-rag-era-is-ending-for-agentic-ai-a-new-compilation-stage-knowledge-layer-is-what-comes-next]
