## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic partners with TCS to bring Claude to regulated industries** — Tata Consultancy Services will deploy Claude to 50,000 of its own employees across 56 countries and build Claude-powered products for clients in financial services, healthcare and the public sector, joining the Claude Partner Network. It follows a same-week DXC deal (June 11) to embed Claude into systems used by banks, airlines and other regulated sectors. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic is building a systems-integrator channel to push Claude into compliance-heavy enterprise verticals. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/tcs-anthropic-partnership]

**Anthropic confidentially files draft S-1 with the SEC** — Anthropic submitted a confidential draft registration statement for a proposed IPO, days after OpenAI filed its own confidential S-1 (June 8). Anthropic is reported as the most valuable standalone AI startup following a large Series H. _Why it matters:_ Both frontier labs are now on a public-markets path, a structural shift for the sector. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec]

**Google ships Gemini 3.5 Flash GA plus Managed Agents in the Gemini API** — Gemini 3.5 Flash reached general availability as Google's most intelligent model for sustained agentic and coding work. Alongside it, Google launched Managed Agents in public preview — autonomous, stateful agents running in Google-hosted isolated Linux sandboxes — and the general-purpose Antigravity Agent that can plan, write/execute code, manage files and browse inside its container. _Why it matters:_ Google is moving agent execution into its own managed runtime, competing directly with agent SDKs and sandboxed-agent platforms. [https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/what-google-cloud-announced-in-ai-this-month]

**OpenAI to acquire Ona; ships GPT-Rosalind life-sciences upgrade** — OpenAI announced plans to acquire Ona (June 11) and earlier in the month expanded GPT-Rosalind with stronger biological reasoning, medicinal-chemistry, and genomics-analysis capabilities (June 3). ChatGPT also gained a new "Dreaming" memory system (June 4). _Why it matters:_ Continued vertical push into scientific research plus consumer memory features. [https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/]

**SAP invests in n8n at $5.2B valuation, embeds it in Joule Studio** — SAP took a stake (~$60M) in Berlin's n8n, more than doubling its October 2025 $2.5B valuation and making it Germany's most valuable AI company. n8n's visual AI workflow canvas will be embedded natively in Joule Studio, SAP's agent-building environment, by Q3 2026. _Why it matters:_ Workflow-automation is consolidating into the enterprise agent stack; SAP is buying its orchestration layer. [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/n8n-valuation-doubles-to-5-2bn-as-sap-makes-strategic-investment-and-plans-to-embed-the-ai-platform-into-joule-studio-302767222.html]

**SAP begins enforcing API policy that blocks external AI agents** — SAP's API Policy v4/2026, which prohibits AI systems that independently schedule or execute SAP API calls, took effect June 9 — meaning third-party agents can no longer call SAP APIs autonomously, unlike Salesforce and ServiceNow which keep theirs open. _Why it matters:_ A live battle over who controls the agent gateway into enterprise systems of record; SAP is positioning as gatekeeper. [https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141323/sap-blocks-external-ai-agents-salesforce-and-servicenow-dont/]

**EU AI Act "Digital Omnibus" deal restructures deadlines** — Following the May 7 provisional agreement among Council, Parliament and Commission, the first amendments to the AI Act extend high-risk compliance deadlines (Annex III to Dec 2, 2027; Annex I to Aug 2, 2028), add simplifications and new rules on AI-generated intimate content. Prohibited-practice bans remain enforceable now, with fines up to €35M or 7% of global turnover. _Why it matters:_ Major timeline relief for high-risk AI providers while core prohibitions stay active. [https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/eu-ai-act-update-timeline-relief-targeted-simplification-and-new-prohibitions/]

**Snowflake Summit 2026 ships 26 capabilities, agent identity in GA** — At its June 1-4 summit Snowflake rebranded its agent surfaces to CoWork and CoCo and made AI Agent Identity generally available, giving every agent a cryptographic identity, per-agent RBAC and full audit trails, alongside Horizon Context, Cortex Sense and Iceberg v3. _Why it matters:_ Agent identity/governance is becoming a first-class enterprise data-platform primitive. [https://atlan.com/know/snowflake/summit-2026-announcements/]

**MCP June spec targets server-as-agent composition** — The June 2026 Model Context Protocol spec release is expected to add server-as-agent capabilities, letting MCP servers connect to other MCP servers for recursive composition, with fine-grained authorization slated for a later release. MCP is now governed by the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation after Anthropic's December 2025 donation. _Why it matters:_ Recursive MCP composition deepens the standard's role as the de-facto tool-integration layer. [https://chatforest.com/guides/mcp-ecosystem-2026-state-of-the-standard/]

**Mistral in talks to raise ~€3B at ~€20B valuation** — Mistral is reportedly seeking about €3 billion (~$3.5B) at roughly a €20 billion (~$23B) valuation to fund European AI infrastructure, including a new 10 MW inference site at Les Ulis opening Q3 2026. _Why it matters:_ Europe's leading frontier lab is scaling sovereign compute capacity. [https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/mistral-seeks-3-billion-dollars-build-european-ai-infrastructure/]
