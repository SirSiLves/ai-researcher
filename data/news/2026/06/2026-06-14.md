## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI to acquire Ona (ex-Gitpod) to run Codex agents in the cloud** — OpenAI announced on June 11 it will acquire German startup Ona (formerly Gitpod GmbH), whose platform runs coding agents in cloud-based sandboxes. The deal lets Codex keep working on a task for hours or days unattended after the developer closes their laptop, shifting from supervised tool to delegated worker. Terms undisclosed; Codex now has 5M+ weekly users, up ~400% since early 2026. _Why it matters:_ Frontier labs are racing to own the long-running, autonomous-execution layer of coding agents, not just the model. [https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/11/openai-acquires-ai-agent-orchestration-startup-ona/)

**Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5 (GA) and Mythos 5 (limited)** — Claude Fable 5 went GA on June 9 across the Claude API, AWS, Bedrock, Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry — billed as Anthropic's most capable widely-released model, built for demanding reasoning and long-horizon agentic work (reported 95% SWE-bench Verified, 80% SWE-bench Pro; $10/$50 per M tokens). Mythos 5 is in limited availability to Project Glasswing customers. _Why it matters:_ A new frontier-tier Anthropic model reset the agentic-coding bar mid-month and is already the default in enterprise channels. [https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5)

**Anthropic + TCS bring Claude to regulated industries** — Announced June 12: Tata Consultancy Services will deploy Claude to 50,000 of its own employees across 56 countries and build Claude-powered products (claims processing for insurers, lending advisory for banks) for clients in financial services, healthcare, public sector and other regulated industries, joining the Claude Partner Network. Diligenta (TCS's UK life & pensions arm) will use Claude across 22M+ policyholders. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic is locking up the systems-integrator channel that decides what regulated enterprises actually deploy. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/tcs-anthropic-partnership](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tcs-anthropic-partnership)

**Google ships Gemini 3.5 Flash and Gemini Omni at I/O 2026** — Google introduced the Gemini 3.5 family ("frontier intelligence with action"), leading with Gemini 3.5 Flash, which Google says rivals flagship models at Flash speeds and outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic benchmarks; Gemini 3.5 Pro was slated to follow in June. Google also released Gemini Omni (any-input generation, starting with video) and previewed Gemini Spark, a 24/7 background personal agent that connects to Google tools and third parties via MCP. _Why it matters:_ Google is pushing native agentic action and multimodal generation into the consumer Gemini stack and adopting MCP for third-party reach. [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/)

**SAP enforces API lockdown blocking external AI agents (June 9)** — SAP began enforcing new API restrictions on June 9 via a security patch that technically blocks noncompliant ODP-via-RFC calls; the policy forbids autonomous/generative AI systems from planning or executing API-call sequences outside SAP-endorsed pathways — putting Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce, ServiceNow AI and third-party integrators formally out of bounds except through SAP's mediating layer. Salesforce countered with "Headless 360," an all-API/MCP/CLI architecture with no mandatory proprietary AI gateway. _Why it matters:_ The enterprise-agent interoperability fight is now an explicit platform-control battle — SAP gatekeeping vs. Salesforce/ServiceNow openness. [https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141323/sap-blocks-external-ai-agents-salesforce-and-servicenow-dont/](https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141323/sap-blocks-external-ai-agents-salesforce-and-servicenow-dont/)

**Microsoft 365 Copilot Wave 3 — Cowork, Agent Mode, Agent 365 with Claude** — Microsoft's Wave 3 turns Copilot into a broader agentic work platform: Copilot Cowork (long-running, multi-step delegated work, built in close collaboration with Anthropic), Agent Mode, and the GA of Agent 365, plus a new Microsoft 365 E7 "Frontier Suite." Claude Opus 4.7 is now selectable inside M365 Copilot alongside OpenAI models. _Why it matters:_ Microsoft is making multi-model (incl. Anthropic) and long-running agents native to the enterprise productivity suite millions already pay for. [https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/in-case-you-missed-it-frontier-transformation-and-wave-3-of-microsoft-365-copilo/4504765](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/in-case-you-missed-it-frontier-transformation-and-wave-3-of-microsoft-365-copilo/4504765)

**SpaceX (incl. xAI) prices record $1.75T IPO; trading opens June 12** — SpaceX priced its IPO on June 11 at $135/share, opening on Nasdaq under SPCX on June 12 — ~555.56M Class A shares, ~$75B in proceeds, a ~$1.75T market cap, the largest IPO in history. The xAI segment (Colossus, 220,000+ NVIDIA GPUs) was absorbed via the February 2026 merger. FY2025 revenue $18.5B (+33%), GAAP net loss ~$4.9B driven by xAI infra and Starship. _Why it matters:_ The largest-ever IPO bundles a frontier-AI lab inside SpaceX, setting a new public-market benchmark for AI infrastructure value. [https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261957856-spacex-ipo-at-135-what-the-s-1-financials-actually-say-tradingkey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261957856-spacex-ipo-at-135-what-the-s-1-financials-actually-say-tradingkey)

**OpenAI and Anthropic both file confidential draft S-1s with the SEC** — OpenAI confirmed June 10 it confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC; Anthropic separately confirmed its own confidential draft S-1. Context: Anthropic is now the most valuable standalone AI startup at ~$965B post-money after a $65B Series H closed late May (revenue reportedly jumping from $14B ARR in Feb to $47B run-rate by late May); OpenAI's last private round valued it at $852B. _Why it matters:_ The two frontier labs are simultaneously moving toward public listings — a structural shift in how AI gets capitalized and disclosed. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec)

**EU AI Act high-risk obligations take effect August 2; Connecticut signs AI law** — With the EU AI Act's Annex III high-risk obligations (biometric ID, critical infrastructure, employment, essential services, law enforcement) binding from August 2, 2026, governance teams are in final-prep mode aligning EU AI Act + NIST AI RMF + ISO 42001 into single control sets. On June 2, Connecticut Governor Lamont signed Public Act 26-15, becoming the second state that week to impose specific legal requirements on AI companion apps. _Why it matters:_ The hard EU compliance deadline is now under two months out, and US state-level AI law continues to fragment. [https://techjacksolutions.com/eu-ai-act-news/june-2026/](https://techjacksolutions.com/eu-ai-act-news/june-2026/)
