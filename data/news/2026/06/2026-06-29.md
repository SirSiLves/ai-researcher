## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI previews the GPT-5.6 family (Sol / Terra / Luna)** — OpenAI unveiled a limited preview of GPT-5.6 on June 26: Sol (flagship for hard problems), Terra (balanced, ~2x cheaper than GPT-5.5 at comparable performance), and Luna (fast, lowest-cost tier). It ships with OpenAI's "most robust safety stack to date" and is now subject to Washington's new frontier-AI review process; general availability is expected "in the coming weeks." _Why it matters:_ The biggest architecture shift since GPT-5, and the first OpenAI flagship gated behind a US government frontier-model review. [https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/]

**US Commerce Department partially restores Anthropic's Mythos 5 / Fable 5** — After a June 12 export-control directive (signed by Commerce Secretary Lutnick, citing a reported jailbreak path enabling cyber-misuse) suspended all foreign-national access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5, Commerce on June 26 greenlit release of Mythos 5 to roughly 100 "trusted" companies and federal agencies. _Why it matters:_ A live demonstration that a government directive can take a frontier model offline in 24 hours — a structural new variable for any enterprise standardizing on a single model. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing] (coverage: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/27/commerce-anthropic-mythos-restrictions-lift))

**Qualcomm reportedly in advanced talks to acquire Tenstorrent for $8–10B** — Qualcomm is in reported negotiations to buy Jim Keller's RISC-V AI-chip startup Tenstorrent at an $8–10B valuation; no formal announcement landed at Qualcomm's June 24 Investor Day. Separately, Qualcomm confirmed an all-stock ~$3.9B acquisition of Modular (Mojo language / MAX inference engine). _Why it matters:_ Qualcomm is assembling an open, cross-vendor inference stack as a direct challenge to NVIDIA's CUDA moat. [https://www.qualcomm.com/news] (coverage: [Yahoo Finance/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/qualcomm-talks-acquire-ai-chip-230401789.html))

**Mistral ships OCR 4, tops OlmOCRBench** — Mistral released OCR 4 on June 23 with bounding boxes, block classification, and inline confidence scores. Independent annotators preferred it over every leading OCR/document-AI system tested (avg 72% win rate) and it took the top overall score on OlmOCRBench (85.20), covering 170 languages and running fully self-hosted in a single container. _Why it matters:_ A strong, self-hostable document-ingestion layer for enterprise RAG and retrieval pipelines. [https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/]

**ServiceNow opens its action layer to every enterprise AI agent via GA MCP Server** — ServiceNow introduced "Otto" (unified conversational AI + autonomous workflows + enterprise search) and made its Action Fabric / MCP Server generally available, letting any external AI agent invoke governed enterprise actions headlessly. _Why it matters:_ MCP is graduating from dev-tool plumbing to a governed enterprise control plane for cross-system agent action. [https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx]

**Cognition raises $1B at a $25B pre-money valuation** — The maker of the Devin autonomous software engineer raised over $1B at $25B pre- ($26B post-) money — roughly a 2.5x jump from its $10.2B post-money round eight months earlier. _Why it matters:_ Capital is concentrating hard in autonomous coding agents, the most commercially proven agentic category. [https://cognition.ai/blog] (coverage: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-1b-at-25b-pre-money-valuation/))

**MCP June spec adds server-as-agent / recursive composition; both MCP and A2A now under the Linux Foundation** — The June MCP spec release adds "server-as-agent" capability (MCP servers connecting to other MCP servers for recursive composition). MCP — now governed by the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation with 18,000+ indexed servers — and Google's A2A (also donated to the Linux Foundation) have reportedly committed to a joint interoperability spec. _Why it matters:_ The two-layer "MCP for tools, A2A for agent-to-agent" stack is hardening into the enterprise default, now under neutral governance. [https://zylos.ai/research/2026-03-26-agent-interoperability-protocols-mcp-a2a-acp-convergence/]

**EU AI Act high-risk deadlines slip after Parliament passes the digital omnibus** — On June 16 the European Parliament approved the digital omnibus (423–57, 174 abstentions), moving Annex III stand-alone high-risk obligations from Aug 2026 to Dec 2027 and Annex I embedded high-risk from Aug 2027 to Aug 2028, while preserving the risk-based architecture. _Why it matters:_ Compliance teams get materially more runway, but the core obligations and August 2026 enforcement baseline remain. [https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/news/]

**Reflection AI raises ~$2B at an ~$8B valuation** — The DeepMind-alumni startup focused on autonomous software development and large-scale model workflows closed a ~$2B round at ~$8B, on the heels of a reported $6.3B SpaceX/Colossus compute deal. _Why it matters:_ Another nine-figure-plus bet on agentic coding/automation infrastructure, paired with a major compute commitment. [https://reflection.ai/blog]
