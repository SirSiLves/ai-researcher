## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic opens Seoul office and lands Korea's largest AI deployments** — Anthropic opened its third Asia-Pacific office (after Tokyo and Bengaluru), led by KiYoung Choi, alongside an MOU with Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT on AI safety and cybersecurity (Korean-language model safety evals with the Korea AI Safety Institute, cyber-threat info sharing) and a research tie-up with the National AI Research Lab consortium (KAIST, Korea University, Yonsei, POSTECH). Launch enterprise deployments include NAVER (Claude Code org-wide), Samsung SDS (Claude Cowork + Claude Code across Samsung Electronics), LG CNS, Nexon, and Hanwha (Claude via AWS Bedrock with in-region controls). _Why it matters:_ A major land-grab in a key APAC market just as Anthropic edges toward IPO — and it expands sovereign-grade Claude footprint days after the US export-control episode that pulled Fable 5/Mythos 5. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/seoul-office-partnerships-korean-ai-ecosystem]

**OpenAI releases LifeSciBench — and the best model passes only 36%** — OpenAI published LifeSciBench, a 750-task, expert-authored benchmark built with 173 PhD-level biotech/pharma scientists, spanning seven life-science workflows (evidence handling, design/optimization, scientific reasoning, validation, translation, communication) with 1,062 attached artifacts (figures, sequence files, structures). The strongest model scores just 36.1%; GPT-Rosalind (now built on GPT-5.5's agentic coding) leads but absolute pass rates stay modest (21.6% on hardest genomics to 63.2% on wet-lab protocol support). _Why it matters:_ A rigorous, externally-judged reality check on AI-for-science hype — the kind of grounded eval enterprises and regulators increasingly want before trusting agents with high-stakes scientific work. [https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/]

**At the G7, Anthropic and DeepMind CEOs call for a US-led AI coalition** — In a closed-door G7 session with heads of state (including President Trump), Dario Amodei and Demis Hassabis pressed for a US-led coalition to set rules and standards for advanced AI; Amodei flagged structured access to frontier models and chip/component trade that excludes China, plus cooperation on cyber/bio/intelligence risks. Sam Altman pitched an international forum for globally accepted testing standards and impartial capability/risk analysis. Canadian PM Mark Carney agreed the US could lead; no binding commitments emerged. _Why it matters:_ Frontier-lab leaders are now actively shaping the geopolitics of AI governance at the highest table — a coalition framing that dovetails with the week's US export-control actions. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/anthropic-amodei-google-hassabis-us-ai-coalition-g7.html]

**Microsoft ships Work IQ APIs to GA — 10 generic MCP tools as the agent gateway to Microsoft 365** — Generally available June 16, Work IQ builds a continuously-updated semantic understanding of an organization from email, calendar, meetings, chats, files, people and line-of-business systems, and exposes it to agents as just 10 generic tools via Model Context Protocol (with progressive disclosure) instead of hundreds of data-specific tools. It underpins Microsoft's new "Autopilots" category and the flagship Scout always-on agent, which carries its own governed Entra identity. _Why it matters:_ Collapsing M365 access to a handful of MCP tools is a concrete bet that the context/semantic layer — not tool sprawl — is the real bottleneck for enterprise agents. [https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/02/announcing-the-new-work-iq-apis/]

**ServiceNow, SAP and Workday turn agent access into a metered toll** — As external AI agents erode two decades of per-seat pricing, the big enterprise platforms are erecting paid gateways: ServiceNow's Action Fabric (Anthropic's Claude as launch partner) charges per agent operation; SAP routes agents through its Business Accelerator Hub to reach business rules, also metered; Workday's CEO has framed agent-access charging as significant financial upside. _Why it matters:_ How incumbents monetize — and gate — third-party agents touching their data will shape the economics of the entire agentic-enterprise market. [https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/servicenow-sap-and-workday-make-ai-agents-pay-to-play/]

## Other notable items

- **[Linux Foundation's DNS-AID: agent discovery over plain DNS](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-dns-aid-project-to-advance-decentralized-ai-agent-discovery)** — Infoblox-led project (announced late May; first production system June 3) lets agents/MCP servers be published, discovered and verified via RFC 9460 SVCB records — a decentralized alternative to centralized registries; backers include Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Equinix.
- **[Anthropic expands Project Glasswing to ~150 orgs in 15+ countries](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing)** — Critical-infrastructure (power, water, healthcare, comms) vulnerability hunting with Claude Mythos Preview; ~50 partners had already found 10,000+ high/critical-severity flaws.
