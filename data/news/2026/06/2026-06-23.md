## Major announcements & releases

**Gartner: AI-agent software spend to hit $206.5B in 2026, +139% YoY.** Gartner's latest forecast pegs worldwide AI-agent software spending at ~$206.5B for 2026 (up from $86.4B in 2025), rising to $376.3B in 2027 — the fastest-growing slice of enterprise software. It sits inside Gartner's broader $2.59T total-AI-spend forecast for the year. _Why it matters:_ Hard budget numbers from the analyst CIOs actually quote — the agent line item is now real money, not a pilot. [https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-19-gartner-forecasts-worldwide-ai-spending-to-grow-47-percent-in-2026]

**Cognizant wires ServiceNow AI Agents into its Neuro multi-agent orchestration layer over MCP.** Cognizant's Neuro AI Multi-Agent Accelerator can now discover and invoke ServiceNow AI Agents directly through the Model Context Protocol — no custom connectors, with ServiceNow's existing governance, access controls and audit logging preserved. The accelerator is open source (github.com/cognizant-ai-lab/neuro-san-studio) and model/hyperscaler-agnostic. _Why it matters:_ A concrete production example of MCP as the cross-vendor agent interop substrate, not just a tools-to-model connector. [https://news.cognizant.com/2026-06-18-Cognizant-expands-cross-platform-agentic-AI-with-new-ServiceNow-AI-Agent-interoperability]

**ServiceNow vs SAP split over external AI agents hardens.** Reporting this week underscores the divide: ServiceNow and Salesforce are going "headless" — opening their platforms as the execution layer to any agent (Claude, Copilot, homegrown) via Action Fabric — while SAP blocks external AI agents from its APIs. SAP, ServiceNow and Workday are also moving to meter/charge for agent access. _Why it matters:_ The control-point war over who owns the agent-orchestration layer in the enterprise stack is now the defining platform fight of 2026. [https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141323/sap-blocks-external-ai-agents-salesforce-and-servicenow-dont/]

**Google ships Gemini 3.5 Flash and cuts subscription prices.** Google opened the Gemini 3.5 series with 3.5 Flash, positioned as its strongest agentic and coding model yet, claiming it outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on key benchmarks at Flash-tier speed. Alongside the launch, Google cut the Ultra subscription from $250 to $200/month and introduced a $100/month Developer tier. _Why it matters:_ Frontier-grade agentic capability at Flash pricing keeps the cost-per-capability race accelerating downward. [https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/innovations-from-google-io-26-on-google-cloud]

**Mistral folds Le Chat into "Vibe," one agent for work and code.** Mistral rebranded Le Chat as Vibe — a single agent and license spanning a Work Mode for long-running tasks and a Code Mode with remote coding agents, a VS Code extension and CLR updates. It also added Emmi AI's physics simulation to its enterprise platform for industrial/engineering digital twins. _Why it matters:_ Mistral is consolidating its consumer and developer surfaces into one agentic product to compete with ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini's unified agents. [https://mistral.ai/news/]

**NVIDIA's enterprise agent push: Agent Toolkit, NemoClaw and Nemotron 3 Ultra.** NVIDIA's June enterprise wave includes the Agent Toolkit for secure long-running "digital coworker" agents, NemoClaw (Cadence, Dassault, Siemens, Synopsys building autonomous engineering agents), and Nemotron 3 Ultra — a smaller open model claiming 5x faster inference and up to 30% lower cost for agentic tasks. _Why it matters:_ NVIDIA is moving up the stack from silicon into the agent-software and open-model layer, courting the EDA/industrial verticals directly. [https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/Enterprise-Software-Leaders-Build-AI-Agents-With-NVIDIA/default.aspx]

**EU AI Act "digital omnibus" delays high-risk obligations after Parliament vote.** Following the European Parliament's June 16 approval (423–57, 174 abstentions), stand-alone Annex III high-risk obligations slip from Aug 2, 2026 to Dec 2, 2027, and Annex I embedded systems to Aug 2028; provider watermarking moves to Dec 2, 2026. The omnibus also adds bans on AI-generated CSAM and non-consensual intimate imagery. _Why it matters:_ A material softening of the AI Act's near-term enforcement timeline — compliance teams get breathing room, but transparency obligations still bite from August. [https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/]

**Zensar launches ZenseAI.AgentMesh for enterprise agent governance at scale.** Zensar's new agentic platform helps organizations discover, build, deploy and govern autonomous agents, shipping with open connectors across SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Snowflake and Databricks. _Why it matters:_ Another systems-integrator-built "agent mesh" — evidence that the SI layer (Cognizant, Zensar, Accenture) is racing to own enterprise agent governance and integration. [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zensar-technologies-launches-zenseaiagentmesh-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-adoption-at-scale-302805437.html]
