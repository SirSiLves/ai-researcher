## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna) in a government-gated limited release** — On June 26 OpenAI began a restricted preview of its GPT-5.6 family: Sol (flagship, with agentic gains in coding, biology and cybersecurity), Terra (balanced, ~2x cheaper than GPT-5.5), and Luna (low-cost). New "max reasoning effort" and an "ultra" multi-subagent mode debut here. Access is limited to ~20 US-government-approved partners, with broader GA "in the coming weeks." _Why it matters:_ A frontier model launched under explicit government access gating — a first that signals tighter coupling of frontier capability and national-security review. [https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/] (coverage: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov))

**Z.AI ships GLM-5.2 open-weight flagship** — GLM-5.2 (753B total / 40B active, 1M context, up from 200K in 5.1) posts 62.1 on SWE-Bench Pro — above GPT-5.5's 58.6 — and 81.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, making it one of the strongest open-weight coding/agentic models to date. _Why it matters:_ Chinese open-weight labs continue closing the coding/agentic gap with US frontier labs, and an MIT-class open release at this tier pressures both pricing and the closed-vs-open debate. [https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates]

**Qualcomm in advanced talks to acquire Tenstorrent for $8–10B** — Qualcomm is reportedly negotiating to buy Jim Keller's RISC-V-based AI chip startup Tenstorrent, which would hand Qualcomm a credible data-center AI silicon position against NVIDIA and AMD. _Why it matters:_ A major consolidation move in AI accelerators built on the open RISC-V standard — a bet on an alternative to the CUDA/NVIDIA stack at the hardware layer. [https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates]

**NAVER and NVIDIA expand sovereign-AI buildout to gigawatt scale** — NAVER will scale its sovereign AI infrastructure on NVIDIA's DSX platform, starting at 55 MW at its GAK Sejong data center with plans to reach gigawatt capacity, powering next-gen HyperCLOVA X models and a Korea AI Agent Platform launching H2 2026. _Why it matters:_ Sovereign-AI compute deals at gigawatt scale are becoming the template for how nations secure frontier-model capacity outside the US hyperscalers. [https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates]

**Anthropic extends Claude Mythos vulnerability-hunting preview to ~150 orgs across 15+ countries** — Anthropic widened access to Mythos (a model noted for finding software vulnerabilities unknown even to vendors). Japan became the first Asian entrant: the government and three megabanks (MUFG, Mizuho, SMBC) gained access, with a 36-entity public-private working group formed around the model's cybersecurity implications. _Why it matters:_ Frontier models are now being deployed as national cyber-defense infrastructure, and a dedicated government working group around a single model is a new governance pattern. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing] (coverage: [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/finance/japan-megabanks-to-gain-access-to-anthropic-s-powerful-ai-model-mythos))

**EU Parliament endorses the Digital Omnibus, deferring high-risk AI Act deadlines** — On June 16 the European Parliament formally backed the simplification deal: Annex III high-risk obligations slip from Aug 2026 to Dec 2027, synthetic-content marking from Aug 2026 to Dec 2026, plus new prohibitions on AI-generated non-consensual intimate material and CSAM (effective Dec 2026). Formal Council adoption is expected July 2026. _Why it matters:_ The single most consequential AI regulation just bought industry 16 more months on its hardest obligations — directly reshaping enterprise compliance timelines. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/]

**SAP unifies its AI stack into one Business AI Platform plus an Autonomous Suite** — SAP folded BTP, Business Data Cloud and Business AI into a single SAP Business AI Platform, and layered on the SAP Autonomous Suite that adds AI agents across existing SAP apps to automate end-to-end processes. _Why it matters:_ The dominant enterprise ERP vendor is repositioning around an agentic control plane — the layer most large enterprises will actually deploy agents through. [https://www.ciodive.com/news/sap-creates-single-platform-enterprise-ai/820015/]

**ServiceNow opens its full system of action to any AI agent via Action Fabric** — At Knowledge 2026 ServiceNow launched Action Fabric, exposing its platform's governed enterprise actions to any agent — ServiceNow-native, Claude, Copilot or homegrown — headlessly through its now-GA MCP Server. _Why it matters:_ A major workflow platform betting on agent interoperability (via MCP) rather than lock-in — concrete enterprise validation of the protocol layer. [https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx]

**Mistral OCR 4 ships with bounding boxes and confidence scoring** — Released June 23, Mistral OCR 4 adds bounding boxes, block classification and inline per-token confidence scores alongside extracted text, targeting document-intelligence pipelines. _Why it matters:_ Document parsing remains the unglamorous bottleneck of enterprise RAG; SOTA OCR with confidence signals materially improves retrieval-grounding quality. [https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/]
