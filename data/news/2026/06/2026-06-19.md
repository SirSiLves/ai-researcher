## Major announcements & releases

**xAI's Grok 4.3 lands on Amazon Bedrock — frontier reasoning model joins AWS's catalog** — AWS made Grok 4.3 generally available on Amazon Bedrock (announced June 16, propagating through June 18), marking xAI's first appearance as a Bedrock model provider. Grok 4.3 is a reasoning-first model with a 1M-token context window, configurable reasoning effort (none/low/medium/high), strong tool-use and structured-output support for agents, and what xAI claims is the lowest hallucination rate among frontier models. It runs on Mantle, a new price-performance inference engine in Bedrock. _Why it matters:_ Putting Grok inside Bedrock's governed enterprise plane — alongside Anthropic, Meta and others — is a real distribution win for xAI and another sign that the model layer is consolidating onto the hyperscaler procurement rails enterprises already trust. [https://x.ai/news/grok-amazon-bedrock] (AWS: https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/06/grok-amazon-bedrock/)

**Cognizant wires ServiceNow AI Agents into its Neuro orchestration layer over MCP** — Cognizant announced (June 18) that ServiceNow AI Agents now interoperate with its Neuro AI Multi-Agent Accelerator, letting enterprises discover and invoke ServiceNow-native agents alongside custom and third-party agents from a single orchestration layer. The integration runs over the Model Context Protocol — the open standard ServiceNow supports — so Neuro can call ServiceNow agents without bespoke connectors. The accelerator is open source (github.com/cognizant-ai-lab/neuro-san-studio) and model/hyperscaler-agnostic. _Why it matters:_ A concrete, shipping example of MCP doing the cross-vendor agent-interop job it was designed for — and of systems integrators (not just platforms) positioning themselves as the neutral orchestration control plane for the multi-agent enterprise. [https://news.cognizant.com/2026-06-18-Cognizant-expands-cross-platform-agentic-AI-with-new-ServiceNow-AI-Agent-interoperability]

**HPE Discover: AI Factory with NVIDIA gets a Vera CPU, Agent Toolkit and confidential computing for agentic production** — At HPE Discover Las Vegas (June 16, propagating this week), HPE added the NVIDIA Vera CPU (purpose-built for agent orchestration, tool execution and real-time data), the NVIDIA Agent Toolkit (to manage autonomous multi-agent systems), and NVIDIA Confidential Computing across its AI Factory line, plus Vera Rubin NVL72 rack-scale systems. The pitch is moving agentic AI into production with hardware-rooted security, governance and sovereignty; new Private Cloud AI features land July 2026. _Why it matters:_ The infra layer is now being reshaped specifically for agents — a dedicated "CPU for agents" and confidential computing signal that orchestration overhead and data protection, not raw model FLOPs, are the next enterprise bottleneck. [https://www.hpe.com/us/en/newsroom/press-release/2026/06/hpe-brings-agentic-ai-into-production-with-nvidia-delivering-security-governance-scale-and-sovereignty.html] (NVIDIA: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/hpe-ai-factory-agentic-enterprise/)

**Anthropic's Fable 5 / Mythos 5 stay dark a week into the US export-control freeze** — A week after the US government's June 12 export-control directive ordering Anthropic to block Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for all foreign nationals (which Anthropic met by disabling the two models for everyone), both frontier models remain suspended as of June 19 with no restoration announced — despite executives signaling access "could return within days." Anthropic maintains the cited jailbreak (reading a codebase to find vulnerabilities) is narrow and already present in public models, and says it has not received detailed written justification. Opus 4.8 and other models stay available. _Why it matters:_ The first sustained case of a government pulling a frontier model on national-security grounds is now a multi-week reality, not a one-day event — a live precedent for how export controls bite on cloud-served AI and a real planning problem for security teams that built on those models. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access] (Al Jazeera: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/13/us-orders-anthropic-to-disable-ai-models-for-all-foreign-nationals)

**EU AI Act enforcement clock now under seven weeks out** — Compliance guidance updated through June 2026 confirms the European Commission's enforcement powers fully activate on 2 August 2026 (investigations, orders, fines up to €35M or 7% of global turnover), with the GPAI Code of Practice as the reference compliance tool. The provisional Digital Omnibus deal (7 May 2026) pushes the heaviest high-risk obligations to fixed dates — 2 December 2027 for standalone Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for product-embedded Annex I systems — easing near-term load while keeping the August transparency/GPAI deadline intact. _Why it matters:_ The single most consequential governance deadline of the year is now imminent; enterprises deploying GPAI in the EU have weeks, not quarters, to align with Code-of-Practice expectations. [https://www.surecloud.com/resource-hub/eu-ai-act-complete-compliance-guide]
