## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI ships GPT-5.6 family to general availability and launches ChatGPT Work.** On July 9, OpenAI made the GPT-5.6 line — Sol (frontier), Terra (balanced), Luna (high-volume/low-cost) — generally available across ChatGPT, the API, and Codex after a late-June trusted-partner preview, and merged Codex and ChatGPT into a single app. Alongside it, ChatGPT Work (powered by GPT-5.6) pulls context across connected apps and files to draft documents, spreadsheets, and decks. _Why it matters:_ this was the anchor of a historic 48 hours in which OpenAI, xAI, and Anthropic all shipped frontier updates — GPT-5.6 resets the price/perf frontier and pushes OpenAI squarely into enterprise "work" territory. [https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/) (context: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/09/ai-openai-gpt-release))

**xAI/SpaceXAI releases Grok 4.5, an "Opus-class" coding-and-agent model.** Launched July 8 (public rollout July 9), Grok 4.5 is xAI's first model built specifically for coding and long-horizon agentic work, trained partly on real Cursor session data. It lands at $2/$6 per million tokens — ~60% cheaper than Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 — and is far more token-efficient (~14K output tokens/task vs ~67K for Opus 4.8), placing #4 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 54. _Why it matters:_ price/efficiency, not raw benchmark wins, is the pitch — a direct shot at the cost of frontier coding agents. [https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5) (context: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-releases-grok-4-5-which-elon-describes-as-an-opus-class-model/))

**Meituan open-sources LongCat-2.0, a 1.6T MoE trained entirely on Chinese chips.** Announced ~July 5–6 under an MIT license on Hugging Face, LongCat-2.0 (1.6T total / ~48B active) offers a native 1M-token context, targets agentic coding, and reportedly edges GPT-5.5 on SWE-bench Pro. Meituan says it is the first trillion-parameter model fully trained and served on a domestic Chinese compute cluster (35T+ pretraining tokens). _Why it matters:_ a near-frontier open-weights model built without NVIDIA hardware is both a supply-chain and an open-source milestone. [https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-2.0](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-2.0) (context: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meituan-open-sources-longcat-2-0-the-1-6t-near-frontier-agentic-coding-model-thats-been-leading-openrouter-trained-entirely-on-chinese-chips))

**Salesforce ships its biggest Agentforce Commerce release yet.** On July 6 Salesforce made three commerce agents generally available — Shopper Agent, Buyer Agent, and Merchant Agent — spanning B2C, B2B, Order Management, and POS, with native integration into ChatGPT and Google Search/Gemini arriving in coming months. Salesforce claims retailers running shopper agents grew sales 59% faster. _Why it matters:_ agentic commerce that plugs directly into third-party assistant surfaces is a concrete enterprise deployment pattern, not a demo. [https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-commerce-announcement/](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-commerce-announcement/)

**SAP launches managed Joule Studio for enterprise-scale agent development.** SAP's fully managed Joule Studio lets enterprises build and govern the full lifecycle of AI agents grounded in live SAP business data, with pro-code support for LangChain, Pydantic AI, LlamaIndex and AutoGen, an embedded n8n environment for visual multi-agent orchestration, and Cursor/Claude Code integration. Free design-time access runs through end of 2026. _Why it matters:_ SAP is positioning Joule as an open orchestration layer over frontier models — and baking in the n8n partnership it invested in. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/new-joule-studio-enterprise-scale-agentic-development/](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/new-joule-studio-enterprise-scale-agentic-development/) (context: [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/sap-joule-studio-managed-agents/))

**Snowflake pushes Cortex Code and Snowflake Intelligence as the "control plane for the agentic enterprise."** Snowflake expanded Cortex Code (its governed AI coding agent, usable in Snowsight, VS Code, or Cursor) and Snowflake Intelligence — rebranded internally CoCo and CoWork — with native MCP support and a new Cortex Sense metadata layer (private preview mid-July) to ground enterprise agents. _Why it matters:_ the data-warehouse vendors are racing to own agent governance and context, with MCP as the connective standard. [https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-expands-snowflake-intelligence-and-cortex-code-to-power-the-control-plane-for-the-agentic-enterprise/](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-expands-snowflake-intelligence-and-cortex-code-to-power-the-control-plane-for-the-agentic-enterprise/)

**Anthropic brings Claude Cowork to web and mobile with background agent execution.** Announced July 7, Cowork now runs sessions remotely so work and files follow you across devices; scheduled tasks execute with no device online, and chat + Cowork share one home on web/desktop. Anthropic notes >90% of Cowork usage is non-coding (business ops, content), and doubled usage limits through Aug 5. _Why it matters:_ the coding-agent wars are spilling into general office work — background, cross-device agents are becoming a mainstream productivity surface. [https://claude.com/blog/cowork-web-mobile](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-web-mobile) (context: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/07/the-coding-agent-wars-are-spilling-into-the-rest-of-the-office-claude-cowork/))

**ServiceNow opens its full "system of action" to any AI agent.** ServiceNow made its MCP Server generally available (included in every Now Assist / AI Native SKU) and opened its platform so external agents — built on Claude, Copilot, or a customer's own stack — can act across ServiceNow, extending its Autonomous Workforce of AI specialists. _Why it matters:_ a major workflow platform betting on interoperability (MCP) rather than a walled agent garden. [https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx)

**EU AI Act enforcement clock hits three weeks: GPAI obligations bite August 2.** With the August 2, 2026 milestone approaching, the European Commission gains enforcement powers over general-purpose AI providers (information requests, model access, recalls; fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover), and high-risk-system obligations plus GPAI transparency rules take effect. The GPAI Code of Practice (Transparency, Copyright, Safety & Security) remains the main compliance on-ramp. _Why it matters:_ the EU's grace period is ending — this is the point where the AI Act shifts from paperwork to enforceable obligations for every frontier provider serving the EU. [https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/code-of-practice-overview/](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/code-of-practice-overview/) (context: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai))

**Together AI raises $800M Series C at an $8.3B valuation.** The AI cloud/inference platform's round (led by Aramco Ventures, with Nvidia, Salesforce Ventures, General Catalyst, Vista and others) more than doubles its early-2025 valuation, part of a record $510B in H1 2026 global VC dominated by AI infra. _Why it matters:_ capital keeps concentrating in the serving/infra layer that powers the agent boom. [https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/)
