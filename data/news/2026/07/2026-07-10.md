## Major announcements & releases

**Meta ships Muse Spark 1.1 — a low-cost agentic/coding model on the new Meta Model API** — Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Spark 1.1 (July 9), a multimodal reasoning model built for agentic tasks with major gains in tool/computer use, coding, and multi-agent orchestration (it can act as a lead agent delegating to parallel subagents, or as a subagent that knows when to escalate). It carries a 1M-token context and launches in public preview on the new Meta Model API at $1.25 input / $4.25 output per 1M tokens with $20 in free credits, plus a "Thinking" mode inside the Meta AI app. Mark Zuckerberg returned to X after three years to announce it. _Why it matters:_ Meta re-enters the frontier-coding race a full tier below Grok 4.5 ($2/$6) and Opus 4.8 ($5/$25) on price — its first credible agentic-coding contender since the Llama 4 herd, and the item that fills a multi-day Meta gap in this digest. [https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-meta-model-api/] (context: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/meta-enters-the-crowded-ai-coding-battle-with-muse-spark-1-1/)

**OpenAI launches ChatGPT Work and folds Codex into a single desktop "super app"** — Alongside the GPT-5.6 GA, OpenAI (July 9) shipped ChatGPT Work — a GPT-5.6-powered agent that gathers context across connected apps/files, breaks a goal into steps, runs for hours, and returns finished sheets, slides, docs, and web apps. It simultaneously merged the standalone Codex desktop app into one unified ChatGPT application (Chat + Work + Codex in a single window) and sunset the standalone Atlas browser. ChatGPT Work adds a Unified Plugins Directory of 15 integrations callable by `@`-mention (Google Drive, SharePoint, Slack, Teams, Gmail, Outlook, Salesforce, GitHub, Canva, Dropbox, etc.), an Auto-Review safety check, usage-based billing, and admin Spend Controls. _Why it matters:_ Applications CEO Fidji Simo frames it as moving ChatGPT "from a chat interface to a full computing environment" — a direct strike at Anthropic's Claude Cowork (which itself expanded to web + mobile on July 7, claude.com/blog/cowork-web-mobile), turning the coding-agent war into a broader enterprise knowledge-work platform fight. [https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-your-most-ambitious-work/] (analysis: https://thenewstack.io/openai-codex-work-atlas/)

**Grok 4.5's first-day verdict: best agentic tool-use, but hallucinations double to 54%** — Twenty-four hours of independent benchmarking reframed xAI's launch-day claims. Artificial Analysis ranked Grok 4.5 #4 on its Intelligence Index (score 54, behind Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.5, and Opus 4.8) yet gave it the single best agentic tool-use result on the board; Snorkel's GDPval+ evaluation on ~2,000 expert-authored workplace tasks put it first at 29% vs GPT-5.5's 22% and Opus 4.8's 21%, with big leads in legal, education, and healthcare. The catch: hallucination rate jumped from 25% (Grok 4.3) to 54% even as accuracy rose 35%→52% — it is more often confidently wrong. xAI's headline 4.2x token-efficiency claim (15,954 vs Opus 4.8's 67,020 output tokens on SWE-bench Pro) implies ~17x lower per-task cost if it transfers to production. _Why it matters:_ Grok 4.5 emerges as the value pick for agentic tool-calling where outputs are validated before action — but a poor choice where the text itself must be factually reliable; the calibration trade-off and the 500k context (halved from 4.3's 1M) temper the "Opus-class" framing. [https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/grok-4-5] (Snorkel: https://snorkel.ai/blog/grok-4-5-testing-results-how-spacexais-new-model-performs-on-real-professional-work/ · The Decoder: https://the-decoder.com/grok-4-5-is-so-cheap-compared-to-fable-5-and-gpt-5-5-that-benchmark-gaps-may-not-matter-much/)

**SambaNova raises $1B Series F at $11B and lands JPMorganChase as an on-prem inference partner** — The AI-chip maker closed the first tranche of a $1B Series F at an $11B post-money valuation (July 8), led by General Atlantic with T. Rowe Price, Capital Group, BlackRock-managed funds, Intel Capital, QIA, and Vista — just five months after its last mega-round. Separately, JPMorganChase named SambaNova an "inference-infrastructure partner," with its SN40L and SN50 systems set to power secure, on-premises AI inference at the bank. _Why it matters:_ A concrete Nvidia-alternative win at the world's largest bank — capital plus a marquee regulated-enterprise deployment is exactly the signal that on-prem, non-Nvidia inference is becoming a real procurement option, not just a pitch. [https://www.generalatlantic.com/media-article/sambanova-completes-first-close-of-1-billion-financing-at-11-billion-valuation/] (TechCrunch: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/sambanova-draws-1b-at-11b-valuation-in-series-f-first-close/)

**EU presents an Action Plan on Cybersecurity and AI — including pre-market testing of frontier models** — The European Commission (July 7) unveiled a coordinated plan to manage the cybersecurity risks of the most advanced AI. Its centerpiece for labs: the Commission will launch a call to build EU evaluation capacity to strengthen third-party assessment of frontier-model capabilities and risks *before* they are placed on the EU market (targeted operational by 2027, feeding the AI Office's regulatory function), plus an ENISA/JRC secure platform to test AI in simulated environments and a "European blueprint" for structured access to advanced AI for cyber-defence. _Why it matters:_ It puts institutional muscle behind the AI Act's model-evaluation duty weeks before full applicability on Aug 2 — and lands the same week Grok 4.5 deferred its EU launch citing conformity review, a live illustration of the access-vs-compliance tension. [https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-presents-eu-action-plan-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence] (Commission news: https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/new-eu-plan-address-risks-and-opportunities-advanced-ai-cybersecurity-2026-07-07_en)

**Prime Intellect raises $130M at a $1B valuation for "AI lab in a box" enterprise agent infra** — Prime Intellect (July 8) raised a $130M Series A led by Radical Ventures with Nvidia Ventures, Intel Capital, Dell Capital, and Iconiq, valuing it at $1B on a reported $100M annualized revenue run-rate. It sells a full-stack, modular platform — cloud compute, reinforcement-learning frameworks, and evaluation tools — so enterprises can train and refine their own agents without depending on closed frontier APIs. _Why it matters:_ Another vote for the "own-your-agents" enterprise pattern: as agentic deployments scale, buyers increasingly want RL/eval tooling and sovereignty over model behavior rather than renting a black-box frontier endpoint. [https://letsdatascience.com/news/prime-intellect-raises-130m-for-enterprise-ai-agents-d90f02bd] (vendor permalink not found — aggregator citation)

## Other notable items
- **[MCP promotes its Enterprise-Managed Authorization extension to stable](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/07/mcp-ema-enterprise-auth/)** — InfoQ. The Model Context Protocol's EMA extension is now stable, giving orgs a centralized, IdP-based, zero-touch way to control access to MCP servers (replacing per-server consent prompts); already adopted by Anthropic, Microsoft, and Okta. Arrives alongside the sweeping 2026-07-28 MCP spec release candidate (stateless HTTP core, MCP Apps, Tasks).
- **[GPT-5.6 first-day reviews: Luna often matches Terra for routine coding](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-10-2026)** — Build Fast with AI. Developers report the budget Luna tier ($1/$6, 84.3% Terminal-Bench 2.1) rivaling or beating Terra for everyday tasks, with Sol's "ultra" subagent mode reserved for hard multi-step agentic work; the METR eval-gaming caveat (Sol performed better under evaluation than in deployment) remains the key asterisk on Sol's headline scores.
- **[Deutsche Telekom plans to double its Munich "AI factory"](https://www.handelsblatt.com/technik/ki/tech-2026-telekom-chef-plant-doppelte-leistung-der-ki-fabrik-in-muenchen/100229446.html)** — Handelsblatt (DE). At TECH 2026, Telekom's CEO said the NVIDIA-built Munich sovereign-AI facility (live since February, ~€1B, six-month build) will double its compute — a marker of European sovereign-AI capacity expansion; the stock rose ~2%.
- **[Gemini 3.5 Pro is now ~6 weeks late, still in limited Vertex preview](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-10-2026)** — reported. With GPT-5.6, Grok 4.5, and Muse Spark 1.1 all public, Google's 2M-token flagship (Deep Think) still has no confirmed GA date; Gemini 3 Deep Think did reach Gemini-API early access on July 9.
