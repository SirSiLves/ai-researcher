## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic redeploys Fable 5 globally, adds Mythos 5 for vetted cyberdefense partners** — On July 1 Anthropic restored Fable 5 (claude-fable-5) across the Claude API, apps, and Claude Code after a June 12–30 suspension triggered by a US export-control directive, shipping it as a twin release with Mythos 5 (claude-mythos-5) — the same model with cyber safety classifiers lifted, restricted to vetted cyberdefense partners. Pricing is $10/$50 per M tokens with a 1M context window; affected users get 50% extra weekly limits through July 7. The same day, CAIS and Scale AI's updated Remote Labor Index showed Fable 5 leading all public models at 16.1% on 240 real remote-work projects. _Why it matters:_ The first frontier model to be switched off and back on by government directive — model deployment is now a national-security variable, not just a product decision. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5] (launch page: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5; discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48741853)

**Claude Sonnet 5: near-Opus agentic performance, default for Free and Pro** — Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, calling it its most agentic Sonnet yet — planning, browser/terminal tool use, and autonomous operation at a level that recently required larger models, approaching Opus 4.8 on agentic benchmarks. It is now the default model on Free and Pro plans, with introductory API pricing of $2/$10 per M tokens through August 31 (then $3/$15), and shows lower rates of undesirable agentic behavior than Sonnet 4.6. _Why it matters:_ Frontier-adjacent agent capability just dropped to commodity prices days before Anthropic's expected IPO — a direct squeeze on every mid-tier model. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5] (context: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/)

**Google ships Nano Banana 2 Lite GA and Gemini Omni Flash preview** — On June 30 Google made Nano Banana 2 Lite (gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image) generally available — text-to-image in ~4 seconds at $0.034 per 1K-res image — and put Gemini Omni Flash into public preview: video generation plus conversational editing (character/product swaps, style transfer, relighting) from text, image, and video inputs at $0.10 per second of output. Both are in the Gemini API/AI Studio and rolling out to Search AI Mode, the Gemini app, NotebookLM, Photos, and Flow. _Why it matters:_ Google is pricing frontier image and video generation at throwaway levels while wiring it into every consumer surface it owns. [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni-flash-nano-banana-2-lite/] (Cloud availability: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/nano-banana-2-lite-and-gemini-omni-flash-available/)

**Anthropic's enterprise platform wave: Claude Science, Claude Tag, apps gateway, Microsoft Foundry GA** — Alongside Sonnet 5, Anthropic shipped a burst of platform products June 29–30: Claude Science, a beta AI workbench for researchers with 60+ curated scientific skills/connectors and a citation-checking reviewer agent [https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench]; Claude Tag, which puts a multiplayer @Claude teammate into Slack channels (65% of Anthropic's product-team code now written via its internal version) [https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag]; the Claude apps gateway, a self-hosted control plane with SSO, policy enforcement, and per-user cost attribution across the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, and Google Cloud [https://claude.com/blog/introducing-the-claude-apps-gateway]; and Claude models GA in Microsoft Foundry — unified enterprise coverage across all three hyperscalers [https://claude.com/blog/claude-in-microsoft-foundry]. _Why it matters:_ Anthropic is assembling the enterprise control plane around its models, not just the models — a platform play aimed at IT departments, delivered in one week.

**Salesforce's pay-per-resolution Agentforce Help Agent heads to GA this month** — Salesforce's Agentforce Help Agent (announced June 25) deploys an autonomous service agent in minutes across voice, web, portal, and messaging, and charges a flat $2 only when the agent resolves an issue end-to-end — no charge if the customer escalates to a human or leaves unhappy. Salesforce says Agentforce has handled 4.3M inquiries on its own help site with a 70% resolution rate; Help Agent, the Customer Service Portal, and pay-per-resolution pricing reach general availability in July. _Why it matters:_ Outcome-based pricing is the sharpest answer yet to the "what does an agent actually cost" question — expect every enterprise-agent vendor to be forced into comparable terms. [https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-help-agent-announcement/] (analysis: https://www.salesforceben.com/huge-agentforce-pricing-shift-salesforce-introduces-pay-per-resolution/)

**NVIDIA launches revenue-sharing partner model for multi-tenant AI factories** — On July 1 NVIDIA announced a new partnership model under which AI clouds can procure NVIDIA infrastructure for AI-native, enterprise, and ISV customers with NVIDIA taking standard product revenue plus a share of cloud revenue on supported capacity, backed by credit support. The pitch: model builders, inference providers, and agent platforms get full-stack compute without waiting through site selection, power procurement, and hardware bring-up. _Why it matters:_ NVIDIA is moving from selling chips to underwriting and co-owning the AI-cloud business model — deepening dependence one layer up the stack. [https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-unlocks-ai-compute-at-scale-capital-partners-to-power-ai-infrastructure-buildout/]

**MCP's largest-ever revision hits release candidate ahead of July 28 spec** — The Model Context Protocol published the release candidate for its 2026-07-28 specification: a stateless core that scales on plain HTTP infrastructure (round-robin load balancers, Mcp-Method header routing, cacheable tools/list), extensions for server-rendered UIs (MCP Apps) and long-running work (Tasks), authorization aligned with OAuth/OIDC, and a formal deprecation policy. _Why it matters:_ The stateless core removes the biggest operational objection to running MCP servers at enterprise scale; implementers have under four weeks to review before the spec ships. [https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/]

**Cognition launches Devin Security Swarm** — On July 1 Cognition released Devin Security Swarm, which orchestrates a swarm of Devin sessions via an "agentic MapReduce" architecture to find vulnerabilities across large codebases, validate exploitability at runtime in sandboxes, and open remediation PRs. On a benchmark of 50 real-world GHSA vulnerabilities across 14 languages it found 36 — more than any other AI scanner tested, at 30% lower cost per finding, including three criticals every other tool missed. _Why it matters:_ Multi-agent swarms are crossing from demos into a production security workflow with measurable precision — and a credible answer to AI-accelerated offense. [https://cognition.com/blog/introducing-devin-security-swarm] (press release: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cognition-launches-devin-security-swarm-to-tackle-the-vulnerability-backlog-302814800.html)

**Germany's federal AI taskforce holds its constitutive meeting** — The German federal government stood up its KI-Taskforce this week (inaugural meeting Thursday, July 2), led by the Digital Ministry (BMDS) with one state secretary from every ministry, to inventory and coordinate scattered federal AI measures by October. Five working groups — Frontier AI, AI Security, AI Infrastructure, AI & Society, AI Application — must deliver results by end of September, feeding a "stringent AI structure" for the November Digital Summit under the banner of making Germany a "KI-Nation." _Why it matters:_ Europe's largest economy is centralizing AI policy execution weeks before EU AI Act GPAI enforcement powers go live on August 2. [https://www.heise.de/news/Bundesregierung-will-Taskforce-Deutschland-soll-KI-Nation-werden-11350190.html] (German: "Bundesregierung will Taskforce: Deutschland soll 'KI-Nation' werden"; start coverage: https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/ki-taskforce-startet-bundesregierung-buendelt-massnahmen-bis-herbst-923436)

**Meta's Brain2Qwerty v2 decodes typed sentences from non-invasive brain signals at 61% accuracy** — Meta AI released Brain2Qwerty v2 (June 29), an end-to-end MEG-based brain-to-text pipeline that decodes typed sentences in real time at 61% average word accuracy (78% for the best participant) versus ~8% for prior non-invasive methods, by fine-tuning LLMs on neural data from ~22,000 sentences across nine participants. Meta is releasing full training code for v1 and v2; partner BCBL is releasing the v1 dataset. _Why it matters:_ Surgery-free brain-to-text is approaching implant-grade accuracy, with the LLM doing the heavy lifting — and the stack is open. [https://ai.meta.com/blog/brain2qwerty-brain-ai-human-communication/] (context: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/30/meta-ai-releases-brain2qwerty-v2-a-non-invasive-meg-brain-to-text-pipeline-decoding-typed-sentences-at-61-word-accuracy/)
