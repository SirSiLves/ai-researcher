## Major announcements & releases

**White House accuses Moonshot of distilling Anthropic's Fable for Kimi K3; Treasury threatens sanctions** — White House OSTP Director Michael Kratsios stated publicly (on X, 22 Jul) that Moonshot AI ran "large-scale, covert industrial distillation" of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 to build its 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3, and separately alleged Moonshot trained on banned NVIDIA GB300 servers accessed via Thailand to sidestep export controls. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent followed with a threat of sanctions and export blacklisting; NVIDIA shares slid on the export-routing claim. The technical case leans on independent cross-entropy analysis (Ryan Greenblatt / Redwood Research) showing K3 self-identifies as Claude far more often than chance — suggestive but not conclusive, since Fable has only been public since 1 Jul and web-scraped Claude transcripts are a plausible innocent source.
_Why it matters:_ First time a senior US official has named a specific Chinese lab for copying a specific US model — it moves the US–China AI race from benchmarks into theft allegations and export-control enforcement, days before K3's weights go free (27 Jul). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/treasury-threatens-sanctions-after-white-house-claims-moonshot-distilled-anthropics-fable/) · [CNBC — GB300 via Thailand](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/moonshot-kimi-nvidia-ai-chips-export-ban.html) (context: [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/white-house-accuses-moonshot-ai-anthropic-model-distillation/), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5984510-white-house-moonshot-ai-anthropic-nvidia/), [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/07/23/tech/white-house-moonshot-banned-nvidia/))

**OpenAI unveils Project Camellia — a $30B, 3.2 GW self-built data center in Georgia** — OpenAI announced a 3.2-gigawatt AI campus on ~1,400 acres in Effingham County, Georgia (near Savannah), its first data center it will design and build itself, with reported spend exceeding $30B. Georgia Power delivers electricity in phases from 2028 to 2032; OpenAI says it will fully fund the required grid infrastructure so existing ratepayers aren't subsidizing it, use closed-loop cooling to limit water draw, and add $80M in community benefits plus $71M in Codex credits for Georgia students.
_Why it matters:_ 3.2 GW is roughly three large reactors dedicated to one company's compute — and the ratepayer/water commitments are a direct response to the local opposition now delaying frontier-scale builds. The 2028–2032 timeline underlines that today's compute scarcity can't be spent away quickly. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/22/openai-savannah-georgia-data-center-project) · [Georgia Power (PR Newswire)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/georgia-power-to-serve-openai-project-in-effingham-county-302832224.html) (context: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/openai-plans-to-spend-over-30-billion-on-georgia-data-center), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/openai-is-spending-usd30-billion-on-a-new-mega-3-2-gw-data-center-in-georgia-and-its-trying-really-hard-to-quell-local-opposition))

**Alphabet Q2 2026: Google Cloud +82%, 2026 capex guide raised to $195–205B** — Alphabet reported revenue up 24% to $119.8B, with Google Cloud surging 82% YoY to $24.8B and Cloud operating income tripling to $8.8B; Cloud backlog reached $514B (up >$50B sequentially). Management lifted full-year 2026 capex guidance to $195–205B (from $180–190B), citing "an acceleration in the delivery of capacity to meet growing demand"; Q2 capex was $44.9B and free cash flow turned negative. GOOGL slipped after hours on the capex hike despite the revenue beat.
_Why it matters:_ The clearest read yet on AI-infrastructure economics — the capex acceleration and record Cloud backlog are what ultimately underwrite the compute-supply story every lab and enterprise is planning against. [blog.google — Alphabet Q2 2026](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/) (context: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/google-earnings-q2-goog-live-updates.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/alphabet-q2-2026-earnings-revenue-203058727.html), [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/alphabet-q2-fy-2026-google-cloud-leads-growth-amid-rising-ai-investment/))

**Open-weight wave lands this week under a provenance cloud: DeepSeek V4 (24 Jul) and Kimi K3 weights (27 Jul)** — DeepSeek's V4 graduates from preview to a stable release on 24 Jul, introducing peak/off-peak API pricing (2× during 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00) and retiring the legacy `deepseek-chat` / `deepseek-reasoner` aliases at 15:59 UTC the same day; the MIT-licensed V4-Pro/V4-Flash keep a 1M-token context and a ~$0.44/M-output price floor. Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T params, top of the Frontend Code Arena, 88.3 Terminal-Bench) frees its weights on 27 Jul — now shadowed by the White House distillation accusation.
_Why it matters:_ The largest concentration of open-weight releases yet arrives into a newly complicated risk calculus — enterprises now weigh a contested-provenance question on top of the technical eval, while the DeepSeek price floor keeps resetting what everyone else's inference budgets are measured against. [TechNode — DeepSeek V4](https://technode.com/2026/06/30/deepseek-to-launch-v4-in-mid-july-with-new-peak-time-api-pricing/) · [Tom's Hardware — Kimi K3](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/moonshot-releases-2-8-trillion-parameter-kimi-k3) (context: [DeepSeek API docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/))

**Enterprise agent platforms consolidate into a four-way fight** — With OpenAI's Presence now in limited GA (launched 22 Jul; BBVA, SoftBank and IAG exploring it), the enterprise "deploy fleets of governed agents" category now has four heavyweight entrants pitching the same buyer: OpenAI Presence, Google Cloud's expanded Gemini Enterprise, Meta's Business Agent Platform, and the NVIDIA × ServiceNow "Project Arc" self-evolving desktop agent. All four compete on governance, permissions, audit and integration rather than raw model quality.
_Why it matters:_ The enterprise-agent race has fully shifted from "whose model is smartest" to "whose deployment-and-governance layer is most trustworthy" — and agent-platform switching costs are far higher than swapping an API endpoint, so first large deployments will be sticky. [The Register — OpenAI Presence](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/22/openai-tries-the-consulting-path-with-presence-charging-enterprises-boots-on-the-ground-prices-to-deploy-agents/5275867) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/openai-unveils-presence-a-new-platform-that-lets-enterprises-launch-and-manage-realtime-voice-agents-and-chatbots) (context: [NVIDIA × ServiceNow](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/servicenow-autonomous-ai-agents-enterprises/))
