## Major announcements & releases

**Moonshot launches Kimi K3 — a ~2.5T open-weight model pitched at Claude Opus 4.8, for a fraction of the cost.** On July 16 Moonshot AI made Kimi K3 (K3·Max and K3 Cluster·Max) available to logged-in users ahead of a formal announcement. It's a Mixture-of-Experts model with roughly 2.5 trillion total parameters, a 1M+ token native context window and multimodal input (text, image, audio), released open-weight — and early benchmark chatter has it matching or surpassing Anthropic's flagship Opus 4.8 while costing far less to run. _Why it matters:_ China's open-weight labs keep landing frontier-class models on release day, and an Opus-class open model dropping the morning before WAIC Shanghai resets the price/performance floor for anyone building on closed frontier APIs. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/moonshots-upcoming-kimi-3-is-expected-to-close-the-gap-with-anthropics-opus-4-8/) (also [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/moonshot-s-kimi-k3-model-now-available-to-users), [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com/kimi-k3-moonshot-ai-model-preview-jul2026)) _(vendor permalink not found — soft launch, formal post pending)_

**Mira Murati's Thinking Machines ships its first open model, Inkling — an American answer to Chinese open weights.** Released July 15 under the Apache 2.0 license, Inkling is a Mixture-of-Experts system with 975B total parameters (~41B active per query), trained on 45 trillion tokens of text, image, audio and video, and built to trade cost against performance across media. It's open-weight (downloadable and customizable), and lands two months after the lab's full-duplex voice demo and days after Murati's "The Future Worth Building Is Human" manifesto. _Why it matters:_ the most competitive open-weight models have been overwhelmingly Chinese; Inkling puts a well-funded US lab into that lane on the same 48 hours as Kimi K3, framing an explicit open-weights contest. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/thinking-machines-amps-up-its-bet-against-one-size-fits-all-ai-with-its-first-open-model-inkling/) (also [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/15/mira-murati-thinking-machines-open-weight-model-inkling), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-15/murati-s-thinking-machines-releases-first-ai-model-for-broad-use), vendor: [thinkingmachines.ai](https://thinkingmachines.ai/))

**TSMC posts record Q2 profit, raises full-year guidance again, and adds $100B to its US build-out.** In its July 16 earnings, TSMC reported Q2 revenue of ~$40.2B (above guidance) with gross margin of 67.7%, and lifted its 2026 revenue-growth outlook to more than 40% (from >30%) while raising full-year capex to a record $60–64B (from $52–56B). CEO C.C. Wei said the company will invest an additional $100B in Arizona capacity and that "our conviction in the multi-year AI megatrend remains very high." _Why it matters:_ TSMC is the cleanest single read on whether the AI build-out has a ceiling — and it just told the market demand for advanced 3nm/5nm and AI HPC is still accelerating, not cooling. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/earnings/tsmc-q2-profit-blows-past-estimates-on-robust-aifueled-demand-4794650) (also [Yahoo Finance/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/tsmc-raises-capex-and-revenue-forecast-highlighting-growing-ai-chip-demand-113101950.html))

**DeepSeek seeks a ~$74B valuation in a fresh round as it preps a mainland IPO.** Per Reuters (July 15), DeepSeek is raising up to 50 billion yuan at a ~500 billion yuan (~$74B) valuation and has begun early work on a Shanghai STAR Market listing, with an internal goal of filing an IPO later this year. The round comes just weeks after its first-ever external financing (~50B yuan at a ~450B yuan post-money), underscoring how expensive frontier AI has become even for a lab famed for cost efficiency. _Why it matters:_ the best-known Chinese AI startup is institutionalizing — capital-raising plus onshore-IPO prep — days before it fronts WAIC and just after confirming V4's mid-July GA. [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/chinas-deepseek-to-raise-fresh-capital-at-74-billion-valuation-ahead-of-onshore-ipo-sources-say-4792624) (also [heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/DeepSeek-apparently-wants-to-go-public-IPO-filing-possible-by-2026-11366108.html), [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/15/deepseek-eyes-74-billion-valuation-in-new-funding-round-ahead-of-planned-ipo/))

**WAIC Shanghai opens July 17 at record scale — 300+ global debuts and a High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance.** China's 2026 World AI Conference and its High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance run July 17–20, with 1,000+ exhibitors, 3,000+ technologies, 300+ global product launches, 140+ forums and, for the first time, a Turing-award-led academic track, across 100,000+ m². International sessions target global AI governance and security, "underlying standards for world models and AI agents," the digital-intelligence gap, and norms for humanoid robotics. _Why it matters:_ WAIC is where China stakes its claim on both the frontier-tech and the governance agenda — and this year it collides with the expected July 17 Gemini 3.5 Pro launch and the run-up to EU AI Act GPAI enforcement (Aug 2), making mid-week the summer's densest AI news window. [CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-07-07/Shanghai-to-host-record-breaking-World-AI-Conference-July-17-to-20-1OAHU3xSEog/p.html) (also [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/07/08/shanghais-waic-2026-to-spotlight-global-ai-governance-and-industry-collaboration/), [People's Daily](https://en.people.cn/n3/2026/0708/c90000-20475542.html))

**Coinbase says 95–100% of its code is now AI-assisted, up from ~40% in February.** Rob Witoff, Coinbase's head of platform, told Cointelegraph that "close to 100%" of the company's code is now written by or with LLMs — a jump from ~40% in February, ~80% in Q1 2026 and just 5.7% a year earlier. Nearly all employees use AI daily, engineers run 5–10 coding agents in parallel, and the cumulative effort is pegged at roughly 1,200 employee-equivalents; humans still gate cryptography and core security. _Why it matters:_ a regulated, security-sensitive public company putting a hard number on near-total AI-assisted development is one of the strongest data points yet on how fast agentic coding is reshaping engineering org design. [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/coinbase-ai-assisted-code-95-percent/) (also [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/coinbase-executive-says-95-100-of-company-code-is-now-ai-assisted/))

**MCP publishes its 2026-07-28 spec release candidate — stateless core, MCP Apps and Tasks.** The Model Context Protocol steering group put out the release candidate for the 2026-07-28 specification, with the final due July 28. Headline changes: a stateless core that scales on ordinary round-robin HTTP (no sticky sessions or shared session store), method-header routing and cacheable `tools/list`, plus new extensions — server-rendered UIs via **MCP Apps** and long-running work via the **Tasks** extension — and authorization aligned to OAuth/OpenID Connect. _Why it matters:_ MCP now sits at ~97M monthly downloads and 5,800+ servers; making remote servers run behind commodity load balancers and adding first-class UI/async primitives is the plumbing that lets agent tool-use scale in production. [Model Context Protocol blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/)
