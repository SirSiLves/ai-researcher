## Major announcements & releases

**Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5 — near-Fable-5 intelligence at half the price** — Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, the fourth member of the Claude 5 family (after Sonnet 5, Fable 5 and the cyber-focused Mythos 5). Anthropic frames it as the everyday workhorse: it "comes close to the frontier intelligence of Claude Fable 5 at half the price," is the new state-of-the-art on the Frontier-Bench and GDPval-AA coding/knowledge-work evals (still behind Mythos 5 on cybersecurity), and delivers a large jump over Opus 4.8 at the same cost. A new low/medium/high effort toggle lets users trade cost against capability per request; Opus 5 becomes the default on Claude Max and the strongest option on Claude Pro, and it landed same-day in GitHub Copilot for long-running agentic coding.
_Why it matters:_ The half-price-of-frontier positioning is the story — it pushes strong agentic capability down the cost curve just as the EU AI Act's GPAI enforcement window opens and as Anthropic reportedly preps an IPO later this year. [Anthropic — Introducing Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5) · [System Card (24 Jul)](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/c5fbac3f0b1280a933ebd26d3cb8bb9f5bdeaf48/Claude%20Opus%205%20System%20Card.pdf) (context: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-debuts-claude-opus-5-with-feature-that-lets-users-toggle-between-cost-and-capability/), [GitHub Copilot changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-24-claude-opus-5-is-now-available-in-github-copilot/))

**DeepSeek V4 graduates to stable — legacy aliases retired at 15:59 UTC today** — DeepSeek's V4 line exits preview: at 2026-07-24 15:59 UTC the legacy `deepseek-chat` and `deepseek-reasoner` aliases are deprecated and fold into `deepseek-v4-flash` (non-thinking and thinking modes) and `deepseek-v4-pro`, per DeepSeek's own API docs. The MIT-licensed, open-weight V4 models keep the 1M-token context and introduce peak/off-peak API pricing (2× during Beijing business windows) that continues to reset the inference-cost floor everyone else is benchmarked against.
_Why it matters:_ This is the first of the week's open-weight double-header — Moonshot frees Kimi K3's weights on 27 Jul under a distillation-provenance cloud — and the alias retirement forces every DeepSeek API caller to migrate today. [DeepSeek API docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/) (context: [Artificial Analysis — V4 Pro](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-pro))

**OpenAI brings hands-free Codex and a Health experience to ChatGPT** — OpenAI shipped two consumer-and-developer updates this window. ChatGPT Voice now runs inside Work and Codex in the desktop app with GPT-Live full-duplex control, so engineers can orchestrate multi-threaded coding jobs, review PRs and debug by voice while agents run in the background. Separately, OpenAI launched Health in ChatGPT (US, 18+): logged-in users connect supported health records and Apple Health data into one dashboard and ask questions grounded in their own records, on web and iOS.
_Why it matters:_ The Codex voice layer is the more consequential half — it turns Codex further from a chat box into an always-on agent command center, the same "govern fleets of agents" direction the enterprise-platform vendors are racing toward. Health is a reminder OpenAI is also pushing ChatGPT into regulated, high-liability consumer verticals. [OpenAI — Work with Codex from anywhere](https://openai.com/index/work-with-codex-from-anywhere/) · [OpenAI — Health in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/health-in-chatgpt/) (context: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/agentic-coding-goes-hands-free-as-openai-brings-gpt-lives-full-duplex-voice-control-to-codex-and-chatgpt-on-the-desktop))

**MCP's biggest revision since launch — the 2026-07-28 release candidate goes final in four days** — The Model Context Protocol's 2026-07-28 spec, called the largest revision since the protocol launched, is in its release-candidate window ahead of Monday's final. It removes protocol-level sessions and the initialization handshake for a stateless core that scales on ordinary HTTP, deprecates three core features, rewrites authorization to align with OAuth / OpenID Connect, and moves long-running work (Tasks) and server-rendered UIs (MCP Apps) into a new extensions framework.
_Why it matters:_ MCP is now the default vertical tool-integration layer under enterprise agent stacks; a stateless, OAuth-aligned core is a direct answer to the auth and scaling complaints that made MCP hard to deploy in production, and it lands the same week vendors are shipping agent fleets. [Model Context Protocol — 2026-07-28 release candidate](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/) (context: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/07/23/model-context-protocol-prepares-to-break-with-its-stateful-past/5276722), [WorkOS](https://workos.com/blog/mcp-2026-spec-agent-authentication))

**ServiceNow Q2: agentic deployments up 9× in nine months, AI ACV crosses $1B** — ServiceNow reported Q2 FY2026 revenue of $3.99B (+24% YoY, ahead of consensus) and, more tellingly for the AI thesis, said agentic deployments of its AI grew ninefold in nine months and that ServiceNow AI passed $1B in annual contract value in the quarter. CEO Bill McDermott anchored the narrative on the AI Control Tower and an Autonomous Workforce of "specialists" that resolve 80–85% of service requests without human intervention at 40+ customers; full-year subscription guidance was raised.
_Why it matters:_ This is one of the first hard revenue signals that governed enterprise agents are converting to spend at scale, not just pilots — the $1B AI ACV and 9× deployment curve are the kind of numbers the whole enterprise-agent race has been promising. [ServiceNow Newsroom](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-turns-enterprise-AI-chaos-into-control-with-the-platform-for-governed-autonomous-work/default.aspx) (context: [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/servicenow-q2-fy-2026-ai-security-and-workflow-expansion-fuel-growth/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/23/servicenow-beats-q2-earnings-estimates-market-starts-to-buy-ceo-bill-mcdermott-ai-narrative/))

**SAP Q2: cloud +24%, Joule Work heads to market in Q3 — but M&A dilutes profit guide** — SAP reported Q2 revenue of €9.9B (+11%) with cloud revenue up 24% to €6.3B and current cloud backlog up 26% to ~€23B. The AI story centred on the Business AI Platform (build / context-and-reason / run-and-govern) and the "Autonomous Enterprise": Joule Work, SAP's agentic assistant, had 4,000 internal users in an oversubscribed beta and ships to market in Q3, with SAP citing ~30% developer-productivity gains. Completed acquisitions trimmed 2026 non-IFRS operating-profit guidance (>€100M dilution), knocking the stock.
_Why it matters:_ SAP is wiring an agentic layer directly into the systems that run European enterprise back-offices; the profit-guide cut shows the current cost of buying that AI capability (it recently absorbed Prior Labs), even as cloud backlog keeps compounding. [SAP News — Business AI Q2 2026 highlights](https://news.sap.com/2026/07/sap-business-ai-release-highlights-q2-2026/) (context: [StockTitan — Q2 statement](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/SAP/sap-quarterly-statement-q2-mu9r28yruw3r.html), [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_SAP_2026-07-23))

**EU AI Act's GPAI enforcement powers go live 2 August** — In nine days the European Commission's enforcement powers over general-purpose AI models become active: the power to demand documentation and information, run model evaluations, order risk-mitigation / market-restriction / recall measures, and impose fines of up to €15M or 3% of global revenue, whichever is higher. GPAI obligations themselves have applied since Aug 2025; only the enforcement teeth were delayed a year, and they now bite just as a wave of new frontier and open-weight models (Opus 5, DeepSeek V4, Kimi K3) hits the EU market.
_Why it matters:_ 2 Aug converts the GPAI Code of Practice from voluntary posture into enforceable exposure for every model provider serving the EU — the compliance clock every lab and enterprise deployer has been watching finally starts ticking. [EU AI Act — Enforcement of Chapter V](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/enforcement-of-chapter-v-under-the-eu-ai-act/) (context: [ComplianceHub readiness guide](https://compliancehub.wiki/eu-ai-act-gpai-enforcement-august-2026-readiness/))
