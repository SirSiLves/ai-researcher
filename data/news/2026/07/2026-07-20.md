## Major announcements & releases

**Alibaba previews Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4T-parameter multimodal model, at WAIC in Shanghai** — At the World AI Conference (Jul 19), Alibaba unveiled Qwen3.8-Max, its first Qwen model above 1 trillion parameters to go natively multimodal (text, images, video, documents) at ~2.4T total parameters, and said the family will go open-weight "soon." A purchasable preview build is live via Alibaba's Token Plan subscription at ~10% of standard pricing. It lands just three days after Moonshot's 2.8T Kimi K3, extending the Chinese open-weight surge into a second trillion-scale release inside a week. _Why it matters:_ Two Chinese labs shipping 2.4–2.8T-class frontier models days apart — while WAIC positions China's open ecosystem against closed US labs — keeps compressing the capability gap despite export controls. [https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/19/alibaba-previews-qwen3-8-max-a-2-4-trillion-parameter-multimodal-model-days-after-moonshots-kimi-k3-open-weight-launch/] (no vendor permalink surfaced; Qwen typically publishes at qwenlm.github.io)

**Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro slips again as the widely-reported July 17 target passes with no launch** — The July 17 date that trackers had pinned for Gemini 3.5 Pro came and went with no public release; Bloomberg reported (Jul 16) a further delay after the model fell short of Google's own internal quality bar, following an earlier rebuild in which DeepMind reportedly scrapped and rebuilt the base model over recursive tool-calling and reasoning failures. Google has confirmed neither a date, the rumored 2M-token context window, nor pricing. _Why it matters:_ While Chinese labs ship trillion-parameter models weekly, Google's flagship keeps slipping — a notable stumble for the lab that set the pace a year ago. [https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a] (also TechTimes: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm; no Google permalink — unannounced)

**EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations go live Aug 2 as the Transparency Code signatory window closes Jul 22** — With Article 50 (transparency for AI systems and AI-generated/synthetic content, including deepfake labeling and "you're talking to an AI" disclosure) becoming applicable on 2 August 2026, the Commission is pressing providers to sign its finalized Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content, with the signatory form due by 18:00 CET on July 22. This is distinct from the Chapter V GPAI-enforcement powers that also activate Aug 2. _Why it matters:_ Every provider and deployer serving the EU has a two-day window to signal Code adoption and under two weeks before transparency/labeling duties become enforceable. [https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content] (Article 50 guide: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/)

**"Stop the AI Race" protesters march on OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind in San Francisco** — Roughly 200 demonstrators marched Saturday (Jul 18) from OpenAI's headquarters past the offices of Anthropic and Google DeepMind, demanding labs halt training of more powerful models until their societal effects are better understood — the coalition's second street action of 2026. It lands the same week three lab CEOs converged publicly on the case for regulation and days before EU transparency rules bite. _Why it matters:_ Public, organized opposition to frontier scaling is becoming a recurring feature of the AI-safety debate, adding social pressure alongside the regulatory clock. [https://cryptobriefing.com/protesters-demand-openai-anthropic-deepmind-halt-ai/]

**OpenAI unifies the ChatGPT desktop app around Chat, Work and Codex** — OpenAI shipped a desktop-app update (Jul 18, now live for all plans on macOS and Windows) that adds a global switcher between ChatGPT and Codex, a clearer Chat-vs-Work layout (Chat for quick help, Work to complete end-to-end tasks), Projects surfaced natively in the app, and cloud-synced Work conversations that carry across web, mobile and desktop. _Why it matters:_ It hardens the "agent that does work" surface into OpenAI's default client and blurs the line between assistant and background worker on the desktop. [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes] (vendor release notes; no openai.com/index permalink surfaced for the desktop update — also https://openai.com/news/product-releases/)

**ServiceNow, SAP and Workday move to meter AI-agent access to enterprise data** — A cluster of coverage crystallized the enterprise shift from per-seat to per-action pricing for agents: ServiceNow's Action Fabric charges by operations an external agent completes (Anthropic's Claude Cowork is a launch connector), Workday's Agent Gateway/System of Record opens its rails to outside agents on a per-call basis, and SAP routes agent traffic through its Business Accelerator Hub, also metered. _Why it matters:_ The incumbents are turning agent access into a billable resource — a structural repricing of enterprise software as agents, not humans, become the heaviest "users." [https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/servicenow-sap-and-workday-make-ai-agents-pay-to-play/]

**Fireworks AI raises $1.5B Series D at a $17.5B valuation to scale its enterprise inference platform** — Fireworks AI announced a $1.505B Series D (led by Atreides Management, Index Ventures and TCV, with existing backers) at a $17.5B valuation, funding expansion of its enterprise model-serving and fine-tuning platform. The round underscores how fast the "run open models in production" inference layer is being capitalized as enterprises deploy Kimi/Qwen-class open weights. _Why it matters:_ Serving infrastructure for open-weight frontier models is now a multi-billion-dollar category of its own, sitting between the labs and the enterprises that deploy them. [https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/07/16/fireworks-raises-1-5b-to-expand-enterprise-ai-model-platform/]
