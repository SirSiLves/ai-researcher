## Major announcements & releases

**Xi Jinping opens WAIC Shanghai in person and China launches WAICO, a rival AI-governance bloc.** Xi delivered the opening address at the 2026 World AI Conference on July 17 — the first time a Chinese president has addressed the summit in person since it began in 2018 — calling for AI development to be "a symphony of international cooperation" rather than "a solo performance by a single country," and warning against both safety risks and security overreach. A day earlier, 29 countries signed an agreement in Shanghai establishing the **World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO)**, to be headquartered in the city. Xi paired it with an explicit Global South offer: 5,000 AI training places for developing countries, cooperation tracks with ASEAN, the Arab League and the African Union, and free access for 30 countries to a Chinese AI meteorological early-warning tool. More than 1,100 companies and 1,400 guests are attending. _Why it matters:_ this is the first standing international AI body China has convened rather than joined — a structural counterweight to the EU AI Act / AI Safety Institute axis, launched 16 days before the Commission gains power to fine GPAI providers (Aug 2). Governance is now a two-bloc contest, and vendors will have to answer to both. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/17/x-china-ai-summit-risks-security.html) (also [SCMP takeaways](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-war/article/3360870/top-takeaways-xi-jinpings-opening-address-world-ai-conference-shanghai), [Al Jazeera on WAICO](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/17/chinas-xi-jinping-launches-new-ai-alliance-what-is-it), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/17/nx-s1-5897285/chinas-xi-calls-for-step-up-of-global-effort-in-ai-as-us-curbs-squeeze-chinas-tech-access), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/xi-vows-to-make-ai-for-all-in-debut-at-china-s-top-tech-summit))

**Kimi K3's real numbers land: 2.8T parameters, $3/$15 pricing, weights on July 27 — and it beat every US model on Arena front-end coding.** Yesterday K3 was a soft launch with no formal post; the specifics are now public. It's a sparse MoE with ~2.8 trillion total parameters (16 of 896 experts active) built on Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residuals, with a 1M-token context window, native vision and always-on reasoning, shipping as K3 Max and K3 Swarm Max. API pricing is **$3 per million input / $15 per million output** — the highest of any Chinese lab, but roughly **half the per-task cost of Claude Opus 4.8**. In blind Arena testing, developers preferred K3 over every leading US model for front-end coding, including Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol; on Arena's broader text ranking it outranked standard Opus 4.8 and tied Sol. Crucially, **the weights don't drop until July 27** — until then nobody outside Moonshot can independently inspect or self-host it. _Why it matters:_ "largest open-weights model ever" is doing real work in the headlines, but the ten-day gap between benchmark glory and downloadable weights is the part engineers should hold judgment on — Arena preference on front-end coding is not the same as verified frontier parity. [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/16/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-a-2-8-trillion-parameter-open-moe-model-with-kimi-delta-attention-and-1m-context/) (also [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/16/moonshot-kimi-ai-china-model-openai-anthropic), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/16/moonshots-kimi-k3-pushes-chinese-ai-into-fable-level-territory/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/china-s-powerful-new-moonshot-ai-model-closes-gap-with-us-rivals), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/16/chinas-moonshot-throws-gauntlet-kimi-k3-worlds-largest-open-weights-model/)) _(vendor permalink not found — formal Moonshot post still pending; weights promised 07-27)_

**OpenAI ships its first hardware, and it's a $230 macropad for driving coding agents.** Launched July 15 in limited numbers and picked up by the German press today, **Codex Micro** is a programmable macro pad built with keyboard maker Work Louder: 13 low-profile mechanical keys, a joystick, a rotary encoder and configurable layers. The interesting part is the six **agent keys** — shine-through caps that carry live RGB status from Codex, so white means idle, blue thinking, green done, red error; one tap selects that agent, a double-tap foregrounds it. The joystick launches common workflows (review a PR, debug an error, refactor), and the dial adjusts reasoning level in the moment. USB-C or Bluetooth, silent or clicky switches. _Why it matters:_ OpenAI's first branded device isn't a phone or a pendant — it's an admission that the bottleneck in agentic coding is now **supervising several agents at once**, and that the terminal is a bad dashboard for it. A physical status light per agent is a small idea with a loud implication about where developer workflow is heading. [OpenAI × Work Louder](https://openai.com/supply/co-lab/work-louder/) (also [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2215952/openai-launches-a-physical-keypad-for-controlling-agents/), [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-codex-micro-macropad/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/15/openai-keyboard-codex-agents), [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/peripherals/keyboards/openais-first-hardware-device-is-an-rgb-macropod-codex-micro-features-13-low-profile-keys-and-a-joystick-for-controlling-ai-coding-agents))

**Anthropic, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman formally launch Ode — a $1.5B bet that implementation, not models, is the next huge AI business.** Ode with Anthropic launched July 15 as a joint venture: Anthropic, Blackstone and H&F each put in roughly $300M, Goldman Sachs ~$150M, with General Atlantic, Leonard Green, Apollo, GIC and Sequoia filling out the $1.5B consortium. It employs ~100 engineers, works alongside Anthropic's applied AI team to find where the technology actually moves a given business, and has already acquired AI engineering services startup Fractional AI. It operates "Claude-first" — deploying Anthropic tech including Claude Tag in Slack wherever possible — but will use rival products where needed. _Why it matters:_ a frontier lab openly conceding that the model is not the product and the deployment is, then capitalizing that belief at $1.5B and pointing it straight at Accenture/Deloitte/McKinsey territory. If the "trillion-dollar AI business is implementation" thesis holds, this is the template every lab copies. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-blackstone-bet-the-next-trillion-dollar-ai-business-is-implementation-not-models/) (also [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260715205134/en/Anthropic-Blackstone-and-Hellman-Friedman-Introduce-Ode-with-Anthropic-an-Enterprise-AI-Services-Firm)) _(no anthropic.com/news permalink — not posted to the Anthropic newsroom)_

**NVIDIA and Japan's METI stand up what they call the world's first national AI infrastructure — 140MW of Vera Rubin for physical AI.** Announced July 16, NVIDIA is working with **Noetra Corp.** to build a Vera Rubin AI factory with **13,750 Vera CPUs and 27,500 Rubin GPUs**, delivering **140 megawatts** of data-center capacity on the NVIDIA DSX platform with Vera Rubin NVL72 racks and Spectrum-X Ethernet. It's the compute foundation for METI's **FRONTia Project** ("Development of Multimodal Foundation Models with a View to AI Robotics and Physical AI"), and the pretrained weights of Noetra's multimodal foundation models will be **made broadly available to domestic developers and enterprises** alongside Nemotron, Cosmos and Isaac GR00T. Japan's AI Robotics Strategy targets >30% of the global AI robotics market by 2040, an estimated $133B opportunity. _Why it matters:_ sovereign AI stops being a slogan when a government wires a named 140MW cluster to a named industrial program and commits to publishing the weights. It's also the clearest signal yet that the next compute land-grab is **physical** AI — robotics, digital twins, factory floors — not chatbots. [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/japan-government-industrial-leaders-and-nvidia-launch-the-worlds-first-national-ai-infrastructure) (companion releases July 15: [Japan's robotics leaders on Cosmos](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/japans-robotics-and-manufacturing-leaders-build-on-nvidia-cosmos-to-advance-physical-ai-frontier), [Japanese enterprises on Nemotron open models](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/japans-enterprises-and-startups-build-industry-specialized-ai-with-nvidia-nemotron-open-models))

**Google renames NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook and gives it a code interpreter.** Announced July 16, the product keeps its standalone identity but folds into the Gemini brand — new blue/purple gradient logo, notebooks syncing across the Gemini app and Google Search, and a **secure cloud computer that runs code inside notebooks** for deeper data analysis (rolling out to Pro users). Google says nothing breaks: existing notebooks, links and extensions keep working. The product is now used by **30M+ people and 600,000+ organizations**, and a Gemini 3.5 + Antigravity upgrade is queued for AI Pro subscribers. _Why it matters:_ the most-loved grounded-retrieval product on the market just got execution. A knowledge tool that can both cite your sources and run code against them collapses the gap between "RAG over my documents" and "analysis of my documents" — which is most of what enterprise knowledge work actually is. [blog.google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/) (also [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/google-continues-its-renaming-streak-by-turning-notebooklm-to-gemini-notebook/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/16/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/16/google-rebrands-notebooklm-gemini-notebook-focusing-ecosystem-accessibility/))

**IBM lost a quarter of its value in a single day — and blamed the AI build-out.** _(Backfill: July 14, missed by the 07-15 and 07-16 files.)_ IBM fell **25.2%** on Tuesday July 14, the worst trading day in its 115-year history — worse than Black Monday 1987 (−23%) — erasing ~$67B of market cap and closing near $217. The trigger was a pre-announcement from CEO Arvind Krishna that Q2 came in "worse than our expectations": revenue of $17.2B against a $17.9B consensus, with infrastructure down 7%. Krishna's explanation is the part worth reading: enterprise customers **redirected spend toward AI hardware, servers and storage** — hoarding capacity against AI-driven shortages and price hikes — leaving IBM's software and consulting deals unclosed. He also said the release of Anthropic's Mythos stalled several large deals as customers weighed its cybersecurity implications. _Why it matters:_ the first large-cap casualty where AI is the stated cause of the *miss* rather than the growth story. It reframes the build-out as a **zero-sum budget fight** — every dollar into GPUs is a dollar out of traditional enterprise software — and that is a far more uncomfortable read for the sector than TSMC's record quarter is a comforting one. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/ibm-warns-second-quarter-earnings-fell-short-of-expectations.html) (also [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/07/14/ibm-shares-crashed-25-in-worst-day-ever-heres-why/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/ibm-stock-ai-arvind-krishna), [Fortune analyst reaction](https://fortune.com/2026/07/15/ibm-stock-price-crash-25-percent-analyst-reaction/))
