## Major announcements & releases

**xAI's Grok Build CLI was shipping entire code repositories — secrets and all — to the cloud; Musk promises a purge.** A July 12 wire-level analysis (grok 0.2.93) showed the Grok Build coding CLI packaging developers' full tracked Git repositories — complete history and committed secrets — and uploading them to a Google Cloud Storage bucket, moving ~5.1 GiB from a repo where the model's actual task needed ~192 KB (a ~27,800× gap). Planted canary markers (an `API_KEY=CANARY…` and a mock DB password) appeared verbatim in captured traffic, and disabling the "Improve the model" toggle did not stop the uploads. After the finding hit the Hacker News front page on July 14, Elon Musk posted that all previously uploaded user data would be "completely and utterly deleted"; xAI staff countered that Zero Data Retention has been available since launch, and follow-up testing found a new server-side `disable_codebase_upload: true` flag had halted the transfers. _Why it matters:_ agentic coding CLIs run with broad filesystem access — this is a concrete reminder that "local-first" claims need wire-level verification, and that opt-out toggles may not do what their labels imply. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/14/musk-promises-purge-after-grok-build-caught-sending-entire-repos-to-the-cloud/) (wire analysis: [cereblab gist](https://gist.github.com/cereblab/dc9a40bc26120f4540e4e09b75ffb547); also [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/grok-build-uploads-entire-git.html))

**European Commission unveils an Action Plan on Cybersecurity and AI.** Presented July 7, the plan sets a coordinated EU approach to the cyber-risks of the most advanced AI models: it commits the Commission to strengthen pre-market model evaluation "in line with the AI Act," to work with ENISA on a "European Blueprint for secure access to advanced AI systems for cybersecurity," and to stand up a secure testing platform for critical sectors (energy, transport, health, finance, public administration). It deliberately fuses previously separate regimes — the AI Act, Cyber Resilience Act, NIS2, DORA and the Cyber Solidarity Act. _Why it matters:_ with GPAI enforcement powers activating August 2, Brussels is wiring AI-model security into its cyber rulebook — and organizations already under NIS2/DORA now face overlapping AI-security obligations. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/eu-action-plan-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence) (context: [AI Act governance & enforcement](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-act-governance-and-enforcement))

**Helsing raises $1.8B at an $18B valuation — Europe's biggest-ever defense-tech round.** On July 13 the Munich-based defense-AI company (military strike drones, underwater surveillance, battlefield decision software) closed a Series E it says was significantly oversubscribed, with Dragoneer, Lightspeed, Goldman Sachs Alternatives, JPMorganChase, CPP Investments, General Catalyst and Iconiq among the backers. Founded only in 2021, Helsing is now among Europe's most valuable defense companies and the continent's clearest answer to US-based Anduril. _Why it matters:_ capital is pouring into sovereign, AI-native defense — a fast-growing enterprise-AI vertical with its own governance and dual-use questions, and a rare European frontier-scale raise. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/13/helsing-fund-raise-defense-18-billion.html) (also [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/drone-startup-helsing-raises-at-18-billion-with-goldman-backing), [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/07/13/european-defence-ai-leader-helsing-secures-18b-series-e-at-18b-valuation/))

**Meta's in-house "Iris" AI accelerator heads to production this fall.** An internal memo (surfaced July 9) confirms Meta will begin manufacturing Iris — the fourth generation of its MTIA (Meta Training and Inference Accelerator) line, co-designed with Broadcom and fabbed at TSMC — in September, after a six-week test cycle reportedly found no major issues. It is meant to run alongside the large Nvidia/AMD GPU fleets Meta already buys, as the company targets 7 GW of compute by end-2026 (14 GW in 2027) against up to ~$145B of 2026 AI-infrastructure spend. _Why it matters:_ the largest buyers of merchant GPUs are moving real custom silicon into production — a structural pressure on the accelerator market beneath the model race. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/metas-new-ai-chips-will-begin-production-in-september/) (also [US News/Reuters](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-07-09/exclusive-meta-to-put-ai-chip-into-production-in-september-as-it-looks-to-double-computing-capacity-memo-shows))

**Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro stays locked on July 17 — the same day WAIC 2026 opens with Xi Jinping attending in person.** As of July 13 there is still no public model card, pricing, or `gemini-3.5-pro` API listing; third-party reporting holds a July 17 target after Google scrapped the original base model and restarted pretraining. That date now doubles as the opening of Shanghai's World Artificial Intelligence Conference — the first WAIC China's top leader will attend in person — making mid-next-week a genuine East-vs-West signpost for the summer. _Why it matters:_ the West's most-anticipated model of the summer and China's marquee AI-governance stage may land on the same day; expect a dense news cycle around July 17. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm) (context: [buildfastwithai](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-14-2026)) _(unconfirmed by Google — third-party reporting)_
