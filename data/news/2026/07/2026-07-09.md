# AI News — 2026-07-09

## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI makes GPT-5.6 (Sol / Terra / Luna) generally available** — OpenAI ended the 13-day, government-coordinated preview (opened June 26) and rolled GPT-5.6 out publicly today across ChatGPT, the API, and Codex, with a staged rollout completing over ~24h. The three durable tiers ship at (per 1M tokens) Sol $5/$30, Terra $2.50/$15, and Luna $1/$6; Sol adds a `max` reasoning effort and an `ultra` subagent mode (91.9% Terminal-Bench 2.1 in ultra vs 88.8% standard). The `gpt-5.5-latest` endpoint does not auto-migrate — developers must pin explicit `gpt-5.6-*` model IDs. _Why it matters:_ A frontier-tier GA whose staggered, White-House-gated release is itself a governance precedent, and whose sub-$1 Luna input price resets the low end of the Western frontier-family market. [https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/] (rollout confirmation: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/openai-expanding-gpt-5point6-ai-model-release-ending-government-limits.html)

**xAI/SpaceXAI ships Grok 4.5 the same day — "Opus-class," $2/$6** — SpaceXAI released Grok 4.5 publicly today, built on the V9 foundation model and co-trained with Cursor IDE data (following SpaceX's acquisition of Anysphere). It is available in Grok Build, in Cursor on all plans, and via the SpaceXAI console/API at $2 input / $6 output per 1M tokens, with a free-usage promo; EU availability is deferred to mid-July. Musk frames it as "Opus-class, but faster, more token-efficient and lower cost," positioning it directly against Claude Opus 4.8's $5/$25. _Why it matters:_ For the first time three frontier labs (OpenAI, SpaceXAI, Anthropic) all have a publicly available new model on the same day — an unusually competitive inflection — though xAI published pricing but no system card or benchmark table at launch. [https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5]

**OpenAI launches GPT-Live full-duplex voice models** — Ahead of the GPT-5.6 GA, OpenAI (July 8) introduced GPT-Live and GPT-Live-1 mini, a new default ChatGPT Voice experience built on a full-duplex architecture that listens and speaks simultaneously (backchannels like "mhmm," interruptible turns) and delegates deeper reasoning/web-search to a frontier model behind the scenes. GPT-Live-1 becomes default for Go/Plus/Pro; the mini replaces Advanced Voice Mode for Free users, rolling out globally. _Why it matters:_ Moves consumer voice from turn-based silence-detection to genuine duplex conversation — a UX step-change for voice agents — even as early users flag "over-enthusiastic" filler. [https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live/]

**Anthropic appoints former Fed Chair Ben Bernanke to its Long-Term Benefit Trust** — Anthropic (July 9) named Ben Bernanke — 2022 Nobel laureate and Fed Chair through the 2008 crisis — to its independent Long-Term Benefit Trust, the body that can appoint board members and advises leadership on AI risk. He joins Neil Buddy Shah, Richard Fontaine, and Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar; trustees hold no equity and are paid only for their time. _Why it matters:_ Adds heavyweight macroeconomic-governance credibility to Anthropic's mission-oversight structure at a moment when the labs' societal-impact governance is under scrutiny — and ahead of a reported Q4 IPO track. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/ben-bernanke] (coverage: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/anthropic-fed-chair-bernanke-independent-trust.html)

**Anthropic opens a public "hard questions" commitment** — Also July 9, Anthropic launched an initiative inviting the public to submit their hardest questions about AI, committing to publish its work as it addresses them. _Why it matters:_ A transparency/accountability posture aimed at the trust gap around frontier AI — signaling the labs' governance surface is broadening from safety cards to public engagement. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/hard-questions]

**EU Commission issues its Opinion on the AI-generated-content Transparency Code of Practice** — On July 9 the Commission published its Opinion assessing the Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated content, which operationalizes Article 50 of the AI Act (labelling deepfakes and AI-generated public-interest content; disclosing chatbot interactions). The voluntary Code's transparency obligations apply from Aug 2, 2026; providers must submit signatory forms by July 22 to appear on the initial list. _Why it matters:_ Another concrete Aug-2 scaffolding step — this one on the transparency/labelling track — clarifying how frontier providers demonstrate Article 50 compliance weeks before it bites. [https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content]

**GPT-5.6 Sol serving on Cerebras at up to 750 tokens/sec begins in July** — Confirmed alongside the GA: OpenAI is running flagship Sol on Cerebras wafer-scale hardware at up to ~750 tok/s this month (select customers first), roughly 15x typical GPU serving. _Why it matters:_ For agentic loops that chain many generations across tool calls, wafer-scale throughput collapses end-to-end latency, making conversational frontier-quality agents practical — reframing the inference-hardware race. [https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/]

**Together AI raises $800M at an $8.3B valuation for open-model cloud** — The AI neocloud (renting NVIDIA GPU clusters + a platform to train/serve open models like DeepSeek, MiniMax, Kimi) closed an $800M Series C led by Aramco Ventures (with Vista, General Catalyst, NVIDIA, others), more than doubling its $3.3B Series B valuation from ~16 months ago; Q2 annual bookings surpassed $1.15B. _Why it matters:_ Signals how much enterprise inference demand is flowing to open-model infrastructure as a cheaper alternative to closed frontier APIs — a key enterprise-deployment vector, and part of a record H1-2026 AI funding wave. [https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/neocloud-together-ai-raises-800m-leaps-to-8-3b-valuation/] (BusinessWire: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260701243402/en/)
