## Major announcements & releases

**Jensen Huang's GTC 2026 keynote: Vera Rubin, Groq 3 LPU, $1T order book, Uber robotaxi fleet** — At the 11:00 a.m. PT keynote at the SAP Center in San Jose, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang declared the arrival of the "agentic AI inflection point." Headline announcements: the Vera Rubin platform — rack-scale Rubin R100 GPU plus 88-core Vera CPU, 5× the inference performance of Blackwell at ~10× lower cost per token, shipping later in 2026; the NVIDIA Groq 3 Language Processing Unit (first chip from the late-2025 Groq asset purchase), with the Groq 3 LPX rack holding 256 LPUs, shipping Q3; and a $1T Blackwell + Vera Rubin purchase-order forecast through 2027. Uber will deploy NVIDIA Drive AV across 28 cities on four continents by 2028, starting LA and San Francisco. _Why it matters:_ This was the single biggest infrastructure announcement of 2026 Q1 — Huang explicitly positions NVIDIA to own training and the economics of inference and agentic AI infrastructure, not just GPUs. Resets the unit-economics conversation for every frontier-lab pricing roadmap. [NVIDIA — GTC 2026 keynote on demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/keynote/) · [NVIDIA — GTC 2026 live blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/gtc-2026-news/) · [CNBC — $1 T orders for Blackwell and Vera Rubin](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/16/nvidia-gtc-2026-ceo-jensen-huang-keynote-blackwell-vera-rubin.html) · [eWeek — full AI stack](https://www.eweek.com/news/nvidia-gtc-2026-keynote-jensen-huang/) · [Tom's Hardware — keynote live blog](https://www.tomshardware.com/news/live/nvidia-gtc-2026-keynote-live-blog-jensen-huang)

**IBM and NVIDIA announce expanded enterprise AI collaboration at GTC** — IBM and NVIDIA used GTC 2026 to announce a substantially expanded collaboration centred on helping enterprises operationalise AI at production scale. Key strands: IBM Cloud will offer NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs in early Q2 2026 for large-scale training and high-throughput inference; IBM Consulting will bring Red Hat AI Factory with NVIDIA to clients through IBM Consulting Advantage; and joint work spans GPU-native data analytics, intelligent document processing, on-prem/regulated infrastructure, and consulting. _Why it matters:_ The biggest non-hyperscaler NVIDIA partnership of the conference and the clearest signal yet that Red Hat AI Factory is IBM's bet to own the on-prem agentic stack — competing with VMware Private AI and Cisco's enterprise AI bundle. [IBM Newsroom — IBM and NVIDIA expanded collaboration at GTC 2026](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-03-16-ibm-and-nvidia-announce-expanded-collaboration-at-gtc-2026-to-advance-ai-for-the-enterprise)

**NVIDIA NemoCLAW + OpenCLAW: enterprise agent orchestration frameworks debut** — Among the largest-attended GTC sessions were the new NemoCLAW and OpenCLAW frameworks for enterprise agent orchestration — the Agent Toolkit-based stack for building, deploying, and securing autonomous agents in regulated environments. Coverage from Digitimes framed GTC's clearest signal: agentic AI moved from demo to production this week. _Why it matters:_ NVIDIA now has a first-party answer to LangChain/LlamaIndex/CrewAI at the orchestration layer — bundled with NeMo, Nemotron and Blueprints — and positioned as the "enterprise-safe" route on top of its own silicon. [Digitimes — GTC 2026 agentic AI era arrives](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260317VL200/nvidia-2026-gtc-infrastructure-ceo.html) · [GenEngNews — agentic AI inflection hits healthcare](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-gtc-2026-agentic-ai-inflection-hits-healthcare-and-life-sciences/)

**Google Cloud lights up its GTC presence — Gemini × NVIDIA infrastructure preview** — Google Cloud detailed its NVIDIA-anchored AI infrastructure expansion at GTC 2026: Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform compute footprint built on Blackwell Ultra and forthcoming Vera Rubin instances, with co-marketing into Cloud Next (April 21). _Why it matters:_ Confirms the multi-vendor pattern — Google buys NVIDIA at the top of the stack even as its own 7th/8th-gen TPU programmes scale — and frames the Anthropic-on-Google compute story that lands later in April. [Google Cloud Blog — at NVIDIA GTC 2026](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/compute/google-cloud-ai-infrastructure-at-nvidia-gtc-2026)

**NVIDIA Agent Toolkit launches with 17 enterprise software anchor partners** — Jensen Huang unveiled the Agent Toolkit, an open-source platform for building autonomous AI agents, with 17 enterprise software companies committing to build their next generation of AI products on it: Adobe, Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow, Siemens, CrowdStrike, Atlassian, Cadence, Synopsys, IQVIA, Palantir, Box, Cohesity, Dassault Systèmes, Red Hat, Cisco and Amdocs. _Why it matters:_ The 17-vendor commitment is the broadest single-day endorsement of any enterprise agent framework to date. With LangChain/LlamaIndex/CrewAI fragmented across labs and SDKs, Agent Toolkit's neutral-substrate framing instantly becomes a procurement default for buyers who want one runtime across multiple model vendors. [VentureBeat — NVIDIA launches enterprise AI agent platform with 17 adopters at GTC 2026](https://venturebeat.com/technology/nvidia-launches-enterprise-ai-agent-platform-with-adobe-salesforce-sap-among) · [NVIDIA — GTC 2026 live blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/gtc-2026-news/)

**Cisco × NVIDIA expand Secure AI Factory from data center to edge** — Cisco announced a major expansion of its Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA giving customers a framework for deploying AI across the entire infrastructure — central data center to local edge sites where data is created and decisions made. New: Cisco UCS and Unified Edge support for NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs at the enterprise edge; embedded security from the start; deployment timelines compressed from months to weeks. _Why it matters:_ Distributed AI factories — pushing inference to the edge for latency, sovereignty, and cost reasons — is the architecture every regulated-industry buyer is asking for. Cisco's partnership gives them a single-vendor reference design that spans the full topology. [Cisco Newsroom — Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA](https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m03/cisco-secure-ai-factory-with-nvidia-GTC-2026.html) · [NVIDIA Blog — Cisco and NVIDIA advance security for enterprise AI factories](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/cisco-nvidia-security-enterprise-ai/)

**AWS and NVIDIA expand partnership: 1M+ GPUs and Nemotron 3 Super coming to Amazon Bedrock** — AWS used GTC to commit to deploying more than 1 million NVIDIA Blackwell- and Rubin-architecture GPUs across global AWS regions starting in 2026, with Amazon EC2 the first major cloud to ship NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super (hybrid MoE for multi-agent reasoning) lands on Amazon Bedrock, with reinforcement fine-tuning of Nemotron models directly inside Bedrock arriving for finance/healthcare/legal verticalisation. _Why it matters:_ This is the largest single GPU-procurement signal of the GTC week and finally puts NVIDIA's enterprise-tuned Nemotron family inside the most-deployed managed-LLM service. [AWS Machine Learning Blog — AWS and NVIDIA deepen strategic collaboration](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/aws-and-nvidia-deepen-strategic-collaboration-to-accelerate-ai-from-pilot-to-production/) · [AWS at NVIDIA GTC 2026](https://aws.amazon.com/events/aws-at-nvidia-gtc26/)

**xAI's Colossus runs on NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet at GTC scale** — NVIDIA published its GTC-day press release confirming Spectrum-X Ethernet networking powers xAI's Colossus, the world's largest AI supercomputer training the Grok family. xAI is doubling Colossus to 200,000 NVIDIA Hopper GPUs. _Why it matters:_ The first detailed NVIDIA disclosure of the xAI training topology — Spectrum-X (vs InfiniBand) at this scale validates Ethernet as the AI-training fabric and ties Grok roadmap capacity directly into NVIDIA's networking story. [NVIDIA Newsroom — Spectrum-X Ethernet powers xAI Colossus](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/spectrum-x-ethernet-networking-xai-colossus)
