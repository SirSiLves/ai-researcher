## Major announcements & releases

**White House unveils National AI Legislative Framework** — President Trump released the U.S. National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence on 20 March, marking the most significant federal step toward a unified U.S. approach to AI regulation. The framework lays out federal priorities around frontier-model export controls, agentic-AI safety attestations and a coordinated federal–state pre-emption stance for high-risk deployments. _Why it matters:_ Sets up the U.S. side of the regulatory contrast for the spring — EU AI Act trilogue ramps the same month — and gives U.S. labs an explicit policy environment to operate against. The pre-emption framing will be the key fault line between the framework and California/New York state AI bills. [White House — President Donald J. Trump unveils national AI legislative framework](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/03/president-donald-j-trump-unveils-national-ai-legislative-framework/)

**Alibaba launches Wukong enterprise multi-agent platform** — Alibaba launched Wukong, an enterprise AI platform designed to manage multiple agents performing tasks such as document editing, approvals and research. Wukong targets Chinese-domestic enterprise users with deep Office 365/WPS integrations and bundled with Alibaba Cloud's Qwen models for inference. _Why it matters:_ The clearest Chinese-domestic answer to ServiceNow's AI Control Tower and Salesforce's Agentforce — and accompanies a wave of Chinese AI agent platforms positioning ahead of DeepSeek V4 (preview lands 24 April). [Crescendo — agentic AI news and breakthroughs](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**NVIDIA Agent Toolkit + OpenShell + AI-Q reach detailed launch coverage** — Friday's roundups crystallised the NVIDIA agent stack into the form enterprise architects will spec against: Agent Toolkit (the open platform), OpenShell (kernel-level secure agent runtime, including a "privacy router" that monitors all outgoing agent communications), Nemotron models for grounded reasoning, and AI-Q blueprints for vertical workloads. _Why it matters:_ The "privacy router" component reframes the kill-switch debate for autonomous agents — agent-level packet inspection moves from research note to shipping product. Compliance teams now have a concrete answer to "how do you stop an agent from exfiltrating?" [MarketingProfs — AI Update March 20, 2026](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54448/ai-update-march-20-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-week) · [Crescendo — agentic AI news and updates](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Meta-backed Manus launches desktop agent app** — Manus released a desktop application allowing its AI agent to operate directly on users' local devices — files, applications and workflows — moving the agent from cloud-only to local-host execution. _Why it matters:_ A meaningful local-agent debut from a Meta-backed startup, joining the desktop-agent category (Anthropic's Claude desktop, Operator-style products, Magic). Local-execution agents are the next axis of competition after MCP-based remote agents. [Crescendo — agentic AI news and updates](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Congressional Democrats introduce GUARDRAILS Act in counter to Trump national AI framework** — Same day as the White House framework, Democrats introduced the GUARDRAILS Act to prohibit President Trump's December 2025 executive order from taking effect — explicitly preserving state authority "to adopt laws to protect the American public in the face of rapidly evolving AI technologies." Sets up the federal-vs-state pre-emption fight that becomes the defining U.S. AI-regulation storyline of Q2. _Why it matters:_ The legislative counter sets up the year's most consequential U.S. policy fight — federal-pre-emption-with-light-touch (White House) vs state-level enforcement-and-rules (California SB-53, NY RAISE Act, GUARDRAILS Act). The outcome shapes which AI obligations OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral and the enterprise platform vendors actually have to comply with through 2027. [Holland & Knight — White House releases a National Policy Framework for AI](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/white-house-releases-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial) · [Crowell & Moring — White House National AI Policy Framework calls for preempting state laws](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/white-house-national-ai-policy-framework-calls-for-preempting-state-laws-protecting-children) · [CSET Georgetown — unpacking the framework](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/unpacking-the-white-house-national-policy-framework-for-ai/)
