## Major announcements & releases

**Weekend research wave: DyMoE for continual learning (CVPR 2026), MIT Han Lab FourOverSix for NVFP4 quantisation** — Two notable academic-adjacent releases circulated over the weekend. (1) DyMoE (CVPR 2026, from zhaoc5) — a Dynamic Mixture of Experts with drift-aware token assignment for continual learning of large vision-language models. (2) MIT Han Lab released FourOverSix — adaptive block-scaled data types for more accurate NVFP4 quantisation. Both target the engineering layer underneath frontier model deployment. _Why it matters:_ DyMoE is the most promising of the spring's continual-learning approaches for VLMs without catastrophic forgetting; FourOverSix is a practical step toward making NVFP4 (the Blackwell/Vera Rubin native low-precision format) deliver better accuracy at production scale. Both push the inference-cost story Jensen Huang opened GTC with. [Build Fast with AI — 12+ AI models in March 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-models-march-2026-releases)

**Sunday "month-in-review" framing crystallises: March as "the month that changed AI"** — Multiple long-form retrospectives positioned March 2026 as a pivot month: GTC's $1 T order book, the four-frontier-model release window, the Mythos disclosure, harness-engineering reference architecture, MCP crossing 97 M installs and Sora deprecation. The framing carries into the early April newsletter cycles. _Why it matters:_ The retrospective narrative settles around three themes — (1) inference economics, not training breakthroughs, drive procurement; (2) agentic-AI is now production-default; (3) the harness, not the model, is the product. Sets up the OpenAI fund-close (31 Mar) and Salesforce/Slack overhaul (31 Mar) as natural culminations. Industry analysts framed Spud as the OpenAI side of the "production over experimental" portfolio focus that Sora's discontinuation also signalled. _Why it matters:_ Sets the empirical anchor for the GPT-5.5 release timeline driving OpenAI's enterprise-platform answer to Anthropic's Mythos.
