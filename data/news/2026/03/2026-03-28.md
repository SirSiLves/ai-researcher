## Major announcements & releases

**Liquid AI ships LFM2.5-350M — 350 M-parameter agentic-loop model trained on 28 T tokens** — Liquid AI released LFM2.5-350M, a 350 M-parameter model trained on 28 T tokens with scaled RL — engineered specifically for reliable agentic loops, data extraction, and tool use on CPUs, GPUs and mobile (under 500 MB quantised). Pitch is "frontier-grade tool-use behaviour in a model small enough for on-device deployment." _Why it matters:_ Joins the OpenAI mini/nano (17 Mar) and the upcoming PrismML "1-bit Bonsai" iPhone-resident model in the spring's sub-1 B-parameter agent-tier surge. The 28 T-token training pre-conditions LFM2.5 as a true agent specialist — not a chat model that happens to be small. [Build Fast with AI — 12+ AI models in March 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-models-march-2026-releases)

**xAI loses last original co-founder — all 11 founders now gone, Musk in middle of "rebuild from foundations" (Mar 28)** — TechCrunch reported Saturday that Elon Musk's last remaining xAI co-founder has departed, completing the exodus of all 11 original founders. Comes two weeks after Musk publicly conceded "xAI was not built right first time around, so is being rebuilt from the foundations up." New hires include Andrew Milich, Jason Ginsberg (formerly Cursor) and Devendra Chaplot (Mistral co-founder); Musk and engineering-talent lead Baris Akis are reportedly working through past interview pipelines. _Why it matters:_ The structural-talent question for the third frontier-lab in the U.S. is now a 6-month overhang — directly relevant for procurement teams weighing Grok 4.20 against GPT-5.4 and Claude Opus 4.6 in agentic-deployment RFPs. Coincides with the SpaceX/xAI valuation-merger reframing. [TechCrunch — Elon Musk's last co-founder reportedly leaves xAI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/28/elon-musks-last-co-founder-reportedly-leaves-xai/) · [TheNextWeb — all 11 xAI co-founders have now left](https://thenextweb.com/news/xai-all-cofounders-departed-musk-spacex-rebuild)

**Saturday Mythos coverage wave: NewsGhana, Techzine carry international framing** — International tech press picked up the Mythos disclosure in earnest on Saturday — NewsGhana, Techzine and a handful of regional outlets framed the leak as a cybersecurity-policy concern with international implications. Anthropic's "human error" framing carried forward; security-research bodies signalled interest in the leaked benchmark numbers. _Why it matters:_ The international pickup is what shifts Mythos from "Fortune scoop" to "ongoing policy story" — particularly important in EU and Commonwealth jurisdictions where the cybersecurity framing intersects with AI Act trilogue dynamics. [NewsGhana — Anthropic's leaked Mythos raises cybersecurity alarm](https://www.newsghana.com.gh/anthropics-leaked-mythos-model-raises-cybersecurity-alarm/) · [Techzine — details leak on Anthropic's step-change Mythos model](https://www.techzine.eu/news/applications/140017/details-leak-on-anthropics-step-change-mythos-model/)

**Waymo doubles to 500K paid rides per week — half of end-of-year target reached in Q1** — Weekend reporting confirmed Waymo doubled to 500K paid rides per week in under a year, reaching half its end-of-year target of 1M rides just a quarter into 2026. Zoox simultaneously expanded to Austin and Miami, quadrupling its San Francisco service area. Uber separately announced plans to launch Europe's first robotaxi service with Pony AI and Verne in Zagreb. _Why it matters:_ AV scaling is now visibly compounding — the Q1-2026 throughput numbers materially de-risk Waymo's $16 B February raise at $126 B valuation. Zoox's 4× SF expansion and Uber's Zagreb plans together signal that the autonomy-as-service category has crossed from "demo" to "category formation" globally, paralleling the agentic-AI thesis Jensen Huang opened GTC with.
