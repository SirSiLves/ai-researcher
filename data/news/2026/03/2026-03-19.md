## Major announcements & releases

**GTC 2026 wraps: agentic AI declared the dominant 2026 theme** — Thursday closed out NVIDIA's GTC 2026 with the week's framing solidified: agentic AI is the production conversation, NVIDIA owns the silicon-to-orchestration substrate (Vera Rubin → Agent Toolkit → NemoCLAW), and the $1 T order book through 2027 is the metric that floors every macro-AI argument for the rest of the quarter. End-of-week coverage from CNBC, Tom's Hardware and TechRadar settled on the takeaway: Jensen Huang reframed "AI infrastructure" to mean the full inference and agent economics, not just GPUs. _Why it matters:_ The competitive consequence: Anthropic's harness-engineering blog landing on Monday 23 March, OpenAI's funding-round close on 31 March, and the Salesforce/Slack agentic overhaul also on 31 March all read against the new GTC framing. [CNBC — NVIDIA GTC 2026 wrap: agentic AI takes center stage](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/20/nvidia-gtc-2026-agentic-ai-chips-tech-download.html) · [TechRadar — relive the Jensen Huang keynote](https://www.techradar.com/pro/live/nvidia-gtc-2026-live-coverage-all-the-news-and-updates-as-it-happens)

**Google DeepMind researcher publishes "The Abstraction Fallacy" — consciousness-cannot-be-instantiated paper** — Alexander Lerchner, affiliated with Google DeepMind, published "The Abstraction Fallacy: Why AI Can Simulate But Not Instantiate Consciousness" on 19 March, arguing computation structurally cannot instantiate consciousness. The paper explicitly notes views do not reflect Lerchner's employer. By late April it had accumulated 28,304 downloads on PhilArchive, ranking fifth among all papers over the preceding six months. _Why it matters:_ Surfaces the philosophical front of the AI-safety debate ahead of EU AI Act trilogue activity in April. Useful counter-weight to the AGI-imminent narrative running through Hassabis's separate 10×-industrial-revolution claims. [PPC.land — Google DeepMind researcher argues AI can never be conscious](https://ppc.land/google-deepmind-researcher-argues-ai-can-never-be-conscious/)

**MCP install/use data circulates: ~97 M monthly SDK installs and MCP roadmap shift** — The Model Context Protocol team's transition to a priority-area-based 2026 roadmap (originally posted 9 Mar) continued to circulate alongside the ~97 M monthly install figure for the official TypeScript and Python SDKs that Anthropic surfaced. Coverage frames MCP as having decisively crossed the threshold from experimental standard to foundational agentic infrastructure. _Why it matters:_ Underwrites the procurement justification every enterprise MCP-server pitch (AWS, Jama, Slack, ServiceNow's Action Fabric) will use through Q2 — "more installs than most package managers see in a year" is now the gravity argument. [MCP blog — 2026 MCP roadmap](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-mcp-roadmap/) · [Digital Applied — MCP hits 97 M downloads](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mcp-97-million-downloads-model-context-protocol-mainstream)

**Hugging Face publishes "State of Open Source on Hugging Face: Spring 2026"** — Hugging Face's spring open-source state report dropped on Thursday 19 March, surveying open-weight model and dataset trends across the platform — usage rankings, family-tree maps for the top open-weight models (Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek, Mistral, Gemma), and downloads/finetune ratios. _Why it matters:_ The community's reference document for the next two quarters of "what's actually getting used" in open-source LLMs — important counterweight to the closed-frontier narrative dominating the GTC week. Sets the empirical baseline that ml-intern (Hugging Face's open-source AI agent automating LLM post-training) and similar Hugging Face tooling are pitched against. [Hugging Face — State of Open Source spring 2026](https://huggingface.co/blog/huggingface/state-of-os-hf-spring-2026)

**Perplexity launches Perplexity Health — Apple Health, EHR + wearable integration for medical Q&A** — Perplexity unveiled Perplexity Health, a personal health-data search engine that connects Apple Health, EHRs from 1.7M+ care providers (via b.well Connected Health), plus Fitbit/Ultrahuman/Withings/Clue (via Terra API). Rolling out to Pro/Max US subscribers across iOS and perplexity.ai/health. _Why it matters:_ First mainstream consumer-AI surface with first-party EHR aggregation — meaningful test of whether HIPAA-compliant data integrations are now table stakes for AI search products and a direct foothold in the territory Amazon Health, Apple, and Epic have been circling. [Perplexity blog — Introducing Perplexity Health](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-perplexity-health) · [9to5Mac — Apple Health integration](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/19/apple-health-integrates-with-newly-announced-perplexity-health-ai-feature/)

**ServiceNow AI Gateway March 2026 release lands — MCP server registry imports, automated CIMD client registration, PII detection toggle** — ServiceNow's March 2026 release of AI Gateway (its governance layer for AI-agent connections) shipped key MCP-control features: AI Stewards can browse/import MCP servers directly from the MCP community registry into the AI Control Tower; enforcement now active for approved servers in AI Agent Studio; automated client registration via CIMD (Client Identity Metadata Document) lets a single registration apply across all MCP servers on a host; PII detection toggle per individual MCP server. _Why it matters:_ ServiceNow's the second hyperscaler-class enterprise vendor (after AWS) to ship deep MCP governance primitives. The CIMD model — register the client once, automatically apply across all servers — is the operational pattern other enterprise platforms are likely to adopt. [ServiceNow Community — AI Gateway: What's New in the March 2026 Release](https://www.servicenow.com/community/now-assist-articles/ai-gateway-what-s-new-in-the-march-2026-release/ta-p/3501670)
