## Major announcements & releases

**xAI ships Grok 4.20 — real-time web access, full GPT-5.4 / Gemini 3.1 head-to-head** — xAI released Grok 4.20 on Sunday 22 March, with enhanced real-time web access, longer context windows and benchmark gains targeted at coding, reasoning and tool use. Positioned explicitly against GPT-5.4 standard and Gemini 3.1, the release closes out the most compressed competitive launch window of the year (12 models from six labs across roughly two weeks). _Why it matters:_ Grok 4.20 narrows the gap on agentic-coding evaluations and resets the "third-place frontier lab" conversation. The real-time web access is the differentiator — it's the only frontier-tier model whose default behaviour treats fresh X/web data as first-class context. [Build Fast with AI — 12+ AI models in March 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-models-march-2026-releases) · [Mean.ceo — March 2026 AI model releases](https://blog.mean.ceo/new-ai-model-releases-news-march-2026/)

**Weekend Mythos rumours circulate — Anthropic CMS draft still inside its content store** — Independent observers continued to speculate on Anthropic's quiet Mythos work over the weekend, ahead of the formal Fortune disclosure on Thursday 26 March. The draft blog post and ~3,000 unpublished assets were still sitting inside the misconfigured CMS, publicly searchable. _Why it matters:_ The four-day gap between weekend chatter and the formal disclosure is the period in which the security researchers Roy Paz (LayerX) and Alexandre Pauwels (Cambridge) appear to have done the bulk of their data-collection work. The eventual disclosure recasts everything Anthropic did in this window. [Fortune — Anthropic Mythos AI model representing step change](https://fortune.com/2026/03/26/anthropic-says-testing-mythos-powerful-new-ai-model-after-data-leak-reveals-its-existence-step-change-in-capabilities/) · [Techzine — details leak on Anthropic's "step-change" Mythos model](https://www.techzine.eu/news/applications/140017/details-leak-on-anthropics-step-change-mythos-model/)

**Grok 4.20 highest-scoring frontier model on news/current-events factuality** — Sunday's launch of Grok 4.20 came with internal benchmark claims that the model scored highest among all March frontier model releases on benchmarks measuring accuracy on news and events published within the prior 30 days. xAI tied the differentiation explicitly to Grok's first-class real-time web/X data access and source-attribution improvements over Grok 4.10. _Why it matters:_ Sets the post-March frame for "factual recency" as a measurable axis the other frontier labs now have to compete on — particularly relevant for enterprise newsroom, legal-research, and finance use cases where the cost of stale answers is highest. [Build Fast with AI — 12+ AI models in March 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-models-march-2026-releases) · [AI Flash Report — Model Release Timeline 2025-2026](https://aiflashreport.com/model-releases.html)
