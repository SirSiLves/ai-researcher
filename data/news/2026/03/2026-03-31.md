## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI closes $122 B mega-round at $852 B valuation — largest private funding round in history** — OpenAI formally closed its February-launched funding round at $122 B committed capital, $852 B post-money valuation. SoftBank co-leads with Andreessen Horowitz, D.E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG and T. Rowe Price; Amazon, Nvidia and Microsoft participate; ~$3 B from individual investors via banks. Revolving credit facility expanded to ~$4.7 B across JPMorgan, Citi, Goldman, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, Mizuho, RBC, SMBC, UBS, HSBC and Santander. OpenAI confirms $2 B/month revenue, 900 M weekly ChatGPT users, enterprise above 40% of revenue, and an IPO timeline "as soon as late 2026." _Why it matters:_ Sets the funding ceiling for the entire frontier-lab race for the rest of 2026; turns the late-2026 IPO into the explicit base case; and locks in the OpenAI/Microsoft/Amazon/Nvidia partnership triangle as structural for the rest of the decade. **Salesforce reframes Slack as the agentic operating system — 30+ new AI features, Slack MCP server GA, Claude under the hood** — Salesforce launched a comprehensive Slackbot AI overhaul: meeting transcription across any video platform, desktop activity monitoring, MCP-driven task execution against 2,600+ Slack-marketplace apps and 6,000+ Salesforce AppExchange apps, and a lightweight CRM. Slack's MCP server is generally available; 50+ partners (including Anthropic, Google, OpenAI) are building context-aware agents inside Slack. Powered by Anthropic Claude. Live for Business+/Enterprise+ now; Pro/free roll-out from April. _Why it matters:_ Salesforce is down ~30% YTD on AI-disintermediation fears ("Saaspocalypse"); this is Benioff's signature counter-pitch — turning the most-used enterprise chat surface into the agent control plane, using MCP as the lever to keep Workday/Notion/ServiceNow inside Salesforce's orbit. **OpenAI Workspace Agents: enterprise successor to custom GPTs plugs into Slack, Salesforce and more** — OpenAI unveiled Workspace Agents, the successor product to custom GPTs aimed at enterprises and built to plug directly into Slack, Salesforce and adjacent SaaS surfaces. Positioned as the OpenAI side of the same "agents inside the SaaS surfaces" play Salesforce/Anthropic launched on Slack. _Why it matters:_ The OpenAI launch is the explicit competitive answer to the Salesforce/Anthropic Slack overhaul announced the same day — the two product launches together formalise the "every SaaS app is now an agent host" market structure. [VentureBeat — OpenAI unveils Workspace Agents](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/openai-unveils-workspace-agents-a-successor-to-custom-gpts-for-enterprises-that-can-plug-directly-into-slack-salesforce-and-more)

**Anthropic signs Memorandum of Understanding with Australian Government on AI safety** — Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei met PM Anthony Albanese in Canberra to formalise an MOU for collaboration on AI safety research and Australia's National AI Plan. Centrepiece: work with Australia's AI Safety Institute, sharing emerging-capability and risk findings, joint safety/security evaluations, and research collaborations with Australian academic institutions. Anthropic shares Economic Index data with the Australian government covering natural resources, agriculture, healthcare, financial services. AUD$3 M in research partnerships with ANU, Murdoch Children's Research Institute, the Garvan Institute, and Curtin University. Sydney office (Anthropic's fourth Asia-Pacific office) opens this year; data-centre and energy investments under exploration. _Why it matters:_ The first major frontier-lab AI-safety MOU outside the U.S./UK; the template Anthropic uses for its broader public-sector engagement strategy and a counter-balance to the bruising Mythos leak. [Anthropic — Australian government MOU for AI safety and research](https://www.anthropic.com/news/australia-MOU) · [Industry.gov.au — MOU between Australian Government and Anthropic](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/memorandum-understanding-between-australian-government-and-anthropic-collaboration-ai-opportunities) · [W.media — Australia signs AI MoU with Anthropic, flags data centre investment](https://w.media/australia-signs-ai-mou-with-anthropic-flags-data-centre-investment/)

**Anthropic ships `@anthropic-ai/claude-code` v2.1.88 — the missing `.npmignore` that becomes the Claude Code source-code leak** — Late-evening / overnight U.S. Pacific time, Anthropic shipped v2.1.88 of the Claude Code npm package, which (unknown at publish time) included a 59.8 MB JavaScript source map containing ~513,000 lines of unobfuscated TypeScript across roughly 1,900 files — the full client-side agent harness, including hidden subsystems ("BUDDY" companion, user-frustration-detection layer). Discovery and a clean-room rewrite hitting 50 K GitHub stars in two hours land within the next ~24 hours. _Why it matters:_ The second Anthropic packaging incident in a week (after the Mythos CMS leak on 26 March) — and the most detailed public look ever at how a frontier lab builds an agentic coding tool. Five days after the harness-design engineering post lands, the full harness is in the wild. Sets up Anthropic's DMCA-bombing of ~8,100 GitHub repos on 1 April. [Bloomberg — Anthropic scrambles to address Claude Code source leak (Apr 1)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-01/anthropic-scrambles-to-address-leak-of-claude-code-source-code) · [CNBC — Anthropic leaks Claude Code internal source (Apr 1)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/31/anthropic-leak-claude-code-internal-source.html) · [Axios — Anthropic leaked 500,000 lines of own source code (Apr 1)](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/31/anthropic-leaked-source-code-ai)

**OpenAI total revenue and ARR signals — ChatGPT enterprise eclipses 40% of revenue** — Alongside the fund-close announcement OpenAI confirmed $2 B/month run-rate, 900 M weekly active ChatGPT users, and that enterprise represents above 40% of revenue. The figures frame the basis for the early-April reporting around Anthropic's competing $30 B ARR run-rate. _Why it matters:_ The first time OpenAI has publicly disclosed enterprise as the majority-growth contributor to revenue. Reframes ChatGPT as a B2B platform with a consumer top-of-funnel, not the other way around — and supports the late-2026 IPO base case.
