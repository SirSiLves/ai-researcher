## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

- **Why Most Enterprise MCP Architectures Are Already Broken** — Prashanth Kurimella (MuleSoft Principal SE), 2026-05-16. Six anti-patterns in enterprise MCP rollouts: shipping raw tool surfaces, conflating UI-permissions with agent-permissions, inconsistent auth across servers, agents burning cycles on 403-Forbidden tools, pulling thousands of records into context instead of routing to specialist agents, and Franken-stacks of Zapier glue + "system prompt" governance. Argues MCP needs an enterprise control plane with tool-level governance, identity-aware execution, destructive-action guardrails. _Why interesting:_ One of the cleanest practitioner taxonomies of the MCP-governance gap; concrete enough to map onto our own stack. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-most-enterprise-mcp-architectures-already-broken-kurimella-slfkc/]

- **Shadow MCP: The Governance Gap Already Inside Your Agent Stack** — Kranthi Manchikanti (Agentic AI Architect), 2026-05-15. Frames "Shadow MCP" as the 2014 shadow-IT problem compressed into 18 months: 97M+ monthly SDK downloads, 10,000+ active servers, OAuth flows never designed for agents acting on behalf of users acting on behalf of other agents. Lays out a four-layer fix (discovery + tool-list hashing, signed manifests in a private registry, Enterprise-Managed Authorization, observability with hash-diff alerts) and a week-by-week build order. _Why interesting:_ Names the EMA extension (Nov 2025, from Okta's Cross App Access) as the first credible answer to the confused-deputy problem, and is honest about what EMA does not solve. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/shadow-mcp-governance-gap-already-inside-your-agent-manchikanti-n52ce/]

- **Anthropic Took the Unfair Bullet for Ending Finger-Based Pricing** — Anh-Tho Chuong (CEO, Lago), 2026-05-18. Reads the Claude Agent SDK pricing split (from June 15: Agent SDK / `claude -p` / GitHub Actions usage moves out of the normal Claude subscription pool into a separate monthly credit pool — $20 Pro, $100 Max 5x, $200 Max 20x) as the first vendor publicly admitting that "human at keyboard" was a hidden 15–30x subsidy that doesn't survive agents. Compares to GitHub Copilot's June 1 move from premium requests to AI Credits and argues every provider will end up unbundling interactive vs agentic usage. _Why interesting:_ Direct relevance to our Claude/Claude-Code spend planning; cites Zed and theo's reactions so the developer-side backlash is documented. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/anthropic-took-unfair-bullet-ending-finger-based-pricing-chuong-k6nic/]

- **How Anthropic removed the biggest security blocker for AI agents?** — GenAI Works newsletter (The Atlas), 2026-05-20. Recap of Anthropic's Code with Claude (London) announcements: self-hosted sandboxes and MCP tunnels for Claude Managed Agents — orchestration still runs in Anthropic, but tool execution, file access, package installation, repo work, long-running builds stay inside the customer's security perimeter; MCP tunnels (research preview) reach private-network MCP servers without public exposure. Also condenses the "five mistakes scaling Claude Code" playbook (CLAUDE.md stuffing, prompts that should be hooks, tribal setups, premature MCP, Skills vs CLAUDE.md). _Why interesting:_ One-stop summary of the Anthropic enterprise/security push that landed this week — directly on the user's focus areas. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-anthropic-removed-biggest-security-blocker-ai-agents-7e2we/]

- **What Anthropic's 80x Quarter Tells Enterprise IT Leaders About GPU Utilization** — DDN Newsroom (Stephanie Giard), 2026-05-14. Pulls a single Dario Amodei line from Code with Claude — Anthropic planned for 10x growth in 2026 and got 80x; $30B annualized run rate up from ~$9B — and uses it as the setup for an enterprise GPU-utilization argument (Cast AI's 5% average, Cornell's ~50% on production clusters). Goes into vendor-pitch territory (DDN EXAScaler + Infinia + BlueField) but the framing of "the gap will be filled, or not filled, by your data infrastructure" is useful. _Why interesting:_ Has the cleanest public summary I've seen of the Anthropic capacity build-out (Colossus 1 / 220k NVIDIA GPUs, 5 GW AWS, 3.5 GW Google/Broadcom from 2027, $30B Azure). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-anthropics-80x-quarter-tells-enterprise-leaders-gpu-utilization-2b6fc/]

- **SAP's "Autonomous Enterprise" Isn't Another Product Launch — It's SAP Admitting Oracle Was Right** — Braincranx / Jyotiee Insights, 2026-05-15. Reads SAP Sapphire 2026's Autonomous Enterprise announcement (SAP Business AI Platform + SAP Autonomous Suite, partnerships with Anthropic, Nvidia, AWS, Google, Microsoft) as SAP explicitly abandoning the "conversational AI is enough, autonomous agents are premature" positioning it had held against Oracle since 2023–24. Anthropic-specific take: SAP is betting that Claude's safety/reasoning posture is the right fit for ERP-grade autonomy and chooses partnership over Oracle-style in-house AI build. _Why interesting:_ This is the SAP×Anthropic deal in narrative form, and explicitly connects to Joule + the autonomous-suite story we've been tracking. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/saps-autonomous-enterprise-isnt-another-product-launchits-sap-7h88c/]

- **THE AI OMNIBUS: EUROPE POSTPONES THE DEADLINE TO SAVE THE APPLICATION OF THE AI REGULATION** — Mathieu Gitton (FuturEUroScope), 2026-05-15. Long, careful walk-through of the May 6–7, 2026 provisional political agreement on Omnibus VII: high-risk Annex III obligations now apply 2 December 2027 (was August 2026); embedded AI in sectoral safety legislation (lifts, toys) pushed to 2 August 2028; Machinery Regulation moves out of dual-conformity; tightened "safety component" definition; nudification/CSAM tools added to Article 5 bans; sandboxes deadline shifted to 2 August 2027; synthetic-content labelling kept at 2 December 2026. _Why interesting:_ Closest thing to a full-text walkthrough of the Omnibus deal — directly relevant to "AI governance / EU AI Act" focus area. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-omnibus-europe-postpones-deadline-save-application-mathieu-gitton-smkde/]

- **The 16-Month Breathing Room: What the EU AI Act's New December 2027 Deadline Means for HR Leaders** — Kazi Adnan Raihan, 2026-05-17. HR-flavoured but useful summary of why the Annex III high-risk deadline slipped (harmonised technical standards and accredited conformity assessment bodies aren't ready), plus an action plan oriented around inventory, vendor due diligence on EU AI Act roadmap, cross-functional governance committee, anchoring on ISO/IEC 42001 while EU standards land. _Why interesting:_ Pairs well with the Gitton piece — same deadline shift, but read through the high-risk-deployer lens. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/16-month-breathing-room-what-eu-ai-acts-new-december-2027-raihan-vxhmc/]

- **Your RAG Application Probably Sends More Sensitive Data Than You Think** — Rupesh Tiwari (Senior CSL @ AWS), 2026-05-20. Quick but pointed piece: most RAG teams optimise for accuracy/latency and never trace what actually leaves the system in the assembled context payload — system prompts, retrieved vector chunks, user prompts merged before inference can carry PII, internal docs, customer IDs, financial data into provider logs. Calls out prompt-lifecycle visibility, retrieval control, secure context assembly, provider retention awareness, runtime payload governance as the new RAG security primitives. _Why interesting:_ Short, but useful framing for the "what does our RAG actually exfiltrate" conversation. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/your-rag-application-probably-sends-more-sensitive-data-rupesh-tiwari-nvgmc/]

- **Context Engineering: RAG** — Rajesh Shenoi, 2026-05-15. Practitioner primer: RAG is fundamentally context engineering — retrieval quality, chunking, hybrid search (BM25 + dense + re-ranking), metadata filtering matter more than model size. Catalogues ~16 named RAG variants (Standard, GraphRAG, Recursive/Multi-step, Agentic, Multi-modal, Self-RAG, Contextual RAG…) and argues chunking is the most under-discussed lever. _Why interesting:_ Useful as a baseline reference to point colleagues at; readable, not vendor-flavoured. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-engineering-rag-rajesh-shenoi-g6ftc/]

- **Bottom Up braucht Top Down** — Paul Meyrat (Digitale Verwaltung), 2026-05-17, _lang: de_. Swiss-German governance piece arguing for a "sandwich model" of AI adoption — top-down strategic framing plus bottom-up grassroots experimentation — and that doing only one fails. Ties together OpenAI/Bain's "AI Transformation" report, Andrew McAfee's "Putting AI to Work" essay, Ethan Mollick's "secret cyborgs", and Stanford's Enterprise AI Playbook. Calls out that ~50% of workers hide their AI use because psychological safety is missing, and that without enterprise-sanctioned tools/sandboxes, shadow-IT consumer-tool usage with confidential data is inevitable. _Why interesting:_ Swiss-Verwaltung-specific framing and an explicit Stadt Zürich citation; one of the few substantive de-CH AI-governance pieces this week. [https://de.linkedin.com/pulse/bottom-up-braucht-top-down-paul-meyrat-iki0e/]
