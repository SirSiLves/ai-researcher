## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**Pick Your Agent Control Plane Before It Picks You** — Marc Kermisch (en). A 60-day scan showing Microsoft (Agent 365), ServiceNow (AI Control Tower), Salesforce (Agent Fabric), SAP (Joule Studio), Google (Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform) and AWS (Bedrock AgentCore Policy) all shipped an "agent control plane" — the governance/inventory/policy layer is now the product, not the agent. _Why interesting:_ Best single map of the enterprise agent-governance land-grab and the model-portability-vs-runtime-lock-in argument; notes SAP picking Claude as Joule's reasoning model. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/pick-your-agent-control-plane-before-picks-you-marc-kermisch-eoz9c]

**The EU AI Act now treats multi-agent systems as one system** — Pieter van Schalkwyk (en). Field note arguing the 7 May 2026 high-risk deferral is "operationally irrelevant": the 19 May Commission Article-6 draft guidelines treat compound/multi-agent systems as a single high-risk system assessed end-to-end, and the recast Product Liability Directive (9 Dec 2026), AI insurance exclusions, and ISO 42001 procurement demands all bite earlier. Maps five Act articles (9/10/12/14/15) to concrete agent-architecture controls. _Why interesting:_ Unusually rigorous, heavily-sourced governance + engineering piece tying regulation to runtime design ("agents propose; the platform validates, governs, audits, writes"). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-act-now-treats-multi-agent-systems-one-system-van-schalkwyk-ondmc]

**From Prompt to Context to Harness: The Three Eras of AI Engineering** — Ahmed A. (en). Argues the unit of AI engineering moved from prompt (2020–23) to context window (2023–25) to the agent harness (2025–26), with quantitative evidence that identical models swing 20–35pp on SWE-bench-class tasks purely from the harness (Cursor 46%→80%, Endor Labs GPT-5.5 61.5%→87.2%). _Why interesting:_ Densely cited (Karpathy, Lütke, Chase, Anthropic, OpenAI's Feb-2026 "harness engineering" post); strong "treat the base model as commodity, invest in the harness/evals" thesis with CTO-level recommendations. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-prompt-context-harness-three-eras-ai-engineering-ahmed-albadri-wifgf]

**Building an Official MCP Server: What Every Product Team Needs to Know** — Yifat Matatof Yaacov (en). Frames shipping an MCP server as six product decisions (single vs domain servers, tool-load management, security sign-off, legal, distribution, success metrics). Concrete patterns: Salesforce's 3-tool facade over ~200 Data 360 ops; Anthropic's defer_loading Tool Search (>85% context reduction); AWS consolidating back to one unified server. _Why interesting:_ Practitioner playbook for enterprise MCP, not a hype piece; "design for agents, not humans" and prompt-injection threat model. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/building-official-mcp-server-what-every-product-team-matatof-yaacov-r2tmf]

**Preparing for the Upcoming Updates to the Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — amit singh (en). Operator-focused breakdown of the 2026-07-28 MCP spec revision: stateless protocol core (initialize handshake and Mcp-Session-Id removed — remote servers can run behind plain round-robin LBs), new ext-* extensions (MCP Apps, Tasks), Mcp-Method/Mcp-Name routing headers, OAuth/OIDC authorization hardening, full JSON Schema 2020-12 for tool schemas, and a per-team migration checklist. _Why interesting:_ One of the clearest digests of what actually changes for people running/hosting MCP servers. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/preparing-upcoming-updates-model-context-protocol-mcp-amit-singh-zziye]

**Rethinking RAG applications and purposes: A case study** — Moov AI / Daphne Lafleur (en). Consulting case study using a RAG architecture for *classification* rather than search: an LLM "describes" a support ticket, a fine-tuned bi-encoder (BatchAllTripletLoss) clusters descriptions in a vector DB, and nearest-neighbour classes shrink the final prompt from ~40 classes to ~5 — cutting cost while keeping prompt-level flexibility and a cold-start fallback. _Why interesting:_ Concrete, non-obvious RAG reuse with real engineering detail; antidote to generic "what is RAG" posts. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rethinking-rag-applications-purposes-case-study-moov-ai-expoe]

**MCP: The Quiet Protocol That's Rewriting What It Means to Build a Product** — Luciano Lykkebo (en). Product-management angle on MCP as "USB-C for AI": with 97M+ monthly SDK downloads and the protocol donated to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation, products that don't expose MCP become invisible to agents. Uses Figma Dev Mode MCP (Affirm rebuilt flows in <2 days) as the exemplar; argues PMs now define machine capabilities, not just human features. _Why interesting:_ Reframes MCP adoption as product strategy / "App Store" gatekeeping rather than an engineering detail. (Note: closes with a conference plug.) [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-quiet-protocol-thats-rewriting-what-means-build-product-lykkebo-fq93e]

**PART 1: Navigating the EU AI Act in 2026 — The Digital Omnibus and the New Regulatory Reality** — Reply (en). Timeline-driven explainer of the Digital Omnibus (unveiled 5 Feb 2026, political agreement 7 May 2026): high-risk deadlines extended to 2 Dec 2027 / 2 Aug 2028, SME/small-mid-cap relief from Article 17, consolidated DORA/NIS2 reporting, expanded sandboxes, and new bans on AI 'nudification'/CSAM. _Why interesting:_ Clean operational summary of where the Act stands post-Omnibus; pairs well as the regulatory baseline for the van Schalkwyk piece above. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/part-1-navigating-eu-ai-act-2026-digital-omnibus-new-regulatory-iayee]
