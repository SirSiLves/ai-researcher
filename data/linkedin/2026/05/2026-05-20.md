## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

- **Why Most Enterprise MCP Architectures Are Already Broken** — Prashanth Kurimella (Principal Solutions Engineer, MuleSoft), May 16, 2026.
  Argues enterprises are treating MCP servers like 2010s point-to-point integrations, exposing tools without governance, identity propagation, or a control plane. Walks through six anti-patterns (raw DB tools, no rate limits, no audit trail, no schema review, ad-hoc auth, no environment separation) with concrete remediation per pattern.
  _Why interesting:_ Direct counter-narrative to "ship an MCP server per system" hype — concrete enterprise governance lens.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-most-enterprise-mcp-architectures-already-broken-kurimella-slfkc

- **Inside the Agent Harnesses of Anthropic, OpenAI, LangChain, and Stripe** — Kanis Patel (Co-founder, Founsi AI), May 17, 2026.
  Compares the four published harness designs from the last six months; strips branding and shows the blueprints converge on the same primitives (initializer/runner split, tool registry, message batches, middleware). References Anthropic's coding-agent split, OpenAI's AgentMiddleware, LangChain's "five-primitives" decomposition, and Stripe's Minions one-shot agents. Cites Birgitta Boeckeler's harness-engineering framing.
  _Why interesting:_ Cross-vendor synthesis is rare; useful mental model for building/picking agent platforms.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/inside-agent-harnesses-anthropic-openai-langchain-stripe-kanis-patel-vv6bc

- **Agent codebases that break at month six all break the same way.** — Mike Piccolo (Founder, iii.dev), May 13, 2026.
  Catalogs failure modes that appear once a second user/agent shows up: class-level mutable defaults shared between agents, tool functions swallowing errors with None, LLM-mutated session memory, no separation between invocation source and execution layer. Proposes a registry/memory-worker/engine split where these bugs become impossible at the layer teams normally make them.
  _Why interesting:_ Hard-engineering perspective on agent systems past the demo stage — failure-mode taxonomy with a structural fix.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agent-codebases-break-month-six-all-same-way-mike-piccolo-aqvpc

- **The Quiet Quarter Before the Storm: Why AI Governance Just Became a Board-Level Problem** — ALIGNMT AI, May 16, 2026.
  Frames Q2/Q3 2026 as the runway before the EU AI Act high-risk obligations bite (August 2026 milestones). Argues boards can no longer treat AI governance as quarterly counsel update; outlines what evidence frameworks regulators will ask for, and what changes in director liability when the GPAI/high-risk regimes go live in parallel jurisdictions (EU, UK, US states).
  _Why interesting:_ Maps the regulatory calendar to board-level action items rather than legalese.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/quiet-quarter-before-storm-why-ai-governance-just-became-board-level-toiye

- **EU AI Act Conformity Assessment: What Providers Must Do Before August 2026** — Tijana Zunic Maric (Whisperly AI / AI Comply), May 18, 2026.
  Long-form walkthrough (guest contribution by Jelena Djukanovic) of which high-risk AI systems need a notified-body assessment vs. internal control, what Annex VI and Annex VII require, the relationship between Article 6 classification and Article 50 transparency, and the open-source exemption carve-outs.
  _Why interesting:_ Most actionable conformity-assessment piece this week — useful directly for compliance planning, not summary-of-the-law content.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-act-conformity-assessment-what-providers-must-do-zunic-maric-0dfof

- **RAG Isn't Just Vector Search — Here's the Bigger Picture** — Sanjay Negi (GenAI Mentor & Corporate Trainer), May 17, 2026.
  Pushes back on the "RAG = vector DB" reduction; reframes retrieval as a spectrum (lexical, structured, graph, agentic search) and proposes asking "what retrieval strategy fits my problem?" before picking storage. Short but well-argued primer.
  _Why interesting:_ Useful framing for teams defaulting to pgvector/pinecone for every RAG problem.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rag-isnt-just-vector-search-heres-bigger-picture-sanjay-negi-h66hc

- **Why enterprise AI agents fail in production and what it takes to build them right** — ASSIST Software, May 15, 2026.
  Distills threads from the Future Intelligence Think Tank group: the problem is rarely the model — it's the surrounding infrastructure (eval harnesses, retrieval quality, observability, ownership). Lists the failure patterns recurring across enterprise teams.
  _Why interesting:_ Sober counter-narrative to "we shipped an agent" announcements, decent enumeration of the actual gaps.
  https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-enterprise-ai-agents-fail-production-what-takes-ojmff
