## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

- **Why Most Enterprise MCP Architectures Are Already Broken** — Prashanth Kurimella, MuleSoft (en). Six anti-patterns the author sees repeatedly in production MCP deployments: shipping connectors without tool-level governance, conflating UI access with autonomous-agent access, inconsistent auth (user-identity propagation vs broad service accounts), unfiltered tool catalogs causing 403-storms and wasted tokens, "pull everything into context" rather than agentic specialization, and Franken-stacks of webhooks/wrappers/.env keys. Argues the fix is a control plane with tool-level governance, identity-aware execution, destructive-action guardrails, scoped discovery and orchestration. _Why interesting:_ rare deep-dive on the operational governance gaps MCP itself does not solve — directly relevant to anyone running enterprise MCP servers. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-most-enterprise-mcp-architectures-already-broken-kurimella-slfkc](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-most-enterprise-mcp-architectures-already-broken-kurimella-slfkc)

- **EU AI Act Conformity Assessment: What Providers Must Do Before August 2026** — Tijana Zunic Maric, Whisperly AI (en). Step-through of Article 43 conformity routes: Annex VI internal self-assessment (most Annex III systems if harmonised standards fully applied), Annex VII third-party assessment by a notified body (mandatory for biometric ID systems), and Annex I sectoral track (MDR/IVDR/Machinery). Walks through QMS requirements (Article 17, 11 areas), Annex IV technical documentation, post-market monitoring (Article 72), declaration of conformity (Article 47, retained 10 years), CE marking (Article 48), database registration (Article 49), and substantial-modification triggers (Article 43(4)). Flags two practical bottlenecks in 2026: harmonised standards from CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 not yet finalised, and the pool of designated notified bodies remains very small. _Why interesting:_ procedurally specific — closest thing to a checklist for the August deadline. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-act-conformity-assessment-what-providers-must-do-zunic-maric-0dfof](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-act-conformity-assessment-what-providers-must-do-zunic-maric-0dfof)

- **The Narrow Waist Architecture of the Agent Transfer Protocol** — Chris Hood (en). Proposes AGTP as a sub-application transport layer for agent traffic, applying the IP "narrow waist" principle: identity, scope and audit move out of the JSON payload into transport headers so infrastructure can route and govern without parsing. Specifies AGTP-Cert (X.509v3 with Ed25519 authority-scope commitment integrated into TLS 1.3 mutual auth, validated once by a Scope Enforcement Point), AGTP-Log (governance-platform-signed append-only chained log so agents cannot edit their own audit trail), and an Agent Name Service with behavioral trust score (0.0-1.0) plus tiered cryptographic verification. Frames it as a sibling/substrate for MCP and A2A rather than a replacement. _Why interesting:_ if this gains traction it's the missing transport-layer governance piece that MCP/A2A don't address. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/narrow-waist-architecture-agent-transfer-protocol-chris-hood-cedec](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/narrow-waist-architecture-agent-transfer-protocol-chris-hood-cedec)

- **Context Engineering for Enterprise AI Agents** — Dr. Nabanita Sinha, Deloitte (en). Frames context engineering as managing an agent's working memory rather than just writing prompts. Seven techniques: structured system-prompt design (background/objectives/instructions/tool guidance/constraints/output specs); context compression / compaction (summarise execution state and reinitialise); dynamic/just-in-time retrieval via lightweight references (file paths, queries, IDs); semantic caching at meaning level; layered memory architectures (short/long-term/episodic); sub-agent architectures with context isolation; and context observability (what was retrieved, what memory was injected, where reasoning failures originated). Coins "context rot" for gradual degradation as irrelevant info accumulates. _Why interesting:_ comprehensive practitioner taxonomy for the discipline now widely called "context engineering." [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-engineering-enterprise-ai-agents-dr-nabanita-sinha--ldqtf](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-engineering-enterprise-ai-agents-dr-nabanita-sinha--ldqtf)
