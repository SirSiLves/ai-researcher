## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

- **Shadow MCP: The Governance Gap Already Inside Your Agent Stack** — Kranthi Manchikanti (en). Eighteen months after Anthropic shipped MCP, most enterprises cannot list the servers reachable from their agents; the piece lays out the OAuth confused-deputy + executable tool-description threat model and a four-layer playbook (inventory with tool-list hashing, signed-manifest registry, Enterprise-Managed Authorization, observability with hash-diff alerts). _Why interesting:_ concrete, this-quarter MCP security posture — directly relevant to bank/enterprise rollouts. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/shadow-mcp-governance-gap-already-inside-your-agent-manchikanti-n52ce

- **ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act: Certification is not the same as compliance** — Matej Jambrich, Dentons CTO Europe (en). Walks the gap between ISO/IEC 42001 management-system certification and AI Act legal compliance, with timeline on Digital Omnibus trilogue (29 April failure, ~13 May 2026 next round) and a practical AI register + classification gateway + supplier-control + change-trigger overlay. _Why interesting:_ legal practitioner framing of the exact Annex III / 2 Aug 2026 question Swiss-EU enterprises are facing now. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/isoiec-42001-eu-ai-act-certification-same-compliance-matej-jambrich-fdjnc

- **The Compliance Layer Is Becoming the Company Operating System** — Alex Galert (en). Ties Anthropic Claude Managed Agents pricing (~$0.08 per active session-hour), Google A2A crossing 150 orgs, and the 2 Aug 2026 EU AI Act deadline into one thesis: governance moves from legal-wrapper to runtime control plane (identity, permissions, audit, A2A policy routing). _Why interesting:_ frames "governed autonomy" as a product layer, not paperwork — pairs well with the Shadow-MCP piece. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/compliance-layer-becoming-company-operating-system-alex-galert-vvs5e

- **Agents Don't Replace Integration. They Change Where Integration Happens** — Steve Harris (en). Maps when to use data warehouse vs. API vs. MCP vs. A2A as four coexisting integration layers, with a decision checklist (separate information needs from action needs, decide approved context sources, build the governance layer early). _Why interesting:_ clear enterprise-architecture framing of the MCP/A2A debate — useful for solution-architecture conversations. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agents-dont-replace-integration-change-where-happens-steve-harris-yfskc

- **Agent codebases that break at month six all break the same way** — Mike Piccolo, iii.dev (en). Engineering essay arguing that the surviving production-agent shape uses Worker / Function / Trigger primitives rather than tool-laden Agent classes — typed function boundaries, no class-level state, engine-level OTEL, MCP/A2A collapsed into the same vocabulary. Cites the April 2026 Claude Code postmortem as evidence the runtime, not the model, is where reliability lives. _Why interesting:_ rare deep technical pattern piece on production agent runtimes; directly applicable to internal agent harness design. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agent-codebases-break-month-six-all-same-way-mike-piccolo-aqvpc

- **3DEXPERIENCE: Multi-Agent Workflows Using A2A and MCP** — Raghunath Torase (en). Working prototype linking PLM (3DEXPERIENCE), ALM (Tuleap) and middleware (Kafka) via three heterogeneous agents (CrewAI+Claude, OpenAI Agents SDK+GPT, Google ADK+Gemini) collaborating over A2A, each with its own custom MCP server. _Why interesting:_ rare hands-on A2A+MCP cross-framework demo with named tool stacks — proof point for protocol interoperability. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/3dexperience-multi-agent-workflows-using-a2a-mcp-raghunath-torase-daw1c

- **Einführung in Agents und Subagents in Claude Code** — Ralph Hutter (de). Detailed German walkthrough of Anthropic's 11 May 2026 Agent View release in Claude Code: the Model → Agent → (Tools / Skills / MCP plugins / Subagents) hierarchy, when to convert a Skill to a Subagent, and how `~/.claude/agents/` plus the `description` field drives auto-routing. _Why interesting:_ first substantive German-language explainer of Agent View; useful internal training reference. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/einf%C3%BChrung-agents-und-subagents-claude-code-ralph-hutter-fivqe

- **Vom Hackathon zur Praxis: KI bei den Basler Verkehrs-Betrieben mit Ina Stern** — Thomas Jenewein / EducationNewscast (de). Case study of BVB's SAP AI Hackathon win in Zürich: a "High Quality Order Recommendation" prototype on S/4HANA on-premise using conversational AI + similarity search over five years of SAP-PM history, with Joule as the front-end and ~1,000 hours/year savings plus unblocked warranty billings as the business case. _Why interesting:_ concrete Swiss enterprise GenAI deployment story with named partners (BVB, SAP, Lighthouse) — not vapor. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/vom-hackathon-zur-praxis-ki-bei-den-basler-mit-ina-stern-jenewein-xoqyf

- **KI-Burnout: Der Preis der schnellen Antwort** — Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung / Digitalwirtschaft (de). Synthesises Champniss's HBR "psychological debt" survey, MIT Kosmyna/Maes EEG preprint on "cognitive debt", Microsoft Research's shift-from-execution-to-supervision finding, the UC Berkeley Haas study on AI-driven task densification, and BCG's "AI Brain Fry" data (peak productivity at three concurrent AI tools, 39% more severe errors at higher supervision load). Six leadership levers proposed. _Why interesting:_ the strongest current evidence pack against the "AI just frees up time" sales pitch — useful for change-management framing. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ki-burnout-der-preis-schnellen-antwort-q9kle

- **Multi-Agent Systems Are Becoming the New Enterprise Architecture — And Tasks Are the Wrong Unit of Measurement** — Arkadiusz Gos, KanBo (en). Short essay arguing tasks/hours/activity stop being the right unit once multi-agent systems can generate unlimited activity; replaces it with Purpose → Necessity → Output → Outcome as the measurement chain. _Why interesting:_ a measurement-model framing that complements the runtime/governance pieces above. https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/multi-agent-systems-becoming-new-enterprise-architecture-gos-ezfyf
