## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**The EU AI Act now treats multi-agent systems as one system** — Pieter van Schalkwyk (en). Field note from building an industrial agentic platform: the 19 May 2026 draft Commission Article 6 guidelines treat compound multi-agent systems as a single high-risk system assessed end-to-end, so the Digital Omnibus deadline deferral (high-risk Annex III pushed to Dec 2027) does not actually relieve operators. Maps five concrete engineering obligations to AI Act articles and argues the real forcing functions are the recast Product Liability Directive (Dec 2026), insurer AI exclusions, and procurement demanding ISO 42001 + AI BoM. _Why interesting:_ Heavily-sourced, architecture-level read on how agentic systems break product-safety regulation assumptions — directly on MCP/A2A + governance. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-act-now-treats-multi-agent-systems-one-system-van-schalkwyk-ondmc]

**Pick Your Agent Control Plane Before It Picks You** — Marc Kermisch (en). Argues that across late-March–end-May 2026 every major enterprise vendor (Microsoft Agent 365, ServiceNow AI Control Tower, Salesforce Agent Fabric, SAP Joule Studio, Google Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, AWS Bedrock AgentCore) shipped an "agent control plane" — the governance/inventory/identity layer is now the product, not the agent. Includes a CIS MCP Companion Guide reference and a concrete agent-inventory action plan. _Why interesting:_ Sharp synthesis of 60 days of enterprise agent-platform announcements and the runtime-vs-model lock-in argument. (Note: contains an unverified disparaging aside about a vendor's support — not reproduced here.) [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/pick-your-agent-control-plane-before-picks-you-marc-kermisch-eoz9c]

**Preparing for the Upcoming Updates to the Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — amit singh (en). Operator-focused walkthrough of the 2026-07-28 MCP spec revision: stateless protocol core (initialize handshake and Mcp-Session-Id removed), new Mcp-Method/Mcp-Name routing headers, first-class ext-* extensions, MCP Apps (sandboxed server-rendered UI) and a Tasks extension for long-running work, OAuth/OIDC authorization hardening, deprecation of Roots/Sampling/Logging, full JSON Schema 2020-12 for tool schemas, and one breaking error-code change (-32002 to -32602). _Why interesting:_ Concrete, infrastructure-relevant breakdown of the next MCP spec — exactly the protocol-standards layer in focus. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/preparing-upcoming-updates-model-context-protocol-mcp-amit-singh-zziye]

**What Building Ten MCP Servers Taught Me About Where the Protocol Bites** — Björn D. (en). Practitioner field notes after shipping ten MCP servers in .NET/Python: the protocol is trivial; the real costs are that the tool schema is a prompt (not a signature), that a default value is a public contract whose flip is a breaking change, that stdio is a shared channel where stray output corrupts JSON-RPC, and that unit tests can pass while a cold-start init path is broken. _Why interesting:_ Grounded, non-promotional engineering lessons on MCP tool design — useful counterweight to vendor MCP hype. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-building-ten-mcp-servers-taught-me-where-bites-bj%C3%B6rn-dijkhuizen-c0dwe]

**Why SAP Brought Claude Into Joule as a Primary Reasoning Engine** — Shival T. (en). Analysis of the Sapphire 2026 SAP–Anthropic announcement (12 May): Claude embedded as one of several primary reasoning capabilities (alongside Mistral, Cohere) in the SAP Business AI Platform via MCP, enabling Joule agents to act across finance/HR/procurement/supply chain on governed SAP data. Engages with the "walled garden" critique and argues durable value sits in the context/governance/model-optionality layer above the model. _Why interesting:_ Concrete enterprise deployment of Claude + MCP inside an ERP system of record; balanced treatment of the skeptical (Gartner) read. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-sap-just-made-claude-default-reasoning-engine-joule-shival-tailor-zclfc]

**How I triage AI governance problems** — James Kavanagh (en). Uses Rasmussen's "drift to danger" model to explain why governance attention ends up inversely proportional to real AI risk (visible chatbots get scrutiny; quietly-drifted clinical models get none). Proposes a four-step rapid harm-triage method (stakeholder identification; six impact types; severity/breadth+vulnerability/detectability scoring borrowed from FMEA; priority tiers) as a Day-1 alternative to ISO 42005, Microsoft RAI Impact Assessment, and EU AI Act classification. _Why interesting:_ Practical, framework-aware governance methodology rather than another EU-AI-Act explainer; introduces detectability as the overlooked prioritisation dimension. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-i-triage-ai-governance-problems-james-kavanagh-ucdoc]

**ISO 42001 vs NIST AI RMF: Which One Does Your Organisation Actually Need?** — Latha Karthigaa, PhD (en). Clear side-by-side: ISO 42001 is a certifiable management-system standard (use when proving governance externally — banks, EU AI Act Article 17, regulated procurement); NIST AI RMF is a free, non-certifiable internal risk framework (use for early-stage/internal maturity, US federal alignment). Notes NIST's official crosswalk between the two and five decision scenarios. _Why interesting:_ Useful decision rule for the governance-framework question. (Note: article body is substantive but ends with vendor/course CTAs.) [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/iso-42001-vs-nist-ai-rmf-which-one-does-your-actually-karthigaa-phd-eyxpc]

**Die KI-Illusion: Warum echte Produktivität erst beginnt, wenn wir aufhören zu flicken** — Guido Greber (de). Report from the Digital Gipfel Schweiz 2026 in Andermatt: 88% of Swiss firms now use AI but 41% of CEOs report no ROI; argues productivity only comes from redesigning workflows around AI (the "electricity paradox") rather than bolting Copilot onto old processes, and ties this to Swiss digital sovereignty — data control, the choice between proprietary cloud AI vs locally-hosted open-weight models, energy/compute, the E-ID, and the 2027 Geneva Global AI Summit. _Why interesting:_ Substantive Swiss-perspective piece on enterprise GenAI adoption and sovereignty; quotes Swiss leaders (Swiss Re, ETH, EPFL, Mobiliar). [https://de.linkedin.com/pulse/die-ki-illusion-warum-uns-kleine-tools-nicht-retten-und-guido-greber-8rxhe]
