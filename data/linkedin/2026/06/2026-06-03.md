[https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tollbooth-appears-dave-medd-mctbc](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tollbooth-appears-dave-medd-mctbc)

**Why Agentic AI Will Fail in Enterprises Without an Operating Model** — Michael Curry (en). Argues regulated enterprises (banking, insurance, healthcare, manufacturing) hit an inflection point as automation moves from advisory to acting, and need an operating model — not just tooling — to deploy agents safely. _Why interesting:_ governance-as-prerequisite argument grounded in mission-critical systems. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-agentic-ai-fail-enterprises-without-operating-model-michael-curry-vdlee](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-agentic-ai-fail-enterprises-without-operating-model-michael-curry-vdlee)

**The Context & Control Model: The missing piece of the production AI stack** — Spiros Economakis (en). Posits agents can't run autonomously in production without an explicit behavior model plus governance constraining every action; declares the demo era of AI-in-production over for engineering leaders. _Why interesting:_ concrete production-stack framing of context + control as a discipline. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-control-model-missing-piece-production-ai-stack-economakis-vw9de](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-control-model-missing-piece-production-ai-stack-economakis-vw9de)

**Context is the new code** — James Kaplan (en). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-new-code-james-kaplan-mdkje](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/context-new-code-james-kaplan-mdkje)

**As AI agents evolve, we need to look past the RAG pipeline** — AlphaSignal / Ben Dickson (en). Argues coding agents expose a flaw in traditional RAG pipelines; agentic search needs dynamic plan revision, so giving agents the same direct-corpus tools humans use (grep-style) can beat static retrieval. _Why interesting:_ live debate on RAG vs. agentic direct-corpus interaction. **From the Tower of Babel to the Boardroom: Part 2 – AI Governance Is a Compliance Issue** — Thomas Fox (en). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-tower-babel-boardroom-part-2-ai-governance-compliance-thomas-fox-jyiec](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-tower-babel-boardroom-part-2-ai-governance-compliance-thomas-fox-jyiec)
