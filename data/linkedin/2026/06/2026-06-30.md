## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**The Real Claude Tag Question Is Context Ownership** — AlphaSignal (lang: en). Argues Claude Tag did not invent the Slack-native AI coworker (OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, Viktor already existed) but made it enterprise-credible via agent identity, hosted sandboxes, audit trails and Opus 4.8. Real risk is memory lock-in — proposes a 5-question "Context Sovereignty Test" before broad rollout. _Why interesting:_ sharp, sourced take on the lock-in cost of agent memory ("rent the agent, own the memory"). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/real-claude-tag-question-context-ownership-alphasignal-rijje](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/real-claude-tag-question-context-ownership-alphasignal-rijje)

**SAP and Anthropic: the prerequisite behind the AI hype** — Codestone Group (lang: en). Unpacks SAP Sapphire's Autonomous Enterprise (200+ agents, 50+ Joule Assistants, Claude as primary reasoning engine via MCP) and argues the unspoken prerequisite is a "clean core" — accurate data, standardised processes, minimal legacy customisation. Most mid-market SAP customers aren't there yet. _Why interesting:_ practitioner counterweight to the keynote hype, naming data/process readiness as the real gate. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/sap-anthropic-prerequisite-behind-ai-hype-codestone-group-aryqe](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/sap-anthropic-prerequisite-behind-ai-hype-codestone-group-aryqe)

**The Agent Wars Just Moved: Why Governance, Not Models Is the New Battleground** — Marc Kermisch (lang: en). Connects six weeks of enterprise launches (Microsoft Agent 365, AWS Context/AgentCore, ServiceNow Action Fabric, Salesforce Agent Fabric, Google Gemini Enterprise, SAP Business AI Platform) into one pattern: registry + identity + gateway + observability. The pitch shifted from "smartest agent" to "we govern any agent." Notes Claude becoming the shared reasoning layer; GitHub Copilot's move to token billing as the FinOps twin. _Why interesting:_ best synthesis of the spring/summer vendor convergence around agent governance as the new battleground. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agent-wars-just-moved-why-governance-models-new-marc-kermisch-xw36c](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agent-wars-just-moved-why-governance-models-new-marc-kermisch-xw36c)

**MCP + A2A: The New Operating Layer for Enterprise Agentic AI** — Sanjay Kumar (lang: en). Long, careful walkthrough of how MCP (agent-to-tool) and A2A (agent-to-agent, Agent Cards, task lifecycle, Contract-Net bidding) become the connective tissue of a production agentic stack — plus identity, least-privilege, audit and AgentOps. Argues the durable advantage is architecture around the model, not the model itself. _Why interesting:_ rigorous, vendor-neutral treatment of the two interoperability protocols as a combined operating layer. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-a2a-new-operating-layer-enterprise-agentic-ai-sanjay--smdqc](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-a2a-new-operating-layer-enterprise-agentic-ai-sanjay--smdqc)

**Governing the Agentic Mesh: Why agentgateway and MCP Belong Together** — Shriram Vasudevan (lang: en). Frames MCP as the protocol/contract and agentgateway as the governed data plane: one chokepoint for auth, rate limits, observability, audit and protocol normalisation (MCP/A2A/HTTP). Gives an incremental adoption path. Written for the AI Agents & Infrastructure Foundation. _Why interesting:_ clean architectural separation of the "language problem" (MCP) vs the "control problem" (gateway). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/governing-agentic-mesh-why-agentgateway-mcp-belong-shriram-lmdxc](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/governing-agentic-mesh-why-agentgateway-mcp-belong-shriram-lmdxc)

**MCP Is the Missing Layer Your Enterprise RAG System Didn't Know It Needed** — Brudite (lang: en). Argues enterprise RAG fails at the connector layer, not the model: connector drift, auth fragmentation, observability blind spots, schema coupling. Positions MCP (Resources/Tools/Prompts) as the standard connector contract and gives a connector-audit checklist. _Why interesting:_ reframes RAG reliability as a connector/observability problem and ties it to MCP adoption. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-missing-layer-your-enterprise-rag-system-didnt-know-needed-xzklf](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-missing-layer-your-enterprise-rag-system-didnt-know-needed-xzklf)

**RAG in the Real World: What Nobody Tells You After the Tutorial** — Hyperlink Insights (lang: en). Production-grade survey of where naive RAG breaks (document diversity, retrieval ceiling, semantic-only gaps, context budgeting) and the patterns that work: hybrid retrieval + RRF, cross-encoder re-ranking, query transformation (HyDE, multi-query, step-back), metadata filtering, parent-document retrieval. Covers failure modes (lost-in-the-middle, chunk boundaries) and 2026 best practices. _Why interesting:_ thorough, honest production RAG reference grounded in real failure modes. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rag-real-world-what-nobody-tells-you-after-tutorial-glzaf](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rag-real-world-what-nobody-tells-you-after-tutorial-glzaf)

**RAG : Avant de changer de LLM, regardez votre moteur de recherche** — Massamba Mbengue (lang: fr). _(EN: "RAG: before swapping your LLM, look at your search engine.")_ French-language deep-dive on retrieval strategy as the real lever in production RAG: pre-filtering on metadata, BM25 vs vector vs hybrid (RRF), query expansion, why HyDE has fallen out of favour, reranking, and the 2026 baseline (Agentic RAG, GraphRAG, Contextual Retrieval). Frames the retriever as the first FinOps firewall against million-token prompts. _Why interesting:_ strong non-English practitioner piece arguing retrieval, not the model, is where RAG quality is won. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rag-avant-de-changer-llm-regardez-votre-moteur-massamba-mbengue-vpkoe](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rag-avant-de-changer-llm-regardez-votre-moteur-massamba-mbengue-vpkoe)

**The EU AI Act's New Transparency Code: More Nuance, Less Panic** — Birgit J. Oberer (lang: en). Corrects common misreadings of the Commission's new Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content (Article 50): the Code is voluntary while Article 50 obligations are binding; not every AI image needs a visible label; the provider-vs-deployer distinction; detection tools verify provenance/metadata, they are not reliable "AI detectors." Argues the real challenge for education is governance, not watermarks. _Why interesting:_ measured governance read that separates legal obligation from headline panic. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-acts-new-transparency-code-more-nuance-less-panic-oberer-gwddf](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-acts-new-transparency-code-more-nuance-less-panic-oberer-gwddf)
