## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**The August 2026 Regulatory Cliff That Moved** — Tomislav Vazdar (en). The EU AI Act's high-risk obligations, long pinned to 2 Aug 2026, have slid to 2 Dec 2027, opening cross-framework seams between the AI Act, DORA and NIS2. _Why interesting:_ concrete, dated read on how the delay reshapes enterprise compliance roadmaps. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/august-2026-regulatory-cliff-moved-tomislav-vazdar-dqcse]

**From Compliance as Code to Agentic Policy: How the EU AI Act Must Be Applied to Autonomous Systems** — Philipp Braun (en). Argues existing IT-governance building blocks already cover most EU AI Act needs; the gap is translation into runtime policy for autonomous agents, not new frameworks. _Why interesting:_ contrarian governance take that pushes back on the "agentic AI is uncharted regulatory territory" narrative. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-compliance-code-agentic-policy-how-eu-ai-act-must-philipp-braun-kpyif]

**Trusted Context is the New Enterprise Data Architecture** — Eric Du (en). Agents fail not from missing data but from not knowing what to trust; cites Anthropic figures of ~21% analytics accuracy without curated skills/context vs >95% with them. _Why interesting:_ frames a governed-context "harness" as the architectural unit for reliable enterprise agents. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/trusted-context-new-enterprise-data-architecture-eric-du-jhwjc]

**MCP & Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Architecture: The Future of Enterprise Agentic AI** — Prophecy Technologies (en). Walks through how MCP (model-to-tool context) and A2A (agent-to-agent communication) compose into multi-agent enterprise workflows. _Why interesting:_ one of the cleaner side-by-side explainers of the two interoperability layers together. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-agent-to-agent-a2a-architecture-future-enterprise-agentic-ewtkc]

**MCP vs RAG vs Fine-Tuning: Which AI Architecture Should Your Business Choose in 2026?** — Malgo Technologies (en). Decision framework mapping the three approaches to business objective, data ecosystem, governance needs and long-term strategy. _Why interesting:_ practical architecture-selection lens that treats MCP, RAG and fine-tuning as complementary, not competing. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-vs-rag-fine-tuning-which-ai-architecture-should-harsc]

**Securing Agentic AI at Enterprise Scale: A Practical Framework for Governance, Security, and Trust** — Susant Mallick (en). Framework for securing autonomous and multi-agent systems while keeping governance, compliance and operational control. _Why interesting:_ moves the agentic-security conversation past PoC toward enterprise-grade controls. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/securing-agentic-ai-enterprise-scale-practical-security-mallick-gr73e]

**How to build reliable RAG systems for enterprise knowledge** — Mallow Technologies (en). Catalogs the production failure modes of naive RAG (stale policies, contradictory answers, permission leakage, latency at scale) and how to engineer around them. _Why interesting:_ grounded post-PoC view of why enterprise RAG breaks in production. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-build-reliable-rag-systems-enterprise-xezoc]

**Business-Nutzen durch GenAI: 2026 ist das Jahr der KI-Assistenten** — Swisscom Business (de-CH). Swiss firms tested GenAI but saw little productivity gain; argues 2026 is the moment to roll out legally compliant, productive AI assistants, with adoption and change management mattering more than the tech. _Why interesting:_ Swiss-market enterprise perspective tying GenAI value to organizational adoption over tooling. [https://de.linkedin.com/pulse/business-nutzen-durch-genai-2026-ist-das-jahr-der-5hb6e]

**AI-Twins, Bezahl-Agenten und der Stimmklon des CEO** — Andy Huber (de-CH). Roundup on Mastercard/Visa/PayPal building payment rails for autonomous shopping agents, Google's diagnostic AI in real clinics, and million-scale deepfake fraud; plus a podcast on AI twins and synthetic customers. _Why interesting:_ Swiss practitioner digest connecting agentic-payment infrastructure with the fraud/deepfake risk surface. [https://de.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-twins-bezahl-agenten-und-der-stimmklon-des-ceo-andy-huber-axbne]

**Why Context Engineering is Replacing Prompt Engineering** — Anudeep Katangoori (en). Makes the case that durable agent reliability comes from engineering the context window (retrieval, memory, tool grounding) rather than prompt phrasing. _Why interesting:_ rides the "context engineering" shift that's becoming the dominant frame for agent quality. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-context-engineering-replacing-prompt-anudeep-katangoori-khoqe]
