**Agents Have Been Building One Layer Too High** — Chris Hood (lang: en). Argues MCP/A2A/ACP all ride on HTTP, a document-and-human protocol; proposes AGTP, a transport-layer protocol with cryptographic identity, scoped authority and replay protection as primitives, composing with certificate authorities, GLEIF LEIs and credit bureaus. _Why interesting:_ A genuinely contrarian architectural take — push agent identity/trust/settlement down to the transport layer rather than re-solving them in every framework. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agents-have-been-building-one-layer-too-high-chris-hood-gfuhc]

**MCP + A2A: The New Operating Layer for Enterprise Agentic AI** — Sanjay Kumar PhD, MBA, MS (lang: en). A thorough walkthrough of how MCP (agent-to-tool) and A2A (agent-to-agent) compose into a production agentic stack: Agent Cards, Contract-Net bidding, workload identity, least-privilege scopes, audit, and an AgentOps observability layer. _Why interesting:_ One of the clearest end-to-end treatments of the MCP+A2A enterprise architecture, framed as a distributed-systems problem rather than prompt engineering. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-a2a-new-operating-layer-enterprise-agentic-ai-sanjay--smdqc]

**Governing the Agentic Mesh: Why agentgateway and MCP Belong Together** — Shriram Vasudevan (lang: en). Separates the "language problem" (MCP standardises capability discovery/invocation) from the "control problem" (agentgateway as an open-source data plane enforcing auth, rate limits, observability, audit and protocol normalisation at a single chokepoint). _Why interesting:_ Concrete governance pattern for running agent fleets at scale — the MCP-as-protocol vs gateway-as-data-plane framing is a useful mental model. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/governing-agentic-mesh-why-agentgateway-mcp-belong-shriram-lmdxc]

**MCP Is the Missing Layer Your Enterprise RAG System Didn't Know It Needed** — Brudite Private Limited (lang: en). Reframes enterprise RAG failure as a connector-layer problem, not a model problem: connector drift, auth fragmentation, observability blind spots and schema coupling cause silent wrong answers. Proposes MCP servers per source with capability negotiation, change-event refresh and unified observability. _Why interesting:_ Sharp, practical RAG-ops angle — ACLs and connector freshness as first-class compliance metrics. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-missing-layer-your-enterprise-rag-system-didnt-know-needed-xzklf]
