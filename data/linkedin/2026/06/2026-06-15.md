## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**MCP is the new API — treat it as a do-over** — ReversingLabs (Chainmail newsletter), June 12 (lang: en). MCP is repeating the API-security story at machine speed: the spec leaves auth, authorization, input validation and sandboxing "to the implementer," so tool-poisoning, rug-pull and confused-deputy attacks slip past legacy static/dynamic analysis because the exploit vector is the model's reasoning, not the input parser. _Why interesting:_ concrete framing of MCP-specific threat classes plus quoted practitioners, not a "what is MCP" explainer. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-new-api-treat-do-over-reversinglabs-paxxe](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-new-api-treat-do-over-reversinglabs-paxxe)

**The big shift: MCP is moving toward a stateless AI agent infrastructure** — Frontwalker Sri Lanka, June 15 (lang: en). Walks through the 2026-07-28 MCP spec release candidate: SEP-2575 removes the init handshake, SEP-2567 removes protocol-level session IDs, SEP-2243 adds Mcp-Method/Mcp-Name routing metadata, plus a Tasks proposal for long-running jobs and OAuth 2.1 / OIDC alignment — pushing state into the application layer for horizontal scaling. _Why interesting:_ specific SEP-numbered breakdown of what changes for teams running hosted MCP servers. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/big-shift-mcp-moving-toward-stateless-ai-agent-infrastructure-wfckc](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/big-shift-mcp-moving-toward-stateless-ai-agent-infrastructure-wfckc)

**Agentic AI's Missing Layer: Why MCP, A2A, and Agent Identity Will Become the HTTP of AI** — Chandra Sekhara Baipalli (Pegasystems), June 9 (lang: en). Argues the next architectural bottleneck is agent interoperability, not model intelligence: MCP as the context layer, A2A for discovery/delegation/negotiation, and emerging DNS-based agent-identity standards as the trust layer — proposing an Experience/Agent/Protocol/Capability/Data stack. _Why interesting:_ clear synthesis of the three-protocol "protocol layer" thesis from an enterprise-architecture angle. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agentic-ais-missing-layer-why-mcp-a2a-agent-identity-become-baipalli-xgjdc](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agentic-ais-missing-layer-why-mcp-a2a-agent-identity-become-baipalli-xgjdc)

**From Compliance as Code to Agentic Policy: How the EU AI Act Must Be Applied to Autonomous Systems** — Philipp Braun, June 11 (lang: en). Contrarian rebuttal of four common claims (accountability collapse, Art. 14 oversight vs autonomy, agents as unauditable black boxes, a wholly new framework): argues MCP connections are explicitly provisioned so accountability is a design decision (Art. 25), graduated-authority models already satisfy Art. 14, HMAC-SHA256-chained audit trails map to Art. 12/13, and the August 2026 high-risk deadline is mostly a documentation task for orgs with mature SDLC/ITSM. _Why interesting:_ substantive, opinionated mapping of agentic architecture to specific EU AI Act articles. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-compliance-code-agentic-policy-how-eu-ai-act-must-philipp-braun-kpyif](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-compliance-code-agentic-policy-how-eu-ai-act-must-philipp-braun-kpyif)

**Building a High-Volume Enterprise RAG System: Lessons Learned Beyond the Demo** — Satish Venketachalam, June 11 (lang: en). Ten production-RAG lessons: structure-aware chunking over fixed-size, metadata as a first-pass filter, hybrid (vector + BM25 + metadata) search, multi-stage retrieval with reranking, independently scaling embedding pipelines, incremental indexing, aggressive context-window trimming, architecture-level hallucination safeguards (source attribution, grounding, citations), and caching as a high-ROI win. _Why interesting:_ practitioner checklist for scaling RAG to millions of docs, not a prototype tutorial. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/building-high-volume-enterprise-rag-system-lessons-venketachalam-j0dhe](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/building-high-volume-enterprise-rag-system-lessons-venketachalam-j0dhe)

**Business-Nutzen durch GenAI: 2026 ist das Jahr der KI-Assistenten** — Swisscom Business, June 12 (lang: de). Swiss firms piloted GenAI but the productivity jump stalled; the piece argues now is the moment for legally compliant, productive AI assistants. Cites the Computerworld Swiss IT study (GenAI seen as strategic by 60%+, up from 35.5% in 2024), warns of Shadow AI data-leak risk, frames RAG as the way to connect assistants to internal knowledge, and stresses adoption/change-management (usage often collapses 2-3 months post-rollout) as the real lever. _Why interesting:_ Swiss enterprise lens (Swisscom, Christof Zogg) tying GenAI assistants, RAG and AI governance together. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/business-nutzen-durch-genai-2026-ist-das-jahr-der-5hb6e](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/business-nutzen-durch-genai-2026-ist-das-jahr-der-5hb6e)
