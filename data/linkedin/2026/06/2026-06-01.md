**The EU AI Act now treats multi-agent systems as one system** — Pieter van Schalkwyk (lang: en). Argues that between late March and end of May, Microsoft (Agent 365), Google, AWS, Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP and Oracle each shipped an "agent control plane" — the governance layer, not the agent, is now the headline as agents proliferate across SaaS by default. _Why interesting:_ synthesises 60 days of enterprise announcements into a single strategic pattern relevant to platform/governance focus areas. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/pick-your-agent-control-plane-before-picks-you-marc-kermisch-eoz9c]

[https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-quiet-protocol-thats-rewriting-what-means-build-product-lykkebo-fq93e]

**What Building Ten MCP Servers Taught Me About Where the Protocol Bites** — Björn Dijkhuizen (lang: en). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-building-ten-mcp-servers-taught-me-where-bites-bj%C3%B6rn-dijkhuizen-c0dwe]

**The Rise of Multi-Agentic: Why the Next AI Platform War Won't Be Won by Models** — Joon Nyip Koh (lang: en). Thesis that enterprises are buying "systems of agents" — specialists that hand off work, run inside permissions and audit trails — rather than smarter single models; production reality is chains of tasks across tools and identities. _Why interesting:_ a clear framing of the multi-agent / orchestration shift. **The Emerging Middle Layer of Agentic AI** — Cobus Greyling (lang: en). Citing a Meta/Stanford study, argues for a third layer beyond model and harness: the code an agent writes for itself mid-task to handle new integrations in real time ("universal agents" transcending fixed tools). _Why interesting:_ surfaces a fresh research-backed architectural distinction. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/emerging-middle-layer-agenticai-cobus-greyling-xfqff]
