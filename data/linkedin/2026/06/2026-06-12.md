**MCP is the new API — treat it as a do-over** — ReversingLabs / Chainmail Newsletter (lang: en). MCP is repeating the API security story at machine speed: the spec leaves auth, authorization, input validation and sandboxing "to the implementer," opening tool-poisoning, rug-pull and confused-deputy attacks that legacy static/dynamic analysis cannot detect. Bundled headlines: ENISA finds the EU Cyber Resilience Act driving SBOM adoption; Meta AI support chatbot used to hijack Instagram accounts; an OpenClaw email agent handed over AWS/SSH credentials to a phishing email. _Why interesting:_ Concrete, source-backed survey of agentic/MCP security failure modes — exactly the MCP + governance intersection in focus, dated today. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-new-api-treat-do-over-reversinglabs-paxxe]

**Zero Trust for AI Agents Needs an Identity Layer** — Patrick Parker (lang: en). Responds to Anthropic's "Zero Trust for AI agents" paper, arguing Zero Trust must evolve into "AIdentity": the control point moves from login to the material action, proof moves from logs to signed receipts, and the actor splits across human principal, agent, model, policy engine, credential vault, executor and downstream system. Argues agents should never hold credentials directly (least-exposure), and that "on behalf of" hides three distinct authorization meanings. _Why interesting:_ A rigorous, vendor-flavoured but substantive take on agent identity/MCP authorization governance — a current debate the field has not settled. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/zero-trust-ai-agents-needs-identity-layer-patrick-parker-1jkle]

**KW24 — Der KI-Sog: Drei Börsengänge, viel Kapital und wer ist hier der Wurm?** — Markus Begerow (lang: de). ~2,125-word weekly newsletter. Lead essay frames the AI capital rotation around three potential mega-IPOs (SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic) as a "liquidity vacuum" that draws in passive ETF investors, citing Goldman's Solomon ("more greed than fear") and a deliberately sharp Bear Stearns / Big Short analogy — KI is real, but the financing story risks overheating. Also covers Google renting extra datacentre capacity, Anthropic's proposed joint frontier-risk early-warning mechanism, and a University of Toronto study showing an open-weight LLM on a single GPU can drive an adaptive, self-replicating network worm. _Why interesting:_ Substantive German-language market+security analysis tying the AI macro/capital narrative to concrete governance and cyber risk. [https://de.linkedin.com/pulse/kw24-der-ki-sog-drei-b%C3%B6rseng%C3%A4nge-viel-kapital-und-wer-begerow-0kisf]

**Claude Just Changed the Economics of Knowledge Work #166** — Paul Storm / DigitalStormWeekly (lang: en). Argues Opus 4.8 (launched June 2) crosses a threshold — 2.5x faster, ~1/3 cheaper in fast mode, 69.2% vs 58.6% on SWE-Bench Pro agentic coding — and that Dynamic Workflows (orchestrating up to ~1,000 parallel subagents) shifts Claude from "completing tasks" to "orchestrating a workforce." Notes 40,000+ firms applied to the Claude Partner Network, 10,000+ certified consultants, and 20+ new legal MCP connectors (Westlaw, Harvey, Everlaw, DocuSign). _Why interesting:_ Captures the Claude enterprise-distribution-moat + Dynamic Workflows narrative. _Caveat:_ heavily promotional (embedded workshop/affiliate ads, "mega prompt," tool plugs) — read for the structural claims, discount the sales framing. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/claude-just-changed-economics-knowledge-work-166-paul-storm-vwr2f]
