## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**The big shift: MCP is moving toward a stateless AI agent infrastructure** — Frontwalker Sri Lanka (en). The 2026-07-28 MCP release candidate goes stateless at the protocol layer (SEP-2575 drops the init handshake, SEP-2567 drops session IDs), enabling horizontal scaling, edge routing via Mcp-Method/Mcp-Name headers, app-layer state handles, a Tasks proposal for long-running jobs, and OAuth 2.1 / OIDC auth. _Why interesting:_ Concrete, dated spec roadmap with an actionable migration checklist for teams running hosted MCP servers. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/big-shift-mcp-moving-toward-stateless-ai-agent-infrastructure-wfckc]

**From Compliance as Code to Agentic Policy: How the EU AI Act Must Be Applied to Autonomous Systems** — Philipp Braun (en). Rebuts four common claims (accountability collapse, Article 14 vs autonomy, agents-as-black-boxes, "wholly new framework") by mapping them onto existing IT/ITSM governance, MCP provisioning, graduated-authority models, and cryptographically signed audit trails tied to Articles 9/12/13/14/17/25. _Why interesting:_ Counter-narrative arguing Aug-2026 enforcement is a documentation/translation task, not a new compliance theory. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-compliance-code-agentic-policy-how-eu-ai-act-must-philipp-braun-kpyif]

**Securing Agentic AI at Enterprise Scale: A Practical Framework for Governance, Security, and Trust** — Susant Mallick / CloudHub (en). An 11-layer (Layer 0–10) security framework spanning GRC, AI gateway, zero-trust agent identity, runtime guardrails, A2A/MCP security, RAG/memory protection, observability, and red-teaming, aligned to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OWASP LLM/Agentic Top 10, CSA AICM, and MITRE ATLAS. _Why interesting:_ Enumerates concrete agentic risk classes (prompt injection, memory poisoning, agent impersonation) against named standards. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/securing-agentic-ai-enterprise-scale-practical-security-mallick-gr73e]

**What are the key components of a scalable RAG architecture for enterprises?** — n8n (en). Deep technical breakdown of five enterprise-RAG components: CDC-driven ingestion with chunk timestamps and deletion handling; hybrid retrieval (dense + BM25 + RRF + cross-encoder reranking); coupled vector-store/embedding choices and MRL; access control enforced inside the retriever (cites EchoLeak CVE-2025-32711); and observability via Ragas/Langfuse with chunk-level lineage. _Why interesting:_ Unusually concrete, with named tools, failure modes, and a real CVE — not marketing fluff. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-key-components-scalable-rag-architecture-enterprises-n8n-fzpnf]

**Claude Just Doubled the Price of Agent Work. This Is a Market Reset, Not Just a Price Hike.** — Alexandre Mesguich (en). Argues Anthropic's 15-Jun-2026 split of Claude billing into an interactive pool and a metered agent/programmatic pool (with monthly dollar credits) ends the hidden subsidy of cheap agents on flat subscriptions, framed against memory-price spikes, the $65B Series H at $965B, ~$47B run-rate, and a confidential IPO filing. _Why interesting:_ Reads the pricing change as pre-IPO unit-economics discipline and argues for multi-model/multi-provider architecture. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/claude-just-doubled-price-agent-work-market-reset-hike-mesguich-dwame]

**Your AI Agents Are Wasting Half Their Budget Talking to Each Other** — Nathan Olson (en). A practitioner reports that inter-agent natural-language chatter consumed 5–11% of a 200K context window; their LIACL structured compression protocol (drawing on TOON, Google A2A, LangGraph, AgentPrune, LLMLingua-2) cut messages ~53% and memory retrieval ~91% with full roundtrip fidelity; lite version on GitHub. _Why interesting:_ Names a concrete, under-discussed cost center in multi-agent systems with measured numbers. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/your-ai-agents-wasting-half-budget-talking-each-other-nathan-olson-fzave]

**Business-Nutzen durch GenAI: 2026 ist das Jahr der KI-Assistenten** — Swisscom Business (de-CH). Argues Swiss firms piloted GenAI but saw little productivity gain; the lever now is rolling out legally-compliant AI assistants grounded in internal data via RAG, with adoption/change-management (not tech) as the decider. Cites a Swiss IT study showing AI's strategic relevance rising from 35.5% (2024) to >60%, and warns of Shadow AI data-leakage risk. _Why interesting:_ Swiss-market enterprise view tying RAG, AI governance, and Shadow AI to concrete local survey data. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/business-nutzen-durch-genai-2026-ist-das-jahr-der-5hb6e]

**How to build reliable RAG systems for enterprise knowledge** — Mallow Technologies (en). Contrasts easy demo RAG with reliable production RAG, arguing the bottleneck is knowledge architecture (document/version/permission/ownership chaos) and retrieval quality rather than the model itself. _Why interesting:_ Reinforces the "retrieval engineering, not model intelligence" thesis for enterprise teams (note: article truncates to an off-platform link). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-build-reliable-rag-systems-enterprise-xezoc]
