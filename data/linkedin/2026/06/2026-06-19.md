## Pulse / long-form articles (priority layer)

**The Protocol Stack** — David Matousek (en). Frames MCP as the "vertical" protocol (agent reaching down to tools/data) and A2A as the "horizontal" protocol (agent coordinating across to peer agents), mapping each onto a four-layer agent topology and the OOP method-call vs. message-passing distinction. Includes April-2026 ecosystem numbers (9,400+ MCP registry servers, A2A past 150 production orgs, both under Linux Foundation). _Why interesting:_ A clean, architecturally rigorous mental model for when to reach for MCP vs. A2A, plus a roadmap recommendation (MCP now, design for A2A). [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/protocol-stack-david-matousek-t0tdc]

**The Agent-to-Agent Handoff Problem: Session Trust and Privilege Escalation Across Agent Boundaries** — Binod Kumar (en). Argues the unsolved 2026 security problem is the handoff, not the individual agent — the "confused deputy" problem recurring in recursive, cross-org agent delegation. Lays out seven requirements for real session trust (verifiable delegation chain, multi-principal attribution, holder-side attenuation, etc.) and a reference control architecture grounded in NIST AI RMF, with extensive cited sources. _Why interesting:_ The most concrete, standards-aware treatment of agent identity/authorization gaps (OAuth/OIDC/SPIFFE/macaroons) and what enterprises should do this quarter. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/agent-to-agent-handoff-problem-session-trust-privilege-binod-kumar-b1ttf]

**The August 2026 Regulatory Cliff That Moved** — Tomislav Vazdar (en). Explains the May 7, 2026 Digital Omnibus provisional agreement postponing the EU AI Act's high-risk (Annex III) obligations from Aug 2026 to Dec 2, 2027 (Annex I products to Aug 2, 2028), why it moved (harmonised standards never arrived, not reduced risk), and what did NOT move (prohibitions live since Feb 2025, GPAI obligations since Aug 2025, full penalty architecture). Contrasts with DORA (now enforcing) and NIS2 (uneven transposition). _Why interesting:_ Sharp, accurate read of the omnibus as still-provisional, and the cross-framework seams (AI Act/DORA/NIS2) where autonomous adversaries operate. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/august-2026-regulatory-cliff-moved-tomislav-vazdar-dqcse]

**AI-Twins, Bezahl-Agenten und der Stimmklon des CEO** — Andy Huber (de-CH). Swiss-focused AI news roundup: Mastercard Agent Pay / Visa Trusted Agent Protocol building the payment rails for autonomous shopping agents (agentic tokens that never expose the real card number); Google's AMIE diagnostic-AI clinical study; deepfake CEO voice-clone fraud (avg. $680k/incident); Swiss AI startup funding tripling (CHF 1.1bn in 2025); and the Bundesrat's lighter-touch, sectoral AI-regulation path vs. the EU AI Act. _Why interesting:_ Dense, sourced Swiss-market briefing tying agentic commerce, governance, and the practical "don't wait for the 2026 Swiss law" governance message. [https://de.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-twins-bezahl-agenten-und-der-stimmklon-des-ceo-andy-huber-axbne]

**When to RAG vs. When Not to RAG: A Practical Decision Framework from Real AI Builds** — Arinze Obieze (en). A practitioner's intent-routing framework: use RAG for proprietary/changing/verifiable data and high-stakes domains (with hybrid search, reranking, metadata filters, GraphRAG for multi-doc); skip it for pure reasoning, creative work, small/static knowledge bases, and ultra-low-latency flows. Stresses retrieval quality over chasing the latest model. _Why interesting:_ Grounded, opinionated decision criteria from shipped products rather than the usual "RAG everything" advocacy. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/when-rag-vs-practical-decision-framework-from-real-ai-arinze-obieze-ledse]

**Building a Semantic Foundation for Enterprise Agentic AI** — Dr. Shivagami Gugan (en). Argues models and compute are commoditized; the moat is data that carries meaning. Distinguishes three "semantic" layers — similarity (vectors), structure (knowledge graphs), logic (ontologies) — and why agentic AI needs all three (retrieve → ground → reason/validate). Details why RAG/embeddings alone fail for agents (context fragmentation, explainability gaps, multi-source reasoning) and the four progressive barriers to agent autonomy. _Why interesting:_ A thorough articulation (from an AWS chief technologist) of why knowledge graphs + ontologies, not just vector RAG, underpin trustworthy enterprise agents. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/building-semantic-foundation-enterprise-agentic-ai-gugan-owlwe]

**The Specialist Premium: Why Anthropic Just Proved That AI Experts Are More Valuable Than Ever** — Business Intelligence Group / Russ Fordyce (en). Reads the Anthropic–TCS partnership (TCS spinning up a dedicated unit to deploy Claude in financial services, healthcare, telecom, aviation) as the clearest signal that domain expertise, not the model, is the moat — "the model isn't the moat." Uses B2B founder anecdotes to argue AI superpowers specialists rather than replacing them, especially for high-trust/regulated deployments. _Why interesting:_ Concrete, current Anthropic enterprise go-to-market data point (TCS deal) with a contrarian-but-grounded take on the consultant-displacement narrative. [https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/specialist-premium-why-anthropic-just-znbac]
