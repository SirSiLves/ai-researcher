**#55 Anthropic Solved MCP's Biggest Hidden Problem with Tool Search** — Imdad Areeph (en), 2026-07-27.

**MCP Grows Up on July 28. Enterprise Governance Has Two Weeks to Catch Up.** — Nasiko (en), 2026-07-27.

**The Hardest Problem in Agentic AI Is Not Reasoning. It Is State.** — Pierre Gilot (en), 2026-07-28.
Frames enterprise agentic AI as a distributed-systems problem, not an intelligence problem: concurrent conflicting updates, ordering, reconciliation, durable execution, idempotency, replay. Cites recent work on multi-agent memory as a computer-architecture problem and on durable execution as a fault-tolerant agent-loop pattern; points at CRDTs and event sourcing as the returning primitives.
_Why interesting:_ The strongest "agents are just services with new names" piece this week, and it lands the operational question — can the agent stay *correct* when reality gets messy, not just sound smart.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/hardest-problem-agentic-ai-reasoning-state-pierre-gilot-pware

**Benchmarking two agentic harnesses: Microsoft Agent Framework and the GitHub Copilot SDK** — Aqib Sherwani (en), 2026-07-24.
Controlled head-to-head holding the model fixed (mock wire-protocol server, then live gpt-5.4). _Why interesting:_ Rare methodology discipline — it re-ran Copilot on the Responses API to prove the context-growth gap is a runtime-history trait, not a wire-protocol artefact, and publishes its own limitations section.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/benchmarking-two-agentic-harnesses-microsoft-agent-github-sherwani-bz72e

**ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Governance Operating System or Just Another Compliance Tattoo?** — SHANKAR S. (en), 2026-07-29.
States flatly that 42001 certification is not EU AI Act compliance: under Articles 40/42 presumption of conformity attaches only to harmonised standards, and 42001 is not one today. Separates what the standard does deliver (process orientation, portable governance dossier, Annex A's 38 lifecycle controls) from what it cannot.
_Why interesting:_ Directly attacks the "certificate as shield" assumption that shows up in most vendor 42001 material, and reframes the board question from "are we certified" to "does our AI governance give reliable insight into objectives".
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/isoiec-420012023-governance-operating-system-just-shankar-subramanian-t7ryc

**One week from today, EU AI Act high risk enforcement becomes fully applicable.** — Jyoteesh Gupta (en), 2026-07-25.
Countdown to 2 August 2026 for Annex III high-risk obligations (risk management, data governance, technical documentation, human oversight, cybersecurity), with fines up to EUR 15m or 3% of global turnover. Cites a EUR 4.2m Belgian retail fine over an unassessed facial-recognition entry system.

**EU AI Act für KMU: Was ab August 2026 wirklich zu tun ist** — Tim Höchel (de), 2026-07-23.

## Substantive hashtag posts

**"The 2026-07-28 Model Context Protocol specification shipped yesterday"** — Muhammad Abdullah, #MCP / #AIAgents.
Line-by-line migration read: `initialize`/`initialized` handshake gone (version, client info and capabilities move into a meta field, `server/discover` on demand), `Mcp-Session-Id` gone, explicit state handles passed as ordinary tool arguments, new `Mcp-Method` / `Mcp-Name` headers for gateway routing without body inspection. Also flags what breaks or starts a clock: `tasks/list` removed, roots/sampling/logging and HTTP+SSE deprecated on 12 months, error `-32002` becomes `-32602`.
_Takeaway:_ Hand-rolled servers not on an official SDK take the hardest hit; the generalisable line is that implicit state is a debt you only discover during an outage.
https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7488295211668885504
