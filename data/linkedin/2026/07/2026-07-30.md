**Runtime Ownership in Agent Systems: Where the Loop Runs and What Changes When It Moves** — Rohit Sharma (en, 25 Jul 2026)

_Why interesting:_ Longest and most rigorous piece in today's batch; a distinction most agent-architecture writing collapses into one axis.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/runtime-ownership-agent-systems-where-loop-runs-what-changes-sharma-c6qvc
