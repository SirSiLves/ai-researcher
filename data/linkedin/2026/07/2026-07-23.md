### MCP & agent-interoperability protocols

**MCP Server July Roundup on API Evangelist** — Kin Lane (en), July 22.
A deduplicated map of the last 30 days of MCP: ~260 distinct stories from 142 providers, distilled to five themes — a spec release candidate landing July 28 ("re-test everything"), a security/authorization reckoning, API-gateway incumbents (Tyk, Kong, Gravitee) claiming the MCP layer, a wave of first-party servers (Apple, Meta, Shopify, Datadog), and early pushback ("should this even be MCP?").
_Why interesting:_ The single best signal-over-noise snapshot of where the MCP ecosystem actually stands this month.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-server-july-roundup-api-evangelist-kin-lane-kaytc

**Why MCP May Be the Most Important Piece of AI Technology** — Kurt Cagle (en), July 18.
Argues MCP's importance is categorical, not incremental: it turns any authenticated endpoint into a natural-language-queryable "smart endpoint," collapsing the integration layer that has dominated project costs for decades. Extends to a provocative thesis that most applications trend toward obsolescence by the early 2030s — tempered with honest counter-arguments about discoverability and complex workflows.
_Why interesting:_ A grounded, first-person account of an MCP-mediated workflow plus a sharp architectural argument about grounding, hallucination and inference economics.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-mcp-may-most-important-piece-ai-technology-kurt-cagle-o8bpc

**MCP becomes governed infrastructure inside SAP Integration Suite** — Gaurav Singh (en), July 22.
SAP's MCP Gateway is now GA, aggregating SAP + non-SAP APIs, integration flows and external MCP servers into one governed entry point with auth, scoped tokens, rate limiting and audit. Details how MCP servers are built from any API or RFC over Streamable HTTP (SSE deprecated), with OAuth bearer tokens treated as non-human identities — and flags tool descriptions as a prompt-injection surface.
_Why interesting:_ Concrete enterprise-platform view of MCP shifting from experiment to something you must govern like any privileged integration; relevant to regulated/Swiss enterprise contexts.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/mcp-becomes-governed-infrastructure-inside-sap-suite-btp-gaurav-singh-kk96e

**Your Agents Can Talk. That Was Never the Hard Part.** — Dr. Jerry A. Smith (en), July 20.
A practical field guide to Agent2Agent (A2A): the spec shipped v1.0 in March and was donated to the Linux Foundation, but the protocol only standardizes the handshake — not trust, meaning, authority or accountability. Offers an eight-point readiness checklist, the maxim "authenticated is not trusted," and three concrete tests (technical, governance, economic), with an honest mid-2026 read that cross-vendor production remains thin.
_Why interesting:_ Cuts through A2A hype to locate the real work in the operating model above the wire — directly on the agent-interoperability focus area.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/your-agents-can-talk-never-hard-part-dr-jerry-a-smith-awxfe

### RAG, retrieval & agent engineering

**RAG and Agents Are Both Retrieval: Stateless Pipes Can't Make Stateful Decisions** — Alok Srivastava (en), July 22.
Contends that RAG and agentic systems are architecturally the same stateless "fetch-and-inject" pattern, which is why ~79% of orgs have launched initiatives but only ~11% reach production (with ~380% cost overruns). Argues the missing layer is persistent "Context Intelligence" — a living representation of enterprise entities, relationships and temporal state — rather than more middleware.
_Why interesting:_ A clear diagnosis of why enterprise RAG stalls, framed as an architecture problem, not a model problem. (Closes with a DeepGraph AI plug — author is founder/CEO.)
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rag-agents-both-retrieval-stateless-pipes-cant-make-alok-srivastava-niqmc

**Long-Horizon AI Agents in Production: Agent Harness Design, State Management and Failure Recovery** — Govardhan M Reddy (en), July 21.
A deep treatment of production agent harnesses: a "Recoverable Agent Runtime" with eight control layers (objective contract, plan graph, durable state ledger, side-effect envelope, observation commit, failure classifier, recovery policy, completion proof) and a "Resumption Proof" gate for safely continuing after interruption. Grounds idempotency/reconciliation rules in METR, Anthropic, LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK and AWS reliability guidance.
_Why interesting:_ Unusually rigorous engineering content on the harness layer that determines whether long-running agents survive real infrastructure. (Ends with a consulting pitch.)
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/long-horizon-ai-agents-production-agent-harness-design-reddy-suoxf

### AI governance, EU AI Act & responsible AI

**August 2, 2026: The EU AI Act Enters Full Enforcement** — Mark E. S. Bernard (en), July 17.
Lays out the Aug 2, 2026 enforcement milestone and its three pillars: high-risk AI system obligations, GPAI requirements for pre-Aug-2025 foundation models, and Article 50 transparency duties. Maps a current-vs-future governance state and a ten-step program anchored on an ISO/IEC 42001 AI Management System.
_Why interesting:_ A board-level checklist for the imminent enforcement date. (Some Bernard Institute self-promotion, but the regulatory substance is real.)
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/august-2-2026-eu-ai-act-enters-full-enforcement-while-vinic

**How Top AI Companies Fall Short of the EU GPAI Code** — Risto Uuk (en), July 22.
The Future of Life Institute's Head of European Policy dissects the new FLI AI Safety Index (Summer 2026) against specific GPAI Code measures: no human-uplift trials on current flagship models (incl. Claude Opus 4.7), external audits whose independence is "constrained by developer-offered compensation," and pause commitments quietly weakened to be competitor-contingent (Anthropic RSP 3.0, OpenAI, DeepMind). Notes OpenAI reported "no policy" for external serious-incident notification.
_Why interesting:_ Rigorously sourced, measure-by-measure evidence that even Code signatories fall short — the strongest governance read of the day.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-top-ai-companies-fall-short-eu-gpai-code-risto-uuk-vtewf

**The AI Schism: Your AI Compliance Deadline Just Split in Two** — Subhojit Chandra (en), July 20.
Explains how the EU's Digital Omnibus split the AI Act into two clocks: Annex III stand-alone high-risk obligations deferred to Dec 2, 2027 (Annex I embedded to Aug 2028), while Article 50 transparency duties (chatbot disclosure, synthetic-media labeling, deepfake filtering) stay due Aug 2, 2026. Recommends an EU-facing API gateway, a NIST-RMF-based modular core, and governance-as-code CI/CD gates.
_Why interesting:_ Corrects the widespread "blanket delay" misreading and gives an architecture to absorb regional regulatory forks. (Includes an Arthashastra framing digression.)
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-schism-your-compliance-deadline-just-split-two-nobody-chandra-la9yf

**3 AI incidents — One reality: AI is now the attacker, the attack surface, and the governance challenge** — Pamela Gupta (en), July 22.
Synthesizes this week's three headline incidents: (1) the autonomous AI-agent breach of Hugging Face — a full RCE/credential-theft/lateral-movement chain via a malicious dataset, notably *not* prompt injection, where commercial guardrails blocked responders from analyzing ~17k attack logs; (2) a SANS survey showing AI use in cyber jumping 50%→78% while governance lags; (3) ServiceNow CVE-2026-6875 (CVSS 9.5) pre-auth sandbox escape under active exploitation.
_Why interesting:_ The clearest single synthesis of the week's dominant security thread, framed around the adoption-vs-governance gap.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/3-ai-incidents-one-reality-now-attacker-attack-surface-pamela-gupta-fylhe

### German-language

**„AI Augmented Work": Worauf es ankommt, um die großen Potenziale zu heben** — Prof. Dr. Yasmin Weiß (de), July 21.
Summary (EN): From conversations with two German corporate CEOs, argues the shift to "AI Augmented Work" is a structural and cultural transformation, not a tooling choice. Forecasts to 2030: smaller teams, flatter middle management, broader roles, and new core skills — "Human Agency," critical judgment, and a "new bilingualism" for communicating precisely with AI agents (context, clear goals, guardrails).
_Why interesting:_ Substantive German thought-leadership on how agentic AI reshapes work and skills. Caveat: explicitly a paid HP/AMD partnership newsletter; the second half is sponsored hardware content — the strategic thesis above stands on its own.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-augmented-work-worauf-es-ankommt-um-die-gro%C3%9Fen-potenziale-wei%C3%9F-visxf

**Wenn bei KI-Agenten die Leitplanken fehlen** — Christian Scharrer (de), July 22.
Summary (EN): A Dell lead architect argues the first governance question is whether a task even needs an agent (vs. cheaper, more controllable RPA/rule-based flows). An agent should be treated like a new employee — with defined responsibilities, access rights and an accountable owner — not a software license. Multi-agent orchestration multiplies risk (redundant, contradictory decisions), IT becomes the ecosystem orchestrator, and on-prem/hybrid gains weight for regulated data.
_Why interesting:_ Pragmatic German-language governance framing tailored to regulated enterprises; complements the English governance cluster.
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/wenn-bei-ki-agenten-die-leitplanken-fehlen-christian-scharrer-i14re
