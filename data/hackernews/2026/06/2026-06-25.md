**[Needle: We Distilled Gemini Tool Calling into a 26M Model](https://github.com/cactus-compute/needle)** — 667 pts · 188 comments · 1 day ago · `github.com` · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48111896)

_What this is:_ Cactus open-sourced Needle, a 26M-parameter function-calling (tool-use) model that runs at ~6000 tok/s prefill / ~1200 tok/s decode on consumer devices (phones, watches, glasses). Architecture is attention-and-gating only — no MLP/FFN layers — on the thesis that tool calling is retrieval-and-assembly, not reasoning. MIT licensed, weights on HuggingFace.

_Comment signal:_
- @simonw: suggests publishing a live "needle playground" demo — small enough to host cheaply on a VPS.
- _Notable claim from author:_ the "no FFN" finding reportedly generalizes to any task with external structured knowledge (RAG, retrieval) — model needn't memorize facts in FFN weights if they're in the input. Beats FunctionGemma-270M, Qwen-0.6B, Granite-350M, LFM2.5-350M on single-shot function calling.
