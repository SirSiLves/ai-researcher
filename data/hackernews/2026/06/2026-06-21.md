**[GPT-5.5 hallucinates 3x more than MIT-licensed GLM-5.2](https://arrowtsx.dev/bigger-models/)** — 547 pts · 280 comments · ~1 day ago · `arrowtsx.dev` · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48600167)

_What this is:_ A blog post arguing that frontier-model intelligence has plateaued and that scaling bigger models is increasing hallucination rates, contrasting GPT-5.5 against the MIT-licensed open-weight GLM-5.2.

_Comment signal:_
- @wolttam: Calls the "intelligence has plateaued / bigger models get worse" framing wild and unsupported.
- @aesthesia: Hallucination-rate scores are conditional on the model not knowing the answer, so they don't reflect real-world frequency of encountering a hallucination.
- @stcg: On the cited AA-Omniscience benchmark, GLM-5.2 (max) got 25% correct vs GPT-5.5 (xhigh) at 57% — prefers a verifiable answer over abstention.
- _Notable critique:_ Top thread pushes back hard on the post's methodology — hallucination scores are conditional, and the model with the lower hallucination "score" also answers far fewer questions correctly, so the headline comparison is misleading.
