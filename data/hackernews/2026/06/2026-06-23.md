**[Prompt Injection as Role Confusion](https://role-confusion.github.io)** — 213 pts · 109 comments · 1 day ago · `role-confusion.github.io` · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48631888)

_What this is:_ A readable blog writeup of a research paper reframing prompt injection as the model "forgetting who it is" — role confusion triggered by writing style, not by structural markers like `<think>` tags.

_Comment signal:_
- @JohnMakin: Highlights the paper's core finding — human red-teamers hit near-100% attack success on frontier models that score near-perfectly on standard injection benchmarks, exposing how weak those benchmarks are.
- @lelanthran: Reads it as showing the `<think>...</think>` wrapper is nearly irrelevant; policy-style prose in plain user input is enough to bypass.
- _Notable critique:_ @ipython pushes back on the framing of roles as a "security architecture" — argues current LLMs provide no real security boundaries or guarantees and we should stop implying otherwise. @simonw praises the paper+blog dual-publishing pattern.
