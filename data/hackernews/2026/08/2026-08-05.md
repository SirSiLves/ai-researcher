_What this is:_ Argues against the emerging habit of humans acting as a relay between an LLM and their colleagues — pasting model output at someone else and asking them to verify it, instead of understanding it themselves.

_Comment signal:_

- _Notable critique:_ @jvanderbot — the prompt/paste-error/retry loop has simply been rebranded "agentic engineering"; same loop, faster, minus whatever understanding manual inspection between steps used to buy.
