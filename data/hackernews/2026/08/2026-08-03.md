**[Don't be a meat proxy](https://gruhn.me/blog/2026-08-03/)** — 1590 pts · 653 comments · 14 hours ago · `gruhn.me` · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49151933)

_What this is:_ A short essay asking people to stop pasting "Claude said: [giant response verbatim]" into Slack threads, PR feedback, and group chats. The argument: reading raw AI output is extra effort, it's verbose and jargon-dense and "frequently contains all too plausible nonsense," and the recipient can prompt the model themselves with better context. Read it, validate it, then write in your own words — the rewrite is the certificate that you did the prior steps. Applied to code review: if you paste the ticket into Claude Code and paste reviewer feedback back in without reading either, the reviewers did the implementation and you were the meat proxy.

_Comment signal:_

- @maccard: responded publicly once with "thanks but I can ask Claude myself" and nobody on the team ever pasted an LLM response at him again. @LtWorf tried the same and got reported to HR.
- @deepsummer poses the honest inverse: if Claude gets the answer in 3 minutes and it would take you 15, do you disclose that you asked Claude, or accept credit for powers you don't have? @black6's answer: "'I don't know' is a better answer than 'Claude said…'"
