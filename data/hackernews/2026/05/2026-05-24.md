**[Constraint Decay: The Fragility of LLM Agents in Back End Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18247)** — 85 pts · 44 comments · 9 hours ago · `arxiv.org` · [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48256912)
_What this is:_ Arxiv preprint documenting "constraint decay" — coding agents that excel at greenfield generation degrade sharply when forced to follow explicit architectural rules across eight web frameworks. Conclusion: rapid prototyping fine, production-grade backend dev not yet.
_Comment signal:_
- @jdlshore: Notes the study didn't run frontier models for cost reasons, so the specific numbers are soft — but the directional finding (degradation under structural constraint) is consistent with field experience.
- @vishvananda: Coins a useful term — "calcification" — where a pattern emerging in the codebase dominates context and becomes self-reinforcing for the agent. Front-loading constraints into prompts works better than retro-fitting them.
- _Notable critique:_ @dwa3592 — frames the whole issue as the underlying context-window tax: keeping "everything in mind from these 6 directories" to make one ticket-sized change burns context fast enough that guardrails fuzz.
