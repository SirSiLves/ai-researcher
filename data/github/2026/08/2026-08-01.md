> **Read the tags.** `board` = the repo appeared on a `github.com/trending` page today, and `+N today` is GitHub's own stars-today counter (rolling from 00:00 UTC). `topic` = surfaced only from a `/topics/*` page, which is ranked by total stars and carries no daily counter — for those, `+N today` is this run's 20:0x reading diffed against yesterday's 20:1x reading. The two clocks differ, so a repo can show `+473` as a board figure and `+436` as a watch-list diff (`NousResearch/hermes-agent` does exactly that today). Neither is wrong; don't subtract them from each other.
