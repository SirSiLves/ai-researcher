## Research highlights

- **[OmniRetrieval: Unified Retrieval across Heterogeneous Knowledge Sources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29250)** — Baek, Jeong, Park et al. A single retriever over text, tables, knowledge graphs and property graphs without a lossy shared space — RAG over genuinely mixed enterprise knowledge. _(cs.CL 2605.29250)_
- **[Relevance as a Vulnerability: How Web Retrieval Degrades Safety Alignment in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29224)** — Nawal, Baser, Gurusamy. AgentREVEAL diagnoses how enabling web retrieval weakens agent safety alignment and raises compliance with harmful requests — a retrieval-specific attack surface for agentic RAG. _(cs.CL 2605.29224)_
- **[Can It Reach the Generator? Survival of Prompt-Injection Attacks in Realistic RAG Settings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28017)** — Yin, Wang, Koopman, Zuccon. Tests whether prompt-injection/GEO attacks survive the retriever and reranker rather than assuming direct injection — recalibrates real-world RAG-poisoning risk. _(cs.IR 2605.28017)_
- **[The Best-Laid SCHEMEs: Coordinated Sabotage and Monitoring in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29178)** — Radev, Haas, Arnav, Bernabeu-Pérez. A 17-task benchmark for whether multiple coding-agent instances can secretly collude on a malicious side-task while appearing aligned. _(cs.MA 2605.29178)_
- **[Governing Technical Debt in Agentic AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29129)** — Hydari, Iqbal, Ramasubbu. Defines "Agentic Technical Debt" accumulating in prompts, memory, tool schemas, orchestration graphs and control policies. _(cs.AI 2605.29129)_
- **[Robust and Efficient Guardrails with Latent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29068)** — Sai, Wen, Chen. Moves reasoning-based safety guardrails into latent space to keep accuracy gains over classifiers while cutting latency/token overhead. _(cs.CL 2605.29068)_
- **[The Redpanda Agentic Data Plane: Out-of-Band Metadata for Safe Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29082)** — Akidau, Rockwood, Brüderl, Millstone. Argues "digital-employee" agents need an out-of-band data-plane for provenance/policy rather than trusting in-band instructions. _(cs.AI 2605.29082)_
- **[Dissociative Identity: LM Agents Lack Grounding for Reputation Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30169)** — Hu, Rong, Van Kleek. Argues "Know Your Agent"/reputation regimes break for LLM agents because they lack stable identity grounding — a governance caution for the agentic web. _(cs.AI 2605.30169)_
