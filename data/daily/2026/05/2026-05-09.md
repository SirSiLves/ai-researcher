**Securing the Model Context Protocol (MCP): Risks, Controls, and Governance** — _Takeaway:_ Threat model and control set for MCP server ecosystems (tool-name spoofing, prompt-injection at the tool boundary, schema-leakage), with proposed governance primitives. Most concrete MCP-security paper to date and pairs cleanly with the wave of MCP CVEs in early 2026. _Category:_ cs.CR/cs.AI. [arxiv 2511.20920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20920)

Sharp critique of how the field measures progress. _Category:_ cs.CY/cs.AI. [arxiv 2605.04135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04135)

**Redefining AI Red Teaming in the Agentic Era: From Weeks to Hours** — _Takeaway:_ Treats red-teaming as an orchestratable agent task with 29 advanced adversarial attacks (AutoRedTeamer, NEXUS, Siren, CoT Jailbreak, Genetic Persona, JBFuzz) — collapses week-long red-team cycles into hours. _Category:_ cs.CR/cs.AI. [arxiv 2605.04019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04019)

**ContextualJailbreak: Evolutionary Red-Teaming via Simulated Conversational Priming** — _Takeaway:_ Black-box evolutionary search over multi-turn primed dialogues; multi-turn contextual priming consistently beats single-turn jailbreaks against frontier safety alignment. _Category:_ cs.CL/cs.CR. **Coordination as an Architectural Layer for LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems** — _Takeaway:_ Empirical study (prediction-market testbed) treating coordination as a configurable layer separable from agent logic and information access; clean abstraction for orchestration designers. _Category:_ cs.MA/cs.AI. **LatentRAG: Latent Tokens for Thoughts and Subqueries** — _Takeaway:_ Generates latent thought/subquery tokens directly from hidden states; matches explicit agentic-RAG quality with ~90% lower inference latency. _Category:_ cs.CL/cs.IR. [arxiv (cs.CL listing)](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/new)
