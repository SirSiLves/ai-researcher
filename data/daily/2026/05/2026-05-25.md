## Research highlights

- **[Same Model, Different Weakness: How Language and Modality Reshape the Jailbreak Attack Surface in Frontier MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23157)** — Ford, Van Doren, Jin, Dix. First systematic cross-lingual + multimodal red-team of Claude Sonnet 4.5, GPT-5, Pixtral Large, Qwen Omni across 363 prompts in en-US vs es-MX, text-only vs multimodal. Alignment failures are not uniform — same model, different attack surface by language and modality. Multimodal Spanish is a sharp gap.
- **[Foundation Protocol: A Coordination Layer for Agentic Society](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23218)** — Liu, Gu, Zhang, Yu, Hong, Song et al. Proposes a protocol layer one level above MCP/A2A — identity, relationships, multi-agent work organisation, value exchange, accountability primitives.
- **[How to Steer Your Multi-Agent System: Human-LLM Collaborative Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23023)** — He, Kim, Zhang, Hruschka. Formalises three axes (semantic vs structural, global vs targeted, low vs high-level edits) for human co-planning in orchestrated multi-agent systems. Ships AMBIPOM prototype. First paper to treat the "human-in-the-loop on a multi-agent plan" as a first-class HCI primitive.
- **[MemAudit: Post-hoc Auditing of Poisoned Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23723)** — Tan, Yao, Jin, Yu, Wang et al. Memory-poisoning attack class — an adversarial user injects records through ordinary interaction that later steer reasoning. Targets post-hoc forensic auditing via causal attribution + anomaly detection. Concrete agent-security primitive that pairs with persistent-memory architectures shipping now.
- **[OpenSkillEval: Automatically Auditing the Open Skill Ecosystem for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23657)** — Ying, Ai, Tang, Liu, Cao. First end-to-end auditor for the skill marketplace (Anthropic Skills, OpenAI plugins, LangChain). Highly aligned with current Claude/Cowork direction.
- **[Benchmarking Google Embeddings 2 against Open-Source Models for Multilingual Dense Retrieval and RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23618)** — Cirillo, Desiato, Polese, Solimando. GE2 (Vertex bi-encoder, 2,048-token context, task-type conditioning) vs BGE-M3, E5-large, mE5-L, LaBSE, mMPNet across four BEIR subsets + synthetic Italian RAG. Practical buyer's-guide numbers for multilingual embedding selection.
- **[Query-Adaptive Semantic Chunking for RAG (QASC)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22834)** — Rastogi. Dynamically constructs chunks at query time. Reports +18-27% F1 vs fixed chunking and +8-12% vs semantic/agentic chunking across 100 docs / 200 queries.
- **[Convex Optimization for Alignment and Preference Learning on a Single GPU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23244)** — Feng, Pilanci. Reformulates preference-learning as a convex program — single-GPU alternative to RLHF/DPO with no hyperparameter sweep. If empirical results hold, a substantial democratisation of alignment training.
