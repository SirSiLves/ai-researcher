## The day in 90 seconds

**Anthropic buys the SDK pipeline its rivals depend on, hours before Microsoft open-sources the runtime.**

The agent-interop layer split into two camps in a single working day. Anthropic moved to consolidate it privately by acquiring Stainless; Microsoft answered by giving its Agent Framework to the Linux Foundation. In between, Cursor shipped a coding model that claims to undercut both, and a California jury threw out Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI in under two hours.

The structural move came from Anthropic. It acquired Stainless — founded in 2022, deal reportedly above $300 million per The Information — and with it the codegen pipeline that produces the official SDKs not only for Claude but for OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare and Meta, across TypeScript, Python, Go, Java and Kotlin. Read alone, it looks like vertical integration. Read against the May 14 announcement that splits Claude Pro and Max usage into a separately metered Agent SDK pool starting 15 June, with caps of 20, 100 and 200 dollars a month for the three subscription tiers, it is something colder: Anthropic now both prices third-party agent harnesses and owns the tooling that ships SDKs to its direct competitors. Magna Capax and r/ClaudeAI threads are already modeling effective price increases between 12x and 175x for harness-heavy workloads. The acquisition lands with a separate report that Anthropic is closing a $30 billion primary round at a valuation north of $900 billion, on Q1 ARR above $44 billion and more than a thousand customers paying at least a million dollars a year.

Microsoft used Open Source Summit North America to stake the opposite position. It open-sourced the Microsoft Agent Framework SDK and runtime, the Agent Governance Toolkit for OS-level agent security and compliance, and parked all of it under the Agentic AI Foundation at the Linux Foundation — already the fastest-growing project in that foundation's history. The pitch is that the open layer of the multi-agent stack belongs to neutral governance, not to a single vendor's managed runtime. The microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners repo picked up 1,013 stars in a single day on the back of the announcement, and IBM Research and Hugging Face simultaneously launched the Open Agent Leaderboard, the first benchmark that scores the harness rather than the model. It is the IaaS playbook from a decade ago, run in reverse against the lab that just bought its SDK printer.

The third thread is Cursor's Composer 2.5, the first credible test of whether an application-layer company can train its own model and undercut the labs on a workload it controls. Built on Kimi K2.5 with 25x more synthetic tasks than Composer 2, sharded Muon, dual-mesh HSDP and RL with textual feedback at trajectory choice points, headline pricing is 0.50 dollars per million input tokens and 2.50 per million output, with double-usage in week one. The-decoder.com reports benchmark parity with Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 at a fraction of the price. If that parity holds in independent evals, the downstream-vendor exit from Anthropic's new metering regime stops being theoretical — and tomorrow's Google I/O keynote, expected to anchor on Gemini Omni and Gemini 4.0, walks into a market where both the SDK layer and the application layer just moved.

Around the edges, the legal and governance picture moved too. A unanimous nine-juror California verdict threw out Musk's claim that Altman, Brockman, OpenAI and Microsoft stole a charity, deliberating for less than two hours on statute-of-limitations grounds and removing the last legal restraint on OpenAI's for-profit conversion. The White House blocked Anthropic Mythos expansion to roughly 70 outside firms while drafting an executive workaround for federal agencies, and Sven Malvik's LinkedIn Pulse essay arguing that the 98.4 percent of Claude Code that is not AI is exactly Europe's sovereignty opening reads, today, less like a manifesto and more like a strategy memo.

## Research highlights

- **[FORGE: Self-Evolving Agent Memory With No Weight Updates via Population Broadcast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)** — Bogdanov, Lung, Kunz, Gao, Taylor, Zaman. Population-based protocol that evolves prompt-injected natural-language memory for hierarchical ReAct agents — failed trajectories become reusable rules, best-performing instance's memory is broadcast, converged instances freeze. Concrete blueprint for self-improving agents without touching model weights.
- **[Differentiable Mixture-of-Agents Incentivizes Swarm Intelligence of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15706)** — Wu, Lu, Yan et al. Replaces hand-wired multi-agent topologies with a differentiable, context-aware router that dynamically activates agents at each reasoning step — implicitly simulating diverse communication graphs without static workflows.
- **[Agentic Discovery of Neural Architectures: AIRA-Compose and AIRA-Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15871)** — Pepe, Lin, Magka et al. LLM agents autonomously design foundation-model architectures beyond Transformers — produces 14 AIRAformer/AIRAhybrid (Transformer-Mamba) variants that beat Llama 3.2 at 1B/3B scales and find more compute-efficient frontiers. Tangible recursive self-improvement evidence.
- **[Argus: Evidence Assembly for Scalable Deep Research Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16217)** — Zhang, Su, Chen et al. Treats deep research as jigsaw assembly: Searcher gathers evidence per sub-query, Navigator maintains a shared evidence graph and identifies missing pieces. Useful pattern for production research agents.
- **[RecMem: Recurrence-based Memory Consolidation for Efficient Long-Running LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16045)** — Replaces eager per-step memory writes with recurrence-triggered consolidation; cuts memory-construction token cost by up to 87% while preserving recall quality. Practical fix for the memory-write bottleneck in deployed agents.
- **[SkillSmith: Compiling Agent Skills into Boundary-Guided Runtime Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15215)** — Compiler-runtime that compiles skill packages offline into minimal executable interfaces — 57.44% token reduction versus exposing raw skill definitions at runtime. Pattern for MCP/tool-use systems that need to keep agent prompts bounded as skill libraries grow.
- **[Granite Embedding Multilingual R2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13521)** — IBM's open enterprise-scale encoder embedding family spanning 200+ languages with 32K-token context. Direct competitor to BGE/E5/voyage releases for production RAG and cross-lingual retrieval at long-context.
- **[Judge Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16023)** — Feldhus et al. Mechanistically locates a shared "Latent Evaluator" sub-graph in mid-to-late MLPs of Gemma-3/Qwen2.5/Llama-3; zero-ablating it collapses LLM-as-judge scores while preserving world knowledge. Explains format-induced LLM-eval inconsistency at the circuit level.
