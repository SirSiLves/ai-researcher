## Research highlights

**LatentRAG: Latent Reasoning and Retrieval for Efficient Agentic RAG** — Shifts agentic RAG reasoning/subquery generation into continuous latent space; matches explicit-loop quality at ~90% lower inference latency. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06285. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06285)

**Coordination as an Architectural Layer for LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems** — 41–87% of multi-agent prod failures are coordination defects, not model capability. Proposes coordination as a configurable layer separable from agent logic. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.03310. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03310)

**Beyond the Black Box: Interpretability of Agentic AI Tool Use** — Sparse Autoencoders + linear probes on hidden states predict whether a tool call is needed and how consequential — internal observability for agent risk monitoring. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06890. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06890)

**Bridging Protocol and Production: MCP Design Patterns** — Three production-deployment patterns (identity-scoped broker, adaptive timeout budget, structured error recovery); addresses the gap between the spec and ~10k active servers / 97M monthly SDK downloads. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13417. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13417)

**AI Harness Engineering: A Runtime Substrate for Foundation-Model Software Agents** — Enumerates the 11 component responsibilities of a production agent harness. Useful taxonomy for platform teams. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.13357. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13357)

**Benchmarking Retrieval Strategies for Biomedical RAG** — Head-to-head of dense / hybrid BM25+dense / cross-encoder rerank / multi-query / MMR. Cross-encoder rerank wins on composite (0.827) and contextual precision (0.852). _Category:_ arXiv:2605.02520. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02520)

**AI Safety as Control of Irreversibility** — Reframes AI safety as governance of irreversibility under rising decision density — bridges alignment, security engineering, organizational economics, institutional design. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.01415. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01415)
