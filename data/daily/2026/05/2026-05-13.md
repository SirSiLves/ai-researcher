**Authorization Propagation in Multi-Agent AI Systems: Identity Governance as Infrastructure** — cs.MA. **The RL-for-LLM-reasoning trio — opposing conclusions** — Three cs.LG submissions, read as a set: "Beyond Reasoning" argues RL post-training unlocks parametric knowledge (~27% gains across families); "Sparse Policy Selection, Not Capability Learning" argues observed RL gains come from selecting over existing policies; "Expressiveness Is Key" finds RL gains scale with logic expressiveness, not chain length. Defines the open question. [Parametric knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07153) · [Sparse selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06241) · [Expressiveness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06638)

**Byte-Exact Deduplication in RAG: A Three-Regime Empirical Analysis** — cs.IR. Three regimes: BeIR (0.16% reduction), constructed enterprise patterns (24%), multi-turn conversational AI (80%). Deterministic compute savings at no quality cost — concrete production-RAG plumbing. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.09611. [arxiv.org/abs/2605.09611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09611)

**STALE: Can LLM Agents Know When Their Memories Are No Longer Valid?** — cs.CL. Probes whether agents can detect that stored beliefs have been invalidated by new evidence. Current memory architectures (Mem0, etc.) largely fail — sharp eval gap for agent-memory. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06527. [arxiv.org/abs/2605.06527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06527)
