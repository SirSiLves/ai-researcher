## Research highlights

- **Efficient Reasoning with Balanced Thinking** — Xia, Chen et al. Trains LRMs to dynamically calibrate reasoning length — counters both overthinking on easy problems and underthinking on hard ones with one learned controller. Competes with fixed CoT budgets and TERMINATOR-style early-exit. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17187 — reasoning efficiency. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17187)
- **MetaClaw: Just Talk — An Agent That Meta-Learns and Evolves in the Wild** — Su, Tang et al. LLM agent that updates its own behavior over deployment from user feedback rather than staying frozen between releases. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.14935 — deployed agent learning. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14935)
- **Alignment Makes Language Models Normative, Not Descriptive** — Muhtar, Liu et al. Side-by-side study of 120 base/aligned model pairs on 10,000+ responses; alignment objectives systematically shift models from modeling _what people do_ toward _what they should do_. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17621 — alignment / persona. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17621)
- **Complementary Reinforcement Learning** — Mur-Labadia, Muckley et al. Adds dense, complementary supervision alongside sparse outcome rewards for LLM-agent RL; tackles sample-efficiency wall on long-horizon tasks. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.14482 — agent RL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14482)
- **AdaMem: Adaptive User-Centric Memory for Long-Horizon Dialogue Agents** — Zhao, Ding et al. Memory primitives (write, decay, retrieval) designed around stable user identity across sessions rather than chronological logs. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13033 — agent memory. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13033)
- **BenchPreS: A Benchmark for Context-Aware Personalized Preference Selectivity of Persistent-Memory LLMs** — Luo, Chen et al. Probes whether models can _selectively_ apply stored preferences across third-party/institutional contexts (vs always-on personalization). _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15618 — memory governance. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15618)
- **Efficient Exploration at Scale** — Zhang, Yang et al. Online RLHF that incrementally updates reward and policy as preferences arrive; sharp gains in data efficiency vs periodic batch RLHF. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.18004 — RLHF. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18004)
- **Expert Threshold Routing for Autoregressive Language Modeling with Dynamic Computation Allocation** — Goel, Gagrani et al. Per-token threshold routing replaces fixed top-k MoE; drops auxiliary load-balance loss, allocates compute adaptively. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17942 — MoE architecture. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17942)
- **AI Scientist via Synthetic Task Scaling** — Aydın, Yilmaz et al. Argues scaling synthetic ML-research tasks (vs scaffolding bigger agents) is the cleaner path to autonomous discovery — gives a training recipe rather than an orchestration loop. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17079 — research agents. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17079)
