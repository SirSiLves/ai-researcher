## The day in 90 seconds

**A misconfigured CMS hands the press a model above Opus, and Claude Code ships the bug that breaks its own users.**

Anthropic spent Thursday losing control of two narratives at once. Fortune broke the existence of an internal model called Capybara, externally branded Claude Mythos, after security researchers found a draft launch post and roughly 3,000 unpublished assets sitting in a public-facing content-management system. Hours earlier — on the same engineering org's other side of the house — a Claude Code release shipped a caching change that began clearing thinking history on every turn instead of once an hour. The two stories will be re-read in late April as a single story about distribution-layer mistakes.

The Mythos disclosure is the one the rest of the quarter bends toward. Roy Paz of LayerX Security and Alexandre Pauwels of Cambridge surfaced the exposed datastore; the leaked draft describes Capybara as "the most capable we've built to date" with step-change scores over Claude Opus 4.6 on software coding, academic reasoning and cybersecurity, and warns in Anthropic's own words of "unprecedented cybersecurity risks." Anthropic confirmed the leak as human error in CMS configuration, removed public search, and acknowledged Mythos is in early-access customer testing. The cybersecurity framing pre-stages two follow-ups: the Claude FreeBSD kernel-RCE writeup expected on 1 April and a second packaging mistake five days out, when Claude Code source ships publicly through a missing `.npmignore`. Two distinct distribution-layer failures from the same engineering org inside one working week now bracket the roll-out of the company's most consequential model.

The operational regression is the smaller story today and the bigger story in retrospect. The caching optimisation that landed in Claude Code on 26 March made the model lose short-term memory and turn repetitive — the postmortem on 23 April will name today as the inflection where user-reported degradation broke out of the noise floor. It sits awkwardly alongside Monday's Anthropic engineering post on canonical agent-harness architecture: the same team publishing the reference design ships a measurable harness regression three days later. The harness-versus-model distinction Anthropic has been carefully drawing in public becomes, by Thursday afternoon, very hard to draw at all.

The third thread is European. Mistral formally launched Voxtral TTS, a 4-billion-parameter open-weights text-to-speech model spanning English, French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi and Arabic, with three-second voice cloning and pricing of $0.016 per 1,000 characters — roughly a tenth of ElevenLabs at what early coverage calls parity quality. The weights are on Hugging Face under CC BY-NC 4.0. It is the first credible open-weights TTS at that bar, and it threads directly into Le Chat's voice mode at a moment when European-sovereign voice stack has been a slogan looking for a model. Around it, the governance and capital pieces moved in the same direction: the European Parliament adopted its Digital Omnibus on AI negotiating mandate 569 to 45, the precondition for the eventual May trilogue deal that shifts the regulatory-sandbox deadline to 2 August 2027; Shield AI closed a $1.5 billion Series G at $12.7 billion led by Advent and JPMorgan Strategic Investment Group, with Blackstone adding a $500 million preferred tranche plus $250 million delayed-draw for roughly $2.25 billion total — the first concrete Blackstone defense-AI commitment ahead of its later Anthropic joint venture. Bain extended its global Palantir AIP partnership the same day, joining Accenture and the SAP-Palantir data-migration alliance and making three top-tier consultancies and two labs explicit competitors for the same enterprise-deployment lane.

## Major news & releases

### Disclosure of the quarter: Mythos

**Fortune breaks Anthropic "Mythos" / "Capybara" data leak — step-change model revealed in unsecured CMS.** Fortune published the exclusive on Thursday 26 March: a draft Anthropic blog post and nearly 3,000 unpublished assets were left publicly accessible in a misconfigured content-management system. The draft described a new Anthropic model — internally Capybara, externally branded Claude Mythos — as a "step change" in AI performance and "the most capable we've built to date." Capybara is described as a new tier above Opus: dramatically higher scores on software coding, academic reasoning, and cybersecurity than Claude Opus 4.6, with the blog post warning of "unprecedented cybersecurity risks." Security researchers Roy Paz (LayerX Security) and Alexandre Pauwels (University of Cambridge) discovered the exposed data store. Anthropic confirmed the leak ("human error" in CMS configuration), removed public search, and acknowledged Mythos is in early-access customer testing. _Why it matters:_ Easily the disclosure of the quarter. Three immediate consequences: (1) the cybersecurity-risk framing pre-stages the Claude FreeBSD kernel-RCE writeup on 1 April (CVE-2026-4747, Carlini's MAD Bugs project) and the Cohere/Aleph Alpha "sovereign AI" rationale; (2) it's the second Anthropic packaging mistake in the same window — five days before the Claude Code source-code leak via the missing `.npmignore` on 31 March; (3) it forces Anthropic's Mythos/Glasswing roll-out roadmap into the public eye six days before the company had planned. ### Operational regression at Anthropic

**Anthropic Claude Code: caching-optimisation bug ships, degrading quality across the user base.** A caching optimisation shipped on 26 March in a Claude Code release contained a critical bug: instead of clearing thinking history once after an hour of inactivity, it cleared the history on every subsequent turn, causing the model to lose short-term memory and become repetitive or forgetful. The fix lands in v2.1.116 ahead of the formal post-mortem on 23 April. _Why it matters:_ Second documented Claude Code quality regression in March (the first being the 4 March default-reasoning-effort change). Anthropic's eventual postmortem identifies this date as a key inflection in user-reported degradation — the same month the Mythos disclosure and the source-code leak landed. The harness-vs-model distinction the engineering team had been making becomes very public, very fast. [Anthropic engineering — Apr 23 postmortem](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mystery-solved-anthropic-reveals-changes-to-claudes-harnesses-and-operating-instructions-likely-caused-degradation)

### Open-weights voice: Voxtral lands

**Mistral releases Voxtral TTS open-weights — 4B parameters, nine languages, ~3-second voice cloning.** Mistral formally launched Voxtral TTS on Thursday, an open-weights text-to-speech model targeting nine languages (English, French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi, Arabic). 4B parameters runnable on consumer hardware; voice cloning from a 3-second reference; available open-weights on Hugging Face under CC BY NC 4.0; API priced at $0.016 per 1,000 characters, undercutting ElevenLabs at parity quality. _Why it matters:_ The first open-weights TTS at credible-vs-ElevenLabs quality — and the most consequential European-sovereign voice-stack release of 2026 to date. Threads Voxtral into Le Chat's voice mode and undercuts the OpenAI Realtime / ElevenLabs voice-agent economics by ~10×. ## Research highlights

- **CUA-Suite: Massive Human-annotated Video Demonstrations for Computer-Use Agents** — Xiangru Jian et al. Large-scale human-annotated demonstration corpus for CUAs; addresses the trajectory-data bottleneck. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.24440 — CUA / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24440)
- **UI-Voyager: A Self-Evolving GUI Agent Learning via Failed Experience** — Zichuan Lin et al. Self-evolving GUI agent that learns specifically from failed trajectories. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.24533 — GUI agents / cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24533)
- **Why Does Self-Distillation (Sometimes) Degrade the Reasoning Capability of LLMs?** — Jeonghye Kim et al. Diagnoses a recurring failure mode where reasoning degrades in self-distillation; counter-evidence to the "free lunch" framing. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.24472 — post-training / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24472)
- **T-MAP: Red-Teaming LLM Agents with Trajectory-aware Evolutionary Search** — Hyomin Lee et al. Red-team agent that uses trajectory-aware evolutionary search to find jailbreaks/exploits in target agents — concrete safety-eval instrument. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22341 — agent safety / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22341)
- **Understanding the Challenges in Iterative Generative Optimization with LLMs** — Allen Nie et al. Failure-mode analysis of DSPy/MIPRO-style loops — characterizes when iteration helps vs. when it churns. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.23994 — agent platforms / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23994)
- **Can LLM Agents Be CFOs? A Benchmark for Resource Allocation in Dynamic Enterprise Environments** — Yi Han et al. Pushes agent eval past static toolbench into stateful budget-and-priority decisions. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.23638 — enterprise agents / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23638)
- **CarePilot: A Multi-Agent Framework for Long-Horizon Computer Task Automation in Healthcare** — Akash Ghosh et al. Multi-agent CUA framework for healthcare where compliance, audit, and stateful workflow are first-class. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.24157 — CUA / healthcare. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24157)
- **EVA: Efficient Reinforcement Learning for End-to-End Video Agent** — Yaolun Zhang et al. End-to-end RL recipe for video agents — concrete artifact in the still-thin RL-for-multimodal-agent literature. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22918 — video agents / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22918)
- **When Models Judge Themselves: Unsupervised Self-Evolution for Multimodal Reasoning** — Zhengxian Wu et al. Self-judging unsupervised self-evolution for MLLM reasoning — data point on the contested "models can bootstrap reasoning without external verifiers" claim. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.21289 — multimodal / cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21289)
