## Major news & releases

### Frontier model releases — Qwen breaks open-source

**Alibaba Qwen team releases Qwen3.5-Omni — 30B-MoE multimodal beats Gemini 3.1 Pro on audio.** Unified 30B-MoE (3B active) handling text, image, audio, and video in one computational pipeline. Specs: 256k context, 10+ hours of audio, 400+ seconds of 720p video at 1 FPS, speech recognition in 113 languages, generation in 36. Plus variant claims 215 SOTA results across audio/video tasks and outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on general audio understanding/translation. Three variants (Plus/Flash/Light), available via Alibaba Cloud API, Qwen Chat, and an HF demo — but closed-weights this time, breaking the open-source streak. _Why it matters:_ Qwen's first closed-weights flagship is a strategic shift toward direct cloud-API monetisation and a credible Asia-Pacific competitor to Gemini Live and OpenAI Realtime in the multimodal-voice category — directly relevant to enterprise agent deployments where Western cloud APIs are politically constrained. [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/03/30/alibaba-qwen-team-releases-qwen3-5-omni-a-native-multimodal-model-for-text-audio-video-and-realtime-interaction/) · [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/qwen3-5-omni-alibaba-multimodal-ai-launch/)

### Tuesday news-cluster pre-staging

Anthropic's Claude is the back-end model; the Slack MCP server reaches general availability with the launch. _Why it matters:_ Slack with MCP is the single biggest enterprise-platform validation of the protocol to date — three days behind Anthropic's MCP 97M install-count milestone announcement. ## Research highlights

- **Composer 2 Technical Report** — Cursor Research (Aaron Chan, Ahmed Shalaby, Alexander Wettig et al.). Second-generation in-IDE coding model technical report — most actionable data point of the day on the coding-model frontier from the team shipping against real developer workflows. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.24477 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24477)
- **Natural-Language Agent Harnesses** — Linyue Pan, Lexiao Zou, Shuo Guo, Jingchen Ni. Empirical comparison of natural-language vs. code vs. schema as the harness representation around agents — direct contribution to the agent-platform thread on what the harness should be. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.25723 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25723)
- **Trace2Skill: Distill Trajectory-Local Lessons into Transferable Agent Skills** — Jingwei Ni, Yihao Liu, Xinpeng Liu, Yutao Sun. Distils local lessons from agent trajectories into reusable, transferable skills — concrete recipe for the contested "skill library" abstraction layer in long-horizon agent platforms. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.25158 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25158)
- **Learning to Commit: Generating Organic Pull Requests via Online Repository Memory** — Mo Li, L. H. Xu, Qitai Tan, Ting Cao. Generates organic PRs (not just patches) by maintaining online repository memory — moves the "long-running coding agent" line beyond SWE-bench single-issue framing. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.26664 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26664)
- **Lie to Me: How Faithful Is Chain-of-Thought Reasoning in Reasoning Models?** — Richard J. Young. Empirical probe of CoT faithfulness in modern reasoning models — direct input to the CoT-monitorability debate central to current alignment thinking. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.22582 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22582)
- **MedOpenClaw: Auditable Medical Imaging Agents Reasoning over Uncurated Full Studies** — Weixiang Shen, Yanzhu Hu, Che Liu, Junde Wu. Domain-grounded counterpart to the agent-safety/governance thread, with a regulated-domain instantiation (auditability over full uncurated studies). _Category:_ arXiv:2603.24649 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24649)
- **RealChart2Code** — Jiajun Zhang, Yuying Li, Zhixun Li, Xingyu Guo. Real-data chart-to-code benchmark with multi-task eval — fills the synthetic-chart gap that inflated prior results on this task; useful for document-AI / structured extraction. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.25804 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25804)
- **Sommelier: Scalable Open Multi-turn Audio Pre-processing for Full-duplex Speech LMs** — Kyudan Jung, Jihwan Kim, Soyoon Kim, Jeongoon Kim. Open audio preprocessing stack — addresses the rate-limiting data-pipeline problem for voice-agent training. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.25750 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25750)
