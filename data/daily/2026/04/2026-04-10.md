## The day in 90 seconds

**Treasury hauls in the bank CEOs over Mythos as CoreWeave seals Anthropic's third compute lane.**

Friday closed the Glasswing week with the political-economic capstone that had been building since Tuesday's gated release. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Fed Chair Jerome Powell convened the five biggest U.S. bank CEOs at Treasury HQ over a single named frontier model, while CoreWeave wrapped a 48-hour run of compute deals worth roughly $28 billion. Both stories run through Anthropic, and together they put the company at the center of U.S. financial-stability policy and the inference build-out in the same trading day.

The Treasury convening, broken by Fortune, is the first time U.S. financial regulators have pulled the bank-CEO class together around one frontier AI model. In the room: Jane Fraser of Citi, Ted Pick of Morgan Stanley, Brian Moynihan of Bank of America, Charlie Scharf of Wells Fargo, and David Solomon of Goldman Sachs. The brief was the cyber-risk profile presented by Mythos and successor models — Mythos's documented finding of 10-, 20-, and 27-year-old vulnerabilities, including in OpenBSD, makes the case that the threat is no longer hypothetical. Bloomberg's same-day explainer reframed the gated release itself as the policy intervention, language that Treasury and Bank of England staff briefings would lean on for the rest of the month. The Bank of England Financial Policy Committee published its parallel April record the same afternoon, coordinating with NCSC on Mythos-class systemic-risk testing — three days from product launch to an Anglo-American CEO-level regulatory frame.

The compute side moved in parallel. CoreWeave announced a multi-year agreement with Anthropic worth about $6.8 billion to supply NVIDIA GPU capacity for Claude production inference in U.S. data centres. Coming 24 hours after the $21 billion Meta expansion, that takes CoreWeave's two-day announcement total to roughly $28 billion and its stock up about 12 percent; nine of the top ten frontier model providers now run on the platform, and market commentary started calling the "neocloud" category settled. For Anthropic the deal completes a third compute lane alongside AWS for training and Google TPU, layered on top of Tuesday's $30 billion ARR confirmation and the Mythos disclosure. With diversified GPU, TPU and Trainium supply and a demand profile to match, Anthropic now has the structural shape to credibly run as the second-largest pure-frontier serving footprint.

A second policy thread reframes the first. Fortune's companion piece, drawing on Kemba Walden and the Jen Easterly circle, argues Mythos is not the start of AI-driven hacking but the wake-up call on a capability that has been quietly emerging for more than a year. That shifts the policy response from preventive — gate the capability — to compensatory: uplift the defenders, via the Project Glasswing alumni-network expansion already running through April. Banks cannot gate adversaries; they can be uplifted, which is exactly why Bessent and Powell could move from disclosure on Tuesday to a CEO convening by Friday. MarketingProfs called this the most consequential single week in AI policy and capability disclosure since the GPT-5 launch, and on the same day Simon Willison, quoting Andrej Karpathy, noted that ChatGPT Advanced Voice Mode still self-reports an April 2024 cutoff while Codex-tier models restructure codebases over the course of an hour — the capability gap by access point is now wide enough to see from the regulator's chair.

## Research highlights

- **Rethinking Generalization in Reasoning SFT** — anonymous. Conditional analysis shows SFT and RL exhibit asymmetric cross-domain generalization on reasoning, and reasoning gains and safety degradation track each other. Documents a "dip-and-recovery" pattern in long-CoT training. Sharp empirical input to the RL-for-reasoning thread + reasoning-vs-safety tradeoff. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.06628 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06628)
- **SkillClaw: Let Skills Evolve Collectively with an Agentic Evolver** — anonymous. Multi-user agent ecosystem where an autonomous evolver aggregates per-user interactions to refine a shared skill repository — explicit cross-user knowledge transfer with cumulative capability improvement. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08377 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08377)
- **Externalization in LLM Agents: A Unified Review of Memory, Skills, Protocols and Harness Engineering** — anonymous. Position/survey framing the agent stack as moving from parametric capability to externalized cognitive artifacts (memory, skills, MCP/A2A protocols, harness code). Useful reference for "what's outside the weights." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08224 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08224)
- **MolmoWeb: Open Visual Web Agent and Open Data for the Open Web** — anonymous. Open-weight web agent on screenshots only (no HTML or accessibility tree). Mixes synthetic trajectories, human demos, atomic web-skill trajectories; gains from parallel rollouts + best-of-N. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08516 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08516)
- **Structured Distillation of Web Agent Capabilities Enables Generalization** — anonymous. Uses Gemini 3 Pro as teacher in an agent-as-annotator pipeline (task designer / annotator / supervisor / judge filter) to produce structured synthetic trajectories; transfers across WebArena and WorkArena L1. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.07776 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07776)
- **Act Wisely: Cultivating Meta-Cognitive Tool Use in Agentic Multimodal Models** — anonymous. Most agentic-multimodal failures are meta-cognitive (when/whether to invoke a tool), not capability gaps. HDPO decouples accuracy and efficiency into separate optimization channels. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08545 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08545)
- **Graph of Skills: Dependency-Aware Structural Retrieval for Massive Agent Skills** — anonymous. Executable skill graph with reverse-weighted Personalized PageRank + context-budgeted hydration. SkillsBench + ALFWorld show reward gains + token reduction. Answers "how do you retrieve over a skill library at scale." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.05333 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05333)
- **The Master Key Hypothesis: Cross-Model Capability Transfer via Linear Subspace Alignment** — anonymous. Claims a post-trained capability lives in a low-dimensional latent subspace and can be transferred across model scales by linear alignment of "capability directions" — no retraining required. If it holds up, dramatically changes the economics of post-training. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.06377 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06377)
