## The day in 90 seconds

**Anthropic ships a Figma rival, Cerebras files at $23 billion, and xAI returns to the frontier — all on one Friday.**

W16 closes with three load-bearing moves stacked in a single day. Anthropic turns Opus 4.7's vision uplift into a consumer-shaped product 24 hours after the model itself shipped. Cerebras drops the most customer-disclosure-heavy AI S-1 the US market has seen. xAI re-enters the frontier conversation without a press release.

The product story is Claude Design. Anthropic launched it as a research preview that takes a prompt to a finished visual deliverable — presentations, websites, prototypes, one-pagers, mockups, brand videos, landing pages, full app shells — and made it available to every Claude Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise subscriber rather than packaging it as a separate SKU. The system can ingest a team's codebase and design files to hold a consistent design system across outputs, and it exports to PDF, URL, PPTX or hand-off to Canva. The technical enabler is Thursday's Opus 4.7 vision uplift at 3x resolution; the strategic enabler is that Anthropic priced it inside the existing subscription. This is the most ambitious horizontal-creative play any frontier lab has run to date, and a deliberate, simultaneous incursion into Figma's prototype workflow and Canva's design-from-prompt territory.

The capital-markets story is Cerebras. The wafer-scale alternative to NVIDIA publicly filed its S-1 with the SEC to list on Nasdaq under "CBRS" at roughly $23 billion. 2025 revenue came in at $510 million, up from a $272 million H1 2024 annualised baseline, with non-GAAP net income of $237.8 million against a $75.7 million GAAP loss. The disclosures that move the market are downstream: a $20 billion-plus Master Relationship Agreement with OpenAI covering 750 megawatts of inference capacity expandable to 2 gigawatts, and a binding term sheet with AWS to integrate CS-3 hardware into Amazon Bedrock. Morgan Stanley, Citi, Barclays and UBS are joint lead, with Mizuho and TD Cowen as bookrunners. The OpenAI line is the single largest customer-disclosure on a US AI-IPO S-1 to date, G42's revenue share has dropped to 24 percent on the back of the win, and UBS's first-tier AI-underwriting credential is now on the cover of a deal.

The frontier-model story is Grok 4.3 Beta. xAI rolled it out via the model selector on grok.com with no press release — Musk announced it on X late evening — adding enhanced long-context, native multimodal video understanding, and in-chat slide generation, plus same-day standalone Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech APIs and an App Store launch for XChat, xAI's encrypted messenger. Access is gated behind SuperGrok Heavy at $300 a month, with full rollout slated for mid-to-late May. After being absent from the W16 conversation through Thursday, xAI is now the third lab to ship a frontier-class drop in 48 hours, alongside Opus 4.7 and GPT-Rosalind. The "razor-thin frontier" thesis MIT TR ran on Monday has its receipts.

Underneath, the structural threads from Mon to Thu finished forming. Cloudflare Agents Week wrapped with 20-plus launches across compute, dev tools, inference, security and memory — Sandboxes GA, Durable Object Facets, the AI Platform unifying 14-plus providers, Outbound Workers, Cloudflare Mesh, Agent Memory private beta and the Project Think SDK — the most cohesive single-week vendor push for an end-to-end agent OS yet. Red Hat's Friday Five named the defender's stack opposite Anthropic Mythos: CIO Marco Bill on end-to-end inventory, Mythos AI-driven triage commentary, and Stefanie Chiras on an open-source-anchored hardware-agnostic AI layer — laying the editorial groundwork for Red Hat AI 3.x and Summit/Think 2026 on May 12. The MarketingProfs and VentureBeat analytical wave around OpenAI's enterprise pivot — business customers at roughly 40 percent of revenue trending to 50 percent by year-end, "switching costs" pitched as the explicit answer to Anthropic's enterprise-share lead — supplies the read-through into the OpenAI Deployment Company landing 11 May.

## Research highlights

- **Dive into Claude Code: The Design Space of Today's and Future AI Agent Systems** — anonymous cs.AI submission. Reverse-engineering analysis of Claude Code as a representative production agent system, used to map the design space of next-gen agent stacks. Useful synthesis paper for the "what makes a good agent harness" thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14228 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14228)
- **UniDoc-RL: Coarse-to-Fine Visual RAG with Hierarchical Actions and Dense Rewards** — anonymous cs.IR submission. Visual RAG over documents trained with hierarchical actions (page → region → token) and dense rewards instead of end-of-trajectory only. Directly addresses the "agentic visual retrieval" gap for document AI. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14967 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14967)
- **Don't Retrieve, Navigate: Distilling Enterprise Knowledge into Navigable Agent Skills** — anonymous cs.IR submission. Replaces retrieve-then-read with distill-into-navigable-agent-skills. A direct challenge to vanilla RAG for enterprise knowledge — relevant to long-context-vs-RAG and skill-based-memory threads. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14572 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14572)
- **DR^3-Eval: Towards Realistic and Reproducible Deep Research Evaluation** — anonymous cs.IR submission. Evaluation framework for deep-research agents emphasising realism + reproducibility — needed as ad-hoc deep-research benchmarks proliferate. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14683 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14683)
- **RAD-2: Scaling Reinforcement Learning in a Generator-Discriminator Framework** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Scales generator-discriminator RL for LLMs — a structured alternative to RLHF/RLVR that puts a learned discriminator (not a verifier) in the reward seat. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.15308 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15308)
- **LongAct: Harnessing Intrinsic Activation Patterns for Long-Context Reinforcement Learning** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Long-context RL post-training conditioned on intrinsic activation patterns rather than synthetic-needle objectives. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14922 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14922)
- **TRACER: Trace-Based Adaptive Cost-Efficient Routing for LLM Classification** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Cost-aware routing across model tiers based on per-trace features — concrete production primitive for the LLM-router subfield as multi-model deployments scale. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14531 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14531)
- **KV Packet: Recomputation-Free Context-Independent KV Caching for LLMs** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Context-independent KV blocks ("packets") that can be reused without recomputation across prompts. Pairs with byte-exact dedup and IceCache from earlier in the week. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13226 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13226)
