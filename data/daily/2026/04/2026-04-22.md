## The day in 90 seconds

**Google ships its enterprise agent stack and splits TPUs in two as per-request pricing breaks.**

Cloud Next day two converted Tuesday's keynote rhetoric into general availability across the whole stack: a Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform with governance built in, two eighth-generation TPUs that abandon a single chip for training and inference, and channel commitments from Salesforce and Accenture. On the same day, GitHub paused Copilot Individual signups, and Mozilla quietly confirmed that 271 Firefox vulnerabilities in the upcoming 150 release were found by an early version of Anthropic Mythos.

The headline announcement was the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform — a direct evolution of Vertex AI into one workspace for building, deploying, governing, and observing agents. Google shipped Agent Designer as a visual builder, Inbox for agent activity, long-running agents that hold state across multiple days, and a governance trinity of Agent Identity, Agent Registry, and Agent Gateway that enterprise CISOs have been asking for since the start of the year. Model Garden offers first-class access to more than 200 models, including Gemini 3.1 Pro, the Nano Banana 2 image variant, Lyria 3 for audio, and Gemma 4. Salesforce announced Agentforce and Gemini Enterprise as a single context layer with agents deployable inside Slack and Workspace, while Accenture committed sector-specific Gemini Enterprise agent libraries for financial services, retail, healthcare, and public sector. In seventy-two hours, Snowflake, Salesforce, and Accenture have all aligned around Gemini Enterprise as the substrate — a multi-partner ecosystem aimed squarely at Microsoft 365 plus Azure.

Underneath the platform, Google previewed an eighth-generation TPU architecture that splits training and inference silicon for the first time. TPU 8t, codenamed Sunfish and designed with Broadcom, pairs two compute dies with an I/O chiplet and eight stacks of 12-high HBM3e for roughly thirty percent more memory bandwidth than Ironwood and up to 2.7 times the perf-per-dollar on large-scale training. TPU 8i, codenamed Zebrafish and designed with MediaTek, is a single compute die targeted at low-latency mixture-of-experts inference at up to eighty percent better perf-per-dollar than Ironwood. Both ship on TSMC 2nm in late 2027. Ironwood itself, the TPU 7x generation, went GA the same day at 4.6 petaFLOPS per chip and 42.5 exaFLOPS in a 9,216-chip superpod. Pulling MediaTek — a consumer-mobile silicon house — into a hyperscaler inference roadmap is the most aggressive cost-engineering bet any cloud has made, and the chip-architecture analogue to AWS Graviton5's CPU agentic move earlier in the month.

The third thread is the same hardware story read from the other end of the wire. GitHub announced same-day that it is pausing Copilot Individual signups, tightening usage limits across the line, restricting Opus 4.7 to the new $39 Pro+ tier, and dropping older Opus models entirely. The load-bearing quote in the announcement — "long-running, parallelized sessions now regularly consume far more resources than the original plan structure was built to support" — is the cleanest market signal yet that per-request bundles cannot survive agentic workloads; the unit economics shift back to per-token. Anthropic spent part of the morning adding then quietly removing a $100/month Claude Code Max tier from its pricing page, which Simon Willison untangled by mid-afternoon. Sitting alongside, Bobby Holley at Mozilla confirmed that Firefox 150 ships fixes for 271 vulnerabilities found by an early Mythos Preview applied to the browser, with the closing line that "defenders finally have a chance to win, decisively" — the most concrete single data point of the entire Mythos arc to date. And Alibaba dropped Qwen3.6-27B, a 27-billion-parameter dense model that beats the previous 397B/17B-active MoE on agentic coding and runs locally as a 16.8GB GGUF.

**Read this first:** [Introducing Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise-agent-platform) — the most coherent enterprise-agent pitch any hyperscaler has made in 2026, and the move every competitor will be answering through the rest of the quarter.

### 8th-gen TPUs: training vs inference

**Google's 8th-generation TPUs: TPU 8t "Sunfish" (Broadcom, training) + TPU 8i "Zebrafish" (MediaTek, inference).** Google previewed its eighth-generation TPU architecture, splitting training and inference silicon for the first time. **TPU 8t** (Broadcom-designed): two compute dies + one I/O chiplet + 8 stacks of 12-high HBM3e — ~30% more memory bandwidth vs Ironwood, up to **2.7× perf-per-dollar** for large-scale training. **TPU 8i** (MediaTek-designed): single compute die + 1 I/O die + 6 stacks of HBM3e — engineered for ~20–30% lower inference cost vs the training variant, up to **80% perf-per-dollar improvement** vs Ironwood, particularly for low-latency MoE workloads. Both target TSMC 2nm, late 2027. **Ironwood (TPU 7x)** also went GA today — 4.6 petaFLOPS per chip, **42.5 exaFLOPS in a 9,216-chip superpod**. _Why it matters:_ The training/inference split is the chip-architecture analogue to what AWS Graviton5 telegraphed for CPU agentic work — the AI economy is bifurcating between heavy training silicon and ultra-efficient inference silicon. Google's involvement of MediaTek (consumer-mobile heritage) for the inference chip is the most aggressive cost-engineering bet any hyperscaler has made. ## Research highlights

- **AgentSPEX: An Agent SPecification and EXecution Language** — anonymous (cs.AI). Formal specification + execution language for agents — relevant to the "compile-once, execute-many" pattern and to making agent behavior auditable. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13346 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13346)
- **AJ-Bench: Benchmarking Agent-as-a-Judge for Environment-Aware Evaluation** — anonymous (cs.AI). Benchmark for agent-as-a-judge specifically targeting environment-aware (rather than purely outcome-based) evaluation — useful as LLM-judge usage outgrows static rubrics. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18240 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18240)
- **TEMPO: Scaling Test-time Training for Large Reasoning Models** — anonymous (cs.LG). Test-time training (not just test-time search) for reasoning models, scaled. Concrete instantiation of the "post-training never ends" inference paradigm. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.19295 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19295)
- **Chat2Workflow: A Benchmark for Generating Executable Visual Workflows from Natural Language** — anonymous (cs.AI). Natural-language → executable visual workflow generation — the n8n / Zapier-AI thread getting a proper benchmark. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.19667 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19667)
- **Dual-View Training for Instruction-Following Information Retrieval** — anonymous (cs.IR). Two-view training for instruction-following retrievers — directly relevant to the "follow nuanced retrieval instructions" gap in current dense retrievers (companion to LLM-Based Dense Retriever Robustness from yesterday). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18845 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18845)
- **Code-Switching Information Retrieval: Benchmarks, Analysis, and the Limits of Current Retrievers** — anonymous (cs.IR). Code-switched queries (multilingual within a single query) break current retrievers in characteristic ways; introduces benchmark + analysis. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17632 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17632)
- **SkillLearnBench: Benchmarking Continual Learning Methods for Agent Skill Generation** — anonymous (cs.AI). Continual-learning benchmark for agent skill generation — pairs with SkillFlow (yesterday) as the eval surface for the skill-bank/Voyager-style agent thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20087 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20087)
- **ClawNet: Human-Symbiotic Agent Network for Cross-User Autonomous Cooperation** — anonymous (cs.MA). Multi-agent network designed for cross-user cooperation (different users' agents collaborating) — concrete A2A-style direction with explicit attention to identity boundaries. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.19211 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19211)
