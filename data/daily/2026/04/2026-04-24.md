## The day in 90 seconds

**Anthropic banks a third hyperscaler in four days as DeepSeek and Cohere redraw the rest of the map.**

The heaviest single week of 2026 AI to date ended on a Friday that did four structural things at once: it closed Anthropic's capital-and-compute trifecta, it pushed a frontier-class Chinese model onto Huawei silicon, it gave Europe its first credible non-US/non-Chinese frontier-lab consolidation, and it forced the US executive branch to name "industrial-scale distillation" as a policy line.

The capital story is the one to read first. Google said it will invest up to $40 billion in Anthropic — $10 billion in cash now at a $350 billion valuation, $30 billion contingent on performance, and 5 gigawatts of Google Cloud compute over five years. Add Monday's Amazon deal at $5 billion plus a $20 billion expansion and 5GW, and Tuesday's Anthropic-Google-Broadcom 3.5GW TPU partnership, and Anthropic has now disclosed roughly $45 billion in committed equity capital and more than 8.5GW of multi-cloud compute inside four trading days. That is not comparable to a tech-IPO any more; it is comparable to national-infrastructure capex. The $350 billion sticker held at the same level as February, which Motley Fool called "a screaming bargain" against $850-900 billion preemptive offers reportedly floating around the round.

DeepSeek picked the same Friday to ship V4 Preview, and the choice of silicon is what makes it the day's second-most-important story. V4 Pro is a 1.6-trillion-parameter, 49B-active mixture-of-experts; V4 Flash is 284B/13B; both run 1M-token context and post top-tier coding benchmarks. Pricing of $0.14 input and $0.28 output per million tokens on Flash undercuts Gemini, GPT and Claude by roughly ten times on the cheap end. The model was trained on Huawei's Ascend 950 chips clustered through Huawei's "Supernode" interconnect — the most explicit demonstration to date that the US export-control regime is being routed around rather than enforced. Anthropic and OpenAI accused DeepSeek of model distillation; OSTP Director Michael Kratsios used the phrase "industrial-scale distillation" in public. That is the policy escalation point, and it landed within hours of Cohere announcing in Berlin its merger with Germany's Aleph Alpha — a $20 billion combined entity, Schwarz Group adding $600 million to a Series E, and Germany's Karsten Wildberger and Canada's Evan Solomon on stage endorsing it as a "sovereign AI" alternative to US labs. It is the cleanest non-US, non-Chinese consolidation of 2026 so far.

Underneath the three big threads, two pieces of plumbing-grade reading dropped. Anthropic published the postmortem for the April 23 Claude Code regression, and Simon Willison's annotated version makes clear the cause was not the model but three separate harness bugs — the standout being a March 26 change to clear stale thinking context after an hour of idle time that, due to a bookkeeping error, fired every turn for the rest of the session, leaving Claude "forgetful and repetitive." SemiAnalysis's "Coding Assistant Breakdown" then put a number on what coding workloads actually cost: a true blended Opus 4.7 price of about $0.99 per million tokens, despite the $5/$25 sticker, because real coding traffic runs roughly 300-to-1 input-to-output with cache-hit rates above 90 percent. Read together, they reframe the doubled-rate-card debate of the last week: the sticker price is not the price, and the harness is not the model. Quieter side notes — OpenAI moved GPT-5.5 and 5.5 Pro into the API with a system card that for the first time gates API and ChatGPT evaluations separately; Anthropic deepened M365 Copilot integration with Opus 4.7 as a primary reasoning option ahead of the 6-9 May Excel/Word/PowerPoint general availability; Amazon disclosed its custom-silicon business at a $20 billion-plus annual run rate, more than doubling year over year; and Snap booked roughly 1,000 layoffs with Evan Spiegel attributing the restructure to 65 percent of new code now being AI-generated.

## Research highlights

- **VLAA-GUI: Knowing When to Stop, Recover, and Search — A Modular Framework for GUI Automation** — anonymous (cs.AI). Modular GUI-agent framework that explicitly handles termination, recovery, and re-search as first-class control primitives rather than implicit policy behaviors. Concrete answer to "strong navigators but weak at knowing when to stop." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21375 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21375)
- **Co-Evolving LLM Decision and Skill Bank Agents for Long-Horizon Tasks** — anonymous (cs.AI). Joint co-evolution of decision policy and skill bank — pushes the Voyager-style skill-library pattern further by training both layers together. Companion to SkillFlow / SkillLearnBench. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20987 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20987)
- **WorldMark: A Unified Benchmark Suite for Interactive Video World Models** — anonymous (cs.AI). Unified benchmark for interactive video world models — needed eval surface as world-model-augmented agents proliferate. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21686 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21686)
- **TingIS: Real-time Risk Event Discovery from Noisy Customer Incidents at Enterprise Scale** — anonymous (cs.AI). Production-scale risk-event discovery system over noisy incident streams — concrete enterprise deployment pattern for an LLM-on-noisy-events workload. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21889 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21889)
- **WebGen-R1: Incentivizing LLMs to Generate Functional and Aesthetic Websites** — anonymous (cs.AI). RL with explicit functional + aesthetic rewards for website generation — generative agents producing artifacts evaluated on both correctness and design dimensions. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20398 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20398)
- **Trust but Verify: DAVinCI — A Framework for Dual Attribution and Verification** — anonymous (cs.AI). Dual-attribution + verification framework — extends provenance and attribution from generation into a structured trust-and-verify primitive. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21193 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21193)
- **Hybrid Policy Distillation for LLMs** — anonymous (cs.LG). Hybrid (on-policy + off-policy) distillation for LLMs — slots into the very active distillation-mechanism thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.20244 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20244)
- **3D-VCD: Hallucination Mitigation in 3D-LLM Embodied Agents through Visual Contrastive Decoding** — anonymous (cs.AI). VCD extended to 3D-LLM embodied agents — concrete mitigation for embodied-agent hallucinated objects where the consequences are physical. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08645 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645)
