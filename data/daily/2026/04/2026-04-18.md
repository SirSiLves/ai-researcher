## The day in 90 seconds

**Figma drops 7% on the Saturday Claude Design mass-test, and ServiceNow's default-model choice gets reframed as the bigger Anthropic enterprise story than M365.**

Saturday turned Friday's Claude Design launch into a markets event. The design community spent the day running Anthropic's new prompt-to-prototype surface against Figma Make, Canva Magic Design and Adobe Express, and by close of trading Figma had fallen roughly seven percent on Anthropic's encroachment into a market where Figma still holds 80 to 90 percent of UI/UX share. The cleanest "AI disintermediation" trade of Q2 — cleaner than the Salesforce Saaspocalypse selloff — and the day's reminder that when frontier labs ship product surfaces rather than APIs, incumbent SaaS multiples reprice within hours.

The unit economics did the analytical work. Get AI Perks published a first-impression mass-test framing Claude Design as "the AI tool that wants to replace Figma," and BuildFast With AI shipped a same-day guide for non-designers, both anchoring on the Pro/Max bundling. Every existing Claude subscriber — a roughly fifty-million user surface — now has Claude Design included by default, against Figma's roughly thirteen million paid seats. Anima added the first explicit Claude-Design-to-Figma interop guide, giving the design community its first round-trip workflow rather than a clean either/or. Gizmodo, designrush and Fast Company carried the seven-percent move, and "design's frenemies era" took shape as the consensus framing inside a single Saturday news cycle.

The structural Saturday story sat one layer down, in enterprise software. ServiceNow's default-model standardisation on Claude in Build Agent — announced Thursday and largely treated as another integration — got weekend reframing in CIO and Cloud Wars as the single most consequential enterprise-stack standardisation in the Anthropic playbook to date, bigger than the Microsoft 365 Claude integration earlier in the quarter. Build Agent sits in the developer surface of every ServiceNow customer; when the 8,500-customer base hits it, they land on Claude unless they actively opt out. The radar's "agent walled-garden vs. open-MCP" topic moves with this. Default-model choice is the new platform power dynamic, and ServiceNow is the first large enterprise vendor to hand it cleanly to one lab.

The third thread is a price-segmentation story and a research-surface story rolling together. The Friday xAI Grok 4.3 Beta release drew its practitioner-review wave on Saturday — sixteen-agent Heavy system retained, two-million-token context retained, native video and audio APIs added, batch image and video generation, XChat, plus standalone speech-to-text and text-to-speech APIs. The flagged gap is persistent memory: still absent at three hundred dollars a month, against ChatGPT and Claude on the same agent surface. The Grok 4.3 ceiling is real on context window and multi-agent size; the floor is the absence of session continuity at the highest tier in the market. In parallel, Simon Willison turned Saturday into a detailed diff walk-through of Anthropic's published Claude.ai system prompts across the Opus 4.6 to 4.7 transition, and built a "fake git history" repo from Anthropic's Markdown archive so you can diff Claude versions through standard GitHub commit views. Anthropic remains the only major lab publishing these prompts; the harness is now a research surface, not a product detail. Matt Mochalkin's "6 Critical Challenges Facing MCP in 2026" carried the counterweight from the protocol side — auth, audit, and trust-by-default are the fragile parts as MCP scales — while the day's research backfill surfaced Modality-Native Routing in A2A networks (MMA2A), which posts 52 percent task completion against 32 percent for text-only on vision-dependent tasks, the first real multimodal extension of the A2A protocol.

## Research highlights

_Saturday — arXiv has no Saturday submission slot; HF Papers `/date/2026-04-18` redirects to the prior Friday. Picks below are the strongest items from the Friday cohort plus late-week MCP/A2A submissions, deduped against the canonical 04-17 slice._

- **Dive into Claude Code: The Design Space of Today's and Future AI Agent Systems** — Jiacheng Liu, Xiaohan Zhao. Architectural anatomy of Claude Code as an agentic system — five core values driving the design, with comparison to OpenClaw to show how deployment context shapes agent architecture. Closest thing yet to a "how this is actually built" reference for frontier coding agents — baseline reading for any agent-platform team. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14228 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14228)
- **Don't Retrieve, Navigate: Distilling Enterprise Knowledge into Navigable Agent Skills for QA and RAG** — Yiqun Sun, Pengfei Wei. Corpus2Skill turns document collections into hierarchical skill directories that LLM agents navigate, instead of consuming flat retrieval results. Beats traditional RAG on enterprise QA where structure matters — important reframing of the "retrieval vs navigation" axis for agentic RAG. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14572 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14572)
- **UniDoc-RL: Coarse-to-Fine Visual RAG with Hierarchical Actions and Dense Rewards** — Jun Wang, Shuo Tan. DeepGlint RL framework progressively refining visual evidence from document retrieval through image selection to active region cropping, with a dense reward shaping the trajectory. Up to 17.7% gains over prior RL-based methods — concrete advance for visual/multimodal RAG. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14967 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14967)
- **Modality-Native Routing in Agent-to-Agent Networks: A Multimodal A2A Protocol Extension** — Vasundra Srinivasan. MMA2A inspects A2A Agent Card capability declarations to route voice, image, and text in their native modality; 52% task completion vs 32% for text-only on vision-dependent tasks. Concrete A2A protocol evolution. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12213 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12213)
- **DR³-Eval: Towards Realistic and Reproducible Deep Research Evaluation** — Qianqian Xie, Qingheng Xiong. NJU-LINK Lab benchmark for deep-research agents using authentic user-provided materials paired with a static research corpus and multi-dimensional eval rubric. Surfaces real retrieval-robustness and hallucination gaps as the category proliferates. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14683 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14683)
- **KV Packet: Recomputation-Free Context-Independent KV Caching for LLMs** — Chuangtao Chen, Grace Li Zhang. Reusable cached documents via trainable soft-token adapters instead of recomputation; near-zero FLOPs and lower TTFT than recomputation baselines. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13226 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13226)
- **SafeDream: Safety World Model for Proactive Early Jailbreak Detection** — Bo Yan, Weikai Lin. Safety state world model + statistical detection + contrastive analysis to flag multi-turn jailbreak ~1.2 turns before compliance — proactive defence complement to ICD/CRA attack work. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.16824 — cs.CR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16824)
