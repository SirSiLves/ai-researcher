## The day in 90 seconds

**Anthropic answers OpenAI's cyber-defender pitch in 24 hours by putting Opus 4.7 inside Falcon, Cortex and SentinelOne the same morning.**

Twenty-four hours after OpenAI confirmed it would route GPT-5.5-Cyber to "critical cyber defenders," Anthropic opened Claude Security to public beta and landed Opus 4.7 across five SecOps platforms in a single coordinated announcement. The UK AI Security Institute, separately, said GPT-5.5 is already at Mythos-level vulnerability-finding capability — only this time publicly available. The cyber-AI lane stopped being a roadmap and became a live integration race.

The structural move was Anthropic's. Claude Security exits private preview powered by Opus 4.7, with the receipt being 500 production vulnerabilities surfaced in two months, including bugs that survived expert review for years. Alongside the beta, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, SentinelOne, Trend Micro and Wiz all announced that Opus 4.7 now runs inside their platforms; CrowdStrike's Project QuiltWorks puts it directly in Falcon for vulnerability discovery and remediation. Five vendor-platform integrations in a single press cycle is the most concrete answer to the "where do enterprises actually run frontier-defensive models" question of the cycle, and it lands as the UK AISI publishes an evaluation finding GPT-5.5's cyber capabilities comparable to Claude Mythos — but, unlike Mythos, available to anyone with a developer key. The gating model that justified Mythos Preview's restricted access a month ago is, by Thursday, a policy artefact.

Underneath the security headline ran two structural threads. IBM dropped the Granite 4.1 family ahead of Think 2026 — 3B, 8B and 30B dense models under Apache 2.0, trained on roughly 15 trillion tokens with progressive annealing toward technical and tool-calling data, shipped same-day on watsonx, Hugging Face, OpenRouter and Ollama. The 8B and 30B sizes target watsonx Orchestrate and Granite agents directly, and the companion Granite Speech 4.1 2B release ships an unusual dual track — autoregressive ASR with translation, plus a non-autoregressive editing model for fast inference. Read against Mistral Medium 3.5 going open-weights at 77.6 percent SWE-Bench the day before, two open-weights enterprise drops in 48 hours pull the open lane out of being a Chinese-only story and onto US enterprise rails.

The third thread was a price-and-distribution one. xAI quietly closed the Grok 4.3 API rollout with no livestream and no Elon thread: 1M context, 207 tok/s, $1.25 input and $2.50 output per million tokens — about 20 percent cheaper than Grok 4.20 v2, scoring 53 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, above Sonnet 4.6 and behind GPT-5.5, Opus 4.7 and Gemini 3.1 Pro. At that price it undercuts Sonnet 4.6 and Gemini 3.1 Pro on agentic loops where Grok has parity. The same day, GPT-5.5 reached general availability inside Microsoft Foundry — one day after Microsoft's Q3 print and three days after the OpenAI restructure that ended Azure exclusivity. The "Foundry-first" gap is now measured in days, not quarters, but it survived the non-exclusivity, which is the only thing that makes the multi-cloud OpenAI strategy work for either side. Swedish legal-AI startup Legora confirmed a $5.6 billion valuation with NVIDIA's NVentures fund taking what is reportedly its first legal-AI position, making Legora the first European applied-agent company past $5B and sharpening the fight with Harvey.

Governance moved sideways. The EU AI Act Digital Omnibus trilogue broke up at dawn on 29 April after roughly twelve hours of talks; reporting on 30 April clarified the breakdown was over Annex I conformity assessment architecture for AI embedded in products under existing sectoral safety law — not over the headline postponement of the 2 August high-risk obligations. The Cypriot Presidency has 13 May targeted for a third trilogue, with the August deadline still legally in force.

## Research highlights

- **Safe Bilevel Delegation (SBD): A Formal Framework for Runtime Delegation Safety in Multi-Agent Systems** — Formalizes hierarchical agent delegation as bilevel optimization: outer meta-weight network learns context-dependent safety-efficiency weights, inner loop optimizes the delegation policy subject to a probabilistic safety constraint. Proves Safety Monotonicity, Inner Policy Convergence, and an Accountability Propagation bound across multi-hop delegation chains. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27358 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27358)
- **Security Attack and Defense Strategies for Autonomous Agent Frameworks: A Layered Review with OpenClaw as a Case Study** — Layered review organized into four layers — context/instruction, tool/action, state/persistence, ecosystem/automation — and shows how threats propagate across them. Useful frame for the "agent supply chain" governance discussion. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27464 — cs.CR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27464)
- **End-to-End Evaluation and Governance of an EHR-Embedded AI Agent for Clinicians** — Field report on continuous governance for Hyperscribe: rubric validation (20 clinicians, 1,646 rubrics, 823 cases), live feedback, perf monitoring, controlled experimentation. Seven versions evaluated, median quality 84%→95%; live error reports dropped 79%→30% over three months. Real-world template for governance-as-product. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27309 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27309)
- **NuggetIndex: Governed Atomic Retrieval for Maintainable RAG** — Stores atomic information units ("nuggets") as managed records with temporal validity intervals and lifecycle states, filtering deprecated facts before ranking. +42% nugget recall, +9pp temporal correctness, −55% conflict rate, −64% generator input length vs. passage/proposition baselines. SIGIR '26. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27306 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27306)
- **ObjectGraph: From Document Injection to Knowledge Traversal — A Native File Format for the Agentic Era** — Argues traditional document formats designed for sequential human reading create inefficiencies for retrieval-driven agents; proposes OBJECTGRAPH (.og), a typed, directed knowledge graph traversed rather than injected as a string. Up to 95.3% token reduction at parity accuracy — provocative reframing of "documents as agent inputs." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27820 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27820)
- **Claw-Eval-Live: A Live Agent Benchmark for Evolving Real-World Workflows** — Dynamic agent benchmark that refreshes against real-world demand signals rather than staying static; emphasizes verifiable agent action through execution traces and audit logs. Even leading models reach only 66.7% completion, with persistent failure on business workflows and multi-system operations. Live, audit-grade complement to OSWorld/WebArena. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.28139 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.28139)
- **A Brief Overview: Agentic Reinforcement Learning In Large Language Models** — Position-style overview of LLM-based Agentic RL — meta-reasoning, self-reflection, multi-step decision-making integrated into the learning loop; useful vocabulary check on the "agent RL" cluster. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27859 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27859)
- **LLM-as-a-Judge for Human-AI Co-Creation: A Reliability-Aware Evaluation Framework for Coding** — Reliability-aware LLM-as-judge framework for human-AI coding sessions; reports modest inter-judge agreement, useful caveat for the "scale up LLM-judge" reflex. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.27727 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27727)
