## Major news & releases

### Glasswing capability-disclosure week opens — AISI evaluates Mythos

**Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview rattles cybersecurity — thousands of zero-days, public release withheld.** UK regulators and the AI Security Institute disclosed evaluations of Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview (announced 7 April under Project Glasswing). In pre-release testing, Mythos autonomously identified and exploited thousands of previously unknown vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser, including CVE-2026-4747, a 17-year-old NFS RCE in FreeBSD. Anthropic withheld general access, instead routing the model to an 11-partner consortium (AWS, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorgan Chase, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks). Fortune ran a long-form interview with Dario Amodei and security veterans on what Mythos means for defence. _Why it matters:_ This is the first concrete "we built it but we're not shipping it" call from a frontier lab over offensive-cyber risk; AISI's report on the same day frames Mythos as a step-change over previous frontier capability. The Glasswing consortium becomes the de-facto template for future capability-gated rollouts. ## Research highlights

- **Multi-User Large Language Model Agents** — anonymous cs.AI submission. Most agent stacks are implicitly optimized for a single-principal interaction; this work designs for multiple users with distinct roles, preferences and authority — a direct match for enterprise agent platforms moving beyond personal-assistant framing. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08567 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08567)
- **Process Reward Agents for Steering Knowledge-Intensive Reasoning** — anonymous cs.CL/cs.AI submission. Reasoning in knowledge-intensive domains needs non-local verification — can't grade a step without synthesizing external evidence. Builds process-reward agents that score intermediate steps via retrieval-conditioned judgments instead of math-style local checks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09482 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09482)
- **LLMs Generate Harmful Content Using a Distinct, Unified Mechanism** — anonymous cs.CL submission. Mechanistic-interpretability claim that jailbreaks and fine-tuning-induced emergent misalignment share a common underlying circuit. Useful structural data for the safety/alignment radar on why current alignment is brittle. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09544 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09544)
- **p1: Better Prompt Optimization with Fewer Prompts** — anonymous cs.CL submission. Studies what makes a task amenable to prompt optimization at all: variance of reward across system-prompt candidates is the binding constraint. Practical guidance for when DSPy/MIPRO-style search pays off vs noise-chasing. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08801 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08801)
- **Backdoor Attacks on Decentralised Post-Training** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Decentralised post-training (data + pipeline parallelism across untrusted workers) is vulnerable to coordinated poisoning by malicious participants — formalises the threat model and demonstrates concrete backdoor channels. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.02372 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02372)
- **Robust Reasoning Benchmark** — anonymous cs.CL submission. 14-technique perturbation pipeline showing reasoning models are heavily overfit to standard textual formatting on math benchmarks; modest formatting/notation shifts collapse performance. Sharp counter-evidence to "reasoning models generalize." _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08571 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08571)
- **Cactus: Constrained Acceptance Speculative Sampling** — anonymous cs.CL submission. Standard speculative sampling enforces exact distribution match with the verifier; Cactus relaxes this with a constrained acceptance criterion that preserves quality while widening accept rates — concrete decoding-throughput win for production inference. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.04987 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04987)
- **EXAONE 4.5 Technical Report** — LG AI Research. First open-weight VLM from LG, integrating a dedicated visual encoder into the EXAONE 4.0 framework for native multimodal pretraining. Adds another open-weights option to the Qwen-VL / Llama-3-Vision / InternVL frontier. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08644 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08644)
