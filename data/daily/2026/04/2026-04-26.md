## Research highlights

_Sunday — HF Papers `/date/2026-04-26` redirects to the prior Friday; arXiv has no weekend submission slot. Picks below are the Friday cohort surfaced as Sunday backfill, deduped against 04-24 and 04-25._

- **Evaluating Multi-Hop Reasoning in RAG Systems — CARE: Context-Aware Retriever Evaluation** — Brehme & Ströhle. Compares LLM-as-judge strategies for retriever evaluation in multi-hop RAG; introduces CARE and shows it consistently beats existing approaches across HotPotQA, MuSiQue, SQuAD with OpenAI/Meta/Google models. Concrete eval-stack contribution — accepted at SynIRgy/ECIR 2026. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18234 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18234)
- **AeroRAG: Structured Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented LLM for Fine-Grained Aerial Visual Reasoning** — Xue & Deng. Converts aerial images into structured visual knowledge (objects, quantities, locations, relations) and retrieves query-relevant slices for VQA. Wins in dense aerial scenes and relation-based tasks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17889 — cs.CV. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17889)
- **PersonalAI: A Systematic Comparison of Knowledge Graph Storage and Retrieval Approaches for Personalized LLM agents** — Menschikov & Evseev. Hybrid graph design with standard edges plus hyper-edges for LLM-built personal knowledge graphs, with per-task evaluation across multiple benchmarks. Pairs with mnemonic-sovereignty + STALE on the personalized-memory line. _Category:_ arXiv:2506.17001 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17001)
- **Explainable Disentangled Representation Learning for Generalizable Authorship Attribution (EAVAE)** — Man & Pham. Separates style from content via architectural design, combining supervised contrastive learning with a VAE using separate encoders and an explainable discriminator. Contribution to AI-text-detection that's becoming a governance concern. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21300 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21300)
- **3D-VCD: Hallucination Mitigation in 3D-LLM Embodied Agents through Visual Contrastive Decoding** — Ogunleye & Abdelrahman. Inference-time decoding contrast between original and distorted 3D scene graphs to suppress hallucination in embodied agents; gains on 3D-POPE and HEAL without retraining. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.08645 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08645)
- **CoFEE: Reasoning Control for LLM-Based Feature Discovery** — Westermann & Griffin. Reasoning-control framework that enforces cognitive behaviors via structured prompts only — no model surgery. +15.2% quality, −29% feature count, −50% cost on feature-discovery tasks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21584 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21584)
- **Context Unrolling in Omni Models** — Yang & Lin. Unified omni-modality model with a Context Unrolling mechanism that aggregates complementary modal representations across text/image/video/3D — multimodal-agent foundation work. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.21921 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21921)
- **Adaptive Prompt Embedding Optimization for LLM Jailbreaking** — Li & Fung. White-box attack optimizing embeddings of original prompt tokens (not appended adversarial suffixes); extends jailbreak threat model to embedding-level interventions. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.24983 — cs.CR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24983)
