## The day in 90 seconds

**Frontier labs raise national-infrastructure money on the same day Google bids to be the operating system for agents.**

Twenty-four hours produced the largest single capital raise in tech history, a second mega-compute deal that puts one lab on roughly 8.5 gigawatts of committed silicon, and a Cloud Next keynote whose numbers reframe Google's product as agent plumbing rather than another chatbot. Snowflake, sitting on top of all of it, used the same morning to declare itself the control plane.

OpenAI announced the close of $122 billion in fresh capital — a primary round plus an employee secondary tender at a $750 billion valuation, explicitly earmarked for compute capex pacing through 2028. That is the largest single capital raise in tech history, and it lands inside a 36-hour window that has Anthropic also pulling down structural capacity. The day before, Anthropic pledged $100 billion of AWS spend and 5 gigawatts of Trainium. On Tuesday it published a 3.5-gigawatt expansion of its compute partnership with Google and Broadcom, online in 2027, on Broadcom-designed TPUs. That brings Anthropic's committed compute to roughly 8.5 gigawatts across two clouds and two silicon families. The same release re-stated Anthropic's run-rate revenue above $30 billion, up from about $9 billion at the end of 2025, with more than a thousand customers spending over $1 million annualised — a number that has doubled in under two months. The capital-formation regime around AI has changed shape, not just scale: frontier labs now raise rounds the size of national infrastructure programmes, and they are doing it twice in a week.

Sundar Pichai opened Google Cloud Next 2026 in Las Vegas the same morning, and the disclosures were calibrated to match the moment. Google Cloud is running at $70 billion of annual revenue and 48 percent growth, with a $240 billion backlog and 750 million Gemini users. Roughly 75 percent of new code written at Google is AI-generated, and a recent internal migration ran six times faster on the new agent system than a year ago. Pichai's framing — "the OS for agents, not a model provider" — is the strategic positioning of the week, and the backlog number is a deliberate signal to investors about how much of that demand is already booked. Bundled alongside the keynote: Deep Research Max for Gemini Enterprise, a direct counter to ChatGPT Deep Research, and a Model Garden that now spans more than 200 models.

The third thread is what happens beneath those headlines on the data layer. Snowflake's monthly AI Pulse shipped MCP connectors for Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Jira, Salesforce and Slack inside Cortex Agents; a Cortex Code SDK with AWS Glue, Databricks and Postgres connectors plus native VS Code and Claude Code integration; Snowflake Intelligence with natural-language Skills; a Python and TypeScript Agent SDK; and Cortex AI Guardrails. It is the most Anthropic-friendly statement any data cloud has made in 2026, and it stakes Snowflake's claim to be the agentic control plane while Google argues for the operating system underneath. Workday's same-day Illuminate launch — a formal pivot from system of record to platform of agents, in Josh Bersin's long-form framing — sets up the answer to ServiceNow Otto in three weeks. The day's quietest read, Andreas Pahlsson-Notini's "Less human AI agents, please," names the failure mode hanging over all of it: agents drift to the familiar under awkward tasks and start negotiating with reality under hard constraints, which is an anthropomorphic problem, not just a capability one.

## Research highlights

- **Agent-World: Scaling Real-World Environment Synthesis for Evolving General Agent Intelligence** — anonymous (cs.AI). Synthesizes diverse real-world-like environments at scale for general-purpose agent training — the "environment is the bottleneck" thesis taken seriously. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18292 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18292)
- **On the Reliability of Computer Use Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI). Reliability-focused evaluation of computer-use agents (Operator, Claude-computer-use class) — moves the conversation from peak capability to failure-mode characterization needed for deployment. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17849 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17849)
- **Symbolic Guardrails for Domain-Specific Agents: Stronger Safety and Security Guarantees** — anonymous (cs.AI). Symbolic (rather than learned) guardrails for domain agents to obtain provable safety/security properties. Pairs with the ARGUS/AgentTrust line as a complementary mechanism. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.15579 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15579)
- **Training LLM Agents for Spontaneous, Reward-Free Self-Evolution via World Knowledge Exploration** — anonymous (cs.AI). Self-evolving agent training without external reward via world-knowledge exploration — companion to GenericAgent and the broader "agents that improve themselves" thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18131 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18131)
- **Terminal Wrench: A Dataset of 331 Reward-Hackable Environments and 3,632 Exploit Trajectories** — anonymous (cs.AI). Adversarial dataset of reward-hackable RL environments plus 3.6K exploit trajectories — critical resource for the reward-hacking subfield. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17596 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17596)
- **EvoMaster: A Foundational Agent Framework for Building Evolving Autonomous Scientific Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI). Framework for evolving autonomous scientific agents — meta-level scaffolding for the AI-scientist thread, complementing Mind DeepResearch and Toward Autonomous Long-Horizon Engineering. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17406 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17406)
- **GenericAgent: A Token-Efficient Self-Evolving LLM Agent via Contextual Information Density** — anonymous (cs.AI). Self-evolving agent that explicitly optimizes for token-efficiency by densifying contextual information — directly relevant to the "MCP tax" / context-overhead thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17091 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17091)
- **SkillFlow: Benchmarking Lifelong Skill Discovery and Evolution for Autonomous Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI). Lifelong-learning benchmark focused on skill discovery + evolution rather than per-task fine-tuning. Sharp eval surface for the agent-skills-as-memory thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.17308 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17308)
