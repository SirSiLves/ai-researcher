## The day in 90 seconds

**Salesforce turns its entire platform into MCP tools as Microsoft and Cloudflare close the agent-runtime gap.**

The enterprise-platform pivot that has been running underneath Q2 broke into the open on Wednesday. Salesforce called Headless 360 the biggest platform change in 25 years and meant it commercially: every Salesforce surface is now an API, an MCP tool, or a CLI command. Microsoft and Cloudflare moved on the runtime layer the same day, and OpenAI's chief revenue officer told staff in writing what the comparable strategy looks like for a frontier lab.

The structural move came from San Francisco. At TrailblazerDX 2026 Salesforce shipped 60+ new MCP tools and 30+ preconfigured coding skills callable from Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf or any MCP-compatible runtime, with DevOps Center MCP pulling the same access into CI/CD and Agentforce Vibes 2.0, Session Tracing and the Experience Layer all going GA. The framing is deliberate. Where SAP spent April banning external agents from v4/2026 in its API policy, Salesforce is opening every surface as a tool the rival agents can drive. The Register, VentureBeat and CIO all read it the same way — Salesforce as the open-MCP-everywhere pole of the enterprise stack, with ServiceNow and Workday now stuck between the two positions for the rest of Q2.

Microsoft and Cloudflare answered the parts of the stack Salesforce does not run. The April Foundry release wave pushed MAI-Transcribe-1, MAI-Voice-1 and MAI-Image-2 into public preview — enterprise speech across 25 languages at roughly half the GPU cost of alternatives, 60 seconds of expressive audio in under a second on a single GPU, and a #3 placement on Arena.ai for image families — pricing first-party speech, voice and image alternatives to OpenAI and Anthropic inside Foundry for Azure-first builders. Cloudflare's Agents Week reached day three with Sandboxes GA, nine months after the June 2025 beta. Each agent now gets a persistent isolated Linux environment with shell, filesystem, background processes, live preview URLs and credential injection, plus Durable Object Facets for per-tenant SQLite multi-tenancy and the Dynamic Workers spec underneath it. Read against E2B and Daytona, this is the execution primitive Cloudflare's stack was missing; read against Salesforce, it is the third primitive — model routing, network identity, ephemeral compute — landing through a single vendor.

The third thread is the one customers will feel later. TechCrunch and The Information surfaced an internal memo from OpenAI's chief revenue officer naming the enterprise pivot as a strategy rather than a side effect: business customers now around 40 percent of revenue, up from 20 percent, projected to hit 50 percent by year-end, with the company moving toward multiproduct adoption, an agent platform and full-stack deployment to raise switching costs. That is the textual setup for the OpenAI Deployment Company and the Tomoro joint venture that lands on 11 May. Meta added the open-vs-closed counterweight: Muse Spark, the first proprietary model from Superintelligence Labs, needs less than a tenth of the training compute Llama 4 Maverick required a year earlier — thanks to "thought compression" RL — and ships closed, with open weights deferred. Nathan Lambert's mid-2026 bets piece on Interconnects formalises the open side of the same argument around sovereign funding, local agents, AI-native backends and open post-training as the next differentiator.

Around the edges, KnowRL and the Rethinking On-Policy Distillation paper land as sharp counterpoints to the longer-CoT consensus, the Blind Spot of Agent Safety paper shows benign user instructions exposing tool-using agents through untrusted intermediate content, and Kyle Kingsbury's "meat shields" coinage names the human-as-accountability-shield pattern the governance discourse has been circling for months.

### Frontier-model releases — Meta Muse Spark + Microsoft MAI wave

**Meta's Muse Spark + "scaling ladder" — proprietary first model from SI Labs, 10× less training compute.** Meta's Superintelligence Labs continued the rollout of Muse Spark (originally code-named Avocado) — its first proprietary model since SI Labs formed — and surfaced fresh April commentary on the "scaling ladder" training-efficiency curve. The model needed less than one-tenth the training compute of Llama 4 Maverick a year earlier, achieved via "thought compression" RL that penalises excessive reasoning tokens. Ships **proprietary**; open-source versions hoped-for rather than committed. _Why it matters:_ The cleanest concrete proof point this month that the Density Law (capability doubling every ~88 days) is holding even on the training side. Signals Meta's strategic pivot away from Llama-style open release. [VentureBeat — Muse Spark](https://venturebeat.com/technology/goodbye-llama-meta-launches-new-proprietary-ai-model-muse-spark-first-since) · [36kr — Density Law verified by Meta + METR](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3765157269684738)

**Microsoft Foundry's April release wave — MAI-Transcribe-1, MAI-Voice-1, MAI-Image-2 in public preview.** Microsoft's mid-April Foundry release notes wrapped a wave of in-house MAI model availability. MAI-Transcribe-1 (enterprise STT, 25 languages, ~50% lower GPU cost than alternatives), MAI-Voice-1 (60s of expressive audio in <1s on a single GPU), and MAI-Image-2 (#3 on Arena.ai for image families) all in Foundry public preview. ### Agent infrastructure — Cloudflare Sandboxes GA + DO Facets

**Cloudflare Agents Week day-3: Sandboxes GA, Durable Object Facets, Dynamic Workers.** Cloudflare moved Sandboxes to GA — persistent, isolated Linux environments with shell, filesystem, background processes, live preview URLs and credential injection — nine months after the June 2025 beta. ## Research highlights

- **KnowRL: Boosting LLM Reasoning via RL with Minimal-Sufficient Knowledge** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Argues current RL post-training over-rewards verbose chains; KnowRL grounds reward at the minimal-sufficient knowledge unit needed to derive the answer. Sharp counterpoint to "longer CoT = better" — relevant to the test-time-compute thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12627 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12627)
- **Rethinking On-Policy Distillation of LLMs: Phenomenology, Mechanism, and Practice** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Systematic study of when on-policy distillation actually helps vs when it's noise — phenomenology + mechanism + practical guidance. Important for teams deploying student models from frontier teachers. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13016 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13016)
- **Toward Autonomous Long-Horizon Engineering for ML Research** — anonymous cs.AI submission. End-to-end autonomous ML research agent targeting genuinely long-horizon engineering rather than narrow benchmark wins — the "AI scientist" thread continues to mature. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13018 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13018)
- **The Blind Spot of Agent Safety: How Benign User Instructions Expose Critical Vulnerabilities** — anonymous cs.AI submission. Even completely benign user instructions can expose tool-using agents to attacks via untrusted intermediate content. Structural critique of safety frameworks that assume adversarial intent lives at the user-prompt boundary. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.10577 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10577)
- **Many-Tier Instruction Hierarchy in LLM Agents** — anonymous cs.AI submission. Generalises the system/developer/user instruction hierarchy to many tiers — directly relevant to MCP/A2A stacks where instructions arrive from heterogeneous principals with different trust levels. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09443 — cs.AI. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09443)
- **CONSCIENTIA: Can LLM Agents Learn to Strategize? Emergent Deception and Trust in a Multi-Agent Setting** — anonymous cs.MA submission. Tracks emergence of strategic deception and trust dynamics in multi-agent LLM populations — behavioral-eval probe complementary to formal protocol work. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09746 — cs.MA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09746)
- **LASA: Language-Agnostic Semantic Alignment at the Semantic Bottleneck for LLM Safety** — anonymous cs.CL submission. Aligns safety at a language-agnostic semantic bottleneck rather than per-language post-hoc filters — addresses the long-tail of multilingual jailbreaks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12710 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12710)
- **You Only Judge Once: Multi-response Reward Modeling in a Single Forward Pass** — anonymous cs.LG submission. Reward model that scores multiple candidate responses in one forward pass instead of independent passes — substantial throughput win for RM-heavy pipelines (RLHF, best-of-N, judge-as-eval). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.10966 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10966)
