## The day in 90 seconds

**Anthropic's source code leaks, its revenue passes OpenAI, and an agent writes a kernel exploit on first try.**

Q2 opens on the worst kind of triple for Anthropic and the most consequential one for everyone else. The full Claude Code agent harness is in the wild after a packaging error, the company's annualised revenue has reportedly overtaken OpenAI for the first time, and a single Claude instance with no human in the loop has written two working FreeBSD kernel exploits in roughly four hours. The three stories share a lab and a date and very little else.

The leak landed first. A missing `.npmignore` line in `@anthropic-ai/claude-code` v2.1.88 shipped a 59.8 megabyte source map containing about 513,000 lines of unobfuscated TypeScript across some 1,900 files — effectively the entire client-side harness. Researchers immediately surfaced previously unknown subsystems, including a Tamagotchi-style "BUDDY" companion and a user-frustration-detection layer, and a clean-room rewrite hit 50,000 GitHub stars in under two hours. Anthropic filed a DMCA notice that GitHub then executed against roughly 8,100 repositories, including legitimate forks of Anthropic's own public claude-code project. Within hours Boris Cherny, head of Claude Code, apologised publicly and narrowed the action to a single repo and 96 forks. It is the second packaging incident at Anthropic inside a week, and the cleanest public look anyone has ever had at how a frontier lab actually builds an agent.

The financial story took longer to surface but is the more durable one. SaaStr and Epoch AI reported Anthropic's revenue run-rate crossing $30 billion annualised — up from $14 billion in February and $19 billion in March — with the number of enterprise customers spending more than $1 million a year doubling from around 500 to over 1,000 in under two months. That puts Anthropic ahead of OpenAI's roughly $25 billion ARR on the headline number, the same metric that underwrites OpenAI's $852 billion valuation. OpenAI disputes the gross-revenue accounting by about $8 billion, and the dispute is unresolved, but the growth-curve gap is the part that does not bend with accounting choices.

Nicholas Carlini's MAD Bugs kickoff write-up gave the day its empirical artefact. Given only the FreeBSD kgssapi.ko source and a single prompt, Claude autonomously developed two working remote-root exploits for CVE-2026-4747 — a stack buffer overflow in the RPCSEC_GSS authentication module — each succeeding on the first attempt after about four hours of compute. The same pipeline goes on to produce more than 500 validated high-severity vulnerabilities through April. Read against the day's other news, it is the smoking gun the harness-leak headlines were missing: the capability is not theoretical, it is on a laptop somewhere, and the harness that runs it is now public domain in everything but copyright.

Underneath those three threads, the enterprise platform fight had its first formal split. SAP quietly published API Policy v4/2026, with Section 2.2.2 prohibiting AI systems that "independently schedule or execute calls" against SAP APIs without a human in the loop, forcing external agents to route through SAP's own Joule assistant. Hours later, Salesforce reframed Slackbot as an agentic OS with MCP-connected tools and ServiceNow began rolling out its "Australia" release with 30 new cross-SaaS integrations under AI Control Tower. MCP Dev Summit North America opened in New York with 1,200 attendees, the first developer summit under the new Linux Foundation governance. And Meta-Harness, a paper that treats the agent harness itself as a searchable, outer-loop-optimised object and reports wins on TerminalBench-2, landed within 24 hours of the leak — the field's response to a leaked reference implementation in less than a day.

**Read this first:** [Anthropic accidentally publishes the full Claude Code source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-01/anthropic-scrambles-to-address-leak-of-claude-code-source-code) — the move that reframes every other Anthropic story below.

## Research highlights

- **Meta-Harness: End-to-End Optimization of Model Harnesses** — anonymous (cs.CL/cs.AI). Treats the agent harness (context manager, retriever, proposer, scheduler) as a searchable object; outer-loop optimiser rewrites harness code from execution traces. Wins on TerminalBench-2 and retrieval-augmented math; concrete instantiation of "harness as a first-class artifact." _Category:_ arXiv:2603.28052 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28052)
- **MonitorBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Chain-of-Thought Monitorability** — anonymous (cs.CL). Measures whether CoTs faithfully reveal the decision-critical factors a model used; monitorability drops sharply when structural reasoning isn't required and degrades further under stress test. Both open- and closed-weight models fail. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.28590 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590)
- **FIPO: Eliciting Deep Reasoning with Future-KL Influenced Policy Optimization** — anonymous (cs.LG). RL post-training method using discounted future-KL divergence to fix credit-assignment over long reasoning chains; dense advantage pulls reward signal closer to the steps it actually grades. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.19835 — cs.LG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19835)
- **Think Anywhere in Code Generation** — anonymous (cs.CL). Code-generation model invokes "thinking" on-demand at high-entropy positions during generation rather than as a single up-front block. Cold-start SFT + outcome RL teaches when to think. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.29957 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29957)
- **daVinci-LLM: Towards the Science of Pretraining** — anonymous (cs.CL). Systematic pretraining study with an L0–L9 "Data Darwinism" taxonomy of data-processing depth and adaptive curriculum; controlled ablations on token scaling and compositional balance. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.27164 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27164)
- **Falcon Perception** — TII Falcon team (cs.CL/cs.CV). Dense Transformer that processes image patches and text tokens in shared parameter space from layer 1, using hybrid bidirectional/causal attention. Companion 300M Falcon OCR hits 80.3% olmOCR / 88.64 OmniDocBench. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.27365 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27365)
- **FlowPIE: Test-Time Scientific Idea Evolution with Flow-Guided Literature Exploration** — anonymous (cs.IR/cs.CL). Retrieval+generation pipeline for scientific ideation combining GFlowNet-style flow-guided MCTS with a generative reward model; "isolation island" selection/crossover/mutation over literature-grounded candidates. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.29557 — cs.IR. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29557)
- **All Roads Lead to Rome: Incentivizing Divergent Thinking in Vision-Language Models** — anonymous (cs.CL/cs.LG). Identifies GRPO-style RL collapse into a narrow reasoning mode for VLMs; proposes Multi-Group Policy Optimization to maintain solution diversity. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.00479 — cs.CL. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00479)
