Worth watching next: the EU AI Act's high-risk obligations bind from August 2, now under two months out, even as US state law keeps fragmenting.

## Research highlights

ArXiv RSS was empty (Sunday — arXiv skips weekends), so today's pool came from HF Papers (44 listed) cross-checked against papers.cool. The signal skews to agent safety, evaluation, and tool-use infrastructure.

- **[The Cold-Start Safety Gap in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07867)** — Sun, Liu, Weng. Tool-calling agents are most vulnerable at the very start of a session and get safer after a few benign tasks; the SODA benchmark quantifies this across up to 20 preceding tasks and 7 models.
- **[MiniMax Sparse Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13392)** — Lai, Xu, Yang, Chen. Blockwise sparse attention over GQA with a lightweight index branch that scores/selects KV blocks — aimed at the quadratic-cost wall for ultra-long-context agentic and repo-scale workloads.
- **[Risk Under Pressure: Compute-Aware Evaluation of Adversarial Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11409)** — Ehghaghi et al. Argues fixed-budget attack-success-rate hides the true cost of jailbreaks (attack compute varies by orders of magnitude) and proposes a compute-aware robustness metric.
- **[Evoflux: Inference-Time Evolution of Executable Tool Workflows for Compact Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12674)** — Bhandari et al. Closes the MCP-style tool-use gap for small models by evolving executable workflows at inference time — discovering tools from live catalogs, satisfying schemas, grounding in executed evidence.
- **[Getting Better at Working With You: Compiling User Corrections into Runtime Enforcement for Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13174)** — Zhou et al. Shows memory layers (e.g. Mem0) still violate 57.5% of applicable preference checks; TRACE compiles corrections into runtime rules so a fix persists across sessions.
- **[See What I See, Know What I Think: Dense Latent Communication Across Heterogeneous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13594)** — Chen et al. Replaces lossy text passing between multi-agent systems with KV-cache communication across *heterogeneous* models, tackling cross-model latent alignment.
- **[EvoBrowseComp: Benchmarking Search Agents on Evolving Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13120)** — Wang et al. A 400-question evolving benchmark that resists contamination and parametric memorization, separating genuine retrieval from fact recall.
