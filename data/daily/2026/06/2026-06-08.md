## The day in 90 seconds

**Apple puts three rival AI labs on 2.2 billion devices as OpenAI declares chat dead**

Today was about distribution, not capability. The frontier models stopped fighting over benchmarks and started fighting over where they live — the home screen, the spreadsheet, the enterprise action layer. The single thing to take away: the AI platform war just moved from "whose model is smartest" to "whose model is everywhere," and Apple, OpenAI, and the enterprise stack all made that move on the same day.

At Tim Cook's final WWDC keynote, Apple rebuilt Siri on a custom ~1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model licensed from Google for a reported ~$1B a year, and — more consequentially — shipped an Extensions system that lets users pick ChatGPT, Gemini (the default), or Anthropic's Claude to power Apple Intelligence. That makes three frontier vendors first-class providers on roughly 2.2 billion devices in one stroke, with heavy reasoning kept on Apple's Private Cloud Compute rather than Google's infrastructure. It is the largest AI-distribution event of the year, and it turns Gemini into a default at a scale no model has ever reached. Microsoft echoed the theme at Build, finalizing its Foundry catalog at 11,000+ models and dropping a Claude option into Excel Agent Mode — putting a frontier model directly inside the spreadsheet ~750 million people open.

Underneath the distribution land-grab sits a product-shape shift. OpenAI told the FT that "chat is dead" and is undertaking its biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch — a "superapp" bundling coding tools, agents, and partner apps like Canva and Booking.com, rolling out in the coming weeks ahead of an expected IPO. The pivot from answering questions to completing tasks is now explicit, and it reframes the consumer chatbot as a business-client funnel. The enterprise plumbing moved in lockstep: ServiceNow shipped a generally available MCP Server through its new Action Fabric, hard evidence that the Model Context Protocol is graduating from spec to enterprise default, while SAP doubled n8n's valuation to $5.2B to buy its way into citizen-developer orchestration.

What to watch: the regulatory clock is now explicit and short. EU AI Act high-risk and GPAI obligations bite in 55 days (Aug 2), Colorado's AI Act takes effect June 30, and OpenAI's new ChatGPT Lockdown Mode is the first mainstream consumer hardening primitive against prompt-injection exfiltration — a control enterprises will start demanding as agents gain tool and web access.

## Research highlights

- **[Think Fast: Estimating No-CoT Task-Completion Time Horizons of Frontier AI Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07157)** — Gould, Ward, Woodruff, Arike et al. Measures how far frontier models reason internally without explicit chain-of-thought across 30,000+ questions / 43 benchmarks — bears directly on whether CoT monitoring remains a viable safety oversight mechanism.
- **[Attack Selection in Agentic AI Control Evaluations Meaningfully Decreases Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06529)** — Ge-Wang et al. Red-teamers who strategically choose *when* to attack defeat trusted-monitor AI-control protocols far more than indiscriminate attackers — current control evals overstate safety.
- **[OpenSkill: Open-World Self-Evolution for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06741)** — Yan, Song, Zhang, Liang et al. Agent framework that builds both its skills *and* its own verification signals from scratch given only a task prompt — no curated skills, trajectories, or verifier signals required.
- **[Declarative Skills for AI Agents in Knowledge-Grounded Tool-Use Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06923)** — Lim et al. Empirical case that natural-language "skill files" appended to the system prompt beat imperative state machines for orchestrating tool-using agents over unstructured KBs — direct support for the skills/declarative-agent paradigm.
- **[HKVM-RAG: Key-Value-Separated Hypergraph Evidence Organization for Multi-Hop RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07218)** — Zhang, Ma. Organizes retrieved text into answer-path hyperedges rather than independently-scored passages, exposing multi-hop evidence chains under fixed retrieval budgets.
- **[Towards Retrieving Interaction Spaces for Agentic Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06880)** — Zhuang et al. Direct-corpus-interaction search (grep/file-read over raw corpora) doesn't scale; proposes retrieving bounded "interaction spaces" to keep agentic-search latency manageable as corpora grow.
- **[AdMem: Advanced Memory for Task-solving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06787)** — Wang et al. Unified, automatic memory framework integrating semantic + procedural memory that handles failure cases and online scalability — addresses the "replay past successes only" limit of prior agent memory.
- **[Act As a Real Researcher: Benchmarks Evaluating Frontier LLMs and Agentic Harnesses in the Research Lifecycle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07462)** — Wang et al. Probes where autonomous research agents still fail — field sensitivity, research ethics, nuanced scientific judgment — beyond the coding tasks they already do well.
