## Research highlights

- **[Recursive Self-Evolving Agents via Held-Out Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28374)** — Apples-to-apples re-evaluation of weight-free self-improving agents (reflections, playbooks, optimized prompts), introducing held-out selection to avoid single-benchmark overfitting.
- **[Agentic Abstention: Do Agents Know When to Stop Instead of Act?](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.28733)** — Studies whether agents recognize when to abstain rather than take a risky action — a core reliability/safety gap for autonomous tool-use.
- **[OSWorld2.0: Benchmarking Computer-Use Agents on Long-Horizon Tasks](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.29537)** — Refreshed computer-use benchmark stressing long-horizon, realistic desktop workflows.
- **[Scaling the Horizon, Not the Parameters](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.30616)** — Argues extending the agentic reasoning horizon, rather than scaling parameters, can match trillion-parameter performance with a 35B agent.
- **[PixelRAG: Web Screenshots Beat Text for RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28344)** — Retrieves and reads websites in native visual form, preserving layout that HTML-linearizing pipelines discard. (Zaharia/Gonzalez/Min et al.)
- **[A Systems-Level Analysis of Sensitivity, Robustness and Stability in RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28337)** — Controlled 56-run study isolating where RAG actually fails (chunking, top-k, reranking, generation) rather than scoring only final answers.
- **[Always-On Agents: A Survey of Persistent Memory, State and Governance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30306)** — Frames always-on agents as persistent-state systems (memories, ledgers, permissions, audit records) and the governance primitives they demand.
- **[When Latent Agents Lie: KV-Cache Integrity in Multi-Agent Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28958)** — Identifies a hidden attack surface where agents pass tampered KV-cache state between models behind a benign visible message.
