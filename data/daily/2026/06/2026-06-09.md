**Anthropic ships its first public Mythos-class model as Google and Microsoft race to the floor on price**

The frontier moved and the bottom dropped out on the same day. Anthropic made its most capable model generally available, Google undercut everyone on a fast frontier tier, and Microsoft quietly declared partial independence from OpenAI. The story isn't a single launch — it's three of the largest AI vendors hitting "capability up, price down" at once, while the enterprise platforms scramble to wire it all into live business data. Yesterday's arc was about distribution; today's is about raw capability and the unit cost of using it.

The headline belongs to Anthropic. ## Research highlights

**[A Unified Framework for the Evaluation of LLM Agentic Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27898)** — Config-driven harness where benchmark selection, environment instantiation, tool loading, model serving, and eval settings are all specified via config files — aims to standardize fragmented agent evals.

**[Self-Aware Vector Embeddings for RAG: A Neuroscience-Inspired Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20598)** — "SmartVector" augments dense embeddings with temporal awareness, confidence decay, and relational awareness via a hippocampal–neocortical lifecycle; roughly doubles top-1 accuracy over plain cosine RAG (62.0% vs 31.0%) and cuts the stale-answer rate from 35.0% to 13.3%.

**[Structure Guided Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Factual Queries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22843)** — SG-RAG reframes retrieval as embedding-based subgraph matching and uses retrieved topological structure to guide generation, reducing semantic-noise failures on complex factual queries.

**[The Hierarchy of Agentic Capabilities: Evaluating Frontier Models on Realistic RL Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09032)** — Scores frontier models across 150 workplace tasks in a realistic e-commerce RL environment, proposing a tiered capability hierarchy rather than a single score.

**[MCP Tool Descriptions Are Smelly!](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14878v2)** — Audits Model Context Protocol tool descriptions for "smells," then shows that augmenting/standardizing them improves agent tool-selection efficiency — practical signal for MCP server authors.

**[Defending LLMs Against Jailbreak Attacks via In-Decoding Safety-Awareness Probing](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10543v1)** — "SafeProbing" probes latent safety signals during decoding (rather than at input filtering) to detect and block jailbreak trajectories mid-generation.

**[Rethinking Reinforcement Fine-tuning of LLMs: A Multi-armed Bandit Learning Perspective](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.14599)** — Recasts RL fine-tuning through a multi-armed-bandit lens to better balance exploration/exploitation during reasoning-oriented training.

**[ClawWorm: Self-Propagating Attacks Across LLM Agent Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15727)** — Demonstrates a self-propagating worm spreading across interconnected LLM-agent ecosystems — a concrete agent-security risk as multi-agent deployments grow.
