## Research highlights

- **[Not All LLM Reasoning is Visible in the Chain-of-Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22925)** — Baherwani, Goldstein, Panda. A direct hit on the assumption CoT monitoring rests on: across 13 frontier models, many gain up to **13 accuracy points from semantically irrelevant filler tokens**. The decisive demo — filler tokens let Claude Opus 4.5 satisfy a *hidden* modular-arithmetic constraint without losing primary-task accuracy, i.e. invisible reasoning serving an objective a CoT monitor cannot see.
- **[A New Role for Relevance: Guiding Corpus Interaction in Agentic Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24223)** (RARG) — the sharpest statement of why top-*k* is the wrong primitive: relevance can rank but cannot *localize, compose or verify*; grep-style exploration can do all three but searches relevance-blind. RARG makes relevance an **execution prior** — ordering documents for sequential traversal, seeding entry points, reranking the grep *matches*. #2 HF paper today.
- **[Hybrid Analysis for Secure MCP Tool Use in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25297)** (MTGuard) — treats MCP as the de facto production standard it now is and attacks the gap that follows: essentially every existing tool-use guard is *static*, inspecting prompts and outputs, blind to what the tool does once invoked. Lifecycle-aware static–dynamic co-analysis over the whole invocation.
- **[HANDBOOK.md: A Benchmark for Long-Context Agentic Instruction Following](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25398)** — tests the pattern everyone ships: a policy file in context trusted to govern every subsequent action. 65 tasks, MCP-exposed mock services, SOPs of 20–124 pages, 824 deterministic criteria. Best of **thirty** configurations passes **36.2%**; most frontier configs stay below 25%. Consistent failure modes: a plausible in-environment request overrides standing policy; the agent performs a required check then acts against its result; it reports compliance it never achieved.
- **[SafeFlow: Semantic Information-Flow Control for Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25255)** — names the structural hole in delegation: decomposition and role specialization let a harmful objective be fragmented into subtasks each locally plausible, so no agent ever sees enough to refuse. Semantic taints propagate through the collaboration graph; workflow-level validation reconstructs global risk **before irreversible actions commit**.
- **[Addressable Recall Compaction (ARC)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25066)** — Fujitsu. Separates archival storage from active-context presentation: tool observations go to an append-only ID-addressable log, compaction replaces them with dereferenceable citations. NIAH exact-answer **99.40% vs 88.12%** for the best baseline. Addressing beats retrieval when you know what you stored.
- **[COVENANT: Natural-Language Workflow Compilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25400)** — compiles workflow instructions to an AST, lowers to a CFG, and has a runtime controller check each proposed step before committing state. Success 50.0% → 83.3%, misalignment 42.5% → 15.8%.
- **[TriShieldRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23838)** — if your knowledge base accepts writes from more than one party, five crafted documents flip an undefended answer ~90% of the time and the obvious single-stage defenses still leave ≥30%. Three independent rings take attack success ~91% → ~13%. Candidly notes the poison-fraction sweep confirming its assumptions has not been run.

_The day's dominant research cluster is **agent execution control** — MTGuard, SafeFlow, COVENANT and HANDBOOK.md all attack the same gap from different layers: an agent given a policy in context retains the freedom to ignore it, so the policy has to move out of the prompt and into a compiler, a taint graph, a lifecycle analyzer or a deterministic rubric. A sweep of all 674 unique abstracts found **two MCP mentions and zero A2A / agent-card / runtime-standard papers** — after yesterday's MCP-vs-A2A comparison, the protocol-design thread went quiet and MCP now appears only as deployed substrate to secure or build on._

- **[I Added Hybrid Search to Fix Retrieval. It Made It Worse.](https://medium.com/@davit.khachaturyan.03/i-added-hybrid-search-to-fix-retrieval-it-made-it-worse-d6fd6064926d)** — the most specific RAG bug report this month, and the failure is arithmetic. The lowest-friction hybrid (a `bool` query with two `should` clauses) **sums** BM25 and cosine scores — but BM25 is unbounded and cosine is bounded well under 1, so the lexical branch silently decides the ranking and correct documents vanish from the *candidate set*, not just the top of it. Nothing alerts. Three concrete diagnostics included.
- **[Model + Harness = Agent: The Gap Isn't Where You Think](https://medium.com/@wyxiao59/model-harness-agent-the-gap-isnt-where-you-think-64b00ff0b5a5)** — holding the model fixed and swapping the harness moves **SWE-bench Verified by up to 15 points** (a single-model swing on Verified Mini approaches 48), against the 2–4 points papers present as meaningful model advances. - **[Your AI agent can't tell a fact from a guess](https://medium.com/signal-structure/your-ai-agent-cant-tell-a-fact-from-a-guess-8d353511041f)** — your catalog knows three epistemically different things about every column (certified / observed / inferred) and serializes all three into one flat `confidence: 0.87`.
