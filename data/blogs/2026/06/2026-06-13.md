**Open and closed models are on different exponentials** — Nathan Lambert (interconnects.ai). _Takeaway:_ Makes the case that open and closed models are improving along distinct curves, and that the value of marginally higher intelligence — not raw benchmarks — is what determines where each wins. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different)

**LLM Research Papers: The 2026 List (January to May)** — Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI). _Takeaway:_ Curated, opinionated roundup of the most important LLM papers from the first five months of 2026 — a high-signal reading list spanning architectures, inference scaling, and training methods. **Recent Developments in LLM Architectures: KV Sharing, mHC, and Compressed Attention** — Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI). _Takeaway:_ Technical deep dive on long-context efficiency mechanisms (KV sharing, multi-head compression, compressed attention) shaping the newest architectures — the engineering behind cheaper long contexts. [https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/recent-developments-in-llm-architectures](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/recent-developments-in-llm-architectures)

**State of the software engineering job market in 2026 (Part 2)** — Gergely Orosz (The Pragmatic Engineer). _Takeaway:_ Data-driven read on how AI labs have overtaken Big Tech as the most attractive employers, plus where AI is reshaping demand for native mobile and frontend roles.
