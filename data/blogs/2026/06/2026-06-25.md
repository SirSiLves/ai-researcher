# AI Blogs — 2026-06-25

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Welcome to the AGI era of AI governance** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues the U.S. executive branch forcing Anthropic to cut access to its latest Claude 5 Mythos/Fable models marks a turn where frontier-model governance is exercised informally and directly by the state, not statute — a structural shift worth watching. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance)

- **State of the blog (mid-2026)** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ A frank post-Ai2 reflection laying out his three goals — clarity on frontier-model evolution, a diverse open-model ecosystem, and institutions to sustain both — useful as a map of where independent open-model commentary is heading. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/state-of-the-blog-mid-2026](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/state-of-the-blog-mid-2026)

- **simonw/browser-compat-db** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ A hands-on log of converting MDN's browser-compat-data into a 66MB SQLite DB and CDN-serving it from a GitHub orphan branch with open CORS — a neat pattern (driven by Claude Code + Codex) for shipping queryable data, riffing off Mozilla's new MDN MCP service. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/24/browser-compat-db/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/24/browser-compat-db/)

- **Porting the Moebius 0.2B image inpainting model to run in the browser with Claude Code** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Walks through getting a small inpainting model running client-side via WebGPU with a working demo — a concrete example of agent-assisted model porting to the browser. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/22/porting-moebius/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/22/porting-moebius/)

- **How to Work and Compound with AI** — Eugene Yan. _Takeaway:_ Practical eval-driven agent workflow: isolated containers per eval suite, logging metrics + transcripts, and subagents that read transcripts to confirm runs were valid — plus reusable skills like a `/polish` loop that iterates until evals pass. [https://eugeneyan.com/writing/working-with-ai/](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/working-with-ai/)

- **LLM Research Papers: The 2026 List (January to May)** — Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI). _Takeaway:_ A curated, opinionated roundup of the year's notable LLM papers so far — a high-signal index for catching up on architecture and training-method shifts (RLVR/GRPO, inference-time scaling) without drowning in arXiv. [https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/archive](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/archive)

- **AI Companies Can't Regulate Themselves. They Should Regulate Each Other.** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Proposes federally supervised self-regulatory organizations (FINRA-style) for frontier labs, noting the existing Frontier Model Forum lacks statutory authority and mandatory membership — a concrete institutional design for the governance gap. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-companies-can-t-regulate-themselves-they-should-regulate-each-other](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-companies-can-t-regulate-themselves-they-should-regulate-each-other)

- **AI Governance by Phone Call** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Dissects the White House's move to a voluntary pre-release review process for frontier models negotiated in the Oval Office — a case study in informal, relationship-driven AI policy and its accountability risks. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-governance-by-phone-call](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-governance-by-phone-call)

## Medium roundup

### rag
- **[Modern RAG in 2026: The Components That Actually Matter](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/modern-rag-in-2026-the-components-that-actually-matter-3f6a138ef117)** — Anubhav (Data Science Collective). Argues most RAG systems fail on retrieval quality, not the LLM, and breaks down the components that actually move the needle.
- **[RAG is DEAD! (and why that's the best news you'll hear all year)](https://medium.com/@reliabledataengineering/rag-is-dead-and-why-thats-the-best-news-you-ll-hear-all-year-0f3de8c44604)** — Reliable Data Engineering. Provocation that naive 2023-style chunk-and-embed RAG is dead while retrieval-augmented generation as a concept is more central than ever; contextual memory becomes table stakes.

### ai-agents
- **[The Agentic AI Engineer Roadmap for 2026: Skills, Stack, and Order](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/the-agentic-ai-engineer-roadmap-for-2026-skills-stack-and-order-fc1dfa17948d)** — Anubhav (Data Science Collective). Frames the agentic engineer's job as building systems where the model decides next steps, calls tools, holds state, and recovers from failure — with an opinionated learning order.

### model-context-protocol
- **[MCP 2026: The Biggest Protocol Revision Since Launch ("MCP 2.0")](https://medium.com/@vishnubhargavsitra/mcp-2026-the-biggest-protocol-revision-since-launch-a-k-a-mcp-2-0-3ebff4e91167)** — Vishnu Bhargav. Walks the 2026-07-28 release candidate: stateless core, first-class extensions, better authorization, formal deprecation rules, and full JSON Schema for tools.
- **[6 Critical Challenges Facing the MCP in 2026](https://medium.com/@MattLeads/6-critical-challenges-facing-the-mcp-in-2026-06258e914402)** — Matt Mochalkin. Counterweight to the hype: tool definitions can eat 10%+ of a context window, plus security and reliability concerns that make MCP structurally fragile at scale.

### ai-governance
- **[Is the EU AI Act Delayed? 2026 Status Check](https://www.modulos.ai/blog/is-the-eu-ai-act-delayed/)** — Modulos. Status read on the AI Act ahead of its 2 Aug 2026 high-risk applicability, including the Digital Omnibus provisional agreement and shifting deadlines.
