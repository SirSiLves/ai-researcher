# AI Blogs — 2026-06-22

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Banning Open Source AI Would Be A Mistake** — Nathan Lambert & Kevin Xu (Interconnects), Jun 19. _Takeaway:_ A general-audience op-ed (rejected by the outlets they pitched, so self-published) staking out the open-weights side of the policy fight. Ties the executive order to review models, the Anthropic foreign-access ban, and rumored government equity stakes into a single argument that regulating or banning open source would chill education, competition, and innovation rather than improve safety. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/banning-open-source-ai-would-be-a](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/banning-open-source-ai-would-be-a)

- **RL Systems Mind the Gap: Matching Trainer and Generator Throughput** — Kimbo Chen, Cheang Kang Wen & Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis), Jun 16. _Takeaway:_ Deep engineering treatment of RL training infra, framing efficiency as a queue-health problem between three actors (generator, RL environment, trainer). Walks GRPO, PipelineRL async updates, policy staleness budgets, sandbox scaling failures, and partial-rollout state staleness, grounded in real multi-node Qwen3-235B / GLM-5 runs. Concrete systems detail, not hype. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/rl-systems-mind-the-gap-matching](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/rl-systems-mind-the-gap-matching)

- **GLM-5.2 is the step change for open agents** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues GLM-5.2 is the moment open-weight models become genuinely usable as agents, not just chat models — a position on where the open vs closed gap actually closes (agentic capability) rather than benchmark snapshots. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-52-is-the-step-change-for-open](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-52-is-the-step-change-for-open)

- **GLM-5.2 is probably the most powerful text-only open weights LLM** — Simon Willison, Jun 17. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on assessment positioning Z.ai's GLM-5.2 at the frontier of open-weight text models, with concrete notes on where it lands versus closed competitors. A useful practitioner counterpart to the Interconnects analysis. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/)

- **sqlite-utils 4.0rc1 adds migrations and nested transactions** — Simon Willison, Jun 21. _Takeaway:_ Release notes documenting a real engineering pattern: schema migrations and nested-transaction support in a widely-used data tool that underpins a lot of LLM data-prep and eval tooling. Hands-on detail on backwards-compatible API evolution. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/21/sqlite-utils-40rc1/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/21/sqlite-utils-40rc1/)

- **LLM Research Papers: The 2026 List (January to May)** — Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI). _Takeaway:_ Curated, opinionated roundup of the year's notable LLM papers across new models, training methods, agents, reasoning, and efficiency — original synthesis rather than recap, and a high-signal map of where research energy is concentrating. [https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/llm-research-papers-2026-part1](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/llm-research-papers-2026-part1)

- **State of the blog, mid-2026** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects), Jun 17. _Takeaway:_ Reflective post on Lambert's career move after AI2 and where independent AI analysis is heading — substantive on the economics and incentives of being an independent voice in a field increasingly captured by lab PR. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/state-of-the-blog-mid-2026](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/state-of-the-blog-mid-2026)

- **AI Governance by Phone Call** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Examines the informal, discretionary mechanism behind the Anthropic model-access crackdown — governance happening via direct executive pressure rather than statute — and what that precedent means for rule-of-law constraints on frontier AI. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-governance-by-phone-call](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-governance-by-phone-call)

- **Import AI 455: Automating AI Research** — Jack Clark (Import AI). _Takeaway:_ Clark's take on AI systems automating parts of the research loop, including Anthropic's reported ~8x jump in merged code versus 2021–2024. A grounded read on recursive self-improvement signals from inside a frontier lab. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/)

## Medium roundup

### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[MCP Is Dead? A Deep Dive Into Why Developers Are Questioning the Model Context Protocol](https://the-agent-report.com/2026/05/mcp-is-dead-developer-critique/)** — The Agent Report. Aggregates a Quandri Engineering critique and a heated HN debate into a substantive look at MCP's real friction points rather than the usual booster framing.

### rag / retrieval-augmented-generation
- **[Beyond Naive RAG: A Step-by-Step Guide to Building Agentic RAG in 2026](https://medium.com/@vkrishnan9074/beyond-naive-rag-a-step-by-step-guide-to-building-agentic-rag-in-2026-fceddd989c74)** — V S Krishnan. Hands-on Python walkthrough of agentic RAG with context caching and adaptive retrieval, treating naive vector RAG as a 2024 relic.
- **[Azure Agentic Retrieval: A Deep Dive into RAG with AI Search and Foundry](https://medium.com/@codebob75/azure-agentic-retrieval-a-deep-dive-into-rag-with-ai-search-and-foundry-1f6318d031a2)** — Bob Code, Apr. Concrete implementation detail on multi-pass agentic retrieval over AI Search, useful as a real architecture reference.

### ai-agents / agentic-ai
- **[Agentic AI in 2026: From Models to Systems and Why That Matters](https://medium.com/@nsr16/agentic-ai-in-2026-661f5b099c2f)** — Nikita S Raj Kapini. Argues the unit of progress has shifted from models to systems, and why most multi-agent setups still fail in production.
- **[7 Agentic AI Trends Redefining the "Year of the Proof" in 2026](https://medium.com/towards-artificial-intelligence/7-agentic-ai-trends-redefining-the-year-of-the-proof-in-2026-83661449ede4)** — Towards AI. Frames 2026 as the year agents must prove ROI, surveying the patterns separating demos from deployments.

### llm / large-language-models
- **[LLM Inference Engineering — An End-to-End Deep Dive](https://medium.com/@vamshire/llm-inference-engineering-an-end-to-end-deep-dive-e39e868272df)** — Vamshi Nagireddy. Walks the full inference stack (batching, KV cache, quantization, speculative decoding) with enough detail to be a real engineering reference.
