# AI Blogs — 2026-06-20

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Welcome to the AGI era of AI governance** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Reads the US executive branch forcing Anthropic to suspend external/internal access to Claude 5 Mythos/Fable (after Amazon tipped off the White House over a narrow Fable jailbreak) as the "starting gun" of a new governance era. Argues an export ban on model weights is a lasting negative for the US, that Anthropic's years of nuclear-weapons framing accelerated this moment, and warns the open-source crowd celebrating it isn't ready for when rapid-response policy turns on open models. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance]

- **Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Primary-source commentary on the Commerce Department directive cutting off Anthropic's top models to foreign nationals/users abroad — a concrete data point on "governance by export letter" with contested technical facts. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/13/us-government-directive-to-suspend-access/]

- **GLM-5.2 is probably the most powerful text-only open weights LLM** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Z.ai's GLM-5.2 (753B MoE, ~40B active, 1M-token context, MIT license) tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index for open weights and ranks #2 on Code Arena WebDev behind only Claude Fable 5 — at ~$1.40/$4.40 per M tokens vs GPT-5.5's $5/$30. Open weights keep closing the gap on closed frontier. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/]

- **Datasette Apps: Host custom HTML applications inside Datasette** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ A hands-on engineering pattern for safely running untrusted LLM-generated HTML/JS: sandboxed iframes + an immutable `<meta>` CSP header, `MessageChannel()` transport for an allow-listed SQL API, and stored queries for writes. Notably, Claude Fable 5 found a real CSP-allow-list privilege-escalation exfiltration bug during a security eval — an ability that "would get it banned by the US government shortly afterwards." [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/18/datasette-apps/]

- **Frontier post-training recipe review (with Finbarr Timbers)** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) is the convergent 2026 frontier pattern — train domain-specialist teachers, then distill them on-policy into one student. Traces it across MiMo Flash v2, GLM-5's staged-by-capability RL, and Nemotron 3 Ultra's multi-round MOPD over 10+ teachers. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/frontier-post-training-recipe-review]

- **What the Defense Production Act Can and Can't Do to Anthropic** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Legal analysis of the statutory levers behind the Anthropic suspension, set against a Pentagon blacklisting and a "standardless export letter" — sharp framing of the US drift toward AI "governance by contract" between government and individual vendors rather than statute. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/what-the-defense-production-act-can-and-can't-do-to-anthropic]

- **AI Governance by Phone Call** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Critiques ad-hoc, bilateral, phone-call-style enforcement of frontier-AI rules as a structurally unstable substitute for legislation — directly relevant to the unfolding Anthropic/White House standoff. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-governance-by-phone-call]

- **The Inference Shift** — Ben Thompson (Stratechery). _Takeaway:_ Distinguishes "answer inference" (optimize for token speed) from "agentic inference" (optimize for memory: context, state, KV cache, SSDs, DBs). Predicts agentic inference becomes the largest market and gradually unbundles the GPU in favor of cheaper, higher-capacity memory hierarchies with "good enough" compute. [https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/]
