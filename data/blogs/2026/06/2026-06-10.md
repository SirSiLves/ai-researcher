## Long-form picks

- **AI enthusiasts are in a race against time, AI skeptics are in a race against entropy** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Reframes the AI debate as two camps running opposite races — boosters betting capability outpaces the clock, skeptics betting hype decays — and argues both can be partly right at once. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/4/ai-enthusiasts-ai-skeptics/]

- **WWDC 2026: Apple's Gemini-derived Siri** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on read of Apple's new Siri running a custom Gemini-derived model on Private Cloud Compute — a notable concession that Apple is leaning on a third-party frontier model rather than its own. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/8/wwdc/]

- **Microsoft's new MAI models** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Breaks down Microsoft's first in-house frontier text models, MAI-Thinking-1 (1T params / 35B active reasoning) and MAI-Code-1-Flash (137B / 5B active), signalling Microsoft's push to reduce OpenAI dependence. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/]

- **Import AI 459: AI oversight is difficult; scaling laws for protein folding; pricing AI extinction risk** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Argues most of AI's economic impact now comes from inference rather than the datacenter buildout, and that per-unit price falls mask the true scale of quality-adjusted output growth. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/06/01/import-ai-459-ai-oversight-is-difficult-scaling-laws-for-protein-folding-models-and-pricing-the-extinction-risk-of-ai-systems/]

- **My bets on open models, mid-2026** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Lays out concrete predictions on where open weights can and cannot keep up with closed labs, arguing coding agents are the first market willing to pay a real premium for frontier intelligence — a gap open models will struggle to close. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/my-bets-on-open-models-mid-2026]

- **How China's Unitree Will Dominate Global Robotics** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Data-heavy supply-chain analysis of Unitree's G1 humanoid ramp (nearing its 10,000th unit) and why Chinese vertical integration positions it to undercut Western robotics on cost. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/chinas-unitree-will-dominate-global]

- **An Interview with Benedict Evans About AI and Software** — Ben Thompson (Stratechery). _Takeaway:_ Wide-ranging conversation on whether AI dissolves the traditional software business model, with Evans pushing back on the most aggressive "software is dead" framings. [https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-benedict-evans-about-ai-and-software/]

- **How the Executive Branch Is Reshaping AI Federalism** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Analyzes the June 2 executive order and the March follow-up framework, arguing the administration is quietly centralizing AI regulatory authority and pre-empting state-level rules. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/how-the-executive-branch-is-reshaping-ai-federalism]
