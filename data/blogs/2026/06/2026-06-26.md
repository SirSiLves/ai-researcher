## Long-form picks

- **Scaling Laws, Carefully** — Lilian Weng (Lil'Log). _Takeaway:_ A 24-min deep dive reconciling Kaplan vs. Chinchilla and walking through data-constrained scaling, including the new Lovelace et al. (2026) overfitting-penalty term keyed on capacity ratio N/U_D. The practical thrust: scaling-law fits are extrapolated from cheap small runs, so rounding, loss-averaging, and fit-region choices can swing predictions wildly — fit them carefully. [https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-06-24-scaling-laws/]

- **GLM-5.2 is the step change for open agents** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues Z.ai's MIT-licensed GLM-5.2 (June 16) is the first open-weight model that "feels right" inside coding harnesses as a general agent — a DeepSeek-R1-scale moment landing ~204 days behind Claude Opus 4.5. Frames it as direct pricing pressure on Anthropic's Claude Code revenue, and notes it arriving while Claude Fable/Mythos sit banned makes it an economic dagger for the US frontier labs. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-52-is-the-step-change-for-open]

- **AI and Liability** — Simon Willison (link post citing Bruce Schneier). _Takeaway:_ Highlights the landmark German ruling holding Google liable for false answers in its AI Overviews, with Schneier's framing that AI agents are legal agents of whoever deploys them — letting firms "hide behind faulty AI" would be a massive handout and a perverse incentive to replace accountable humans. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/25/ai-and-liability/]

- **GLM-5.2 is probably the most powerful text-only open weights LLM** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on read on Z.ai's GLM-5.2 rollout — coding-plan members got it June 13, full MIT-licensed open weights dropped June 16 — corroborating the community benchmark groundswell from an independent practitioner. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/]

- **Red-Teaming after Mythos — Zico Kolter & Matt Fredrikson, Gray Swan** (Latent Space podcast). _Takeaway:_ OpenAI board member Kolter and Gray Swan's Fredrikson make the case that AI security is not just "cybersecurity with AI" — a substantive position piece on red-teaming frontier models in the wake of the Mythos/Fable safety episode. [https://www.latent.space/p/gray-swan]
