# AI Blogs — 2026-06-04

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Open and closed models are on different exponentials** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues the open-vs-closed balance of power is fundamentally an economic question — whether users keep paying large margins for top closed models. Coding agents are the first market willing to pay a premium for better intelligence, but claims that recursive self-improvement gives closed labs an unassailable lead are overblown. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different)

- **Microsoft's new MAI models** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on read of Microsoft's two new in-house models: MAI-Thinking-1 (reasoning, 1T params / 35B active, early-partner access) and MAI-Code-1-Flash (137B / 5B active, purpose-built for GitHub Copilot) — a clear signal Microsoft is de-risking its OpenAI dependence with its own frontier-ish stack. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/)

- **Uber Caps Usage of AI Tools Like Claude Code to Manage Costs** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notes Uber rate-limiting internal coding-agent usage to control spend — an early, concrete data point that agentic coding costs are now a real line item enterprises actively meter, not an unlimited perk. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/3/uber-caps-usage/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/3/uber-caps-usage/)

- **Satya Nadella: No Priors × Latent Space Crossover at Microsoft Build** — swyx / Latent Space. _Takeaway:_ Nadella's first Latent Space appearance, recorded live at Build — substantive on Microsoft's agent platform strategy, the MAI model push, and how Microsoft frames the build-vs-buy line against OpenAI. [https://www.latent.space/p/satya-2026](https://www.latent.space/p/satya-2026)

- **To Boldly Go: The Case for Space Datacenters** — SemiAnalysis (Dylan Patel et al.). _Takeaway:_ Detailed cost teardown of orbital compute — finds space deployment runs ~4× the cost of a comparable terrestrial B300 datacenter today, puncturing the hype while mapping where the economics could eventually bend. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/to-boldly-go-the-case-for-space-datacenters](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/to-boldly-go-the-case-for-space-datacenters)

- **White House Releases Executive Order on AI** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Analysis of the June 2 EO "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," reading it against the December 2025 order and March follow-up framework to trace how the administration is reshaping AI federalism and regulatory authority. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/white-house-releases-executive-order-on-ai](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/white-house-releases-executive-order-on-ai)

- **An LLM-as-Judge Won't Save The Product — Fixing Your Process Will** — Eugene Yan. _Takeaway:_ Makes the contrarian case that evals are a practice, not an artifact: build balanced ~50:50 pass/fail datasets spanning the input distribution and treat eval-driven development as the scientific method, rather than bolting on an LLM judge and hoping. [https://eugeneyan.com/writing/eval-process/](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/eval-process/)
