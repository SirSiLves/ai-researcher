## Long-form picks

- **I think Anthropic and OpenAI have found product-market fit** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues coding agents (Claude Code) hit a new inflection in April 2026 — cites Uber maxing its full-year AI budget months in — as concrete evidence the labs have moved past hype to durable demand. [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/27/product-market-fit/)
- **The last six months in LLMs in five minutes** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Annotated PyCon US lightning-talk slides compressing six months of model/tooling shifts; a fast, opinionated map of what actually changed vs. what was noise. [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/19/5-minute-llms/)
- **My bets on open models, mid-2026** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Predicts closed models pull further ahead in 2026 in directions open models can't easily follow, while Qwen/DeepSeek dominate open-weight adoption — a substantive position in the open-vs-closed debate. [link](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/my-bets-on-open-models-mid-2026)
- **Some ideas for what comes next, May 2026** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Roundup of recent open releases (Gemma 4, DeepSeek V4, Kimi K2.6) framed around which form factors — deep research, CLI agents, search tools — stick as daily-use products. [link](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/some-ideas-for-what-comes-next-may)
- **A Dream of Spring for Open-Weight LLMs: 10 Architectures from Jan–Feb 2026** — Sebastian Raschka. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on architectural comparison of ten recent open-weight models (incl. Qwen3-Coder-Next, 80B/3B-active outperforming larger DeepSeek V3.2 on coding) — detailed, not recap. [link](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/a-dream-of-spring-for-open-weight)
- **The Revenge of the Data Scientist** — Hamel Husain. _Takeaway:_ Walks through five concrete eval pitfalls seen across 50+ companies and argues the data-scientist skill set is exactly what AI-product eval work needs — practical engineering pattern with examples. [link](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/revenge/)
- **Evals Skills for Coding Agents** — Hamel Husain. _Takeaway:_ Ships a reusable set of eval "skills" to guard against common mistakes when evaluating coding agents; distilled from teaching 4,000+ students. [link](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-skills/)
- **How to Work and Compound with AI** — Eugene Yan (Anthropic). _Takeaway:_ Practitioner argument for compounding leverage from AI tooling rather than one-off gains; written from inside a frontier lab's field-and-frontier work. [link](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/working-with-ai/)
- **The Agent Development Lifecycle: Build, Test, Deploy & Monitor** — LangChain. _Takeaway:_ Lays out a build/test/deploy/monitor loop for shipping agents safely and iteratively; ties LangChain 1.0's state-and-messages contract and LangGraph runtime into a concrete delivery process. [link](https://www.langchain.com/blog/the-agent-development-lifecycle)
