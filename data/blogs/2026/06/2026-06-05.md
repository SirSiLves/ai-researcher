## Long-form picks

- **Open and closed models are on different exponentials** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues closed labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, soon Google) own an "integrated exponential" — coding agents past the Opus 4.5 / Codex 5.2 threshold prove a large market will pay big margins for marginal intelligence — while the open ecosystem rides a separate, slower-but-larger exponential of commoditized inference and niche enterprise fine-tunes. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different)

- **AI enthusiasts are in a race against time, AI skeptics are in a race against entropy** — Simon Willison (linking Charity Majors). _Takeaway:_ Frames the enthusiast/skeptic split inside engineering teams as both correct: leaning in hard yields real discontinuous gains, but shipping code faster than humans can read it drains the institutional-trust account. The org-design problem is building a feedback loop between the two camps. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/4/ai-enthusiasts-ai-skeptics/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/4/ai-enthusiasts-ai-skeptics/)

- **Microsoft's new MAI models** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notes on MAI-Thinking-1 (1T params, 35B active, early partners) and MAI-Code-1-Flash (137B/5B active, built for Copilot). Microsoft claims MAI-Thinking-1 beats Sonnet 4.6 in blind side-by-sides and was trained from scratch on clean, licensed data with no distillation — a notable in-house-frontier signal from a company long reliant on OpenAI. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/2/microsofts-new-models/)

- **GitHub's plan for Agents — Kyle Daigle** — swyx / Latent.Space. _Takeaway:_ Coding agents grew ~1400% in 2026; GitHub now sees ~275M commits/week (on pace for ~14B this year). Daigle lays out GitHub's positioning as the substrate for agent-authored code and the review/governance problems that follow at that volume. [https://www.latent.space/p/github](https://www.latent.space/p/github)

- **Import AI 458: Reckoning with the future; and a singularity story** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Adapted from his Oxford Cosmos/HAI lecture, Clark lays out his case for a takeoff toward automated AI R&D with dated predictions (Nov 2026 bio-relevant systems; Apr 2027 AI-assisted Nobel discovery; Nov 2027 autonomous companies at scale) and a fictional "positive singularity" vignette. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/26/import-ai-458-reckoning-with-the-future-and-a-singularity-story/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/26/import-ai-458-reckoning-with-the-future-and-a-singularity-story/)

- **Inside the AI Index: 12 Takeaways from the 2026 Report** — Stanford HAI. _Takeaway:_ GenAI hit 53% population adoption within three years (faster than PC or internet); estimated US consumer value ~$172B/yr with median per-user value tripling 2025→2026; US/China models keep trading the top spot, with Anthropic's lead down to 2.7% as of March 2026. Useful data backbone for governance and adoption arguments. [https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report)

- **How the Executive Branch Is Reshaping AI Federalism** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Traces how the March 2026 federal framework recalibrated the Dec 2025 EO — framing federal policy as a national baseline while preserving state police powers — and what that preemption posture means for the patchwork of US state AI laws. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/how-the-executive-branch-is-reshaping-ai-federalism](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/how-the-executive-branch-is-reshaping-ai-federalism)

- **How AI Data Centers Are Shaping Politics** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Magnificent Seven capex hit $102.5B in a quarter with 2026 projections toward $650B; the piece connects datacenter siting, grid strain, and household electricity costs to emerging local and national political fights over the AI buildout. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/how-ai-data-centers-are-shaping-politics](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/how-ai-data-centers-are-shaping-politics)
