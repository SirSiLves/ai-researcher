# AI Blogs — 2026-06-28

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Scaling Laws, Carefully** — Lilian Weng (Lil'Log). _Takeaway:_ Walks through Lovelace et al. (2026), who train ~300 models (15M–1B params, 50M–6B unique tokens) to model the interaction between model size and data repetition explicitly, rather than treating overparameterization as a simple diminishing return on effective size. A careful rethink of how data-repetition shapes the loss curve. [https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-06-24-scaling-laws/]

- **GLM-5.2 is the step change for open agents** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues GLM-5.2 (Z.ai, ~753B params, 1M-token context) is the moment open-weight models become genuinely competitive for agentic workloads, landing ~6.8 months behind frontier closed models. Frames the open/closed gap as a measurable lag, not a chasm. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-52-is-the-step-change-for-open]

- **Welcome to the AGI era of AI governance** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Uses the executive branch forcing Anthropic to suspend Claude 5 Mythos/Fable access as the inflection point where AI governance stops being voluntary and becomes state leverage over frontier labs. A substantive position on where regulation is actually heading. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance]

- **Using Local Coding Agents** — Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI). _Takeaway:_ Hands-on tutorial for standing up a local coding agent on open-source tooling and open-weight LLMs — a concrete engineering pattern for those wanting agent workflows off the frontier APIs. [https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/using-local-coding-agents]

- **Using LLMs to Secure Source Code** — Eugene Yan. _Takeaway:_ Practitioner write-up on applying LLMs to source-code security, paired with his patterns for building cybersecurity evals — real engineering detail on a security-critical use case. [https://eugeneyan.com/writing/cybersecurity-evals/]

- **AI and Liability** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ On a German ruling holding Google liable for errors in its AI overviews, via Bruce Schneier — a concrete legal precedent that reshapes the risk calculus for AI-generated content at scale. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/25/ai-and-liability/]

- **Incident Report: CVE-2026-LGTM** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ A (hypothetical) incident where two competing vendors' AI review agents enter a disagreement loop over whether a package is malicious — a sharp illustration of emergent failure modes in multi-agent ecosystems. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/26/incident-report/]

- **The EU Cloud and AI Development Act** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Analysis of the Commission's European Technological Sovereignty Package (semiconductors, AI, cloud, open source), published as the bloc moves toward the 2 Aug 2026 full-applicability date of the AI Act. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-eu-cloud-and-ai-development-act]

- **The Moral of Fable** — Dan Shipper (Every / Chain of Thought). _Takeaway:_ Reflection on Claude Fable 5 — billed as the best coding model in the world before being pulled — and what its brief life says about the model-release/governance collision of mid-2026. [https://every.to/chain-of-thought/the-moral-of-fable]

- **RL Systems: Mind the Gap — Matching Trainer and Generator Throughput** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Systems-level look at the throughput mismatch between RL trainers and generators, set against a coding-assistant market now cited at $30B+ ARR across the six largest players. Infra detail behind the agentic-coding boom. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/rl-systems-mind-the-gap-matching]
