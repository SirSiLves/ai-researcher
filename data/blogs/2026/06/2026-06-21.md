## Long-form picks

- **Welcome to the AGI era of AI governance** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues the US executive branch forcing Anthropic to suspend access to Claude 5 Mythos/Fable marks a phase shift: model access is now a national-security lever, not a product decision. A substantive position on where state control of frontier models is heading. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance)

- **Banning Open Source AI Would Be A Mistake** — Nathan Lambert & Kevin Xu (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ A general-audience op-ed staking out the open-weights side of the policy debate, tying the Mythos/Fable suspension and export-control threats to a broader argument that closing off open models would cede ground rather than improve safety. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/banning-open-source-ai-would-be-a](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/banning-open-source-ai-would-be-a)

- **GLM-5.2 is probably the most powerful text-only open weights LLM** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on assessment positioning GLM-5.2 at the frontier of open-weight text models, with concrete notes on where it lands relative to closed competitors. Useful read on the open vs closed exponential. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/)

- **Open and closed models are on different exponentials** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Makes the data-backed case that open and closed model capability are improving along distinct curves, reframing the "is open catching up?" debate around rate-of-change rather than a snapshot gap. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/open-and-closed-models-are-on-different)

- **RL Systems Mind the Gap: Matching Trainer and Generator Throughput** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Engineering deep-dive on a real bottleneck in RL training infrastructure — keeping trainer and generator throughput matched — set against the backdrop of a $30B+ ARR coding-assistant market. Concrete systems detail, not hype. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/rl-systems-mind-the-gap-matching](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/rl-systems-mind-the-gap-matching)

- **Multi-Agents: What's Actually Working** — Cognition. _Takeaway:_ Practitioner argument on making multi-agent systems coherent in production, drawn from Devin deployment experience — a useful counterweight to the agentic-AI hype cycle, focused on what survives contact with real workloads. [https://cognition.ai/blog/multi-agents-working](https://cognition.ai/blog/multi-agents-working)

- **The Moral of Fable** — Every (Chain of Thought). _Takeaway:_ Analysis of Claude Fable 5 — billed as the best coding model in the world — and the abrupt government-directed suspension, reading the episode as a turning point for how frontier coding models get governed. [https://every.to/chain-of-thought/the-moral-of-fable](https://every.to/chain-of-thought/the-moral-of-fable)

- **AI Cyber Risks Are Testing the Office Built to Coordinate Them** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Examines whether the Office of the National Cyber Director can actually coordinate the government's whipsawing response to AI cyber risk — a substantive governance/policy read beyond the headlines. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-cyber-risks-are-testing-the-office-built-to-coordinate-them](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-cyber-risks-are-testing-the-office-built-to-coordinate-them)

- **DiffusionGemma** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notes on a diffusion-based language model variant in the Gemma family, with hands-on observations on what text diffusion changes about generation. A concrete look at a non-autoregressive direction. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/10/diffusiongemma/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/10/diffusiongemma/)
