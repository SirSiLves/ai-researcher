# AI Blogs — 2026-06-29

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Scaling Laws, Carefully** — Lilian Weng (Lil'Log). _Takeaway:_ Revisits the over-training / data-repetition question via Lovelace et al. (2026), who explicitly model the interaction between model size and data repetition (~300 models, 15M–1B params, 50M–6B unique tokens) rather than treating overparameterization as a flat diminishing return — a careful, empirical correction to the standard Chinchilla framing. [https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-06-24-scaling-laws/]

- **GLM-5.2 is probably the most powerful text-only open-weights LLM** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Z.ai released GLM-5.2 to coding subscribers, then dropped full MIT-licensed weights — a 753B-param / 1.51TB MoE with ~40B active. Hands-on read on why the open-weights frontier is now genuinely competitive with closed labs on agentic tasks. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/17/glm-52/]

- **GLM-5.2 is the step change for open agents** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues GLM-5.2 is the first open model trading blows with OpenAI/Anthropic's latest on agent leaderboards, and reads it as evidence that China's open-source trajectory is closing the agentic gap faster than the closed/open "different exponentials" thesis predicted. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-52-is-the-step-change-for-open]

- **Welcome to the AGI era of AI governance** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Substantive position piece on the US executive branch forcing Anthropic to suspend access to its latest Mythos/Fable models — frames it as the moment "voluntary" safety commitments collide with government-as-customer leverage. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance]

- **Datasette Apps: Host custom HTML applications inside Datasette** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Engineering write-up on shipping a Claude-Artifacts-style mechanism for Datasette — sandboxed iframes for untrusted LLM-generated code, copy-prompt-to-model workflow, and a path to safe read-write data apps. A concrete pattern for embedding model-generated UIs without DOM/cookie exposure. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/18/datasette-apps/]

- **The Revenge of the Data Scientist** — Hamel Husain. _Takeaway:_ Contrarian argument that, far from ending the data-scientist role, the agentic-coding era hands it back to people who can do rigorous error analysis and evals — the scarce skill that LLM-as-judge pipelines keep getting wrong. [https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/revenge/]

- **Patterns for Building Cybersecurity Evals** — Eugene Yan. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on patterns for evaluating LLMs on security tasks (now writing from inside Anthropic) — how to construct cybersecurity eval sets, avoid contamination, and measure real capability rather than benchmark theater. [https://eugeneyan.com/writing/cybersecurity-evals/]

- **Using Local Coding Agents** — Sebastian Raschka (Ahead of AI). _Takeaway:_ Practical walkthrough of standing up coding agents fully on open-source/local stacks — model choices, tooling, and the tradeoffs versus hosted Claude Code / Codex, for teams that need on-prem or cost control. [https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/using-local-coding-agents]

- **RL Systems Mind the Gap: Matching Trainer and Generator Throughput** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Systems-level analysis of a real RL-training bottleneck (trainer vs generator throughput mismatch), set against the claim that coding assistants are now a $30B+ ARR market across the top six players, on track to clear $100B by year end. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/rl-systems-mind-the-gap-matching]

- **Import AI 460: Reward-hacking society; RSI data from Anthropic** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Reads newly-surfaced Anthropic data as a signal of recursive self-improvement — ~8x more code merged into the codebase in 2026 vs the 2021–2024 baseline — and works through what an accelerating internal-R&D loop implies for timelines and governance. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/06/08/import-ai-460-reward-hacking-society-rsi-data-from-anthropic-and-rl-based-quadcopter-racing/]

- **The EU Cloud and AI Development Act** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Analysis of the European Commission's Technological Sovereignty Package (published June 3) and its centerpiece, the Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA) — what a sovereignty-first industrial strategy means for compute, open source, and the next phase of EU AI regulation. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-eu-cloud-and-ai-development-act]

## Medium roundup

### rag
- **[Modern RAG in 2026: The Components That Actually Matter](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/modern-rag-in-2026-the-components-that-actually-matter-3f6a138ef117)** — Anubhav, Data Science Collective. Cuts through the "RAG is dead" hype to argue retrieval still owns most of the pain in production; long context shrinks but does not replace RAG for large, fast-changing, auditable corpora.
- **[RAG, end to end: the whole picture of retrieval-augmented generation](https://medium.com/@Alex647/rag-end-to-end-the-whole-picture-of-retrieval-augmented-generation-4aca31111a42)** — Alex647. Frames RAG as nearest-neighbor search bolted onto an LLM and walks the full pipeline, with the load-bearing claim that almost all failures live in retrieval, not generation.

### ai-agents
- **[The Agentic AI Engineer Roadmap for 2026: Skills, Stack, and Order](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/the-agentic-ai-engineer-roadmap-for-2026-skills-stack-and-order-fc1dfa17948d)** — Anubhav, Data Science Collective. Opinionated learning order for the agent stack — LangGraph, MCP/A2A, function calling, RAGAS, and observability (Langfuse/Phoenix/Helicone) — useful as a concrete map of what the role now requires.
- **[The AI Update — June 5, 2026: Agents Are Working, Regulation Is Moving, and the Hype Is Over](https://medium.com/adi-insights-innovations-collective/the-ai-update-june-5-2026-agents-are-working-regulation-is-moving-and-the-hype-is-over-b475b737bd76)** — Adi Insights. Argues the June story is convergence (policy + agents + multimodal + distribution), not any single launch, and flags the gap between heavy spend and only ~15% production-readiness.

### model-context-protocol
- **[A Pentester's Guide to the Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://armx64.medium.com/a-pentesters-guide-to-the-model-context-protocol-mcp-f932cd62ee2e)** — Abdur Rahman Maheer. Security-focused deep dive backed by a June 2026 study of 2,214 real-world MCP servers (19,200 description-code pairs) — a rare adversarial look at the attack surface of the "USB-C of AI."
