# AI Blogs — 2026-07-19

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **[AI Mania Is Eviscerating Global Decision-Making](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/19/ai-mania/)** — Simon Willison (Jul 19). _Takeaway:_ Sharp link-commentary arguing that the scramble to look "AI-forward" is degrading the quality of real institutional decisions — hype as an active tax on judgment, not just noise.
- **[Claude Code uses Bun written in Rust now](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/19/claude-code-in-bun-in-rust/)** — Simon Willison (Jul 19). _Takeaway:_ Notes Claude Code's runtime move to a Rust-based Bun build; a concrete data point on AI-tool teams chasing startup/latency wins by shedding Node — pairs with the Pragmatic Engineer piece below.
- **[Controlling Reasoning Effort in LLMs](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/controlling-reasoning-effort-in-llms)** — Sebastian Raschka (Jul 18). _Takeaway:_ Technical deep-dive on the mechanisms now used to dial reasoning/"thinking" budget up or down — from length-penalty RL to explicit effort tokens — and what each trade-off costs in accuracy vs. latency.
- **[The Pulse: What can we learn from Bun's rapid Rust rewrite with AI?](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-what-can-we-learn-from-buns-rapid-rust-rewrite-with-ai/)** — Gergely Orosz / The Pragmatic Engineer (Jul 16). _Takeaway:_ Uses Bun's fast AI-assisted Rust rewrite as a case study on where LLM coding help actually compresses timelines — and where senior judgment still gates a large systems migration.
- **[Mainframes and Main Characters](https://stratechery.com/2026/mainframes-and-main-characters/)** — Ben Thompson / Stratechery (Jul 17). _Takeaway:_ Reads IBM's mainframe moat against the AI platform shift, arguing durable enterprise lock-in — not model quality — is what decides who captures the value. (Paywalled.)
- **[Do Automated Evals Work?](https://parlance-labs.com/blog/posts/auto-evals.html)** — Hamel Husain (Jul 11). _Takeaway:_ Hands-on interrogation of whether automated LLM-as-judge evals hold up in practice; makes the case that they only work when tightly grounded in human-labeled error analysis, not as a drop-in replacement.
- **[What will be left for us to work on?](https://www.normaltech.ai/p/what-will-be-left-for-us-to-work)** — Arvind Narayanan & Sayash Kapoor / AI Snake Oil (Normal Tech) (Jul 13). _Takeaway:_ Pushes back on total-automation narratives, arguing comparative advantage and ever-rising expectations keep generating human work even as AI absorbs tasks — a governance/economics counter to the "jobless" framing.
- **[The Lab of the Future Should Feel Like a Data Center](https://www.latent.space/p/the-lab-of-the-future-should-feel)** — Latent.Space (Andy Beam & Rafa Gómez-Bombarelli, Lila Sciences) (Jul 16). _Takeaway:_ Argues autonomous scientific labs should be architected like compute clusters — agents scheduling experiments as jobs — and what that means for AI-for-science infrastructure.
- **[Stanford Scientists Build an AI Lab Partner](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/stanford-scientists-build-an-ai-lab-partner)** — Stanford HAI (Jul 9). _Takeaway:_ Profiles Biomni, a biomedical research agent that analyzes data, spots patterns, and proposes experiments — a concrete look at where agentic "co-scientist" systems actually help vs. overreach.
- **[Import AI 464: Fables writes GPU kernels; AI automation; and analog computation](https://jack-clark.net/2026/07/06/import-ai-464-fables-writes-gpu-kernels-ai-automation-and-analog-computation/)** — Jack Clark / Import AI (Jul 6). _Takeaway:_ Weekly research-and-policy roundup; this issue centers on models writing their own GPU kernels, the automation debate, and a revival of analog compute for inference efficiency.

## Medium roundup

### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[How to Prevent MCP Tool Poisoning at Runtime](https://medium.com/@agentg.dev/how-to-prevent-mcp-tool-poisoning-at-runtime-d74bae4a7bdd)** — AgentG. Runtime defenses (allowlisting, signature/description checks) against MCP tool-poisoning and rug-pull attacks.
- **[ARD vs. MCP: The Plug, the Directory, and the Quiet Fight for AI's Front Door](https://shoaibalimir.medium.com/ard-vs-mcp-the-plug-the-directory-and-the-quiet-fight-for-ais-front-door-dbaec2d69c63)** — Shoaibali Mir. Frames an emerging turf war between tool-connection protocols and agent/tool directories over who owns discovery.

### tool-use
- **[Part III | 'tool_use', End to End: The Agentic Loop in Three Iterations](https://medium.com/@akshat.mistry/tool-use-end-to-end-the-agentic-loop-in-three-iterations-87cde7765dfc)** — Akshat A. Mistry. Hands-on trace of a full tool-use loop, iterating through stop-reasons and tool results to a completed task.

### ai-engineering
- **[Markdown Is a Knowledge Format, Not a Production Agent Architecture](https://medium.com/@mohammadghadri18/markdown-is-a-knowledge-format-not-a-production-agent-architecture-5c530a48cc3e)** — Max Ghadri. Argues against overloading markdown files as agent "architecture," separating knowledge representation from control flow.
- **[Beyond the Agent Hype: An Architectural Blueprint for Production-Grade Reliability](https://medium.com/@muhammad.awais.professional/beyond-the-agent-hype-an-architectural-blueprint-for-production-grade-reliability-15bd0af0ca0f)** — Muhammad Awais. Reliability patterns — retries, idempotency, guardrails, observability — for moving agents from demo to production.

### prompt-engineering
- **[AI Loop Engineering: Beyond Prompt Engineering](https://medium.com/@dileepapeiris5/ai-loop-engineering-beyond-prompt-engineering-7815b248ad05)** — Dileepa Peiris. Part of today's visible "harness/loop engineering" wave — argues the unit of design has shifted from the prompt to the agent's control loop.

### vector-database
- **[SQL Is All You Need: RAG without a Vector Database in a Dozen Lines of BigQuery](https://medium.com/google-cloud/sql-is-all-you-need-rag-without-a-vector-database-in-a-dozen-lines-of-bigquery-ff08ed5d3ac4)** — Brice Fotzo (Google Cloud). Non-obvious, code-backed claim that native SQL + embeddings in BigQuery can deliver competitive RAG without a dedicated vector store.

### rag
- **[Beyond the Hype: Why Google's Open Knowledge Format is Not the "RAG Killer"](https://secret-dev.medium.com/beyond-the-hype-why-googles-open-knowledge-format-is-not-the-rag-killer-add704eddcb3)** — Secret Dev. Tempers claims that Google's Open Knowledge Format replaces retrieval, positioning it as complementary structure rather than a RAG substitute.
- **[Where RAG Fails: Understand the Limitations](https://medium.com/@lumesh2911/where-rag-fails-understand-the-limitations-b5d7e16cac08)** — Lumesh Kumar Sahu. Catalogs concrete failure modes (chunking, retrieval mismatch, stale indexes) of naive RAG and how to detect them.

### ai-governance
- **[The GenAI Readiness Gap Is an Engineering Problem, Not a Governance One](https://medium.com/@ravisat/the-genai-readiness-gap-is-an-engineering-problem-not-a-governance-one-4479bf940e00)** — Ravindra Satyanarayana. Contrarian take: what blocks enterprise GenAI is data/eval/infra maturity, not the absence of policy.
- **[The Politics of Weights](https://medium.com/@adnanmasood/the-politics-of-weights-25ebd8515c43)** — Adnan Masood, PhD. Treats open- vs. closed-weight release as the real governance fault line, tracing who controls capability and access.

### ai-safety
- **[The Autonomy of Adversarial AI: From Prompt Injection to Autonomous Jailbreak Agents](https://medium.com/@tarifabeach/the-autonomy-of-adversarial-ai-from-prompt-injection-to-autonomous-jailbreak-agents-ae43b1bf3810)** — Martino Agostini. Traces the escalation from one-shot prompt injection to self-directed agents that probe and jailbreak other models.

### agentic-ai
- **[The Illusion of Prompt-Based Security: Exploiting BOLA in AI Agents](https://medium.com/@wisdomajokuu/the-illusion-of-prompt-based-security-exploiting-broken-object-level-authorization-bola-in-ai-fcf8b6156c9e)** — Wisdom Ajoku. Shows classic Broken Object-Level Authorization flaws resurfacing in agent tool layers, where prompt instructions are no substitute for real authz.

### llm / large-language-models
- **[The 4 Lines Every Claude Skill Needs](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/the-4-lines-every-claude-skill-needs-586c9f1dd4ac)** — Haden (Data Science Collective). Minimal front-matter recipe for a reliably-triggering Claude Skill, with the failure modes each line prevents.

### generative-ai
- **["Regenerate" Is Not a Complete AI Product Interface](https://pranaysuyash.medium.com/regenerate-is-not-a-complete-ai-product-interface-474cec298a13)** — Pranay Suyash. UX argument that regenerate-only interfaces offload correction onto users; makes the case for steer/edit affordances instead.
