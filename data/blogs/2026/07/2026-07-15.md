## Long-form picks

**How I tricked Claude into leaking your deepest, darkest secrets** — Simon Willison (Jul 15, 2026). _Takeaway:_ Willison praises how Claude's `web_fetch` tool is engineered to blunt prompt-injection data-exfiltration attacks, then walks through a technique (building on work by Ayush Paul) that can still coax private context back out of the model. It's a concrete, hands-on case study of exactly where an agent tool's security guardrails hold and where a determined attacker can still leak data. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/15/claude-web-fetch-exfiltration/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/15/claude-web-fetch-exfiltration/)

**Context engineering with Dex Horthy** — Gergely Orosz, The Pragmatic Engineer (Jul 15, 2026). _Takeaway:_ A deep-dive with Dex Horthy (HumanLayer) making the case that "context engineering" — deciding precisely what goes into the model's window and how it's structured — is the discipline that separates effective AI-assisted software from steady code-quality erosion. Distinct from yesterday's "loop engineering" piece, this one is about the inputs, not the control loop, with practical tactics for keeping long-running agents on track. [https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/context-engineering-with-dex-horthy](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/context-engineering-with-dex-horthy)

**5 Trends That Defined AI Engineering at World's Fair 2026** — Latent Space (swyx & team) (Jul 14, 2026). _Takeaway:_ A field-level synthesis from the AI Engineer World's Fair arguing the discipline has crossed into a new phase: building whole systems *around* agents rather than merely building *with* them. It reads as a state-of-the-practice snapshot of where production AI engineering is heading and what that shift demands of engineers. [https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends](https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends)

**Up the Stack: How AI's Escape From the Commodity Trap Risks Enterprise Lock-in** — Arvind Narayanan & Sayash Kapoor, AI Snake Oil / Normal Tech (Jul 9, 2026). _Takeaway:_ The authors argue both boosters and critics are watching the wrong layer: as frontier labs move "up the stack" from raw models toward integrated products and agents, the real danger for enterprises isn't commoditization but a new form of vendor lock-in higher in the stack. A governance/economics counterweight to the usual "models are becoming a commodity" narrative. [https://www.normaltech.ai/p/up-the-stack-how-ais-escape-from](https://www.normaltech.ai/p/up-the-stack-how-ais-escape-from)

**LangChain and NVIDIA Launch the NemoClaw Deep Agents Blueprint** — LangChain (with NVIDIA) (Jul 2026). _Takeaway:_ LangChain and NVIDIA introduce an open, "governed" blueprint for enterprise deep agents that pairs LangChain's Deep Agents stack with NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra and a secured runtime, so teams can tune agents to their own workloads and run them under cost/quality/security controls. A notable data point on the enterprise agent-framework race; corroborated by NVIDIA's own Nemotron-LangChain announcement. (Exact publish date unverified — surfaced via the langchain.com/blog listing plus WebSearch fallback, as the old blog RSS is retired.) [https://www.langchain.com/blog/langchain-and-nvidia-launch-the-nemoclaw-deep-agents-blueprint](https://www.langchain.com/blog/langchain-and-nvidia-launch-the-nemoclaw-deep-agents-blueprint)
