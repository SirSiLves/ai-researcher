# AI Blogs — 2026-07-01

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Ornith-1.0: Self-Scaffolding LLMs for Agentic Coding** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Hands-on review of DeepReinforce's first release, an MIT-licensed self-scaffolding coding model (9B/31B Dense, 35B/397B MoE) built on Gemma 4 + Qwen 3.5. Ran the 35B GGUF locally in LM Studio via Pi and found it handles multi-tool agent harnesses proficiently — a concrete data point that competent local agentic coding is now reachable on 20GB of weights. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/29/ornith/]

- **Have your agent record video demos of its work with shot-scraper video** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ New shot-scraper feature lets a coding agent capture video of the UI it just built, closing the verification loop so the agent (and you) can watch the result rather than trust a screenshot. A practical pattern for agent self-verification. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/30/shot-scraper-video/]

- **Can Frontier AI Labs Lawfully Agree to Pause?** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Legal analysis of Anthropic's proposal for a coordinated, verified AI-development pause and whether US antitrust law would treat such a multi-lab agreement as illegal collusion. Substantive take on a live governance debate where safety coordination collides with competition law. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/can-frontier-ai-labs-lawfully-agree-to-pause]

- **The EU Cloud and AI Development Act** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Breaks down the European Commission's June 3 Technological Sovereignty Package — semiconductors, AI, cloud, and open-source measures — and what a sovereignty-framed industrial policy means for the EU AI regulatory stack. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-eu-cloud-and-ai-development-act]

- **Summary of METR's predeployment evaluation of GPT-5.6 Sol** — METR. _Takeaway:_ Independent external eval of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol with API + "rail-free" access and a Codex harness. Detecting powerseeking/alignment-faking propensities was "reassuring," credited to OpenAI not training against chain-of-thought and running extensive internal monitoring — a rare concrete look at third-party frontier-model safety practice. [https://metr.org/blog/2026-06-26-gpt-5-6-sol/]

- **Welcome to the AGI era of AI governance** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Argues the US executive branch forcing Anthropic to shut off access to its Claude 5 Mythos/Fable models is the "starting gun" of a new governance era where state power directly gates model deployment. Sharp position on where AI policy is actually heading. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/welcome-to-the-agi-era-of-ai-governance]

- **Scaling Laws, Carefully** — Lilian Weng (Lil'Log). _Takeaway:_ A careful 25-minute treatment of scaling laws — what they do and don't predict, and where the naive extrapolations break. Grounded technical writing on a topic usually handled with hand-waving. [https://lilianweng.github.io/]

- **Checking In on AI and the Big Five** — Ben Thompson (Stratechery). _Takeaway:_ Strategic read on AWS's AI infrastructure bets (Bedrock, Trainium) and whether Amazon's comparatively quiet position is a liability or an adaptability advantage as the stack shifts to agents. [https://stratechery.com/2025/checking-in-on-ai-and-the-big-five/]

- **June 2026 LangChain Newsletter — Fleet On-Call Copilot, Deep Agents Rubrics** — LangChain. _Takeaway:_ Documents a concrete agent-engineering pattern shift: Deep Agents Rubrics + programmatic subagents, LangSmith computer-use and voice-trace debugging, and a Box case study claiming 3x faster agent iteration while preserving permissions. Real deployment detail, not just PR. [https://www.langchain.com/blog/june-2026-langchain-newsletter]

## Medium roundup

### rag
- **[RAG, end to end: the whole picture of retrieval-augmented generation](https://medium.com/@Alex647/rag-end-to-end-the-whole-picture-of-retrieval-augmented-generation-4aca31111a42)** — Alex647. Splits RAG into offline indexing vs. online retrieval and argues almost all system complexity lives in retrieval, not generation — bad answers usually mean the model got the wrong chunks.

### ai-agents / agentic-ai
- **[The AI Update — June 5, 2026: Agents Are Working, Regulation Is Moving, and the Hype Is Over](https://medium.com/adi-insights-innovations-collective/the-ai-update-june-5-2026-agents-are-working-regulation-is-moving-and-the-hype-is-over-b475b737bd76)** — Adi Insights. Argues mid-2026's real story is convergence of policy + agents + enterprise distribution rather than any single launch — agents shifting from demos to production business systems.
- **[The Hidden Truth About Agentic AI in 2026](https://medium.com/@rsatech/the-hidden-truth-about-agentic-ai-in-2026-0e147bd426fb)** — RSA Tech Group. Data-backed reality check: only 35% have deployed agents broadly, 17% across nearly all workflows, and 68% say half or fewer employees interact with agents daily — a useful corrective to the "agents everywhere" narrative.

### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[Model Context Protocol (MCP): Evolution, Capabilities, and the Rise of Peta](https://bytebridge.medium.com/model-context-protocol-mcp-evolution-capabilities-and-the-rise-of-peta-ff2967b45d48)** — ByteBridge. Reviews MCP entering 2026 as a "mature but still-evolving" ecosystem after a whirlwind first year, tracing where the protocol's capabilities are consolidating.
