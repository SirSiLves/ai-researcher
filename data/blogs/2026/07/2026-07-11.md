## Long-form picks

- **Why AI Infrastructure Must Evolve for Agent Experience — Akshat Bubna, Modal CTO** (Latent.Space, 2026-07-08). _Takeaway:_ Serving infrastructure built for human-facing, latency-first request/response breaks down for agent workloads, which are long-running, bursty, highly parallel and tool-heavy. Bubna argues the new design target is "Agent Experience" — fast cold starts, cheap sandboxes, and stateful long-horizon execution — framed against Modal's $355M Series C and its bet that agent-native infra, not model quality, is the next bottleneck. [link](https://www.latent.space/p/modal2026)

## Medium roundup

### ai-agents
- **[What I Believe About LLM Agents in Production That Most People Get Wrong](https://pauldeepakraj-r.medium.com/what-i-believe-about-llm-agents-in-production-that-most-people-get-wrong-3ede38793219)** — Paul Deepakraj Retinraj. Argues ~90% of agent "wisdom" from demos, papers, and framework docs doesn't survive contact with latency-constrained, safety-critical production; running agents observably and cost-effectively is a different discipline from making one work in a notebook.

### rag
- **[RAG Architecture in 2026: How to Keep Retrieval Actually Fresh](https://medium.com/real-time-data-evolution/rag-architecture-in-2026-how-to-keep-retrieval-actually-fresh-3a9bae9ec8f9)** — Real-Time Data Evolution. A concrete engineering pattern, not a framework tour: use streaming databases with native CDC connectors and built-in embedding functions to continuously re-index, keeping embeddings fresh within seconds of source-document changes.

### model-context-protocol
- **[CLI-Based Agents vs MCP: The 2026 Showdown That Every AI Engineer Needs to Understand](https://lalatenduswain.medium.com/cli-based-agents-vs-mcp-the-2026-showdown-that-every-ai-engineer-needs-to-understand-7dfbc9e3e1f9)** — Lalatendu Keshari Swain. Takes a side on a live design debate — when a lightweight CLI/tool-calling wrapper beats standing up a full MCP server, and when MCP's structured, stateless connectivity earns its overhead at enterprise scale.

### ai-engineering
- **[The Death of Prompt Engineering, and How Evals Are Rising in Its Place](https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/the-death-of-prompt-engineering-and-how-evals-are-rising-in-its-place-f8467871a815)** — Yahav M. Position piece: the prompt-engineer role is being displaced by the eval engineer — someone who defines what "good" means, builds infrastructure to measure it at scale, and treats eval suites with the version-controlled rigor of production code.
