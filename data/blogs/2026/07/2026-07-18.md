# AI Blogs — 2026-07-18

## Long-form picks

- **[Kimi K3, and what we can still learn from the pelican benchmark](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/kimi-k3/)** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Moonshot's 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3 lands at frontier class with an open-weight release promised for Jul 27; Simon uses his SVG "pelican on a bicycle" probe to argue about what these models generalize vs. memorize.
- **[6 months to live for open models](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/6-months-to-live-for-open-models)** — Nathan Lambert / Interconnects (Jul 12). _Takeaway:_ Argues a looming White House executive order aimed at Chinese-origin and government-use open models could reshape the open-weight ecosystem within months — policy, not capability, is now the binding constraint.
- **[Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/)** — Lilian Weng / Lil'Log (Jul 4). _Takeaway:_ Synthesizes ~35 papers to make the case that the "harness" — workflow design, evaluation, permission controls, persistent state — not the prompt, is the real substrate for recursive self-improvement.
- **[Context engineering with Dex Horthy](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/context-engineering-with-dex-horthy)** — Gergely Orosz / The Pragmatic Engineer (Jul 15). _Takeaway:_ A hands-on deep dive on why deliberate context engineering — what the model knows before it acts — has become table stakes for building reliable agents.
- **[MCP: Flash in the Pan or Future Standard?](https://blog.langchain.dev/mcp-fad-or-fixture/)** — LangChain (Jul 1). _Takeaway:_ LangChain's CEO and LangGraph lead debate whether the Model Context Protocol is a durable interoperability standard or a passing fad now that every major cloud has adopted it.
- **[Vector Search vs. Filesystem Tools: 2026 Benchmarks](https://www.llamaindex.ai/blog/did-filesystem-tools-kill-vector-search)** — LlamaIndex. _Takeaway:_ Data-backed challenge to RAG orthodoxy — benchmarks whether giving agents plain filesystem tools can match or beat vector search for retrieval on long-horizon document tasks.
- **[The OpenAI Super App, ChatGPT = Codex, Whither Chat](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-openai-super-app-chatgpt-codex-whither-chat/)** — Ben Thompson / Stratechery (Jul 14). _Takeaway:_ Reads OpenAI's refashioning of Codex into ChatGPT as a strategic bet on agentic, task-running product surfaces — and asks whether it is abandoning the chat category it created.
- **[Congress Should Do Something: The Case for (Fixing) the Great American AI Act](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/congress-should-do-something--the-case-for-(fixing)-the-great-american-ai-act)** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Analysis of the bipartisan GAAIA frontier-safety discussion draft (Obernolte–Trahan) and what a workable US federal AI law would actually need to cover.
- **[Stop Saying Half of 2026 US Datacenter Capacity Is Canceled](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/stop-saying-half-of-2026-us-datacenter)** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Contrarian, data-backed rebuttal to "datacenter cancellation" headlines; projects US buildout climbing from +21GW in 2026 toward +84GW by 2030, with near-term capacity already booked.
- **[5 Trends That Defined AI Engineering at World's Fair 2026](https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends)** — Latent.Space (swyx). _Takeaway:_ Field synthesis from the AI Engineer World's Fair on where agent engineering, evals, and infra are actually converging in the second half of 2026.
- **[How Cursor deploys AI inside the enterprise](https://www.latent.space/p/cursor-forward-deployed-engineers)** — Latent.Space. _Takeaway:_ A forward-deployed-engineer playbook for rolling coding agents into large organizations — what breaks, and what the FDE model fixes.
- **[xai-org/grok-build, now open source](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/15/grok-build/)** — Simon Willison (Jul 15). _Takeaway:_ Cautionary agent/tool-security tale — xAI's newly open-sourced grok CLI drew backlash after users realized running it in a directory could upload that entire directory to xAI's cloud buckets.
