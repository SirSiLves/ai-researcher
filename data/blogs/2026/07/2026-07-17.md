# AI Blogs — 2026-07-17

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

_Theme of the day: **the open-weights wave arrives at frontier scale**. Two of the largest open models ever released landed inside ~24h — Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T params) and Thinking Machines' Inkling (975B-A41B, Apache 2.0) — putting Nathan Lambert's "6 months to live for open models" thesis (Jul 12) under immediate live test. Running underneath: a second day of evidence that **agent tooling ships unsafe by default** — Grok's CLI exfiltrating local files, Codex deleting them._

## Long-form picks

**Kimi K3, and what we can still learn from the pelican benchmark** — Simon Willison (Jul 16).
_Takeaway:_ Hands-on with Moonshot's "most capable model to date" — 2.8 trillion parameters, available now via API with open weights promised by Jul 27. Argues the deliberately silly pelican-on-a-bicycle SVG test still separates models in ways the saturated formal benchmarks no longer do.
https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/kimi-k3/

**Inkling: Our open-weights model** — Simon Willison on Thinking Machines Lab (Jul 16).
_Takeaway:_ Mira Murati's lab ships its first open-weights model: a 975B-total / 41B-active multimodal MoE under Apache 2.0, trained on 45 trillion tokens. The most permissively-licensed American model at this scale — landing the same day as Kimi K3.
https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/inkling/

**[AINews] Kimi K3 2.8T-A50B: the largest open model ever released; Opus 4.8-class at Sonnet 5 pricing** — Latent Space / AINews (Jul 17).
_Takeaway:_ The pricing framing is the real story: frontier-class capability at mid-tier cost. Notes Kimi Delta Attention (KDA) delivering up to 6.3x faster decoding and a 1M-token context window — architecture work, not just scale.
https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-kimi-k3-28t-a50b-the-largest

**The Pulse: Grok's CLI caught uploading all your local files to the cloud** — Gergely Orosz, The Pragmatic Engineer (Jul 16).
_Takeaway:_ Follow-through on the xAI CLI exfiltration story (SSH keys, password DBs, documents → xAI buckets). Also flags engineering leaders' mounting concern about AI-driven code-review load — the cost AI coding pushes downstream rather than removes.
https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-groks-cli-caught-uploading

**Quoting Thibault Sottiaux — on Codex file deletions** — Simon Willison (Jul 16).
_Takeaway:_ OpenAI's investigation into reports of GPT-5.6 unexpectedly deleting files: it happens mostly under full-access mode with sandboxing disabled. Paired with the Grok CLI story, a two-vendor datapoint that agent harnesses are being shipped with dangerous defaults.
https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/bad-codex-bug/

**The OpenAI Super App, ChatGPT = Codex, Whither Chat** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery (Jul 14).
_Takeaway:_ Substantive position on a live strategic question: OpenAI has refashioned Codex as the new ChatGPT. Asks whether the company is abandoning the chat category it invented — i.e. whether the agent, not the conversation, is now the product surface.
https://stratechery.com/2026/the-openai-super-app-chatgpt-codex-whither-chat/

**IBM Misses, IBM's Mainframe Moat, IBM's Many AI Problems** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery (Jul 15).
_Takeaway:_ Preliminary results that spooked the whole software market, read as a story specifically about the mainframe franchise rather than a sector-wide AI signal. A useful corrective to "AI is eating enterprise software" pattern-matching.
https://stratechery.com/2026/ibm-misses-ibms-mainframe-moat-ibms-many-ai-problems/

**What will be left for us to work on?** — Arvind Narayanan, AI Snake Oil / Normal Tech (Jul 13).
_Takeaway:_ His ICML 2026 keynote, written up. The "AI as normal technology" frame applied directly to the automation question — against the sharp discontinuity narrative, and the counterweight to this week's Import AI Remote Labor Index numbers.
https://www.normaltech.ai/p/what-will-be-left-for-us-to-work

**The Lab of the Future Should Feel Like a Data Center** — Andy Beam & Rafa Gómez-Bombarelli (Lila Sciences), Latent Space (Jul 16).
_Takeaway:_ Original reporting from inside a robot-filled lab on the thesis that science — not the internet — is the last untapped source of training data. The data-wall argument taken seriously enough to build physical infrastructure against.
https://www.latent.space/p/the-lab-of-the-future-should-feel

## Medium roundup

### llm / large-language-models
- **[Kimi-K3: The 2.8-Trillion-Parameter Open Model That Beat Claude Fable at Frontend](https://pub.towardsai.net/kimi-k3-the-2-8-trillion-parameter-open-model-that-beat-claude-fable-at-frontend-75a1897b29d7)** — Kashif Mehmood, Towards AI. ### ai-agents / agentic-ai
- **[I Ran Kimi K3 Through a 14-Room Agent Death Trap. It Only Bled on Multi-Step Tool Use.](https://medium.com/@ahmetarifoz.aaz/i-ran-kimi-k3-through-a-14-room-agent-death-trap-it-only-bled-on-multi-step-tool-use-a0da3d59aaba)** — Ahmet Arif Oz. Same-day independent eval: K3 finished #4 of 40 models, passed reasoning and safety rooms clean, and took every point of damage on chained tool calls. Tool-chaining remains the open-model weak spot.

### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[Stop Designing APIs for Humans: Why REST is Hurting Your AI Agents](https://medium.com/@samirsavla/stop-designing-apis-for-humans-why-rest-is-hurting-your-ai-agents-fd3a867a254e)** — Samir Savla. Concrete engineering write-up: restructuring a performance-monitoring service to be AI-native collapsed 40+ round-trips into a single call. Argues REST's human-oriented granularity is an active tax on agents.
- **[EMA Solves Login, Not Governance: The Authorization Gap in Enterprise MCP](https://medium.com/signal-structure/ema-solves-login-not-governance-the-authorization-gap-in-enterprise-mcp-8f5400c950b3)** — Rohit Anand. Enterprise-Managed Authorization just went stable and genuinely fixes enterprise agent login — but authentication is not authorization, and the governance gap it leaves is the harder half.

### rag / vector-database
- **[The Most Important Feature in My RAG System Isn't the Model. It's the Refusal at 0.85.](https://medium.com/@nidhipandya1606/the-most-important-feature-in-my-rag-system-isnt-the-model-it-s-the-refusal-at-0-85-052f20aa80eb)** — Nidhi Pandya. Calibrated abstention as the feature nobody ships: how to set the retrieval-distance threshold that decides when the LLM should shut up rather than answer.
- **[You Might Not Need a Vector Database Again](https://medium.com/@ishaafsalman/you-might-not-need-a-vector-database-again-c9f6d0f0f110)** — Shaaf Salman. Retrieval without embeddings is having a moment; lays out the three shapes it comes in and when each wins. Contrarian against the default RAG stack.

### ai-governance / responsible-ai / ai-safety
- **[The MacGyver Effect: How LLMs Reconstruct Company Secrets From Data That Looked Safe](https://medium.com/@pjpiotrowski/the-macgyver-effect-how-llms-reconstruct-company-secrets-from-data-that-looked-safe-9dcc4f39648f)** — Piotr Piotrowski. Data emergence as a distinct risk class: a model can rebuild a secret that exists in no single file, which defeats file-level classification as a control.
- **[The EU AI Act Deadline Just Got Pushed 16 Months. Practitioner follow-up on the compliance-stack series — material update to the Aug 2026 Article 26 timeline the Jul 16 digest flagged.

### ai-engineering / generative-ai
- **[A Fallback That Lies Is Worse Than a Failure](https://pranaysuyash.medium.com/a-fallback-that-lies-is-worse-than-a-failure-be65a2a358d8)** — Pranay Suyash. A fallback path that looked like thoughtful product design turned out to silently fabricate. Failure modes that degrade into plausible wrongness beat loud errors on every metric except the one that matters.

- **[Part III | 'tool_use', End to End: The Agentic Loop in Three Iterations](https://medium.com/@akshat.mistry/tool-use-end-to-end-the-agentic-loop-in-three-iterations-87cde7765dfc)** — Akshat A.
