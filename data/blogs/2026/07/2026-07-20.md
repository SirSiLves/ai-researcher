## Long-form picks

- **[Kimi K3: The open-weights escalation](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k3-the-open-weights-escalation)** — Nathan Lambert / Interconnects (Jul 20). _Takeaway:_ Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8T-param MoE, weights dropping Jul 27) is Lambert's evidence that the open-to-closed — and American-to-Chinese — capability gap has compressed from a debated 6–9 months to roughly 3–5. The open-weights escalation is accelerating, not stalling.

Altman); a small primary-source window into the governance fractures now being litigated in public.

## Medium roundup

### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[MCP 2026-07-28: Stateless core, enterprise authorization, and SDK betas](https://fmind.medium.com/mcp-2026-07-28-stateless-core-enterprise-authorization-and-sdk-betas-2646a980d594)** — Médéric Hurier (Fmind). Walks the imminent 07-28 spec: a stateless core (no handshake/session — any instance can serve any request), stabilized enterprise ID-JAG authorization, and beta SDKs across Python/TS/Go/C#.
- **[MCP Goes Session-less — What the 2026-07-28 Release Candidate Actually Changes](https://medium.com/@sainitesh/mcp-goes-session-less-what-the-2026-07-28-release-candidate-actually-changes-99b669ad1f61)** — Sai Nitesh. Zeroes in on the session-less shift in the release candidate — fungible servers, cache-control hints (ttlMs/cacheScope), and first-class extensions (Apps, Tasks) — and what it breaks vs. enables.

### generative-ai / large-language-models
- **[GPT-5.6 Isn't Smarter. It's Cheaper Per Task — And That Changes More](https://medium.com/@rajanTheSilentCompiler/gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-and-the-first-frontier-model-to-ship-on-the-governments-schedule-599479c713a7)** — Rajan Singh. Contrarian read: the GPT-5.6 story isn't raw IQ but cost-per-finished-task — matching prior flagships at a fraction of the tokens and price, and the first frontier model shipped on the government's access schedule.

### agentic-ai
- **[The 3 Loops That Break AI Agents in Production](https://odsc.medium.com/the-3-loops-that-break-ai-agents-in-production-fcfda14a7662)** — ODSC. Names three concrete failure loops — retry-the-same-strategy, unbounded tool loops, and silent broken-integration loops — that blow up token budgets and latency, with the guardrails (turn/tool budgets, stop conditions) that contain them.
- **[How I'd Build an AI Agent That Actually Survives Production](https://medium.com/@fahimulhaq/how-id-build-an-ai-agent-that-actually-survives-production-8d4889f45a65)** — Fahim ul Haq. Build-guide argument that agent reliability is a software-engineering problem, not a model one: bounded scope, tool budgets, permission boundaries, and human review before irreversible actions.

### rag
- **[10 RAG Shifts Redefining Production AI in 2026](https://medium.com/microsoftazure/10-rag-shifts-redefining-production-ai-in-2026-7acbdd66076c)** — Ozgur Guler (Microsoft Azure). Production-RAG shifts from an Azure engineer — moving retrieval past naive chunk-and-embed toward agentic, evaluated, and structured pipelines.

### ai-governance / responsible-ai
- **[The EU Just Put High-Risk AI Compliance on a New Timer](https://medium.com/@shaneculbertson/the-eu-just-put-high-risk-ai-compliance-on-a-new-timer-0bf0b24c9c56)** — Shane Culbertson. Explains the EU's June-2026 amendment that reset the high-risk timeline ahead of the Aug 2 obligations — what deployers actually have to show and when, minus the checklist-vendor hype.
