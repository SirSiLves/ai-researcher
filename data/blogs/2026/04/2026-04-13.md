## Long-form picks

- **The peril of laziness lost** — Bryan Cantrill (bcantrill.dtrace.org), quoted by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues LLMs "inherently lack the virtue of laziness" — work costs them nothing, so they happily pile garbage onto systems and skip the crisp abstractions humans build to save their own finite time; a sharp framing for why human laziness is load-bearing in good engineering. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/13/bryan-cantrill/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/13/bryan-cantrill/)

- **Steve Yegge on Google's internal AI adoption (plus Addy Osmani and Demis Hassabis rebuttals)** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Yegge claims Google looks like "John Deere" on agentic coding adoption (20% power users / 20% refusers / 60% Cursor-equivalent); Osmani says 40K+ Googler SWEs use agentic coding weekly with internal Antigravity/Gemini CLI/MCPs; Hassabis calls it clickbait. Useful snapshot of the agentic-adoption discourse during Opus 4.7 week. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/13/steve-yegge/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/13/steve-yegge/)

- **Exploring the new `servo` crate** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Walks through using Claude Code for web to wrap Servo's newly-released `servo` crate into a CLI screenshot tool (`servo-shot`) and a WebAssembly playground for `html5ever`; a worked example of agent-driven exploration of an unfamiliar Rust library. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/13/servo-crate-exploration/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/13/servo-crate-exploration/)

- **Import AI 453: Breaking AI agents; MirrorCode; and ten views on gradual disempowerment** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Shorter-than-usual issue (Clark was at Bilderberg) covering METR/Epoch's MirrorCode benchmark — evidence frontier models can already do some week-long coding tasks — and arguing capability progress over the past ~year accelerated above prior trend lines in domains like cyberoffense. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/04/13/import-ai-453-breaking-ai-agents-mirrorcode-and-ten-views-on-gradual-disempowerment/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/04/13/import-ai-453-breaking-ai-agents-mirrorcode-and-ten-views-on-gradual-disempowerment/)
