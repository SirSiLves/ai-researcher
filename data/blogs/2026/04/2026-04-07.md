Notable: unprompted, the model produced a pelican SVG **plus** a separate CSS animation in one HTML page; when the animation broke, a single follow-up prompt diagnosed the `transform`-override issue and shipped working `<animateTransform>`-based fixes. Strongest open-weights long-horizon demo since GLM-5. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/7/glm-51/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/7/glm-51/)
