## Long-form picks

- **The Axios supply chain attack used individually targeted social engineering** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Walks through the Axios team's postmortem (Jason Saayman's writeup) of the malware-dependency release: a sophisticated, individually-targeted social engineering campaign against one maintainer rather than a typo-squat or commodity attack. Important reference for the rising npm/PyPI threat model. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/supply-chain-social-engineering/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/supply-chain-social-engineering/)

- **Vulnerability Research Is Cooked** — Thomas Ptacek (sockpuppet.org), via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Ptacek argues frontier coding agents are about to step-function the economics of exploit dev: LLMs already encode the bug-class library and cross-codebase correlations, vuln research is exactly the implicit-search problem they're best at, and agents never get bored. Pairs with the Security Cryptography Whatever interview with Nicholas Carlini. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/vulnerability-research-is-cooked/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/vulnerability-research-is-cooked/)

- **AI security reports: from slop to real (Kroah-Hartman, Stenberg, Tarreau)** — three open-source maintainers, collected by Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Greg Kroah-Hartman, cURL's Daniel Stenberg, and HAProxy's Willy Tarreau all say the "AI slop" tsunami has turned, within roughly a month, into a tsunami of real, often duplicate, AI-assisted vulnerability reports — kernel security list has gone from 2–3/week to 5–10/day. Concrete on-the-ground confirmation of Ptacek's macro argument. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/greg-kroah-hartman/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/greg-kroah-hartman/) · [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/daniel-stenberg/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/daniel-stenberg/) · [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/willy-tarreau/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/3/willy-tarreau/)
