## Long-form picks

- **I think Anthropic and OpenAI have found product-market fit** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues April 2026 marks an inflection point where Claude Code / Codex revenue is materially landing in enterprise budgets — the frontier-lab business model has finally crystallized around coding agents rather than chat. [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/27/product-market-fit/)

- **sqlite AGENTS.md** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notes that the SQLite project has added an AGENTS.md file aimed at agents pointed at its codebase — a small but telling sign that the AGENTS.md convention is becoming the default for OSS projects courting coding agents. [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/27/sqlite-agents/)

- **The pressure** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Links Daniel Stenberg's note on the unprecedented load AI-assisted "security report" spam is placing on the curl team — a concrete data point in the broader debate about agents-as-bug-hunters vs. agents-as-noise-generators. [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/26/the-pressure/)

- **Notes on Pope Leo XIV's encyclical on AI** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Reads the new papal encyclical line-by-line for what it actually says about AI labor displacement and human dignity, beyond the headlines. A useful primary-source read for governance-watchers. [link](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/25/encyclical-on-ai/)

- **Some ideas for what comes next, May 2026** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects. _Takeaway:_ Lays out a bifurcation thesis: hyperscalers get bigger, niche businesses thrive on agent leverage, and the squeezed middle is knowledge workers without either talent-density or human-interface roles. 2026 will be the first year "without breaks." [link](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/some-ideas-for-what-comes-next-may)

- **Latest open artifacts (#21): Open model bonanza — Gemma 4, DeepSeek V4, Kimi K2.6, MiMo 2.5, GLM-5.1** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects. _Takeaway:_ Catalogs an unusually packed month of open-weight releases across every frontier open lab, with side-by-side capability notes. Useful as a primary index when planning what to evaluate. [link](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/latest-open-artifacts-21-open-model)

- **The Inference Shift** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Distinguishes "answer inference" (human-in-the-loop, latency-sensitive) from "agentic inference" (long-running, batchable) and argues the agentic regime reshapes who wins in chips, clouds and labs. Frames why semiconductor IPO windows are open right now. [link](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/)

- **The Deployment Company, Back to the 70s, Apple and Intel** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Reads OpenAI's "deployment company" pivot as the start of a broader frontier-lab move from foundation models to packaged labor — and notes other labs following the same playbook. [link](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-deployment-company-back-to-the-70s-apple-and-intel/)

- **Inside the 800VDC Revolution – Part 1** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Walks through NERC's May Level 3 Essential Actions Alert on hyperscaler load growth and the August deadline for response, plus the bottleneck that no vendor has UL-certified an SST for datacenter SST deployment yet. Concrete infrastructure-side reality check. [link](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/inside-the-800vdc-revolution-part)

- **Import AI 455: Automating AI Research** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ Tracks Anthropic's progress on an LLM-training-optimization benchmark (2.9× speedup with Opus 4 → 52× with Mythos Preview in April), uses it as a frame for OpenAI's "automated AI research intern by September" target, and asks how far engineering vs. research can actually be automated. [link](https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/)

- **Inside Anthropic's 2026 Developer Conference** — Dan Shipper, Every / Chain of Thought. _Takeaway:_ Synthesizes the Code w/ Claude announcements around Managed Agents, focusing on the "agents-monitoring-agents" framing — agents that detect when their own models or prompts are stale and upgrade themselves. [link](https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference)

- **AI's impact on software engineers in 2026: key trends, Part 2** — Gergely Orosz, The Pragmatic Engineer. _Takeaway:_ Real survey data on tradeoffs of AI tooling, why company-level adoption is much harder than individual adoption, and what's actually shifted in the last two years. Notably grounded in budget-pain anecdotes from working engineers. [link](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-impact-of-ai-on-software-engineers-2026)

- **How to Work and Compound with AI** — Eugene Yan. _Takeaway:_ A practitioner essay on building durable AI leverage in a daily workflow — the compounding habits that actually transfer, beyond one-off productivity wins. Eugene now writes from Anthropic Field <> Frontier, which adds context. [link](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/working-with-ai/)

### ai-agents
- **[AI Updates — May 15, 2026: Agents Become The New Operating Layer](https://medium.com/codex/ai-updates-may-15-2026-agents-become-the-new-operating-layer-ec9b8c72b3e5)** — alex buzunov (CodeX). ### agentic-ai
- **[Agentic Systems with Open-Source Models: A Financially Sane, Step-by-Step Architecture](https://medium.com/@roanmonteiro/agentic-systems-with-open-source-models-a-financially-sane-step-by-step-architecture-0d716bd66029)** — Roan Brasil Monteiro. 12-week roadmap for building agentic stacks on open-weight models, with explicit economics — addresses the "production agents are too expensive" objection head-on.

### rag
- **[Built a RAG System From Scratch and Finally Understood Why Everyone Is Talking About It](https://medium.com/@richelattafuah/built-a-rag-system-from-scratch-and-finally-understood-why-everyone-is-talking-about-it-9ad0100e2208)** — Richel Ohenewaa Attafuah. ### vector-database
- **[Vector Databases Are Being Oversold — I Ran the Numbers and the Truth Is Uncomfortable](https://medium.com/@kp9810113/vector-databases-are-being-oversold-i-ran-the-numbers-and-the-truth-is-uncomfortable-fe3453f9a984)** — The Concurrent Mind. - **[What Most Vector Databases Get Wrong About Ingestion — And What Databricks Gets Right](https://medium.com/towards-data-engineering/what-most-vector-databases-get-wrong-about-ingestion-and-what-databricks-gets-right-cf488c9e1903)** — Abhirup Pal. ### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[Model Context Protocol (MCP): Evolution, Capabilities, and the Rise of Peta](https://bytebridge.medium.com/model-context-protocol-mcp-evolution-capabilities-and-the-rise-of-peta-ff2967b45d48)** — ByteBridge. Tracks MCP from its 2024 origin to the 2026 stateless-protocol redesign and the new MCP Apps spec, with a clear timeline of which transports/headers ship in the July RC.

### ai-governance
- **[Why AI Governance Is Becoming the Most Important Enterprise Skill in 2026](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/why-ai-governance-is-becoming-the-most-important-enterprise-skill-in-2026-1bfdfc59cfdf)** — Manish Shivanandhan (Data Science Collective). Argues that org-side AI governance is overtaking model-side capability as the binding constraint on rollout — a counterweight to the model-progress-as-everything narrative.
- **[You've Been Governing AI for 30 Years. You Just Called It Something Else.](https://medium.com/@mattcollinsiq/youve-been-governing-ai-for-30-years-you-just-called-it-something-else-269e62a05823)** — Matt Collins. ### ai-engineering
- **[Harness Engineering for AI Agents in 2026](https://medium.com/@visrow/harness-engineering-for-ai-agents-in-2026-114fcb8edf9e)** — Vishal Mysore. Argues that the discipline that actually ships production AI is "harness engineering" — building rigid frameworks around the LLM, not improving the LLM itself.

### prompt-engineering
- **[The Complete Guide to Prompt Engineering in 2026](https://medium.com/@mike7830705/the-complete-guide-to-prompt-engineering-in-2026-b42f92a11ec7)** — 李明軒.
