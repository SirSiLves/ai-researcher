# AI Blogs — 2026-05-17

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **[AINews] Latest open artifacts (#21): Open model bonanza** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects (published May 16). _Takeaway:_ Within one month all open frontier labs — DeepSeek, Moonshot, Z.ai, MiMo, Google — shipped flagship refreshes (Gemma 4, DeepSeek V4, Kimi K2.6, MiMo 2.5, GLM-5.1). Lambert pairs the release map with the new Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) evaluation which concludes the gap between open models and the American frontier is *widening over time*, despite the headline pace. Useful counterweight to the "open is catching up" narrative pulled from Chinese release cadence alone. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/latest-open-artifacts-21-open-model](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/latest-open-artifacts-21-open-model)

- **Inside Anthropic's 2026 Developer Conference** — Every / Chain of Thought (last updated May 16). _Takeaway:_ Single best post-conference debrief on Code w/ Claude. Headline isn't the SpaceX/Colossus capacity deal (everyone covered that), it's the structural shift: doubled rate limits across Pro/Max, peak-hour throttles removed, API limits up ~17x on some tiers, plus Claude Managed Agents — the platform is becoming "an AI model with a harness and host computer, all scaled by the model company." Reads the announcements as Anthropic finally collapsing the dev-vs-capacity friction that fueled three months of complaints. [https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference](https://every.to/chain-of-thought/inside-anthropic-s-2026-developer-conference)

- **Inside the AI Index: 12 Takeaways from the 2026 Report** — Stanford HAI. _Takeaway:_ The numbers that will be quoted for the next 12 months: global corporate AI investment $581.7B in 2025 (+130% YoY), GenAI hit 53% global population adoption in three years (faster than the PC or the internet), U.S. consumer surplus from GenAI ~$172B/yr by early 2026 with median per-user value tripling YoY. Two awkward findings: the Foundation Model Transparency Index *dropped* from 58 to 40, and only 23% of the U.S. public expects AI to positively affect their work versus 73% of AI experts. [https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report)

- **Long Horizon Document Agents** — LlamaIndex blog. _Takeaway:_ Names what "agents from workflows to employees" actually requires: event triggers (redlines, edits, deadlines) instead of chat input, persistent task backlogs, and the unglamorous infra — parsing, extraction, indexing, citations, composable workflows — that becomes load-bearing the moment agents stop being synchronous. Practical rebuttal to the "the model will figure it out" framing of the Sequoia long-horizon-agents thesis. [https://www.llamaindex.ai/blog/long-horizon-document-agents](https://www.llamaindex.ai/blog/long-horizon-document-agents)

- **Devin 2.0** — Cognition (released May 13, still the freshest substantive piece). _Takeaway:_ Cognition reframes Devin as a multi-Devin agent-native IDE — Interactive Planning, Devin Search (cited codebase Q&A), Devin Wiki (auto-rebuilt every few hours with architecture diagrams) — at a $20 entry plan. Notable as a structural pivot away from the "one autonomous agent off doing your work" pitch toward parallel agents with explicit handoff points, mirroring the cognitive-load complaints from the past year of Devin field reports. [https://cognition.ai/blog/devin-2](https://cognition.ai/blog/devin-2)

- **The Pulse: Did capacity shortages turn Anthropic hostile to devs?** — Gergely Orosz, Pragmatic Engineer (May 7, still the canonical reference). _Takeaway:_ Ties together the "dumber Claude" complaints, the Claude Code paid-account access pulls, and the SpaceX capacity deal into one capacity-scarcity story — and notes Amazon's quiet reversal allowing Claude Code/Codex usage after the Kiro-internal-only period, plus Meta forcefully assigning 20–40% of engineers on several teams to data-labeling work ahead of cuts. Provides the field-level context for the Anthropic conference announcements above. [https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-did-capacity-shortages](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-did-capacity-shortages)

- **AI Agents Must Follow the Law** — Lawfare (May 15). _Takeaway:_ Before granting AI agents access to government workflows, the deployer needs a mechanism to verify the agent will obey law *even when instructed not to* — tort liability and fines after the fact don't scale to agentic deployments. Frames law-following AI (LFAI) as a deployment precondition rather than an alignment afterthought, and lays out which laws agents must follow, with what rigor, and how to handle legal uncertainty. The agent-governance counterpart to last week's federal-preemption EO coverage. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-agents-must-follow-the-law](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/ai-agents-must-follow-the-law)

- **Import AI 455: Automating AI Research** — Jack Clark (May 4, still the latest). _Takeaway:_ Anthropic's published numbers on automating AI engineering — Claude Opus 4 hit 2.9x mean speedup (May '25), Opus 4.5 16.5x (Nov '25), Opus 4.6 30x (Feb '26), Claude Mythos Preview 52x (Apr '26) — and OpenAI's stated goal of an "automated AI research intern by September 2026." Clark's argument: AI engineering is being automated; what remains genuinely uncertain is how much of AI *research* (vs engineering) collapses on the same curve. [https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/](https://jack-clark.net/2026/05/04/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research/)

### model-context-protocol / mcp
- **[Stop Hard-Coding AI Tools: The 2026 Guide to Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://medium.com/@kapildevkhatik2/stop-hard-coding-ai-tools-the-2026-guide-to-model-context-protocol-mcp-5d25fabff608)** — Kapil Khatik. - **[The Definitive 2026 Guide to Implementing MCP in Enterprise Environments](https://medium.com/cdata-software/the-definitive-2026-guide-to-implementing-mcp-in-enterprise-environments-d74009a17b07)** — CData Software. ### agent-protocols
- **[A2A Protocol v1 2026: How AI Agents Actually Talk to Each Other](https://medium.com/@richardhightower/a2a-protocol-v1-2026-how-ai-agents-actually-talk-to-each-other-c500079bca73)** — Rick Hightower (Towards AI). The most concrete current write-up of A2A v1 — Agent Cards, task lifecycle states, structured message exchange — with the clarifying frame: MCP is vertical (agent→tools), A2A is horizontal (agent↔agent), and they're complementary not competing.

### tool-use
- **[Building Agents in 2026: Why the Harness Matters More Than the Model](https://medium.com/@Micheal-Lanham/building-agents-in-2026-why-the-harness-matters-more-than-the-model-eb72448fee04)** — Micheal Lanham. Argues the model is rapidly becoming the commodity layer and the harness (tool registry, retry policies, observability, sandbox boundaries) is where competitive advantage now lives — the production-side counterpart to the harness-engineering thread on Medium this month.

### ai-governance
- **[Agentic AI Governance Frameworks 2026: Emerging Standards, Risks and Insights](https://medium.com/@giovannicoletta/agentic-ai-governance-frameworks-2026-risks-oversight-and-emerging-standards-c15bf2e6efca)** — Giovanni Coletta. Walks the Singapore IMDA Agentic AI governance framework (the first national-level one) plus how it maps to EU AI Act / NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001 — the regulatory companion to the Lawfare LFAI argument.

### ai-safety
- **[AI Safety & Alignment 2026: RLHF, Hallucination & EU AI Act](https://medium.com/@siddantvardey/ai-safety-alignment-the-problem-that-keeps-researchers-up-at-night-4544d851184c)** — Siddant Vardey. Synthesises why RLHF + Constitutional AI have meaningfully reduced harmful outputs while the capability/control gap *widens* — i.e. the alignment progress is real but is being lapped by capability progress, with Claude Mythos crossing the ASL-3 threshold as Exhibit A.

### responsible-ai
- **[OECD 2026: Responsible AI Due Diligence Is Now a Business Leadership Test](https://medium.com/@shaneculbertson/oecd-2026-responsible-ai-due-diligence-is-now-a-business-leadership-test-5d6615498bb7)** — Shane Culbertson.
