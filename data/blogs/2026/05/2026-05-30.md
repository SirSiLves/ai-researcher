## Medium roundup

### ai-agents / agentic-ai
- **[Harness Engineering for AI Agents in 2026](https://medium.com/@visrow/harness-engineering-for-ai-agents-in-2026-114fcb8edf9e)** — Vishal Mysore. Treats the LLM as a frozen utility and puts safety, execution accuracy, multi-step orchestration, and memory in the host harness — a concrete engineering discipline rather than a framework pitch.

### mcp / model-context-protocol
- **[How to Design a Production-Ready MCP Server — A Universal Blueprint](https://lakin-mohapatra.medium.com/how-to-design-a-production-ready-mcp-server-a-universal-blueprint-29437ad0cf2b)** — Lakin Mohapatra. A reusable blueprint for hardening MCP servers (containerization, health checks, graceful shutdown, OpenTelemetry observability) beyond the toy stdio demo.

### rag / retrieval-augmented-generation
- **[Modern RAG in 2026: The Components That Actually Matter](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/modern-rag-in-2026-the-components-that-actually-matter-3f6a138ef117)** — Anubhav (Data Science Collective). Argues against "RAG is dead" clickbait: most systems fail on retrieval quality, not the model, and lays out which components survive into the agentic/long-context era.

### ai-governance / responsible-ai
- **[Agentic Tool Sovereignty: The EU AI Act Problem Nobody Saw Coming](https://medium.com/@michael.hannecke/agentic-tool-sovereignty-the-eu-ai-act-problem-nobody-saw-coming-869325cd8352)** — Michael Hannecke. A non-obvious take on how the EU AI Act's August 2026 high-risk provisions collide with autonomous agents that pick and invoke their own tools at runtime.
