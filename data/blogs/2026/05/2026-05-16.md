# AI Blogs — 2026-05-16

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **[AINews] Everything is Conductor** — Latent.Space / smol.ai. _Takeaway:_ Sakana's 7B Conductor orchestrator-model — RL-trained to route subtasks across a pool of frontier workers in natural language — reportedly beats every single worker in its pool (83.9% LiveCodeBench, 87.5% GPQA-Diamond). Reads as the first credible "small router on top of big models" pattern with actual numbers, alongside the Codex Mobile launch and VS Code's new Agents window. [https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-everything-is-conductor](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-everything-is-conductor)

- **The Pulse: Forward deployed engineering heats up again** — Gergely Orosz, Pragmatic Engineer. _Takeaway:_ FDE job postings up 800%+ between Jan–Sep 2025 and OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, Databricks, Cohere, Ramp, Rippling, and Intercom are all building dedicated FDE functions. The new shape of "AI consultant" inside frontier labs — and the canary for where agent products still need humans on-site. [https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-forward-deployed-engineering](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-forward-deployed-engineering)

- **The Inference Shift** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Reads Cerebras' IPO repricing ($150–160/share, up from $115–125) as confirmation that the inference layer is becoming the second compute market — heterogeneous, not GPU-monoculture. Cerebras' wafer-scale design wins on inference latency precisely where agents now spend their token budget. [https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/)

- **AI and the Human Condition** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Even if AI does all the jobs, humans will still pay other humans to do labor _because_ it is labor — and zero-sum status competition won't disappear just because the material floor rises. A counter-thesis to the "abundance solves everything" framing of 2026 capex. [https://stratechery.com/2026/ai-and-the-human-condition/](https://stratechery.com/2026/ai-and-the-human-condition/)

- **Not so locked in any more** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ The Native-to-React-Native rewrite plus Bun's Zig-to-Rust migration argue that language/framework lock-in is no longer load-bearing once coding agents can re-platform a codebase. Decisions that used to be one-way doors are reversible. (Carried over from May 14 — still the freshest substantive Willison post as of this run.) [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/14/not-so-locked-in/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/14/not-so-locked-in/)

- **How open model ecosystems compound** — Nathan Lambert, Interconnects. _Takeaway:_ Most of the compute to build a leading frontier model goes to R&D, not the final training run — which is why Chinese open-model labs (sharing infra knowledge across releases) compound faster than their nominal flop budgets suggest. Reframes the "open weights are catching up" debate as a structural cost-of-experimentation argument. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/how-open-model-ecosystems-compound](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/how-open-model-ecosystems-compound)

- **AI Legislative Update: May 15, 2026** — Transparency Coalition. _Takeaway:_ Colorado sent four AI bills to the governor's desk before adjourning; Georgia signed an AI chatbot safety bill into law; most California AI bills survived suspense file. Useful state-level companion to the federal-only Lawfare coverage — May 2026 is when state AI lawmaking moves from drafts to signed law. [https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-may15-2026](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-may15-2026)

- **AI Update, May 15, 2026: AI News and Views From the Past Week** — MarketingProfs. _Takeaway:_ Tracks Google embedding Gemini deeper into Chrome / Android keyboards, leaks of a "Gemini Omni" video-gen system ahead of I/O 2026, HubSpot's new AEO Sensor dashboard for tracking ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity referral volatility, and OpenAI building the consent/jurisdiction plumbing to launch ChatGPT ads in Europe. The deployment-layer angle on the same week's model news. [https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54786/ai-update-may-15-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-week](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54786/ai-update-may-15-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-week)

### generative-ai
- **[The AI Roundup: Everything That Matters This Week — May 15, 2026](https://medium.com/@kayceEagan/the-ai-roundup-everything-that-matters-this-week-may-15-2026-0aecb75a558c)** — KLynn Eagan. ### ai-agents
- **[Context Engineering for AI Agents: Complete Course](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/context-engineering-for-ai-agents-complete-course-fd509e276a9e)** — Marina Wyss (Data Science Collective). - **[How to Build Production-Ready AI Agents: MCP, CLI, and Skills — the Right Tool for the Right Job](https://medium.com/agentic-builders/how-to-build-production-ready-ai-agents-mcp-cli-and-skills-the-right-tool-for-the-right-job-701dc102863f)** — Ana Bildea, PhD (Agentic Builders). ### agentic-ai

### rag / retrieval-augmented-generation
- **[Most Companies Doing GenAI Are Really Just Doing RAG: RAGOps Explained for analysts](https://medium.com/@squalliahmed/most-companies-doing-genai-are-really-just-doing-rag-ragops-explained-for-analysts-31e0933ac70f)** — Ahmed Squalli.
