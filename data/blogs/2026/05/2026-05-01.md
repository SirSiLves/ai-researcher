## Long-form picks

- **AI Value Capture — The Shift To Model Labs** — SemiAnalysis. _Takeaway:_ Big monthly piece: "A day in AI now feels like a year in any other industry." Walks through TSMC and Vera Rubin VR NVL72 to argue the value-capture center of gravity is moving away from neocloud middlemen and toward the frontier model labs themselves — the structural argument behind the Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI multi-GW deals dominating May. [https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/ai-value-capture-the-shift-to-model](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/ai-value-capture-the-shift-to-model)

- **Tool: iNaturalist Sightings** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Notable not for the bird-watching content but as a fully worked "Claude Code for web on a phone" build — Git-scraping pipeline + browser app via CORS — built while camping. Canonical example of the agent-coded-on-mobile pattern Simon's been documenting. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/1/inat-sightings/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/1/inat-sightings/)

- **[AINews] Agents for Everything Else: Codex for Knowledge Work, Claude for Creative Work** — Latent.Space (smol AI). _Takeaway:_ Frames the de-facto market split crystallizing this week — OpenAI Codex positioning for knowledge work and devtools, Anthropic Claude leaning into creative work (and the Anthropic "Claude for Creative Work" launch on Apr 28 is the explicit signal). Useful one-line summary of where the labs are differentiating product-wise. [https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-agents-for-everything-else](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-agents-for-everything-else)

- **2026.18: Long-term, Peripheral & Myopic Visions** — Ben Thompson (Stratechery). _Takeaway:_ Weekly digest pulling together the week-of-April-27 Stratechery threads — Amazon and AI (Trainium / inference shift), the future of AR devices (Meta Display), and Beijing's myopia. Good index post if you want one link covering Stratechery for the week. [https://stratechery.com/2026/long-term-peripheral-myopic-visions/](https://stratechery.com/2026/long-term-peripheral-myopic-visions/)

- **Executive Branch AI and the Rule of Law: An Emerging Research Agenda** — Lawfare. _Takeaway:_ Argues that frontier AI deployment inside the executive branch (ExecAI) is an under-studied rule-of-law problem; lays out a research agenda — agentic systems first, then non-obvious functions beyond law-enforcement and lethal-force — and warns that government adoption is slow but the failure modes will be specific. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/executive-branch-ai-and-the-rule-of-law--an-emerging-research-agenda](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/executive-branch-ai-and-the-rule-of-law--an-emerging-research-agenda)

- **Kicking the Tires: A Voluntary Path to Pre-deployment AI Vetting** — Lawfare / Foundation for American Innovation. _Takeaway:_ Concrete policy proposal in the wake of the Mythos cyber-capability scare: while the White House can't compel labs into a mandatory review, existing CISA authorities support a 2–3-week voluntary "kick the tires" window before general deployment of frontier models. [https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/kicking-the-tires--a-voluntary-path-to-pre-deployment-ai-vetting](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/kicking-the-tires--a-voluntary-path-to-pre-deployment-ai-vetting)

- **Anthropic — Personal Guidance: How people ask Claude for life advice** — Anthropic Research (released this week). _Takeaway:_ Ahead of Simon Willison's May 3 quote-pull, the underlying paper sampled ~639k unique-user conversations from March–April 2026 and used a sycophancy classifier to land the headline numbers (9% overall, 38% in spirituality, 25% in relationships) plus a synthetic-data fix that halved the rate in Opus 4.7. [https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance)

- **Beijing Is Not Playing the Long Game** — Sharp Text (Stratechery Plus extension), Andrew Sharp. _Takeaway:_ Argues Beijing's recent threats are not strategic patience but acute pressure: foreign investment has fallen all decade, ~55% of Chinese oil moves through the Strait of Hormuz, and the regime can't afford to tolerate trade barriers stacking up. Useful macro context for the AI-export-controls debate. [https://sharptext.net/2026/beijing-is-not-playing-the-long-game/](https://sharptext.net/2026/beijing-is-not-playing-the-long-game/)
