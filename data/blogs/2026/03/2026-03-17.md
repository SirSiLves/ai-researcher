# AI Blogs — 2026-03-17

_Collector: ai-blogs. Slice: long-form blogs + Medium tags._

## Long-form picks

- **Subagents** — Simon Willison (Agentic Engineering Patterns ch.3). _Takeaway:_ Frames subagents as the practical answer to the stagnant ~1M token context ceiling: hand off bounded tasks so the top-level agent's context stays clean. [https://simonwillison.net/guides/agentic-engineering-patterns/subagents/](https://simonwillison.net/guides/agentic-engineering-patterns/subagents/)
- **GPT-5.4 mini and GPT-5.4 nano, which can describe 76,000 photos for $52** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Walks through OpenAI's new GPT-5.4 mini/nano, with a back-of-envelope pricing exercise showing nano can caption ~76k photos for ~$52. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/17/mini-and-nano/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/17/mini-and-nano/)
- **Give Django your time and money, not your tokens** — Tim Schilling (better-simple.com), via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Argues that if you don't understand the ticket, the solution, or the review feedback, leaning on an LLM to contribute to Django actively hurts the project — open-source is a communal endeavour and "the facade of a human" is demoralizing for reviewers. [https://www.better-simple.com/django/2026/03/16/give-django-your-time-and-money/](https://www.better-simple.com/django/2026/03/16/give-django-your-time-and-money/)
- **Python 3.15's JIT is now back on track** — Ken Jin. _Takeaway:_ CPython JIT hits its perf goals a year early on macOS AArch64 (~11–12% over the tail-calling interpreter) and a few months early on x86_64 Linux (~5–6% over the standard interpreter). [https://fidget-spinner.github.io/posts/jit-on-track.html](https://fidget-spinner.github.io/posts/jit-on-track.html)

- **An Interview with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang About Accelerated Computing** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ In-person interview at the close of GTC 2026: Huang pitches CUDA 20-years-on as the foundation for entering new industries (Excel, Photoshop, logic synthesis) — AI super-accelerates the human tools, then agentically uses them. Includes the Nvidia/Groq acquisition framing. [https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-about-accelerated-computing/](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-about-accelerated-computing/)

- **Agents Over Bubbles** — Ben Thompson, Stratechery. _Takeaway:_ Published the morning of Nvidia GTC: Thompson lands on "no, we're not in a bubble" — agentic inference reshapes the demand curve for compute, and the three LLM inflection points (ChatGPT 2022, o1 2024, agents 2026) justify the capex. [https://stratechery.com/2026/agents-over-bubbles/](https://stratechery.com/2026/agents-over-bubbles/)
