## Top picks

- **A Survey on the Security of Long-Term Memory in LLM Agents: Toward Mnemonic Sovereignty** — Zehao Lin, Chunyu Li. _Takeaway:_ Surveys persistent-memory threats — poisoning, unauthorized access, cross-session corruption — and proposes "mnemonic sovereignty" as a verifiable governance frame across write/storage/retrieval/execution/sharing/deletion. Pairs naturally with STALE (2605.06527) and the SSGM line — a structural input for any team thinking about agent-memory governance. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.16548. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16548)
- **SafeDream: Safety World Model for Proactive Early Jailbreak Detection** — Bo Yan, Weikai Lin. _Takeaway:_ Lightweight external safety world model that combines statistical detection and contrastive analysis to flag multi-turn jailbreaks ~1.0–1.2 conversation turns before compliance. Concrete defense-side counterpart to ICD/CRA — moves jailbreak defense from per-turn refusal to trajectory-level prediction. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.16824. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16824)
- **SuperLocalMemory V3.3: The Living Brain — Biologically-Inspired Forgetting, Cognitive Quantization, and Multi-Channel Retrieval for Zero-LLM Agent Memory Systems** — Varun Pratap Bhardwaj. _Takeaway:_ Local-first agent memory with adaptive forgetting, quantization-aware distance metrics, and multi-channel retrieval — explicitly avoids cloud LLM dependence. Niche but interesting position paper for the on-device-agent thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.04514. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04514)
- **How to Fine-Tune a Reasoning Model? A Teacher-Student Cooperation Framework to Synthesize Student-Consistent SFT Data** — Zixian Huang, Kaichen Yang. _Takeaway:_ TESSY: teacher and student cooperate to synthesize SFT data that preserves the student's writing style while inheriting the teacher's reasoning, fixing the "stylistic mismatch" failure mode of distillation. Concrete recipe for the SFT-from-stronger-models cluster. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14164. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14164)
- **Cyber Defense Benchmark: Agentic Threat Hunting Evaluation for LLMs in SecOps** — Alankrit Chona, Igor Kozlov. _Takeaway:_ 106 real attack procedures from OTRF Security-Datasets, 86 MITRE ATT&CK sub-techniques, wrapped as a Gymnasium RL environment. Even Claude Opus 4.6 only flags 3.8% of malicious events on average — sharp reality check on agentic LLMs in SecOps. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.19533. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19533)
- **MASS-RAG: Multi-Agent Synthesis Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Xingchen Xiao, Heyan Huang. _Takeaway:_ Multi-agent RAG with role-specialized agents for evidence summarization, extraction, and reasoning — pre-synthesizes complementary information across retrieved contexts before final answer. Fits the agentic-RAG cluster the SoK survey is consolidating. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.18509. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18509)

## Other interesting items

- **[Agent-World: Scaling Real-World Environment Synthesis for Evolving General Agent Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18292)** — Guanting Dong, Junting Lu. Self-evolving training arena that synthesizes realistic environments at adjustable difficulty for general-agent training; agents and environments co-evolve through identified capability gaps.
- **[MCPThreatHive: Automated Threat Intelligence for Model Context Protocol Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13849)** — Yi Ting Shen, Kentaroh Toyoda. Open-source platform that automates the MCP threat-intelligence lifecycle (collection → AI-driven extraction/classification → knowledge graph) and operationalizes the MCP-38 threat taxonomy.
- **[Single-Agent LLMs Outperform Multi-Agent Systems on Multi-Hop Reasoning Under Equal Thinking Token Budgets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02460)** — Dat Tran, Douwe Kiela (surfaces in cohort). Information-theoretic argument plus empirical results: when reasoning-token budgets are held equal, single-agent setups consistently match or beat multi-agent on multi-hop reasoning.
