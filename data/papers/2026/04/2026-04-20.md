# AI Papers — 2026-04-20

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **GTA-2: Benchmarking General Tool Agents from Atomic Tool-Use to Open-Ended Workflows** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Successor to GTA that spans atomic single-tool invocation through to fully open-ended workflows. Useful eval surface as tool-using agents under MCP/A2A move from narrow benchmarks toward real workflows. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.15715 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15715)

- **The Amazing Agent Race: Strong Tool Users, Weak Navigators** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Dissects current tool-using agents and finds they're strong at tool *use* but weak at *navigation* — i.e., the planning layer is the limiting factor, not the tool-calling primitives. Useful diagnostic for agent-platform investment. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.10261 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10261)

- **Mind DeepResearch Technical Report** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Technical report for a deep-research agent — practical-system writeup that the deep-research benchmark conversation has been short on. Companion to DR^3-Eval (Apr 17). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.14518 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14518)

- **Where Does Output Diversity Collapse in Post-Training?** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Localizes the stage(s) of post-training where output diversity collapses — important diagnostic given the MEDS (Apr 14) and broader concern that RL post-training narrows the policy distribution. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.16027 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16027)

- **Cut Your Losses! Learning to Prune Paths Early for Efficient Parallel Reasoning** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Early-pruning policy for parallel-reasoning trajectories — directly aimed at the cost economics of best-of-N / parallel-scaling decoding. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.16029 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16029](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16029)

- **Qwen3.5-Omni Technical Report** — Qwen team (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Next-iteration omni-modal model from Alibaba — extends the Qwen3 line with unified audio/image/text/video. Open-weights frontier multimodal continues to push. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.15804 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15804)

- **(1D) Ordered Tokens Enable Efficient Test-Time Search** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Argues that imposing a 1D ordering on tokens enables far more efficient test-time search than current tree-of-thought / parallel exploration patterns. Structural angle on inference-time scaling. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.15453 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15453)

- **Web Retrieval-Aware Chunking (W-RAC) for Efficient and Cost-Effective RAG** — anonymous (cs.IR submission, v-update Apr 17). _Takeaway:_ Chunking policy aware of how retrieval will actually be used downstream — practical RAG plumbing optimization that's complementary to byte-exact dedup (Apr 27 ref). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.04936 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04936](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04936)

- **PersonaVLM: Long-Term Personalized Multimodal LLMs** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Long-term personalization for multimodal LLMs — a probe of whether persona/memory mechanisms ported from text generalize to multimodal interaction state. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13074 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13074)

- **QuantCode-Bench: Benchmarking LLMs' Ability to Generate Quantitative Code** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Vertical coding-benchmark for quant/numerical workloads where small numerical bugs are the failure mode — addresses a long-tail gap in coding-agent eval. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.15151 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15151)

## Other interesting items

- **[DiPO: Disentangled Perplexity Policy Optimization for Fine-grained Exploration-Exploitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13902)** — cs.LG. PPO variant that disentangles the perplexity signal to balance exploration/exploitation explicitly.
- **[Can Large Language Models Reinvent Foundational Algorithms?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05716)** — cs.AI. Probe of whether LLMs can re-derive foundational algorithms from scratch — a sharper test than "can they implement what's in the training corpus."
- **[PRL-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark Evaluating LLMs' Capabilities in Frontier Physics Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15411)** — cs.AI. Physics-research benchmark beyond textbook problems — useful for the AI-scientist eval thread.
- **[Universal Statistical Signatures of Evolution in Artificial Intelligence Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10571)** — cs.LG. Looks for universal evolutionary signatures across AI architectures over time — interesting meta-science angle.
