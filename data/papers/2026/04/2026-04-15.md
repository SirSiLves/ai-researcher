# AI Papers — 2026-04-15

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **KnowRL: Boosting LLM Reasoning via RL with Minimal-Sufficient Knowledge** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Argues current RL post-training over-rewards verbose chains; KnowRL grounds reward at the minimal-sufficient knowledge unit needed to derive the answer. Sharp counterpoint to "longer CoT = better" — relevant to the test-time-compute thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12627 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12627)

- **Rethinking On-Policy Distillation of LLMs: Phenomenology, Mechanism, and Practice** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Systematic study of when on-policy distillation actually helps vs. when it's noise — phenomenology + mechanism + practical guidance. Important for teams deploying student models from frontier teachers. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13016 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13016)

- **Toward Autonomous Long-Horizon Engineering for ML Research** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ End-to-end autonomous ML research agent targeting genuinely long-horizon engineering rather than narrow benchmark wins — the "AI scientist" thread continues to mature. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.13018 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13018)

- **The Blind Spot of Agent Safety: How Benign User Instructions Expose Critical Vulnerabilities** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Even completely benign user instructions can expose tool-using agents to attacks via untrusted intermediate content. A structural critique of safety frameworks that assume adversarial intent lives at the user-prompt boundary. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.10577 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10577)

- **Towards Long-horizon Agentic Multimodal Search** — anonymous (cs.IR submission). _Takeaway:_ Agentic search across multimodal corpora extended to long-horizon trajectories — a multimodal companion to the text-only deep-research thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12890 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12890)

- **Many-Tier Instruction Hierarchy in LLM Agents** — anonymous (cs.AI submission). _Takeaway:_ Generalizes the system/developer/user instruction hierarchy to many tiers — directly relevant to MCP/A2A agent stacks where instructions can arrive from heterogeneous principals with different trust levels. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09443 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09443)

- **CONSCIENTIA: Can LLM Agents Learn to Strategize? Emergent Deception and Trust in a Multi-Agent Setting** — anonymous (cs.MA submission). _Takeaway:_ Tracks emergence of strategic deception and trust dynamics in multi-agent LLM populations — a behavioral-eval probe complementary to formal protocol work. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.09746 (cs.MA). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09746)

- **LASA: Language-Agnostic Semantic Alignment at the Semantic Bottleneck for LLM Safety** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Aligns safety at a language-agnostic semantic bottleneck rather than per-language post-hoc filters — addresses the long-tail of multilingual jailbreaks. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12710 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12710)

- **You Only Judge Once: Multi-response Reward Modeling in a Single Forward Pass** — anonymous (cs.LG submission). _Takeaway:_ Reward model that scores multiple candidate responses in one forward pass instead of independent passes — substantial throughput win for RM-heavy pipelines (RLHF, best-of-N, judge-as-eval). _Category:_ arXiv:2604.10966 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10966)

- **Accelerating Speculative Decoding with Block Diffusion Draft Trees** — anonymous (cs.CL submission). _Takeaway:_ Uses a block-diffusion drafter to propose tree-structured candidate continuations for speculative decoding. Companion to DFlash/Block-Diffusion-SD on the radar — block-diffusion drafters keep landing real speedups. _Category:_ arXiv:2604.12989 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12989)

## Other interesting items

- **[SPPO: Sequence-Level PPO for Long-Horizon Reasoning Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08865)** — cs.LG. PPO adapted for sequence-level advantages on long reasoning chains — alternative to GRPO-style baselines.
- **[Lightning OPD: Efficient Post-Training for Large Reasoning Models with Offline On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13010)** — cs.LG. Offline on-policy distillation that aims at reasoning models specifically — a practical efficiency angle on the on-policy-distillation thread.
- **[Parcae: Scaling Laws For Stable Looped Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12946)** — cs.LG. Scaling laws specifically for looped/recurrent transformers — useful data for the recurrent-vs-feedforward architecture debate.
- **[When Reasoning Models Hurt Behavioral Simulation: A Solver-Sampler Mismatch in Multi-Agent Settings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11840)** — cs.MA. Reasoning models *worsen* certain behavioral-simulation tasks; argues for a solver/sampler distinction in multi-agent eval.
- **[ClawGUI: A Unified Framework for Training, Evaluating, and Deploying GUI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11784)** — cs.AI. Unified GUI-agent harness — concrete framework contribution as GUI-agent deployment ramps.
