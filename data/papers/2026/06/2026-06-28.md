**OPID: On-Policy Skill Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning** — Shuo Yang, Jinyang Wu, Zhengxi Lu, Yuhao Shen et al. _Takeaway:_ Adds dense token-level on-policy self-distillation on top of sparse outcome rewards to tell language agents which intermediate decisions to reinforce. _Category:_ 2606.26790. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26790)

**The Verification Horizon: No Silver Bullet for Coding Agent Rewards** — Binghai Wang, Chenlong Zhang, Dayiheng Liu, Jiajun Zhang et al. _Takeaway:_ Argues the classic "verifying is easier than generating" intuition is inverting for modern coding agents, with consequences for reward design. _Category:_ 2606.26300. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26300)

**Why Multi-Step Tool-Use Reinforcement Learning Collapses and How Supervisory Signals Fix It** — Yupu Hao, Zhuoran Jin, Huanxuan Liao, Kang Liu et al. _Takeaway:_ Diagnoses instability in agentic RL for multi-step tool use and shows added supervisory signals stabilize training. _Category:_ 2606.26027. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26027)

**GUI vs. CLI: Execution Bottlenecks in Screen-Only and Skill-Mediated Computer-Use Agents** — Xiao Zhou, Siyue Zhang, Yilun Zhao, Jinbiao Wei et al. _Takeaway:_ A matched-execution benchmark that cleanly separates interaction modality from task differences for computer-use agents. _Category:_ 2606.24551. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24551)

**CoffeeBench: Benchmarking Long-Horizon LLM Agents in Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Economies** — Issa Sugiura, Daichi Hattori, Kazuo Araragi, Keita Ogawa et al. _Takeaway:_ Moves agent evaluation beyond single-agent-vs-passive-environment to long-horizon multi-agent economic systems. _Category:_ 2606.16613. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16613)

**When Does Combining Language Models Help? A Co-Failure Ceiling on Routing, Voting, and Mixture-of-Agents Across 67 Frontier Models** — Josef Chen. _Takeaway:_ Derives a hard accuracy ceiling for multi-model systems (routing/voting/cascades/MoA) driven by an under-reported co-failure quantity. _Category:_ 2606.27288. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27288)

**Running the Gauntlet: Re-evaluating the Capabilities of Agents Beyond Familiar Environments** — Mykola Vysotskyi, Runqi Lin, Grzegorz Biziel, Michal Zakrzewski et al. _Takeaway:_ Stress-tests agentic systems on unfamiliar environments where popular-app benchmarks overstate capability. _Category:_ 2606.14397. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14397)

**Neglected Free Lunch from Post-training: Progress Advantage for LLM Agents** — Changdae Oh, Wendi Li, Seongheon Park, Samuel Yeh et al. _Takeaway:_ Builds step-level process reward signals for long-horizon agents where human annotation and Monte Carlo estimation are infeasible. _Category:_ 2606.26080. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26080)

**Information-Aware KV Cache Compression for Long Reasoning** — Jushi Kai, Zhuiri Xiao, Alexandra Birch, Zhouhan Lin. _Takeaway:_ Moves beyond attention-weight heuristics to compress KV cache for long-reasoning LLMs at prefill and decode. _Category:_ 2606.26875. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26875)

**JetSpec: Breaking the Scaling Ceiling of Speculative Decoding with Parallel Tree Drafting** — Lanxiang Hu, Zhaoxiang Feng, Yulun Wu, Haoran Yuan et al. _Takeaway:_ Parallel tree drafting pushes speculative decoding past the acceptance/overhead scaling ceiling for LLM inference. _Category:_ 2606.18394. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18394)

**Paved with True Intents: Intent-Aware Training Improves LLM Safety Classification** — (HF Papers / papers.cool listing). _Takeaway:_ Intent-aware training improves LLM safety classification robustness across training regimes. _Category:_ 2606.27210. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27210)
