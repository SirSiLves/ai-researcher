# AI Papers — 2026-06-16

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Nemotron 3 Ultra: Open, Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Hybrid Mamba-Transformer for Agentic Reasoning** — NVIDIA. _Takeaway:_ 550B-total / 55B-active MoE hybrid Mamba-Attention model pre-trained on 20T tokens, extended to 1M-token context, post-trained with SFT + RL + multi-teacher on-policy distillation — a major open frontier-scale release targeting agentic reasoning. _Category:_ 2606.15007. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15007)

**VibeThinker-3B: Exploring the Frontier of Verifiable Reasoning in Small Language Models** — Sen Xu et al. (HF trending). _Takeaway:_ Pushes verifiable reasoning into the strictly-small (3B dense) regime via a "Spectrum-to-Signal" post-training paradigm, probing how far compact models can go on checkable reasoning tasks. _Category:_ 2606.16140. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16140)

**OpenClaw-Skill: Collective Skill Tree Search for Agentic LLMs** — Tianyi Lin et al. _Takeaway:_ Auto-constructs reusable agent skills via Collective Skill Tree Search, improving tool use, multi-step reasoning, and dynamic environment interaction without hand-crafted skill libraries. _Category:_ 2606.16774. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16774)

**PathRouter: Aligning Rewards with Retrieval Quality in Agentic Graph RAG** — Bo Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Tackles "answer-path reward aliasing" in agentic GraphRAG, where outcome-only RL rewards shortcuts instead of useful retrieval — aligning rewards to actual retrieval quality. _Category:_ 2606.16409. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16409)

**RL-Index: Reinforcement Learning for Retrieval Index Reasoning** — Yongjia Lei et al. _Takeaway:_ Moves reasoning into the retrieval index itself (not just the query side) so RAG can surface evidence requiring implicit, multi-step reasoning beyond surface semantic/lexical matching. _Category:_ 2606.16316. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16316)

**DEEPRUBRIC: Evidence-Tree Rubric Supervision for RL of Deep Research Agents** — Minghang Zhu et al. _Takeaway:_ Builds evidence-tree rubrics that reliably capture task scope and evidence needs, improving the efficiency of rubric-based RL for long-form deep-research report agents. _Category:_ 2606.17029. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17029)

**Beyond Monolingual Deep Research: Cross-Lingual BrowseComp-Plus** — Yuheng Lu et al. _Takeaway:_ New benchmark testing whether deep-research agents and retrievers can find and reason over evidence written in a different language than the query — a realistic gap most browsing benchmarks ignore. _Category:_ 2606.15345. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15345)

**Control-Plane Placement Shapes Forgetting: An Architectural Study of Agent Memory** — Dongxu Yang. _Takeaway:_ Compares 13 agent-memory configurations on a 385-case adversarial surface, showing that where the LLM sits between the recall plane and the (largely untested) control plane that supersedes/purges facts determines which forgetting failures it recovers from. _Category:_ 2606.15903. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15903)

**TokenPilot: Cache-Efficient Context Management for LLM Agents** — Buqiang Xu et al. (HF trending). _Takeaway:_ Context-management scheme for long-horizon agents that prunes/evicts tokens without breaking KV-cache layouts, cutting inference cost where naive pruning forces expensive recompute. _Category:_ 2606.17016. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17016)

**CoffeeBench: Long-Horizon LLM Agents in Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Economies** — Issa Sugiura et al. _Takeaway:_ Evaluates autonomous agents that must communicate, negotiate, and transact while pursuing competing objectives in a multi-agent economic system — beyond the single-agent-vs-passive-environment benchmark norm. _Category:_ 2606.16613. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16613)

**Misinformation Propagation in Benign Multi-Agent Systems** — Jonas Becker et al. _Takeaway:_ Shows how a single agent reasoning from incorrect tool-call context can propagate errors through turn-based interaction in high-stakes (medical, legal, forensic) multi-agent setups — a reliability/safety failure mode. _Category:_ 2606.16710. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16710)

**MIRAGE: Auditing Anti-Muslim Bias in Frontier LLMs Across Reasoning, Agentic, and Time-Coupled Conditions** — Noor Islam S. Mohammad, Tamim Sheikh. _Takeaway:_ 1,200-prompt benchmark moving bias auditing past single-turn completion into deployment-realistic direct, reasoning, and agentic conditions where frontier models actually operate. _Category:_ 2606.16562. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16562)

- **[Metric Match: A Subset Selection Approach to Evaluating LLM Judge Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15029)** — Alyssa Unell et al., estimates LLM-judge reliability metrics from limited human annotations.
- **[AuAu: A Benchmark for Auditing Authoritarian Alignment in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16127)** — Andreas Einwiller et al., assesses whether models exhibit or promote authoritarian tendencies.
- **[BadWorld: Adversarial Attacks on World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16519)** — Linghui Shen et al. (HF trending), shows visual world models are vulnerable to perturbations standard adversarial attacks miss.
