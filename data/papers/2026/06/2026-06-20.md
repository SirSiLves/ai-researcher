# AI Papers — 2026-06-20

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**ProvenanceGuard: Source-Aware Factuality Verification for MCP-Based LLM Agents** — A. Alvarez, S. Rajan, S. Mugel, R. Orús. _Takeaway:_ Adds source-attribution and factuality verification to agents wired through the Model Context Protocol, directly targeting hallucination in MCP tool-call pipelines. _Category:_ 2606.18037 (cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18037)

**TokenPilot: Cache-Efficient Context Management for LLM Agents** — B. Xu, Z. Xue, D. Chen, et al. (LightMem series). _Takeaway:_ Context-management layer that keeps KV-cache reuse high across multi-step agent runs, cutting token cost without losing relevant history. _Category:_ 2606.17016 (cs.CL/cs.AI/cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17016)

**Oblivion: Self-Adaptive Agentic Memory Control through Decay-Driven Activation** — A. Rana, C.-C. Hung, Q. Sun, J. M. Kunkel, C. Lawrence (NEC). _Takeaway:_ Decay-driven activation lets agent memory forget and re-surface items adaptively, a concrete mechanism for long-horizon agent memory. _Category:_ 2604.00131 (cs.CL/cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00131)

**Search-P1: Path-Centric Reward Shaping for Stable and Efficient Agentic RAG Training** — T. Xia, M. Xu, L. Hu, et al. _Takeaway:_ Reward shaping over retrieval paths stabilizes RL training of agentic RAG systems, addressing reward sparsity in search-and-reason loops. _Category:_ 2602.22576 (cs.CL/cs.IR/cs.LG). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22576)

**SmartChunk Retrieval: Query-Aware Chunk Compression with Planning for Efficient Document RAG** — X. Zhang, K. Goswami, S. Oymak, J. Chen, N. Lipka. _Takeaway:_ Query-aware chunk compression plus a planning step trims retrieved context for document RAG while preserving answer quality. _Category:_ 2602.22225 (cs.IR/cs.AI/cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22225)

**Revisiting RAG Retrievers: An Information Theoretic Benchmark** — W. Zheng, D. Kalaev, N. Fatsi, et al. (Capital One). _Takeaway:_ Information-theoretic benchmark that re-evaluates what RAG retrievers actually contribute, a useful evaluation lens beyond standard recall metrics. _Category:_ 2602.21553 (cs.IR/cs.AI/cs.LG). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21553)

**BeSafe-Bench: Unveiling Behavioral Safety Risks of Situated Agents in Functional Environments** — Y. Li, Y. Lin, P. Wang, S. Liu, X. Wei. _Takeaway:_ 13 agents evaluated across Web/Mobile/Embodied; even the best completes <40% of tasks while fully respecting safety constraints — strong task performance often coincides with safety violations. _Category:_ 2603.25747 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25747)

**Your Agent Has a Genome: Sequence-Level Behavioral Analysis and Runtime Governance of LLM-Powered Autonomous Agents** — S. Deng. _Takeaway:_ Treats agent action traces as sequences ("genomes") for behavioral analysis and runtime governance — a concrete governance/observability primitive for autonomous agents. _Category:_ 2606.15579 (cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15579)

**AIRA_2: Overcoming Bottlenecks in AI Research Agents** — K. Hambardzumyan, N. Baldwin, E. Toledo, et al. (Meta). _Takeaway:_ Async multi-GPU worker pool + Hidden Consistent Evaluation + ReAct operators push MLE-bench-30 to 76.0% at 72h; argues prior "overfitting" was evaluation noise, not memorization. _Category:_ 2603.26499 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26499)

**Finding and Reactivating Post-Trained LLMs' Hidden Safety Mechanisms** — M. Li, W. M. Si, M. Backes, Y. Zhang, Y. Wang. _Takeaway:_ Locates latent safety circuits that post-training suppresses and shows they can be reactivated — relevant to alignment robustness and jailbreak defense. _Category:_ 2604.00012 (cs.CL/cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00012)
