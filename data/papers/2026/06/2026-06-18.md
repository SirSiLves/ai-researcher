# AI Papers — 2026-06-18

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**CEO-Bench: Can Agents Play the Long Game?** — Haozhe Chen, Karthik Narasimhan, Zhuang Liu. _Takeaway:_ Long-horizon agent benchmark that simulates operating a startup for 500 days — pricing, marketing, budgeting, orchestrating moving parts under uncertainty — probing capabilities short-horizon SWE/customer-service benchmarks miss. _Category:_ 2606.18543 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18543)

**SproutRAG: Attention-Guided Tree Search with Progressive Embeddings for Long-Document RAG** — Amirhossein Abaskohi, Issam H. Laradji, Peter West, Giuseppe Carenini. _Takeaway:_ Hierarchical RAG that organizes sentence-level chunks into progressively larger, semantically coherent units via attention-guided tree search, avoiding the cost of LLM-guided chunking and the information loss of summarization. _Category:_ 2606.18381 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18381)

**Towards Scalable Customization and Deployment of Multi-Agent Systems for Enterprise Applications** — Paresh Dashore, Shreyas Kulkarni, Uttam Gurram, Nadia Bathaee, et al. _Takeaway:_ Unified framework for adapting compact models to enterprise domains (continual pretraining + SFT + preference optimization) and deploying multi-agent workflows under real production latency/cost constraints. _Category:_ 2606.18502 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18502](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18502)

**ToolChain-CRC: Conformal Risk Control for Agentic AI Under Retrieval and Tool-Use Drift** — Jeffery Opoku, David Banahene. _Takeaway:_ Treats each agent run as a full trajectory of actions/observations/output and builds calibrated step-level risk scores, catching cases where a final answer looks fine but retrieval was weak or a tool output was wrong. _Category:_ 2606.18467 (stat.ML). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18467)

**VISUALSKILL: Multimodal Skills for Computer-Use Agents** — Ziyan Jiang, Li An, Yujian Liu, Jiabao Ji, et al. _Takeaway:_ Hierarchical multimodal skill artifact (text + figures) consumed through a `load_topic` MCP tool that fetches the relevant topic on demand — a concrete tool-use/skill-library primitive for GUI agents on unseen software. _Category:_ 2606.18448 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18448)

**Breaking the Solver Bottleneck: Training Task Generators at the Learnable Frontier** — Lorenz Wolf, Connor Watts, Roger Creus Castanyer, et al. _Takeaway:_ Tackles the emerging bottleneck in agentic RL — supply of valid, solvable, frontier-difficulty tasks — by training an RL task generator without the cost of solver-in-the-loop rollouts (key for SWE tasks where one rollout takes tens of minutes). _Category:_ 2606.18284 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18284)

**DeFAb: A Verifiable Benchmark for Defeasible Abduction in Foundation Models** — Patrick Cooper, Alvaro Velasquez. _Takeaway:_ Polynomial-time-verifiable reasoning benchmark where a logic solver hits 100% but the best frontier model reaches only 65% (dropping to 23.5% under rendering-robust evaluation) — a sharp capabilities-and-robustness gap measurement. _Category:_ 2606.18557 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18557](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18557)

**SAE Interventions are Unreliable: Post-Intervention Recovery of Suppressed Behavior** — (HF-trending). _Takeaway:_ Shows sparse-autoencoder feature interventions used for steering/safety can be silently reversed as suppressed behavior re-emerges downstream — a caution for SAE-based control and alignment auditing. _Category:_ 2606.18322 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18322)

**SHIFT: Semantic Harmonization via Index-side Feature Transformation for Multilingual Information Retrieval** — Youngjoon Jang, Seongtae Hong, Hyeonseok Moon, Heuiseok Lim. _Takeaway:_ Addresses the strong same-language bias of multilingual dense retrievers with an index-side feature transformation, surfacing more relevant cross-language documents without retraining the encoder. _Category:_ 2606.18801 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18801)

**RODS: Reward-Driven Online Data Synthesis for Multi-Turn Tool-Use Agents** — (HF-trending). _Takeaway:_ Synthesizes training data online, guided by reward signal, to improve multi-turn tool-use agents — directly targeting the data-supply problem for function-calling/agentic training. _Category:_ 2606.19047 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19047)

## Other interesting items

- **[MCompassRAG: Topic Metadata as a Semantic Compass for Paragraph-Level Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18508)** — Amirhossein Abaskohi et al., metadata-guided retrieval using topic signals to make deep-research RAG both fast and precise across heterogeneous corpora.
- **[Querit-Reranker: Training Compact Multilingual Rerankers via Efficient Label-Free Distribution Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19037)** — Yunfei Zhong et al., label-efficient multilingual cross-encoder rerankers (0.4B/4B) for second-stage reranking.
- **[JetFlow: Breaking the Scaling Ceiling of Speculative Decoding with Parallel Tree Drafting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18394)** — Lanxiang Hu et al., parallel tree drafting that lifts the acceptance-vs-overhead ceiling in speculative decoding for faster LLM inference.
- **[Attribution-Guided and Coverage-Maximized Pruning for Structural MoE Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18304)** — Yifu Ding et al., channel-level MoE pruning that cuts memory/inference cost beyond coarse expert-level methods.
- **[DRIFT: Refining Instruction Data via On-Policy Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18307)** — Zefan Wang et al., influence-function data curation that raises the SFT capability ceiling rather than just shrinking the dataset.
- **[Characterizing Opinion Evolution of Networked LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18276)** — Caleb Probine et al., finds classical opinion-dynamics models need modification to track how beliefs propagate across networks of interacting LLMs (relevant to multi-agent and influence-operation risk).
- **[Externalizing Research Synthesis and Validation in AI Scientists through a Research Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18874)** — HF-trending; a research harness that externalizes synthesis/validation steps for autonomous-research agents.
- **[From Trainee to Trainer: LLM-Designed Training Environment for RL with Multi-Agent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17682)** — HF-trending; uses an LLM to design RL training environments with multi-agent reasoning.
