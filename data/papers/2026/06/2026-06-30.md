# AI Papers — 2026-06-30

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Recursive Self-Evolving Agents via Held-Out Selection** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Apples-to-apples re-evaluation of weight-free self-improving agents (reflections, playbooks, optimized prompts), introducing RSEA with held-out selection to avoid single-benchmark overfitting. _Category:_ 2606.28374. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28374)

**Agentic Abstention: Do Agents Know When to Stop Instead of Act?** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Studies whether agents recognize when to abstain rather than take a risky action — a core reliability/safety gap for autonomous tool-use agents. _Category:_ 2606.28733. [link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.28733)

**OSWorld2.0: Benchmarking Computer Use Agents on Long-Horizon Real-World Tasks** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Refreshed computer-use-agent benchmark stressing long-horizon, realistic desktop workflows — a key eval for the agentic-capabilities frontier. _Category:_ 2606.29537. [link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.29537)

**TUA-Bench: A Benchmark for General-Purpose Terminal-Use Agents** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Evaluation harness for agents operating in the terminal/shell, complementing browser- and GUI-agent benchmarks for coding-agent capability measurement. _Category:_ 2606.28480. [link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.28480)

**Scaling the Horizon, Not the Parameters: Reaching Trillion-Parameter Performance with a 35B Agent** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Argues that extending the agentic reasoning horizon, rather than scaling parameters, can match much larger models — a capabilities/efficiency result for agent architectures. _Category:_ 2606.30616. [link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.30616)

**TACO: Tool-Augmented Credit Optimization for Agentic Tool Use** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Process-level credit assignment that attributes final correctness to individual tool calls, fixing outcome-only reward's inability to flag redundant/misleading agent actions. _Category:_ 2606.30251. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30251)

**A Systems-Level Analysis of Sensitivity, Robustness, and Stability in Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Controlled 56-run study isolating where RAG fails (chunking, top-k, reranking, generation) rather than scoring only final-answer accuracy. _Category:_ 2606.28337. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28337)

**PixelRAG: Web Screenshots Beat Text for Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Yichuan Wang, Matei Zaharia, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Sewon Min, et al. _Takeaway:_ Retrieves and reads websites in native visual (screenshot) form, preserving layout/structure that HTML-linearizing RAG pipelines discard. _Category:_ 2606.28344. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28344)

**High-Dimensional Concentration and Retrieval Instability in Embedding Spaces** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Shows how distance/cosine concentration in high-dimensional embeddings degrades nearest-neighbor retrieval and destabilizes RAG — implications for vector-store design. _Category:_ 2606.28330. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28330)

**When Latent Agents Lie: KV-Cache Integrity in Multi-Agent LLM Collaboration** — Luís Brito, Carlos Baquero. _Takeaway:_ Identifies a hidden attack surface where agents pass tampered KV-cache state (not just text) between models, with a benign-looking visible message — a multi-agent safety/interoperability risk. _Category:_ 2606.28958. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28958)

**Always-On Agents: A Survey of Persistent Memory, State, and Governance in LLM Agents** — Tianyu Ding, Aditya Nannapaneni, Bingfan Liu, Ling Zhang. _Takeaway:_ Frames always-on agents as persistent-state systems (memories, ledgers, permissions, credentials, audit records) and the governance primitives they demand. _Category:_ 2606.30306. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30306)

**A Gravitational Interpretation of Fine-Tuning Reversion** — Samuele Poppi, Nils Lukas. _Takeaway:_ Unifies safety erosion, capability re-emergence, and latent-trait transfer under benign fine-tuning into one training-history view — relevant to alignment durability and AI safety. _Category:_ 2606.28525. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28525)

## Other interesting items
- **[Minority Sentinel: When to Overturn Majority Voting in Multi-Agent LLM Debates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29270)** — Shows correlated LLM errors break the Condorcet assumption in multi-agent debate, suppressing correct minority answers, and proposes when to override the majority.
- **[ECHO: Learning Epistemically Adaptive Language Agents with Turn-Level Credit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29745)** — Belief-state (Epistemic Decision Process) formulation for multi-turn information-seeking agents with turn-level credit assignment.
- **[MAS-Lab: A Specification-Driven Validation Framework for Reliable Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30546)** — Moves multi-agent systems from ad-hoc imperative assembly toward specification-driven, production-grade validation.
- **[SEAD: Competence-Aware On-Policy Distillation via Entropy-Guided Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28562)** — Uses entropy to make on-policy distillation supervise adaptively across tokens, phases, and prompts instead of uniformly.
- **[One-Step Gradient Delay is Not a Barrier for Large-Scale Asynchronous Pipeline Parallel LLM Pretraining](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.30634)** — Shows async pipeline-parallel pretraining tolerates one-step gradient delay, easing large-scale training infrastructure.
