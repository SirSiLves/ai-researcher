# AI Papers — 2026-06-25

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Improved Large Language Diffusion Models (iLLaDA)** — Shen Nie, Qiyang Min, Shaoxuan Xu et al. _Takeaway:_ An 8B masked diffusion LM trained from scratch with fully bidirectional attention, scaling pre-training to 12T tokens with variable-length generation — a credible non-autoregressive alternative to causal LLMs. _Category:_ 2606.25331. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25331)

**Is GraphRAG Needed? From Basic RAG to Graph-/Agentic Solutions with Context Optimization** — Long Chen, Ryan Razkenari, Yuxuan Zhou et al. _Takeaway:_ Standardized head-to-head of regular RAG, GraphRAG, Modular RAG and Agentic RAG across 9 implemented scenarios on semi-structured KBs — practical guidance on when each variant actually pays off. _Category:_ 2606.25656. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25656)

**BitNet Text Embeddings (BITEMBED)** — Zhen Li, Xin Huang, Liang Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Extreme low-bit framework for LLM-based embeddings targeting both encoder inference cost and vector-index storage, attacking the deployment economics of retrieval at scale. _Category:_ 2606.25674. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25674)

**ReM-MoA: Reasoning Memory Sustains Mixture-of-Agents Scaling** — Heng Ping, Arijit Bhattacharjee, Peiyu Zhang et al. _Takeaway:_ Adds a ranked reasoning-memory to Mixture-of-Agents pipelines so accuracy keeps improving with depth instead of plateauing — addresses a known MoA scaling failure. _Category:_ 2606.24437. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24437)

**Constraint Tax in Open-Weight LLMs: Tool Calling Suppression Under Structured Output Constraints** — Fangzheng Li, Aimin Zhang, Chen Lv. _Takeaway:_ Documents a reproducible production failure — open-weight models stop invoking tools when tool-calling and JSON-schema constraints are enabled together — directly relevant to agent reliability. _Category:_ 2606.25605. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25605)

**Bayesian Control for Coding Agents** — Theodore Papamarkou, Vladislav Smirnov, Viktor Mazanov et al. _Takeaway:_ Frames agent orchestration as cost-sensitive sequential hypothesis testing; a Bayesian controller decides when to gather evidence, refine, verify, or stop — uncertainty-aware tool-use over fixed rules. _Category:_ 2606.24453. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24453)

**Can Trustless Agents Be Trusted? An Empirical Study of the ERC-8004 Decentralized AI Agent Ecosystem** — Xihan Xiong, Zelin Li, Wei Wei et al. _Takeaway:_ First empirical audit of ERC-8004, a permissionless trust layer (Identity/Reputation/Validation registries) for cross-org agent economies — an emerging agent-interoperability standard. _Category:_ 2606.26028. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26028)

**Manipulation Is Task-Dependent: A Multi-Axis, Multi-Environment Evaluation of Frontier LLMs** — Adeeb Zaman, Erik Nordby, Fred Heiding. _Takeaway:_ 13,590 scenarios across six frontier models and six environments measuring manipulation by framing, incentive, and difficulty — shows single-axis benchmarks understate the risk. _Category:_ 2606.25899. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25899)

**PolicyAlign: Direct Policy-Based Safety Alignment for LLMs** — Chang Wu, Junfeng Fang, Houcheng Jiang et al. _Takeaway:_ Aligns models directly from natural-language safety policies when labeled supervision data is unavailable — closing the gap between fast-evolving policy and slow data-driven alignment. _Category:_ 2606.25442. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25442)

**Reinforcement Learning Towards Broadly and Persistently Beneficial Models** — Akshay V. Jagadeesh, Rahul K. Arora, Khaled Saab et al. (OpenAI-affiliated). _Takeaway:_ Studies whether RL on beneficial behavior generalizes alignment beyond training domains, probing reward hacking and deception — frontier alignment-generalization work. _Category:_ 2606.24014. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24014)

## Other interesting items

- **[CompressKV: Semantic-Retrieval-Guided KV-Cache Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24467)** — Xiaolin Lin et al., head-aware KV eviction for resource-efficient long-context inference, avoiding loss of critical tokens.
- **[Tracing Target Answers in Poisoned Retrieval Corpora via Token Influence Attribution (TRACE)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25721)** — Yan-Lun Chen, Pin-Yu Chen et al., lightweight detection of RAG corpus-poisoning attacks without auxiliary classifiers.
- **[What Intermediate Layers Know: Detecting Jailbreaks from Entropy Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25182)** — Sofiia Nikolenko et al., uses cross-layer predictive-entropy trajectories (logit lens) to detect jailbreaks from internal representations.
- **[How Reliable Is Your Jailbreak Judge?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25487)** — Yang Gao, calibration and adversarial-robustness audit of automated attack-success-rate judges against human labels.
- **[Probing the Misaligned Thinking Process of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24251)** — Kaiwen Zhou et al., linear probes over internal activations to detect deception, sandbagging, and self-preservation.
- **[ExTra: Exploratory Trajectory Optimization for LM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24994)** — Wenyang Hu et al., GRPO-compatible exploration signals fixing all-correct/all-incorrect rollout collapse in RLVR.
- **[Beyond Trajectory Imitation: Strategy-Guided Policy Optimization (SGPO)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24064)** — Tianyuan Shi et al., distills *how to reason* rather than specific solution trajectories for better generalization.
- **[Memory Makes the Difference: How Different Memory Roles Shape Conversational Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25361)** — Yuxin Wang et al. (Microsoft-affiliated), studies how functionally distinct memories change RAG agent response behavior.
- **[RIFT-Bench: Dynamic Red-teaming for Agentic AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23927)** — Yarin Yerushalmi Levi et al., graph-driven methodology for unified red-team evaluation across heterogeneous agent systems.
- **[Adaptive Re-Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25249)** — Ata Cinar Genc et al. (UMass/Allan), utility-based cost-aware routing so heavy cross-encoders only run on hard queries.
