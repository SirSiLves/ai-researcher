## Top picks

**Supersede: Diagnosing and Training the Memory-Update Gap in LLM Agents** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Isolates a distinct agent failure on real multi-session conversations — using the current value of a fact and discarding superseded ones — and trains for it. _Category:_ 2606.27472. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27472)

**Internalizing the Future: A Unified Agentic Training Paradigm for World Model Planning** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Gives LLM agents an internal world model for "what-if" reasoning so long-horizon planning becomes proactive instead of reactive. _Category:_ 2606.27483. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27483)

**Delayed Verification Destabilizes Multi-Agent LLM Belief: Instability Thresholds and Optimal Corrector Placement** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Models how false claims propagate through verifier/critic agent networks during verification delay and derives where to place correctors. _Category:_ 2606.27409. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27409)

**QueenBee Planner: Skill-Evolving Communication Topologies for Token-Efficient LLM Multi-Agent Systems** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Treats inter-agent communication topology as a retrievable, self-improving design skill to cut token cost in multi-agent systems. _Category:_ 2606.27492. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27492)

**Agent-Native Immune System: Architecture, Taxonomy, and Engineering** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Proposes defenses internal to the agent's reasoning loop (vs. perimeter/training-time only) for the expanded autonomous-agent threat surface. _Category:_ 2606.28270. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28270)

**SHARD: Cell-keyed Residual Splitting for Alignment-resistant Private Dense Retrieval** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Hardens RAG vector stores against inversion/alignment attacks by breaking the single global embedding geometry a leaked store exposes. _Category:_ 2606.27976. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27976)

**ToE: A Hierarchical and Explainable Claim Verification Framework with Dynamic Multi-source Evidence Retrieval** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Tree-of-Evidence retrieval/aggregation targeting GEO-poisoned, adversarially surfaced content that contaminates LLM reasoning. _Category:_ 2606.27736. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27736)

**Towards Reliable and Robust LLM Planning: Symbolic Feedback-Driven Iterative Self-Refinement** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Uses symbolic feedback to iteratively repair infeasible/incorrect LLM plans on long-horizon tasks. _Category:_ 2606.27757. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27757)

**Position: The Term "Machine Unlearning" Is Overused in LLMs** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Argues for tightening the term amid regulatory deletion, copyright, and product-policy demands — relevant to AI governance/compliance framing. _Category:_ 2606.27379. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27379)

**EntMTP: Accelerating LLM Inference with Entropy-Guided Multi-Token Prediction** — arXiv authors. _Takeaway:_ Replaces static tree attention in MTP self-speculative decoding with entropy-adaptive topology for faster generation. _Category:_ 2606.27550. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27550)

## Other interesting items
- **[Listwise Explanation of Embedding-Based Rankings via Semantic Chunk Grouping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27980)** — ChunkGroupSHAP, a listwise Shapley method that explains dense retrieval at chunk rather than word granularity.
- **[End-to-End Dynamic Sparsity for Resource-Adaptive LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27743)** — Adapts the computational graph to fluctuating cloud resources (spot preemption, tiered QoS) instead of a fixed static graph.
