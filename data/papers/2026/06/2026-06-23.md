# AI Papers — 2026-06-23

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Sakana Fugu Technical Report** — Yujin Tang, Edoardo Cetin, Jinglue Xu, Qi Sun, Stefan Nielsen, et al. (Sakana AI). _Takeaway:_ Combines the distinct domain specializations of multiple frontier LLMs into a single stronger model — a recipe for capability composition across providers. _Category:_ 2606.21228 (cs.LG). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21228)

**PlanBench-XL: Evaluating Long-Horizon Planning of LLM Tool-Use Agents in Large-Scale Tool Ecosystems** — Jiayu Liu, Qihan Lin, Cheng Qian, Rui Wang, Emre Can Acikgoz, et al. _Takeaway:_ New benchmark stressing tool discovery, implicit sub-goal inference, and adaptation over long horizons in large tool ecosystems — where most agent evals fall short. _Category:_ 2606.22388 (cs.CL, cross). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22388)

**The Token Tax of Epistemic Accuracy: Comparing RAG and Long-Context Architectures for Document-Grounded Generative AI** — Austin Hamilton, Ryan Singh, Michael Wise, Ibrahim Yousif, Arthur Carvalho, Zhe Shan, et al. _Takeaway:_ Quantifies the accuracy-vs-cost trade-off between RAG and long-context for document-grounded assistants, showing how evidence allocation before generation drives epistemic accuracy. _Category:_ 2606.20898 (cs.IR). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20898)

**KaLM-Reranker-V1: Fast but Not Late Interaction for Compressed Document Reranking** — Xinping Zhao, Jiaxin Xu, Ziqi Dai, Xin Zhang, Shouzheng Huang, et al. _Takeaway:_ A reranker that decouples query/passage computation for deployment efficiency while keeping quality — addressing the cost of reranking as retrieval systems scale. _Category:_ 2606.22807 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22807)

**Self-Compacting Language Model Agents** — Tianjian Li, Jingyu Zhang, William Jurayj, Xi Wang, Chuanyang Jin, Mehrdad Farajtabar, et al. _Takeaway:_ Lets agents decide when and what to compact in long traces of chains-of-thought and tool calls, instead of fixed-interval token-threshold compaction — directly relevant to agentic memory and context-window management. _Category:_ 2606.23525 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23525)

**AgentRiskBOM: A Risk-Scoping Security Bill of Materials for Agentic AI Systems** — Srimonti Dutta, Akshata Kishore Moharir. _Takeaway:_ Extends the bill-of-materials concept to agentic systems that retrieve private context, call tools, write files, and act without human approval — a concrete governance/risk-transparency artifact. _Category:_ 2606.21877 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21877)

**Deeper is Not Always Better: Mitigating the Alignment Tax via Confident Layer Decoding** — Xuanming Zhang, Sining Zhoubian, Yuxuan Chen, Tianyi Tang, An Yang, et al. _Takeaway:_ Revisits the assumption that final-layer decoding is best; a "Guess-Refine" pattern across layers motivates confidence-based early decoding to reduce the alignment tax. _Category:_ 2606.21906 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21906)

**Capable but Careless: Do Computer-Use Agents Follow Contextual Integrity?** — Anmol Goel, Iryna Gurevych. _Takeaway:_ Audits whether computer-use agents respect contextual-integrity privacy norms across email/calendar/to-do apps — surfacing a cross-application privacy risk that has been largely overlooked. _Category:_ 2606.23189 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23189)

## Other interesting items
- **[Grouped Query Experts: Mixture-of-Experts on GQA Self-Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20945)** — Vishesh Tripathi, Abhay Kumar. Applies an MoE structure to GQA self-attention to cut the quadratic long-context attention cost.
- **[EvoEmbedding: Evolvable Representations for Long-Context Retrieval and Agentic Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21649)** — Chang Nie, Chaoyou Fu, Junlan Feng, Caifeng Shan. Embeddings that evolve with surrounding context and temporal order, instead of encoding text segments in isolation.
- **[RaMem: Contextual Reinstatement for Long-term Agentic Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22844)** — Wei Yang, Bryce Kan, Shixuan Li, et al. Memory system that reinstates relevant past context for agents operating across extended, evolving interactions.
- **[Agent-as-a-Router: Agentic Model Routing for Coding Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22902)** — Pengfei Zhou, Zhiwei Tang, Yixing Ma, et al. Routes each coding task to the most suitable LLM across providers, none of which dominates all domains.
- **[Self-Evolution for Multi-Turn Tool-Calling Agents via Divergence-Point Preference Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23112)** — Jiaqiang Tang. Unifies inference-time orchestration with parameter-level learning for long-horizon multi-turn tool use.
- **[GIF: Locally Sound Geometric Information Flow Control for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23277)** — Adam Storek, Nikolaus Holzer, Zhuo Zhang, Suman Jana. Information-flow control to mitigate prompt injection and data-exfiltration risks in agentic systems.
- **[Agent Behavior Mining: Generative AI Agent Governance in Business Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20669)** — Hoang Vu, Maximilian Körner, Adrian Rebmann, et al. Process-mining approach to governing non-deterministic generative-AI agents deployed in business workflows.
