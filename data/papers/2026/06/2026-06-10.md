# AI Papers — 2026-06-10

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Less Context, Better Agents: Efficient Context Engineering for Long-Horizon Tool-Using LLM Agents** — Lodha, Pahlavikhah Varnosfaderani, Chakraborty, Mithal. _Takeaway:_ Studies context-overflow/stale-state failures in enterprise GPT-5 agents calling MCP tools (Dynamics 365), showing tool-response pruning + summarization cuts cost and errors on a hotel-expense benchmark. _Category:_ 2606.10209. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10209)

**Pushing the Limits of LLM Tool Calling via Experiential Knowledge Integration and Activation** — Hao, Jin, Liao, Liu, Zhao. _Takeaway:_ Systematic study of how tool-related knowledge is acquired, activated, and internalized for multi-step tool use; finds simple instance-level experiential knowledge already yields strong, reliable gains. _Category:_ 2606.10875. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10875)

**Decentralized Multi-Agent Systems with Shared Context (DeLM)** — Mao, Mirhoseini. _Takeaway:_ Replaces a centralized orchestrator with parallel agents that asynchronously claim subtasks from a queue over a shared verified context, removing the controller bottleneck as multi-agent task count grows. _Category:_ 2606.10662. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10662)

**T1-Bench: Benchmarking Multi-Scenario Agents in Real-World Domains** — Winata, Chakraborty, Lin, Rao, et al. _Takeaway:_ High-fidelity agentic benchmark for realistic customer-facing, multi-domain tasks requiring sustained reasoning and cross-domain coordination — targets the realism gap in existing agent evals. _Category:_ 2606.11070. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11070)

**Trace Only What You Need: Structure-Aware On-Demand Hypergraph Memory for Long-Document QA (DocTrace)** — Zai, Tan, Chen, Wang, Zhang. _Takeaway:_ Structured-RAG method that builds query-driven hypergraph memory, exploits original document structure, and reuses prior reasoning to cut cost on cross-part long-document QA. _Category:_ 2606.10921. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10921)

**REAL: A Reasoning-Enhanced Graph Framework for Long-Term Memory Management of LLMs** — Lu, Chen, Jiang, Qin, Liu, Zhang. _Takeaway:_ Graph-structured long-term memory that captures explicit relations between stored interactions, addressing flat-text and rigid-schema limits of current external-memory systems for long-horizon assistants. _Category:_ 2606.10694. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10694)

**Alignment Collapse Under KV Cache Quantization: Diagnosis and Mitigation** — Xu, Kumarappan, Zhou. _Takeaway:_ Across 11 instruction-tuned models (3.8B–72B) and 1,894 prompts, low-bit KV-cache quantization can silently destroy safety alignment (Mistral-7B loses 15.2% of refusals at ~1.03x perplexity) with no universal safe bit-width. _Category:_ 2606.09864. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09864)

**Does Reasoning Preserve Alignment? On the Trustworthiness of Large Reasoning Models** — Kini, Reddy, Chakraborty, Namburi GNVV, Huang, Bedi, Velasquez. _Takeaway:_ Trustworthiness audit finds that converting instruction-tuned LLMs into reasoning models via SFT/RL is not alignment-preserving by default — safe refusal, bias avoidance, and privacy behaviors degrade. _Category:_ 2606.11046. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11046)

**ABC-Bench: An Agentic Bio-Capabilities Benchmark for Biosecurity** — Liu, Nedungadi, Cai, Kleinman, Bhasin, Donoughe. _Takeaway:_ A task suite measuring agentic biosecurity-relevant capabilities of LLM agents performing in-silico biology, aimed at quantifying dual-use risk as models approach expert-biologist workflows. _Category:_ 2606.11150. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11150)

**When RL Fails after SFT: Rejuvenating Model Plasticity for Robust SFT-to-RL Handoff** — Liu, Liu, Wan, Fu, Pan. _Takeaway:_ Diagnoses why over-SFT'd checkpoints barely improve under RL as a loss of model plasticity, and proposes how to restore reshape-ability for the standard SFT→RL post-training pipeline. _Category:_ 2606.09932. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09932)

**TRACE: A Unified Rollout Budget Allocation Framework for Efficient Agentic Reinforcement Learning** — Zou, Wang, Qu, Jiang, et al. _Takeaway:_ Allocates RLVR rollout budget at step granularity (not just per-prompt) to fix low reward-contrast in multi-turn agentic training where outcome-only rewards give every decision the same terminal signal. _Category:_ 2606.11119. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11119)

**Mind the Gap: Can Frontier LLMs Pass a Standardized Office Proficiency Exam?** — Lv, Zhang, Ding, Jia, et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmarks computer-automation agents on 200 practical Office tasks (Word/Excel/etc.) from China's National Computer Rank Exam, probing long-horizon planning, precise parameter config, and multi-app integration. _Category:_ 2606.10956. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10956)

## Other interesting items
- **[HIPIF: Hierarchical Planning and Information Folding for Long-Horizon LLM Agent Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10507)** — Diao, Lu, Li, et al. Tackles long-context interference in multi-turn agents by folding history so the agent keeps tracking global task state.
- **[Infini Memory: Maintainable Topic Documents for Long-Term LLM Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10677)** — Ji, Wu, Wang, et al. Treats agent memory as topic-structured documents to make evidence aggregation, fact revision, and maintenance tractable across sessions.
- **[Effective RL for Agentic Search by Recycling Zero-Variance Queries During Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10709)** — Coelho, Magalhães, Martins, Xiong. Shows GRPO search-agent training can recycle "too-easy/too-hard" queries that flip to signal-bearing as the policy evolves, instead of discarding them.
- **[Alignment Defends LLMs from Property Inference Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10217)** — Huang, Yadav, Wu, Chaudhuri. Uses alignment, rather than data-distribution edits or retraining, to defend deployed/fine-tuned models against dataset-level property inference.
- **[TabClaw: An Interactive and Self-Evolving Agent for Spreadsheet Manipulation and Table Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10316)** — Cheng, Yu, Wang, et al. Open-source spreadsheet agent with transparent intermediate decisions, multi-table comparison, and preference adaptation.
