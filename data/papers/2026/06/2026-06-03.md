# AI Papers — 2026-06-03

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Capability Advertisement as a Market for Lemons: A Trust Layer for Heterogeneous Agent Networks** — Gaurav Naresh Mittal. _Takeaway:_ Argues MCP/A2A registries naively treat advertised agent capabilities as static and truthful, creating an adverse-selection ("market for lemons") problem, and proposes a trust/verification layer to fix it. _Category:_ 2606.03034 (cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03034)

**OpenAgenet/OAN: Open Infrastructure for Trusted Agent Interconnection** — Jinliang Xu. _Takeaway:_ Protocol-neutral trust layer for open, multi-operator agent networks — identity provenance, governance state, discovery authorization and freshness checks before one agent invokes another. Companion technical-architecture paper (2606.03163) details identity objects, registration workflow and signed invocation. _Category:_ 2606.03161 (cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03161)

**Economy of Minds: Emerging Multi-Agent Intelligence with Economic Interactions** — Zhenting Qi, Huangyuan Su, Ao Qu, et al. _Takeaway:_ Hayek-inspired agent economy where agents bid in auctions for the right to act and accumulate wealth from rewards; economic signals induce decentralized credit assignment and planning without global orchestration or explicit communication protocols. _Category:_ 2606.02859 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02859)

**What Benchmarks Don't Measure: The Case for Evaluating Abstention Competence in Autonomous Agents** — Victor Ojewale, Suresh Venkatasubramanian. _Takeaway:_ Names "compliance bias" — agents trained on human feedback structurally tend to proceed even without sufficient inputs, evidence, or authorization to act safely; argues evals must measure whether an agent *should* have proceeded at all. _Category:_ 2606.02965 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02965)

**When Helping Hurts and How to Fix It: Multi-Agent Debate for Data Cleaning** — Chirag Parmar, Akshat Mehta, Henglin Wu, et al. _Takeaway:_ Across 3 benchmarks, 4 model families and 6,000+ task-conditions, debate's effect reverses sign — it degrades generation (−1.6 to −15.5pp) via "critique-induced confusion" (hallucinated critic feedback the generator accepts) yet improves error detection. _Category:_ 2606.02866 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02866)

**Adaptive Latent Agentic Reasoning (ALAR)** — Dongwon Jung, Peng Shi, Yi Zhang, Junshan Zhang, Muhao Chen. _Takeaway:_ Dual-mode framework that uses compact *latent* reasoning for routine agent turns and escalates to explicit chain-of-thought only when deeper deliberation is needed, cutting the verbose-reasoning overhead of multi-turn agentic trajectories. _Category:_ 2606.02871 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02871)

**Adaptive Auto-Harness: Sustained Self-Improvement for Agentic System Deployment on Open-Ended Task Streams** — (HF Papers). _Takeaway:_ Self-improving harness that lets an agentic system keep adapting across an open-ended stream of deployment tasks rather than a fixed benchmark suite. _Category:_ 2606.01770. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01770)

**Thinking Past the Answer: Evaluating Harmful Overthinking in Large Reasoning Models** — Simone Caldarella, Davide Talon, Rahaf Aljundi, Elisa Ricci, Massimiliano Mancini. _Takeaway:_ Challenges the "longer reasoning is always better" assumption — once an LRM reaches the correct answer, additional reasoning can degrade it; quantifies harmful overthinking with test-time compute. _Category:_ 2606.02835 (cs.AI). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02835)

**Fast-dLLM++: Fréchet Profile Decoding for Faster Diffusion LLM Inference** — Siva Rajesh Kasa, Yasong Dai, Sumit Negi, Hongdong Li. _Takeaway:_ Training-free decoder for diffusion LLMs that drops the homogeneous high-confidence assumption of Fast-dLLM, exploiting heterogeneous per-step confidence profiles to commit more masked tokens in parallel and speed inference. _Category:_ 2606.02955 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02955)

**Linear Probes Detect Task Format, Not Reasoning Mode in Language Model Hidden States** — Subramanyam Sahoo, Vinija Jain, Aman Chadha, Divya Chaudhary. _Takeaway:_ Probing Qwen3-14B across deductive/inductive/abductive benchmarks, near-perfect probe accuracy is shown to be driven by task-format confounds rather than distinct internal "reasoning-type" representations — a caution for interpretability claims. _Category:_ 2606.02907 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02907)
