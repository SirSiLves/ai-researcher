## Top picks
For each: **Title** — authors. _Takeaway:_ 1 line. _Category:_ {arxiv id}. [link]

- **NRT-Bench: Multi-Turn Red-Teaming of LLM Agents in Safety-Critical Systems** — Hanwool Lee et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmark simulates a nuclear plant control room with a five-role LLM operator team; adaptive multi-turn attacks push the team past a hard safety limit in 8.7–12.1% of runs across four frontier models, with harm measured objectively rather than LLM-judged. _Category:_ 2606.20408. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20408)
- **Continual Learning Bench: Evaluating Frontier AI Systems in Real-World Stateful Environments** — Parth Asawa et al. _Takeaway:_ New benchmark for continual learning — whether AI systems actually improve through sequential experience in stateful real-world settings, a capability current eval stacks don't measure. _Category:_ 2606.05661. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05661)
- **Enhancing Decision-Making with Large Language Models through Multi-Agent Fictitious Play** — (cs.CL, submitted Jun 17). _Takeaway:_ Applies game-theoretic fictitious play to LLM multi-agent systems, distributing subtasks across cooperative agents to improve decisions on execution-complex tasks. _Category:_ 2606.19308. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19308)
- **Self-Aware Vector Embeddings for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (SmartVector)** — _Takeaway:_ Augments dense embeddings with temporal awareness, confidence decay, and relational structure; reports accuracy gains over plain cosine RAG and a measurable drop in stale-answer rates. _Category:_ 2604.20598. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20598)
- **Empirical Comparison of Agent Communication Protocols for Task Orchestration** — _Takeaway:_ Head-to-head evaluation of agent-to-agent communication protocols (MCP/A2A-style) for orchestrating multi-agent task execution — useful grounding for the interop-standards debate. _Category:_ 2603.22823. [link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.22823)
- **SoK: Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation — Taxonomy, Architectures, Evaluation, and Research Directions** — _Takeaway:_ Systematization-of-knowledge over agentic RAG: a taxonomy and architecture map separating retrieval-as-tool designs from static pipelines, plus an evaluation framework. _Category:_ 2603.07379. [link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.07379)

## Other interesting items
- **[The Evolution of Tool Use in LLM Agents: From Single-Tool Call to Multi-Tool Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.22862)** — traces the shift from one-shot function calls to orchestrated multi-tool workflows, relevant to MCP/tool-use standardization.
- **[Externalization in LLM Agents: A Unified Review of Memory, Skills, Protocols and Harness Engineering](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08224)** — unifies the agent "externalization" stack (memory, skills, protocols, harness) into one framework.
- **[Agent Skills for Large Language Models: Architecture, Acquisition, Security, and the Path Forward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12430)** — Renjun Xu, Yang Yan — composable, on-demand skill packages as an alternative to retraining for capability extension; covers the security surface.
- **[Benchmark Health Index: A Systematic Framework for Benchmarking the Benchmarks of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.11674)** — proposes a meta-metric for benchmark quality as frontier models saturate legacy evals (GSM8K, MMLU, HellaSwag).
