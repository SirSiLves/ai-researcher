## Top picks

**MiniMax Sparse Attention** — Xunhao Lai, Weiqi Xu, Yufeng Yang, Qiaorui Chen et al. _Takeaway:_ Blockwise sparse attention on top of GQA with a lightweight index branch that selects top-k KV blocks per group, targeting million-token agentic/long-context workloads without softmax's quadratic cost. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.13392. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13392)

**The Cold-Start Safety Gap in LLM Agents** — Chung-En Sun, Linbo Liu, Tsui-Wei Weng. _Takeaway:_ Tool-calling agents are most vulnerable to safety threats at the very start of a session and get substantially safer after a few benign tasks; introduces the SODA benchmark (up to 20 priming tasks) across 7 models from 4 families. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.07867. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07867)

**Getting Better at Working With You: Compiling User Corrections into Runtime Enforcement for Coding Agents** — Yujun Zhou, Kehan Guo, Haomin Zhuang, Xiangqi Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ TRACE mines user corrections and compiles them into runtime-enforced rules for coding agents; Mem0-style memory alone still violates 57.5% of applicable preference checks, exposing the gap between preference recall and compliance. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.13174. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13174)

**Evoflux: Inference-Time Evolution of Executable Tool Workflows for Compact Agents** — Kushal Raj Bhandari, Ling Yue, Ching-Yun Ko, Dhaval Patel et al. _Takeaway:_ Addresses MCP-style tool use for small models — discovering tools from live catalogs, satisfying schemas, preserving cross-step dependencies — by evolving executable workflow graphs at inference time rather than relying on small-corpus distillation. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.12674. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12674)

**ToolSense: A Diagnostic Framework for Auditing Parametric Tool Knowledge in LLMs** — Ashutosh Hathidara, Sai Shruthi Sistla, Sebastian Schreiber, Sahil Bansal. _Takeaway:_ Audits parametric tool retrieval (encoding each tool as a virtual vocabulary token) versus embedding-based retrieval, showing ToolBench-style benchmarks overstate performance because of verbose, fully-specified queries. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.12451. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12451)

**EvoBrowseComp: Benchmarking Search Agents on Evolving Knowledge** — Yunhan Wang, Jiaan Wang, Lianzhe Huang, Xianfeng Zeng et al. _Takeaway:_ A contamination-free, evolving benchmark (400 EN + 400 ZH items) for search agents; static benchmarks like BrowseComp let models score high via parametric recall rather than genuine retrieval. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.13120. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13120)

**FORT-Searcher: Synthesizing Shortcut-Resistant Search Tasks for Training Deep Search Agents** — Jia Deng, Yimeng Chen, Xiaoqing Xiang, Ziyang Zeng et al. _Takeaway:_ Formalizes a shortcut-aware difficulty framework (evidence co-coverage, single-clue selection, etc.) to synthesize training questions whose answers are genuinely unreachable without multi-step search. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.12087. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12087)

**See What I See, Know What I Think: Dense Latent Communication Across Heterogeneous Agents** — Siyi Chen, Xiaoyan Zhang, Meng Wu, Jonathan Tremblay et al. _Takeaway:_ Multi-agent KV-cache (latent) communication across *different* models, tackling cross-model latent alignment to avoid the lossy decode/re-encode cost of text-based agent-to-agent messaging. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.13594. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13594)

**HarnessBridge: Learnable Bidirectional Controller for LLM Agent Harness** — Xiaoxuan Wang, Haixin Wang, Alexander Taylor, Jason Cong et al. _Takeaway:_ Replaces hand-engineered agent harnesses with a trainable, end-to-end plug-in module that mediates agent–environment interaction, improving scalability on long-horizon tasks. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.12882. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12882)

**Risk Under Pressure: Compute-Aware Evaluation of Adversarial Robustness in Language Models** — Malikeh Ehghaghi, Boglárka Ecsedi, Marsha Chechik, Colin Raffel. _Takeaway:_ Argues fixed-budget attack-success-rate hides true jailbreak cost; proposes a compute-aware robustness framework that accounts for attack strategies differing in expense by orders of magnitude. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.11409. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11409)

**EvoArena: Tracking Memory Evolution for Robust LLM Agents in Dynamic Environments** — Jundong Xu, Qingchuan Li, Jiaying Wu, Yihuai Lan et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmark suite modeling environments as sequences of progressive updates (terminal/software/social), plus EvoMem, a patch-based memory mechanism for agents that must continually realign with changing conditions. _Category:_ arXiv:2606.13681. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13681)

## Other interesting items
- **[Demystifying Hidden-State Recurrence: Switchable Latent Reasoning with On-Policy RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13106)** — Jiayu Yang, Chao Chen, Shengen Wu et al. — explicit boundary tokens make latent chain-of-thought compatible with standard on-policy RL and mechanistically interpretable.
- **[MaxProof: Scaling Mathematical Proof with Generative-Verifier RL and Population-Level Test-Time Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13473)** — Jiacheng Chen, Xinyu Zhang, Shunkai Zhang et al. — MiniMax-M3 trains generation/verification/repair, then searches a population of candidate proofs at test time.
- **[WeaveBench: A Long-Horizon, Real-World Benchmark for Computer-Use Agents with Hybrid Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09426)** — Wanli Li, Bowen Zhou, Yunyao Yu et al. — 114 cross-interface tasks (GUI + CLI + code + browser + tools) testing long-horizon orchestration.
- **[WebChallenger: A Reliable and Efficient Generalist Web Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10423)** — Jayoo Hwang, Xiaowen Zhang, Vedant Padwal — open web agent replicating selective attention, persistent site memory, and procedural fluency to cut reliance on costly proprietary reasoning models.
- **[EurekAgent: Agent Environment Engineering is All You Need for Autonomous Scientific Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13662)** — Amy Xin, Jiening Siow, Junjie Wang et al. — argues the bottleneck for autonomous discovery is shifting from agent workflows to designing agent environments.
- **[Rethinking Psychometric Evaluation of LLMs: When and Why Self-Reports Predict Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12730)** — Rafal Kocielnik, Pengrui Han, Peiyang Song et al. — examines when low-cost self-report probes reliably predict downstream LLM behavior for safe deployment.
