# AI Papers — 2026-06-11

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Finding and Reactivating Post-Trained LLMs' Hidden Safety Mechanisms** — Mingjie Li, Wai Man Si, Michael Backes, Yang Zhang, Yisen Wang. _Takeaway:_ Shows safety behavior in post-trained models is governed by latent mechanisms that can be located and reactivated, with implications for both alignment robustness and jailbreak defense. _Category:_ 2604.00012. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00012)

**The Persistent Vulnerability of Aligned AI Systems** — Aengus Lynch. _Takeaway:_ A 157-page systematization (UCL thesis) arguing aligned systems retain structural attack surface; consolidates adversarial findings into a durable threat model rather than point attacks. _Category:_ 2604.00324. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00324)

**Do Language Models Know When They'll Refuse? Probing Introspective Awareness of Safety Boundaries** — Tanay Gondil. _Takeaway:_ Probes whether models internally represent their own refusal boundaries before generating, relevant to predictable guardrail behavior and red-team evaluation. _Category:_ 2604.00228. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00228)

**Do LLMs Know What Is Private Internally? Probing and Steering Contextual Privacy Norms** — Haoran Wang, Li Xiong, Kai Shu. _Takeaway:_ Finds steerable internal representations of contextual privacy norms, a concrete handle for governance/PII-leakage controls at the representation level. _Category:_ 2604.00209. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00209)

**Oblivion: Self-Adaptive Agentic Memory Control through Decay-Driven Activation** — Ashish Rana, Chia-Chien Hung, Qumeng Sun, Julian Martin Kunkel, Carolin Lawrence. _Takeaway:_ Decay-driven activation for agent memory that adaptively forgets/retains context; targets a core scaling pain point in long-running agents (code released). _Category:_ 2604.00131. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00131)

**Asymmetric Actor-Critic for Multi-turn LLM Agents** — Shuli Jiang, Zhaoyang Zhang, Yi Zhang, Shuo Yang, Wei Xia, Stefano Soatto. _Takeaway:_ An asymmetric actor-critic RL recipe tailored to multi-turn agent trajectories, addressing credit assignment across long tool-use episodes. _Category:_ 2604.00304. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00304)

**Agent Q-Mix: Selecting the Right Action for LLM Multi-Agent Systems through RL** — Eric Hanchen Jiang et al. _Takeaway:_ Brings value-decomposition (QMIX-style) action selection to LLM multi-agent systems for better coordinated decisions. _Category:_ 2604.00344. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00344)

**Think Twice Before You Write — an Entropy-based Decoding Strategy to Enhance LLM Reasoning** — Jiashu He, Meizhu Liu, Dan Roth, et al. _Takeaway:_ A training-free entropy-gated decoding strategy that improves reasoning quality by re-deliberating at high-uncertainty steps. _Category:_ 2604.00018. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00018)

**Scheduling LLM Inference with Uncertainty-Aware Output Length Predictions** — Haoyu Zheng et al. _Takeaway:_ Inference-serving scheduler that exploits predicted output-length uncertainty to improve throughput/latency — practical platform/observability angle. _Category:_ 2604.00499. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00499)

**WHBench: Evaluating Frontier LLMs with Expert-in-the-Loop Validation on Women's Health Topics** — Sneha Maurya, Pragya Saboo, Girish Kumar. _Takeaway:_ Expert-validated frontier-model benchmark for a high-stakes domain, useful as a capabilities/safety eval template. _Category:_ 2604.00024. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00024)

## Other interesting items
- **["Who Am I, and Who Else Is Here?" Behavioral Differentiation Without Role Assignment in Multi-Agent LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00026)** — Houssam EL Kandoussi, on emergent role differentiation in multi-agent setups without explicit assignment.
- **[SoK: Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Taxonomy, Architectures, Evaluation, and Research Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07379)** — systematization of agentic RAG patterns (reflection, planning, tool use, multi-agent retrieval).
- **[Experience as a Compass: Multi-agent RAG with Evolving Orchestration and Agent Prompts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00901)** — multi-agent RAG that evolves orchestration and prompts from accumulated experience.
- **[Diversity-Aware Reverse KL Divergence for Large Language Model Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00223)** — Hoang-Chau Luong, Dat Ba Tran, Lingwei Chen; distillation objective that preserves output diversity.
- **[Robust Multimodal Safety via Conditional Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00310)** — Anurag Kumar et al. (submitted ACL 2026); decoding-time defense for multimodal safety.
