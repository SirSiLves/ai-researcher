# AI Papers — 2026-06-12

## Top picks

**Oblivion: Self-Adaptive Agentic Memory Control through Decay-Driven Activation** — Rana, Hung, Sun, Kunkel, Lawrence (NEC). _Takeaway:_ Decay-driven activation gives LLM agents a self-adaptive memory that forgets stale context instead of accumulating it indefinitely. _Category:_ 2604.00131. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00131)

**Asymmetric Actor-Critic for Multi-turn LLM Agents** — Jiang, Zhang, Zhang, Yang, Xia, Soatto. _Takeaway:_ Gives the critic privileged state the actor never sees, stabilizing RL training for multi-turn agentic tasks. _Category:_ 2604.00304. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00304)

**Agent Q-Mix: Selecting the Right Action for LLM Multi-Agent Systems through Reinforcement Learning** — Jiang, Li, Sun, Liang, et al. (UCLA). _Takeaway:_ Q-Mix-style value decomposition to pick coordinated actions in LLM multi-agent systems. _Category:_ 2604.00344. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00344)

**Reinforcement Learning for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems through Orchestration Traces** — (survey/method). _Takeaway:_ Frames orchestration as temporal interaction graphs (spawn, delegate, communicate, aggregate, stop) and maps eight reward families; notes no existing RL method trains the stopping decision. _Category:_ 2605.02801. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02801)

**Self-Aware Vector Embeddings for Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — neuroscience-inspired temporal/confidence/relational framework. _Takeaway:_ Augments dense embeddings with temporal validity, confidence decay, and graph-relational signals; roughly doubles top-1 retrieval accuracy (62% vs 31%) over plain cosine RAG and halves calibration error. _Category:_ 2604.20598. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20598)

**A-RAG: Scaling Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — hierarchical retrieval interfaces. _Takeaway:_ Exposes keyword-, sentence-, and chunk-level retrieval tools so agents pick the right granularity rather than a single fixed chunk size. _Category:_ 2602.03442. [link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.03442)

**Finding and Reactivating Post-Trained LLMs' Hidden Safety Mechanisms** — Li, Si, Backes, Zhang, Wang. _Takeaway:_ Locates latent safety circuits that post-training leaves dormant and shows they can be reactivated to restore refusals. _Category:_ 2604.00012. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00012)

**Do Language Models Know When They'll Refuse? Probing Introspective Awareness of Safety Boundaries** — Gondil. _Takeaway:_ Probes whether models internally represent their own refusal boundary before generating, with implications for safety eval and steering. _Category:_ 2604.00228. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00228)

**Governing Evolving Memory in LLM Agents: Risks, Mechanisms, and the SSGM Framework** — Stability and Safety Governed Memory. _Takeaway:_ Catalogs risks of self-updating agent memory (poisoning, drift) and proposes a governance framework to keep evolving memory stable and safe. _Category:_ 2603.11768. [link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.11768)

## Other interesting items
- **[Hierarchical Chain-of-Thought Prompting: Enhancing LLM Reasoning Performance and Efficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00130)** — Huang, Li, Nikpour, Omidi; structures CoT hierarchically for better reasoning at lower token cost.
- **["Who Am I, and Who Else Is Here?" Behavioral Differentiation Without Role Assignment in Multi-Agent LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00026)** — Kandoussi; agents self-differentiate behavior without explicit role prompts.
- **[ragR: Retrieval-Augmented Generation and RAG Assessment in R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23515)** — R-native pipeline unifying ingestion, embedding, retrieval, generation, and RAGAS-style scoring (precision, recall, faithfulness, relevance).
- **[Do LLMs Know What Is Private Internally? Probing and Steering Contextual Privacy Norms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00209)** — Wang, Xiong, Shu; probes and steers internal representations of contextual privacy.
- **[Think Twice Before You Write — an Entropy-based Decoding Strategy to Enhance LLM Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00018)** — He, Liu, et al. (incl. Dan Roth); entropy-guided decoding to improve reasoning.
