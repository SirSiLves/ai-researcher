# AI Papers — 2026-06-21

## Top picks

**Decentralized Multi-Agent Systems with Shared Context (DeLM)** — Yuzhen Mao, Azalia Mirhoseini. _Takeaway:_ Replaces the central orchestrator bottleneck with parallel agents that asynchronously claim subtasks from a queue and build on a shared, verified context — scaling test-time reasoning without routing every update through one controller. _Category:_ 2606.10662 (cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10662)

**NRT-Bench: Multi-turn Red-teaming of LLM Agents in Safety-Critical Systems** — (NRT-Bench authors). _Takeaway:_ Benchmarks four frontier "operator" models running a simulated nuclear control room under sustained adaptive adversarial pressure; multi-turn attacks push the team past a safety limit 8.7–12.1% of the time, exposing weak robustness under prolonged pressure. _Category:_ 2606.20408 (cs.AI / cs.CR). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20408)

**No Free Lunch: Rethinking Internal Feedback for LLM Reasoning** — (paper authors). _Takeaway:_ Critically re-examines "zero RL" / internal-feedback reward signals (à la DeepSeek-R1) for eliciting reasoning, showing where intrinsic feedback helps and where it silently fails versus verifiable rewards. _Category:_ 2506.17219 (cs.LG / cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.17219)

**Beyond Context Sharing: A Unified Agent Communication Protocol (ACP) for Federated A2A Orchestration** — (ACP authors). _Takeaway:_ Proposes a security-first communication protocol layered above MCP, governing secure, federated, autonomous agent-to-agent orchestration — positioning context management (MCP), messaging (ACP), and collaboration (A2A) as a protocol stack. _Category:_ 2602.15055 (cs.AI / cs.MA). [link](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15055v1)

**Self-Aware Vector Embeddings for Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — (SmartVector authors). _Takeaway:_ A neuroscience-inspired RAG framework adding temporal awareness and confidence-weighted, relational retrieval; lifts version-sensitive QA accuracy from ~58% (standard RAG) to ~90% by making embeddings aware of recency and confidence. _Category:_ 2604.20598 (cs.IR / cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20598v1)

**SafeNeuron: Neuron-Level Safety Alignment for Large Language Models** — (SafeNeuron authors). _Takeaway:_ Localizes safety behavior to specific neurons and aligns at that granularity, aiming for harm reduction with less collateral damage to helpfulness than full-model post-training. _Category:_ 2602.12158 (cs.CL). [link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12158)

## Other interesting items

- **[Agent Skills for Large Language Models: Architecture, Acquisition, Security, and the Path Forward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12430)** — (survey/position, revised Jun 2026), maps the shift from monolithic models to modular, skill-equipped agents that extend capability without retraining, plus the security surface that opens up.
- **[Topic-Enriched Embeddings to Improve Retrieval Precision in RAG Systems](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.00891v1)** — proposes hybrid topic-aware embeddings to sharpen retrieval precision where strict, accurate retrieval is critical.
- **[The Orchestration of Multi-Agent Systems: Architectures, Protocols, and Enterprise Adoption](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13671v1)** — delineates MCP (tool/context access) vs A2A (peer coordination, negotiation, delegation) and their enterprise adoption path.
