# AI Papers — 2026-05-31

## Top picks

**DeepWeb-Bench: A Deep Research Benchmark Demanding Massive Cross-Source Evidence and Long-Horizon Derivation** — anon. _Takeaway:_ Evaluates nine frontier models on open-web deep research; finds retrieval is not the bottleneck (only 12–14% of errors) while derivation and calibration failures account for over 70%. _Category:_ 2605.21482. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21482)

**Reinforcement Learning for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems through Orchestration Traces** — anon. _Takeaway:_ Frames RL over orchestration traces (spawn, delegate, communicate, tool-use, aggregate, stop) with eight reward families and five orchestration sub-decisions — a structured taxonomy for training multi-agent orchestrators. _Category:_ 2605.02801. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02801)

**LatentRAG: Latent Reasoning and Retrieval for Efficient Agentic RAG** — anon. _Takeaway:_ Shifts both reasoning and retrieval from discrete language space to continuous latent space, producing latent thought/subquery tokens from hidden states in a single forward pass and aligning the LLM with a dense retriever in latent space. _Category:_ 2605.06285. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06285)

**LEMON: Learning Executable Multi-Agent Orchestration via Counterfactual Reinforcement Learning** — anon. _Takeaway:_ LLM orchestrator emits an executable orchestration spec (roles, duties, capacity, dependency structure) trained with counterfactual RL into a single deployable multi-agent system. _Category:_ 2605.14483. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14483)

**MultiBreak: A Scalable and Diverse Multi-turn Jailbreak Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Safety** — anon. _Takeaway:_ 10,389 multi-turn adversarial prompts spanning 2,665 distinct harmful intents — a large benchmark for evaluating safety under conversational, multi-turn attack pressure. _Category:_ 2605.01687. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01687)

**SciResearcher: Scaling Deep Research Agents for Frontier Scientific Reasoning** — anon. _Takeaway:_ Fully automated agentic framework synthesizing conceptual and computational scientific tasks grounded in academic evidence, eliciting information acquisition, tool-integrated reasoning, and long-horizon capabilities. _Category:_ 2605.01489. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01489)

**Structure Retention in Embedding Spaces as a Predictor of Benchmark Performance** — anon. _Takeaway:_ Evaluates 25 contemporary embedding models across five MTEB tasks and shows high-performing models organize their embedding space consistently — structure retention predicts downstream benchmark performance. _Category:_ 2605.22202. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22202)

**AgenticRAG: Agentic Retrieval for Enterprise Knowledge Bases** — anon. _Takeaway:_ Practical agentic harness for retrieval and analysis over enterprise KBs, lifting the fixed-candidate-set constraint of standard RAG pipelines by letting the model drive grounding. _Category:_ 2605.05538. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05538)
