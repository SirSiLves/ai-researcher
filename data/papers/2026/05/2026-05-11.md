# AI Papers — 2026-05-11

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **LLMs Improving LLMs: Agentic Discovery for Test-Time Scaling** — Tong Zheng, Haolin Liu, Chengsong Huang, Sheng Zhang, et al. _Takeaway:_ AutoTTS reframes test-time scaling as an environment-design problem so LLMs discover their own reasoning patterns rather than relying on hand-crafted heuristics. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.08083 (cs.CL, top HF paper today, 53 upvotes). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08083](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08083)

- **DecodingTrust-Agent Platform (DTap): A Controllable and Interactive Red-Teaming Platform for AI Agents** — Zhaorun Chen, Xun Liu, Haibo Tong, et al. _Takeaway:_ Production-grade red-team harness for agent workflows (API-key leaks, unauthorized actions, data deletion) with controllable adversaries — fills a real gap as enterprises deploy long-horizon agents. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.04808 (cs.AI / cs.CR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04808](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04808)

- **Tool Calling is Linearly Readable and Steerable in Language Models** — Zekun Wu, Ze Wang, Seonglae Cho, et al. (UCL / Holistic AI). _Takeaway:_ The tool a model is about to call is encoded in a linear direction in hidden state across 12 instruction-tuned models (Gemma 3, Qwen 3, Llama 3.1) — gives a cheap pre-execution probe for catching wrong-tool failures before they fire. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.07990 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07990)

- **SkCC: Portable and Secure Skill Compilation for Cross-Framework LLM Agents** — Yipeng Ouyang, Yi Xiao, Yuhao Gu, Xianwei Zhang. _Takeaway:_ Treats the emerging `SKILL.md` standard as source code and compiles it per-framework — addresses up to 40% performance swings between agent runtimes; directly relevant to agent-interoperability tooling. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.03353 (cs.SE / cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03353)

- **Beyond the Black Box: Interpretability of Agentic AI Tool Use** — Hariom Tatsat, Ariye Shater. _Takeaway:_ Studies how agents skip required tools, over-call tools, or take silent harmful actions — proposes interpretability hooks for diagnosing enterprise agent failures. Pair with DTap above for an evaluation-plus-debugging stack. _Category:_ arXiv:2605.06890 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06890)

## Other interesting items

- **[AEM: Adaptive Entropy Modulation for Multi-Turn Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00425)** — Haotian Zhao et al. Dense intermediate supervision for agentic RL without process reward models; tackles sparse-reward credit assignment over long trajectories.
- **[Listwise Policy Optimization: Group-based RLVR as Target-Projection on the LLM Response Simplex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06139)** — Yun Qu et al. Unifies GRPO-style RLVR recipes under a single geometric framework on the response simplex; HF top-3 today (57 upvotes).
- **[HyperEyes: Dual-Grained Efficiency-Aware RL for Parallel Multimodal Search Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07177)** — Guankai Li et al. Parallelizes tool calls within a single agent round — "search wider rather than longer" — useful pattern for multi-tool RAG agents.
- **[Agentick: A Unified Benchmark for General Sequential Decision-Making Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06869)** — Roger Creus Castanyer, Pablo Samuel Castro, Glen Berseth. First benchmark to put RL-from-scratch agents and foundation-model agents on the same playing field.
- **[Tools as Continuous Flow for Evolving Agentic Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07339)** — Tairan Huang et al. Reformulates tool use as a continuous flow instead of step-wise calls; aims to cut error accumulation on long-horizon tasks.
- **[MASPO: Joint Prompt Optimization for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06623)** — Zhexuan Wang et al. Co-optimizes role prompts across collaborating agents rather than tuning each one independently.
- **[MISA: Mixture of Indexer Sparse Attention for Long-Context LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07363)** — Ruijie Zhou et al. Successor to DeepSeek Sparse Attention; cuts indexer cost (the dominant cost on long contexts) at DeepSeek-V3.2 scale.
- **[UniPrefill: Universal Long-Context Prefill Acceleration via Block-wise Dynamic Sparsification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06221)** — Qihang Fan et al. Long-context prefill acceleration that works across hybrid architectures, not just dense transformers.
- **[Hallucination Detection via Activations of Open-Weight Proxy Analyzers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07209)** — Akshita Singh, Prabesh Paudel, Siddhartha Roy. Read closed-model outputs through a small open-weight model's own activations to flag hallucinations — no access to the generator needed.
- **[Beyond Retrieval: A Multitask Benchmark and Model for Code Search (CoREB)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04615)** — Siqiao Xue et al. Contamination-limited code retrieval + reranking benchmark with a fine-tuned reranker; covers the realistic RAG-for-code pipeline.
- **[What if AI systems weren't chatbots?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07896)** — Sourojit Ghosh, Pranav Narayanan Venkit, Sanjana Gautam, Avijit Ghosh. Governance/STS argument that the chatbot interface itself is a sociotechnical lock-in with structural costs — useful framing piece for policy and AI-governance discussions.
- **[Behavior Cue Reasoning: Monitorable Reasoning Improves Efficiency and Safety through Oversight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07021)** — Christopher Z. Cui, Taylor W. Killian, Prithviraj Ammanabrolu. Makes reasoning traces oversight-friendly so misaligned behavior surfaces before the final answer.
