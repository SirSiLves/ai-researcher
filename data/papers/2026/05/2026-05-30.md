## Top picks

**AgentDoG 1.5: A Lightweight and Scalable Alignment Framework for AI Agent Safety and Security** — Dongrui Liu, Yu Li, Zhonghao Yang, Peng Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Open-world agents (e.g. OpenClaw) introduce broad new safety risks as frontier models lower attack barriers; proposes a lightweight, scalable alignment framework for real-world agent deployment. _Category:_ 2605.29801. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29801)

**OmniRetrieval: Unified Retrieval across Heterogeneous Knowledge Sources** — Jinheon Baek, Soyeong Jeong, Sangwoo Park, Woongyeong Yeo et al. _Takeaway:_ Single retriever spanning unstructured text, relational tables, knowledge graphs, and property graphs — addresses fragmentation of knowledge behind fixed query languages in RAG. _Category:_ 2605.29250. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29250)

**Alignment Tampering: How RLHF Is Exploited to Optimize Misaligned Biases** — Dongyoon Hahm, Dylan Hadfield-Menell, Kimin Lee. _Takeaway:_ Identifies a vulnerability where a model under alignment influences its own preference dataset, causing RLHF to amplify undesired behaviors. _Category:_ 2605.27355. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27355)

**When Should Models Change Their Minds? Contextual Belief Management in LLMs** — Haoming Xu, Weihong Xu, Zongrui Li, Mengru Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Frames long-horizon agent reliability as Contextual Belief Management — when to update, preserve, or ignore state while staying aligned with formal evidence. _Category:_ 2605.30219. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30219)

**AsyncTool: Evaluating the Asynchronous Function Calling Capability under Multi-Task Scenarios** — Kou Shi, Ziao Zhang, Shiting Huang, Avery Nie et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmarks tool-use under realistic tool-response latency and concurrent multi-task settings, a dimension prior function-calling evals overlook. _Category:_ 2605.27995. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27995)

**When Cloud Agents Meet Device Agents: Lessons from Hybrid Multi-Agent Systems** — Corrado Rainone, Davide Belli, Bence Major, Arash Behboodi. _Takeaway:_ Studies cost/performance trade-offs of pairing cloud frontier LLMs with on-device small models in hybrid multi-agent inference. _Category:_ 2605.30102. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30102)

**LaRA: Layer-wise Representation Analysis for Detecting Data Contamination in RL Post-Training** — Minju Gwak, Minseo Kwak, Dongseok Lee, Guijin Son et al. _Takeaway:_ First focused look at data contamination during RL post-training, with a layer-wise representation detector to protect generalization and eval reliability. _Category:_ 2605.29888. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29888)

**Why Larger Models Learn More: Effects of Capacity, Interference, and Rare-Task Retention** — Jing Huang, Daniel Wurgaft, Rachit Bansal, Laura Ruis et al. _Takeaway:_ Phenomenological scaling argument for why larger models acquire parts of the data distribution smaller ones cannot, even with infinite data. _Category:_ 2605.29548. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29548)

**Towards Verifiable Multimodal Deep Research: A Multi-Agent Harness for Interleaved Report Generation** — Chenghao Zhang, Guanting Dong, Yufan Liu, Tong Zhao et al. _Takeaway:_ Moves agents from deep search to deep research — a multi-agent harness that synthesizes scattered multimodal evidence into verifiable long-form reports. _Category:_ 2605.29861. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29861)

**How LoRA Remembers? A Parametric Memory Law for LLM Finetuning** — Ziwen Xu, Haiwen Hong, Linsong Yu, Benglei Cui et al. _Takeaway:_ Quantifies the capacity limits of LoRA-based knowledge updates, moving beyond qualitative downstream evals toward a parametric memory law. _Category:_ 2605.30260. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30260)

## Other interesting items
- **[Xetrieval: Mechanistically Explaining Dense Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29507)** — Zhixin Cai, Jun Bai, Yang Liu et al., mechanistic interpretability of why dense retrievers assign high relevance, beyond surface lexical signals.
- **[Is Position Bias in Dense Retrievers Built In — or Learned from Data?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26578)** — Daegon Yu, SeungYoon Han, Woomyoung Park, traces dense-retriever positional bias to data distribution rather than architecture.
- **[CoHyDE: Iterative Co-Training of LLM Rewriter & Dense Encoder for Tool Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29271)** — Vaishali Senthil, Ashutosh Hathidara, Sebastian Schreiber, bridges colloquial queries and technical API vocabulary for agent tool retrieval.
- **[RUBRIC-ARROW: Alternating Pointwise Rubric Reward Modeling for LLM Post-training in Non-verifiable Domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29156)** — Haoxiang Jiang, Zihan Dong, Tianci Liu et al., rubric-decomposed reward modeling that avoids dependence on frontier-LLM judges and Boolean ties.
- **[Verifiable Rewards Beyond Math and Code: Lightweight Corpus-Grounded Process Supervision for Factual QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29648)** — Shicheng Fan, Haochang Hao, Dehai Min et al., finer-grained RL reward design for factual accuracy in knowledge-intensive QA.
- **[WorldMemArena: Evaluating Multimodal Agent Memory Through Action-World Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29341)** — Chengzhi Liu, Yuzhe Yang, Sophia Xiao Pu et al., benchmark for evolving-world agent memory beyond static-dialogue recall.
- **[Skill0.5: Joint Skill Internalization and Utilization for OOD Generalization in Agentic RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28424)** — Jiapeng Zhu, Jianxiang Yu, Yibo Zhao et al., separates general vs task-specific agent skills in skill-based reinforcement learning.
- **[PRISM: A Multi-Dimensional Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Peer Reviewers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26730)** — Ngoc Phan Phuoc Loc, Toan Huynh La Viet et al., measures how LLM reviewers compare to humans at catching scientific gaps.
