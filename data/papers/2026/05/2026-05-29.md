## Top picks
For each: **Title** — authors. _Takeaway:_ 1 line. _Category:_ {arxiv id}. [link]

- **OmniRetrieval: Unified Retrieval across Heterogeneous Knowledge Sources** — Jinheon Baek, Soyeong Jeong, Sangwoo Park, et al. _Takeaway:_ A single retriever that queries unstructured text, tables, knowledge graphs and property graphs without collapsing them into one lossy shared space — a step toward RAG over genuinely mixed enterprise knowledge. _Category:_ cs.CL 2605.29250. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29250)
- **CoHyDE: Iterative Co-Training of LLM Rewriter & Dense Encoder for Tool Retrieval** — Vaishali Senthil, Ashutosh Hathidara, Sebastian Schreiber. _Takeaway:_ Jointly trains the query rewriter and the dense encoder to bridge colloquial user queries and technical API vocabulary — directly targets the tool-retrieval bottleneck for large-catalog agents. _Category:_ cs.AI 2605.29271. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29271)
- **GenesisFunc: Multi-Agent Data Generation for Accurate and Generalizable Function-Calling** — Hao-Xiang Xu, Chong Deng, Jiaqing Liu, et al. _Takeaway:_ An automated multi-agent pipeline for synthesizing reliable, diverse function-calling training data, addressing the brittle-API / weak-quality-control problems of existing FC datasets. _Category:_ cs.CL 2605.28835. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28835)
- **How Consistent Are LLM Agents? Measuring Behavioral Reproducibility in Multi-Step Tool-Calling Pipelines** — Abel Yagubyan. _Takeaway:_ Systematic study of whether tool-calling agents pick the same tools, order, and arguments across identical repeated runs — a reliability dimension production teams routinely overlook. _Category:_ cs.CL 2605.28840. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28840)
- **The Importance of Out-of-Band Metadata for Safe Autonomous Agents: The Redpanda Agentic Data Plane** — Tyler Akidau, Tyler Rockwood, Johannes Brüderl, Marc Millstone. _Takeaway:_ Argues that "digital-employee" agents need an out-of-band data-plane layer for provenance and policy rather than trusting in-band instructions — a concrete infra pattern for safe enterprise agents. _Category:_ cs.AI 2605.29082. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29082)
- **Robust and Efficient Guardrails with Latent Reasoning** — Siddharth Sai, Xiaofei Wen, Muhao Chen. _Takeaway:_ Moves reasoning-based safety guardrails into latent space to keep the accuracy gains over classifiers while cutting the query latency and token overhead that make reasoning guardrails impractical at high throughput. _Category:_ cs.CL 2605.29068. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29068)
- **Relevance as a Vulnerability: How Web Retrieval Degrades Safety Alignment in LLM Agents** — Aditya Nawal, Manit Baser, Mohan Gurusamy. _Takeaway:_ Introduces AgentREVEAL to diagnose how enabling web retrieval in agents weakens safety alignment and raises compliance with harmful requests — a retrieval-specific attack surface for agentic RAG. _Category:_ cs.CL 2605.29224. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29224)
- **Governing Technical Debt in Agentic AI Systems** — Muhammad Zia Hydari, Raja Iqbal, Narayan Ramasubbu. _Takeaway:_ Defines "Agentic Technical Debt" — the liability accumulating in prompts, memory, tool schemas, orchestration graphs and control policies — and frames governance gaps not covered by classic software or ML tech-debt. _Category:_ cs.AI 2605.29129. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29129)
- **The Best-Laid SCHEMEs: Coordinated Sabotage and Monitoring in Multi-Agent Systems** — Nikolay Radev, Lennart Haas, Benjamin Arnav, Pablo Bernabeu-Pérez. _Takeaway:_ A 17-task benchmark testing whether multiple coding-agent instances can secretly collude on a malicious side-task while appearing aligned — a sharp multi-agent safety / monitoring probe. _Category:_ cs.MA 2605.29178. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29178)
- **Dissociative Identity: Language Model Agents Lack Grounding for Reputation Mechanisms** — Botao Amber Hu, Helena Rong, Max Van Kleek. _Takeaway:_ Argues "Know Your Agent" / reputation regimes borrowed from human KYC fundamentally break for LLM agents because they lack stable identity grounding — a governance caution for the emerging agentic web. _Category:_ cs.AI 2605.30169. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30169)
- **Uncertainty Quantification for Multimodal Retrieval Augmented Generation** — Simon Binz, Heydar Soudani, Faegheh Hasibi. _Takeaway:_ Extends UQ methods to multimodal RAG over vision-language models, estimating answer reliability when visual + textual evidence is combined. _Category:_ cs.IR 2605.29956. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29956)
- **Can It Reach the Generator? Investigating the Survival of Prompt-Injection Attacks in Realistic RAG Settings** — Yu Yin, Shuai Wang, Bevan Koopman, Guido Zuccon. _Takeaway:_ Tests whether prompt-injection / GEO attacks survive the retriever and reranker rather than assuming direct injection into the generator — recalibrates the real-world risk of RAG-recommendation poisoning. _Category:_ cs.IR 2605.28017. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28017)

## Other interesting items
- **[Beyond Consensus: Trace-Level Synthesis in Mixture of Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29116)** — Shreyas Fadnavis, Praitayini Kanakaraj, Felix Wyss. An aggregator reading full reasoning traces beats majority voting — the "aggregation paradox" recovers correct answers even when agents unanimously agree.
- **[Evolve as a Team: Collaborative Self-Evolution for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29790)** — Zhezheng Hao, Tianfu Wang, Huanshuo Dong, et al. Experience-driven self-improvement for multi-agent systems that learn from their own long-horizon execution failures.
- **[MINDGAMES: A Live Arena for Evaluating Social and Strategic Reasoning in Multi-Agent LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29512)** — Kevin Wang, Anna Thöni, Benjamin Kempinski, et al. A multi-game live arena for sustained social/strategic reasoning, going beyond static vignettes and single-game benchmarks.
- **[Provably Secure Agent Guardrail](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29251)** — Benlong Wu, Weiming Zhang, Kejiang Chen, et al. Aims for deterministic security lower bounds for agent guardrails against semantic decoupling attacks, vs. probabilistic LLM-adjudicator defenses.
- **[GRASP: Plan-Guided Graph Retrieval with Adaptive Fusion and Reranking on Semi-Structured Knowledge Bases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30237)** — Yicheng Tao, Yiqun Wang, Xiangchen Song, et al. Three-stage retrieval over typed entity-relation graphs for product, paper, and precision-medicine search.
- **[When Cloud Agents Meet Device Agents: Lessons from Hybrid Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30102)** — Corrado Rainone, Davide Belli, Bence Major, Arash Behboodi. Practical lessons on combining cloud frontier LLMs with on-device SLMs in hybrid multi-agent inference for cost/quality trade-offs.
- **[Benchmarking Open-Source Safety Guard Models: A Comprehensive Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28830)** — Reetu Raj Harsh, Bhaskarjit Sarmah, Stefano Pasquali. Evaluates 14 open-source safety guard models on 79k samples across 8 NIST AI RMF safety categories.
- **[GrepSeek: Training Search Agents for Direct Corpus Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29307)** — Alireza Salemi, Chang Zeng, Atharva Nijasure, et al. Trains search agents to interact with the corpus directly rather than via a fixed pre-indexed retriever.
- **[Xetrieval: Mechanistically Explaining Dense Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29507)** — Zhixin Cai, Jun Bai, Yang Liu, et al. Embedding-level mechanistic framework for why dense retrievers assign relevance, beyond lexical-match surface signals.
- **[Apertus LLM Family Expansion via Distillation and Quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29128)** — Andrei Panferov, Davit Melikidze, Martin Jaggi, Dan Alistarh. Distillation + quantization to expand the open Apertus model family across hardware/budget constraints (Swiss EPFL/ETH-linked open-weights effort).
