# AI Papers — 2026-07-07

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Gemma 4 Technical Report** — Gemma Team (Google DeepMind). _Takeaway:_ New generation of open-weight, natively multimodal Gemma models (2.3B–31B, dense + MoE) with an encoder-free 12B ingesting raw audio/image patches, an integrated "thinking mode," and big gains on STEM/multimodal/long-context — rivaling larger frontier open models in human-rated tasks. _Category:_ 2607.02770. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02770)

**Hierarchical Sparse Attention Done Right: Toward Infinite Context Modeling** — Xiang Hu, Xinyu Wei, et al. _Takeaway:_ HiLS attention learns chunk selection end-to-end under the LM loss, matching or beating full attention in-domain while extrapolating >64× the training context length at 90% retrieval accuracy; existing full-attention models can be converted with lightweight continued pretraining. _Category:_ 2607.02980. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02980)

**Separating Representation from Reconstruction Enables Scalable Text Encoders** — Megi Dervishi, Mathurin Videau, Yann LeCun. _Takeaway:_ CrossBERT decouples representation learning from token reconstruction so encoders finally scale monotonically like decoders — enabling ≥50% masking, 1.5–2× throughput and 2× sample efficiency, with superior MTEB(eng, v2) and frozen-GLUE results. _Category:_ 2607.04011. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04011)

**SelfMem: Self-Optimizing Memory for AI Agents** — Shu Yang, Junchao Wu, Derek F. Wong, Di Wang. _Takeaway:_ Instead of a fixed store/retrieve/summarize pipeline, SelfMem gives the agent memory tools + feedback and lets it learn its own memory strategy — beating retrieval/compression/agent-memory baselines on BEAM by 40–49% across 100K–1M-token conversations. _Category:_ 2607.03726. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03726)

**LLM-as-a-Verifier: A General-Purpose Verification Framework** — Jacky Kwok, Yuejiang Liu, Chelsea Finn, Ion Stoica, Azalia Mirhoseini, et al. _Takeaway:_ Treats verification as a new scaling axis: continuous scores from scoring-token logits (vs. discrete LM-judge scores) scale along granularity, repeated evaluation, and criteria decomposition — SOTA on Terminal-Bench V2 (86.5%), SWE-Bench Verified (78.2%), and MedAgentBench, and ships a Claude Code extension. _Category:_ 2607.05391. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05391)

**The Remarkable Effectiveness of Providing AI Agents with Natural Language Tools (NLT across 14 models)** — Alexander Somma, Isabelle Plante, Fred Premji. _Takeaway:_ Independent replication (14 models, 8,560 trials) confirms natural-language tools beat structured tool-calling by +14.9pp accuracy and cut critical errors 93% while saving 25% tokens — gains largest for non-native-tool, reasoning, and smaller models; a practical alternative for reliability-critical agentic systems. _Category:_ 2607.03953. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03953)

**Oyster-II: Reinforcement Learning for Constructive Safety Alignment in LLMs** — Jiyang Guan, Yong Xie, et al. (Alibaba). _Takeaway:_ Moves past blanket refusal toward "constructive safety" via a Zero-RL multi-stage RL scheme, fixing OOD safety generalization and "safety-CoT over-generalization"; surpasses Qwen3-14B and Oyster-I on safety while staying helpful, matching much larger models. _Category:_ 2607.02914. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02914)

**How to Avoid Debate: Scalable AI Safety via Doubly-Efficient Interactive Proofs** — Liyan Chen, Yael Tauman Kalai, Zoe Xi. _Takeaway:_ Initiates single-prover interactive proofs for AI safety, removing debate's unrealistic assumption of two equal, one-truthful provers; gives doubly-efficient relativizing proofs for oracle-aided computations under noise-tolerant or low-degree-oracle conditions. _Category:_ 2607.03561. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03561)

**MIRAGE: Defending Long-Form RAG Against Misinformation Pollution** — Saadeldine Eletter, Yuxia Wang, Preslav Nakov, et al. _Takeaway:_ Training-free, model-agnostic defense that builds an NLI-based cross-document claim graph and a "Defended-Claims Gate" to condition generation only on multi-source-supported claims (or answer parametrically); restores factuality under mixed/polluted evidence across four long-form QA benchmarks. _Category:_ 2607.05069. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05069)

## Other interesting items
- **[TRIAGE: Trustworthy Retrieval Instrumentation And Graph Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03447)** — Axel TahmasebiMoradi et al. Stage-aware instrumentation for automated Graph-RAG (extraction / graph construction / usage) with no-gold-needed metrics that localize which stage caused a wrong answer.
- **[A Workflow-Aware Serving Layer for Agentic Applications (Dyserve)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02942)** — Jiayi Qian et al. Compiles per-node model/verifier/backend choices for an agent workflow into one ILP over a heterogeneous pool, concentrating strong models + verification on the highest-error-propagation nodes under serving load.
- **[Individual Parameters in Weight-Sparse Transformers Appear Interpretable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02964)** — Arnau Marin-Llobet, Stefan Heimersheim. An automated LLM pipeline writes and verifies a human-readable description of *when* each weight matters; 12–31% of weights in sparse transformers get a single validated description (far more than dense).
- **[Faithfulness to Refusal: A Causal Audit of Neuron Selectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05355)** — Ananth Eswar et al. Causally audits neuron-selection methods used to explain refusal behavior in aligned LLMs (papers.cool top-3 today).
- **[Safety Testing LLM Agents at Scale: From Risk Discovery to Evidence-Grounded Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01793)** — HF Papers daily pick; pipeline for discovering agent risks and verifying them with grounded evidence rather than unverified red-team claims.
- **[Autonomous Information Seeking: A Roadmap for Agentic Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04433)** — Xinyu Lin et al. Synthesis introducing an autonomy-graded taxonomy (agent-assisted / agent-as-recommender / agent-as-user-simulator) and mapping agentic evaluation gaps (trajectory-level, contribution, simulation calibration).
