## Top picks

- **UniClawBench: A Universal Benchmark for Proactive Agents on Real-World Tasks** — Zhekai Chen et al. (HKU-MMLab). _Takeaway:_ First capability-driven benchmark for proactive agents, built around five foundational capabilities (Skill Usage, Exploration, Long-Context Reasoning, Multimodal Understanding, Cross-Platform Coordination) over 400 bilingual tasks. Evaluates agents live in Docker containers with step-by-step checkpoints and a closed-loop executor/supervisor/user-agent design that simulates multi-turn feedback without leaking grading criteria — deliberately disentangling base-model capability from agent-framework design. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08768 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08768)

- **WebSwarm: Recursive Multi-Agent Orchestration for Deep-and-Wide Web Search** — Xiaoshuai Song et al. (incl. Zhicheng Dou). _Takeaway:_ Progressive recursive delegation framework in which agentic search nodes each pair a local objective with a search mode and can either solve directly or spawn child nodes that return evidence upward for parents to revise and aggregate. Beats single- and multi-agent baselines on BrowseComp-Plus, WideSearch, DeepWideSearch, and GISA — a concrete recipe for handling search depth and coverage jointly at inference time. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08662 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08662](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08662)

- **Do You Need a Frontier Model as a Citation Verifier? Benchmarking Rubric LLMs for Deep-Research Source Attribution** — Ethan Leung et al. _Takeaway:_ Scores 8 off-the-shelf judges over 1,248 human-reviewed rubric decisions on citation quality (source relevance + factual support). Cheaper judges stay competitive — GPT-5-mini leads source-relevance F1 (0.908) — but scalar F1 hides directional bias (pass-rate drift, FP/FN rates), which is exactly what a downstream RL reward loop would reinforce. The lesson: calibrate the judge rather than buy the biggest model before using citation rubrics as reward. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08700 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08700](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08700)

- **When the Judge Changes, So Does the Measurement: Auditing LLM-as-Judge Reliability** — Zongyou Yang et al. _Takeaway:_ Frames evaluator-replacement drift as a measurement-validity problem across four datasets and two real upgrade paths (Qwen3 1.7B→32B; successive MiniMax M2 API releases). Judge upgrades are not interchangeable (only Qwen3 1.7B→4B gives a robust adjacent gain), stronger judges only reduce — not remove — position and verbosity bias, and repeated-sample juries add little when errors correlate. Argues LLM-as-judge reports must ship dataset slices, bias probes, error-dependence estimates, and protocol audit trails. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08535 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08535](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08535)

- **Two Axes of LLM Abstention: Answer Correctness and Question Answerability** — Benedikt J. Wagner. _Takeaway:_ Shows refusing wrong answers and refusing unanswerable/false-premise questions are separate axes: ordinary answer-confidence tracks correctness but is nearly blind to answerability, while a hidden-state linear probe does the reverse (0.69–0.77 AUROC on false-premise CREPE, where confidence, P(IK), P(True) all sit near chance). The blind spot doesn't shrink with scale, but routing a premise check with the probe roughly triples challenge precision and yields a calibrated two-threshold abstention policy. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08456 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08456](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08456)

- **Hidden Decoding at Scale: Latent Computation Scaling for Large Language Models** — Aiwei Liu et al. _Takeaway:_ Fixed-backbone scaling that adds computation along the sequence-length dimension instead of widening Transformer layers: each token is expanded into n streams whose intermediate KV cache serves as context, with Stream-Factorized Attention keeping attention cost roughly linear in n. Demonstrated at frontier scale (WeLM-HD4-80B and -617B at n=4), it is the first sequence-length scaling method shown at 100B+ MoE scale, with gains that grow as n increases — a practical continued-pretraining path when a fresh full pretrain is too costly. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08186 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08186)

- **XAlpha: A Memory-Driven AI Quant Researcher for Hypothesis-to-Code Alpha Discovery** — Fengyuan Liu et al. _Takeaway:_ Closes the loop from research reading to executable factor code via three cooperating agents over a multi-source research memory: a Macro Brain plans themes and picks archetypes, a Micro Brain compiles hypotheses into factor code with ex-ante tri-alignment checks (idea / code logic / financial plausibility), and a Cross Brain consolidates outcomes into generation-, cycle-, and archetype-level feedback. Outperforms baselines on CSI300 — a clean instance of memory-driven, multi-agent research automation in a numeric vertical. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08332 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08332)

- **MAESTRO: Markov-chain Approximated Expert Sparsification via Transition-based ROuting** — Palaash Goel et al. _Takeaway:_ Structured pruning for Mixture-of-Experts that models autoregressive expert-activation trajectories as ergodic Markov chains, using their stationary distributions to score experts with cross-layer, routing-aware importance rather than local heuristics blind to MoE routing. Beats SOTA by up to 10.61% average-performance retention under a strict 50% compression regime (across Safety, Bias, Ethics and other domains) with substantially lower cross-task variance — attacking the always-resident expert-bank memory bottleneck. _Category:_ arXiv:2607.08601 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08601)

## Other interesting items

- **[UltraX: Refining Pre-Training Data at Scale with Adaptive Programmatic Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08646)** — cs.CL. Function-calling data-refinement framework that completes the editing space with insertion (alongside deletion/modification) for fine-grained instance-level pretraining-data editing; matches or beats baselines with fewer training tokens. Aimed squarely at the "quality utilization > data expansion" regime as scaling-law gains flatten.
- **[Prompt Compression via Activation Aggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08399)** — cs.CL. Compresses an instruction prompt into a single activation vector (a learned weighted sum of mid-layer activations re-injected at an early layer) with under 2% accuracy drop — cutting per-query compute for fixed prompts and exposing genuine cross-layer structure in the activation space.
- **[DominoTree: Conditional Tree-Structured Drafting with Domino for Speculative Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08642)** — cs.CL. Conditional tree-structured drafting for speculative decoding (evaluated on Qwen3) — an inference-throughput play in the same lineage as recent block-diffusion drafters.
- **[SQuaD-SQL: Efficient Text-to-SQL with Small Language Models via LLM-Guided Knowledge Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08161)** — cs.CL. Distills LLM text-to-SQL competence into small models through LLM-guided synthetic data — a cost-efficient route to enterprise NL2SQL without a frontier model in the loop.
- **[Validity of LLMs as data annotators: AMALIA on authority](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08731)** — cs.CL. Tests whether Portugal's publicly funded 9B sovereign model AMALIA validly measures a theoretical construct (the moral foundation of "authority") rather than merely agreeing with human coders; decomposition recovers only ~half its holistic performance, arguing sovereign-LLM benchmark batteries must test the evidential route, not just agreement. Governance-relevant for national/sovereign-model claims.
- **[Grounded Event Extraction from SEC 8-K Filings with a Fine-Grained Taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08346)** — cs.CL. Quote-grounded event extraction over SEC 8-K disclosures with a fine-grained event taxonomy — structured, auditable financial information extraction for enterprise pipelines.
