# AI Papers — 2026-07-21

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**An Early Warning of Emerging Biosecurity Risks in Frontier LLMs** — Zhida He, Jing Shao, Chaochao Lu, et al. _Takeaway:_ Introduces a biosecurity benchmark to measure frontier models' biological capabilities and warns that these are outpacing current safeguards. _Category:_ cs.CL — 2607.18056. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18056)

**TRACE: Trajectory-Based Safety Patch Learning for LLM Post-Training Realignment** — Changyue Li, Jiaming He, Youliang Yuan, et al. _Takeaway:_ Restores safety alignment eroded by fine-tuning-as-a-service via lightweight trajectory-based "safety patches," without full retraining. _Category:_ cs.LG — 2607.16242. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16242)

**Coercion and Deception in AI-to-AI Management: An Agentic Benchmark of Unprompted Escalation** — Jasmine Brazilek, et al. _Takeaway:_ Agentic benchmark probing whether AI agents that manage other AI agents spontaneously escalate to coercion or deception — a governance/safety red flag. _Category:_ HF Papers — 2607.15434. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15434)

**RECON: Benchmarking Agent Memory for Compositional Reasoning over Long Contexts** — Mihir Shriniwas Arya. _Takeaway:_ New benchmark stress-testing agent memory (retain / access / reason) for compositional reasoning across long contexts, the failure mode behind flaky copilots. _Category:_ cs.AI — 2607.16716. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16716)

**Environment-free Synthetic Data Generation for API-Calling Agents** — Seanie Lee, Alexander T. Toshev, Oncel Tuzel, Raviteja Vemulapalli, et al. (Apple). _Takeaway:_ Generates high-quality tool/API-calling agent training trajectories without needing fully implemented executable environments. HF-featured. _Category:_ cs.AI — 2607.16900. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16900)

**TurboVec: Cost-Efficient Private Retrieval for Enterprise RAG via Codebook-Oblivious Quantization** — Navnit Shukla, Kamal Pandey, Omsankar Tiwari. _Takeaway:_ Addresses privacy leakage and cost in the vector-retrieval layer of enterprise RAG through codebook-oblivious quantization. _Category:_ cs.LG — 2607.16973. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16973)

**Verify, Repair, Repeat, or Stop? Robust Stopping for Noisy Verify-Repair Loops in LLM Agents** — Yitao Wu, Si Shen, Rui Yang, Hong Peng, Bin Hu. _Takeaway:_ Derives robust stopping criteria so agents in noisy verify-repair loops (code, math, tool use) don't damage already-correct outputs. _Category:_ cs.AI — 2607.17641. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17641)

**Group Entropy-Controlled Policy Optimization** — Guangran Cheng, Chengqi Lyu, Songyang Gao, Wenwei Zhang, Kai Chen. _Takeaway:_ RL post-training method that controls entropy at the group level to better balance exploration/exploitation during LLM alignment. HF-featured. _Category:_ cs.CL — 2607.16850. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16850)

## Other interesting items

- **[C$^2$KV: Compressed and Composable KV Cache Reuse for Efficient LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17715)** — Chuheng Du, Junyi Chen, … Fan Wu, et al. Compressed, composable KV-cache reuse to cut long-context inference cost in RAG and multi-document reasoning.
- **[Masked Diffusion Language Models are Strong and Steerable Text-Based World Models for Agentic RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16204)** — Darshan Deshpande, et al. (HF) Positions masked-diffusion LMs as steerable world models for training agents with RL.
- **[jina-reranker-v3.5: An Efficient Listwise Reranker with Hybrid Attention and Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18152)** — Christina Nasika, Feng Wang, Antonis Krasakis, Han Xiao (Jina AI). A 0.6B listwise reranker built for production agentic retrieval pipelines.
- **[FinSAgent: Corpus-Aligned Multi-Agent RAG for Evidence-Grounded SEC Filing QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18102)** — Jijun Chi, et al. (U Toronto). Multi-agent RAG that grounds answers over long, redundant SEC-filing disclosures.
- **[Adapting Embedding Models for Agent Capability Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17347)** — Tingwei Chen, Yunxiao Shi, Qingsong Wen, Min Xu, et al. Tunes embeddings to retrieve across mixed marketplaces of agents, tool bundles, and skills.
- **[FIFA World Cup 2026 as a Contamination-Free Benchmark for LLM Forecasting Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17765)** — Jiacheng Ding, Cong Guo, Jason Xu. Uses the 104 future WC matches as a leakage-free benchmark for frontier LLMs as forecasting agents (HF companion: WorldCupArena, 2607.18084).
- **[Debate-on-Graph: Reliable and Adaptive Reasoning of LLMs on Uncertain Knowledge Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17266)** — Peiji Yu, Xin Chen, Tianxing Wu. Multi-round debate over a knowledge graph to curb hallucination in KG-grounded QA.
