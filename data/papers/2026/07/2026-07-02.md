# AI Papers — 2026-07-02

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **Seed2.0 Model Card: Towards Intelligence Frontier for Real-World Complexity** — ByteDance Seed. _Takeaway:_ ByteDance's new frontier model series, built around an evaluation system abstracted from genuine user needs and realistic complex scenarios rather than legacy benchmarks. _Category:_ cs.AI (2607.00248). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00248)
- **AutoMem: Automated Learning of Memory as a Cognitive Skill** — Shengguang Wu, Hao Zhu, Yuhui Zhang, Xiaohan Wang, Serena Yeung-Levy. _Takeaway:_ Treats agent memory management (file-system ops as first-class actions) as a trainable skill with two automated improvement loops; memory optimization alone lifts long-horizon game performance ~2–4x and makes a 32B open model competitive with Claude Opus 4.5 / Gemini 3.1 Pro Thinking. _Category:_ cs.AI (2607.01224). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01224)
- **Can Agents Generalize to the Open World? Unveiling the Fragility of Static Training in Tool Use** — Song-Lin Lv, Weiming Wu, Rui Zhu, Zi-Jian Cheng, Lan-Zhe Guo. _Takeaway:_ Formalizes "OpenAgent" — tool-use under shifting queries, tool sets, and interaction dynamics — and shows agents trained on static tool-use benchmarks break down in this open-world setting. _Category:_ cs.AI (2607.01084). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01084)
- **Beyond the Prompt: Jailbreaking Function-Calling LLMs via Simulated Moderation Traces** — Junlong Liu, Haobo Wang, Weiqi Luo, Xiaojun Jia. _Takeaway:_ Prompt-level defenses miss a structural vulnerability in stateful function-calling environments — injected fake moderation traces jailbreak tool-calling LLMs; directly relevant to MCP/tool-use runtime security. _Category:_ cs.CR→cs.AI (2607.00481). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00481)
- **MemSyco-Bench: Benchmarking Sycophancy in Agent Memory** — Zhishang Xiang, Zerui Chen, Yunbo Tang, et al. (Jinsong Su group). _Takeaway:_ Retrieved long-term memories can induce sycophancy — agents over-align with the user at the cost of factual accuracy; a benchmark to measure it. Also on HF Papers today. _Category:_ cs.IR (2607.01071). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01071)
- **One Retrieval to Cover Them All: Co-occurrence-Aware Knowledge Base Reorganization for Session-Level RAG** — Shivam Ratnakar, Yixuan Zhu, Cecilia Cheng, Chaya Vijayakumar. _Takeaway:_ A single retrieval covers only 41% of a user's session-level need; offline co-occurrence-aware KB clustering raises it to 58%, cuts retrieval calls 34%, and compresses the KB to 20% — argues session coverage should replace single-query recall as the enterprise RAG metric. _Category:_ cs.IR (2606.31156). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31156)
- **Staleness-Learning Rate Scaling Laws for Asynchronous RLHF** — Jingwei Song, Haofeng Xu, Jie Xiao, et al. _Takeaway:_ Makes the behavior policy explicit in the asynchronous GRPO objective and derives scaling laws linking rollout staleness to learning rate — practical guidance for high-throughput decoupled RLHF systems. _Category:_ cs.LG (2607.01083). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01083)
- **Self-GC: Self-Governing Context for Long-Horizon LLM Agents** — Xubin Hao, Hongjin Meng, Xin Yin, Jiawei Zhu, Chenpeng Cao. _Takeaway:_ Agent context (tool results, files, plans, constraints) is too structured for chronological pruning or end-of-window self-summary; proposes agents that govern their own context as a first-class mechanism. _Category:_ cs.AI (2607.00692). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00692)

## Other interesting items

- **[Adversarial Pragmatics for AI Safety Evaluation: A Benchmark for Instruction Conflict, Embedded Commands, and Policy Ambiguity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01153)** — Brett Reynolds; linguistically controlled taxonomy + validated seed benchmark for instruction conflict, embedded commands, quotation/scope ambiguity, and multi-turn agent transcripts (cs.CL, 2607.01153).
- **[Is One Layer Enough? Training A Single Transformer Layer Can Match Full-Parameter RL Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01232)** — Zijian Zhang, Rizhen Hu, et al. (Mingyi Hong group); RL post-training adaptation is highly localized — one layer can match full-parameter RL, big efficiency lever (cs.LG, 2607.01232).
- **[From Signals to Structure: How Memory Architecture Drives Language Emergence in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00233)** — Yashar Talebirad, Eden Redman, Ali Parsaee, Osmar R. Zaiane; in Lewis signaling games, a persistent private notebook matters more than channel capacity for agents converging on stable conventions (cs.MA, 2607.00233).
- **[Are Performance-Optimization Benchmarks Reliably Measuring Coding Agents?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01211)** — Zhi Chen, Zhensu Sun, Yuling Shi, David Lo, Lingxiao Jiang; audits GSO / SWE-Perf / SWE-fficiency reliability just as their leaderboards become evidence of coding-agent progress (cs.SE, 2607.01211).
- **[GenPage: Towards End-to-End Generative Homepage Construction at Netflix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31031)** — Lequn Wang, Jiangwei Pan, Fengdi Che, Linas Baltrunas; one transformer autoregressively generates the entire Netflix homepage (LLM-style pretrain + post-train recipe), +0.24% core engagement and −20% serving latency in A/B (cs.IR, 2606.31031).
- **[GR2 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31984)** — Yufei Li et al.; generative reasoning re-ranker trained with semantic-ID mid-training, distilled reasoning, and RL with verifiable rewards; notable finding: re-ranking LLMs hack rewards via position bias, motivating conditional verifiable rewards (cs.IR, 2606.31984).
- **[Measuring the Gap Between Human and LLM Research Ideas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01233)** — Ziyu Chen, Yilun Zhao, Arman Cohan; large-scale ideation eval shows LLM research ideas cluster on bridge-like/synthesis patterns — systematically narrower than human research taste (cs.CL, 2607.01233).
