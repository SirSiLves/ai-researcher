# AI Papers — 2026-07-08

## Top picks

**The Balkanization of Execution-Security Research for AI Coding Agents** — Mohammadreza Rashidi. _Takeaway:_ Systematizes 39 papers (2023–2026) into 17 categories spanning sandbox isolation, capability/access control, TOCTOU races, MCP threats, identity delegation, execution provenance and egress control — a fragmented literature that rarely cites itself — into one map of the coding-agent execution-security landscape. _Category:_ 2607.05743. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05743)

**Unicode TAG-Block Concealment of Tool-Metadata Payloads in the Model Context Protocol** — Mohammadreza Rashidi. _Takeaway:_ Exposes an "approval-view fidelity gap" in MCP: nothing requires the tool metadata shown in the one-time approval dialog to match the bytes injected into the model every turn; Unicode TAG-block concealment hides payloads across three independent server implementations, model-free and protocol-free. _Category:_ 2607.05744. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05744)

**Memory in the Loop: In-Process Retrieval as Extended Working Memory for Language Agents** — Yusuf Khan, Carlo Lipizzi. _Takeaway:_ Argues memory latency is a property of *where the store lives*, not the once-per-turn pattern; moving retrieval in-process (read/written every step) removes the up-to-83× latency blowup that forced prior "memory-first" designs to ration retrieval to once per turn. _Category:_ 2607.05690. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05690)

**DynaKRAG: A Unified Framework for Learnable Evidence Control in Multi-Hop RAG** — Yaqi Wu, Xiaolei Guo, et al. _Takeaway:_ Formulates multi-hop evidence acquisition as a state-conditioned policy that chooses among currently valid operations (iterative retrieval, query reformulation, evidence critique, sufficiency judging) instead of hard-coding them into method-specific pipelines or fixed control topologies. _Category:_ 2607.06507. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06507)

**Beyond the Leaderboard: A Synthesis of Tool-Use, Planning, and Reasoning Failures in LLM Agents** — Wael Albayaydh, Rui Zhao, Ivan Flechais. _Takeaway:_ Synthesizes 27 benchmark/taxonomy/audit papers (2023–2026) across 19 benchmarks into the first cross-cutting taxonomy of agent limitations spanning tool use, planning, long-horizon reasoning, multi-agent coordination, safety and measurement validity — surfacing recurring failure modes that reported benchmark gains obscure. _Category:_ 2607.05775. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05775)

**Doomed from the Start: Early Abort of LLM Agent Episodes via a Recall-Controlled Probe Cascade** — Kai Ruan, Zihe Huang, et al. _Takeaway:_ Lightweight per-round probes on hidden activations predict eventual episode failure as early as the first interaction round (where behavior-only scorers are near chance), turned into a distribution-free calibrated abort cascade that reclaims inference compute wasted on doomed trajectories. _Category:_ 2607.06503. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06503)

**Evaluating Calibrated Refusal and Safe Usefulness in Dual-Use Biology Settings** — Edwin H. Wintermute, Harmon Bhasin, et al. _Takeaway:_ Introduces BioSecBench-Refusal (61 legitimate Routine tasks paired with 46 concealed-hazard Red-Team tasks); across 16 model-harness configs refusal ranged 7–74% (Routine) and 1–62% (Red-Team), with many setups refusing legitimate work as often as hazardous requests — poor refusal calibration, not just under-refusal. _Category:_ 2607.05462. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05462)

**Information Gain-based Rollout Policy Optimization (IGRPO) for Multi-Turn LLM Agents** — Yijun Zhang, Fan Xu, et al. _Takeaway:_ Adaptive tree-structured RL that allocates the rollout budget by the information gain of intermediate states instead of spending compute uniformly on low-value branches — targeting the long-horizon search regime where branches vary drastically in informativeness. _Category:_ 2607.06223. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06223)

## Other interesting items
- **[KAT-Coder-V2.5 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05471)** — Bo Huang et al. Coding-focused agentic model trained to act autonomously inside real executable repos; AutoBuilder reconstructs multilingual repos into sandboxed environments with fail-to-pass / pass-to-pass verification at scale to supply reproducible rewards and high-value trajectories.
- **[Controlling Tool Use with Heading-Specific Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05790)** — Yuqi Chen, Vincent Siu, Dawn Song, et al. Steering vectors from heading-anchor positions give bidirectional causal control over tool-invocation behavior across five open models and three domains, curbing unnecessary tool calls despite tools existing only in context.
- **[Lingering Authority: Revocable Resource-and-Effect Capabilities for Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22504)** — Igor Santos-Grueiro. PORTICO reference monitor compiles a task contract into initial capabilities, grant rules and deny rules; a request-grant-invoke lifecycle issues epoch-bound handles and revokes them on subgoal closure, closing the gap where broad tool access lingers past the subgoal that justified it.
- **[Auditing of Unlearning Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05898)** — Sahasrajit Sarmasarkar, Anastasia Koloskova, Sanmi Koyejo. A practical auditor computes data-dependent lower bounds on the unlearning ε via membership-inference attacks, cleanly separating rigorously-guaranteed methods (clipping, rewind-to-delete) from empirical ones that fail to falsify their claims.
- **[Benchmarking KV-Cache Optimizations for Long-Context Serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05399)** — Nikita Agrawal, Ruben Mayer. Workload-aware benchmark comparing quantization/pruning/merging (KIVI, TurboQuant, SnapKV, CaM) on both task quality and system performance across LongBench-style workloads — apples-to-apples where prior evals used mismatched models/tasks/stacks.
- **[Learn to Pool: Lightweight Fine-Tuning for Flexible Multi-Vector Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06036)** — Stefan Josef. Late-interaction (ColBERT-style) retrievers emit many token vectors per doc; even minimal pooling-aware k-means fine-tuning yields strong compression, a practical alternative to costly large-scale pooling-aware training.
- **[CMDR: Contextual Multimodal Document Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05927)** — Ryota Tanaka, Taku Hasegawa, Kyosuke Nishida. New task + benchmark (CMDR-Bench) requiring retrieval to model cross-page document context rather than encode pages independently, plus a contextual multimodal embedding model (CMDR-Embed) preserving text and visual content.
- **[Privilege and Confidentiality in Generative AI Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05479)** — Václav Janeček, Thomas Melham. Governance analysis of how GenAI stores client data three ways (parameters, context window, RAG knowledge bases), each with distinct confidentiality/legal-privilege risks, grounded in the first English and US court decisions on privilege and generative AI.
