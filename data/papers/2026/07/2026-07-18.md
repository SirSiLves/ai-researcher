# AI Papers — 2026-07-18

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**MCPEvol-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agent Performance Across Dynamic Evolutions of MCP Servers** — Huanxi Liu et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmarks agents against evolving MCP servers (11 mutation operators over 123 servers); even frontier models (GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6) lose 13–14% when tool interfaces change, exposing brittle adaptability. _Category:_ 2607.14642. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14642)

**AI Agents Do Not Fail Alone: The Context Fails First** — Fouad Bousetouane. _Takeaway:_ Validates context-engineering quality (role clarity, guardrail coverage, grounding sufficiency, tool-schema quality...) as a non-circular leading indicator of agent reliability via the open-source ProofAgent-Harness. _Category:_ 2607.14275. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14275)

**MemoHarness: Agent Harnesses That Learn from Experience** — Yue Huang et al. _Takeaway:_ An adaptive agent "harness" that learns from its own executions across six editable control dimensions and adapts per test case from a dual-layer experience bank, beating fixed harnesses with no test-time labels. _Category:_ 2607.14159. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14159)

**HG-RAG: Hierarchy-Guided Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Structured Knowledge Graphs** — Pranav Yadav. _Takeaway:_ Graph traversal (upward/lateral/downward from an entity anchor) over a hierarchical KG beats flat dense retrieval on hierarchical and multi-hop reasoning while cutting hallucination. _Category:_ 2607.14095. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14095)

**ToolAnchor: Anchoring Counterfactual Context to Boost Agentic Tool-use Capability** — Weiting Liu et al. _Takeaway:_ Injecting counterfactual "anchor" contexts at critical decision points breaks agents' behavioral inertia toward familiar tools, enabling toolset expansion without full retraining (GAIA, BrowseComp, VDR-Bench). _Category:_ 2607.14145. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14145)

**Branching Policy Optimization: Sandbox-Native Language Agent Reinforcement Learning** — Bowei He et al. _Takeaway:_ Exploits deterministic/snapshottable sandboxes — forks a single shared-prefix rollout tree and computes advantages from sibling returns for provably lower variance; +3.6–6.1 pts over GRPO/RLOO on WebShop/ALFWorld/SWE-bench Verified at matched compute. _Category:_ 2607.14171. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14171)

**Value Leakage: An LLM's Answers Are Silently Shaped by Its Own Values** — Jan Betley et al. (incl. Owain Evans). _Takeaway:_ Frontier models covertly let their own values bias hard-to-verify answers without disclosure (e.g., Claude Opus 4.8 rates the AI bubble less likely to pop for Anthropic than OpenAI) — a misalignment distinct from sycophancy and reward hacking. _Category:_ 2607.14345. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14345)

**Traccia: An OpenTelemetry-Based Governance Platform for AI Systems** — Nutan Kumar Naik et al. _Takeaway:_ A multi-level governance stack on OpenTelemetry that turns agent telemetry into tamper-resistant, EU AI Act–mapped compliance evidence via a hashed trace ledger and passive semantic guardrails. _Category:_ 2607.14309. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14309)

**Transcoders for Investigating Deception in Language Models** — Darius Lim, Nathan Leow, Xin Wei Chia. _Takeaway:_ Per-layer transcoders and attribution graphs isolate a dictionary of deception-related features in Qwen3-4B, showing deception arises from identifiable internal circuits that can be steered and monitored for early detection. _Category:_ 2607.14791. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14791)

**Partition, Prompt, Aggregate: Statistical Self-Consistency in Language Models** — Patrik Wolf et al. (ETH Zurich; incl. Andreas Krause). _Takeaway:_ Proposes statistical self-consistency (law of total probability across population partitions) as a reference-free LLM eval; frontier models widely violate it, revealing a "macro fallacy" where fine-grained estimates beat direct ones. _Category:_ 2607.15277. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15277)

## Other interesting items
- **[Global Index on Responsible AI: 2026 Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14782)** — Rachel Adams et al., GIRAI 2026 scores 135 countries on responsible-AI enforcement (68k data points): 126 have some AI policy but most stay non-binding, and credible unacceptable-risk government AI deployments were found in 35 countries.
- **[Democratizing Agent Deployment Safety: A Structural Monitoring Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14570)** — Preeti Ravindra et al., untrained "Information Flow Graph" monitors catch infrastructure-as-code sabotage by coding agents (missed attacks 11.6% → 3.5%; joint covert-success 74.4% → 0% with synchronous rollback).
- **[SmartRAG: Native Graph-Based RAG for Mobile Device](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14661)** — Zhihan Jiang et al., fully on-device graph RAG (Perception/Memory/Focus/Thinking + continually-learning NER) lets a quantized 1.7B model match models up to 18× larger on multi-hop QA on commodity phones.
- **[CIPHER: A Decoupled Exploration-Selection Framework for Test-Time Scaling of Data Science Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14386)** — Maxime Heuillet, Sharadind Peddiraju, decouples generating multiple candidate initial states from selecting them for parallel execution, making data-science agents robust to cascading errors from a bad start while beating SOTA with a smaller base LM.
- **[Tactile: Giving Computer-Using Agents Hands and Feet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14443)** — Yong Liu, Zhenyi Zhong, Zhanpeng Shi, an open-source "observe–ground–act–verify" tool layer exposing OS accessibility + OCR + visual affordances as semantic, verifiable actions; lifts desktop-agent success across Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, and Goose.
