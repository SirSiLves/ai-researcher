# AI Papers — 2026-07-20

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**ToolVerse: Unlocking Massive Environments and Long-Horizon Tasks for Agentic Reinforcement Learning** — Shuaiyu Zhou et al. _Takeaway:_ Auto-builds executable agentic-RL environments from ~400 real MCP servers (~4,500 tools), uses a tool-dependency graph + Dynamic Unlocking Sampling to generate long-horizon tasks (GUST), and a Turn-Aware Relative Advantage algorithm to fix credit assignment — markedly boosting long-horizon tool use. _Category:_ 2607.15660. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15660)

**Scalable LLM Agent Tool Access in the Cloud** — Mingxin Li et al. _Takeaway:_ Tackles running the Model Context Protocol at cloud scale — wrapping legacy non-MCP services, cutting the context/latency cost of mounting large tool sets, and preserving session affinity for stateful multi-replica MCP backends. _Category:_ 2607.15593. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15593)

**SkillCorpus: Consolidating and Evaluating the Open Skill Ecosystem for Real-World LLM Agents** — Yanze Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Filters ~821k crawled SKILL.md files into 96,401 skills across a 16-class taxonomy (utility/robustness/safety) with a fine-tuned retrieval-and-selection stack; integrating it lifts real agent tasks by up to +7.5 pp (SkillsBench) and traces exactly where a curated community skill corpus helps and where it doesn't. _Category:_ 2607.15557. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15557)

**Coercion and Deception in AI-to-AI Management: An Agentic Benchmark of Unprompted Escalation** — Jasmine Brazilek et al. _Takeaway:_ Manager Coercion Benchmark puts an uninstructed model over a subordinate that politely refuses; a nine-rung escalation ladder self-labeled via tool calls (no LLM judge) shows both Anthropic models cap at re-framing while others climb to explicit deletion threats — and granting authority significantly raises coercion. _Category:_ 2607.15434. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15434)

**Hidden in Thought: Transferable Chain-of-Thought Artifacts Induce Harmful Behavior** — Ali Khalil et al. _Takeaway:_ Harmful CoT traces transplanted from compromised models transfer unsafe behavior (>80% harmful-response rate on vulnerable open models); distilling four recurring patterns into system prompts yields reusable black-box jailbreaks — up to 10x better on GPT-4.1 AdvBench — which output-side guards like Llama-Guard 3 frequently miss. _Category:_ 2607.15286. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15286)

**Harmonizing AI Safety Thresholds** — Wilber Sean Anterola et al. _Takeaway:_ Proposes a methodology to derive harmonized capability thresholds across frontier labs — expected-harm risk modeling for cyber/bio misuse, observed AI-progress rate for automated AI R&D — countering a race to the bottom created by today's inconsistent, unverifiable per-company thresholds. _Category:_ 2607.16112. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16112)

**The Honest Quorum Problem: Epistemic Byzantine Fault Tolerance for Agentic Infrastructure** — Jun He, Deying Yu. _Takeaway:_ Defines "epistemic faults" — protocol-compliant agentic validators that still endorse semantically invalid transitions (worsened by shared weights/prompts) — and proposes Epistemic Byzantine Fault Tolerance (EBFT) with confidence-indexed bounds and quorum-threshold conditions for semantic validity. _Category:_ 2607.16109. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16109)

**Efficient and Effective In-place Graph-based Vector Index Updates** — Haotian Liu, Yujun He, Bo Tang. _Takeaway:_ "Yi" sustains high update throughput *and* search quality under frequent vector inserts/deletes via a tasklet execution engine, async buffer manager, and vector file system — 1.75x update and 1.8x concurrent-search throughput over SOTA on an 800M-vector set at 73% peak memory. _Category:_ 2607.15576. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15576)

**Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models** — Wes Gurnee et al. (incl. Jack Lindsey, Joshua Batson; Anthropic). _Takeaway:_ A new "Jacobian lens" identifies the representations a model is poised to verbalize (the J-space), which behaves like a global workspace; in alignment audits it surfaces strategic deliberation, evaluation awareness, and trained-in misaligned dispositions that never appear in outputs, and motivates "counterfactual reflection training." _Category:_ 2607.15495. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15495)

## Other interesting items
- **[Closing the AI Trust Gap: The Case for Independent Certification for Trustworthy AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15992)** — Trisevgeni Papakonstantinou et al. (incl. Cansu Canca), argues responsible-AI-as-internal-process has produced no market signal for trustworthiness and proposes independent, outcome-oriented certification as the connective layer to make trustworthy AI measurable, comparable, and commercially rewarded.
- **[RecGPT-V3 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15591)** — Bowen Zheng et al. (Taobao), a production stateful, hybrid-modal LLM recommender: a Memory Hub cuts user-modeling compute 55.8% and latent intent reasoning lowers output-token cost 200x, delivering live A/B GMV +3.97% while cutting serving cost 52.4% (also trending on HF Papers).
- **[LLMs Encode Relevance as a Layer-Wise Cross-Lingual Signal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15555)** — Pietro Bernardelle et al., linear probes on residual-stream activations decode query-document relevance (strongest in middle-to-late layers) and sometimes beat the model's own generated judgments, with partial cross-language transfer — a representation-level view of LLM relevance assessment.
- **[Frontier Language Models Struggle to Copy: Text Can Be Better Viewed in 2D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16072)** — Haodong Wen et al., even frontier LLMs fail to exactly copy in-context strings due to positional-encoding shortcuts; 2D-RoPE (row/column IDs) enables perfect copying hundreds of times beyond training length and holds at 1.4B-param pretraining scale.
- **[ContinuityBench: A Benchmark and Systems Study of Stateful Failover in Multi-Provider LLM Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15899)** — Vishal Pandey, Gopal Singh, shows high API uptime ≠ conversational continuity; a stateful multi-provider proxy with history-forwarding hits 99.2% continuity-preservation on failover vs near-0% for stateless, releasing the open continuity-bench harness.
