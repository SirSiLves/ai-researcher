## Top picks

**AgenticSTS: A Bounded-Memory Testbed for Long-Horizon LLM Agents** — Xiangchen Cheng, Yunwei Jiang, et al. _Takeaway:_ Frames agent memory as an explicit "contract" over what each future decision is allowed to see, and benchmarks long-horizon agents under bounded-memory budgets instead of append-everything contexts. _Category:_ arXiv 2607.02255 (via HF Papers). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02255)

**AutoMem: Automated Learning of Memory as a Cognitive Skill** — Shengguang Wu, Hao Zhu, et al. _Takeaway:_ Ports the cognitive-science notion of metamemory to LLM agents — learning what to encode, when to retrieve, and how to organize knowledge, rather than hand-designing memory pipelines. _Category:_ arXiv 2607.01224 (via HF Papers). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01224)

**SkillCoach: Self-Evolving Rubrics for Evaluating and Enhancing Agentic Skill-Use** — Jiayin Zhu, Kelong Mao, et al. _Takeaway:_ Treats skills (SOPs, domain rules, tool workflows, scripts, validation routines) as a reusable operational layer for agents and self-evolves rubrics to evaluate and improve skill selection in repositories with overlapping skills. _Category:_ arXiv 2607.01874 (via HF Papers). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01874)

**PACE: A Proxy for Agentic Capability Evaluation** — Yueqi Song, Lintang Sutawika, et al. _Takeaway:_ Cheap proxy for expensive agent benchmarks (SWE-Bench, GAIA) whose single runs can cost thousands of dollars and take days — directly relevant to agent eval/observability practice. _Category:_ arXiv 2607.02032 (via HF Papers). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02032)

**When Search Agents Should Ask: DiscoBench for Clarification-Aware Deep Search** — Yiling Tao, Shihan Deng, et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmarks whether multi-step LLM search agents recognize underspecified goals and ask clarifying questions instead of ploughing ahead — a gap existing deep-search evals ignore. _Category:_ arXiv 2606.27669 (via HF Papers). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27669)

**CheckRLM: Effective Knowledge-Thought Coherence Checking in Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning** — Dingling Xu, Ruobing Wang, et al. (incl. Maosong Sun). _Takeaway:_ Extracts factual claims from reasoning chains during inference, localizes knowledge inconsistencies, and applies minimal-cost RAG-based corrections — mitigating error accumulation in long-horizon reasoning. _Category:_ cs.CL · 2607.02262. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02262)

**Scaling Laws for Grid-Based Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search in High Dimensions** — Matthew J Liu, Wei Hang Zheng, et al. _Takeaway:_ Systematic scaling characterization of multiprobe grid ANN vs dataset size N and dimensionality d, revealing a previously unreported d-scaling crossover on GloVe embeddings — vector-store fundamentals. _Category:_ arXiv 2607.01283 (via HF Papers). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01283)

**HaloGuard 1.0: An Open Weights Constitutional Classifier for Multilingual AI Safety** — Navaneeth Sangameswaran, Preetham S, Ashmiya Lenin. _Takeaway:_ 0.8B open-weights constitutional classifier (46 policies, 2,940 subcategories, 46 languages) tops guard models up to 27B on prompt-safety F1 (90.9 avg) with 4.3 FPR. _Category:_ cs.CL · 2607.02079. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02079)

**LACUNA: A Testbed for Evaluating Localization Precision for LLM Unlearning** — Matteo Boglioni, Thibault Rousset, Siva Reddy, Marius Mosbach, Verna Dankers. _Takeaway:_ First unlearning testbed with ground-truth parameter-level localization (PII injected into known weights of OLMo models); SOTA unlearning looks good at output level but is imprecise and vulnerable to resurfacing attacks. _Category:_ cs.CL · 2607.02513. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02513)

**Will Scaling Improve Social Simulation with LLMs?** — Caleb Ziems, William Held, Tatsunori Hashimoto, Diyi Yang, et al. _Takeaway:_ Scaling-law study across 85 Qwen3-architecture models (10^18–10^20 FLOPs) plus 35 open-weight models: most opinion/behavior simulation improves with compute, but calibration to human cognitive biases does not. _Category:_ cs.CL · 2607.02464. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02464)

## Other interesting items

- **[OpenSafeIntent: Evaluating Intent-Calibrated Safe Completion Across Dual-Use Prompt Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02047)** — Rheeya Uppaal et al.; benign/dual-use/malicious variants of the same task show prompt-level safety scores hide brittle intent calibration. (cs.CL · 2607.02047)
- **[Transferability for General Reasoning: An Automated Curriculum for Multi-Domain RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25178)** — Yang Yongjin et al.; automated curriculum for RLVR training suites spanning math, programming, and science. (2606.25178, via HF Papers)
- **[Denser ≠ Better: Limits of On-Policy Self-Distillation for Continual Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01763)** — Meng Wang, Haohan Zhao, et al.; probes where on-policy self-distillation stops mitigating forgetting in continual post-training. (2607.01763, via HF Papers)
- **[Morphing into Hybrid Attention Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30562)** — Disen Lan, Jianbin Zheng, et al.; converts pretrained Transformers into hybrid full/linear-attention models for long-context efficiency. (2606.30562, via HF Papers)
- **[Object Aligner: A Configurable JSON Schema Similarity Score for Graphs, Applied to LLM Prompt Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01972)** — Jan Drchal; deterministic scoring of structured LLM outputs (tool calling, extraction, agentic planning) via schema-configured tree/graph alignment, usable as a GEPA reward. (cs.CL · 2607.01972)
