## Top picks

**From Solvers to Research: Large Language Model-Driven Formal Mathematics at the Research Frontier** — Eric Jiang, Xiao Liang, … Terence Tao, Wei Wang. _Takeaway:_ Position paper + systematic review (datasets, auto-formalization, proof synthesis) arguing AI4Math must shift from predefined theorem "solvers" to formal research agents that can attack open, under-specified conjectures; maps the core limitations in datasets, relational structure, tool ecosystem, and human-AI collaboration. _Category:_ 2607.07779. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07779)

**DeepSearch-World: Self-Distillation for Deep Search Agents in a Verifiable Environment** — Xinyu Geng, Xuanhua He, … Yi R. Fung. _Takeaway:_ DeepSearch-Evolve self-distills web agents inside DeepSearch-World, a deterministic, verifiable env with 420K multi-hop QA tasks; with no distillation from a stronger model the 9B agent reaches 31.2% BrowseComp / 61.5% GAIA / 93.4% HotpotQA, and the environment, training pool, and model are released. _Category:_ 2607.07820. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07820)

**Do You Need a Frontier Model as a Citation Verifier? Benchmarking Rubric LLMs for Deep-Research Source Attribution** — Ethan Leung, Elias Lumer, Corey Feld, Austin Huber, Vamse Kumar Subbiah, Kevin Paul. _Takeaway:_ Scores 8 off-the-shelf LLM judges on 1,248 human-reviewed citation-quality rubric decisions for deep-research attribution; cheaper judges stay competitive (GPT-5-mini tops source-relevance F1 at 0.908) but scalar F1 hides directional bias an RL reward loop would reinforce — calibrate the judge before using citation rubrics as reward. _Category:_ 2607.08700. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08700)

**When Implausible Tokens Get Reinforced: Tail-Aware Credit Calibration for LLM Reinforcement Learning (TACO)** — Xiuyi Lou, Zicheng Xu, … Vladimir Braverman. _Takeaway:_ Names "Positive-Credit Contamination" in critic-free RL (GRPO-style), where contextually erroneous low-probability tail tokens receive the same positive credit as plausible ones; TACO scores each token's tail-risk and dampens risky positive updates without killing gradients, improving stability and long-horizon RL across 3 LLMs and 8 benchmarks. _Category:_ 2607.07976. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07976)

**What LLM Forecasters Know but Don't Say: Probing Internal Representations for Calibration and Faithfulness** — Raphaël Sarfati, Pratyush Ranjan Tiwari, … Eric Ho. _Takeaway:_ Representation-pooling probes on intermediate activations calibrate forecaster LLMs far better than their outputs and double as lie detectors — tracking behavioral shifts the chain-of-thought hides and predicting the direction of change in 84% of cases; forecasts are largely fixed before reasoning begins, so routing by pre-reasoning answer spread saves 30-47% of tokens with no accuracy loss. _Category:_ 2607.08046. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08046)

**Two Axes of LLM Abstention: Answer Correctness and Question Answerability** — Benedikt J. Wagner. _Takeaway:_ Refusing likely-wrong answers and refusing unanswerable / false-premise questions are separate axes that a single confidence threshold can't tell apart, and the answerability blind spot does not shrink with scale; a hidden-state probe recovers answerability (0.69-0.77 AUROC on false-premise CREPE, where prompting stays near chance) and yields a calibrated dual-budget answer policy. _Category:_ 2607.08456. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08456)

**Collective Intelligence with Foundation Models** — J. de Curtò, I. de Zarzà. _Takeaway:_ A solver→critic→aggregator multi-agent framework; ablations across eight scientific domains isolate that model *heterogeneity* — not framework structure or redundant sampling — is the key driver, with the heterogeneous config reaching 0.64 vs 0.54 step-wise accuracy at reduced variance, arguing diverse-model coordination is what enables transparent, auditable decisions. _Category:_ 2607.07729. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07729)

**SPL: Orchestrating Workflows with Declarative Deterministic-Probabilistic Composition** — Wen G. Gong. _Takeaway:_ A declarative language that unifies probabilistic LLM calls (GENERATE/EVALUATE) and deterministic/verified computation (SOLVE/ASSERT) in one spec that runs unchanged across local (Ollama), cloud (Anthropic/OpenRouter), and distributed-grid backends; a controlled 1,200-run study shows the solver arm reaching 82-93% machine-verified correctness versus unverified LLM-only fluency. _Category:_ 2607.07727. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07727)

**UltraX: Refining Pre-Training Data at Scale with Adaptive Programmatic Editing** — Xinlong Zhao, Dongsheng Liu, … Zhiyuan Liu. _Takeaway:_ A function-calling data-refinement framework that adds *insertion* (beyond delete/modify) for fine-grained instance-level editing via reliable program supervision; it matches or surpasses baselines with fewer training tokens, targeting higher-quality data utilization now that scaling-law gains from raw data expansion are flattening. _Category:_ 2607.08646. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08646)

**A Reliability Assessment of LALM Audio Judges for Full-Duplex Voice Agents** — A. Sayyad, J. Emmons, S. Jones, T. Lin, H. Krishnan. _Takeaway:_ Tests Gemini audio LMs as judges scoring full-duplex voice-agent conversations directly from the raw stereo waveform against three calibrated human raters on 8 production dimensions; reaches human-parity on most dimensions at ~2 orders of magnitude lower cost, but shows a model swap (2.5→3.1) must be re-validated on calibration specifically, not assumed from rank-correlation. _Category:_ 2607.07985. [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07985)

## Other interesting items

- **[DominoTree: Conditional Tree-Structured Drafting with Domino for Speculative Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08642)** — Saw S. Lin (Zhiqi Zhang), Jyh-Shing Roger Jang; training-free best-first draft tree scored by Domino's path-dependent, non-factorized correction, giving up to 6.6x speedup over autoregressive decoding and the highest mean accept length (10.7 tokens/round) on Qwen3-4B/8B. (2607.08642)
- **[It Takes a MAESTRO To Prune Bad Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08601)** — Palaash Goel, Ayush Maheshwari, Tanmoy Chakraborty; models MoE expert-activation trajectories as Ergodic Markov chains for globally-aware structured pruning, beating SOTA by up to 10.61% performance retention under 50% compression with lower cross-task variance across Safety, Bias, and Ethics domains. (2607.08601)
- **[Uncertainty-gated selection for block-sparse attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07724)** — Thomas Rossi; a value-of-information router doubles the kept key-block set only for queries whose top-k cut was closest, lifting LongBench-v2 paired recall to 0.75 vs 0.47 and preserving 0.81-0.89 of dense accuracy at 128K while running at 0.62-0.80x dense wall time. (2607.07724)
- **[Retrieval-Augmented Generation Must Move Beyond Factual Grounding to Represent Diverse Opinions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12138)** — Aditya Agrawal, … Aman Singh Thakur; position paper (freshly cross-listed) finding only 1 of 34 RAG benchmarks addresses opinion synthesis, formalizing factual (minimize entropy) vs opinion (preserve entropy) queries and warning of echo-chamber / minority-voice-suppression risk, with an Opinion-Aware RAG existence proof. (2604.12138)
