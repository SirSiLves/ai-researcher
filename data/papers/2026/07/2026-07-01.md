# AI Papers — 2026-07-01

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

**Governance Gaps in Agent Interoperability Protocols: What MCP, A2A, and ACP Cannot Express** — Richard Kang, Yudho Diponegoro. _Takeaway:_ Systematic analysis of what today's agent-interop stack (MCP, A2A, ACP, ANP, ERC-8004) structurally cannot express — identity, capability discovery and message exchange are covered, but enterprise governance constraints fall through the gaps. _Category:_ cs.MA (2606.31498). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31498)

**Generative Skill Composition for LLM Agents** — Xinyu Zhao, Zhen Tan, Vaishnav Tadiparthi, Nakul Agarwal, Kwonjoon Lee, Ehsan Moradi et al. _Takeaway:_ Agents compose modular "skills" (procedural knowledge packages) generatively rather than selecting from a fixed library, improving coverage on complex multi-step tasks. _Category:_ cs.CL (2606.32025). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32025)

**CORTEX: Token-Level Hallucination Detection in RAG via Comparative Internal Representations** — Kazuaki Furumai, Shuichiro Haruta, Kazunori Matsumoto, Daisuke Kamisaka. _Takeaway:_ Localizes hallucinated spans inside long-form RAG outputs at token granularity by comparing internal representations, rather than flagging whole responses. _Category:_ cs.CL (2606.31033). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31033)

**One Retrieval to Cover Them All: Co-occurrence-Aware Knowledge Base Reorganization for Session-Level RAG** — Shivam Ratnakar, Yixuan Zhu, Cecilia Cheng, Chaya Vijayakumar. _Takeaway:_ Reorganizes the knowledge base around query co-occurrence so a single retrieval serves a whole session of related enterprise questions instead of per-query fetches. _Category:_ cs.IR (2606.31156). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31156)

**ACE: Pluggable Adaptive Context Elasticizer across Agents** — Ning Liao, Zihao Long, Xiaoxing Wang, Xue Yang, Yaoming Wang, Ziyuan Zhuang et al. _Takeaway:_ A pluggable context-management layer that elastically compresses/expands agent trajectories to fit fixed context windows as agentic tasks grow longer. _Category:_ cs.AI (2606.31564). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31564)

**SeKV: Resolution-Adaptive KV Cache with Hierarchical Semantic Memory for Long-Context LLM Inference** — Amirhossein Abaskohi, Giuseppe Carenini, Peter West, Yuhang He. _Takeaway:_ Tackles the KV-cache memory bottleneck in long-context inference with a resolution-adaptive hierarchical semantic memory rather than a flat full cache. _Category:_ cs.CL (2606.31145). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31145)

**Addressing Over-Refusal in LLMs with Competing Rewards** — Taeyoun Kim, Aviral Kumar. _Takeaway:_ Uses competing reward signals to reduce safety-induced over-refusal (models declining harmless prompts) without sacrificing refusal of genuinely harmful ones. _Category:_ cs.LG (2606.31748). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31748)

**ComplianceGate: Classifier-Gated Multi-Tier LLM Routing for Inference in Regulated Industries** — Abhishek Dey. _Takeaway:_ A classifier gate routes queries across model tiers to enforce PII/compliance constraints before requests reach endpoints, balancing regulatory enforcement against cost. _Category:_ cs.LG (2606.31163). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31163)

**Theory of Mind and Persuasion Beyond Conversation: Assessing the Capacity of LLMs to Induce Belief States via Planning and Action** — Ben Slater, Matteo G. Mecattaf, Lucy G. Cheke, John Burden, Winnie Street. _Takeaway:_ Moves ToM evaluation from passive QA to agentic settings, measuring whether LLMs can plan and act to induce specific belief states in others — a capability/safety-relevant probe. _Category:_ cs.CL (2606.31916). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31916)

**Reinforcement Learning with Metacognitive Feedback Elicits Faithful Uncertainty Expression in LLMs** — see arXiv (HF Papers trending). _Takeaway:_ RL from metacognitive feedback trains models to express calibrated, faithful uncertainty rather than confident hallucination. _Category:_ HF Papers (2606.32032). [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32032)

## Other interesting items
- **[Investigating Multi-Agent Deliberation in Law](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30906)** — Cor Steging, Ludi van Leeuwen, Tadeusz Zbiegień. Multi-agent deliberation applied to legal reasoning and access-to-justice use cases.
- **[Managing Procedural Memory in LLM Agents: Control, Adaptation, and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23127)** — HF Papers trending. Framework for controlling, adapting and evaluating procedural (skill/how-to) memory in agents.
- **[SWE-INTERACT: Reimagining SWE Benchmarks as User-Driven Long-Horizon Coding Sessions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30573)** — HF Papers trending. Reframes software-engineering evals as interactive, long-horizon user-driven sessions rather than one-shot tasks.
- **[QVal: Cheaply Evaluating Dense Supervision Signals for Long-Horizon LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32034)** — Sergio Hernández-Gutiérrez et al. Cheap dense-reward evaluation for long-horizon agent trajectories where outcome-only rewards are too sparse.
- **[MOPD: Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Capability Integration in LLM Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30406)** — HF Papers trending. On-policy distillation from multiple teachers to integrate capabilities during post-training.
- **[LUMOS: A Semantic Operating-System Layer for Accessibility-Grounded AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30697)** — HF Papers trending. A semantic OS layer that grounds AI agents in accessibility primitives for reliable GUI/desktop control.
- **[ShopX: A Foundation Model for Intent-to-Item Fulfillment in Agentic Shopping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31693)** — Jiacheng Chen et al. Foundation model for LLM-agent-orchestrated intent-driven shopping, beyond wrapping search/rec APIs.
- **[Beyond the Library: An Agentic Framework for Autoformalizing Research Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31134)** — Arshia Soltani Moakhar et al. Agentic pipeline that autoformalizes research math into Lean 4 to catch subtle LLM reasoning errors.
