# AI Papers — 2026-03-18

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **MiroThinker-1.7 & H1: Towards Heavy-Duty Research Agents via Verification** — Daxiang Dong, Mingming Zheng et al. _Takeaway:_ Open research-agent stack that adds explicit verification steps for long-horizon multi-step reasoning. Concrete artifact in the open vs closed deep-research-agent thread, alongside OpenSeeker. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13398 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13398)

- **TRUST-SQL: Tool-Integrated Multi-Turn Reinforcement Learning for Text-to-SQL over Unknown Schemas** — Jiaxuan Lu, Kong Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Drops the "give the model the full schema upfront" assumption and instead trains an agent to discover schema through tool calls; multi-turn RL recipe over realistic enterprise databases with hundreds of noisy tables. Concrete grounding for agentic data-platform work. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.08262 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08262](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08262)

- **AgentProcessBench: Diagnosing Step-Level Process Quality in Tool-Using Agents** — Omnilingual MT Team, Belen Alastruey et al. _Takeaway:_ Step-level evaluation harness for tool-using agents where errors are often irreversible (vs. backtrackable math). Measures process quality, not just final-answer accuracy — needed primitive for agent observability. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16309 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16309)

- **SWE-Skills-Bench: Do Agent Skills Actually Help in Real-World Software Engineering?** — Lin Li, Haoran Feng et al. _Takeaway:_ First end-to-end test of whether injected "agent skills" (structured procedural-knowledge packages) translate into real SE wins, vs. just helping on cherry-picked subtasks. Useful counterweight to the skill-library hype. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16869 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16869)

- **One-Eval: An Agentic System for Automated and Traceable LLM Evaluation** — Abhay Deshpande, Maya Guru et al. _Takeaway:_ Agent that selects benchmarks, reproduces evaluation code, and aligns schemas automatically — packaging "running a benchmark" as a traceable agentic task. Direct relevance to the eval/observability tooling stack. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16861 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16861)

- **CCTU: A Benchmark for Tool Use under Complex Constraints** — Udi Barzelay, Ophir Azulai et al. _Takeaway:_ Tool-use evaluation under explicit constraints (preconditions, ordering, mutual exclusion), exposing brittleness in function-calling models that ignore constraints at runtime. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15118 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15118)

- **Test-Time Strategies for More Efficient and Accurate Agentic RAG** — Pierre-Antoine Bannier. _Takeaway:_ Concrete inference-time controls (when to retrieve, when to stop) for iterative agentic RAG pipelines like Search-R1; argues most efficiency wins come from policy, not retrieval depth. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16587 (cs.IR). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16587](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16587)

- **MEMO: Memory-Augmented Model Context Optimization for Robust Multi-Turn Multi-Agent LLM Games** — Shengda Fan, Xuyan Ye et al. _Takeaway:_ Identifies that small early deviations compound and get amplified across agents, making multi-agent benchmark scores unreliable; proposes memory + context primitives that reduce run-to-run variance. Important for any multi-agent eval pipeline. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.14465 (cs.MA). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465)

- **Online Experiential Learning for Language Models** — Ai Jian, Xiaoyun Zhang et al. _Takeaway:_ Frames real-world deployment traces as a training signal — closes the loop between agent execution logs and continued model updates without separate annotation. Concrete pattern for "deployed-traffic as training data." _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16448 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16448)

- **Anticipatory Planning for Multimodal AI Agents** — Shihao Zhu, Ziheng Ouyang et al. _Takeaway:_ Argues most computer-use agents are purely reactive; introduces lookahead over future states for long-horizon goals. Concrete planning primitive missing from current OSWorld-style stacks. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16649 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16649)

## Other interesting items

- **[FinToolBench: Evaluating LLM Agents for Real-World Financial Tool Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13875)** — cs.CL. Domain-specific tool-use benchmark for finance where wrong-tool calls have material consequences — counterpoint to generic ToolBench.
- **[Thinking in Uncertainty: Mitigating Hallucinations in MLRMs with Latent Entropy-Aware Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16669)** — cs.CL. Transition words like "wait" / "however" correlate with hallucination spikes; entropy-aware decoding contains them.
- **[Recursive Language Models Meet Uncertainty: Self-Reflective Program Search for Long Context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16844)** — cs.CL. Wraps a long-context LLM in self-reflective program search — a surprising baseline beat on long-context retrieval.
