# AI Papers — 2026-03-19

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **Efficient Reasoning with Balanced Thinking** — Peng Xia, Jianwen Chen et al. _Takeaway:_ Trains LRMs to dynamically calibrate reasoning length — counter both overthinking on easy problems and underthinking on hard ones with a single learned controller. Concrete competitor to fixed CoT budgets and to TERMINATOR-style early-exit. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17187 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17187](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17187)

- **MetaClaw: Just Talk — An Agent That Meta-Learns and Evolves in the Wild** — Qile Su, Jing Tang et al. _Takeaway:_ LLM agent that updates its own behavior over deployment from user feedback rather than staying frozen between releases. Addresses the "deployed agents are static" failure mode under continuous-service constraints. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.14935 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14935](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14935)

- **Alignment Makes Language Models Normative, Not Descriptive** — Dilxat Muhtar, Jiashun Liu et al. _Takeaway:_ Side-by-side study of 120 base/aligned model pairs on 10,000+ responses showing alignment objectives systematically shift models away from modeling _what people do_ toward modeling _what they should do_. Substantive empirical input to the alignment-tax / persona-distortion debate. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17621 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17621)

- **Complementary Reinforcement Learning** — Lorenzo Mur-Labadia, Matthew Muckley et al. _Takeaway:_ Adds dense, complementary supervision alongside sparse outcome rewards for LLM-agent RL — tackles the sample-efficiency wall that pure outcome-RL keeps hitting on long-horizon tasks. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.14482 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14482)

- **AdaMem: Adaptive User-Centric Memory for Long-Horizon Dialogue Agents** — Yanpeng Zhao, Wentao Ding et al. _Takeaway:_ Designs memory primitives (write, decay, retrieval) around stable user identity across sessions rather than chronological logs. Concrete pattern for persistent-memory agent platforms. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13033 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13033)

- **BenchPreS: A Benchmark for Context-Aware Personalized Preference Selectivity of Persistent-Memory LLMs** — Yulin Luo, Hao Chen et al. _Takeaway:_ Probes whether a model can _selectively_ apply stored preferences across third-party / institutional contexts (vs. always-on personalization). Directly relevant to memory governance and disclosure. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15618 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15618)

- **Efficient Exploration at Scale** — Jianrui Zhang, Yue Yang et al. _Takeaway:_ Online RLHF algorithm that incrementally updates reward and policy as preferences arrive — sharp gains in data efficiency vs. periodic batch RLHF. Important for any team running RLHF on a moving target. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.18004 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18004)

- **Expert Threshold Routing for Autoregressive Language Modeling with Dynamic Computation Allocation** — Raghavv Goel, Mukul Gagrani et al. _Takeaway:_ Replaces fixed top-k MoE routing with per-token threshold routing — drops the auxiliary load-balance loss and allocates compute adaptively. Concrete architectural step beyond TC-MoE. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17942 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17942)

- **PRISM: Demystifying Retention and Interaction in Mid-Training** — Md Jahidul Islam. _Takeaway:_ Systematic mid-training study across Granite, LLaMA, Mistral, Nemotron families — how mid-training data composition and ordering affect downstream retention. One of the few open methodology papers on this phase. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16653 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16653)

- **AI Scientist via Synthetic Task Scaling** — M. Arda Aydın, Melih B. Yilmaz et al. _Takeaway:_ Argues that scaling synthetic ML-research tasks (vs. scaffolding bigger agents) is the cleaner path to autonomous discovery; gives a principled training recipe rather than just an orchestration loop. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.17079 (cs.AI). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17079)

## Other interesting items

- **[Efficient Training-Free Multi-Token Prediction via Embedding-Space Probing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17891)** — cs.CL. Extracts latent MTP behavior from next-token-trained LLMs without retraining — cheap speedup primitive.
- **[RAMP: Reinforcement Adaptive Mixed Precision Quantization for Efficient On-Device LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17965)** — cs.LG. Per-layer mixed-bit quantization learned by RL; closes the accuracy gap that uniform PTQ leaves.
- **[ESPIRE: Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Spatial Reasoning of VLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17375)** — cs.CL. Exposes that VLM "spatial cognition" mostly does not survive controlled diagnostics.
