# AI Papers — 2026-03-17

_Collector: ai-papers. Slice: ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MA, stat.ML, cs.IR), HF Papers, papers.cool._

## Top picks

- **OpenSeeker: Democratizing Frontier Search Agents by Fully Open-Sourcing Training Data** — Shiva Krishna Reddy Malay, Shravan Nayak, Jishnu Sethumadhavan Nair et al. _Takeaway:_ Releases the full training-data pipeline (queries, traces, verifier outputs) behind a competitive deep-search LLM agent — concrete artifact for the "open frontier-agent" thread; addresses the gap where strong search agents are only reproducible inside industrial labs. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13594 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13594)

- **EnterpriseOps-Gym: Environments and Evaluations for Stateful Agentic Planning and Tool Use in Enterprise Settings** — Lianghui Zhu, Yuxin Fang, Bencheng Liao et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmark of long-horizon enterprise workflows that exercise stateful tool use, role/permission boundaries, and persistent context — the missing middle between toy ToolBench and SWE-bench-style coding. Useful pressure test for "AI worker" agent platforms. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15619 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15619)

- **TERMINATOR: Learning Optimal Exit Points for Early Stopping in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning** — Yuhong Dai, Yanlin Lai, Mitt Huang et al. _Takeaway:_ Learns a stop-policy that decides when a reasoning chain has produced enough signal to commit to an answer, cutting overthinking without harming accuracy. Concrete inference-time mechanism for the "spend tokens only when they help" thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13391 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13391)

- **Understanding Reasoning in LLMs through Strategic Information Allocation under Uncertainty** — Seth Karten, Jake Grigsby, Tersoo Upaa et al. _Takeaway:_ Information-theoretic decomposition of CoT into procedural information vs. epistemic verbalization; reframes "Aha moments" as bandwidth-allocation events rather than self-correction. Useful conceptual scaffold for CoT-monitorability and faithfulness debates. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15563 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15563](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15563)

- **Supervised Fine-Tuning versus Reinforcement Learning: A Study of Post-Training Methods for LLMs** — Zixin Zhang, Chenfei Liao, Hongfei Zhang et al. _Takeaway:_ Controlled head-to-head between SFT and RL post-training across tasks and budgets — argues the two should be viewed as complementary phases under specific data regimes rather than substitutes. Concrete reference for the "RL vs SFT" recipe debate. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15558 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15558)

- **When Does Sparsity Mitigate the Curse of Depth in LLMs** — Quanhao Li, Zhen Xing, Rui Wang et al. _Takeaway:_ Studies under what conditions activation/parameter sparsity counteracts deep-layer under-utilization caused by variance growth in Pre-LayerNorm. Direct relevance to long-network training stability and MoE-vs-dense scaling arguments. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.12146 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12146](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12146)

- **Mixture-of-Depths Attention** — Lukas Hauzenberger, Niklas Schmidinger, Thomas Schmied et al. _Takeaway:_ Per-token depth routing for attention layers; reuses informative shallow features instead of letting them dilute through the residual stack. Concrete architectural counter to the "curse of depth" identified above. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15590 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15590)

- **POLCA: Stochastic Generative Optimization with LLM** — Gangda Deng, Zhaoling Chen, Zhongming Yu et al. _Takeaway:_ Formalizes prompt/agent-pipeline tuning as a stochastic optimization where the LLM itself acts as a proposer guided by numeric and textual rewards. Concrete pattern for the agent-platform / harness-search thread. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13428 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13428)

- **EvoClaw: Evaluating AI Agents on Continuous Software Evolution** — Alliot Nagle, Jakhongir Saydaliev, Dhia Garbaya et al. _Takeaway:_ Benchmark for long-running coding agents that must extend and maintain software across many sessions, not just one-shot patches. Probes the failure mode SWE-bench cannot see — drift, regression, accumulated tech debt. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.12529 (cs.CL). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12529)

- **Code-A1: Adversarial Evolving of Code LLM and Test LLM via Reinforcement Learning** — Fangran Miao, Jian Huang, Ting Li et al. _Takeaway:_ Co-evolves a code generator and a test generator under verifiable rewards, escaping the static-test-suite ceiling that limits coding-agent RL. Adversarial recipe for self-play that produces fresher verifiers rather than fresher reasoning. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15016 (cs.LG). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15016)

## Other interesting items

- **[Anatomy of a Lie: A Multi-Stage Diagnostic Framework for Tracing Hallucinations in VLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10505)** — cs.CL. Recasts VLM hallucinations as dynamic failure modes traceable through stages of the forward pass; concrete diagnostic harness.
- **[Effective Distillation to Hybrid xLSTM Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15557)** — cs.CL. Distillation recipe that finally closes the gap between quadratic-attention teachers and sub-quadratic linearized students.
- **[Safe and Scalable Web Agent Learning via Recreated Websites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15478)** — cs.CL. LLM as environment creator that clones real sites for safe, resettable web-agent training; addresses verifiable-reward gap in web-agent RL.
- **[AI Can Learn Scientific Taste](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15031)** — Kimi Team, cs.CL. Probes whether models can judge research-impact potential rather than just execute experiments — adjacent to research-agent thread.
