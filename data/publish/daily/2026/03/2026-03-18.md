## Research highlights

- **MiroThinker-1.7 & H1: Towards Heavy-Duty Research Agents via Verification** — Dong, Zheng et al. Open research-agent stack with explicit verification steps for long-horizon multi-step reasoning. Open-vs-closed deep-research-agent thread, alongside yesterday's OpenSeeker. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.13398 — research agents. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13398)
- **TRUST-SQL: Tool-Integrated Multi-Turn RL for Text-to-SQL over Unknown Schemas** — Lu, Wang et al. Drops the "full schema upfront" assumption; trains agents to discover schema through tool calls; multi-turn RL over realistic enterprise databases with hundreds of noisy tables. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.08262 — data-platform agents. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08262)
- **AgentProcessBench: Diagnosing Step-Level Process Quality in Tool-Using Agents** — Alastruey et al. Step-level evaluation harness where errors are often irreversible (vs backtrackable math). Measures process quality, not just final-answer accuracy. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16309 — agent observability. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16309)
- **SWE-Skills-Bench: Do Agent Skills Actually Help in Real-World Software Engineering?** — Li, Feng et al. First end-to-end test of whether injected "agent skills" (structured procedural-knowledge packages) translate into real SE wins. Counterweight to skill-library hype. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16869 — coding agents. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16869)
- **One-Eval: An Agentic System for Automated and Traceable LLM Evaluation** — Deshpande, Guru et al. Agent that selects benchmarks, reproduces evaluation code, aligns schemas automatically — packaging "running a benchmark" as a traceable agentic task. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16861 — eval tooling. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16861)
- **CCTU: A Benchmark for Tool Use under Complex Constraints** — Barzelay, Azulai et al. Tool-use evaluation under explicit constraints (preconditions, ordering, mutual exclusion); exposes brittleness in function-calling models. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.15118 — tool use. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15118)
- **Test-Time Strategies for More Efficient and Accurate Agentic RAG** — Bannier. Inference-time controls (when to retrieve, when to stop) for iterative agentic RAG pipelines; argues most efficiency wins come from policy, not retrieval depth. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16587 — RAG. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16587)
- **MEMO: Memory-Augmented Model Context Optimization for Robust Multi-Turn Multi-Agent LLM Games** — Fan, Ye et al. Small early deviations compound across agents, making multi-agent benchmark scores unreliable; proposes memory + context primitives that reduce run-to-run variance. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.14465 — multi-agent eval. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465)
- **Anticipatory Planning for Multimodal AI Agents** — Zhu, Ouyang et al. Argues most computer-use agents are purely reactive; introduces lookahead over future states for long-horizon goals. Concrete planning primitive missing from current OSWorld stacks. _Category:_ arXiv:2603.16649 — CUA / planning. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16649)
