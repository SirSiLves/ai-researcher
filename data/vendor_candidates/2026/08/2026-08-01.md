## ⚠️ Silent covered vendors (0) — but the detector is blind to today's real problem

No vendor on `priority_vendors` or `enterprise_vendors` is past its silence threshold, so this section is empty by the rule. **That is the wrong answer, and it is worth stating plainly why.**

`covered_silent` is computed from `discovered_orgs.last_seen` — i.e. "was this org *mentioned anywhere* recently" — not from whether the vendor's own feed still resolves. Today's collectors reported eleven broken vendor feeds; every one of those vendors is still being written about by third parties, so all of them read `covered_healthy`:

| `workday_oracle` | Workday 404, Oracle 403 | `covered_healthy` (7/7d) |

| `pinecone` / `llamaindex` / `databricks` | 404 | `covered_healthy` (10 / 14 / 12 per 7d) |

**This skill has no dead-source handling at all** — no step reads collector feed-health output, and the nearest mechanism it does have (`covered_silent` → deep-watch demotion) cannot see a dead feed by construction. Fixing feed URLs is therefore **outside this sweep's remit**; it needs either a collector-side URL repair pass or a new SKILL step that consumes the "Sources scanned" failure lines. Flagged, not actioned.
