The fetch layer now enforces a per-URL provenance rule - it refuses any URL that has not already appeared in a WebSearch result. That structurally breaks every hardcoded feed endpoint: all six arXiv RSS feeds, all 17 Medium tag feeds, github.com/trending and its four language boards were refused tonight. Search-first-then-fetch is the only working pattern.
