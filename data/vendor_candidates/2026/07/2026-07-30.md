- **60 entries: 19 manual (indices 0–18, sacred) + 41 auto-added.** The collector's "roughly 40 of ~60" is accurate.
- **27 of the 41 auto-added are silent ≥45 days** — i.e. already past `keyword_sweep_config.auto_apply.deep_watch_demote.min_silence_days` (45). Eight have been silent **77 days** (`governance policy`, `governance policy safety`, `mistral medium`, `open weights`, `openai deployment`, `openai deployment company`, `policy safety`, `powerpoint word`).
- **Root cause of the stall:** `max_per_list.news_web_search_queries` is **60** and the list is at **exactly 60**. The demotion rule fires only when `len(list) > max_per_list`, so at 60/60 it never triggers and the 27 eligible entries never drain. This is a boundary condition, not a threshold problem — the list is stuck one entry below its own cleanup trigger. `_deep_watch_meta` already holds 31 previously-demoted phrases, so the mechanism works; it simply cannot start.
- Suggested fix for that sweep: either change the comparison to `>=`, or lower `max_per_list.news_web_search_queries` to ~40 so the 27 silent entries drain at the configured 5/run.

**2. `github_collector.watched_repos` staleness → `ai-github-sweep`.** Unchanged remit call from 07-29. Today's collector reports **12 of 133** entries are stale names resolved only via redirect — the 11 carried from yesterday plus **new today: `ogulcancelik/herdr` → `herdrdev/herdr`**.
