## Notes

- **The headline correction of the run: yesterday's "swissaijob.ch is stale" call is withdrawn — and the reason is a specific, repeatable parsing trap.** The board serves **two different numbers on the same page**. On `/jobs/zurich` the `<title>`, meta-description and FAQ all read **321 active roles, "as of 2026-08-01"** (today), and the "Top hiring" widget reads **Google 31 · Nvidia 23 · ETH Zürich 16 · Kaiko.AI 12 · Flexion Robotics 12**. But the intro **prose paragraph** on the same page reads "Zürich hosts **271** active AI roles. Top employers include Google (**49** open), Nvidia (**19**), Flexion Robotics and Microsoft (**14** each), and ETH Zürich (**11**)" — which is **verbatim the figure set this file recorded on 2026-07-30 and 2026-07-31**. The prose block is a stale cached string; the title, FAQ and widget are live. Two consecutive runs read the prose, saw it unchanged, and classified the source as dead. **It was not.** Practical rule going forward: **on swissaijob.ch, trust the page title, the meta-description and the FAQ date; ignore the intro paragraph entirely.** The same split appears on `/jobs/sector/industry`, where the title says **349** and the FAQ dates it **2026-07-26** while the prose says 307 — so different pages also carry different cache ages, and the FAQ date is per-page, not global.
- **Forward Deployed Engineer is the emergent job title of this slice.** Counted today in Zurich alone: **OpenAI** ×2 (FDE + Technical Deployment Lead), **Salesforce** ×5 (Lead FDE ×3 across three IDs, FDE ×2), **Google** FDE III Cloud GTM, **Rockstar Recruiting** FDE – AI/ML, **Ethon** FDE (manufacturing AI), **ANYbotics** Technical Deployment Lead. That is **11+ FDE-titled openings in one city**, and Glassdoor independently reports **24 forward-deployed-engineer jobs in Switzerland**. The pattern is consistent across all of them: customer-embedded delivery of LLM systems, 25–50% travel, evals as a named deliverable. This is the first time the title has been common enough in Switzerland to constitute a category rather than a curiosity, and it is a better description of where Swiss LLM hiring actually sits than "AI Engineer".
