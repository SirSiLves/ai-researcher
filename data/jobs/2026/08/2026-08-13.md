## Notes

1. **Rough denominator this run: ~318 live AI roles on swissaijob (263 engineering + 55 business), 261 jobs.ch hits for "ai engineer", 21 swissdevjobs ML openings, 317 LinkedIn "AI Engineer" hits for Switzerland.** These overlap heavily and are not additive. The only *hard* number in this file is the **21 same-day postings**, which is the first genuinely clean daily-flow measurement this collector has produced.
2. **Salary caveat, unchanged and important: every `est. CHF` band from swissaijob is LLM-estimated by the board from the job description, not employer-published.** The board says so in its own FAQ. Treat as ordinal signal only. The one source of employer-supplied Swiss ML bands remains swissdevjobs (CHF 80–145K, avg ~117K).
3. **Board churn is now measurable and it is high.** Engineering 258 → 263 over 24h with 18 new postings implies ~13 removals in the same window (~5% daily turnover). If that rate holds, the median Swiss AI listing has a shelf life of roughly three weeks — which matches the age distribution on the index (the tail thins sharply past 21 days).
4. **Top hiring employers seen this run:** Nvidia (22), Google (18, and 5 posted today), Kaiko.AI (9), Salesforce (8), Nexthink (8); on the business side Deloitte (10) and Vector8 (6).
5. **The robotics/embodied-AI cluster in Zürich is now the largest single vertical in this file** — Gravis, Mimic, Flink, Flexion, ANYbotics, Hexagon, Verity, Destinus, Harmattan, Voliro, Odyssey. Eleven companies, all Zürich, most ETH-descended. **This is arguably the defining structural feature of the Swiss AI job market and it has been under-weighted in previous runs**, which over-indexed on LLM/GenAI titles because those are what the queries ask for.
6. **Governance remains structurally thin and structurally mis-filed.** The board's business index has 55 listings and exactly **one** with "Governance" in the title (Vector8, 21 days old and unchanged). The live governance-shaped work is showing up as *product* (Google Trust & Verification), *security* (Pictet, LGT), *legal* (ABB), and *quality* (Alcon) — never under a governance heading. **Any query strategy that searches for the word "governance" will keep under-reading this by roughly 5×.**
7. **swissdevjobs is degrading as a source.** 21 openings, of which 7 are a single employer (Ergon) posting IAM/DevOps/SecOps roles with no AI content, surfaced only because the board tags them ML-adjacent. Signal-to-noise is now poor; recommend demoting it below datacareer.
8. **All four fetches succeeded — no dead sources hit this run.** `ai-jobs-switzerland.ch` was not attempted (10 consecutive failures; recommend formally marking dead in `sources.json`). Wellfound surfaced at search level only and yielded no individual listings for the fifth consecutive run — **recommend demoting or dropping it from `boards`.**
9. **Recommendation carried forward from 08-12 and reinforced: add `swissaijob.ch` to `sources.json` `jobs_ch_collector.boards` as the primary index.** Two consecutive clean fetches, a self-declared daily-accurate date stamp, structured employer/city/seniority/age/tags/band per row, and — as of today — a usable `today` marker that makes daily deltas trivial. No other source in this collector's list offers that.
