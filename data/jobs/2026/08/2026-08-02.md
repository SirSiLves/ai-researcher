Today the widening continues in the most direct way possible: Mistral — a European lab shipping open-weight models — is advertising an **AI Scientist** in Zürich at an estimated CHF 150–170K and a **Research Engineer** whose JD names **sparsified 70B+ runs, thousands of GPUs, and embedded squads for Alignment, Pre-training, Multimodal and Safety**. The honest qualifier is that only the AI Scientist post is Zürich-specific; the Research Engineer post lists Paris/London/Zürich/Warsaw and is likely Paris-weighted. Even discounted, the count of employers advertising **pre-training-adjacent** work into the Zürich market is now five (Google/DeepMind, Apple, Microsoft AI, Mistral, ETH's Swiss AI Initiative) — and only one of them is Swiss.
- **Forward Deployed Engineering has acquired a management layer.** Yesterday's note counted 11+ FDE-titled openings in Zürich and called it a category. Salesforce is simultaneously carrying **six** FDE-family posts (Lead FDE ×3, FDE ×2, Builder Manager), and OpenAI's Zürich pair includes a Technical Deployment Lead.
