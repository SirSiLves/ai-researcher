- Rough denominators across boards this run (from search snippets, not a curated count): Glassdoor ~369 artificial-intelligence and ~400 machine-learning roles in Switzerland (Jul 2026), ~224 machine-learning roles in Zürich; Indeed 200+ "AI Software Engineer" Zürich, 50+ "Generative AI Engineer" CH, 100+ "AI Governance" and 100+ "Responsible AI" Zürich; ai-jobs-switzerland.ch 96 "AI engineer" hits; aijobs.net Switzerland board showed several dozen CH postings dated within the last week.
- Top hiring employers seen this run: Microsoft (MAI, Zürich), Google Cloud (three distinct Zürich agent/GenAI roles), Exeon Analytics, Nexthink, Bjak, Zuger Kantonalbank, Rivia, SIX, Emil Frey Digital.
- Shift vs. yesterday: the centre of gravity moved from academic/insurer anchors (ETH, EPFL, Swiss Re) to big-tech Zürich (Microsoft MAI + Google Cloud agent teams) and to Swiss mid-caps building in-house GenAI platforms (Zuger Kantonalbank, Emil Frey Digital).
- Governance signal is now concrete, not just a denominator: SIX (privacy + AI governance, temp) and Emil Frey Digital (governance standards inside a new AI division) are named-employer Swiss listings — the gap flagged on 2026-07-25 has partly closed.
- Salary signals are mostly aijobs.net *estimates* and are labelled as such above; the Microsoft (CHF 183–309K) and Google (CHF 122–236K) bands appeared without the "estimate" tag.
- Data-quality caveat: aijobs.net relative timestamps were inconsistent between page snapshots this run (the Nexthink role showed "21h ago" in one list view and "Found 26d ago" on its own page), so "listed ~N days ago" is indicative only.
- Excluded (expired): **Senior ML Engineer – Agentic AI** at Kaiko (Zürich, CHF 128–192K est., clinical agentic workflows) — marked Expired; **Scientist in Generative AI and LLMs** at Hitachi Energy (Baden-Dättwil) — marked Expired; all three CERN ML Engineer postings (IT-CD-PI-2026-63-LD, IT-CD-PI-2026-98-GRAE, TE-DPS-AIM-2026-94-GRAE) — application windows closed 20–29 April 2026.
