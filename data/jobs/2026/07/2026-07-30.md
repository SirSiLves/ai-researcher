- **New freshness caveat — aijobs.net serves at least three different build snapshots simultaneously, and its relative dates are unreliable.** Pages returned footers `v1.3.6`, `v1.3.7` and `v1.3.99` within the same session, and the *same job ID* carries different ages depending on which build rendered it: job `241863` reads "Found 4d ago" on a v1.3.7 page and "8d ago" in a v1.3.99 panel; job `246916` reads "5d" vs "9d"; job `198948` reads "2d ago" in a v1.3.6 panel but "**Expired** 14d ago" on its own v1.3.7 page. The `/jobs/switzerland` index itself is a stale build — its job IDs top out around 203000 while v1.3.99 pages list IDs up to 254000. Practical rule adopted: **treat aijobs.net job IDs as the only reliable ordering, treat "Xd ago" as ±5 days, and re-check expiry on the highest-version page available.** The 350 count above is therefore a floor, not a current figure.
