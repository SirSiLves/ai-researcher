- **Freshness caveat, sharpened.** Yesterday's rule was "treat 'Xd ago' as ±5 days". Today's evidence says that understates it. Same-run, same-ID contradictions observed: job **204525** reads "**21h ago**" in a v1.3.6 related-jobs panel and "**Found 26d ago**" on its own v1.3.7 page — a 25-day spread. Jobs **245171**, **246513**, **246891** and **244508** each read ~5 days *younger* on v1.3.7 pages than on v1.3.99 pages, a consistent offset in one direction. Because the higher build reports uniformly older ages, **v1.3.99 is the more current clock and the lower builds are serving stale age values computed at an earlier snapshot**. Revised practical rule: **prefer the age shown on the highest-version page available, treat related-panel ages as unreliable entirely, and keep job IDs as the only trustworthy ordering.** Three build versions (v1.3.6, v1.3.7, v1.3.99) were again served within one session.

Worth watching: if it persists, jobs.ch is effectively unverifiable for this collector and the sibling boards (datacareer, talent.com, zuercher-jobs) all returned empty bodies too.
