## Long-form picks

- **A pelican for GPT-5.5 via the semi-official Codex backdoor API** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ GPT-5.5 launch day. Available in OpenAI Codex first and rolling out to paid ChatGPT subscribers; Willison had preview access and ships a deliberately impish `llm-openai-via-codex` plugin that hijacks Codex CLI credentials to call the model from LLM. Core verdict: "fast, effective and highly capable" — the headline GPT-5.5 launch read of the day. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/23/gpt-5-5/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/23/gpt-5-5/)

- **Extract PDF text in your browser with LiteParse for the web** — Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Long write-up of porting LlamaIndex's LiteParse Node.js PDF-extraction tool to run entirely in-browser, reusing most of the same upstream libraries. Concrete worked example for anyone shipping PDF/OCR pipelines that need to run on the client. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/23/liteparse-for-the-web/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/23/liteparse-for-the-web/)

- **Gathering Structures (quoted)** — Maggie Appleton, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ "Learn in public" framed as a credentials hack — digital gardens / podcasts / streams cause people to over-estimate your competence and slip you into rooms full of high-achievers you "have no right to be there." Underrated angle on why public technical writing is durably mispriced. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/23/maggie-appleton/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/23/maggie-appleton/)

- **Myth and Mythos** — Ben Thompson (Stratechery). _Takeaway:_ "Anthropic Anthropic Anthropic" — the company "is wearing the crown" after two-year-trajectory-realized, with Mythos and Project Glasswing on the field. Thompson's Aggregation-Theory take on why Anthropic now has "undoubtedly the most powerful model in the world." Paywalled but the headline framing is public. [https://stratechery.com/2026/myth-and-mythos/](https://stratechery.com/2026/myth-and-mythos/)

- **AIE Europe Debrief + Agent Labs Thesis: Unsupervised Learning x Latent Space Crossover Special (2026)** — Swyx & Jacob Effron (Latent Space x Unsupervised Learning). _Takeaway:_ Recorded just after AIE Europe (and before the Cursor–xAI deal). Thesis: 2026 is the year coding agents "break containment" and start doing everything else — memory, personalization, and the competitive landscape across labs and applications. Useful annual-checkpoint episode. [https://www.latent.space/p/unsupervised-learning-2026](https://www.latent.space/p/unsupervised-learning-2026)
