## Long-form picks

- **The inevitable need for an open model consortium** — Nathan Lambert (Interconnects). _Takeaway:_ Lambert argues that as frontier models get more expensive and more internally valuable, the for-profit funding structures that have supported open-weights frontier work (Meta/Llama, Mistral, etc.) will break down on a 2+ year horizon — and a multi-company consortium is the only long-term-stable path to well-funded near-frontier open models. Frames the post-Llama-4 American open-models policy debate. [https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-inevitable-need-for-an-open-model](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-inevitable-need-for-an-open-model)

- **SQLite 3.53.0** — sqlite.org release notes, via Simon Willison. _Takeaway:_ Big release after the withdrawn 3.52.0: `ALTER TABLE` can now add/remove `NOT NULL` and `CHECK` constraints, new `json_array_insert()`, and a new Query Results Formatter library that Simon compiled to WebAssembly on his phone via Claude Code to ship an interactive playground. A neat agentic-engineering vignette riding alongside a substantive infra release. [https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/11/sqlite/](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Apr/11/sqlite/)
