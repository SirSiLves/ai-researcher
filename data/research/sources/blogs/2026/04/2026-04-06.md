- **Import AI 452: Scaling laws for cyberwar; rising tides of AI automation; and a puzzle over GDP forecasting** — Jack Clark. _Takeaway:_ GPT-5.3 Codex and Opus 4.6 hit 50% success on cyberoffense tasks that take human experts ~3.1–3.2 hours; doubling-time on cyber capability has steepened to 5.7 months since 2024; open-weight GLM-5 lags ~5.7 months behind. ### rag

Walks the whole stack — embedding choice, chunking strategy, reranker, eval harness — with explicit attention to where production pipelines tend to break. Best generalist RAG primer of the week.

### agentic-ai
- **[From Generative to Agentic AI: A Roadmap in 2026](https://medium.com/@anicomanesh/from-generative-to-agentic-ai-a-roadmap-in-2026-8e553b43aeda)** — Arash Nicoomanesh. ### mcp

- **[Building AgentoCore: Enterprise-Grade Autonomous AI Agents with AWS Bedrock and EventBridge](https://medium.com/codex/building-agentocore-enterprise-grade-autonomous-ai-agents-with-aws-bedrock-and-eventbridge-017914a7069a)** — Roman Ceresnak (CodeX).
