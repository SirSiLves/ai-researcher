## Major announcements & releases

**Google AI Studio ships annotate/focus mode, Firebase integration, one-click publishing, "Feeling Lucky"** — Google AI Studio rolled out an interface refresh over the weekend with an annotate-and-focus mode (point at any part of the app, get AI suggestions), one-click Firebase integration for hosting generated apps, one-click publishing, and a "Feeling Lucky" button that builds apps from a screenshot in under two minutes. _Why it matters:_ AI Studio is Google's developer-onboarding moat for Gemini — the Sunday update closes the screenshot-to-deployed-app loop end-to-end, an experience comparable to v0 / Bolt / Lovable but with Gemini 3.1 Pro as the underlying model and Firebase as the deploy target. Sets up the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform announcements at Cloud Next on 21 April. [The Neuron — AI This Weekend 4-5 April 2026](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/-around-the-horn-digest-everything-that-happened-in-ai-this-weekend-saturday-sunday-april-4-5-2026/)

**Waymo lands at San Antonio International — first U.S. airport with full public robotaxi service expected** — Waymo extended its robotaxi service to San Antonio International Airport with full public access expected to follow shortly. Lands the same weekend as WeRide/Uber's driverless robotaxi service in Dubai, with 1,200+ vehicles planned across the Middle East. _Why it matters:_ Airport-curb pick-up is the highest-margin, lowest-defection use case for autonomous-vehicle services and the one that finally pulls B2C economics into the black. Waymo's $16 B February raise at $126 B valuation looks more defensible with each airport landed. [The Neuron — AI This Weekend 4-5 April 2026](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/-around-the-horn-digest-everything-that-happened-in-ai-this-weekend-saturday-sunday-april-4-5-2026/)

**MAD Bugs running disclosures pile up to ~1,000 validated vulnerabilities by Sunday** — The MAD Bugs project's running counter passed 1,000 validated high-severity vulnerabilities by the end of the weekend across multiple major OSes and open-source projects. Anthropic's pre-Mythos briefings cite these numbers privately. _Why it matters:_ Sets the empirical bar for Tuesday's Mythos disclosure. The Mythos "thousands of zero-days" framing is no longer a marketing claim — it's the documented running total from a public 30-day campaign that uses the publicly available Claude harness, not a special model. [Blog Calif — MAD Bugs Claude wrote FreeBSD kernel RCE](https://blog.calif.io/p/mad-bugs-claude-wrote-a-full-freebsd)

**The Neuron weekend wrap-up: "AI is outgrowing its institutions"** — The Sunday digest framed the weekend's story arc explicitly: OpenAI's executive bench thinning at the worst possible moment; AWS in the Gulf disrupted by missile strikes; an autonomous AI agent that hacked FreeBSD in 4 hours; Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis glitching on Chinese highways. The piece argued the through-line is institutional capacity falling behind capability deployment — a frame that becomes the standard journalist gloss on the rest of April. _Why it matters:_ This is the explanatory frame the rest of the month converges on — Anthropic's gated-Glasswing model on 7 April, the Bessent/Powell Wall Street briefings on 10 April, the Bank of England banks briefings on 11 April, all line up against this thesis. [The Neuron — weekend digest](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/-around-the-horn-digest-everything-that-happened-in-ai-this-weekend-saturday-sunday-april-4-5-2026/)

**Goose moves to AAIF — Block donates Goose to Agentic AI Foundation alongside MCP and AGENTS.md** — Block's Goose AI coding agent formally moved to the new Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) at the Linux Foundation over the weekend, with documentation transition to https://goose-docs.ai/ and the GitHub repo migrating from block/goose to aaif-goose. Lands as part of AAIF's anchor-project set alongside Anthropic's Model Context Protocol and OpenAI's AGENTS.md. _Why it matters:_ Three of the most-used agent-ecosystem primitives now live under a Linux Foundation umbrella with neutral governance — important for enterprise procurement teams that have hesitated to bet on vendor-controlled standards. The AAIF + MCP + AGENTS.md stack is the open-source counterweight to NVIDIA Agent Toolkit and the Microsoft Agent Framework. [Goose docs — Goose has a new home: the Agentic AI Foundation](https://goose-docs.ai/blog/2026/04/07/goose-moves-to-aaif/) · [Linux Foundation — Agentic AI Foundation announcement](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation) · [aaif.io — AAIF homepage](https://aaif.io/)
