## Major announcements & releases

**CoreWeave and Meta announce $21 B AI-infrastructure expansion — Vera Rubin's first deployments** — CoreWeave officially announced on 9 April an expanded long-term agreement with Meta to provide AI cloud capacity through December 2032 for ~$21 B. The new commitment is on top of the prior ~$14.2 B arrangement, putting CoreWeave's total contracted backlog with Meta at ~$35 B and making Meta one of CoreWeave's largest customers. Distributed across multiple locations, the deployment will include some of the initial NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform installs. _Why it matters:_ This is the day Meta's strategic stack — Wang (CAIO) → Muse Spark (model) → CoreWeave ($35 B backlog) → Vera Rubin (silicon) — becomes visible in full. It also makes CoreWeave one of the most important nodes in frontier-lab inference economics: nine of the top ten AI model providers now run on the platform. **Cisco announces intent to acquire Galileo Technologies — Splunk + Galileo for full AI-agent observability** — Cisco announced on 9 April its intent to acquire Galileo Technologies, an AI-agent observability and evaluation platform founded in 2021 by Google / Uber AI / Apple alumni. The combined offering extends Splunk Observability Cloud's AI Agent Monitoring with a single instrumentation layer covering prompt optimisation, model selection, evaluation, and production monitoring, plus real-time guardrails for multi-agent systems. Expected close: Q4 of Cisco's fiscal 2026. _Why it matters:_ Two days after joining Project Glasswing, Cisco moves to buy the leading independent agent-observability platform. The pattern — Glasswing access → observability tooling → enterprise control plane — is what Cisco is now selling end-to-end. With Salesforce/Slack also embedding agent monitoring via Agentforce and ServiceNow doing the same in AI Control Tower, observability is becoming the next consolidation lane. [Cisco Blog — Cisco intent to acquire Galileo](https://blogs.cisco.com/news/cisco-announces-the-intent-to-acquire-galileo) · [Futurum Group — AI agent observability at human speed](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/cisco-to-acquire-galileo-ai-agent-observability-cant-run-at-human-speed/) · [Network World — Cisco to acquire Galileo for AI observability](https://www.networkworld.com/article/4156855/cisco-to-acquire-galileo-for-ai-observability.html) · [APMdigest — Cisco intent to acquire Galileo](https://www.apmdigest.com/cisco-announces-intent-acquire-galileo) · [Channel Insider — Cisco targets AI trust with Galileo deal](https://www.channelinsider.com/channel-business/mergers-and-acquisitions/cisco-galielo-acquisition-2026/)

**Anthropic Mythos coverage hits financial press — Bessent/Powell briefing reported as Tuesday lock-in** — Reporting on 9 April confirmed that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Fed Chair Jerome Powell were preparing to convene Wall Street CEOs on Tuesday 14 April (reported by Fortune on 10 April under an embargo) for an emergency-style briefing on Mythos cybersecurity implications. Attendees expected included Citi's Jane Fraser, Morgan Stanley's Ted Pick, BofA's Brian Moynihan, Wells Fargo's Charlie Scharf, Goldman Sachs's David Solomon. _Why it matters:_ This is the first time post-Glasswing that the federal financial regulators are organising banks at CEO level around a specific named frontier model. Sets up the Bank of England equivalent on 11 April and the European Central Bank's follow-on dialogue mid-month. [Fortune — Bessent and Powell convened Wall Street CEOs](https://fortune.com/2026/04/10/bessent-powell-anthropic-mythos-ai-model-cyber-risk/)

**OpenAI Agents SDK ships v1.0 — Microsoft Agent Framework / Azure compatibility surfaced** — OpenAI shipped a v1.0 release of its Agents SDK on 9 April with broader cross-vendor compatibility — specifically integrating with Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0 (shipped 6 April) and into Azure AI Foundry runtime. Together with the Agent Governance Toolkit, this brings the OpenAI Agents SDK into the same runtime governance lane as Anthropic's Computer Use and Google ADK. _Why it matters:_ The Agents SDK becoming explicitly Azure-native (alongside OpenAI API parity) accelerates the broader Microsoft/OpenAI alignment ahead of the next-phase Microsoft–OpenAI partnership announcement (lands 27 April). It also closes a meaningful enterprise procurement gap vs Anthropic's Claude Platform.

**Snowflake AI Pulse prep — Cortex Code adoption hits 50% of customers** — Snowflake pre-briefed analysts on 9 April for its AI Pulse event on 21 April: Cortex Code (launched November 2025) is now in active use by 50%+ of Snowflake customers; Snowflake Intelligence is being positioned as the "personal work agent" surface; deep research with multi-step citation reports going public-preview soon. _Why it matters:_ The 50% Cortex Code adoption number is the first hard metric showing enterprise data-platform vendors moving from "AI feature" to "AI control plane" with measurable uptake. Sets the table for the agentic-enterprise framing at the 21 April event. [Snowflake — expands Snowflake Intelligence and Cortex Code](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-expands-snowflake-intelligence-and-cortex-code-to-power-the-control-plane-for-the-agentic-enterprise/) · [SiliconANGLE — Snowflake targets agentic enterprise](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/21/snowflake-targets-agentic-enterprise-unified-control-plane-ai-data/)
