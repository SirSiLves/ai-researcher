## Major announcements & releases

**OpenAI releases "Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age" — 13-page blueprint with seven big proposals** — OpenAI published a 13-page policy document, "Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age: Ideas to Keep People First," on 6 April. The seven core proposals: treat AI access as a fundamental right; tax reform for the AI economy; a four-day work week; support for displaced workers; faster electrical-grid build-out; a public wealth fund; rapid-response social-safety-net programmes; workers given a voice in AI deployment. OpenAI is opening a fellowships-and-grants pilot of up to $100K and $1M in API credits per project for research that builds on these ideas (newindustrialpolicy@openai.com). _Why it matters:_ This is OpenAI's most explicit move yet to claim the AI-policy narrative ahead of the IPO. Critics (TechPolicyPress, Innovation Attorney) read it as a "policymercial" rather than neutral analysis, but the document sets the agenda the AI safety / labour / industrial-policy conversation will have to engage with for the rest of the year. [OpenAI — Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age](https://openai.com/index/industrial-policy-for-the-intelligence-age/) · [Bloomberg — electric grid, safety net spending for AI era](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-06/openai-advocates-electric-grid-safety-net-spending-for-new-ai-era) · [OpenAI PDF (cdn.openai.com)](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/561e7512-253e-424b-9734-ef4098440601/Industrial%20Policy%20for%20the%20Intelligence%20Age.pdf) · [TechPolicy.Press — "the doublespeak"](https://www.techpolicy.press/the-doublespeak-in-openais-industrial-policy-for-the-intelligence-age/)

**Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0.0 — control-plane separation, Azure pre-config** — Microsoft released Agent Framework 1.0.0 on Monday 6 April, fundamentally rethinking how developers build agents. The new architecture separates the agent control plane from the application, making it simpler to connect to pre-configured systems in Azure. Sits alongside the Agent Governance Toolkit shipped 2/3 April. _Why it matters:_ Closes the second piece of Microsoft's agent-platform triad: Agent Framework (build), Agent Governance Toolkit (run-time policy), and Copilot Studio (distribution). With multi-agent A2A GA from last week, the Microsoft agent stack is now feature-complete in production. [Microsoft Copilot Blog — Copilot Studio April 2026 updates](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/blog/copilot-studio/new-and-improved-agent-governance-intelligent-workflows-and-connected-app-experiences/)

**Mizuho Financial Group launches "Agent Factory" — agent-build time cut from 2 weeks to days** — Mizuho Financial Group rolled out its "Agent Factory" on Monday 6 April, cutting AI-agent development time by 70% (from ~2 weeks to days). The bank is moving beyond proofs-of-concept to mass-produce autonomous agents across its operations, addressing what the industry calls the "scaling wall." _Why it matters:_ First major Japanese megabank to industrialise agent build — pattern other regulated-industry players (Nomura, MUFG, Daiwa) are tracking. The 70% time-to-deploy compression is the key enterprise economic story of Q2: agent platform leverage is no longer abstract. [Daily AI Agent News — April 2026](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/2026-april)

**Anthropic Mythos draft leak coverage expands — Fortune, CNN, Schneier framing the disclosure cycle** — Coverage of the leaked Mythos draft expanded across major outlets on 6 April. Bruce Schneier's blog framed the model as the first "we built it but we're not shipping it" call from a frontier lab on offensive-cyber grounds. Fortune's pre-disclosure reporting estimated Mythos had already found thousands of high-severity vulnerabilities in red-team testing including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug and a 16-year-old flaw in FFmpeg. _Why it matters:_ The 6 April coverage frames the Tuesday Mythos announcement as a fait-accompli rather than a surprise — which is the choreography needed to land Project Glasswing's gated-consortium model without market panic. [Schneier on Security — on Anthropic's Mythos Preview and Project Glasswing](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/04/on-anthropics-mythos-preview-and-project-glasswing.html) · [Fortune — Anthropic leaked Mythos cybersecurity risk](https://fortune.com/2026/03/27/anthropic-leaked-ai-mythos-cybersecurity-risk/)

**The Neuron — Everything That Happened in AI Today, 6 April 2026** — Daily digest confirms the Industrial Policy publication, Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0.0, Mizuho Agent Factory, and the Mythos disclosure choreography building up to Tuesday. _Why it matters:_ The daily-digest framing of "production over research" hardens — five enterprise-platform stories in 24 hours, no foundation-model release. **Rocket launches Rocket 1.0 — "vibe-McKinsey" AI consulting platform, 1.5M users in 180 countries** — Indian AI startup Rocket (Surat) launched Rocket 1.0 on April 6, a vertically integrated platform that combines research, product building, and competitive intelligence to generate "McKinsey-grade" strategy reports (pricing, unit economics, GTM). Subscription plans run $25/$250/$350 per month; user base scaled from 400K to 1.5M across 180 countries since the September seed. Salesforce Ventures + Accel + Together Fund led the $15M round. _Why it matters:_ First scaled "agent disrupts management consulting" story to break out of niche tech press — concrete data point for the Anthropic+Blackstone enterprise services play (lands May 4) and the broader Big-3-consulting-disintermediation thesis. [TechCrunch — Rocket vibe McKinsey-style reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/06/indian-startup-rocket-wants-its-ai-to-do-mckinsey-style-consulting-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/) · [Yahoo Finance syndicate](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/ai-startup-rocket-offers-vibe-053000905.html)
