**CoreWeave–Anthropic $6.8 B multi-year deal — NVIDIA GPU capacity for Claude production inference** — CoreWeave announced on 10 April a multi-year agreement with Anthropic to provide NVIDIA GPU cloud infrastructure for Claude production workloads in U.S. data centres. Comes 24 hours after the $21 B Meta expansion, putting CoreWeave's two-day announcement total at ~$28 B and stock up ~12%. _Why it matters:_ CoreWeave fills the third compute lane for Anthropic — alongside AWS (training) and Google (TPU). With the Mythos disclosure three days earlier and the $30 B ARR confirmation on 7 April, Anthropic now has both the demand profile and the diversified GPU/TPU/Trainium supply to credibly run as the second-largest pure-frontier serving footprint. **Bessent and Powell convene Wall Street CEOs over Mythos — Citi, Morgan Stanley, BofA, Wells, Goldman attend** — Fortune broke on 10 April that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Fed Chair Jerome Powell convened a group of Wall Street CEOs at Treasury HQ over Anthropic's Mythos model. Confirmed attendees: Jane Fraser (Citi), Ted Pick (Morgan Stanley), Brian Moynihan (Bank of America), Charlie Scharf (Wells Fargo), David Solomon (Goldman Sachs). The brief: make sure banks are aware of the cyber-risk profile presented by Mythos and similar future models. _Why it matters:_ This is the first time U.S. financial regulators have convened the bank-CEO class around a single named frontier AI model. Sets the political-economic frame for Q2 financial-sector AI compliance and forces every major bank to put a formal Mythos-class risk policy in front of their board within weeks. [Fortune — Bessent and Powell convened Wall Street CEOs](https://fortune.com/2026/04/10/bessent-powell-anthropic-mythos-ai-model-cyber-risk/)

**Anthropic's Mythos discussed as cyber wake-up call — experts: era is already here** — Fortune ran a companion piece on 10 April from cybersecurity veterans (Kemba Walden, Jen Easterly's circle) framing Mythos not as the start of AI-driven hacking but as the wake-up call about a capability that's been quietly emerging for 12+ months. The piece highlights Anthropic's report of Mythos finding 10-, 20-, and 27-year-old vulnerabilities including in OpenBSD — historically the most secure mainstream operating system. _Why it matters:_ The "already here" framing matters because it pushes the policy response from preventive (gating capability) to compensatory (defender uplift). Schneier and Forrester make the same call. This frame becomes the foundation for the Project Glasswing alumni-network expansion through April.
