## Major announcements & releases

**Cerebras prices the year's largest IPO at $185/share, ~$56B fully-diluted** — AI inference chipmaker Cerebras Systems priced its long-awaited IPO above the marketed range at $185 (raised range was $150–160), with the order book ~20x oversubscribed and total raise of roughly $5.55B. The 2026 AI infrastructure trade is now an investable public asset alongside Nvidia. _Why it matters:_ Validates the disaggregated-serving / non-Nvidia inference thesis with concrete public-market capital and gives OpenAI's Cerebras compute deal a marked-to-market price. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/cerebras-prices-ipo-above-expected-range-wall-street-expects-ai-flood.html]

**SAP Sapphire unveils the "Autonomous Enterprise" — n8n becomes Germany's most valuable AI startup at $5.2B** — At SAP Sapphire 2026 (May 12–14, Orlando), SAP introduced Joule Studio for enterprise-scale agentic development and announced a strategic investment in n8n at a $5.2B valuation (doubled from $2.5B last year), embedding n8n natively in Joule Studio for multi-agent workflow orchestration. SAP also expanded its partner web with Anthropic, AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palantir, Accenture, Mistral and Cohere. _Why it matters:_ The enterprise-AI distribution layer is now the contested ground, and SAP is renting frontier models from everyone while owning the business-context graph. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-sap-unveils-autonomous-enterprise/]

**Anthropic + Blackstone + Hellman & Friedman + Goldman Sachs form new enterprise-AI services company** — Announced May 4 and rolling out this week alongside finance-agent templates and Microsoft 365 add-ins (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Outlook coming soon), the JV will deploy Claude into mid-market operations with forward-deployed engineering. Anthropic also launched Claude for Small Business (May 13) bundling Quickbooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace and M365 connectors. _Why it matters:_ Frontier labs are now stand-up services companies, not just API providers — the implementation-revenue thesis going operational. [https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-ai-services-company]

**OpenAI launches the "OpenAI Deployment Company" and agrees to acquire Tomoro** — Mirroring Anthropic's move, OpenAI stood up a deployment vehicle to embed forward-deployed engineers in enterprise rollouts, day-one staffed via the Tomoro acquihire. Coupled with GPT-5.5 Instant (May 5, default ChatGPT model, –52.5% hallucinated claims vs 5.3 on high-stakes prompts) and new Realtime voice models (May 7). _Why it matters:_ Both frontier labs picked the same week to vertically integrate into services — coverage-of-enterprise is now considered table-stakes margin protection. [https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/]

**IBM Think 2026: watsonx Orchestrate becomes an "agentic control plane"; IBM Confluent + Sovereign Core launch** — At Think (May 5), IBM announced next-gen watsonx Orchestrate (multi-agent orchestration, private preview), IBM Bob (agentic dev partner, GA), IBM Confluent for real-time data, IBM Concert for ops, and Sovereign Core for OpenShift-based operational independence. _Why it matters:_ IBM is staking its blueprint on running other people's agents under one governed control plane — a thesis ServiceNow and Salesforce are converging on too. [https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-05-05-think-2026-ibm-delivers-the-blueprint-for-the-ai-operating-model-as-the-ai-divide-widens]

**ServiceNow opens its full system of action to every AI agent; MCP Server now GA** — Coinciding with SAP Sapphire week, ServiceNow extended AI Control Tower governance to discover/observe/govern any agent in the enterprise (with Microsoft Agent 365 integration and NVIDIA partnership), and made its MCP Server generally available so Claude, Copilot, or homegrown agents can drive ServiceNow workflows headlessly via Action Fabric. Australia GA of Now Assist for Creator (May 5) added Test Agent, Build Agent, MCP client support and semantic search. _Why it matters:_ MCP is no longer a frontier-lab experiment — the largest workflow vendor is shipping it as the agent ingress for an entire enterprise stack. [https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-opens-its-full-system-of-action-to-every-AI-Agent-in-the-enterprise/default.aspx]

**EU AI Act "Omnibus" agreement: shorter transparency grace, postponed sandboxes, "nudifier" ban** — On May 7 the Council and Parliament reached political agreement on amendments simplifying the AI Act. The transparency-marking grace period for AI-generated content drops from 6 to 3 months (Dec 2, 2026 deadline); national AI regulatory sandboxes are pushed to Aug 2, 2027; "nudifier" apps producing intimate content (incl. CSAM) are prohibited. _Why it matters:_ First major rebalancing of the AI Act since enactment — pulls transparency forward, pushes high-risk obligations back, and clarifies AI Office vs national authority competence. [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/]

**Cohere acquires Aleph Alpha to form a ~$20B transatlantic AI champion** — Cohere's acquisition of Heidelberg-based Aleph Alpha (backed by Schwarz Group plus Canadian and German governments) closes the on-again-off-again European "sovereign AI" gap by pairing Cohere's enterprise large models with Aleph Alpha's small-language-model and European-language IP. _Why it matters:_ Europe finally has a flagship enterprise-AI vendor that isn't a US subsidiary; expect French (Mistral) and German policymakers to push hard on procurement preference. [https://cohere.com/blog]

**Microsoft signs joint AI-evaluation agreements with US CAISI and UK AISI** — Announced May 5: Microsoft will work with the US Center for AI Standards & Innovation (CAISI) and the UK AI Security Institute (AISI) on collaborative testing of frontier models, safeguards evaluation, and research on AI behavior in sensitive user contexts. _Why it matters:_ The US/UK government test-bench is becoming a permanent fixture of frontier-model launch hygiene — and Microsoft just locked itself in alongside Anthropic and OpenAI. [https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/05/advancing-ai-evaluation-with-the-center-for-ai-standards-us-and-innovation-and-the-ai-security-institute-uk/]

**Google rebuilds Android around Gemini Intelligence ahead of Apple's AI reboot** — Reported May 12, Google's Sameer Samat confirmed Gemini Intelligence will move across apps, read screen context, and complete multi-step actions (cart-building, reservations) — initially on Samsung Galaxy / Pixel this summer, extending to watches, cars, glasses, laptops, and a redesigned Android Auto. _Why it matters:_ The "operating system → intelligence system" transition is now a competitive sprint with Apple; on-device + agentic UX becomes the consumer-AI battleground for 2026–2027. [https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/gemini-intelligence/]

**Amdocs launches Telco Agents on Google's Gemini Enterprise Agent Marketplace** — Announced May 13, Amdocs Telco Agents (built on its aOS agentic OS for telcos and Cognitive Core) are now live on Google Cloud's partner agent marketplace, automating CSP customer-care, service requests, and BSS/OSS issue resolution under Gemini Enterprise. _Why it matters:_ First high-volume vertical agent marketplace listing — proves the "Gemini Enterprise as agent exchange" model and gives telcos pre-built Gemini agents to plug in rather than build. [https://finance.yahoo.com/news/amdocs-announces-availability-telco-agents-202000772.html]

**Moonshot AI raises $2B at $20B valuation as Chinese frontier-model race intensifies** — Kimi-maker Moonshot's round (led by Meituan's Long-Z, China Mobile, Tsinghua Capital, CPE) lands as Kimi K2.6 (1T MoE, 32B active, 256K context, native multimodal, INT4) leads the open-source LiveBench coding leaderboard at 78.57 (May 12 snapshot). DeepSeek-V4 (1.6T/49B-active Pro and 284B/13B-active Flash, 1M context, MIT) sits alongside in production. _Why it matters:_ Chinese open-weight models are now competitive at the frontier — and they have the capital to keep iterating. [https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates]

**AWS MCP Server hits General Availability — one tool for 300+ services, 15,000+ APIs** — Announced May 6, AWS shipped GA of its managed MCP Server (part of the Agent Toolkit for AWS). Coding agents can now call any AWS API through a single MCP tool, with sandboxed Python execution against AWS services, IAM-context-key authorization, CloudTrail audit logging, and unauthenticated documentation retrieval. Free to use; pay only for the AWS resources agents consume. _Why it matters:_ The world's largest public cloud just made MCP the default agent ingress for its entire control plane — a major step toward MCP becoming the de-facto agent-to-cloud protocol, on par with ServiceNow's GA earlier this week. [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/the-aws-mcp-server-is-now-generally-available/]

**SAP Sapphire Day 3: Joule Studio 2.0 + 51 Joule Assistants + 224 agents; Claude becomes primary reasoning engine on SAP Business AI Platform** — On May 14, SAP rolled out Joule Studio 2.0 (intent-based agent development on SAP-managed infra; no-code, pro-code and AI frameworks side-by-side; auto-generates PRDs, specs, workflow logic, evals, and multi-agent orchestration) and pre-built 224 agents and 51 Joule Assistants across finance, supply chain, procurement, HCM, and CX. The Autonomous Suite will deploy 50+ Joule Assistants orchestrating 200+ specialized agents end-to-end. SAP separately announced expanded Anthropic partnership: Claude becomes a primary reasoning and agentic capability across the SAP Business AI Platform via Joule. First customers receive Joule Studio 2.0 in June; design-time access free through end of 2026. _Why it matters:_ SAP just operationalized "the autonomous enterprise" with concrete agent counts and made Anthropic the preferred frontier-model partner — a clear shift away from the OpenAI-by-default assumption in enterprise AI. [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/new-joule-studio-enterprise-scale-agentic-development/] (See also: [https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-anthropic-to-bring-claude-sap-business-ai-platform/])

**Anthropic splits Claude subscriptions: programmatic usage gets its own monthly credit ($20–$200) starting June 15** — Effective June 15, Anthropic is separating programmatic Claude usage (SDKs, CLIs, third-party tools, Claude Agent SDK) from interactive subscription quotas. Pro users get $20/mo, Max 5x get $100, Max 20x get $200 in dedicated credits, billed at full API rates. Interactive Claude Code use is unchanged; activation email goes out June 8. _Why it matters:_ The price floor for autonomous-agent workloads has just been made explicit — heavy programmatic users (agent farms, CI bots, multi-agent harnesses) will now see real per-action billing visibility instead of the soft soft-cap that previously absorbed it. Signals Anthropic's read that agent-driven inference is now the dominant cost driver. [https://the-decoder.com/claude-subscriptions-get-separate-budgets-for-programmatic-use-billed-at-full-api-prices/]

**Subquadratic exits stealth with $29M and SubQ — claims first sub-quadratic frontier LLM, 12M-token context** — Miami-based Subquadratic (CEO Justin Dangel; CTO Alex Whedon, ex-Meta GenAI head) launched May 5 with $29M seed at a reported $500M valuation. SubQ uses an SSA architecture that scales linearly with context, claims ~1,000x lower attention compute at 12M tokens, and posts 95.0% RULER@128K, 65.9% MRCR@1M, and 81.8% SWE-Bench Verified. Three private-beta products: SubQ API (full 12M context), SubQ Code (CLI agent), SubQ Search. _Why it matters:_ First credible frontier-class challenger to attention-as-default in two years. If the benchmarks survive independent verification, the long-context-vs-RAG calculus shifts materially. Treat with skepticism — no peer review yet — but the talent and investor list is serious. [https://venturebeat.com/technology/miami-startup-subquadratic-claims-1-000x-ai-efficiency-gain-with-subq-model-researchers-demand-independent-proof]

**Novo Nordisk and OpenAI strike multi-year, cross-functional partnership — full deployment by end of 2026** — Announced April 14 and rolling out through May, the deal places OpenAI models across Novo's R&D (drug discovery, clinical trials), manufacturing, supply chain, and corporate functions, plus a workforce upskilling program. Pilot programs are now live; full integration target is end of 2026 with strict data-protection / human-oversight governance. _Why it matters:_ A second pharma giant (after Lilly) commits to OpenAI as the operating-system layer, not just a tool — the implementation-revenue thesis hitting a regulated, high-margin vertical at scale. [https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/14/3273010/0/en/novo-nordisk-and-openai-partner-to-transform-how-medicines-are-discovered-and-delivered.html]
