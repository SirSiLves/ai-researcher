## Major announcements & releases

**Sakana AI unveils KAME — tandem speech-to-speech architecture injecting LLM knowledge in real time** — Tokyo-based Sakana AI released KAME ("turtle" in Japanese) on 3 May, a tandem speech architecture that pairs a fast speech-to-speech (S2S) frontend with an asynchronous backend LLM via an "oracle" stream. Partial transcripts flow to the LLM as the user speaks; the LLM emits tentative "oracle" guesses that refine in real time as more speech is processed — flipping the paradigm from "think then speak" to "speak while thinking." Sakana trained KAME using Simulated Oracle Augmentation: a simulator LLM generates synthetic responses at various completeness levels, used to train the front-end to lean on partial backend signals. Result: MT-Bench score climbs from 2.05 (vanilla S2S) to 6.43 while maintaining near-zero latency. Backend-agnostic — swap GPT-4.1, Claude-Opus-4-1, or Gemini-2.5-Flash without retraining the front-end. ICASSP 2026 paper; open weights on Hugging Face. _Why it matters:_ This is the cleanest architectural innovation in voice AI since OpenAI's GPT-Realtime separation of audio reasoning/translation/transcription. The async oracle pattern is portable — likely to show up inside Sierra, Vapi and enterprise voice stacks within weeks. It also reframes the "speech-to-speech vs cascade pipelines" debate as a false dichotomy.
