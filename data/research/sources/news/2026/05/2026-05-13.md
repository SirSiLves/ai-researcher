## Major announcements & releases

**Google's Android Show — Gemini Intelligence everywhere, Googlebook unveiled** — At the Android Show on 12 May, Google previewed "Gemini Intelligence" as a proactive AI layer across Android phones, Chrome, Android Auto and a new laptop class. Gemini can read on-screen context, pull live data from Gmail/Calendar/Drive, and complete multi-step tasks (building shopping carts, booking reservations, drafting replies). Googlebook is a new laptop category designed around Gemini Intelligence — built with Acer, Asus, Dell, HP and Lenovo, shipping this fall — with Magic Pointer (point at anything → Gemini suggestions), Create My Widget (generative UI), and Cast My Apps mirroring phone apps to the laptop. Features roll out first on Samsung Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10 lineups. _Why it matters:_ Google is timing this directly against Apple's expected Siri/Apple Intelligence reboot at WWDC, and is using Gemini as the OS-layer differentiator. Bloomberg framed it as "putting Gemini at the center of Android before Apple's AI reboot." [Gemini Intelligence on Android](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/gemini-intelligence/) · [Introducing Googlebook](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/meet-googlebook/) · [TechCrunch — everything announced](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/everything-google-announced-at-its-android-show-from-googlebooks-to-vibe-coded-widgets/) · [Bloomberg — Google vs Apple AI](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/google-unveils-android-ai-updates-ahead-of-apple-s-siri-revamp) · [CNBC framing](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/12/google-races-put-gemini-at-center-of-android-before-apples-ai-reboot.html)

**Google DeepMind unveils Gemini-powered Magic Pointer (experimental)** — DeepMind researchers detailed an AI-enabled mouse pointer powered by Gemini that captures visual and semantic context around the cursor — understanding not just where you point but what you point at and why. Two demos are live in Google AI Studio today (image editing, map place-finding), with a deeper "Magic Pointer" rolling into Chrome and the new Googlebook laptops. _Why it matters:_ This is the productization piece behind the Googlebook announcement and one of the first concrete examples of "context-aware" agents that sit between the user and the screen rather than in a chat window. [MarkTechPost coverage of the DeepMind pointer paper](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/google-deepmind-introduces-an-ai-enabled-mouse-pointer-powered-by-gemini-that-captures-visual-and-semantic-context-around-the-cursor/)

**DeepSeek nears $45 B valuation in first-ever VC round, led by China's "Big Fund"** — DeepSeek is in talks to raise $3–4 B in its first outside funding round, with valuation up from $20 B to $45 B in weeks. China's state-backed Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund ("Big Fund") is set to lead — its first known investment in a frontier LLM developer — with Tencent and Alibaba also reportedly participating. Capital targets compute capacity, infra, and talent retention as the V4 Preview / Huawei-chip-integrated stack scales. _Why it matters:_ Beijing is now visibly underwriting a frontier-lab champion. The Big Fund's involvement signals state-aligned compute access at a level Western frontier labs cannot match on cost; the round closes the funding-gap critique that has dogged DeepSeek since the V3/R1 surprises. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/06/deepseek-could-hit-45b-valuation-from-its-first-investment-round/) · [Bloomberg via FT — Big Fund leading](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-06/china-chip-fund-in-talks-to-lead-mega-deepseek-funding-ft-says) · [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/07/deepseek-reportedly-seeks-first-funding-round-at-45-billion-valuation/)

**OpenAI's enterprise chief: AI adoption is "at a tipping point"** — OpenAI revenue chief Giancarlo Dresser said on 11 May that enterprise AI adoption has crossed an inflection — anchored by the OpenAI Deployment Company (announced 11 May, $4 B raise from TPG/Advent/Bain Capital/Brookfield/Goldman Sachs/Capgemini/McKinsey/Bain & Co/11 others; acquiring Tomoro for ~150 Forward Deployed Engineers). Dresser cited stepped-up Fortune 500 wins and the new vehicle's role in turning pilots into production. _Why it matters:_ OpenAI is explicitly betting that the bottleneck is no longer model capability but enterprise rollout — and is building a global SI footprint to capture that margin rather than ceding it to Accenture/Deloitte. Accenture's stock dropped 3% on the news. Context carries between Office apps; Claude Opus 4.7 leads Vals AI's Finance Agent benchmark at 64.37%. Claude Opus 4.7 finance benchmark lead and Microsoft 365 distribution close the "but how do enterprises actually use it?" loop. **OpenAI Voice Intelligence — three new realtime models (GPT-Realtime-2, Translate, Whisper)** — On 7 May OpenAI released a new generation of realtime voice in the API: GPT-Realtime-2 (first voice model with GPT-5-class reasoning), GPT-Realtime-Translate (70+ input languages → 13 output languages, live), and GPT-Realtime-Whisper (streaming live transcription). Pricing: $32 / 1M audio in, $64 / 1M audio out for Realtime-2; $0.034/min for Translate; $0.017/min for Whisper. Available via Azure AI Foundry as well. _Why it matters:_ Real-time voice was the missing primitive for "voice-first agentic" deployments; this is the developer-facing splitting of audio into reasoning, translation and transcription as separate specialized models — what enterprise voice-bot stacks (Sierra, Vapi) had been hand-rolling. **Sierra raises $950 M at $15 B+ valuation as enterprise-AI agent race intensifies** — AI agent company Sierra (cofounded by OpenAI chair Bret Taylor) closed a $950 M round led by Tiger Global and GV, lifting valuation from $10 B (fall 2025) to north of $15 B. Sierra's pitch: customer-facing AI agents for the Fortune 500, deployed and operated. _Why it matters:_ Combined with the OpenAI Deployment Company launch the same week, this signals where venture capital believes the AI margin is — not in foundation models, but in customer-facing applied-agent companies positioned to absorb the SI revenue. **Cloudflare details its high-performance LLM infrastructure (split-stage inference, Infire, Unweight)** — InfoQ published a deep-dive on 12 May into Cloudflare's Workers AI architecture. The platform separates prefill (input processing) and decode (output generation) onto different optimized machines; uses Cloudflare's custom Infire inference engine for multi-GPU efficiency; and adds Unweight, a weight-compression system that shrinks LLM weights by 15–22% without accuracy loss. Moonshot's Kimi K2.5 is the first open-source flagship running on the stack. _Why it matters:_ This is the clearest public account yet of edge-LLM serving design choices — disaggregated prefill/decode is being adopted outside hyperscaler labs, and Unweight reframes the weight-loading bottleneck for global edge inference. [InfoQ — Cloudflare LLM infrastructure](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/cloudflare-llm-infrastructure/) · [Cloudflare blog — running extra-large LLMs](https://blog.cloudflare.com/high-performance-llms/)

**Cerebras prices IPO at $185 — $5.55B raised, $56.4B fully-diluted, year's biggest tech IPO; rejected SoftBank acquisition** — Cerebras Systems priced its initial public offering at $185 per share on May 13, 2026 — $25 above the marketed range that itself had been raised twice (from $115–$125 → $150–$160). The wafer-scale AI inference vendor sold 30M shares for $5.55B in proceeds, valuing the company at $56.4B fully diluted; orders ran 20× over-subscribed. Cerebras (ticker CBRS) trades on Nasdaq starting May 14. The company explicitly rejected a SoftBank acquisition approach to list independently. _Why it matters:_ The first major AI-inference IPO outside NVIDIA / Groq, and the largest tech IPO of 2026. Public-market validation of disaggregated AI inference (Cerebras CS-3 + AWS Trainium architecture) and a forcing function for inference-economics comparables that NVIDIA / Groq investors have so far had to estimate from private rounds. [Bloomberg — Cerebras to price at $185](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-13/ai-chipmaker-cerebras-said-poised-to-price-ipo-at-185-per-share) · [CNBC — Wall Street braces for AI tsunami](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/cerebras-prices-ipo-above-expected-range-wall-street-expects-ai-flood.html) · [SiliconANGLE — biggest tech IPO in years](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/13/cerebras-prices-shares-185-ahead-biggest-tech-ipo-years/) · [TradingKey — pricing raised three times, SoftBank rejected](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261892914-cerebras-185-ipo-price-49b-valuation-turning-down-softbank-tradingkey)
