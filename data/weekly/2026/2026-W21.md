## The week in 90 seconds

**Inside 48 hours, the agent-interop layer split three ways — Anthropic consolidated, Microsoft open-sourced, Google shipped the first frontier-lab commerce stack.**

The opening two days of W21 settled questions that had been hanging since March. The story is who owns the agent-interop layer — the SDK, the protocol, the payment rails, the consumer surface — and every credible competitor staked its position before Wednesday. The HN crowd, normally generous on launch days, has been skeptical-by-default for three days running.

Monday delivered the structural moves. Anthropic acquired Stainless for a reported $300M — the codegen pipeline that has shipped every official Anthropic SDK since 2022 and also generates SDKs, CLIs and MCP servers for OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare and Meta across TypeScript, Python, Go, Java and Kotlin. Microsoft answered the same morning at Open Source Summit NA by open-sourcing the Microsoft Agent Framework, the Agent Governance Toolkit and the Agentic AI Foundation at the Linux Foundation, where AAIF is already the fastest-growing project. IBM Research and Hugging Face launched the Open Agent Leaderboard alongside, the first benchmark that scores the harness rather than the model. Cursor shipped Composer 2.5 — a Kimi K2.5-based coding agent priced at $0.50 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output, claiming Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 parity. It was the first credible "application-layer company trains its own model" undercut on a workload Cursor actually controls.

Tuesday made the split operational. Google I/O 2026 answered six months of model-narrative losses in a single keynote: Gemini 3.5 Flash as the new default across Google products, Gemini Omni Flash for unified any-input-to-video, Gemini Spark as a 24/7 personal agent on dedicated Cloud VMs — and the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) with 60+ launch partners spanning Mastercard, American Express, JCB, UnionPay, PayPal, Coinbase, Etsy, Intuit, Salesforce, ServiceNow and Adyen. Paired with Universal Cart across Search, Gemini, YouTube and Gmail for US summer rollout, AP2 is the first end-to-end agent-commerce stack from a frontier lab — credit-card networks, PayPal and crypto rails on the same launch list. Gemini now reports 900M+ monthly users across 230 countries. Antigravity 2.0 absorbs the Gemini CLI, ships subagent orchestration and integrates DeepMind's CodeMender code-security agent (72 upstream fixes across 4.5M-line codebases). Android XR glasses (Samsung "Jinju," XREAL, Warby Parker, Gentle Monster, Kering/Gucci 2027), Aluminium OS as a ChromeOS replacement under HP/Lenovo/Acer/ASUS "Googlebook" branding Q3 2026, and Google Beam at $24,999 round out a Gemini-first hardware story.

The Anthropic counter-move was distributional and operational. Andrej Karpathy — OpenAI founding member, ex-Tesla AI Director, founder of Eureka Labs — joined Anthropic to lead a new pre-training research team under Nick Joseph. The HN reception (702 points) was unusually skeptical: top comments read it as IPO theatre with Eureka Labs and LLM101n left in limbo. Anthropic also closed the Stainless deal and immediately announced it is winding down all hosted Stainless products, including the SDK generator that has been the de-facto OpenAI SDK pipeline. The most-upvoted HN comment: "we just bought OpenAI's front door and we're EOLing it." Claude Managed Agents added self-hosted sandboxes that keep tool execution inside customer infrastructure plus MCP tunnels that reach private MCP servers via a single outbound encrypted gateway — the missing regulated-industries primitive. A global alliance with KPMG (276,000 professionals, 138 countries) follows PwC and the Gates Foundation from May 14 as the third Big Four / strategic-consulting lockup in two weeks.

Three threads to watch through Sunday. First, sentiment: Cursor's CursorBench is being openly distrusted after retroactive score changes between Composer 2 and 2.5, with practitioners reporting Kimi K2.5 fails tool-calling tasks where Qwen3.6 succeeds — if independent benchmarks don't restore credibility, the application-layer-undercut thesis collapses and Anthropic's June 15 Agent SDK billing split (Pro $20 / Max 5x $100 / Max 20x $200 monthly caps; Magna Capax models 12×–175× effective increases for harness-heavy workflows) stays unanswered. Second, governance: Germany's BSI publicly named Anthropic's Mythos as a system that could "drastically lower" cyberattack barriers — the first time a German federal regulator has flagged a specific frontier model — pairing with the White House Mythos export-control template and the EU AI Act Omnibus August enforcement to make 2026 governance bilateral, model-specific and three-jurisdiction. Third, capital: Anthropic's reported $30B round at $900B+ is closing by end of May (Q1 ARR above $44B, 1,000+ customers at $1M+/year), past OpenAI's March $852B mark; Recursive Superintelligence exited stealth with $650M at $4.65B with Nvidia and AMD both on the cap table; Isomorphic Labs closed $2.1B Series B with first AI-designed clinical trials targeted by year-end.

**Read this first:** [Announcing the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2)](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol) — the single document that defines how agents will pay across the open web, with the credit-card networks, PayPal and crypto rails on the same launch list. Pair it with [Anthropic acquires Stainless](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless) for the full week-opening picture.

## Top papers

- **The Scaling Laws of Skills in LLM Agent Systems** — _Why notable:_ Across 15 frontier LLMs, 1,141 real-world skills, and over 3M routing/execution decisions, identifies two coupled laws: single-step routing accuracy decays logarithmically with library size (R² > 0.97), and correct execution rescues routing errors by ~4×. First quantitative scaling theory for agent skill libraries — directly relevant to anyone building skill marketplaces. [arXiv:2605.16508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16508)
- **SkillsVote — Lifecycle Governance of Agent Skills** — _Why notable:_ Treats Agent Skills as a versioned governed experience schema; profiles a million-scale corpus, synthesizes verifiable tasks, agentic library-search step before execution. The governance complement to today's Scaling Laws paper — together they cover the build/run/govern triad for the substrate three of the top five trending GitHub repos are converging on. [arXiv:2605.18401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18401)
- **ADR: An Agentic Detection System for Enterprise Agentic AI Security** — _Why notable:_ Claims first production-proven enterprise framework for securing MCP-based AI agents; ADR Sensor for agentic telemetry + Expert reasoning layer + cost-aware inference. Direct evidence MCP security tooling is moving from research to deployed product. [arXiv:2605.17380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17380)
- **TOBench / MM-ToolBench** — _Why notable:_ 100 executable tasks, 20 subcategories, 27 MCP servers exposing 324 tools — evaluates tool use, computer use, and multimodal reasoning together rather than in isolation. [arXiv:2605.16909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16909)
- **Code as Agent Harness** — _Why notable:_ Reframes code as the operational substrate for reasoning, acting, environment modeling, and verification — a "harness" view unifying interface, mechanism, and infrastructure layers. [arXiv:2605.18747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18747)
- **FORGE: Self-Evolving Agent Memory With No Weight Updates via Population Broadcast** — Bogdanov, Lung, Kunz, Gao, Taylor, Zaman. _Why notable:_ Population-based protocol that evolves prompt-injected natural-language memory for hierarchical ReAct agents — failed trajectories become reusable rules, best-performing instance's memory is broadcast, converged instances freeze. Concrete blueprint for self-improving agents without touching weights. [arXiv:2605.16233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)
- **Differentiable Mixture-of-Agents Incentivizes Swarm Intelligence of LLMs** — Wu, Lu, Yan et al. _Why notable:_ Replaces hand-wired multi-agent topologies with a differentiable, context-aware router that dynamically activates agents at each reasoning step — implicitly simulating diverse communication graphs without static workflows. [arXiv:2605.15706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15706)
- **Agentic Discovery of Neural Architectures: AIRA-Compose and AIRA-Design** — Pepe, Lin, Magka et al. _Why notable:_ LLM agents autonomously design foundation-model architectures beyond Transformers — produces 14 AIRAformer/AIRAhybrid (Transformer-Mamba) variants that beat Llama 3.2 at 1B/3B and find more compute-efficient frontiers. Tangible recursive self-improvement evidence. [arXiv:2605.15871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15871)
- **ConflictRAG** — _Why notable:_ Two-stage knowledge-conflict detection (embedding MLP + selective LLM refinement) cuts API costs 62% while keeping 90.8% detection accuracy; adds Entropy-TOPSIS source-credibility scorer. [arXiv:2605.17301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17301)
- **PropGuard** — _Why notable:_ Multi-agent prompt-injection defense via dual-view spatio-temporal graph (response-centric risk + full-state evidence) that traces propagation paths and remediates contaminated state without disrupting benign collaboration. [arXiv:2605.16346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16346)
- **LongMINT** — _Why notable:_ Memory-under-interference benchmark for long-horizon agents — closes the gap left by LoCoMo/LongMemEval (dynamic interactions, not just static recall). Pairs naturally with rohitg00/agentmemory's day-two GitHub surge. [arXiv:2605.18565](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18565)
- **Judge Circuits** — Feldhus et al. _Why notable:_ Mechanistically locates a shared "Latent Evaluator" sub-graph in mid-to-late MLPs of Gemma-3 / Qwen2.5 / Llama-3; zero-ablating it collapses LLM-as-judge scores while preserving world knowledge. First mechanistic-interpretability paper in this week's daily — explains format-induced LLM-eval inconsistency at the circuit level. [arXiv:2605.16023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16023)
