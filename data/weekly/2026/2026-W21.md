## The week in 90 seconds

**OpenAI files a confidential $1T S-1 as Anthropic prints its first profit and Chinese models cross 60% of OpenRouter.**

The frontier-lab race stopped being a capability story this week and became a listed-equity story. Across five days the platform map, the US policy frame and the cost-curve narrative were all rewritten before the weekend.

The headline event arrived Friday, when OpenAI submitted a confidential draft S-1 to the SEC with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as joint book-runners, targeting a September listing above $1T on roughly $25B in annualised revenue. The Wall Street Journal landed the counter-story the same week: Anthropic's Q2 came in at $10.9B revenue and $559M operating profit, its first profitable quarter ever, with compute-to-revenue down from 71 cents to 56. SpaceX's own prospectus accidentally exposed the underlying compute deal, disclosing a $1.25B-per-month, $45B-through-2029 Cloud Services Agreement with Anthropic for the full output of Colossus 1 in Memphis — the largest single-customer compute contract ever made public. Coinbase prediction markets now imply an 85% chance OpenAI lists before Anthropic, and the cost-curve question that has haunted frontier valuations for two years suddenly has a single-quarter data point that flips it.

The structural story of the first three days was the fork in the agent-interop layer. Monday, Anthropic acquired Stainless — the codegen pipeline that ships SDKs and MCP servers for OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare and Meta — in a deal The Information pegged above $300M. By Tuesday Anthropic confirmed the close and announced every hosted Stainless product was being wound down, including the generator that has been the de facto OpenAI SDK pipeline. The most-upvoted Hacker News comment of the week captured the mood: "we just bought OpenAI's front door and we're EOLing it." The same Monday, Microsoft countered by open-sourcing its Agent Framework and Agent Governance Toolkit under the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation, already that foundation's fastest-growing project. Wednesday added three enterprise platforms in a single day — Dell AI Factory 2.0 on NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8, SAP's MCP/A2A-native Joule Studio, and ServiceNow's cross-vendor AI Control Tower — plus a KPMG global alliance embedding Claude across 138 countries and 276,000 professionals.

Google I/O 2026 was Sundar Pichai's strongest model-narrative answer in a year. Gemini 3.5 Flash became the new default and reached GA by Thursday at $1.50/$9 per million tokens; Gemini Omni Flash unified any-input-to-video; Gemini Spark put a 24/7 personal agent on dedicated Cloud VMs. The Agent Payments Protocol launched as an open spec with sixty-plus co-signers including Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Adyen and Coinbase. Universal Cart rolled across Search, Gemini, YouTube and Gmail with Nike, Sephora, Target, Walmart and Shopify as launch retailers, and Blackstone committed $5B in equity into a Google TPU joint venture targeting 500MW by 2027 — the largest single PE commitment to non-NVIDIA AI compute on record. The counter-current: Gemini 3.5 Flash costs roughly 3x Gemini 3.0 Flash and 6x 3.1 Flash-Lite, the first time anyone can remember the "Flash gets cheaper every generation" expectation breaking.

Underneath all of it, the cost-curve question went mainstream. CNBC's Friday investigation lined up Artificial Analysis numbers on a standard 10-evaluation workload — $4,811 for Claude, $3,357 for ChatGPT, $544 for Zhipu GLM, roughly 9x — and reported that the OpenRouter share for Chinese models climbed from about 1% in 2024 to over 60% in May 2026. Anthropic's own May policy paper conceded US models are only "several months ahead." Trump pulled his AI cybersecurity executive order on signing day, telling reporters "I didn't like certain aspects of it"; by Friday the postponement was indefinite, leaving the FRB/OCC bank-exam pause, BSI warning and G20 FSB briefing without a federal coordinating instrument. The single anchor pulling the other way was an internal OpenAI general-purpose reasoning model disproving Erdős's 1946 planar unit-distance conjecture, independently verified by Noga Alon, Melanie Wood and Thomas Bloom — landing in the exact week three frontier labs needed an evidence point for their narratives. Saturday and Sunday still to come.

**Read this first:** [CNBC — Cheap AI could derail OpenAI and Anthropic's IPOs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/cheap-ai-could-derail-openai-and-anthropics-ipos.html) — the one piece that names the tension between this week's S-1 drumbeat and the cost reality the prospectuses will eventually have to address.

## Top papers
- **The Scaling Laws of Skills in LLM Agent Systems** — Across 15 frontier LLMs, 1,141 real-world skills, and 3M+ routing/execution decisions, identifies two coupled laws: single-step routing accuracy decays logarithmically with library size (R² > 0.97); correct execution rescues routing errors by ~4×. First quantitative scaling theory for agent skill libraries. [arXiv:2605.16508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16508)
- **SkillsVote — Lifecycle Governance of Agent Skills** — Treats Agent Skills as a versioned governed experience schema; profiles a million-scale corpus, synthesises verifiable tasks, agentic library-search step before execution. Governance complement to the Scaling Laws paper. [arXiv:2605.18401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18401)
- **Code as Agent Harness** — Reframes code as the operational substrate for reasoning, acting, environment modelling and verification — a "harness" view unifying interface, mechanism and infrastructure layers. [arXiv:2605.18747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18747)
- **ADR: An Agentic Detection System for Enterprise Agentic AI Security** — Claims first production-proven enterprise framework for securing MCP-based AI agents; ADR Sensor + Expert reasoning layer + cost-aware inference. [arXiv:2605.17380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17380)
- **TOBench / MM-ToolBench** — 100 executable tasks, 20 subcategories, 27 MCP servers exposing 324 tools — evaluates tool use, computer use and multimodal reasoning together rather than in isolation. [arXiv:2605.16909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16909)
- **FORGE: Self-Evolving Agent Memory With No Weight Updates via Population Broadcast** — Population-based protocol that evolves prompt-injected natural-language memory for hierarchical ReAct agents — failed trajectories become reusable rules. [arXiv:2605.16233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)
- **A Survey of Agent Interoperability Protocols: MCP, ACP, A2A, ANP** — Side-by-side of the four leading agent-communication protocols with deployment-context trade-offs. Reference text when picking an agent-runtime substrate. [arXiv:2505.02279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02279)
- **Rethinking How to Remember: Beyond Atomic Facts in Lifelong LLM Agent Memory** — Atomic-fact memory collapses under long-horizon use; structured compositional representation preserves provenance and relational context. Direct relevance to the Dreaming / persistent-memory push at Code with Claude. [arXiv:2605.19952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19952)
- **MCJudgeBench — Constraint-Level Judge Evaluation in Multi-Constraint Instruction Following** — Pushes LLM-as-judge from "did it follow the instruction" to per-constraint verdicts; exposes that judges are weakest exactly where production prompts live. [arXiv:2605.03858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03858)
- **Towards Reliable LLM Evaluation: Correcting the Winner's Curse in Adaptive Benchmarking** — Adaptive prompt/program search inflates reported scores via winner's-curse effect; proposes correction so leaderboards track fresh-data performance. [arXiv:2605.05973](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05973)
- **Towards Dependable RAG Using Factual Confidence Prediction** — Trains confidence predictor over RAG outputs so the system can abstain or escalate when factual grounding is weak — deployable primitive for high-stakes RAG. [arXiv:2605.05244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05244)
- **MARLIN: Multi-Agent Game-Theoretic RL for Sustainable LLM Inference in Cloud Datacenters** — Co-optimises latency, cost and carbon for LLM serving — for platform/operations teams sizing inference fleets and defending cost-per-token economics. [arXiv:2605.13496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13496)
- **MCP Tool Descriptions Are Smelly! Augmented MCP Tool Descriptions** — Empirical study showing real-world MCP tool descriptions exhibit code-smell-like quality problems that hurt agent tool selection; augmentation heuristics lift downstream task success. [arXiv:2602.14878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14878)
