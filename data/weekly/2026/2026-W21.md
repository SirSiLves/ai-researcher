## The week in 90 seconds

**Agent-interop splits, OpenAI files, Anthropic prints a profit, and Trump pulls the cybersecurity EO on signing day.**

Four days into W21 the platform map and the US policy frame have both been redrawn. Anthropic bought the SDK pipeline its biggest rival depends on and started winding it down. Google I/O shipped the first end-to-end agent-commerce stack from a frontier lab and pushed Gemini 3.5 Flash to GA. OpenAI moved toward a confidential SEC filing at a valuation approaching $1T while Anthropic told CNBC it expects $10.9B in Q2 revenue and its first-ever profitable quarter. And on Thursday the federal coordinating instrument designed to sit on top of the Mythos cascade dissolved when Trump pulled his AI cybersecurity executive order on its own signing day. Friday through Sunday still to come.

The structural story of the first three days was the fork in the agent-interop layer. On Monday Anthropic acquired Stainless, the codegen pipeline that ships SDKs and MCP servers for OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare and Meta in five languages; The Information pegged the deal above $300M. By Tuesday Anthropic had confirmed the close and announced that every hosted Stainless product — including the generator that has been the de facto OpenAI SDK pipeline — was being wound down. The most-upvoted Hacker News comment, "we just bought OpenAI's front door and we're EOLing it," captured the mood. The same Monday, Microsoft put its Agent Framework, an Agent Governance Toolkit and the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation on the table at Open Source Summit, already the foundation's fastest-growing project. Wednesday added three enterprise platforms shipping at once — Dell AI Factory 2.0 on NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8, SAP's MCP/A2A-native Joule Studio, ServiceNow's cross-vendor AI Control Tower — and a KPMG global alliance embedding Claude across 138 countries and 276,000 professionals.

Google I/O 2026 was Sundar Pichai's strongest model-narrative answer in a year. Gemini 3.5 Flash became the new default, claimed 4× faster than comparable frontier models at less than half the cost; Gemini Omni Flash unified any-input-to-video; Gemini Spark put a 24/7 personal agent on dedicated Cloud VMs. The Agent Payments Protocol launched as an open spec with 60-plus co-signers including Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Adyen, Coinbase, Intuit, Salesforce and ServiceNow. By Thursday Gemini 3.5 Flash had hit GA, Universal Cart rolled across Search, Gemini, YouTube and Gmail with Nike, Sephora, Target, Walmart and Shopify as launch retailers, and Blackstone committed $5B in equity into a Google TPU joint venture targeting 500MW by 2027 and up to $25B leveraged — the largest single PE commitment to non-NVIDIA AI compute on record. The counter-current: Gemini 3.5 Flash costs roughly 3× Gemini 3.0 Flash and 6× 3.1 Flash-Lite, the first time anyone can remember the "Flash gets cheaper every generation" expectation breaking.

Underneath both threads, Mythos went from research artifact to multi-jurisdiction regulatory primitive in four days, then lost its federal coordinating instrument. Monday the White House blocked expansion of Mythos access to roughly 70 outside organisations. Tuesday Germany's BSI publicly named Mythos as a system that could "drastically lower" cyberattack barriers — the first time a German federal regulator has flagged a specific frontier model. Wednesday Bloomberg broke that the Federal Reserve and OCC are pausing cyber-related examinations of the largest US banks to give them time to patch vulnerabilities Mythos surfaced, including a 17-year-old FreeBSD RCE filed as CVE-2026-4747; Anthropic agreed at Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey's request to brief the G20 Financial Stability Board. Thursday Switzerland's FINMA joined BaFin/BSI/FRB/OCC/FSB in classifying Mythos-class capability "immediate systemic risk," Schwyz-based QPQ IaaS AG shipped a 2FA-hardening countermeasure aimed at the Mythos attack surface, and Trump pulled the cybersecurity EO — which would have given federal agencies up to 90 days to security-review frontier models — telling reporters "I didn't like certain aspects of it."

The Thursday market story is the fourth thread. OpenAI is preparing to confidentially file a draft IPO prospectus with the SEC as soon as Friday with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, targeting a public listing as early as September at a valuation approaching $1T. Anthropic separately told CNBC it projects Q2 revenue of $10.9B — more than twice Q1's $4.8B and larger than all of 2025 — with $559M in operating profit, its first profitable quarter, years ahead of plan. With the federal EO gone, every active governance debate reframes as a listed-equity story. The body below adds Pinecone's Nexus and KnowQL launch as the first Tier-1 vector-DB post-RAG repositioning, Germany's Hightech-Agenda landing as the de facto pan-EU policy anchor, DeepSeek standing up a Beijing "Harness" team to ship a Claude Code competitor, and HN sentiment hardening into a four-day skeptical baseline.

## Top papers
- **The Scaling Laws of Skills in LLM Agent Systems** — Across 15 frontier LLMs, 1,141 real-world skills, and 3M+ routing/execution decisions, identifies two coupled laws: single-step routing accuracy decays logarithmically with library size (R² > 0.97); correct execution rescues routing errors by ~4×. First quantitative scaling theory for agent skill libraries. [arXiv:2605.16508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16508)
- **SkillsVote — Lifecycle Governance of Agent Skills** — Treats Agent Skills as a versioned governed experience schema; profiles a million-scale corpus, synthesises verifiable tasks, agentic library-search step before execution. The governance complement to the Scaling Laws paper. [arXiv:2605.18401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18401)
- **Code as Agent Harness** — Reframes code as the operational substrate for reasoning, acting, environment modelling and verification — a "harness" view unifying interface, mechanism and infrastructure layers. [arXiv:2605.18747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18747)
- **ADR: An Agentic Detection System for Enterprise Agentic AI Security** — Claims first production-proven enterprise framework for securing MCP-based AI agents; ADR Sensor for agentic telemetry + Expert reasoning layer + cost-aware inference. Direct evidence MCP security tooling is moving from research to deployed product. [arXiv:2605.17380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17380)
- **TOBench / MM-ToolBench** — 100 executable tasks, 20 subcategories, 27 MCP servers exposing 324 tools — evaluates tool use, computer use and multimodal reasoning together rather than in isolation. [arXiv:2605.16909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16909)
- **FORGE: Self-Evolving Agent Memory With No Weight Updates via Population Broadcast** — Population-based protocol that evolves prompt-injected natural-language memory for hierarchical ReAct agents — failed trajectories become reusable rules, best-performing instance's memory is broadcast, converged instances freeze. [arXiv:2605.16233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)
- **A Survey of Agent Interoperability Protocols: MCP, ACP, A2A, ANP** — Side-by-side of the four leading agent-communication protocols with deployment-context trade-offs. The reference text when picking an agent-runtime substrate. [arXiv:2505.02279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02279)
- **Rethinking How to Remember: Beyond Atomic Facts in Lifelong LLM Agent Memory** — Atomic-fact memory collapses under long-horizon use; structured compositional representation preserves provenance and relational context. Direct relevance to the Dreaming / persistent-memory push at Code with Claude. [arXiv:2605.19952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19952)
- **Predictive Maps of Multi-Agent Reasoning: A Successor-Representation Spectrum** — Borrows the successor representation from RL to predict ahead of time which communication topology a multi-agent LLM team should adopt. [arXiv:2605.11453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11453)
- **Rethinking RL for LLM Reasoning: It's Sparse Policy Selection, Not Capability Learning** — Token-level analysis across model families shows RL's benefit is a sparse correction at high-entropy decision points — RL chooses among capabilities the base model already has rather than teaching new ones. [arXiv:2605.06241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06241)
- **LongMINT** — Memory-under-interference benchmark for long-horizon agents — closes the gap left by LoCoMo/LongMemEval with dynamic interactions, not just static recall. [arXiv:2605.18565](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18565)
- **MARLIN: Multi-Agent Game-Theoretic RL for Sustainable LLM Inference in Cloud Datacenters** — Co-optimises latency, cost and carbon for LLM serving — for platform/operations teams sizing inference fleets. [arXiv:2605.13496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13496)
