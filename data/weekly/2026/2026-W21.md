## The week in 90 seconds

**The week the agent-interop layer split into "open and neutral" versus "consolidated and metered."**

W21 opens on a single, unusually clean structural story. In one working day — Monday 2026-05-18 — both sides of the agent-interop layer staked out their positions. Microsoft moved to own the open layer at the Linux Foundation; Anthropic moved to own the SDK printer that ships code to everyone else. The rest of the week is a test of which side accumulates faster.

The lead story is the Anthropic-Stainless acquisition, reported by The Information at roughly $300M. Stainless has powered the generation of every official Anthropic SDK since the company's earliest days, and it also generates SDKs, CLIs and MCP servers in TypeScript, Python, Go, Java and Kotlin for OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare and Meta. Anthropic's framing — "agents are only as useful as what they can connect to" — reads cleanly when you stack it against the Agent SDK billing split announced May 14: from 15 June, third-party harnesses like OpenClaw, Aider and Cline get separately metered on Pro and Max with monthly credit caps of $20, $100 and $200, and the Magna Capax policy analysis models 12x to 175x effective price increases for harness-heavy workflows. Within four days, Anthropic has both metered the agent harnesses *and* bought the codegen pipeline that ships SDKs to its direct competitors.

The counterweight landed the same morning at Open Source Summit NA. Microsoft put three coordinated artifacts on the table — the open-source Microsoft Agent Framework, the Agent Governance Toolkit for OS-level autonomous-agent compliance, and the Agentic AI Foundation hosted at the Linux Foundation, already the fastest-growing project there. microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners picked up 1,013 GitHub stars in a day. IBM Research and Hugging Face used the same window to launch the Open Agent Leaderboard — the first benchmark that scores the harness rather than the model. It is the IaaS playbook from a decade ago, run in reverse against the lab that just bought its SDK printer.

The third thread sits underneath both: Cursor Composer 2.5 shipped Monday with claimed Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 parity at $0.50 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output, on a Kimi K2.5 base trained with 25x more synthetic tasks than Composer 2. If independent evals confirm parity, it is the first credible read on whether an application-layer company can train its own model and walk away from frontier-lab Agent SDK metering on the workload it controls. Google I/O 2026 opens tomorrow, with leaks converging on Gemini Omni and either Gemini 3.5 or 4.0 — meaning the model layer, the SDK layer and the application layer all moved in the same 24-hour window the keynote walks into.

A few facts the body adds and the lead does not pad: Anthropic's ~$30B round at >$900B is reportedly closing by end of May, past OpenAI's March $852B mark, on Q1 ARR above $44B and 1,000+ customers at $1M+/yr. The White House blocked Anthropic Mythos's expansion to ~70 outside organizations on cyber-misuse and compute-supply grounds while simultaneously drafting an executive workaround for federal agencies — the first time a US administration has tried to gate frontier deployment by recipient list. AISI UK tightened the AI cyber-capability doubling rate from 8 months to 4.7. arXiv banned unchecked AI-generated submissions for one year — the first hard line from a major preprint server. Musk lost the OpenAI suit in under two hours of jury deliberation. The capital story extended into AI-designed drug discovery with Isomorphic Labs' $2.1B Series B (UK Sovereign + MGX + Temasek joining Alphabet, GV, CapitalG).

What to watch through Sunday: independent benchmark readouts on Composer 2.5, the I/O keynote's response to the Stainless move, and whether any other frontier lab follows Anthropic into harness-level metering before the 15 June effective date.

## Top papers
- **FORGE: Self-Evolving Agent Memory With No Weight Updates via Population Broadcast** — Bogdanov, Lung, Kunz, Gao, Taylor, Zaman. _Why notable:_ Population-based protocol that evolves prompt-injected natural-language memory for hierarchical ReAct agents — failed trajectories become reusable rules, best-performing instance's memory is broadcast, converged instances freeze. Concrete blueprint for self-improving agents without touching weights. [arXiv:2605.16233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)
- **Differentiable Mixture-of-Agents Incentivizes Swarm Intelligence of LLMs** — Wu, Lu, Yan et al. _Why notable:_ Replaces hand-wired multi-agent topologies with a differentiable, context-aware router that dynamically activates agents at each reasoning step — implicitly simulating diverse communication graphs without static workflows. Direct fit for the orchestration-design question Sakana's W20 Conductor raised. [arXiv:2605.15706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15706)
- **Agentic Discovery of Neural Architectures: AIRA-Compose and AIRA-Design** — Pepe, Lin, Magka et al. _Why notable:_ LLM agents autonomously design foundation-model architectures beyond Transformers — produces 14 AIRAformer/AIRAhybrid (Transformer-Mamba) variants that beat Llama 3.2 at 1B/3B and find more compute-efficient frontiers. Tangible recursive self-improvement evidence. [arXiv:2605.15871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15871)
- **Argus: Evidence Assembly for Scalable Deep Research Agents** — Zhang, Su, Chen et al. _Why notable:_ Treats deep research as jigsaw assembly: Searcher gathers evidence per sub-query, Navigator maintains a shared evidence graph and identifies missing pieces. Useful pattern for production research agents. [arXiv:2605.16217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16217)
- **RecMem: Recurrence-based Memory Consolidation for Efficient Long-Running LLM Agents** — _Why notable:_ Replaces eager per-step memory writes with recurrence-triggered consolidation; cuts memory-construction token cost by up to 87% while preserving recall quality. Practical fix for the memory-write bottleneck in deployed agents. [arXiv:2605.16045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16045)
- **SkillSmith: Compiling Agent Skills into Boundary-Guided Runtime Interfaces** — _Why notable:_ Compiler-runtime that compiles skill packages offline into minimal executable interfaces — 57.44% token reduction versus exposing raw skill definitions at runtime. Pattern for MCP/tool-use systems that need to keep agent prompts bounded as skill libraries grow. [arXiv:2605.15215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15215)
- **Granite Embedding Multilingual R2** — IBM. _Why notable:_ Open enterprise-scale encoder embedding family spanning 200+ languages with 32K-token context. Direct competitor to BGE/E5/voyage releases for production RAG and cross-lingual retrieval at long-context. [arXiv:2605.13521](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13521)
- **Judge Circuits** — Feldhus et al. _Why notable:_ Mechanistically locates a shared "Latent Evaluator" sub-graph in mid-to-late MLPs of Gemma-3 / Qwen2.5 / Llama-3; zero-ablating it collapses LLM-as-judge scores while preserving world knowledge. First mechanistic-interpretability paper to surface in the daily — explains format-induced LLM-eval inconsistency at the circuit level. [arXiv:2605.16023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16023)
