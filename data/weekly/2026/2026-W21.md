## The week in 90 seconds

**OpenAI files a confidential $1T S-1; Anthropic prints its first profit; the agent-interop layer forks and MCP gets its biggest spec revision since launch.**

Six days reframed the frontier-lab race on three independent axes. The capability story became a listed-equity story. The agent-interop layer split into a consolidated track and an open-foundation track. And the protocol everyone is building on shipped the revision that fixes how it breaks under load.

The headline event arrived Friday. OpenAI submitted a confidential draft S-1 to the SEC with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as joint book-runners, targeting a September listing above $1T on roughly $25B in annualised revenue. The Wall Street Journal landed the counter-story in the same window: Anthropic's Q2 came in at $10.9B in revenue and $559M in operating profit — the first profitable quarter in its history, compute-to-revenue down from 71 cents to 56, the $1M+/yr customer count doubling from 500 to over 1,000 between February and April, annualised run-rate at Q2-end near $43.6B against roughly $9B at the end of 2025. SpaceX's own prospectus accidentally disclosed Anthropic's compute economics — a $1.25B-per-month, $45B-through-2029 Cloud Services Agreement for the full output of Colossus 1 (220,000-plus GPUs and 300 MW in Memphis) with Colossus 2 GB200 ramping through June. Coinbase prediction markets now imply an 85% probability OpenAI lists before Anthropic. The "frontier labs lose money through 2028" consensus died in a single week.

The structural story of the first three days was the fork in the agent-interop layer. On Monday Anthropic acquired Stainless — the codegen pipeline that ships SDKs and MCP servers for OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare and Meta — in a deal The Information pegged above $300M. By Tuesday Anthropic had confirmed the close and begun winding down every hosted Stainless product, including the generator behind OpenAI's Python and Node clients. The most-upvoted Hacker News comment of the week captured the mood: "we just bought OpenAI's front door and we're EOLing it." The same Monday, Microsoft countered by open-sourcing its Agent Framework and Agent Governance Toolkit under the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation, already that foundation's fastest-growing project; IBM Research and Hugging Face launched the Open Agent Leaderboard, the first benchmark that scores the harness rather than the model. On Saturday MCP closed the loop: the 2026-07-28 release candidate landed with a stateless core, a Tasks extension for long-running work, MCP Apps for server-rendered UI, OAuth/OIDC-aligned authorization and a formal deprecation policy — fixing exactly the failure modes Saurabh Kurimella's Friday "Why Most Enterprise MCP Architectures Are Already Broken" had named.

Around that spine, the week was unusually dense. Tuesday brought Andrej Karpathy to Anthropic's new pre-training team under Nick Joseph, Claude Managed Agents with self-hosted sandboxes and MCP tunnels (the missing deployability story for regulated industries), and a KPMG global alliance embedding Claude across 138 countries and 276,000 professionals. Google I/O the same day shipped Gemini 3.5 Flash, Omni Flash, Spark, Antigravity 2.0 and the Agent Payments Protocol with 60-plus launch partners including Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Coinbase, JCB and UnionPay — the first end-to-end agent-commerce stack from a frontier lab — then on Wednesday tripled Flash's API price from $0.50/$3 to $1.50/$9, the first Flash-tier hike anyone can remember. CNBC put hard numbers behind the cost counter-thesis the IPO will eventually have to answer: Claude is roughly 9× the cheapest Chinese alternative on Artificial Analysis benchmarks ($4,811 vs $544 for Zhipu GLM on a standard 10-evaluation workload), and Chinese-model share of OpenRouter usage has climbed from about 1% in 2024 to over 60% in May 2026. The governance thread escalated and then imploded: the Fed and OCC paused cyber-related examinations of the largest US banks in direct response to Anthropic's Mythos surfacing a 17-year-old FreeBSD RCE, FINMA classified Mythos-class capability as "immediate systemic risk," Germany unveiled its Hightech-Agenda on Wednesday — then Trump pulled the AI cybersecurity executive order from the Oval Office on signing day, and Friday's EU AI Act Omnibus deferred Annex III high-risk obligations to December 2027. FINMA's classification is now the highest-jurisdiction formal designation in the global stack. Saturday closed with OpenAI industrialising forward-deployed engineering as a corporate vehicle — the Deployment Company with TPG, Advent, Bain Capital and Brookfield as co-leads, $4B-plus from a 19-firm consortium and the Tomoro acquisition seeding 150 engineers from day one — and with an OpenAI general-purpose reasoning model disproving Erdős's 1946 planar unit-distance conjecture, independently verified by Noga Alon, Melanie Wood and Thomas Bloom.

**Read this first:** [Shifting Alliances in a Changing World](https://stratechery.com/2026/shifting-alliances-in-a-changing-world/) — Ben Thompson reads the SpaceX/Anthropic compute deal, OpenAI's Deployment Company reorg and the Apple-Intel rapprochement as one story: the shift from answer-inference to agentic-inference is reshuffling chips, datacenters and distribution.

- **A Survey of Agent Interoperability Protocols: MCP, ACP, A2A, ANP** — side-by-side of the four leading agent-communication protocols with deployment-context trade-offs; reference text when picking an agent-runtime substrate, pairs with this week's MCP RC + CVE discourse. [arXiv:2505.02279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02279)
- **The Scaling Laws of Skills in LLM Agent Systems** — Two coupled laws across 15 frontier LLMs, 1,141 real-world skills, 3M+ routing/execution decisions: routing accuracy decays logarithmically with library size (R² > 0.97); correct execution rescues routing errors by ~4×. First quantitative scaling theory for agent skill libraries. [arXiv:2605.16508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16508)
- **SkillsVote — Lifecycle Governance of Agent Skills** — Treats Agent Skills as a versioned governed experience schema; agentic library-search step before execution. The governance complement to the Scaling Laws paper. [arXiv:2605.18401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18401)
- **Code as Agent Harness** — Reframes generated code as the orchestration substrate for LLM agents, replacing JSON tool-call plumbing with executable programs that drive tool use. Formal version of the "agent = model + harness" practitioner slogan. [arXiv:2605.18747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18747)
- **FORGE: Self-Evolving Agent Memory With No Weight Updates via Population Broadcast** — Population-based protocol that evolves prompt-injected natural-language memory for hierarchical ReAct agents — failed trajectories become reusable rules, best-performing instance's memory is broadcast, converged instances freeze. Concrete blueprint for self-improving agents without touching model weights. [arXiv:2605.16233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)
- **Agentic Discovery of Neural Architectures: AIRA-Compose and AIRA-Design** — LLM agents autonomously design foundation-model architectures beyond Transformers; 14 AIRAformer / AIRAhybrid (Transformer-Mamba) variants beat Llama 3.2 at 1B/3B scales. Tangible recursive self-improvement evidence. [arXiv:2605.15871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15871)
- **ADR: An Agentic Detection System for Enterprise Agentic AI Security** — Claims first production-proven enterprise framework for securing MCP-based AI agents; ADR Sensor for agentic telemetry + Expert reasoning layer + cost-aware inference. Direct evidence MCP security tooling is moving from research to deployed product. [arXiv:2605.17380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17380)
- **MCP Tool Descriptions Are Smelly!** — Code-smell-style analysis of MCP tool descriptions; augmented descriptions cut agent failures. Empirical companion to the MCP RC + David Matousek MCP-design-discipline piece. [arXiv:2602.14878](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14878v2)
- **MOSS: Self-Evolution through Source-Level Rewriting in Autonomous Agent Systems** — Agent rewrites its own source code as the evolution substrate, larger search space than prompt/skill mutation. Pairs with FORGE. [arXiv:2605.22794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22794)
- **DeltaBox: Millisecond-Level Sandbox Checkpoint/Rollback for Stateful AI Agents** — Delta-encoded full sandbox snapshots cut C/R from hundreds of ms to single-digit ms, making high-frequency tree-search RL over real shells tractable. Practical primitive for the self-hosted-sandbox era. [arXiv:2605.22781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22781)
- **LCGuard: Latent Communication Guard for Safe KV Sharing in Multi-Agent Systems** — Runtime check on KV-cache messages between agents to block jailbreaks/poisoning while preserving efficiency gains. [arXiv:2605.22786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22786)
- **Rethinking RL for LLM Reasoning: It's Sparse Policy Selection, Not Capability Learning** — Token-level analysis across model families shows RL's benefit is a sparse correction at high-entropy decision points — RL chooses among capabilities the base model already has rather than teaching new ones. Important sobering finding for IPO-era capability claims. [arXiv:2605.06241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06241)
