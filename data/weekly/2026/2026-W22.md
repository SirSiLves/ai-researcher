## The week in 90 seconds

**Anthropic captures the security stack and the Vatican stage; by Wednesday the agent valuation curve resets and the AI supply chain bifurcates underneath.**

Three days in, W22 has already turned through three frames. Monday delivered the legitimacy axis — Pope Leo XIV publishing *Magnifica Humanitas* with Anthropic alone on the co-presentation stage, Anthropic's $30B+/$900B round closing the same week, NVIDIA's H100 and A100 rentals up 20% and 15% year-on-year on Q1 FY27 disclosure, and the $67B NextEra-Dominion all-stock merger that names AI data-center demand as the explicit thesis. Tuesday rotated the lens to security and price: the Project Glasswing 30-day update flagged 10,000+ critical and high vulnerabilities across Cloudflare, Mozilla, and open-source projects, the Claude Compliance API shipped with 28 enterprise-security launch partners covering DLP, SASE, SIEM, IAM and e-discovery in one wave, and Anthropic announced that programmatic Claude — Agent SDK, claude-p, GitHub Actions, third-party agents — moves to a separate fixed-credit meter on June 15, an effective 12× to 175× price increase by independent workload math. The UK AI Safety Institute's first quantified Frontier AI Trends Report landed the same day: apprentice-level cyber task success climbed from 9% to 50% in two years, self-replication eval pass rates from under 5% to over 60%, with 20+ pathways by which oversight is expected to degrade.

Wednesday's numbers are the ones the rest of the year will reference. Cognition raised more than $1B at a pre-money valuation near $26B — 2.5× its $10.2B mark from eight months ago, against $492M ARR and 50% month-on-month enterprise-Devin growth sustained for six straight months, with Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, and Santander named as customers. Coming 30 days after Cursor's $50B reset, this is the second autonomous-coding-agent valuation reset inside a month, and it gives Anthropic's June 15 credit meter and OpenAI's Codex pricing a brand-new referent. Fireworks AI is in talks at $15B and Baseten at $11B in the same news cycle — inference-infra is now being priced like a SaaS-plus-data-cloud category rather than utility compute. In parallel, Jensen Huang committed NVIDIA to $150B per year of Taiwan investment (10-15× prior baseline), anchored by a 4,000-person Constellation Taipei HQ targeted for 2030. On the same day, Beijing extended its overseas-travel-approval regime to senior researchers and executives at DeepSeek, Alibaba, and other private AI firms — a restriction previously reserved for nuclear scientists and SOE executives. Read alongside Google DeepMind's $80-90M acqui-hire of 20+ Contextual AI researchers including founder Douwe Kiela, the AI talent map is being redrawn along explicit geopolitical lines.

Europe answered with governance rather than capital. Pieter van Schalkwyk's field note on the 19 May Commission draft of EU AI Act Article 6 made clear that compound systems where multiple agents feed a single decision-making model will be assessed end-to-end as one high-risk system — the 7 May Digital Omnibus deferral to 2027 is operationally irrelevant once the Product Liability Directive (9 Dec 2026) bites, insurer AI exclusions harden, and ISO/IEC 42001 procurement language tightens. BNP Paribas extended its Mistral partnership for three years and became the first European bank to cite Anthropic Mythos-class vulnerability discovery as the explicit sovereign-AI justification, with Mistral engineers embedded across tens of thousands of investment-banking staff. The German cabinet passed an "active cyber-defence" bill giving BSI, BKA, and Bundespolizei powers to shut down systems, redirect traffic, modify data, and conduct counter-attacks — the most aggressive Western "hack-back" mandate to date, the same week Glasswing surfaced its 10,000-vulnerability number. Below the policy layer, sentiment soured: HN's "I'm Tired of Talking to AI" cleared 1,717 points and 823 comments with a 303-deep thread on humans forwarding AI answers verbatim, and the Vatican encyclical entered its first reputational counter-cycle as Tristan Harris labelled Anthropic's Chris Olah co-presentation a "valid ethics-washing concern" — the first material pushback since Anthropic became the safety-first incumbent.

**Read this first:** [The EU AI Act now treats multi-agent systems as one system](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/eu-ai-act-now-treats-multi-agent-systems-one-system-van-schalkwyk-ondmc/) — if you ship anything compound or agentic into Europe, this is the single piece telling you what changes before the June 23 consultation deadline closes.
