## The week in 90 seconds

**The week the frontier-lab race repriced and the coding harness became a public artifact.**

W14 ran two structural stories on the same seven days, and neither side resolved cleanly. On the financial side, OpenAI closed the largest private funding round in history and Anthropic visibly overtook it on headline run-rate. On the technical side, the Claude Code harness leaked, a single researcher demonstrated remote-root exploits in four hours, and a vulnerability campaign that began as a publicity stunt finished the week with a four-digit running total. By Sunday, The Neuron's "AI is outgrowing its institutions" frame was the standard journalist gloss heading into Tuesday's Mythos disclosure.

The lead story is the OpenAI-Anthropic flip. On Tuesday March 31, OpenAI closed its $122B round at an $852B post-money valuation, with SoftBank and a16z co-leading alongside MGX, TPG, D.E. Shaw and T. Rowe Price, and Amazon, Nvidia and Microsoft participating. The company confirmed $2B per month in revenue, 900 million weekly ChatGPT users, enterprise above 40% of revenue, and an IPO target of "as soon as late 2026." Wednesday April 1, Anthropic disclosed crossing $30B in ARR run-rate, up from $14B in February and $19B in March, with the count of accounts spending more than $1M doubling from roughly 500 to over 1,000 in under two months. OpenAI publicly disputes the gross-revenue accounting by about $8B and the dispute stays unresolved, but the growth-curve gap is the durable signal. The funding ceiling for the rest of the frontier-lab race is now set, and the metric underwriting OpenAI's $852B has a credible challenger.

The counterweight ran on the harness layer. Late Tuesday, a missing `.npmignore` shipped a 59.8MB source map for Claude Code v2.1.88 — roughly 513,000 lines of unobfuscated TypeScript across about 1,900 files, including a hidden "BUDDY" Tamagotchi-companion layer and a user-frustration-detection module. A clean-room fork hit 50,000 GitHub stars within two hours; Anthropic's DMCA wave reached around 8,100 repositories before Boris Cherny apologised and narrowed enforcement to one repo plus 96 forks. Two days later, Cursor 3 "Glass" shipped with the leak's harness vocabulary intact — Agents Window, Design Mode, Agent Tabs, per-agent `/worktree` isolation — and Composer 2 alongside. The same week, Nicholas Carlini published two working remote-root exploits for FreeBSD CVE-2026-4747 written end-to-end by Claude on a 4-hour first attempt, and the Month-of-AI-Discovered-Bugs campaign ran from zero to roughly 1,000 validated high-severity vulns by Sunday. Three independent maintainers — Greg Kroah-Hartman, Daniel Stenberg, Willy Tarreau — confirmed the kernel security list flipping from a handful of slop reports per week to five to ten real AI-assisted reports per day. The "thousands of zero-days" framing the Mythos announcement would use forty-eight hours later stopped being marketing.

The third thread sits underneath both: the agent-control-plane war crystallised in a few days. Salesforce reframed Slack as the agentic operating system on Tuesday with thirty-plus Slackbot AI features, Slack MCP server GA, and Claude as the back-end model — the largest enterprise-platform validation of MCP to date. Microsoft answered on Friday with its governance day: the open-source Agent Governance Toolkit (first to address all ten OWASP agentic-AI risks with sub-millisecond policy enforcement), Copilot Studio multi-agent GA, Agent Framework 1.0 GA, and forty-one M365 Copilot April updates. SAP's API Policy v4/2026 banned external agents from independently scheduling calls without a human in the loop; ServiceNow's Action Fabric kept the system open but routed every agent through governance. By Sunday, Block had donated Goose to the Linux Foundation alongside MCP and AGENTS.md, formalising the open-standards bet.

**Read this first:** [MAD Bugs: Claude wrote a full FreeBSD remote kernel RCE in 4 hours — Nicholas Carlini](https://blog.calif.io/p/mad-bugs-claude-wrote-a-full-freebsd) — the single best primary source on why W14's empirical cyber numbers, not the funding round, are the data point everything in April leans on.

## Top papers
- **Meta-Harness: End-to-End Optimization of Model Harnesses** — anon. _Why notable:_ Treats harness (context manager / retriever / proposer / scheduler) as a searchable object; outer-loop rewrites it from execution traces; wins TerminalBench-2. Ships the day the Claude Code harness leaks. [arXiv:2603.28052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28052)
- **MonitorBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Chain-of-Thought Monitorability** — anon. _Why notable:_ Monitorability drops sharply when structural reasoning isn't required; both open- and closed-weight models fail. [arXiv:2603.28590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590)
- **Therefore I am. I Think** — anon. _Why notable:_ Reasoning models encode action/tool-call decisions in pre-generation activations; CoT may be partly post-hoc rationalisation. [arXiv:2604.01202](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01202)
- **Terminal Agents Suffice for Enterprise Automation** — anon. _Why notable:_ Plain terminal coding agent matches or beats heavily MCP-augmented stacks — counter-thesis to "more tools = better." [arXiv:2604.00073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00073)
- **Single-Agent LLMs Outperform Multi-Agent Systems on Multi-Hop Reasoning Under Equal Thinking Token Budgets** — Dat Tran, Douwe Kiela. _Why notable:_ When budgets are held equal, single-agent matches/beats multi-agent — corrective the same week MS Copilot Studio multi-agent goes GA. [arXiv:2604.02460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02460)
- **EpochX: Building the Infrastructure for an Emergent Agent Civilization** — Huacan Wang et al. _Why notable:_ Infrastructure for large-scale MAS with persistent state — moves beyond toy environments. [arXiv:2603.27304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27304)
- **Composer 2 Technical Report** — Cursor Research (Chan, Shalaby, Wettig et al.). _Why notable:_ Cursor's second-gen in-IDE coding model; counterpart to the Anthropic harness post. [arXiv:2603.24477](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24477)
- **ASI-Evolve: AI Accelerates AI** — Weixian Xu, Tiantian Mi. _Why notable:_ Agentic learn-design-experiment-analyze loop discovers 105 novel linear-attention architectures; gains over human baselines in math + biomedicine. [arXiv:2603.29640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29640)
- **Investigating Autonomous Agent Contributions in the Wild** — Razvan Mihai Popescu, David Gros (TU Delft). _Why notable:_ 110K OSS PRs across Codex/Claude Code/Copilot/Jules/Devin — agent code has elevated churn and lower survival vs human-authored. [arXiv:2604.00917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00917)
- **Brevity Constraints Reverse Performance Hierarchies in Language Models** — anon. _Why notable:_ Length budget flips bigger-is-better — practical implication for evals that ignore length. [arXiv:2604.00025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00025)
